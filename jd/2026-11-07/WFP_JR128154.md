JOB PURPOSE:

To perform routine site maintenance tasks to support safe, comfortable and reliable working environment. To serve as the Fuel Focal Point, ensuring the safe, accurate and accountable management of fuel stocks and associated records. Support effective fuel availability through regular monitoring, reconciliation, reporting and timely replenishment.KEY RESPONSIBILITIES (not all-inclusive, nor exhaustive):

- Provide basic site maintenance and installation work (e.g. generators, water pumps, water heaters, etc.) including carpentry work and basic site repairs, ensuring well-functioning equipment.
- Provide portering and cleaning services (e.g. moving assets, reorganizing/installing workstations, assisting in setting up conference/meeting/event locations, removing waste materials, emergency cleaning, etc.), to facilitate organized and safe work environment.
- Contribute to keeping accurate inventory records through physical check and maintenance of lists/database, and arrange disposal of identified equipment and materials, strictly following supervisor’s instructions and WFP assets management guidelines.
- Report on works undertaken, services provided, materials used for installations/repairs including accurate generator fuel logbooks, to support accurate accounting.
- Serve as the Fuel Focal Point, overseeing fuel receiving, storage, issuance, monitoring, reconciliation, and reporting activities.
- Ensure fuel is issued only against duly approved fuel coupons and that all supporting documentation is complete and properly maintained.
- .Conduct fuel dipstick measurements and monitor daily fuel stock balances to ensure accurate stock control.
- Maintain accurate and up-to-date fuel records in FMS, fuel logbooks, spreadsheets, and other required systems and documentation.
- Participate in monthly physical fuel inventory counts and reconciliation exercises, ensuring that any variances are identified and reported.
- Receive fuel deliveries and transfers, verify quantities against supporting documentation, and immediately report any discrepancies.
- Conduct periodic spot checks to verify the accuracy of fuel issuance and compliance with established procedures.
- Prepare and submit daily and monthly fuel reports, ensuring that all required supporting documentation is complete and accurate.
- Monitor fuel stock levels and initiate replenishment requests in a timely manner when stocks reach established minimum levels.
- Ensure that fuel handling equipment, pumps, fire extinguishers, and associated safety devices are operational and maintained in accordance with applicable safety standards.
- Provide technical guidance and support to personnel involved in fuel management and handling activities.
- Identify and quantify plumbing materials and spare parts required for maintenance activities; monitor stock levels; and initiate timely replenishment requests to ensure uninterrupted service delivery and completion of works to the required standard
- Conduct routine inspections, preventive maintenance, and repairs of plumbing and drainage systems, including pipes, valves, fittings, drains, fixtures, and related installations, to ensure their safe and reliable operation.
- Perform any other related duties required.
