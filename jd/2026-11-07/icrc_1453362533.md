What we do

Since 1863, the International Committee of the Red Cross (ICRC) has worked to preserve human dignity and relieve suffering caused by war and armed violence.

In collaboration with our Red Cross and Red Crescent partners around the world, we do everything we can to deliver life-saving aid, reconnect families and locate missing people – helping those who need it most, regardless of who they are and what side of the front line they are on.

We engage with authorities and armed forces on all sides, often confidentially, pressing for access to detainees to improve their living conditions and urging compliance with international humanitarian law to protect non-combatants, including from digital threats.

Purpose

Under the supervision of the Economic Security (EcoSec) Team Leader, the EcoSec Generalist supports in planning, implementing, assessing, monitoring and evaluating Economic Security programmes in Lebanon. The position also acts as a focal point for Assessment & Evaluation (A&E), programme reporting and PAM-related (Project and Activity Management Platform) processes, and contributes to Cash and Market activities, supporting the Cash and Market Specialist as required.

Accountabilities & functional responsibilities

- Under direct and technical supervision of EcoSec Team Leader, plans, implements, monitors and evaluates EcoSec program activities in accordance with Project Cycle Management and relevant ICRC procedures and guidelines.

- Understands the working context (security, socioeconomic, cultural, and political environment), follows its evolution, and contributes to define the relevant operational EcoSec response and strategies regularly.

- Contributes to programme design, planning and follow-up, including concept notes, proposals, operational plans and programme-related risks.

- Provides regular feedback to EcoSec Team Leader on progress and achievements, lessons learned, gaps, field challenges.

- Prepares relevant documents related to finance, procurement, and logistics, in line with project reporting requirements and ICRC internal procedures.

- Ensures the planning and follow-up of EcoSec assessment and evaluation activities, including assessments, monitoring and Post Distribution Monitoring (PDMs), and contributes to the development and adaptation of relevant data-collection tools.

- Writes Minutes of Meeting, assessment, monitoring, and evaluation reports and contributes to institutional reporting by autonomously collecting economic security data, using data-collection tools and devices and performs basic qualitative and quantitative data analysis.

- Supports identification or relevant stakeholders and interlocutors and maintains key relationships with humanitarian actors, private sector, and authorities, in agreement with hierarchical and technical supervisors.

- On request, can produce high quality assessment, monitoring and evaluation reports and presents findings through presentation internally and externally, and with relevant visual

- Maintains and organizes data on EcoSec, ensuring that all necessary EcoSec related files and logistics data are properly collected and achieved.

- Contributes to the consolidation of contacts relevant to EcoSec for EcoSec interventions.

- Supports data anaylsis to feed all EcoSec Mandatory and ad-hoc reporting (cash assistance and all needed institutional reports)

- Ensures appropriate management, organization and quality of EcoSec programme data, documentation and records and coordinates with relevant internal departments as required
