&lt;h3&gt;&lt;span class="emphasis-2"&gt;&lt;b&gt;&lt;u&gt;What We Can Achieve Together:&lt;/u&gt;&lt;/b&gt;&lt;/span&gt;&lt;/h3&gt;&lt;br /&gt;&lt;p&gt;The Fisheries Policy Associate directly supports TNC’s engagement in Regional Fisheries Management Organisations (RFMOs) and our efforts to ensure effective implementation and use of electronic monitoring systems. For decades, fisheries managers have lacked timely and reliable data to keep pace with the complexity of modern fishing operations, a key barrier to sustainably managing industrial fisheries and protecting the health of our oceans and the communities and economies that depend on them. The position works within established fisheries policy strategies and does not independently set TNC policy positions, make institutional commitments, or serve as the final decision-maker for engagement strategy. This position plays a key role in supporting TNC’s fisheries strategy, providing the necessary administrative research, analysis and writing skills for the team to deliver and have impact. They serve as a liaison and informational contact between the organization and country, sub-national, local agencies, multilateral institutions, business and/or private groups.&lt;/p&gt;&lt;p&gt;This is a term-limited, global remote position ending in June of 2029, with a possibility for extension.&lt;/p&gt;&lt;br /&gt;&lt;p&gt;The location for this position is negotiable (globally) anywhere TNC is authorized to conduct business, with a preference for the Americas. Canadian positions are limited to the following provinces: Alberta, Ontario and the Northwest Territories.&lt;/p&gt;&lt;br /&gt;&lt;p&gt;&lt;b&gt;ESSENTIAL FUNCTIONS&lt;/b&gt;&lt;/p&gt;&lt;p&gt;The Fisheries Policy Associate provides research and policy analysis in support of The Nature Conservancy’s engagement in Regional Fisheries Management Organisations (RFMOs) and our efforts to ensure effective implementation and use of electronic monitoring systems to promote sustainable fisheries. They build and facilitate internal networks, coordinate meetings, agendas, presentations, and support drafting of advocacy materials that will underpin our RFMO engagement strategies. They provide services that assist in influencing the outcome of policy and/or corporate initiatives at the international, country, sub-national, and local levels. They develop strategic partnerships with relevant agencies, conservation organizations, and industry to advance the organization’s conservation agenda. The Policy Associate reports to the International Fisheries Policy Advisor, Global Ocean Policy in the Policy and Public Funding Business Unit. Ability to travel internationally is required for this role. At least 20% of working time will be spent attending RFMO commission, committee, technical working group, and stakeholder meetings across multiple regions each year.&lt;/p&gt;&lt;br /&gt;&lt;p&gt;&lt;b&gt;RESPONSIBILITIES &amp;
