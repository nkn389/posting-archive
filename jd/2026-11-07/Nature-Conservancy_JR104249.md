&lt;h3&gt;&lt;b&gt;YOUR POSITION WITH TNC&lt;/b&gt;&lt;/h3&gt;&lt;p&gt; &lt;/p&gt;&lt;p&gt;The Maine Chapter of The Nature Conservancy seeks a knowledgeable, energetic Forest Stewardship Manager to manage the Wolastoq - St. John River Forest. The Forest Stewardship Manager will help lead the next chapter of TNC Maine’s work at the WSJRF and contribute to a broader Maine forest stewardship strategy. &lt;/p&gt;&lt;br /&gt;&lt;p&gt;This position will manage and advance ecological monitoring, forest stewardship, and partnership projects that integrate ecological, financial, cultural, carbon, and climate considerations into forest management decisions. The position will support TNC’s credibility and trusted conservation voice in northern Maine forest management by ensuring that TNC’s own lands are managed responsibly, transparently, and in alignment with organizational goals, while also maintaining relationships and learning networks with forestry, conservation, Indigenous, academic, agency, and landowner partners. Communicating and sharing forest management and conservation outcomes and lessons learned with Conservancy staff, partners, contractors, and external audiences are important parts of the work.&lt;/p&gt;&lt;p&gt; &lt;/p&gt;&lt;h3&gt;&lt;b&gt;ESSENTIAL FUNCTIONS&lt;/b&gt;&lt;/h3&gt;&lt;p&gt; &lt;/p&gt;&lt;p&gt;Responsibilities include oversight of forest management planning and implementation, ecological reserve inventory and monitoring, roads and infrastructure, commercial sugaring lease, public access, carbon offset project obligations, FSC certification compliance, relationship management and other legal and stewardship commitments.&lt;/p&gt;&lt;p&gt; &lt;/p&gt;&lt;p&gt;This is a hands-on, field-oriented position with substantial time expected in the woods and regular overnight travel to remote areas of northern Maine. The Forest Stewardship Manager works under minimal and distant supervision and is expected to make independent decisions based on analysis, experience, and judgment. The position is embedded with a diverse statewide Land Management team and contributes to the oversight of 2-3 other properties in western Maine as well as policy discussions on land stewardship across the state. The position also requires the ability to gain cooperation from outside parties to accomplish program goals and to communicate effectively with technical and non-technical audiences.&lt;/p&gt;&lt;h3&gt; &lt;/h3&gt;&lt;h3&gt;&lt;b&gt;A LITTLE ABOUT TNC MAINE&lt;/b&gt;&lt;/h3&gt;&lt;p&gt; &lt;/p&gt;&lt;p&gt;Founded in 1951, The Nature Conservancy (TNC) is a global conservation organization dedicated to conserving the lands and waters on which all life depends. Guided by science, we create innovative, on-the-ground solutions to the world’s toughest challenges so that nature and people can thrive together. We work collaboratively with local communities, governments, the private sector, Indigenous partners, academic institutions, and other conservation organizations to advance durable conservation outcomes.&lt;/p&gt;&lt;br /&gt;&lt;p&gt;The Nature Conservancy in Maine owns and manages one of the most significant conservation land portfolios in the eastern United States. Its centerpiece is the Wolastoq - St. John River Forest (WSJRF) – roughly 165,000 acres in the unorganized territory of northern Maine, acquired in 1998 in what was then the largest private conservation land purchase in the Northeast. The property comprises over half of TNC Maine’s entire land base, safeguards a significant portion of a 140-mile free-flowing river with little development along its length, and helps ensure long-term habitat connectivity in the Northern Appalachian forest. Connected conserved lands extend through western Maine and into New Hampshire. Its two ecological reserves are among the ten largest in the state.&lt;/p&gt;&lt;br /&gt;&lt;p&gt;A Carbon offset project covers approximately 125,000 acres of the WSJRF, and approximately half the total acreage is in internally designated reserve areas. Some eighty miles of gravel roads offer limited access and comprise much of the property management responsibilities with maintenance to their surfaces and crossing structures.&lt;/p&gt;&lt;br /&gt;&lt;p&gt;TNC’s ownership and stewardship of this property has supported a generation of ambitious conservation – from the Penobscot River restoration and the Debsconeag Lakes Wilderness Area to ongoing Wabanaki land return and the emerging Kennebec restoration project – and it offers a grand opportunity to humbly and transparently demonstrate one model of how large-scale conservation land ownership can work in the Maine north woods.&lt;/p&gt;&lt;br /&gt;&lt;p&gt;This position is only available for individuals located within the United States.&lt;/p&gt;&lt;p&gt; &lt;/p&gt;&lt;h3&gt;&lt;b&gt; &lt;/b&gt;&lt;/h3&gt;&lt;h3&gt;&lt;b&gt;RESPONSIBILITIES &amp; SCOPE&lt;/b&gt;&lt;/h3&gt;&lt;p&gt; &lt;/p&gt;&lt;h3&gt;Forest Stewardship and Property Management&lt;/h3&gt;&lt;ul&gt;&lt;li&gt;&lt;span style="color:#242429"&gt;Manage all aspects of forest stewardship to meet Conservancy goals for the Wolastoq - St. John River Forest and to meet FSC certification standards, carbon project standards, and other legal commitments.&lt;/span&gt;&lt;/li&gt;&lt;/ul&gt;&lt;ul&gt;&lt;li&gt;&lt;span style="color:#242429"&gt;Plan, implement, monitor, and report on ecologically sound forest harvests, restoration projects, stewardship activities, and other conservation forestry projects that advance habitat, resilience, carbon, water, and ecological management goals.&lt;/span&gt;&lt;/li&gt;&lt;/ul&gt;&lt;ul&gt;&lt;li&gt;&lt;span style="color:#242429"&gt;Work closely with Northern Maine Land Manager to maintain ecological reserve areas, managed forest areas, roads, boundaries, signage, tools, equipment, and field data needed for monitoring and project management.&lt;/span&gt;&lt;/li&gt;&lt;/ul&gt;&lt;ul&gt;&lt;li&gt;&lt;span style="color:#242429"&gt;Coordinate and oversee contractors, vendors, consultants, interns, volunteers, or staff in the field or office, including responsibility for project performance, safety, quality control, timelines, and deliverables.&lt;/span&gt;&lt;/li&gt;&lt;/ul&gt;&lt;h3&gt; &lt;/h3&gt;&lt;h3&gt;Ecological Monitoring, Data, and Adaptive Management&lt;/h3&gt;&lt;ul&gt;&lt;li&gt;&lt;span style="color:#242429"&gt;Coordinate ecological monitoring, forest inventory, forest health assessment, GIS data management, and other information systems needed to guide adaptive management of the Wolastoq - St. John River Forest.&lt;/span&gt;&lt;/li&gt;&lt;/ul&gt;&lt;ul&gt;&lt;li&gt;&lt;span style="color:#242429"&gt;Work with TNC scientists, stewardship staff, external researchers, and partners to identify priority ecological monitoring needs, including forest condition, rare features, water resources, climate change impacts, and long-term stewardship outcomes and lead field data collection efforts&lt;/span&gt;&lt;/li&gt;&lt;/ul&gt;&lt;p&gt; &lt;/p&gt;&lt;h3&gt;Operational Responsibility and Work Environment&lt;/h3&gt;&lt;ul&gt;&lt;li&gt;Maintain regular field presence on the Wolastoq - St. John River Forest through multi-day site visits and participate in remote and occasional (roughly quarterly) in-person meetings at The Nature Conservancy&#39;s Brunswick, Maine office.&lt;/li&gt;&lt;li&gt;Utilize (modern) field accommodations at Baker Lake to support stewardship activities, monitoring efforts, project implementation, and engagement with staff, partners, and visitors.&lt;/li&gt;&lt;li&gt;Maintain a residence that enables consistent access to the Wolastoq - St. John River Forest, ideally within approximately three hours of Baker Lake. Residence in Maine is not required (the Jackman area in western Maine could work well)&lt;/li&gt;&lt;li&gt;Conduct work in variable weather conditions, at remote locations, on difficult and sometimes hazardous terrain, and under physically demanding conditions.&lt;/li&gt;&lt;li&gt;Operate and travel safely in support of stewardship and conservation activities, maintaining a valid driver&#39;s license.&lt;/li&gt;&lt;li&gt;Manage project budgets and financial resources, including vendor and contractor procurement, contract administration, budget planning, and support for fundraising objectives.&lt;/li&gt;&lt;li&gt;Ensure program compliance organizational policies, legal requirements, certification standards, and other applicable program obligations.&lt;/li&gt;&lt;li&gt;Exercise independent judgment and decision-making with limited supervision, applying analysis, experience, and professional expertise to achieve conservation and operational objectives.&lt;/li&gt;&lt;/ul&gt;&lt;p&gt;&lt;b&gt; &lt;/b&gt;&lt;/p&gt;&lt;h3&gt;&lt;b&gt;MINIMUM QUALIFICATIONS&lt;/b&gt;&lt;/h3&gt;&lt;p&gt; &lt;/p&gt;&lt;ul&gt;&lt;li&gt;BA/BS, law or business degree and 5 years’ experience in natural resource management or related field or equivalent combination of education and experience. &lt;/li&gt;&lt;li&gt;Experience with forest management, ecological inventory and monitoring, conservation forestry, or natural systems.&lt;/li&gt;&lt;li&gt;Experience managing complex or multiple projects, including budgets, contracts, timelines, vendors, partners, or staff/contractors.&lt;/li&gt;&lt;li&gt;Experience communicating with technical and non-technical audiences, in writing and verbally.&lt;/li&gt;&lt;li&gt;Experience using GPS and/or GIS technologies for data collection, mapping, analysis, or documentation, and using standard office software.&lt;/li&gt;&lt;li&gt;Experience working with partners, contractors, agencies, landowners, community groups, or conservation organizations.&lt;/li&gt;&lt;li&gt;Must have a valid driver’s license.&lt;/li&gt;&lt;/ul&gt;&lt;br /&gt;&lt;h3&gt;&lt;b&gt;DESIRED QUALIFICATIONS&lt;/b&gt;&lt;/h3&gt;&lt;ul&gt;&lt;li&gt;
