Purpose of Job

Part of a wider Design and Transformation team in CTO, the Associate – Design & Transformation (D&T) works with business stakeholders to design and implement change with a focus on requirements elicitation and business case formulation (using a range of Agile techniques).

The Associate – D&T will work in a range of settings including smaller scale Project Management or Business Analysis on varied-sized enhancements/projects. The team member will work closely with business teams, IT teams like architecture and application teams as well as specialist consultants, as applicable. Where required, they will work in proximity with the Bank’s technology Capability teams to ensure services are technically enabled.

The successful candidate will start as the dedicated Business Analyst on Phase 2 of the Payment Ecosystem project. This is a priority MYIP initiative, delivered with Operations & Service Management (OSM) and the Treasury, Risk & Operations IT capability.

Phase 2 builds on the MVP, which covered loans and bonds. It extends the central Payment Repository and straight-through processing to Equities, Guarantees, Treasury and PPAD. It also introduces a central Payment Service to route payment flows across channels, and improves integrations with SWIFT and integrity screening. Controls and reporting will be strengthened as well.

In this role you will own business analysis across the delivery lifecycle. You will work closely with the Product Owner, business subject matter experts and the engineering team. You will turn business needs into clear, development-ready requirements, maintain a well-refined backlog and help ensure work meets the agreed Definition of Ready before it is built. You will also support target process design, testing and business readiness. Over time, the role is expected to move from project delivery into wider support for the capability area.

Background

The D&T team collaborates with the business to design effective and efficient operating models and advise on their planning and execution enablement, handing off to appropriate teams for the actual execution of the change. Through strong relationships and consultative approach, the team shapes and defines transformational projects aligning functional designs to the strategic objectives and vision of the Bank.

The Associate - D&T will be assigned to 1-2 change projects or initiatives in either a project manager/BA capacity, or as a BA required to work with the project team. In doing so, they will need to interact with specialist consultants and a broad group of Bank staff professionals. The role requires good internal/external stakeholder management at varied levels and must build credible working relationships.

Accountabilities & Responsibilities

The role will be responsible for engaging with business stakeholders as part of a project or as part of the demand management process (early assessment of IT initiatives).

Core D&T responsibilities:

- Work as assigned on 1-2 projects, either as PM/BA on a small project, or as BA working closely with product owners, SME, business stakeholders and other IT disciplines to transpose valued needs into requirements captured by appropriate methods and artefacts.

- Carry out investigation, capture and prioritisation of business requirements using a variety of Agile techniques.

- Capture new technology request outlines from the Business and help to formulate the supporting business cases for funding.

- Engage with stakeholders through interviews, workshops, focus groups and other engagement methods.

- Ensure appropriate requirements sign off from key stakeholders, where necessary.

- Perform analysis across end-to-end business process to understand change impact, where necessary creating alignment from technical to business capabilities.

- Assess data sets and evaluate differences and bottlenecks.

- Promote Agile principles and practices to continuously improve working practices within the team

- Implement and maintain requirements traceability as these mature through sprints/ iterations.

- Contribute towards team best practice.

- Support testing, business readiness, adoption of functionality by business users as it is incrementally deployed into production.

IT Capability alignment responsibilities:

- Work alongside Product Owner and Capability Lead and other leads in the capability (e.g. Architecture, QA, etc.) as a Scrum BA, where agreed with the relevant D&T Principal

Other accountabilities:

- Stay up to up-date with current market and industry trends by attending internal learning opportunities and external events such as seminars and industry events, bringing new thinking and ideas back to the team.
