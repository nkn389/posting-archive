Guided by science, we create innovative, on-the-ground solutions to our world&#39;s toughest challenges so that nature and people can thrive together. We are tackling the twin crises facing our planet – climate change and biodiversity loss.&lt;/p&gt;&lt;br /&gt;&lt;p&gt;One of our core values is our commitment to diversity. To learn more about our global organisation, visit www.nature.org.&lt;/p&gt;&lt;br /&gt;&lt;p&gt;The Nature Conservancy first commenced operations in Australia in 2002. To learn more about our work in Australia visit www.natureaustralia.org.au.&lt;/p&gt;&lt;br /&gt;&lt;p&gt;YOUR POSITION WITH TNC&lt;/p&gt;&lt;br /&gt;&lt;p&gt;The Data and Science Director (Australia) provides senior scientific, technical and strategic leadership for conservation initiatives and programs across TNC Australia. The role leads the Data, Science and Monitoring, Evaluation and Learning (MEL) functions, manages and develops a multidisciplinary team, and ensures that conservation strategies are underpinned by robust science, high‑quality data and credible evidence of impact. The position operates at an enterprise level, supporting conservation teams and organisational leadership through systems leadership, program oversight and organisational accountability.&lt;/p&gt;&lt;br /&gt;&lt;p&gt;ESSENTIAL FUNCTIONS&lt;/p&gt;&lt;br /&gt;&lt;p&gt;The Data and Science Director (Australia) oversees the strategic governance, collection and management of data supporting conservation strategies and programs across TNC Australia and is accountable for the quality, integrity and currency of data submitted to TNC’s global reporting platforms, including The Hub. The role leads the classification of targets, assessment of data and evidence, and application of science to prioritise conservation interventions at scale. The position provides senior oversight of scientific research, analysis and reporting, and leads the development of science‑based proposals and programs that strengthen organisational decision‑making, accountability and impact.&lt;/p&gt;&lt;br /&gt;&lt;p&gt;RESPONSIBILITIES &amp; SCOPE&lt;/p&gt;&lt;br /&gt;&lt;p&gt;• Leads the Data, Science and MEL functions for TNC Australia, ensuring alignment with Conservation by Design, organisational priorities and global standards. Contributes to enterprise decision‑making through senior leadership forums including the Conservation Management Team and the Australia Operations Team, and leads organisation‑wide initiatives across culture, systems and governance. Identifies and addresses strategic risks and opportunities related to science, data quality and evidence of impact.&lt;/p&gt;&lt;p&gt;• Owns and manages a portfolio of complex programs spanning science, data systems, MEL and performance reporting, and oversees governance of organisational science and data infrastructure, including quality, compliance and audit readiness. Leads high‑risk organisational deliverables such as global reporting and data quality initiatives, and holds program‑level responsibility for the Barbara Thomas Fellowship and related initiatives, including program design, partnerships, selection processes and oversight of Fellows.&lt;/p&gt;&lt;p&gt;• Leads and develops a multidisciplinary team, setting priorities, managing performance and building capability across the Business Unit. Oversees workforce planning, recruitment, role design and succession, and provides leadership for funded, fellowship‑supported and project‑embedded roles.&lt;/p&gt;&lt;p&gt;• Acts as a senior scientific authority, advising leadership and teams on scientific quality and application, representing TNC Australia in external scientific, academic and government forums, and overseeing applied research outputs and translation of science into policy and practice.&lt;/p&gt;&lt;p&gt;• Provides scientific leadership for funding proposals and internal funding mechanisms, secures and stewards funding through robust science and defensible impact claims, manages organisational and reputational risk associated with scientific outputs and public claims, and oversees complex vendor, partner and grant arrangements.&lt;/p&gt;&lt;p&gt;• May work in variable weather conditions, at remote locations, in hazardous terrain and under physically demanding circumstances.&lt;/p&gt;&lt;br
