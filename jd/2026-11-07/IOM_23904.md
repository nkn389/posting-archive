Project Context and Scope
The Project is implemented in Armenia to further strengthen the capacities of the national authorities to tackle cross-border organized crime and independent border management capacities. The primary national partners benefiting from its outcomes are the National Security Service of the Republic of Armenia (NSS) and its Border Guard Troops (BGT). The Project will build on the results achieved in recent years and will further advance the alignment of the border guard training/ educational system and standards with European and international best practices, enhance capacity to autonomously manage borders through enhanced secondary inspection as well as capacity to detect and counter irregular travellers, including terrorists and serious criminals through setting up Advance Passenger Information (API) and Passenger Name Record (PNR) data.

Tasks to be performed under this contract
Under the overall supervision of the Head of Office and direct supervision of the Project Coordinator, the International Consultant shall deliver the following outputs:

Inception and Needs Assessment

- Review existing NSS training documentation, legal framework, and operational requirements.

- Conduct consultations with NSS leadership, Border Guard Troops command, instructors, and relevant stakeholders.

- Identify training needs and competency gaps.

- Prepare an Inception Report outlining methodology, work plan, and curriculum development approach

Competency Framework Development

- Define occupational profile(s) for contract border guards.

- Develop learning outcomes and competencies required for operational deployment.

- Align competencies with EU IBM principles and relevant FRONTEX standards where applicable.

Curriculum Design

- Develop a complete six-month training curriculum, including:

- Curriculum structure and sequencing.

- Modules and subjects.

- Learning outcomes for each module.

- Recommended contact hours.

- Theory/practical training ratio.

- Training methods.

- Required training facilities and equipment.

- Evaluation and assessment framework.

Development of Training Materials Framework

- Propose outlines for lesson plans.

- Recommend practical exercises and simulation-based learning scenarios.

- Provide guidance on assessment tools and examination procedures.

Capacity Building of NSS Instructors

- Develop a capacity-building plan for instructors responsible for delivering the new curriculum.

- Design and conduct a Training of Trainers (ToT) program.

- Introduce competency-based training methodologies, practical and simulation-based learning approaches, and learner assessment techniques.

- Provide mentoring and guidance on curriculum implementation, monitoring, and evaluation.

- Recommend mechanisms for continuous professional development of instructors.

Validation and Finalization

- Facilitate a curriculum validation workshop with NSS representatives.

- Incorporate comments and recommendations.

- Submit the final curriculum package.

Performance indicators for the evaluation of results

- All deliverables submitted according to the agreed timeline.

- All reports and curriculum documents accepted without requiring substantial revision.

- Curriculum demonstrates alignment with competency-based vocational education principles and relevant international border management standards.

- NSS stakeholders express satisfaction with the relevance and usability of the curriculum and training framework.

- At least 80% of workshop and ToT participants report increased confidence in implementing the new curriculum.

- Final curriculum approved for piloting and/or institutional adoption by the NSS Border Guard Troops.
