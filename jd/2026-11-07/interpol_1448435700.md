SUMMARY OF THE ASSIGNED DUTIES, INCLUDING GOALS AND OBJECTIVES OF THE POST

Within the Executive Directorate Legal Affairs, the Notices and Diffusions Taskforce (NDTF) Sub-Directorate is a multidisciplinary team composed of legal, law enforcement, and operational experts. It ensures that Notices and Diffusions comply with INTERPOL’s rules. The team liaises with National Central Bureaus and national authorities, providing guidance, training, and capacity building. It also addresses legal and operational issues related to data processing and implements corrective measures when required.

The Counsel Coordinator serves as a senior legal expert within NDTF, providing authoritative legal advice and leading the legal review of complex and sensitive cases related to Notices, Diffusions and other international police cooperation requests. The role ensures the consistent application of INTERPOL's legal framework, coordinates the work of legal staff, advises management on strategic and policy matters, and contributes to the development of legal guidance, policy and operational practices.

PRINCIPAL DUTIES AND ACTIVITIES

DUTY 1 : Senior Legal Advice and Review of Complex Cases

- Provide authoritative legal advice on matters relating to the review, publication, maintenance and deletion of Notices, Diffusions and other requests for international police cooperation.

- Lead the legal assessment of complex, sensitive, novel or high-risk cases involving legal, operational, reputational, political or human rights considerations.

- Formulate legal recommendations for management and support decision-making on cases presenting significant legal implications.

- Ensure the consistent interpretation and application of INTERPOL's legal framework across the NDTF.

DUTY 2 : Coordination of Legal Activities and Quality Assurance

- Coordinate and monitor the legal work of Legal Officers and other staff assigned to case reviews, projects and special initiatives.

- Review and validate legal analyses, memoranda, communications and other legal products prepared by team members.

- Establish and maintain quality-control mechanisms, legal review methodologies and operational standards.

- Ensure consistency in legal positions, recommendations and practices across the Task Force.

DUTY 3 : Member Country and Stakeholder Engagement

- Lead legal engagement with National Central Bureaus (NCBs), judicial authorities and other competent national entities regarding complex cases and legal issues.

- Develop and maintain effective working relationships with external stakeholders, including international organizations, regional organizations, oversight bodies, academic institutions and civil society representatives.

- Represent EDLA and NDTF in consultations, technical exchanges and institutional discussions related to Notices and Diffusions.

DUTY 4 : Meetings, Working Groups and Representation

- Lead and coordinate preparations for internal and external meetings, working groups, expert forums and governance-related discussions.

- Prepare briefing notes, discussion papers, legal analyses, presentations and recommendations for senior management.

- Represent the Organization at international conferences, meetings and expert groups as delegated.

- Support the development of strategic positions and institutional contributions on matters within the mandate of the Task Force.

DUTY 5 : Leadership and Coordination

- May coordinate and support one or more teams through effective leadership, work planning, performance follow-up, coaching and professional development.

- Provide technical guidance and mentoring to Legal Officers and newly recruited personnel.

- Facilitate knowledge sharing and promote consistent legal practices within the team.

- Contribute to workforce planning, prioritization of activities and allocation of resources within the Task Force.

DUTY 6 : Legal Policy Development and Institutional Guidance

- Lead the development and revision of legal guidelines, standard operating procedures, repositories of practice, policy papers and legal frameworks relating to Notices and Diffusions.

- Identify emerging legal risks, operational challenges and trends affecting the processing of INTERPOL data.

- Propose policy, procedural and governance improvements to strengthen compliance, transparency, efficiency and legal certainty.

- Contribute to organizational initiatives concerning data processing, international police cooperation and oversight mechanisms.

DUTY 7 : Capacity Building, Training and Knowledge Development

- Design, coordinate and deliver legal training programmes relating to Notices, Diffusions, INTERPOL's legal framework and international police cooperation.

- Develop training materials, e-learning resources and guidance documents for INTERPOL officials, National Central Bureaus and law-enforcement authorities.

- Promote awareness and understanding of legal developments affecting INTERPOL's information systems and data-processing activities.

Perform any other duties as required by the hierarchy.
