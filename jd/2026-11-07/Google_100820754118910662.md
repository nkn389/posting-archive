About the job
At Google, we put our users first. The world is always changing, so we need Product Managers who are continuously adapting and excited to work on products that affect millions of people every day.

In this role, you will work cross-functionally to guide products from conception to launch by connecting the technical and business worlds. You can break down complex problems into steps that drive product development.

One of the many reasons Google consistently brings innovative, world-changing products to market is because of the collaborative work we do in Product Management. Our team works closely with creative engineers, designers, marketers, etc. to help design and develop technologies that improve access to the world's information. We're responsible for guiding products throughout the execution cycle, focusing specifically on analyzing, positioning, packaging, promoting, and tailoring our solutions to our users.

Secure a future for Search by ensuring Google complies with the global mesh of content-focused rules and regulations and articulating a forward-looking strategy that preserves Google’s independence and optionality across the evolving regulatory landscape.
As a Senior Product Manager for Search Content Regulations, you'll play a critical role in shaping the future of Google Search in an increasingly complex global regulatory landscape. You'll sit at the intersection of product, policy, and Trust and Safety, ensuring our systems comply with global content rules while preserving Google's independence. This is a high-visibility role where you'll build the strategies, scalable tools, and cross-functional processes needed to navigate emerging regulations. If you thrive in ambiguous environments and are passionate about protecting user access to information on the open web, this is the perfect opportunity for you to drive company-level impact.
In Google Search, we're reimagining what it means to search for information – any way and anywhere. To do that, we need to solve complex engineering challenges and expand our infrastructure, while maintaining a universally accessible and useful experience that people around the world rely on. In joining the Search team, you'll have an opportunity to make an impact on billions of people globally.

Responsibilities

- Understand the breadth of applicable global content regulations and their requirements for how the product behaves, the processes it must support (e.g., removals), reporting requirements, and how these requirements are implemented.

- Develop and promulgate a consistent and durable strategy for the best outcomes in this area, anticipating emerging trends and identifying opportunities to better-position Google in an ever-shifting regulatory landscape.

- Work with multiple product, Trust & Safety, and compliance teams to ensure full compliance with these regulations and identify opportunities to improve these systems and better align them with product needs and ways of working.

- Represent the product perspective, emerge regulations and collaborate with policy, legal, and other product teams to develop strategies to ensure regulations are sustainable and well-aligned.
