About UN University (UNU)
The United Nations University (UNU) is a global think tank and postgraduate teaching organization within the United Nations System. UNU engages in policy-relevant research, capacity development, and knowledge dissemination in furtherance of the purposes and principles of the United Nations. The work of UNU contributes to solving pressing global problems that are the concern of the United Nations and its Member States.

For the past five decades, UNU has been a source of impartial research on sustainability, human security, and economic development. With hundreds of researchers working at institutes in 12 countries, UNU's expertise spans the full breadth of the 17 Sustainable Development Goals, generating policy-relevant knowledge to effect positive global change. UNU collaborates closely with other UN entities and leading research institutions across the globe. For more information, please visit http://unu.edu

United Nations University Centre for Policy Research (UNU-CPR)
United Nations University Centre for Policy Research (UNU-CPR) is one of 13 research and training Institutes that comprise the think tanks in the UNU system. UNU-CPR headquarters are in New York with an office in Geneva, Switzerland.

UNU-CPR’s core mission is to produce policy-focused research and thought leadership on issues of strategic importance to the United Nations and its Member States. The Centre prioritizes urgent policy needs requiring innovative, practical solutions for immediate implementation. The Centre’s work also contributes to the long-term resilience of the multilateral system through dialogue, research, strategic convenings, and policy coordination.

More information about its work is available here: https://unu.edu/cpr
Position Overview
UNU-CPR is seeking a part-time (60%) Communications Associate to strengthen the visibility, reach and impact of the Centre’s work in Geneva. Working closely with colleagues in Geneva and the UNU-CPR Communications team in New York, the Consultant will support publications, digital communications, media engagement and multimedia production, while helping position UNU-CPR and its research within the Geneva policy ecosystem.

The successful candidate will demonstrate strong editorial judgement, attention to detail and the ability to manage multiple communications activities simultaneously. They should be comfortable working independently while collaborating closely with researchers and communications colleagues throughout the UNU and UN system.

The role requires an ability to understand complex policy research, identify its relevance to different audiences and translate it into compelling communications products and opportunities. Strong interpersonal skills, sound judgement and an ability to build relationships across the Geneva international community, including with senior UN and diplomatic communities, will be particularly important. They should be excited about building up and maintaining a strong public presence for a relatively young think tank.
Responsibilities
Under the overall guidance and supervision of the Head of the UNU-CPR Geneva Office, the Communications Associate will have the following responsibilities:
Publications and editorial support

- Edit and oversee the layout and design of UNU-CPR Geneva reports, policy briefs, research papers and other publications, ensuring that outputs are clear, accessible and consistent with UN/UNU editorial standards.

- Support the Geneva research team in translating research findings into accessible communications products tailored to policy and practitioner audiences.

- Provide editorial and publication support to the UNU-CPR New York office as required, particularly during periods of high publication activity.

Media engagement and positioning

- Develop and maintain relationships with Geneva-based journalists and relevant international media covering the United Nations, multilateral affairs and UNU-CPR's areas of research.

- Identify opportunities to position UNU-CPR researchers and research in relevant media outlets, including through interviews, commentary, briefings and other forms of engagement.

- Identify opportunities for UNU-CPR to engage with events, multilateral processes, and relevant Geneva-based institutions as part of UNU-CPR’s overall outreach strategy.

- Manage and oversee UNU-CPR Geneva’s public-facing online presence, ensuring that information about the office, its research and activities remain current and engaging.

- Support the development of audio-visual content showcasing UNU-CPR Geneva research and activities, including short videos, interviews, podcasts and event-related content.

Perform other duties as assigned by supervisor.
