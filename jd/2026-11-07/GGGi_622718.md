PROJECT BACKGROUND
GGGI works in partnership with countries around the world to strengthen their capacity and advance green growth policies and investments that can positively impact the lives of millions of people. The organization collaborates with national governments, multilateral institutions, public agencies, and the private sector to support the development of economies that are robust, resource-efficient, low-carbon, and resilient to climate change.

GGGI implements a diverse portfolio of programs across developing countries. Through these in-country initiatives, complemented by global products and services, the organization delivers results through an integrated approach that combines evidence-based policy planning, technical assistance, and investment facilitation, aligned with national development priorities. GGGI also promotes knowledge generation and management activities that strengthen the theoretical and empirical foundations of green growth, provide practical guidance for policymakers, and help create enabling conditions for both public and private green investments.

In Colombia, GGGI has been working closely with the national government since 2014 to advance green growth policies and investments. The organization has established strategic partnerships with institutions such as the Ministry of Environment and Sustainable Development, the Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Development, the Ministry of Finance and Public Credit, and the National Planning Department (DNP), among others.

Within the framework of the UK PACT (Partnering for Accelerated Climate Transitions) Bio Rural Project (2024–2027), the Global Green Growth Institute (GGGI), the Government of Antioquia, and the Caja de Compensación Familiar de Antioquia – COMFAMA signed a Special Cooperation Agreement to implement the initiative "Antioquia BioCompetitiva." The project seeks to accelerate the scaling of innovative bioeconomy solutions in the Department of Antioquia through two strategic components: (I) promoting knowledge transfer and capacity-building processes, with a focus on circular bioeconomy and the internationalization of bioproducts; and (II) strengthening productive, technological, and commercial capacities within the department through an integrated value-chain development approach.

Under the second component, three calls for proposals will be implemented to accelerate the development of the bioeconomy ecosystem while strengthening strategic value chains in the department. These calls include:

-
Bioeconomy acceleration support for microenterprises affiliated with COMFAMA.

-
Investment scaling support for four companies linked to bioeconomy value chains.

-

OBJECTIVES OF THE ASSIGNMENT
The objective of this consultancy is to assist Agroinsumos Villaclara and Tejipaz – Café de la Memoria –, in strengthening their brand identity, supporting the generation of digital content, and supporting the implementation and monitoring of approved social media activities, ensuring consistent quality across all deliverables within established procedures, as part of the operational execution of their communications strategy under the Antioquia BioCompetitiva Project.

DELIVERABLES AND PAYMENT SCHEDULE
Under the overall supervision of the GGGI Colombia team, and in direct coordination with the beneficiary companies, Agroinsumos VillaClara and Tejipaz, the Consultant will provide marketing and communications support, consolidated for monitoring and validation purposes into three (3) deliverable packages. The assignment includes, the following process execution and tracking activities:

1. Operational work plan and process tracking sheet: A detailed work plan incorporating the consultancy operational methodology, execution schedule, information requirements, and data collection support tools. This deliverable must include the compiled outputs for Brand image and Portfolio optimization, the tracking framework for Social media strategy and monitoring, performance metrics logging, and stakeholder coordination arrangements.

2. Brand identitiy, social media and comercial material support

-
Brand identity support and tracking: support the technical execution of a Basic Brand Identity Manual, including corporate colors, typography, logo usage guidelines, brand application mockups, templates for job postings and internal communications, a commercial presentation template, and letterhead for quotations and corporate documents for Agroinsumos VillaClara and review the current portfolio; assist in the visual organization and unification of the portfolio; review existing brand identity materials within the scope approved by the beneficiary company and under GGGI’s supervision; maintain and update identity elements within the scope approved by the beneficiary company under GGGI’s supervision and compile inputs to strengthen graphic consistency and communicate the product's attributes and benefits more clearly for Tejipaz.

-
Social media support and tracking: For VillaClara and Tejipaz, coordinate and facilitate the baseline definition of communication pillars identified by the beneficiary company and GGGI and a an approved social media content framework focused on Instagram and TikTok; assist in the production of 40 content pieces (stories, carousels, reels, and posts), organized into three (3) content grids and publication schedules approved by Agroinsumos Villaclara, including graphic content execution, recording, and editing support, caption and copy development, and direct scheduling and publishing approved content according to established procedures; and compile bi-weekly performance and metrics reports.

-
Commercial and POP material execution support: For VillaClara, support the technical layout and digital formatting (excluding printing or physical production) of a corporate/commercial brochure, a banner, a flyer, an ID badge, and a cover design for a corporate notebook following established brand guidelines.

-
Monitoring, metrics, and tracking: Follow-up meetings should be scheduled with each company to provide guidelines for content creation and review progress. Additionally, metrics reports should be prepared once social media content begins to be published.

3. Final compilation report: Compilation of the 40 content pieces for Villaclara and 40 for Tejipaz publication schedules, Villaclara's commercial/POP material technical layouts, Tejipaz's final brand and portfolio verified files, and the compiled metrics and results tracking report.
