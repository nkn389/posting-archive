Purpose of Job

The Associate, Corporate Sector Advisory (CSA) will contribute across all advisory activities in the Corporate Sector (CS), working on both strategic and operational topics. The role requires knowledge of the core business priorities of the CS group and strong background in the Bank’s strategic priorities, including the Strategic Capital Framework 2026-2030 (SCF), the Green Economy Transition Strategy 2026-2030 (GET), the Food and Agribusiness Strategy 2025 and others. The Associate will be based in the CSA team (Food and Agribusiness) and have matrix responsibility with the Resident Office in Kyiv, Ukraine. The Associate supports the team in managing a portfolio of donor-funded technical cooperation assignments and blended finance activities, coordinating multiple stakeholders including Banking teams, Resident Offices, consultants, donors, government counterparts and private sector clients.

To scale up sustainable growth and develop a strong private corporate sector, the EBRD and its Corporate Sector group invest in the Food and Agribusiness (F&A), Manufacturing and Services (M&S), Natural Resources (NR), Real Estate (RE) and Telecommunications, Media and Technology (TMT). In addition to loans, bonds, and equity investments, the CS group offers advisory support to companies by fostering strong links with the private sector, governments and other development partners in the EBRD countries of operation (COO).

Within this group, the Corporate Sector Advisory (CSA) team facilitates private sector investments by implementing technical cooperation (TC) projects, structuring blended finance instruments, and supporting an enabling investment climate. CSA comprises a unique combination of activities, knowledge and skills that support and complement the CS Group’s banking activities. CSA’s objective is to provide technical support to increase the sustainability of production, improve inclusion smaller companies and stakeholders into value chains, as well as to encourage competitiveness and better governance of companies, also through adoption of digital solutions and new technologies. CSA also partners with development partners, universities and knowledge centres to facilitate the exchange of know-how, spur innovation and improve business models along value chains. The group helps to boost the competitiveness of businesses and diversify exports through higher quality standards, to assist companies to transition to high-end markets. These activities aim to improve overall sector growth and support transition in the EBRD COOs.

EBRD’s partnerships with donors are a crucial part of the Bank’s business model and its use of blended financial instruments and donor support has grown in scale and complexity over time. CSA, in close coordination with Donor Partnerships team, leads on mobilising and managing donor funds in support of the CS’s activities to uphold close partnerships with EBRD’s donor community. CSA organises events for Corporate Sector clients and stakeholders, information-sharing with donors, as well as relevant reporting under specific programmes that are led by CSA.

Ensure alignment with the Bank’s requirements and those of project stakeholders and donors. Coordinate with Banking teams and all relevant internal stakeholders to ensure project design reflects investment and sector needs.

- Manage day-to-day implementation of TC and blended finance projects. Monitor, track progress and ensure timely delivery of activities; track and manage budget disbursements; maintain documentation and produce updates as relevant, including donor reports; address risks.

- Work together with CSA and Banking teams in liaising with project stakeholders including private and public sector, donors, government authorities, as well as other development partners.

- Ensure that all assigned tasks are completed within the specified timeframes and to a high standard, while keeping in line with the policies, procedures and objectives of the Bank.
