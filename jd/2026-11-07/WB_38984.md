Unit Context:
The Senegal Country Management Unit (CMU) is a dynamic unit that requires professional excellence and a strong commitment to teamwork. As a central member of the Front Office team, the Operations Analyst will contribute to the CMU's core deliverables. S/he will report to the Operations Manager.
Duties and Respnsibilities:
Working closely with the Senior Operations Officer and the other Operations Analyst, s/he will be responsible for the following tasks and duties:

1. Analytical and Decision-Support Functions
•Provide analytical and research support to the Division Director (DD) and Operations Manager (OM) on strategic, operational, and emerging issues affecting the Senegal CMU.
•Analyze operational, portfolio, budget, and performance data using corporate systems to identify trends, risks, bottlenecks, and opportunities, and translate findings into actionable insights for management.
•Conduct quantitative and qualitative analysis of country programs, sectors, projects, and operational performance to inform priorities and management decisions.
•Draft concise analytical materials that synthesize complex information, highlight key implications, and support follow-up actions.
•Undertake ad-hoc analysis and research on priority issues at the request of management and/or the Senior Operations Officer.

2. Portfolio and Country Operations Analysis
•Support portfolio monitoring and country operations and provide analysis to support timely resolution, escalation, and management action.
•Conduct cross-country and cross-sector analysis to identify recurring issues, lessons, and opportunities to improve portfolio performance.
•Provide targeted analytical support to country teams on critical operational issues and priorities identified by the DD/OM.
•Track actions arising from portfolio reviews and management discussions and report on progress and outstanding issues.

3. Strategic Research and Executive Briefing
•Research and synthesize internal and external information on Senegal CMU-related strategic, economic, operational, and sectoral issues to support the Front Office.
•Develop briefing notes, speeches, talking points, Q&As, presentations, and other materials for senior management, ensuring key messages are concise and evidence based.
•Prepare analytical inputs for high-level meetings, including background, key issues, and relevant data.

4. Front Office Coordination and Management Support
•Draft minutes of meetings chaired by the DD and OM and follow up on agreed actions.
•Support the preparation and follow-up of high-level meetings, missions, and other management team activities.
•Respond to Front Office queries from internal and external clients, in coordination with the relevant teams.
