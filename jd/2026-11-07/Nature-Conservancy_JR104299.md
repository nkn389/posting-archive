&lt;h3&gt;&lt;span class="emphasis-2"&gt;&lt;b&gt;&lt;u&gt;What We Can Achieve Together:&lt;/u&gt;&lt;/b&gt;&lt;/span&gt;&lt;/h3&gt;&lt;br /&gt;&lt;p&gt;The BahamaReefs Assistant Program Manager (APM) develops, manages, and advances conservation programs, plans and methods and provides technical leadership for the Northern Caribbean Program (The Bahamas and Turks and Caicos), with a focus on the administration and implementation of the program &lt;b&gt;Impact Funding for BahamaReefs&lt;/b&gt;, a long-term blended finance initiative being led by The Nature Conservancy and the Global Fund for Coral Reefs. With guidance and support from the BahamaReefs Program Manager, the APM will support the overall administration and execution of the project, including reporting on project outcomes to the granting agency, development and management of workplans, supporting budgets, and monitoring program progress. They will support project teams, liaise with the implementing partners, and provide adaptive management of project priorities.&lt;/p&gt;&lt;br /&gt;&lt;p&gt;The APM provides technical and programmatic support to BahamaReefs conservation partners, supporting the overall management and coordination of the project. In close coordination with the program manager, staff and partners, they will play a key role in the development of work plans and funder/progress reports and oversee and track implementation of the program’s activities. They will support budgeting, develop project plans and packages, manage multiple workstreams and schedules, set realistic deadlines, coordinate project teams, prepare programmatic/technical reports with input and buy-in from key stakeholders and team members, and maintain project records. They will help to define roles and responsibilities, assist with recruiting, and leading project teams to successfully achieve the program’s goals in the most cost-effective and time-saving manner possible. They will critically assess project scope and objectives, ensuring all risks and dependencies are addressed in the project work plans to deliver successful results that support the fulfilment of the objectives and required deliverables of the project. The APM will assist with monitoring and measuring project progress, conducting corrective action as necessary, and communicating project status information to all stakeholders and the funder. They should be well versed in issue clearing/resolution, conflict management, and collaboratively solving complex problems. They will utilize project management methodologies and processes and ensure all TNC standards are met. They will liaise with TNC team members in the grants, finance, legal, and communications departments as it relates to the project and is responsible for ensuring compliance with funder and TNC procedures. The APM also serves as a key point of contact to other conservation organizations, foundations, businesses, and the academic community engaged with the project.&lt;/p&gt;&lt;br /&gt;&lt;p&gt;Work will be conducted in a hybrid environment including office, virtual and field settings (scientific surveys, scuba diving, site visits for monitoring, evaluation, and learning, etc.).&lt;/p&gt;&lt;br /&gt;&lt;p&gt;&lt;i&gt;This description is not designed to be a complete list of all duties and responsibilities required for this job.&lt;/i&gt;&lt;/p&gt;&lt;br /&gt;&lt;h3&gt;&lt;span class="emphasis-2"&gt;&lt;b&gt;&lt;u&gt;We’re Looking for You:&lt;/u&gt;&lt;/b&gt;&lt;/span&gt;&lt;/h3&gt;&lt;br /&gt;&lt;p&gt;If you’re looking for a career where you can find meaning and purpose, come join our Program as a Conservation Practitioner! Not only will you support critical conservation objectives in the Caribbean Division, but you’ll contribute to conservation goals through many networks!&lt;/p&gt;&lt;br /&gt;&lt;p&gt;The ideal candidate should have experience in project management and coordination, interacting with a variety of stakeholders, and on-the-ground conservation experience in The Bahamas or the Caribbean.
