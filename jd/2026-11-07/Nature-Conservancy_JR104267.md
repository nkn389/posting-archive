This position requires experience working collaboratively with farmers and ranchers, irrigation districts, tribes, and agency partners to identify and implement voluntary water management strategies that enhance instream flows while supporting the long-term viability of agricultural operations. The Project Manager will apply a full range of conservation tools, including scientific and engineering approaches, water transaction and acquisition strategies, irrigation and land management improvements, project management, negotiation, contract administration, and budget management to advance the objectives of the Klamath Acquisition Program.&lt;/p&gt;&lt;br /&gt;&lt;p&gt;The Klamath Project Manager provides technical leadership and support to the Klamath Acquisition Project within the California chapter’s Healthy Rivers strategy by planning, directing and implementing projects including planning, project scope, grant proposals and reporting, contracting, design, implementation, monitoring, and maintenance. The position plays a key role in identifying and advancing opportunities on working lands that change water management, increase streamflow, and enhance aquatic habitat while maintaining productive agricultural operations. They develop and implement conservation strategies, employ a full range of protection tools to acquire varying degrees of legal interest in land and implements a variety of strategies to secure public and private support for TNC conservation priorities. They coordinate community support, facilitate advisory committees, and build relationships with public and private organizations to identify and resolve restoration issues, widely communicate solutions, and gain support for acquisition and restoration strategies in the Klamath.&lt;br /&gt;&lt;/p&gt;&lt;p&gt;&lt;b&gt;RESPONSIBILITIES &amp; SCOPE&lt;/b&gt;&lt;/p&gt;&lt;ul&gt;&lt;li&gt;Geographic scope is the Klamath River Watershed with a focus on the Scott and Shasta Rivers in Siskiyou County, California. &lt;/li&gt;&lt;li&gt;Leads and manages conservation projects.&lt;/li&gt;&lt;li&gt;Establish and maintain optimal standards of performance for the program while controlling costs and administering budgets. &lt;/li&gt;&lt;li&gt;Assist with ensuring that public and private funds are raised to meet project and program needs. &lt;/li&gt;&lt;li&gt;Financial responsibility may include working within/managing a budget to complete projects, negotiating and contracting with vendors, and assisting with budget development. &lt;/li&gt;&lt;li&gt;Be able to communicate effectively to a broad range of audiences.&lt;/li&gt;&lt;li&gt;Ability to work independently and effectively while meeting rigorous deadlines.&lt;/li&gt;&lt;li&gt;Builds cooperation from external parties to accomplish program goals. &lt;/li&gt;&lt;li&gt;Ensures program compliance with internal policies and external requirements. &lt;/li&gt;&lt;li&gt;Under minimal supervision, makes independent decisions based on analysis, experience and context.&lt;/li&gt;&lt;li&gt;May work in variable weather conditions, at remote locations, on difficult and hazardous terrain, and under physically demanding circumstances. &lt;/li&gt;&lt;/ul&gt;&lt;p&gt; &lt;/p&gt;&lt;p&gt;The Klamath Project Manager will work closely and coordinate with a wide variety of Conservancy staff, private individuals, agency staff and contractors in support of strategy goals.
