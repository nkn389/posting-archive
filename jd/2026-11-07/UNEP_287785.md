Duties and Responsibilities
The United Nations Environment Programme (UNEP) is the leading global environmental authority that sets the global environmental agenda, promotes the coherent implementation of the environmental dimension of sustainable development within the United Nations system, and serves as an authoritative advocate for the global environment. Its Industry and Economy Division provides solutions to decision-makers and helps change the business environment by offering platforms for multi- stakeholder dialogue and cooperation, innovative policy options, pilot projects and creative market mechanisms to improve the quality of the environment and the well-being of citizens. Hosted by the United Nations Environment Programme (UNEP), the Life Cycle Initiative is a public-private, multi-stakeholder partnership that enables public and private decision makers worldwide to use credible life-cycle knowledge. Positioned at the interface between users and experts of life-cycle approaches, the Initiative supports the application of life-cycle knowledge to accelerate progress on the global sustainable development agenda, including by building consensus on, and facilitating access to, life cycle assessment (LCA) data, methods and indicators. Through the Life Cycle Initiative, UNEP is convening the Global LCA Atlas (Atlas): a neutral, public-interest infrastructure to improve access to credible, interoperable and policy-grade life cycle data for product-level decision-making. The Atlas responds to a growing policy and market need for reliable product-level environmental evidence. It is being developed as a UNEP-convened operating layer that can connect and steward life cycle datasets, data-generation processes, quality assurance, access rules, interoperability and the institutional arrangements needed to sustain them. A 12-month initiation phase, starting in 2027, will establish and validate four foundational assets: (1) Governance and Institution; (2) Database and Access; (3) Data Alignment and Quality; and (4) Financial Model and Transition Funding. During the initiation phase, UNEP will remain the accountable host, working with implementation partners, contributors, reviewers, users and funders to prepare the Atlas for a later transition to long-term operation. Further information is available on the Life Cycle Initiative website. The purpose of this consultancy is to support UNEP by leading input and oversight for the asset delivery plans of the two more technical assets: (2) Database and Access and (3) Data Alignment and Quality, as well as supporting UNEP in oversight of the remaining Asset delivery plan projects. Working with UNEP and the leads of the other Asset preparation projects, the individual contractor will structure, coordinate and consolidate expert input so that the current working concept becomes an implementation-ready delivery plan as a basis for the 2027 initiation phase, and into the first months of this implementation. This includes: • Supporting the implementation of Assets 2 and 3 by the implementation partners with expert advice and guidance. • Supporting key Atlas workshops and partner consultations. • Preparing the assets for handover. • Providing input and guidance for the work on Assets 1 and 4. • Performing other related duties as required.
