OBJECTIVES OF THE PROGRAMME
The vision of the Division of Health Promotion, Disease Prevention and Care (PPC) is to advance health for all by anticipating change and integrating promotion, prevention and care across all health and disease areas to address today's greatest health burdens and tomorrow's challenges. Within PPC Division, the Department of Sexual, Reproductive, Maternal, Child, Adolescent and Ageing Health (LHR) aims to advance a more just and equitable world where human rights are upheld and every person can achieve health, realize their rights, and live with dignity across the life course by harnessing science, generating and interpreting evidence, monitoring global trends, establishing international norms, and shaping integrated systems that enable individuals to thrive from birth through older age. LHR hosts the UNDP/UNFPA/UNICEF/WHO/World Bank Special Programme of Research, Development and Research Training in Human Reproduction (HRP) which is the main instrument within the United Nations system for identifying research priorities on sexual and reproductive health and rights; for promoting, conducting, evaluating and coordinating related interdisciplinary research; for collaborating with countries to build national capacity to conduct research; and for promoting the use of research results in policy making and sexual and reproductive health programmes. HRP is operated from the World Health Organization's Department of Sexual, Reproductive, Maternal, Child, Adolescent Health and Ageing Health (LHR). The Department draws on HRP research and global expertise to generate evidence, strengthen health systems, and impact lives through work to set norms and standards and develop global guidelines, and to support programme development in sexual and reproductive health and rights. More information about LHR and HRP can be found at https://www.who.int/hrp.
DESCRIPTION OF DUTIES

- Provide scientific and technical leadership and expertise in contraception and fertility care, serving as a technical reference for WHO, HRP cosponsors, Member States, other UN agencies, the scientific community and other relevant stakeholders.

- Contribute to setting research priorities and strategic directions in contraception and fertility care, including men's sexual health drawing on emerging evidence, identified knowledge gaps, recommendations of HRP advisory bodies, and priorities identified by Member States, HRP cosponsors and other stakeholders; and translate these priorities into operational research plans and programmes of work.

- Design, lead and oversee research activities in contraception and fertility care, including protocol development; selection and engagement of research institutions; preparation and management of research agreements; coordination of multicountry and multi-institutional research networks; scientific meetings and research training; site monitoring; data management and analysis; interpretation, publication and dissemination of findings; and evaluation of research processes and outcomes.

- Strengthen research capacity of collaborating institutions and research networks to conduct high-quality research in accordance with internationally accepted scientific, ethical and research governance standards.

- Synthesize and assess evidence relevant to contraception and fertility care, including through systematic and other evidence reviews, and contribute scientific evidence to the development and updating of WHO norms, standards, clinical recommendations and implementation guidance.

- Provide scientific and technical guidance to Member States and partners on contraception and fertility care, working in close collaboration with WHO Regional and Country Offices and relevant technical programmes to support the interpretation, adaptation and application of WHO and HRP evidence and guidance.

- Build and maintain strategic scientific and technical collaborations with governments, research institutions, WHO Collaborating Centres, UN agencies, civil society, professional organizations, the private sector and other relevant stakeholders to generate evidence and support its responsible use to improve equitable access to contraception and fertility care within the broader context of sexual and reproductive health and rights.

- Support the dissemination and uptake of WHO and HRP research and normative products relating to contraception and fertility care, working with technical teams, the HRP communications team and the WHO Department of Communications to ensure accurate and effective communication of scientific findings and recommendations.

- Prepare high-quality scientific, technical and programme reports and inputs for WHO and HRP governing and advisory bodies, donors and other stakeholders, in accordance with WHO and HRP requirements.

- Contribute technical inputs to resource mobilization and partnership development, including the preparation of scientific and programmatic components of funding proposals and engagement with existing and prospective funding partners.

- Perform other related duties as required by the Unit Head and Director.
