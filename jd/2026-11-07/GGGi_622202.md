INTRODUCTION TO GGGI

The Global Green Growth Institute (GGGI) is a treaty-based international, inter-governmental organization dedicated to supporting and promoting strong, inclusive and sustainable economic growth in developing countries and emerging economies. To learn more please visit about GGGI web page.

In India, the Global Green Growth Institute (GGGI) is implementing the Sustainable, Inclusive, and Thermally comfortable Housing for Affordability & Resilience in Andhra (SITHARA) project in Andhra Pradesh, India, with GGGI as the consortium lead and the Administrative Staff College of India (ASCI) as a consortium partner.

SITHARA aims to support the transition towards better-performing, energy-efficient, thermally comfortable and climate-resilient affordable housing by integrating renewable energy, energy efficiency, passive thermal comfort and clean cooking solutions within the existing Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY) 2.0 housing delivery ecosystem. The project will demonstrate and validate integrated solutions while strengthening the market, financing, institutional and implementation mechanisms required for their wider adoption.

The project will work in Visakhapatnam and Kadapa districts of Andhra Pradesh, engaging state housing agencies, energy institutions, electricity utilities, Urban Local Bodies (ULBs), financial institutions, Energy Service Companies (ESCOs), technology providers, industry associations and other relevant stakeholders. In parallel, SITHARA will strengthen implementation and compliance mechanisms for the Energy Conservation and Sustainable Building Code (ECSBC) and Eco-Niwas Samhita (ENS) and develop knowledge and institutional pathways for replication across Andhra Pradesh and other states in India.

POSITION SUMMARY
The Project Officer (Energy) will function as Project Manager (PM) leading the planning, coordination and implementation of the SITHARA Project in Andhra Pradesh, working across its market-enabling, demonstration, financing, capacity-building and replication activities.

The position will coordinate closely with GGGI project team members, ASCI, government agencies and other stakeholders to translate the approved project workplan into effective implementation. The Project Officer will lead technical and market assessments, development of implementation and financing models, demonstration activities, ECSBC/ENS implementation support, capacity development, investment facilitation, monitoring and reporting, and knowledge generation with due guidance of GGGI’s Country Representative (CR) in India. This position is based at New Delhi / Vijayawada (Andhra Pradesh)/ Hyderabad and will report to CR-India.

The position will also oversee coordination of consultants, service providers and other delivery partners; monitor project activities, outputs, milestones and risks; and contribute to timely and high-quality delivery in accordance with GGGI policies, approved budgets and project reporting requirements.

PURPOSE

- Support and oversee demonstration activities and associated baseline, monitoring, measurement and verification, impact assessment and documentation of project results.

- Monitor progress against project outputs, indicators, milestones and budgets and support identification and resolution of implementation risks and delivery constraints.

- Contribute to institutionalization, knowledge sharing and replication of SITHARA approaches within Andhra Pradesh and other relevant programmes and geographies in India.
