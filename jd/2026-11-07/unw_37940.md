Background:

UN Women, grounded in the vision of equality enshrined in the Charter of the United Nations, works for the elimination of discrimination against women and girls; the empowerment of women; and the achievement of equality between women and men as partners and beneficiaries of development, human rights, humanitarian action and peace and security.

The Division of Communications and Advocacy (DCA) leads UN Women’s global communications and advocacy efforts to advance gender equality and women’s empowerment, strengthen the organization’s visibility and positioning, and engage audiences across digital, media and public-facing platforms. Within DCA, the social media team develops and implements content and campaigns for UN Women’s global social media channels and provides guidance to colleagues across the organization on social media account management and engagement.

Reporting to the Social Media Specialist, the Communications Associate manages an assigned portfolio of social media content, campaigns and account-related activities, taking responsibility for delivery from planning and production through publication, engagement and evaluation. The Associate exercises editorial, analytical and operational judgment within agreed strategies and clearance protocols, coordinates with headquarters, regional and country office colleagues, and provides technical guidance to support consistent messaging and operational continuity.

Scope of Work

1. Coordinate social media content and campaign delivery

- Manage the planning and delivery of the assigned social media portfolio, translating agreed strategies into content plans and publishing schedules, and coordinating scheduling adjustments with the Social Media Specialist.

- Develop concepts and guide production of content packages across languages, formats and platforms, including event coverage, video and graphics; create and edit Canva assets as needed.

- Draft, edit, adapt and quality-assure content for global channels, exercising editorial judgment, identifying sensitive content and ensuring the appropriate editorial review and clearance.

- Coordinate inputs, multilingual adaptations, production and clearances with colleagues across headquarters, regional and country offices, resolving delivery issues and ensuring timely publication.

2. Manage audience engagement and account operations

- Manage day-to-day publishing and audience engagement within the assigned portfolio across global accounts serving large and diverse audiences, ensuring consistency and continuity of delivery.

- Assess and respond to comments, queries and sensitive or critical engagement within the assigned portfolio, in line with the agreed escalation protocol; resolve engagement issues within delegated authority and escalate high-risk or reputational matters to the Social Media Specialist.

- Maintain publishing workflows and coordinate technical follow-up on platform changes, maintaining account records and practical guidance to support operational continuity.

3. Coordinate performance analysis, reporting and knowledge sharing

- Analyze quantitative and qualitative social media results using publishing, monitoring and analytics tools, including social listening and sentiment analysis as relevant to the assigned portfolio.

- Evaluate content and campaign performance, prepare analytical reports and use findings to improve content, formats and publishing schedules within the assigned portfolio and agreed social media strategy.

- Assess platform developments and audience trends and advise the Social Media Specialist on adaptations to strengthen audience growth, engagement and effective platform use.

- Share findings, technical guidance and good practices across teams and offices to improve content quality, coordination and social media delivery.

The incumbent performs other duties within their functional profile as deemed necessary for the efficient functioning of the Office and the Organization.
