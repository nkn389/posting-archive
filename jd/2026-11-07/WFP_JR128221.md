JOB TITLE : Logistics Officer (Workshop Manager)
TYPE OF CONTRACT : Regular International Consultant Level 2 (CST-II)
UNIT/DIVISION : Logistics/ Fleet
DUTY STATION (City, Country) : Juba, South Sudan
DURATION:
11 months (renewable subject to performance and budget availability)

BACKGROUND AND PURPOSE OF THE ASSIGNMENT:
The World Food Programme (WFP) South Sudan operates a large fleet of light vehicles, trucks, generators, and other mechanical assets to support the delivery of humanitarian assistance throughout South Sudan, including in Juba and field offices. Effective fleet maintenance and workshop management are critical to ensuring operational readiness, safety, compliance, and cost effectiveness.
The Workshop Manager, based in the WFP Country Office (CO) in Juba, will oversee workshop operations, preventive maintenance programmes, technical inspections, repair activities, spare parts management, and fleet performance monitoring, all in accordance with Global Fleet guidance, to ensure the availability and reliability of WFP fleet assets.

ACCOUNTABILITIES/RESPONSIBILITIES:
Under the direct supervision of the Fleet Manager and in coordination with relevant Fleet staff, the Country office (CO) Logistics Head of Operations and the Field Office (FO) Head of Logistics, the incumbent shall perform the following duties:
Workshop Maintenance and Technical Operations

- Ensure that scheduled maintenance, repairs, overhauls, reconditioning and modifications are carried out efficiently across all WFP workshops to keep vehicles maintained to a high standard and in a roadworthy condition.
- Conduct technical diagnostics and root-cause analysis of recurring mechanical failures, using VMRS (Vehicle Maintenance & Reporting Standards), Reason-For-Repair, Work-Done-Codes and Component Codes to support the analysis. Use the findings to implement corrective actions that improve fleet reliability and reduce vehicle downtime.
- Oversee materials management for workshop operations, particularly the control and issuance of spare parts, tyres, lubricants and consumable materials, ensuring that their issuance is justified.
- Forecast the spare parts, tyres and materials required for workshop operations and submit these requirements to the Transport Manager for approval.
- Manage tyres, including maintenance practices, control mechanisms and performance monitoring.
- Carefully record and maintain an inventory list of all tools and workshop equipment.
- undefined
- Manage oil and lubricants, ensuring that the quality and quantities supplied comply with established standards.
- Manage external maintenance service providers and repair contractors, including technical evaluation of services, monitoring service quality, and validation of completed work and related invoices.
Fleet Cost Efficiency, Performance and Planning

- Contribute to vehicle control and cost effectiveness and fleet asset lifecycle management. Assess asset conditions, support rehabilitation and disposal planning, and technically evaluate assets approaching the end of their useful life to inform recommendations for replacement or write-off, as necessary.
- Investigate ways to continually reduce fleet operating costs related to maintenance activities. Support maintenance budget planning and expenditure monitoring through cost forecasting and analysis of maintenance trends to identify operational efficiencies and cost savings.
- Implement established best practices and monitor fleet and workshop KPIs (Key Performance Indicators) against established parameters. These indicators include vehicle availability, maintenance turnaround time, downtime, maintenance costs, workshop productivity and preventive versus corrective maintenance ratios. Include the results in the reports required under Fleet Administration, Data Management and Reporting.
- Work in accordance with the Vehicle Fleet Management Project Activity Work-Plan established by the Head, WFP Fleet.
Fleet Administration, Data Management and Reporting

- Ensure that workshop administration, record-keeping and vehicle history records are properly maintained through the Fleet Management System.
- Ensure that accurate data is entered into the FMS Repairs, Tyres and Stock Control Modules in a timely manner.
- Manage and ensure the timely production of all reports and information required by the Organisation through WFP Fleet Management System (FMS). Additionally, analyse the information and assess the quality of reports prior to distribution.
Staff Supervision, Teamwork and Knowledge Sharing

- Supervise staff and personnel reporting to the Workshop Manager, including monitoring timekeeping and ensuring that their work meets organisational standards and is carried out in accordance with their established job descriptions.
- Develop the technical capacity of mechanics and workshop personnel through coaching, mentoring, on-the-job training and identification of skills development needs. Share experiences gained with team and strive to promote teamwork and a harmonious working atmosphere.
- Continuously collect and collate valuable data and information to identify improvements needed for fleet management standardisation and development . Communicate the findings to other staff in the Fleet Section.
Health, Safety and Environmental Requirements

- Manage workshop health, safety and environmental compliance, ensuring that minimum health and safety requirements are met in full, with due consideration for environmental needs. Conduct safety inspections and risk assessments, ensure proper use of personal protective equipment (PPE) and proper management of hazardous waste, and implement corrective actions for identified risks.
Travel and Other Duties

- Travel occasionally to Field Offices, as required by the Organisation.
- Maintain emergency operational readiness and provide surge technical support to field locations to keep critical fleet assets operational during emergency and large-scale response operations.
- Perform any other duties as requested by the immediate supervisor.

Working with: The incumbent will liaise and cooperate with all other employees and personnel within the Fleet Unit and other units, with particular emphasis on the Logistics Section and Field Office Heads of Logistics. The incumbent will work closely with other workshops and Field Office (FO) Fleet Managers in South Sudan.

EXPECTED DELIVERABLES & RESULTS AT THE END OF THE CONTRACT:

· Ensure that the Fleet is managed in the most cost-efficient manner to achieve maximum vehicle availability and minimum downtime.

- Ensure that all reporting, administrative and operational functions within the Fleet Section are organised efficiently to maintain the high standards expected by the Organisation.
- Maintain the ratio of scheduled to unscheduled repairs within the established parameters.
- Identify the resources required to meet overall operational requirements. Monitor upward or downward trends in operational demand and adjust resources accordingly.
- Match demand for services to the available resources.

Experience managing mixed fleets consisting of light vehicles, trucks, generators, and heavy equipment, preferably in emergency, hardship, or complex operating environments
Demonstrated hands-on experience managing preventive and corrective maintenance, vehicle diagnostics, repairs, overhauls, and workshop operations.

Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
