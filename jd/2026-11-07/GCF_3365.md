Introduction
The Green Climate Fund is the world’s largest dedicated multilateral climate fund and invests in impactful, innovative, catalytic programs and projects in developing countries across the globe. The GCF has a portfolio of 379 projects across 137 countries, with a total commitment of USD 22.3 billion to date.
In 2023, an additional USD 12.8 billion was pledged, to support our new strategic and programming cycle from 2024-2027; our ambition is to grow to become at least a USD 50 billion fund by 2030. Rooted in the core objectives of impact and efficiency, access and a focus on the most vulnerable, and mobilizing a much higher scale of public and private sector financial flows for climate action we have an unparalleled scale of resources, which, together with our risk appetite and flexibility allows us to bring together different partners and stakeholders in innovative partnerships for profound impact.

About the Green Climate Fund (GCF)
Set up in 2010 by the 194 countries who are Parties to the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change (UNFCCC), GCF takes its responsibility as the world’s largest dedicated multilateral climate fund to heart. GCF funds transformative climate projects across the developing world, supporting countries in their responses and actions to tackle climate change while fostering sustainable development within their communities.
Our country-driven approach ensures that our actions are tailored to the unique circumstances and national aspirations of each nation we support. Besides channeling public and private sector investment through a broad range of financing instruments, GCF builds the capacity of developing countries to take climate action through an extensive Readiness Programme ensuring countries are increasingly well-equipped and financed to manage their climate futures.
Headquartered in the Republic of Korea, GCF operates with a diverse and talented workforce of over 300 people.
Department Introduction
The Department of Information Technology (DIT) enables the Green Climate Fund's mission through secure, reliable, and innovative technology services. DIT oversees enterprise architecture, cybersecurity, digital workplace services, business applications, data and analytics platforms, and artificial intelligence adoption across the Secretariat. Working in partnership with business units, DIT drives digital transformation, strengthens data governance, modernizes business processes, and delivers technology solutions that improve operational efficiency, decision-making, and organizational resilience. As a strategic enabler, DIT ensures that technology, data, and AI are governed, sustainable, and aligned with GCF's institutional priorities and future growth.
Role Summary
The Digital Products Specialist is a senior specialist within the Digital Solutions Team, responsible for the strategic oversight and continuous improvement of critical in-house developed applications, including AI-powered products and agentic AI solutions. This role acts as the primary business owner and subject matter expert for these applications, ensuring they meet evolving user needs and align with GCF's IT and business strategies. The position requires a strong understanding of business processes, stakeholder engagement, and the ability to translate organizational requirements into actionable product decisions, including the responsible adoption of AI agents and intelligent automation. The Digital Products Specialist collaborates closely with business teams, developers, and IT leadership to drive the success and adoption of these applications.
Duties and Responsibilities

- Build strong relationships with business teams, end users, and IT stakeholders to gather requirements and feedback.
- Facilitate workshops, interviews, and user testing sessions to ensure solutions are user-centric and effective.
- Act as a liaison between business users and the development team, translating business needs into clear technical requirements for both traditional and AI-driven solutions.
- Advocate for user experience, accessibility, responsible AI use, and process efficiency in all product decisions.
- Communicate product vision, priorities, and progress to stakeholders at all levels.
- Foster a culture of continuous improvement and innovation within the product team.
- Oversee the end-to-end product lifecycle for homegrown applications, from requirements gathering to deployment and support.
- Coordinate with developers, QA, and IT operations to ensure timely and high-quality delivery of new features and fixes, including the evaluation and integration of AI capabilities.
- Manage product backlogs, release plans, and documentation in accordance with best practices.
- Ensure compliance with GCF's security, data governance, responsible AI, and quality standards.
- Track and report on key performance indicators (KPIs), user adoption metrics.
- Support change management, training, and knowledge transfer activities for new releases, with particular attention to building organizational readiness for AI-driven features and agentic AI solutions.
Requirements

- Master’s degree in Business Administration, Information Systems, Public Administration, or a related field; or a Bachelor’s degree with an additional two (2) years of relevant professional experience may be considered.
- Minimum eight (8) years of experience in digital product management, business analysis, or application ownership roles.
- Demonstrated experience managing or owning business-critical applications in a complex organizational environment, including AI-powered or agentic AI products.
- Strong understanding of business process analysis, requirements gathering, and stakeholder engagement.
- Experience working with cross-functional teams, including business, IT, and development.
- Familiarity with software development lifecycles, agile methodologies, and product management tools; exposure to AI/ML development practices and agentic AI frameworks is an advantage.
- Knowledge of user experience (UX) principles, user-centered design, and responsible AI practices.
- Understanding of AI governance, ethical AI principles, and the considerations specific to deploying AI agents in organizational contexts.
- Experience in the public sector, international organizations, or climate finance is desirable.
- Excellent communication, facilitation, and presentation skills with the ability to work collaboratively in a multicultural environment.
- Fluent written and oral communication skills in English are essential for this position; knowledge of another UN language is an advantage.
