The Team
The mission of the Global Policy & Advocacy (GPA) division is to understand and shape the public policy debates affecting the foundation's work, build support for its major program and policy objectives, and develop partnerships and alliances that can advance the foundation's objectives nationally and globally. Because the foundation's resources alone are not enough to solve the challenges ahead, GPA also supports advocacy efforts to engage diverse stakeholders and promote innovative solutions that advance our program goals. We work in close partnership with grantees, our colleagues in the Global Health, Global Development, Global Growth & Opportunity, U.S. and Gender Equality programs and Communications to build the environment in which all people can lead healthy and productive lives.

The North America Team (NAT) is a team within GPA that develops and executes policy, advocacy, communications and government relations strategies to build political support and advance domestic and global programmatic priorities that are funded by, or executed within, the United States, Canada, and the United Nations.

Your Role
The Senior Officer, Advocacy, International Relations and Policy Planning (IRPP), North America Team, will be responsible for translating foundation strategies and priorities into policy-relevant arguments and advocacy opportunities across priority IRPP markets, including Canada and the UN. The role will also lead special advocacy initiatives to advance maternal and child survival through sport and new partnerships, and related to global AI governance.

Working closely with the Deputy Director, Canada and UN leads, AI policy lead, and partnering with program, policy, communications, and other teams across the foundation, this role will support a collaborative, One Foundation approach to market engagement. The successful candidate will bring strong political and institutional judgment, the ability to synthesize complex global health and development strategies, and experience connecting programmatic priorities to the political economy and institutional context of government and multilateral audiences.

*This is a 16 month limited term employment role. Salary plus benefits are provided. Relocation assistance and employer Visa support are not available for this role. Role can be located in Seattle, WA or Washington, DC.

What You'll Do

- Special Projects: Lead special projects that test and strengthen new advocacy approaches to advance maternal and child survival, including:
- Sports advocacy model: Lead development of a strategic approach to better leverage sport to advance maternal and child survival. Support the operationalization of new ideas and engagement models, connecting efforts and learning across IRPP markets.
- Global political coalition: Lead an emerging initiative to build a new global political coalition in support of maternal and child survival through a new partnership. Help shape the advocacy approach, develop relationships with new champions, and connect the initiative to foundation and market priorities.
- Supporting emerging AI global governance advocacy partnerships

- Partner across the foundation: Build effective working relationships with program, policy, communications, and other relevant teams to bring technical expertise and market insight together, align priorities, and coordinate contributions to shared work.
- Understand foundation priorities: Work with program and issue advocacy teams to understand the global development challenges the foundation seeks to address, the solutions set out in its strategies, and their intended impact, drawing on technical expertise across the foundation.
