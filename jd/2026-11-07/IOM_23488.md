Organizational Context and Scope
Established in 1951, the International Organization for Migration (IOM) is the leading inter-governmental organisation in the field of migration and works closely with governmental, intergovernmental and nongovernmental partners. IOM is dedicated to promoting humane and orderly migration for the benefit of all. It does so by providing services and advice to governments and migrants.
Under the overall supervision of the Head of Unit and direct supervision of the Senior Protection Associate, the Caseworker – Protection will be responsible for supporting programmes that assist Beneficiaries of Temporary Protection (BOTP) in Ireland.
This role will focus on supporting BOTP’s in state accommodation settings nationwide facing barriers to independence. The Caseworker - Protection also requires individualised protection-focused casework, including vulnerability assessments, needs identification, and referrals to appropriate support services, as required, and other programme-related responsibilities.
