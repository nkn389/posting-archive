The Team
This role sits within the Foundation’s Africa Offices (AIO) and is based in Ethiopia as part of the Ethiopia Integrated Health Team (EIHT), working in close partnership with the Government of Ethiopia, development partners, and implementing partners to strengthen primary health care through stronger data systems, measurement, and evidence-based decision-making.

The Foundation’s health and nutrition program in Ethiopia aims to address system inefficiencies, develop data-driven insights, and pilot new delivery approaches and innovations with the potential for scale. It contributes to Ethiopia’s achievement of critical primary health care outcomes — including immunization, nutrition, and reproductive, maternal, neonatal, and child health — as set out in the Government of Ethiopia’s Health Sector Development and Investment Plan (HSDIP II), a three-year roadmap spanning 2026/27 to 2028/29. The EIHT’s support spans service delivery; data systems; supply chain; health financing; innovation, research, and scale; and nutrition.

The team views government-led data systems and data use as core foundations for improving health service delivery, increasing efficiency in resource-constrained settings, enhancing transparency and accountability, and tracking performance — grounded in country ownership, a “One Foundation” approach to internal coordination, and long-term investment in national institutions.

The Role
The Senior Program Officer (SPO) will lead the development and management of a portfolio of strategic investments (grants and contracts) to strengthen Ethiopia’s health data systems and data use, and will lead the Foundation’s monitoring, learning, and evaluation (MLE) of its integrated health and nutrition program in Ethiopia.

This role is designed for a senior data and measurement leader with deep expertise in health information systems, data analysis and use, surveys and surveillance, and program monitoring and evaluation. Experience working alongside Ministries of Health to strengthen national and subnational data systems and analytic capacity is strongly preferred.

Working closely with the Government of Ethiopia, development partners, NGOs, research institutions, and multilateral agencies, the SPO will serve as a technical advisor and honest broker to Ethiopian data partners, a strong grant maker, and an excellent connector who can crowd in internal, national, global, public, and private partners around common agendas. The SPO will identify key issues and priorities emerging from Ethiopia’s health sector, represent the Foundation in national and global technical forums, and partner closely with team leadership to shape the team’s strategic vision and goals.

The role has both an external and an internal focus. Externally, the SPO will strengthen government-led data systems and data use for decision-making, and help sequence and prioritize data, digital, and surveillance reforms. Internally, the SPO will lead MLE for the Ethiopia Integrated Health Program (EIHP) — tracking the Foundation’s contribution to the HSDIP, health system performance, and service coverage outcomes, and enabling adaptive management and learning.

This is a six-month, limited-term backfill position based in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. Candidates must have a strong understanding of Ethiopia’s health system and be fluent in Amharic
