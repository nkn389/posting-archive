ITS Finance accelerates information and technology as a force multiplier supporting WBG core finance and risk while enabling increased financial operations agility through a range of robust, flexible financing instruments and facilities for WBG private and public sector clients. We collaborate closely with our business partners to be a leading developmental financial institution that continues to deepen and sustain developmental impact.

Duties and accountabilities:

• Configure and optimize FX, Money Market (MM), Interest Rate Derivatives (IRD), Bonds, and Securities Finance modules within Murex.
• Work closely with traders and business analysts to capture requirements and translate them into technical configurations.
• Support the ongoing projects phases from reviewing requirements, testing and supporting end users.
• Ensure that production incidents are attended to and supporting end users in their daily activities
• Manage and enhance Pricing, Valuation, Market Data, Deal life cycle and Simulation Views modules to support front-office activities and decision-making.
• Ensure accurate market data setup and consistency across Murex environments.
• Perform impact assessments for new product setups and system changes.
• Collaborate with cross-functional teams (Risk, Back Office, Finance, IT) to ensure end-to-end trade lifecycle integrity.

Selection Criteria
• Master's degree with 5 years of experience or a Bachelors Degree with a
minimum of 7 years of relevant experience, or equivalent combination of
education and experience.

• A Minimum of 4 years of experience in treasury systems, with a minimum of 3 years of hands-on Murex configuration experience.
• Good understanding of project management approaches and, ideally, projects implemented under the Agile Framework.
• Strong understanding of Forex, Money Markets, Derivatives, Bonds, and Securities Finance products, including their pricing, events and valuation concepts.
• In-depth knowledge of Murex Pre-trades, e-trade pad, Market Data, and Simulation modules.
• Experience with trade capture, product configuration, and pricing setup in Murex Front Office.
• Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills with the ability to troubleshoot configuration and pricing issues.
