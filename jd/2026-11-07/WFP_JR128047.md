JOB TITLE: Resilience and food systems Consultant
TYPE OF CONTRACT: International Consultant level 1—CST 1
UNIT/ DIVISION: Programme
DUTY STATION Guatemala, Guatemala
DURATION: 6 months

BACKGROUND AND PURPOSE OF THE ASSIGNMENT:
The WFP Guatemala Country Office is implementing its Country Strategic Plan (CSP) 2021–2026, which prioritizes resilience building, food systems strengthening, and climate change adaptation. As part of these efforts, WFP is expanding its focus on market access, entrepreneurship, and climate-smart agriculture to support smallholder farmers and vulnerable communities in building sustainable livelihoods.
To support this, WFP is recruiting an international consultant to lead efforts in market development, value chain strengthening, and climate-smart agricultural practices. The consultant will also serve as the focal point for the KOICA-funded Integrated Resilience Project and contribute to resource mobilization, including proposal development and donor engagement.

Job Purpose
To provide technical leadership and coordination for WFP Guatemala’s food systems and market access initiatives, with a focus on entrepreneurship, value chain development, and integrated resilience programming. The consultant will also support fundraising efforts and serve as the focal point for KOICA-funded activities. incumbent will join the Programme unit’s team in Guatemala City under the direct supervision of the SO manager for SO3 Resilient Food System (Activities 3&4). The incumbent will be responsible for carrying out the following tasks and responsibilities:

KEY ACCOUNTABILITIES (not all-inclusive, within delegated authority):
1. Support the development of resilience projects and activities, plans and processes, ensuring alignment with the current WFP CSP 2021 – 2024, the new CSP 2026-2030 once approved (under development), WFP Global Policies and other guidance.
2. Drive the development and implementation of SAMS and resilient food system projects, plans, and processes, ensuring alignment with wider programme policies and guidance.
3. Enhance efforts to strengthen and improve market access and food systems approach throughout resilience projects.
4. Serve as the focal point for the KOICA Integrated Resilience Project, ensuring coordination across activities and alignment with donor expectations.
5. Provide technical assistance for capacity strengthening programming to the government around SAMS and resilient food systems.
6. Develop innovative tools and partnerships to support more effective and efficient approaches to resilient food system projects and programmes.
7. Strengthen value chains and market linkages to enhance the sustainability and impact of resilience projects.
8. Prepare accurate and timely reporting on programmes and activities related to resilience-building and food systems.
9. Contribute to fundraising proposals to secure financial support for resilience and food system initiatives, including support the development and follow-up of KOICA proposals and other fundraising initiatives.
10. Coordinate with RAM, Nutrition and Gender/Protection/inclusion teams to ensure integration of cross cutting topics are mainstreamed into resilience and food system workstreams, including supporting environmental assessments, gender analysis, and others as required.
11. Manage partnerships with NGOs and other civil society actors through the coordination of FLAs and MoUs within the scope of assigned resilience projects, including the KOICA-funded resilience project.
12. Facilitate relevant trainings related to food systems and project management, and present lessons learned to the CO team.
13. Analyze how current activities, political and social events of UN and other multilateral bodies/governments may impact WFP activities and programmes, providing information to WFP staff for decision-making, particularly those related to national and international policies and trends.
14. Perform other duties as required to support the resilience and food systems portfolio, as requested by supervisor.

QUALIFICATIONS & EXPERIENCE REQUIRED:
•Experience in diversity of programme approaches, in a variety of contexts, includingClimate/Resilience/VAM.
•Experience in working with international organizations (especially with WFP), development partners, high-level authorities, donors (such as KOICA), local governments and/or civil society organizations.
•Proven ability to conceptualize, develop, plan and manage programmes, leadership and teamwork abilities,good analytical, negotiation, communication and advocacy skills.

STANDARD MINIMUM ACADEMIC QUALIFICATIONS:
Education: Advanced University degree in International Affairs, Economics, Nutrition/Health, Agriculture, Environmental Science, Social Sciences or other field relevant to international development assistance, or First University Degree with additional years of related work experience and/or trainings/courses.
Language: Fluency in oral and written English. Proficiency of Spanish is desirable.
Knowledge & Skills: Experience with UN agency is an advantage, Proficiency in MS Windows and MS Office. Strong qualitative and quantitative skillsets are essential. Collaborative team player, management, innovation, partnership, and client orientation are critical characteristics for the profile.
