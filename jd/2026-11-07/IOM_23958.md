Project Context and Scope
This consultancy contributes to the implementation of two IOM projects in Tajikistan: the “Central Asia Safe Migration” project funded by the UK Integrated Security Fund (ISF), which aims to reduce migrants’ vulnerability to exploitation, recruitment fraud, and unsafe migration while supporting sustainable reintegration for returning migrants across Central Asia; and “Tajikistan: Enhancing the Detection and Prevention of Online Recruitment Fraud and Digital Risks Targeting Migrants”, funded by the IOM Development Fund (IOMDF), which seeks to strengthen the detection and prevention of online labour recruitment fraud and related digital risks affecting migrants in Tajikistan.
Working closely with the Lead National Consultant and under the guidance of IOM National Project Officers, the Secondary National Consultant will assist in ensuring that all research components are integrated into a coherent and evidence-based assessment that examines the risks of online fraud affecting prospective migrants and the effectiveness of existing prevention and response mechanisms.
The assessment will be structured around two complementary thematic pillars:
(1) Legal, Organisational, and Institutional Analysis, which will examine the legal, regulatory, and institutional environment responsible for labour migration, recruitment practices, digital platforms, online fraud, and victim protection in the Republic of Tajikistan. This component will assess the roles, responsibilities, capacities, and coordination mechanisms of relevant government institutions, law enforcement agencies, recruitment intermediaries, civil society organizations, and private-sector actors. It will also identify legislative, regulatory, and operational gaps that may enable fraudulent recruitment practices to persist.
(2) Individual Behavioural Analysis, which will explore the social, behavioural, and informational factors that influence migrants' vulnerability to fraudulent recruitment schemes and online scams. This component will examine how prospective migrants access recruitment opportunities and migration-related information, their perceptions of risk, decision-making processes, and the tactics commonly used by fraudsters to gain trust and exploit vulnerabilities.
The Secondary National Consultant will take primary responsibility for the research and analysis under the second pillar and will support the Lead National Consultant in the implementation of desk research and analysis under the first pillar. The Secondary National Consultant will contribute to the design of research tools, lead the collection of qualitative data from prospective migrants and key informants, conduct preliminary analysis of interview findings, and ensure that primary research evidence is accurately documented and integrated into the overall assessment.
Under the guidance of the Lead National Consultant, the Secondary National Consultant will contribute to the formulation of practical recommendations aimed at strengthening policy and institutional frameworks, improving prevention and enforcement measures, enhancing inter-agency coordination, and informing targeted awareness-raising interventions for populations most at risk of online fraud and fraudulent recruitment practices.
