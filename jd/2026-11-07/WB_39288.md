Unit Context

The Infrastructure Engineering team (ITSIN) designs, builds, and maintains the foundational, end-to-end IT infrastructure that supports the World Bank Group’s global operations. By championing automation, modernizing systems, and collaborating closely with other technology units, the unit delivers secure, scalable, and cost-effective digital services that empower the organization to advance its global development objectives.
Duties & Responsibilities:

• Combine Next Generation Firewall and Network Engineering skills.
• Assist with the Design, Deployment and Maintenance of Next Gen Firewall infrastructure on-prem and in the cloud.
• Maintain User Identification design for a zero trust environment.
• Manage SSL Decryption policies and troubleshoot related issues.
• Maintain hardware templates and Template Stacks.
• Manage Cloud Identity Engine.
• Manage Zone Protection and DOS protection policies design and deployment.
• Manage security and implement policy through Strata Cloud Manager.
• Assist with Palo Alto SD-WAN support
• Evaluate Palo Alto CVEs and recommendations for mitigating risk.
• Enhance the team’s proactive monitoring capability by creating automated reports/alerts (using Splunk) for Firewall events.
• As part of project requirements, use modern scripting languages and automation engines to automate firewall and network tasks.
• Use APIs to integrate the automation tasks with other enterprise systems.
• Update documentation (drawings, spreadsheets, procedures) of firewalls and related network and producing reports for peer and management review.
• Handle escalation calls from the NOC, performing triage, resolving problems, or escalating to team members.
• Travel as needed to other WBG offices to promote mobility support advocacy, knowledge sharing, and facilitate deployments and improvements.
• Flexibility to work in shifts including night shift if required.
• Provide on-call support on rotational basis based on need.
Specific Activities Include:

• Maintain the World Bank Group’s Firewall infrastructure for Headquarters, Country Offices and Cloud.
• Deploy, maintain and replace Palo Alto Firewall Appliances and Virtual Appliances in accordance with the Organizational Standard.
• Enhance the team’s proactive monitoring capability by creating automated reports/alerts (using Splunk, Ansible) for Firewall events.
• Ensure compliance with various security standards for the environment.
• Handle escalation calls from the NOC, perform triage, and resolve problems.
• Provide continuous monitoring and troubleshooting for Firewall Infrastructure including adequate follow up on Problem Resolution tracking using the ServiceNow tool.
• Monitor and review use of systems for World Bank access control policy violations in line with World Bank’s guidelines.
• Ensure that the defined decommissioning and disposal procedures are followed for all hardware systems and media.
• Report and respond to critical security events and take corrective measures per defined security incident management processes.
• Follow and comply with the World Bank Group policies, processes, and procedures.
• Participate in audits, as needed, producing necessary documentation, reports, and explanations, and implement corrective and preventive action plans approved by unit managers.
• Take personal responsibility and accountability for timely response to client queries, requests or needs, while working to remove obstacles that may impede execution or overall success.
• Able to present and explain technical information to diverse types of audience (management, users, vendor, and technical staff)

Selection Criteria
• Bachelor’s or Master’s degree with 2 years of experience or equivalent combination of education and experience (for example, in the IT field: Bachelor’s Degree with a minimum of 1 year of related work
experience).
• Palo Alto Firewall experience is required.
• Network engineering experience is required.
• SAFe Agile Certification is required.
• Hands-on experience with firewalls (e.g. Palo Alto), virtual and physical including security policy management and troubleshooting of issues.
• Hands-on Experience with Palo Alto Panorama Management tool
• Hands-on experience with Spanning Tree, OSPF and BGP routing protocols.
• Hands-on experience with TCP/IP, subnetting, VLSM, VLANs, STP, OSFP and BGP.
• Solid understanding of network engineering fundamentals/services: DHCP, OSI model, VRF, VPN, ACLs, GRE, policy-based routing.
• Ability to work independently with minimum supervision.
• Ability to quickly grasp new ideas and concepts.
• Ability to collaborate within the team and across boundaries.
• Ability to contribute to and readily apply WBG’s body of knowledge for internal and/or external client solutions.
• Ability and willingness to work, learn and contribute to an internationally diverse team environment.
• Fluency in English.
