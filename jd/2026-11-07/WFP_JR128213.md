Background and Purpose of the Assignment

Given the evolving security environment in Burkina Faso and the widespread nature of WFP operations, there is a need to strengthen security risk management, operational preparedness, and compliance across Field Offices. To support this objective, WFP seeks to engage a Roving Security Officer to provide technical security expertise and surge support to locations facing increased security challenges or operational demands.
Under the supervision of the Head of Field Office of Dori and the technical support of the Head of Security, the incumbent will support security assessments, risk mitigation measures, emergency preparedness, security reporting, and capacity-building initiatives. The assignment will also enhance coordination with relevant stakeholders and ensure the consistent application of WFP security policies and procedures.
The overall purpose of the assignment is to strengthen the safety and security of WFP personnel, assets, facilities, and operations, thereby enabling the uninterrupted delivery of humanitarian assistance in a complex and dynamic operating environment.

Key Responsibilities

- Provide surge security support to Field Offices facing increased security risks, operational challenges, or emergency response requirements.

- Advise management on security risks, mitigation measures, and operational security requirements to ensure safe and effective delivery programme.
- Conduct security risk assessments of WFP personnel, assets, facilities, and operations, and recommend appropriate mitigation measures.
- Monitor and analyze security developments and provide timely security advice, reports, and recommendations to support decision-making.
- Support compliance with Security Risk Management (SRM) measures, WFP security policies, procedures, and applicable security standards.
- Strengthen emergency preparedness and crisis management arrangements through the review of contingency plans, SOPs, evacuation procedures, and preparedness exercises.
- Support the planning and implementation of security arrangements for field missions, humanitarian operations, assessments, and official visits.
- Liaise and coordinate with UNDSS, government authorities, security actors, humanitarian partners, and other stakeholders to facilitate security information sharing and operational access.
- Represent WFP in security coordination forums and meetings, as delegated by the Head of Security.
- Build the security capacities of Field Office personnel and security focal points through training, mentoring, briefings, and awareness activities.
- Prepare accurate and timely security assessments, mission reports, incident reports, advisories, and other security-related documentation in accordance with WFP standards.
- Track the implementation of security recommendations and support continuous improvement of security risk management practices across Field Offices.
- Perform other security-related duties as required in support of WFP operations.

Deliverables:

• Security assessment reports with recommendations and action plans.
• Mission reports following each deployment or field mission.
• Monthly activity and security advisory reports.
• Security training materials, attendance records, and mission debriefs.
• Updated SOPs, contingency plans, and preparedness documentation, where required.
• End-of-assignment report summarizing activities conducted, achievements, key findings, recommendations, and lessons learned.
