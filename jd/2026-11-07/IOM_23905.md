Introduction
Established in 1951, IOM is a Related Organization of the United Nations and the leading UN agency in the field of migration. Working closely with governmental, intergovernmental and non-governmental partners, IOM promotes humane and orderly migration for the benefit of all. It saves lives and protects people on the move, drives solutions to displacement, and facilitates pathways for regular migration, while providing services and advice to governments and migrants.
IOM is committed to fostering a respectful, inclusive and supportive workplace where all employees can thrive professionally and feel valued. By creating such an environment, IOM aims to better harness the full potential of migration and strengthen its support to people on the move.
IOM invites candidates from diverse backgrounds to apply and provides reasonable accommodation throughout the recruitment process when required. Learn more about IOM’s workplace culture at IOM workplace culture | International Organization for Migration

For all IOM vacancies, applications from qualified and eligible first-tier candidates are considered before those of qualified and eligible second-tier candidates in the selection process. Organizational Context and Scope
Under the overall supervision of the Chief of Mission (CoM) and the direct supervision of the Chief Migration Health Officer (CMHO), the incumbent will be responsible for technical support to laboratory services with respect to Migration Health Assessments (HAP) and other programmes implemented by the IOM Migration Health Division in Harare,Zimbabwe.

Required Qualifications and Experience
Education

- University degree from an accredited academic institution in

- Medical Laboratory Sciences, Microbiology, Molecular Biology, Medical Microbiology, or a related field.
or a related discipline with four years’ experience or

- Master’s degree from an accredited academic institution in Medical (Clinical) Laboratory Sciences, Microbiology or a related discipline with two years’ experience

Accredited Universities are those listed in the UNESCO World Higher Education Database.
Experience

- Four years of relevant experience in laboratory experience in TB laboratory diagnostics, including smear microscopy, mycobacterial culture, DST, and molecular testing At least two years of of supervisory or laboratory management experience

- Effectively applies and transfers relevant technical knowledge and practices to personnel and laboratory operations.

- Demonstrated expertise in sputum processing and liquid and solid culture methods, first- and second-line drug susceptibility testing, and molecular TB diagnostic platforms.
- Strong knowledge of TB laboratory biosafety, quality assurance, and quality management systems.
- Experience with laboratory accreditation processes and external quality assessment programmes is desirable.
- Computer literate with good understanding of data collection and analysis for monitoring and continuous improvement of laboratory services and performance.

- Excellent awareness of the laboratory role in health systems, optimal use of resources for quality and efficient services delivery.

Skills

- Effectively applies and transfers relevant technical knowledge and practices to personnel and laboratory operations
- Technical expertise in mycobacteriology and TB diagnostics
- Leadership and people management
- Quality and biosafety management
- Analytical and problem-solving skills
- Planning, organization, and resource management
- Effective communication and stakeholder engagement
