The role is based in the WRI Europe office in the Netherlands. This is a hybrid position. You will need to be in office at least 8 days per month and living within reasonable commuting distance of the office.

About WRI Europe:

As the Strategic Coordinator, European Executive Office, you will be part of the WRI Europe Executive Office. You will work closely with the WRI Europe Regional Director to ensure the smooth functioning of key executive functions of the WRI Europe office and support the implementation of its strategy. You will be the key liaison between the Regional Director and internal and external stakeholders and collaborators at the content level and will support the Regional Director in delivery of the goals of the Global Executive Team (GET). You will support WRI Europe’s programmatic delivery, fundraising strategy and core functioning.

You will report directly to the WRI Europe Regional Director.

What you will do:

Support the WRI Europe Regional Director and Executive Office (50%)

- Support the annual strategic planning process in terms of coordination and content design.
- Coordinate outputs and products for key institutional internal and external events with a specific focus on the content and strategic preparation of these events. Support a connection with the Internal Communications team.
- Support the functioning of the European Leadership Team (LT) to deliver on its goals.
- Engage and support Europe-based managers to ensure a successful delivery of their responsibilities and goals as part of the LT team, monitoring progress, and supporting learning.
- Back up of and support to Executive Assistant in terms of planning external engagements.

Strategy Development and Implementation (20%)

- Support the implementation of the Europe Strategy, ensuring compliance with WRI internal cycles and processes, support monitoring, learning and evaluation.
- Work on the process of Theory of Change (ToC) development across various programmatic priority areas within the WRI Europe Strategy, ensuring equity is integrated into the ToC development process.
- Ensure the WRI Europe Strategy Planning, Monitoring, Evaluation and Learning (PMEL) process stays aligned and up to date with Global WRI PMEL guidance and cycles.
- Support the fundraising strategy underpinning the Europe programmatic strategy.

Support Global Programmatic and Institutional Coordination in Key Areas (15%)

- Support the Regional Director in his role as member of the GET and WRI’s global processes.
- Support the set-up and functioning of WRI Europe’s Supervisory Board.
- Coordinate engagement with the WRI wide network of offices to inform both institutional processes, implementation of WRI Europe’s local transition strategy, and multi-country initiatives where relevant.
Support on External Affairs (15%)

- Support the Regional Director in the development and preparation of external speaking engagements and other external responsibilities.
- Work closely with the Europe Communications Director and Executive Assistant to the Regional Director when coordinating external engagements.

What you will need:

- Education: You have a completed bachelor’s degree in Development, Economics, Political Science, International Relations, Journalism / Communications, or another related field.
- Experience: You have 6+ years of full-time, relevant experience in a strategic planning and coordination function in the climate, sustainability or environmental sector
- Demonstrable understanding of WRI’s strategic priorities
- Experience producing written outputs for various audiences
- Experience in presentation tools and speech writing
- Experience collaborating effectively in teams
- Languages: Proficiency in English. Working knowledge of Dutch would be advantageous.
- Requirements: You will need to be resident in the Netherlands at the start of employment.

Potential Salary:

If based in the Netherlands: €53,000 – €66,000 (excluding 8% vacation allowance)

Salary is commensurate with experience and other compensable factors.

About Us:

World Resources Institute works to improve people’s lives, protect and restore nature and stabilize the climate. As an independent research organization, we leverage our data, expertise and global reach to influence policy and catalyse change across systems like food, land and water; energy; and cities. Our 2,000+ staff work on the ground in more than a dozen focus countries and with partners in over 50 nations.

Our mission and values:

WRI’s mission is to move human society to live in ways that protect Earth’s environment and its capacity to provide for the needs and aspirations of current and future generations.

Our values are shared ideals that bind us together: Independence, Integrity, Impact, Partnership and Care.
