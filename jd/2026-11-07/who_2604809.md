OBJECTIVES OF THE PROGRAMME
WHO’s work in countries operationalizes the General Programme of Work (GPW14) and the European Programme of Work 2025-2029 (EPW2). GPW14 focuses on accelerating progress toward Health for All by strengthening systems for health, improving access to essential services, advancing preparedness and resilience, and reducing health inequalities. EPW2, endorsed at RC75 following extensive consultation with Member States, reaffirms WHO/Europe’s role in bringing unique value to countries through five core functions:
1. International knowledge hub for health – providing authoritative evidence, norms, policies and best practices from across the Region and globally to inform national public health decision-making.
2. Impartial coordinator for everyone’s health – convening national stakeholders and partners to align strategies and support coherent, multisectoral responses to shared health challenges.
3. Leader in tracking and managing cross-border health threats – supporting surveillance, preparedness, and coordinated action on public health emergencies in line with the Preparedness 2.0 agenda.
4. Driver of health system transformation – advising national authorities on evidence-informed reforms and policies that strengthen health systems and improve performance.
5. Voice for health equity – helping countries identify who is being left behind and integrating equity considerations into health policies and strategies.
In this context, WHO Country Offices enable the translation of global and regional commitments into national action. They support Member States by providing strategic advice, convening dialogue across sectors and partners, connecting national authorities with WHO’s technical expertise, and facilitating the use of evidence and best practices to inform policy.
DESCRIPTION OF DUTIES
The purpose of this position is to provide technical support to the Ministry of Health (MOH) of Ukraine in strengthening the immunization cold chain system. This position will enable the WHO Regional Office for Europe (WHO/EURO) to be more responsive and to play a leading role in strengthening of vaccine management and safety practices for further diseases preparedness and control. Under the overall guidance of the Head of WHO Office in Ukraine and direct supervision of the Health Programme Lead, with close collaboration with the Vaccine Preventable Diseases and Immunization (VPI) unit in WHO European Regional Office in Copenhagen, the incumbent will undertake the following specific responsibilities:
1. Contribute to the implementation of Standard Operation Procedures (SOPs) for Vaccine storage and distribution at national warehouse and WHO outsource premises. In addition, considering power outages and current situation with electricity system conduct specific risk assessment.
2. Serve as a technical resource to the programme concerning vaccine safety in regards of storage and distribution.
3. Arrange for capacity building and training on storage, logistic and actions on emergency situations.
4. Monitor and conduct situation analysis of available cold chain equipment and prepare tables, graphs and other statistical data and report outcome of analysis for generating evidence for needs and decision making process.
5. Conduct regular risk assessment at the national and district level warehouses for vaccine storage.
6. Provide technical advice to strengthen cold chain capacities.
7. Monitor performance and compliance with established SOPs for performance qualifications of cold rooms, equipment at the level of healthcare delivery providers and remote temperature monitoring system.
8. Liaise with national counterparts, and partners (UN and bilateral) to harmonize implementation activities related to cold chain.

Essential: A university degree (Bachelor's level) in cold chain engineer, logistics, supply chain management, medicine, pharmacy, biomedical engineering, or a related field relevant to this position.
Desirable: Master's degree or advanced training in any of the above.
Experience
Essential: At least 2 years of professional experience at the national level in cold chain logistics, vaccine management, or immunization program implementation. Demonstrated experience working with/within public projects and working with multiple stakeholders, such as national policymakers, NGOs, and the private sector, in the area of cold chain and logistics. Good knowledge of cold chain system in Ukraine.
Desirable: Experience in planning and implementing health-related projects, knowledge of global vaccine supply chains, and familiarity with immunization and cold chain systems in Ukraine.

5. Proven ability to work comfortably in an international, multi-disciplinary, fast-paced environment.
