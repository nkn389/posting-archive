EHSS Support – A/EDA Merak Site

General Purpose:

- To support continuous improvement of Environmental and Occupational Health Management Systems in accordance with company standards and applicable regulations

- To contribute to the development and implementation of environmental management programs, including waste management, pollution prevention, resource conservation, and environmental compliance

- To contribute to occupational health initiatives by promoting employee well-being, industrial hygiene monitoring, health surveillance, and workplace health risk management

Main Responsibility:

Environmental related:

- Ensure ccompliance with environmental regulations, permits, and company standards.

- Monitor and manage waste, wastewater, air emissions, hazardous materials, and other environmental aspects.

- Conduct environmental inspections and audits to identify compliance gaps and improvement opportunities.

- Coordinate environmental monitoring activities and maintain required records

- Take responsibility as the coordinator for hazardous waste handling in Merak Site such as:

-

-

- Hazardous waste (LB3) handling in TPS B3

- Electronic reporting in SIMPEL for hazardous waste (Speed)

- Monitor the implementation of hazardous waste handling

- Ensure compliance of hazardous waste handling to Indonesia regulation and BASF regulation

- Prepare and submit environmental reports to regulatory authorities (PROPER) and management

- Develop and implement occupational health programs and initiatives

- Coordinate employee medical examinations (pre-employment, periodic, special, and return-to-work assessments)

- Monitor workplace health risks including chemical, biological, ergonomic, and physical hazards.

- Coordinate industrial hygiene monitoring activities such as noise, dust, illumination, heat stress, and air quality assessments.

- Support health surveillance programs and maintain employee health records in compliance with applicable regulations.

- Promote health awareness campaigns, wellness programs, and disease prevention initiatives.

- Participate in emergency drills involving environmental spills, chemical exposure, and health emergencies.

- Prepare monthly, quarterly, and annual EHSS performance reports.

- Track environmental and occupational health KPIs.

- Ensure document control requirements are met.

Qualification:

Education:

- Bachelor’s degree in environmental engineering, Environmental Science, Public Health, Occupational Health, Industrial Hygiene, Chemical Engineering, or related field.

Experience:

- 1-2 years of experience in Environmental, Occupational Health, or EHSS functions.

Other Competencies :

- Sound technical knowledge

- High level of initiative to get the work done

- Analytical thinking and troubleshooting

- Reporting method, computer & program literacy
