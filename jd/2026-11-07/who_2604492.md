OBJECTIVES OF THE PROGRAMME
Unitaid saves lives by making new health products affordable and available in low- and middle-income countries. Collaborating with partners, Unitaid identifies innovative treatments, tackles market barriers, and quickly delivers solutions to those in need. Since 2006, Unitaid has unlocked over 100 health products, addressing HIV, TB, malaria, women's and children's health, and pandemic preparedness. Every year, these products benefit more than 300 million people. Unitaid is a hosted partnership of the World Health Organization.

DESCRIPTION OF DUTIES
Under the overall supervision of the Deputy Executive Officer and the direct supervision of the Team Lead, Executive Office, the incumbent will perform the following tasks:

- Support the articulation and internal communication of organizational priorities and strategies across the Secretariat.
- Facilitate and guide departments and teams in translating institutional priorities into coherent, organization-wide operational plans.
- Provide hands-on, day-to-day support to initiatives that strengthen strategic planning, cross-functional coordination, and integrated programme management.
- Contribute to the design and implementation of planning and prioritization processes that enhance institutional coherence and support effective resource allocation, including through close collaboration with Secretariat teams responsible for corporate financial management and organizational performance management.
- Independently plan, prepare, and present clear, data-informed analyses and briefings to support executive-level decision-making and organizational learning.
- Coordinate targeted business management analyses and project management efforts for organization-wide initiatives that advance institutional objectives and priorities.
- Perform other duties as assigned.

REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS
Education
Essential:

- Advanced University degree in Business Administration, Organizational Management, or a related field.

Desirable:

- Professional certification in a formal project management methodology (e.g., Agile, Prince2, Six Sigma).
- Professional certification in a formal organizational planning methodology, ideally the Objectives and Key Results (OKR) methodology.

Experience
Essential:

- Minimum of seven (7) years of progressively responsible experience in Strategic operational planning and programme management within international public health or a closely related field.
- Demonstrated experience in executive-level, cross-functional organizational management, involving multi-stakeholder collaboration.
- Demonstrated experience in organization-wide prioritization, planning, and performance monitoring, with demonstrated impact on institutional effectiveness.
- Experience working in an international cross-cultural environment.

Desirable:

- Experience working in a UN organization/agency Executive Office or equivalent.
- Experience in health projects in at least 2 of the 5 Unitaid programmatic priority areas (i.e., HIV and coinfections, TB, malaria, women and children's health, global health emergencies).

Skills

- Excellent written and verbal communication skills, including presentation skills, with the ability to convey complex information in a clear, concise and compelling way.
- Excellent planning, organizational, and project management skills.
- Outstanding interpersonal skills, tact, discretion, patience, and mature judgment.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with demonstrated ability to assess context and challenges; collect, analyse, and interpret relevant data and information; and develop and communicate robust solutions that can be implemented given constraints and capacities.
- Demonstrated ability to gather, consolidate, manage, and present data and information from multiple providers for organizational planning, monitoring, decision-making, and reporting.
- Proven ability to support cross-functional coordination and facilitate organization-wide planning processes that align with strategic priorities.
- Familiarity with institutional planning cycles and the ability to link operational planning with budgeting and performance monitoring processes.
- Ability to work collaboratively in a fast-paced environment, managing multiple tasks and meeting deadlines.

WHO Competencies

- Teamwork
- Respecting and promoting individual and cultural differences
- Communication
- Producing results
- Ensuring the effective use of resources

Use of Language Skills
Essential:

- Expert knowledge of English.

Desirable:

- Intermediate knowledge of French.
- Intermediate knowledge of any other official UN languages.
