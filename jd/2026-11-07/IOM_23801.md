Introduction
Established in 1951, IOM is a Related Organization of the United Nations and the leading UN agency in the field of migration. Working closely with governmental, intergovernmental and non-governmental partners, IOM promotes humane and orderly migration for the benefit of all. It saves lives and protects people on the move, drives solutions to displacement, and facilitates pathways for regular migration, while providing services and advice to governments and migrants.
IOM is committed to fostering a respectful, inclusive and supportive workplace where all employees can thrive professionally and feel valued. By creating such an environment, IOM aims to better harness the full potential of migration and strengthen its support to people on the move.

Organizational Context and Scope
Under the direct supervision of the Chief of Mission (CoM), in close collaboration with the Regional Office (RO), the Risk and Compliance Officer, will support the development and implementation of accountability, compliance, and quality control systems to ensure adherence to institutional policies, mitigate risks, and enhance programmatic effectiveness across International Organization for Migration (IOM) Venezuela.

Coordination

- Assist in the coordination of the Risk Management and Compliance Unit activities across relevant Country Office’s (CO’s) functions and sub-units: Legal, Risk and Compliance.
- Provide technical inputs to the Chief of Mission and Senior Management on the mission’s accountability, compliance and quality control mechanisms, and contribute to the implementation of measures to strengthen related mechanisms.
- Contribute to the alignment of the pillar’s activities with IOM’s global frameworks, mission’s strategic objectives, and donor requirements, including the appropriate reporting from Risk Management and Compliance Pillar point of view.

Risk and Compliance

- Provide technical support to integrate risk management into planning, decision-making, and resource allocation; support field offices with risk assessments and maintain updated risk registers and treatment plans.
- Assist in developing and improving risk and compliance processes to uphold a strong internal control environment and facilitate alignment with IOM policies and donor requirements.
- Monitor the use of IOM resources and verify compliance with strategic/project plans and donor agreements, maintaining proper documentation through means of verification.
- Draft compliance-related SOPs, deliver Anti-Fraud training, and contribute to strengthening oversight systems and internal controls.
- Analyze fraud-related data, prepare risk reports for internal/external audiences, and escalate risk/compliance issues as needed; travel within response areas as required.

Capacity Building

- Contribute to capacity-building activities for mission staff and partners in risk management, and MEAL/AAP frameworks and practices, including supporting the risk committee efforts and other mission platforms.
- Perform any such other duties as may be assigned.
