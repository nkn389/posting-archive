Duties and Responsibilities
The United Nations Environment Programme (UNEP) is the leading global environmental authority that sets the global environmental agenda, promotes the coherent implementation of the environmental dimension of sustainable development within the United Nations system and serves as an authoritative advocate for the global environment. Its mandate is to coordinate the development of environmental policy consensus by keeping the global environment under review and bringing emerging issues to the attention of governments and the international community for action. The overall objective of the Industry and Economy Division of UNEP is to encourage decision makers in government, local authorities, and industry to develop and adopt policies, strategies, practices, and technologies that promote sustainable patterns of consumption and production, make efficient use of natural resources, ensure safe management of chemicals, and contribute to making trade and environment policies mutually supportive. It promotes the development, use and transfer of policies, technologies, economic instruments, managerial practices, and other tools that assist in environmentally sound decision making and the building of corresponding activities. The Sustainable Mobility Unit (SMU) is part of the Industry and Economy Division and promotes sustainable low and zero emissions transport in low-and-middle-income countries around the world through projects and programmes on cleaner Fuels and Vehicles, Electric Mobility, Active Mobility and Transport Digitalisation. The consultant will support the delivery of the Active Mobility projects across its donor-funded projects and represent/support SMU’s technical engagement with the GEF Working Group on Active Mobility. The consultant will provide technical, coordination and reporting support at regional, national and city level, with particular attention to policy, strategy and action plan development, and to unlocking investment for walking and cycling. The consultant will also assist in the implementation of the Pan African Action Plan for Active Mobility (PAAPAM) In close consultation with the SMU Active Mobility lead, the consultant will be responsible for the following activities: Lead engagement with the GEF Working Group on Active Mobility. Coordinate and host two working group meetings. Prepare technical notes, policy briefs and presentations for Working Group sessions; track agreed actions and follow up on behalf of SMU; ensure GEF-related outputs are coherent with ongoing activities. 1. Develop investment and financing guidance for active mobility. Contribute to guidance notes, business cases and tools that help governments, donors and financial institutions, including those engaging through the GEF Working Group to prioritize and structure investment in walking and cycling infrastructure and programmes. 2. Support policy, strategy and action plan development. Provide technical support to national and city governments developing or reviewing walking and cycling policies, strategies, master plans or action plans, in target countries including drafting inputs, reviewing government-produced drafts, and preparing briefing materials for stakeholder consultations. 3. Support the development of investment and financing guidance for active mobility. Contribute to guidance notes, business cases and tools that help governments, donors and financial institutions – including those engaging through the GEF Working Group - prioritize and structure investment in walking and cycling infrastructure and programmes. 4. Assist the PAAPAM Coordination Hub and regional task forces in monitoring and implementation progress across countries, compiling lessons learned and preparing materials for the PAAPAM Steering Groups and related regional convenings. 5. Prepare case studies, factsheets, reports presentations and web/newsletter content that showcase results across the active mobility portfolio and support knowledge development and dissemination, clearly distinguishing which donor/project each result relates to. 6. Provide general support to the SMU active mobility team. Assist with logistics and substantive preparation for workshops, missions, training and Working Group meetings at regional, country and city level, and undertake any other related tasks assigned by the supervisor within the scope of this assignment.

At least 2 years of experience working on active mobility, urban planning, and/or gender. Proven experience working with national and city governments in Africa is required. Proven experience in design, coordination, delivery of training and virtual knowledge-sharing events is required. Proven technical writing, analytical, presentation and communication skills are required. Proven expertise in sustainable transport projects within Africa is required. Prior experience working in an international organization or government agency is desirable. Solid understanding of walking and cycling trends, and policy frameworks is desirable. Excellent skills in stakeholder engagement, training, technical writing, and effective communication are desirable. Familiarity with national and international development frameworks, including experience with donor-funded projects and the Pan African Action Plan for Active Mobility is desirable.
