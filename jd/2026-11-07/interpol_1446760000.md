SUMMARY OF THE ASSIGNED DUTIES, INCLUDING GOALS AND OBJECTIVES OF THE POST

As part of the General Secretariat of INTERPOL, the world’s largest international police Organization, the Information and Communication Technology (ICT) Executive Directorate delivers trusted, secure, and innovative digital platforms and services that form the technological backbone of the Organization. ICT enables global police cooperation and supports INTERPOL’s strategic and operational objectives, ensuring that law enforcement agencies across member countries can collaborate effectively and securely.

The Information Technology (IT) Project Manager manages IT projects from initiation through Go-Live, transition and formal closure, achieving an optimum result compliant with Business requirements as regards scope, quality (including performance and security), time, cost and risk. The post holder coordinates the resources involved in the assigned projects, acts as the day-to-day counterpart of the Project Owner, maintains alignment with the approved business case and intended outcomes, and works within the methodology, tooling and governance standards set by the ICT Project Management Office. The post holder may, for a given project, assume the Scrum Master role for the delivery squad.

PRINCIPAL DUTIES AND ACTIVITIES

DUTY 1 - Coordinating and mobilizing the project teams

- Produce the initial project assessment: cost evaluation, technical feasibility, risk assessment and expected SLA. Prepares the Project Management Plan (project schedule, operating procedures, reporting mechanisms), and submits it for approval.

- Mobilize the contributors to the projects (business team, ICT teams and external partners) and ensure they share common objectives aligned with the project.

- Supervise and coordinate, within each project, all actions assigned to internal ICT teams, external consultants or contractors.

- Define the required skills profiles for the assigned project and support the recruitment or allocation of the resources concerned.

- Apply the delivery approach approved for the project—predictive, agile or hybrid—in accordance with ICT PMO standards and the needs, complexity and risk profile of the project.

DUTY 2 - Managing scope, budget, planning and risks

- Define and maintain the project plan, deliverables, budget and risk register, and keep the project baseline current.

- Maintain alignment between project deliverables, the approved business case and the intended business outcomes, monitor whether the project remains capable of delivering the expected value, and escalate material deviations to the Project Owner.

- Track deviations and lead the team in identifying remediation and optimization opportunities.

- Initiate the formal revision process as soon as a deviation from the approved plan is foreseen in terms of total budget, budget structure, team allocation, scope or timeline, with updated plan and budget built with the relevant support functions.

- Supervise all aspects of the execution phase, including the production of key project documentation and deliverables (including product documentation such as architecture, hardware and software).

- Act, where required, as Scrum Master for the delivery squad, facilitating the Scrum events, removing impediments and coaching the team in the Scrum framework.

DUTY 3 – Governance, Scrum ceremonies and reporting

- Organize and lead project-level governance (steering, checkpoint and stage-gate meetings) and, where acting as Scrum Master, facilitates the team-level Scrum events.

- Provide regular progress status reports, at the frequency required by the ICT PMO, to the hierarchy and to key project stakeholders (business owners, external sponsors, etc.).

- Provide the ICT PMO and the Project Owner with all information necessary to measure progress, impact and risk levels, and records project data in the corporate project management tool.

- Identify project stakeholders and maintain an appropriate engagement plan, ensuring that their information, consultation and decision-making needs are addressed throughout the project lifecycle.

- Coordinate business-readiness and change activities with the Project Owner and relevant functions, including impact and readiness assessments, communications, training and adoption monitoring.

- Manage escalation and presents options, seeking methodological support from a Senior IT Project Manager or from direct manager when required.

DUTY 4 - Security, data protection and compliance

- Plan, coordinate and track the integration of security requirements by design and by default throughout the project lifecycle, including their verification during acceptance testing, in liaison with the responsible technical and assurance functions.

- Coordinate and track the activities required to demonstrate that data collected, protected and used within the project complies with the Organization's data-protection and information-security framework, and escalate identified gaps to the competent authority and the Project Owner.

- Coordinate the mandatory security, data-protection, architecture and operational assurance reviews, track the resulting actions, and ensure that the relevant authorities provide the required approvals or acceptances before Go-Live.

DUTY 5 - Sourcing and vendor delivery

- Contribute to the procurement process when relevant, including bid evaluations and the presentation of results to the Procurement Committee.

- Responsible for the execution of the IT aspects of the contract with the selected bidder for the assigned project.

- Monitor supplier performance against contractual deliverables, service levels and quality expectations.

DUTY 6 – Transition to product

- Check that all pre-requisites and Go-Live criteria are met, and confirm that training materials, awareness sessions and support documentation are provided.

- Conduct the formal handover of the delivered product or service to the IT Product Manager and to IT Operations, with documented acceptance, and manage Hypercare to its defined exit criteria (duration, incident thresholds, service levels).

- Conduct formal project closure, including confirmation of acceptance, financial closure, archiving of project records, documentation of lessons learned, release of project resources, and transfer of outstanding actions and residual risks to the responsible owners.

- Communicate to the Business Owner any change to the planned running costs of the product or service for the Organization.

DUTY 7 - Methodology adoption and continuous improvement

- Apply project management and agile methodology, templates and tooling defined by the ICT PMO, and keep the project workspace complete and up to date.

- Contribute to the project management community of practice, sharing lessons learned and improvement proposals with the IT Project Management Competency Centre.

Perform any other duties as required by the hierarchy.
