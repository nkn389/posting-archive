Under the overall supervision of the head of sub-office, and the direct supervision of the logistics officer, the successful candidate will maintain IOM boats, vehicle fleet and any motor operated equipment in good running condition. He/ She will ensure that any mechanical faults are corrected in a timely manner, and periodic maintenance is regularly scheduled and promptly done. Guaranteeing to the best of their abilities that all transportation needs and motor equipment at the sub-office are operating in a safe and optimal manner.

EDUCATION

- Bachelor’s degree with four years of relevant work experience; and/or

- Completed secondary education with six years of relevant work experience.

- Skill certification from technical or trade school is preferred.

EXPERIENCE

- Experience with a variety of engines such as gasoline 2-stroke, 4-stroke, diesel, and electric motors.

- Experience with maritime vessels and practical experience in fixing boat/vessel engines

- Experience with building and repairing vehicle and official electrical systems

- Experience with vehicle panel beating/ repair; fibre-glass construction and spraying.

- Experience as a crane operator with safe driving record preferred

- Valid Driver’s License

SKILLS

- Good communication skills

- In-depth knowledge of vehicle diagnostics and mechanical systems

- Great deal of manual dexterity and fine motor skills

- Resourcefulness, strength and agility

- Good interpersonal skills, diligence and dedication
