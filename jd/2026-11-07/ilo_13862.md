Introduction

The International Social Security Association (ISSA) is the world's leading international organization for social security institutions, government departments and agencies. Founded in 1927 under the auspices of the International Labour Organization, the ISSA counts more than 340 member organizations in 165 countries.

The ISSA provides member institutions with knowledge, networking opportunities and activities to promote social security. In implementing its activities, the ISSA aims at good governance, quality service delivery, efficient administration and transparent financial management. The ISSA is financed through member contributions based on voluntary membership and is governed by the ISSA statutory bodies and a range of institutional regulations and policies. The ISSA Secretariat is led by the ISSA Secretary General.

The Director of the Social Security Development (SSD) Branch reports to the Secretary General. The incumbent provides leadership for ISSA’s technical activities and manages the SSD Branch, which is responsible for the relevance and high quality of ISSA social security knowledge output, analysis and data collection. This includes responsibility for the ISSA knowledge-based products and services, such as the preparation and update of professional standards in social security administration (ISSA Guidelines) and related programmes, the development of capacity-building activities, the update and development of information in the ISSA social security databases, social security policy analysis activities and the technical content of ISSA conferences and events. The Branch implements its projects in close collaboration with ISSA Technical Commissions which bring together high level member representatives in various technical fields.

As a member of a compact management team, the incumbent collaborates closely with other managers and staff members in the implementation of the cross-Branch development and production goals of the ISSA Programme and Budget.. The incumbent participates in the strategic decision-making of the ISSA and provides technical input to the development of the ISSA programme of activities.

Reporting lines: The incumbent reports to the Secretary General.

Description of Duties

- Exercise full management responsibility for the implementation of the branch’s deliverables, including identifying priorities, formulating, establishing and monitoring implementation of work plans and intermediary objectives. Provide direction, leadership and guidance to staff to achieve objectives and ensure that all staff under his/her responsibility optimize the use of resources for the achievement of results and that they are held accountable for their performance.

- Lead the development of the ISSA knowledge base, in particular with regard to the ISSA Guidelines and knowledge resources covering the technical content as well as the training and Recognition programmes. Create a long-term strategic plan for the development of ISSA Guidelines with a view to ensuring that the ISSA covers the key business processes in administration and responds to the priority administrative challenges of member organisations.

- Lead the production of ISSA publications on technical areas as well as the regional and global reports, including the production of short articles on social security administration and reforms, ensuring a regular stream of knowledge products in response to the diverse needs of ISSA member institutions.

- Supervise the technical programme for ISSA events, particularly International Conferences and global and regional Forums. Oversee the development of capacity-building products and services aligned with ISSA topical priorities and responsive to the needs of ISSA members.

- Oversee and coordinate the preparation and the implementation of the ISSA Technical Commissions’ work plans aligned with the ISSA triennial programme. Ensure that Technical Commissions are actively engaged in the work programme of the ISSA and maintain close and regular contacts with Technical Commission Chairpersons.

- Provide strategic advice and input to the ISSA Programme of activities to ensure that the ISSA reaches its objective of being the leading worldwide institution with regard to strategic and operational knowledge in social security administration. Ensure the appropriate evolution and extension of ISSA strategic programmes, notably related to innovation, knowledge production on social security administration and social security databases and propose and implement policy analysis activities in response to ISSA members’ needs.

- Lead the evaluation of the deliverables of the branch and participate in overall evaluation initiatives in line with the ISSA results-based programming methodology.

- Represent the ISSA and speak at major international and member events.

- Develop tailored knowledge and technical activities responding to the specific needs of individual, or groups of, member institutions Facilitate and spearhead interdisciplinary efforts within ISSA.

- Undertake other duties as required by the Secretary General.
