About the job
The Director, EMEA YouTube Trust and Safety is a critical role in protecting YouTube from badness. You are responsible to deliver high quality policy enforcement support on the most sensitive policy escalations, from external sources (news outlets, regulatory bodies, customers) as well as internal escalations. You lead our front line operations on cutting-edge policy issues and are comfortable delivering decisions/recommendations under tight time pressure to senior executives. You will directly manage the EMEA based Escalations team and oversee the Market Responsibility Specialist function in EMEA, the team who brings expert regional insights to help improve how YouTube enforces our policies.
You will be responsible for managing complex and evolving incidents from end to end. The role involves sensitive escalation management and working closely with senior stakeholders across the business to ensure alignment in applying policy principles and addressing brand risk. The role will require working cross-functionally across Policy Development, Policy Verticals, Scaled Abuse, Monetization, Product, and Global Scaled Operations to operationalize, enforce, and communicate policy enforcement decisions that keep users safe and minimize brand risk to the platform. The role requires thinking through the unique challenges of a broad array of policy areas (Child Safety, Violent Extremism, Hate/Harassment, Harmful/Dangerous, Misinformation, Adult) while developing workflows that are consistent and resilient. This role will review graphic, controversial, and sometimes offensive video content in line with YouTube’s community guidelines.

Responsibilities

- Manage, grow, and motivate the EMEA Escalations and Market Responsibility Specialist (MRS) teams to resolve complex customer, press, regulator, and executive escalations. Share escalations trends with enforcement managers to optimize policy guidelines.

- Act as regional Incident Commander for high-profile crises, coordinating cross-functionally across YouTube and Google. Work on-call weekends rotationally to review controversial content.

- Drive the regional code of conduct process globally to swiftly evaluate high-profile creators causing ecosystem harm.

- Maintain quality and service levels across all triage, investigation, and resolution paths. Establish weekend coverage plans.

- Represent YouTube Trust & Safety within EMEA Google leadership and serve as the Dublin office Site Lead.
