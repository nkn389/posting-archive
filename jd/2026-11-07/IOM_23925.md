Established in 1951, the International Organization for Migration (IOM) is a related organization of the United Nations (UN), and as the leading UN agency in the field of migration and works closely with governmental, intergovernmental and non-governmental partners. IOM is dedicated to promoting humane and orderly migration for the benefit of all. It does so by providing services and advice to governments and migrants.
Operational in Indonesia for more than 40 years, IOM Indonesia is working on a wide range of activities in partnership with the Government of Indonesia, civil society, private sector actors, migrants and communities. IOM Indonesia is engaged in several thematic areas, including: Counter-Trafficking (CT), Labour Migration and Social Inclusion (LMSI), Disasters, Climate and Resilience (DCR), Immigration and Border Governance (IBG), Refugee Care, Migration Health, Migration Governance and Resettlement and Voluntary Returns.

IOM Indonesia aims to ensure quality of care for refugees and asylum seekers referred to IOM by the Government of Indonesia according to applicable international humanitarian and human rights standards. The Programme supports refugees through healthcare, education basic needs, and contributes to the increased protection of vulnerable subgroups, such victims of sexual and gender-based violence, among others, through referral mechanisms involving the active participation of specialized state and non-state service providers. It also contributes to supporting them with the eventual sustainable pathways.
Under the direct supervision of the Senior Programme Associate, and under the overall supervision of the Head of Sub-Office, working closely with Health Care, Community Liaison Officers, Resource Management, as well as the Community Accountability Coordinator and team in Jakarta, the Programme Associate (Refugee Care) will be responsible for assisting in the implementation of refugee care activities in line with programme requirements and as part of the Programme’s strategy on community engagement and accountability to affected populations, and in line with IOM policies, standards and commitments

Responsibilities and Accountabilities

- Assist in the implementation and monitoring of programme activities in line with the RCA programme procedures and mechanisms.
- Undertake regular visits to gather information on beneficiaries’ situations, document their needs or concerns, and relay this information to the supervisor and relevant colleagues for follow-up; identify and promptly report any observed protection, security, or health-related concerns to the supervisor in line with established procedures.
- Maintain updated files and information for assigned beneficiaries in the case management system and follow up as per programme setup.
- Act as a focal point for routine operational workflows related to protection and care coordination activities, guided by the RCA management team at the Country Office.
- Monitor basic service-related information and activities, document gaps or issues observed during routine duties including those reported by beneficiaries and partners, share these with the supervisor and provide inputs on corrective actions.
- Organize meetings, workshops, training and other events; assist with logistical, arrangements, outreach, participation lists, basic facilitation support, and communication with refugee communities and local stakeholders as part of the community engagement activities.
- Support updating and routine verification of data and relevant information for monthly and other reports in the Refugee Registration and Assistance Monitoring System (RAMS).
- Review payments to service providers, verify that supporting documents are correct, valid and complete, and assist with simple expense tracking tasks working closely with Resource Management.
- Assist in local transfers, including facilitating escort arrangements, in close coordination with Resettlement and Voluntary Returns and Migration Health units in Jakarta.
- Assist in maintaining positive working relationships with local interlocutors including IDC, Immigration and Police, private sector actors, NGOs/CBOs, local representatives and community leaders in the delivery of programme activities.
- Support knowledge-sharing on refugee care activities and provide input on possible improvements to programme activities.
- Perform such other duties as may be assigned.
