About this position

​​We are looking for a collaborative, analytical and persistent consultant to support the operational work and the knowledge agenda of the Social Protection and Labor Markets Division in the area of long-term care. This includes supporting the implementation of project “Long-Term Care Policy Dialogue and Knowledge Generation in Collaboration with Japan in Latin America and the Caribbean”, which is funded by the Japan Trust Funds.

​​The Social Sector (SCL) has a multidisciplinary team convinced that investing in people is the way to improve lives and overcome the challenges of development in Latin America and the Caribbean. Together with the countries of the region, the Social Sector builds public policy solutions to reduce poverty and to improve the education, work, social protection and health services that citizens receive. The Sector's work aims to promote a more productive region with equal opportunities between men and women and with greater inclusion of the most vulnerable populations.

​
The Social Protection and Labor Markets Division (SPL) prepares and supervises operations that strive to advance the goals of poverty reduction, equal opportunities, and economic growth. The team works on safety nets and transfers, early childhood development, and aging by enhancing caregiving services, expanding the coverage of social insurance and pensions, and strengthening support systems for vulnerable populations. SPL also focuses on improving employment services and training programs, ensuring better access to decent jobs, and fostering a more dynamic and inclusive labor market.​​

What you’ll do

-Support the preparation and implementation of technical cooperation and loan projects. This includes writing terms of references, identifying consultants, reviewing project related materials, producing fact sheets, monitoring project milestones, evaluating project results, managing design, execution, and evaluation of pilots, drafting project documents. This also includes supporting the implementation of project “Long-Term Care Policy Dialogue and Knowledge Generation in Collaboration with Japan in Latin America and the Caribbean”, which is funded by the Japan Trust Funds.

-Support and collaborate in the conceptualization, elaboration, publication and dissemination of knowledge products.

-Support the organization and implementation of policy dialogue meetings, events (including those of the Long-Term Care Policy Network in Latin America and the Caribbean (RedCUIDAR+)) and webinars.

-​Support the reporting needed for all the previous items.

-​Support regular updates of the webpage of the Panorama of Aging and Long-Term Care.

-Support dialogue with the Japan Trust Funds and with Japanese experts collaborating with projects funded by these Funds.​
