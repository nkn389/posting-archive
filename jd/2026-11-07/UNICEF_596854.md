Strategic office context:

UNICEF Supply Division (SD) works to ensure equitable and inclusive access to essential supplies and services for children and their families, in development, humanitarian and fragile contexts. These supplies are critical to saving lives, supporting children’s health and education, and protecting them from abuse, exploitation, and neglect. As the UN’s largest end to end supply chain entity, UNICEF leverages its global reach, market influence, supply chain expertise and partnerships with governments, civil society, private sector, industry, and other stakeholders to meet children’s needs and protect their rights around the world.

SD focuses on three result areas to maximize impact, scale, and effectiveness: strategic procurement; supply preparedness and response in humanitarian and fragile contexts; and supply chain optimization. To facilitate and accelerate progress toward these results, SD employs three change strategies: financing for supply and services, supply chain digitalization and supply chain localization.

Job Organizational context:

Immunization Supplies cone focuses on the strategic positioning of supply and procurement of vaccines, safety injection and cold chain equipment and infrastructure needs to support immunization and health programmes. This work is carried out in close collaboration with the Global Programme Group, regional and country offices as well as key partners, such as WHO, the Gavi Alliance, the Global Polio Eradication Initiative, and the Gates Foundation.

The position is located within the Immunization Supplies cone, in the EPI Unit. Within the Immunization Supplies cone, the EPI Unit is accountable for supply, procurement, and market shaping for the portfolio of vaccines that include HepB, BCG, Diphtheria containing vaccines (Td, DTP and DT), Penta, Hexa, Hib and Pneumococcal Conjugate Vaccines for routine, outbreak immunization activities; and engaging in technical partnerships to facilitate access to novel TB vaccines.

Purpose for the job:

Under the general guidance of the Senior Contracts Manager, Vaccine Centre, the incumbent is responsible for the planning, development and implementation of procurement of a range of vaccines working with vaccine programme partners, overseeing market research and long-range procurement planning in order to achieve the most appropriate and cost-effective means of procurement.

This job profile will be supervising the relevant staff required to perform the activities as defined within the Contract Specialist Terms of Reference.

Key functions, accountabilities and related duties/tasks:

Procurement:

- Support timely development of procurement strategies, oversee procurement of vaccine materials.

- Oversee supplier relationships and contracts management to ensure supplier compliance with LTA’s and purchase order terms and conditions.

- Ensure all procurement activities are in compliance with the UNICEF Financial Rules and Regulations, Supply Procedures and Guidance Notes, and all other applicable procurement procedures.

- Oversee and advise requisitioning Country Office/Regional Office regarding contractual conditions and supplier appraisals for purchasing of products.

- Undertake manufacturer missions to troubleshoot and ensure supplier management and oversight, as required.

- Create awareness and understanding of the need for adequate quality standards, best practice and do-no-harm policies. Ensure policy guidelines and regulatory requirements are appropriate and consistently applied to UNICEF procurement in close collaboration with the team of multidisciplinary technical specialists and officers. Ensure compliance with Good Storage and Distribution Practices (GSDP), ISO and other relevant international regulations, standards and guidelines, including integration of Quality Management Systems (QMS) requirements into work processes and functions.

Partnerships:

- Engage with relevant market shaping partners such as [Gavi Alliance, WHO, Gates Foundation]

- Oversee engagement with disease programme teams and extended partners to provide the supply and demand perspectives to guide global programme decisions.

- Contribute to UNICEF led or other industry consultations and immunization meetings.

- Represent UNICEF Supply Division at partner meetings pertaining to immunization to represent supply perspective.

Supervision:

- Establish work plans and priorities, monitor achievements, and evaluate performance of support staff.

- Ensure timely reporting and communication of critical information in support of transparency.

- Contribute to capacity building and knowledge sharing within the team and throughout the Centre.

- Perform other duties or assignments as requested by the supervisor.
