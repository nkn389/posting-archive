Terms of Reference

1. General Background
UNHCR Korea’s Private Sector Partnership Service team has achieved a significant growth since 2009 and broadened the base for continued expansion of fundraising programs. For this, UNHCR Korea aims to have a staff responsible for Faceto-Face (F2F) Agency management that will help the team to drive more effective fundraising in the Face to-Face program, which is the largest acquisition channel for UNHCR Korea.

2. Purpose and Scope of Assignment
The main objective of this LICA is to assist management of the outsourced Agencies which provide Face to-Face Fundraising services to UNHCR to ensure that the F2F Agency Program achieves targets and deliver the most valued services to UNHCR. The LICA will also be responsible for identifying new growth
opportunities for the F2F Agency Program. The following is a list of responsibilities for this LICA:

Manage Face-to-Face Agency Programs
• Assists in establishing productive, professional relationships with Agencies which provide Face to-Face (F2F) Fundraising services to UNHCR;
• Assists in maintaining good professional relationships and rapport with the Agencies and liaise closely with them on a daily basis;
• Ensures that F2F Agency Programs achieve targets and provide services in compliance with UNHCR rules and regulations;
• Monitors and tracks budgets for F2F Agency Programs;
• Forecasts and tracks key performance indicators for F2F Agency Programs;
• Oversees campaign costs and processes invoices for F2F Agency Programs;
• Provides regular training to F2F Agency Fundraisers on UNHCR issues, new fundraising campaigns, F2F Code of Conduct, workflows, and etc.;
• Ensures that F2F Agency Fundraisers are properly trained and represent UNHCR in a professional
manner by coordinating professional mystery shopping activities;
• Proactively assesses, clarifies, and validates F2F Agency Programs needs on an ongoing basis;
• Communicate clearly the results and the progress of the F2F Agency Programs to the Face-to Face Manager;

Administration and Support
• Contributes to developing effective F2F Fundraising materials and campaign contents;
• Assist in procurement processes for Face-to-Face activities including the preparation of RFQs or RFPs, receipt of quotations, bids or proposals in compliance with the procurement standards and rules;
• Assists in managing, organizing and delivering the equipment and supplies necessary for the F2F Agency Programs;
• Provides management support for UNHCR’s Private Sector Partnerships work as a team member;
• Performs other duties as required.

3. Monitoring and Progress Controls
The individual contractor will perform duty in a combination of office and flexible working environment in line with UNHCR Korea’s guidelines under direct supervision of Associate PSP IG Officer.

c. Key Competencies - Excellent communication skills
