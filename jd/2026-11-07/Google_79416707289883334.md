Minimum qualifications:

- Bachelor’s degree in Science, Technology, Chemistry, Engineering, Environmental Science, Occupational Health and Safety, or a related technical field, or equivalent practical experience.

- 12 years of experience in EHS, and in a technical leadership role.

- 5 years of experience in a regional, multi-site, or country-level EHS leadership role managing an EHS team.

Preferred qualifications:

- Master's degree or equivalent practical experience.

- Professional EHS certifications (e.g., CSP, CIH, NEBOSH, DIS).

- Experience designing, implementing, and standardizing EHS management systems across geographically dispersed corporate and R&D real estate portfolios .

- Knowledge of navigating Indian EHS legislation (e.g., the Factories Act, 1948, the upcoming Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, 2020, Water and Air Acts, Hazardous and E-Waste Management Rules) and interfacing with Central/State Pollution Control Boards (CPCB/SPCBs) and local factory inspectorates.

- Excellent leadership, people management, problem-solving and crisis management skills, with ability to influence stakeholders and navigate complex projects in a fast-paced, evolving environment.

About the job
As part of the Environment, Health, and Safety (EHS) team, you focus on best practices to make Google safe and resilient. To support this mission, you promote a culture where safety, security and resiliency are embedded into every facet of Google. You identify, evaluate, and monitor enterprise risks to help make critical decisions to maintain the ability to innovate while keeping Google’s employees safe.
You will partner with cross-functional teams to build agile safety solutions in fast-paced environments. You will proactively identify EHS risks and collaborate with key partners to design and implement effective mitigations.

Responsibilities

- Develop and integrate safety solutions based on risk assessments to maximize worker protection in R&D, lab settings and office environments.

- Oversee the creation of procedures, program documentation, and digital tracking systems to ensure safety solutions scale across the region.

- Conduct regular audits and risk assessments; draft high-level reports for leadership on site safety and health.

- Lead incident investigations, incident response and tactical exercises.

- Support all campus compliance with local, national, and state EHS regulations along with company standards.
