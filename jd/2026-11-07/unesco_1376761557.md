OVERVIEW

As part of the digital transformation of public administration, the Ministry of Culture of Ukraine is implementing electronic registries and information systems that ensure transparency, efficiency of governance, and access to public services.
The “Diya.Engine” platform serves as a core technological infrastructure for the development and operation of the following four state registries: Register of the Basic Network of Cultural Institutions (Реєстр базової мережі закладів культури); State Register of Publishers, Producers, and Distributors of Publishing Products (Державний реєстр видавців, виготовлювачів і розповсюджувачів видавничої продукції); Register of Applications and Recipients of State Support in the Cultural Sector (Реєстр заявок та отримувачів державної підтримки у сфері культури); Register of Masters of Folk Arts and Crafts (Реєстр майстрів народних художніх промислів).
Given the critical importance of these systems, there is a need to implement modern DevOps practices, automate processes, ensure high availability of services, and develop modern, intuitive user interfaces. Furthermore, the Ministry requires comprehensive full-stack development and continuous web administration to modernize existing legacy web portals, deploy cost-effective content management systems, and integrate Artificial Intelligence (AI) solutions into public-facing services.

To ensure stable, secure, and scalable operation of the “Diya.Engine” platform, related state registries, and web interfaces.

To achieve this objective, it is necessary to ensure: - implementation of CI/CD practices; - full-stack development and website creation; - automation of deployment processes; - continuous administration of web interfaces and CMS platforms; - integration of AI technologies; - compliance with modern web development and information security standards.

ASSIGNMENTS

The objective of the assignment is to ensure full DevOps and Full-Stack support of the “Diya.Engine” platform and related web projects, including process automation, infrastructure support, UI/UX development, website administration, CMS deployment, AI integration, and the modernization of legacy web assets.

Scope of Work and Tasks

● Development and maintenance of CI/CD pipelines, including automation of testing, build, and deployment processes.
● Administration of infrastructure, databases, and continuous support of web-based administration interfaces.
● Full-stack development, including the creation of new websites and web portals from scratch.
● Deployment, customization, and administration of informational web interfaces and auxiliary web resources using open-source, cost-effective Content Management Systems (CMS), particularly WordPress, to ensure efficient and free-of-charge content management workflows; not applicable to the core state registries hosted on the "Diya.Engine" platform.
● Re-engineering and modernization of legacy web platforms, including the reconstruction and deployment of fully functional websites based on recovered static data, parsed HTML/CSS archives, or legacy site builders.
● Development, maintenance, and UI/UX optimization of user interfaces, web forms, and public dashboards for the core state registers of the Ministry of Culture of Ukraine on the "Diya.Engine" platform.
● Implementation of Artificial Intelligence (AI) tools and machine learning APIs to optimize data processing, automate tasks, and enhance web user experience.
● Implementation of Infrastructure as Code (IaC) approaches and configuration of monitoring, logging, and auditing systems.
● Integration of front-end components with backend services, databases, and APIs of state systems.
● Management of secrets, certificates, secure environment configurations, and application of security patches.
● Optimizing database performance, web application responsiveness (mobile-first approach), and cross-browser compatibility.
● Active day-to-day administration, content support, and development of the official web resources of the Ministry of Culture of Ukraine, including timely updates, security, accessibility, and performance monitoring.
● Ensuring DevOps support, infrastructure stability, and seamless CI/CD deployment pipelines for the specific state registers hosted on the platform:
- Register of the Basic Network of Cultural Institutions (Реєстр базової мережі закладів культури);
- State Register of Publishers, Producers, and Distributors of Publishing Products (Державний реєстр видавців, виготовлювачів і розповсюджувачів видавничої продукції);
- Register of Applications and Recipients of State Support in the Cultural Sector (Реєстр заявок та отримувачів державної підтримки у сфері культури);
- Register of Masters of Folk Arts and Crafts (Реєстр майстрів народних художніх промислів).
