Global Mobility / Deployment Officer- POOLING 2027

What we do

Since 1863, the International Committee of the Red Cross (ICRC) has worked to preserve human dignity and relieve suffering caused by war and armed violence.

In collaboration with our Red Cross and Red Crescent partners around the world, we do everything we can to deliver life-saving aid, reconnect families and locate missing people – helping those who need it most, regardless of who they are and what side of the front line they are on.

We engage with authorities and armed forces on all sides, often confidentially, pressing for access to detainees to improve their living conditions and urging compliance with international humanitarian law to protect non-combatants, including from digital threats.

Purpose

HR Administration Officers for 2027 will mainly be responsible for the following:

- Coordinating recruitment and contractual arrangements

- Remuneration and applicable benefits

- Coordinating travel, visa, and other deployment requirements.

The role ensures effective collaboration among units involved in the deployment process, supporting timely, compliant, and seamless deployments in line with organizational policies and operational needs.

Accountabilities & functional responsibilities

- Acts as the main point of contact for employees and HR functions, providing guidance on deployment, contractual arrangements, remuneration, benefits, and assignment-related requirements, in coordination with relevant specialists and subject-matter experts.

- Coordinates end-to-end deployment activities, from assignment planning and hiring to contractual arrangements, remuneration, benefits, medical clearance, visas, travel, and end-of-mission arrangements.

- Case management, resolution of complex cases, workload distribution, and implementation of projects and process improvements

- Identifies opportunities to enhance processes, ways of working, and team management practices, and supports the implementation of agreed improvements.
