&lt;h3&gt;&lt;span class="emphasis-2"&gt;&lt;b&gt;&lt;u&gt;What We Can Achieve Together:&lt;/u&gt;&lt;/b&gt;&lt;/span&gt;&lt;/h3&gt;&lt;br /&gt;&lt;p&gt;&lt;span style="color:#000000"&gt;Founded in 1951, The Nature Conservancy (TNC) is a global conservation organization dedicated to conserving the lands and waters on which all life depends. Guided by science and collaboration, we develop practical solutions that address climate change, protect lands, waters, and oceans, advance sustainable food and water systems, and support thriving communities. TNC is committed to building an inclusive, and culturally competent workforce. Working in more than 70 countries and all 50 United States, we partner with communities, governments, Indigenous peoples, businesses, scientists, and other organizations to achieve lasting conservation outcomes. Bristol Bay supports the world’s largest remaining wild salmon fisheries, extraordinary freshwater ecosystems, and vibrant and various communities and cultures. TNC’s Alaska Program works with local and regional partners to conserve these resources through science, community engagement, land and water stewardship, sustainable economic development, and participation in public planning and policy.&lt;/span&gt;&lt;/p&gt;&lt;h3&gt;&lt;br /&gt;&lt;br /&gt;&lt;/h3&gt;&lt;h3&gt;&lt;span class="emphasis-2"&gt;&lt;b&gt;&lt;u&gt;We’re Looking for You:&lt;/u&gt;&lt;/b&gt;&lt;/span&gt;&lt;/h3&gt;&lt;br /&gt;&lt;p&gt;&lt;span style="color:#000000"&gt;The Bristol Bay Sustainable Fisheries Lead provides strategic leadership for the Bristol Bay Program’s sustainable fisheries portfolio. The position takes an integrated approach to fisheries that encompasses habitat protection, Indigenous stewardship, subsistence and commercial fishing, local access and ownership, collaborative science, climate resilience, public policy, and sustainable economic opportunity. The Lead also contributes to related land conservation and community development initiatives that advance ecological, economic, cultural, and social objectives.&lt;/span&gt;&lt;/p&gt;&lt;br /&gt;&lt;p&gt;&lt;span style="color:#000000"&gt;The Lead is a relationship-builder and strategic integrator who works with Tribal governments, Alaska Native organizations and corporations, fishing interests, communities, agencies, scientists, conservation organizations, landowners, businesses, and funders.
