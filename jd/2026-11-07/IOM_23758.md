Introduction
Established in 1951, IOM is a Related Organization of the United Nations and the leading UN agency in the field of migration. Working closely with governmental, intergovernmental, and non-governmental partners, IOM promotes humane and orderly migration for the benefit of all. It saves lives and protects people on the move, drives solutions to displacement, and facilitates pathways for regular migration, while providing services and advice to governments and migrants.
IOM is committed to fostering a respectful, inclusive and supportive workplace where all employees can thrive professionally and feel valued. By creating such an environment, IOM aims to better harness the full potential of migration and strengthen its support to people on the move.
IOM invites candidates from diverse backgrounds to apply and provides reasonable accommodation throughout the recruitment process when required. Learn more about IOM’s workplace culture at IOM workplace culture | International Organization for Migration.

Organizational Context and Scope
Under the overall supervision of the Deputy Regional Director (Operations) and the direct supervision and strategic and technical direction of the Regional Thematic Specialist (Labour Migration), and in coordination with the Head of Unit, Partnerships and Development, Private Sector Partnerships (PSP), Headquarters (HQ), the Programme Development Officer (SHIFT Africa) will be responsible and accountable for coordinating the end-to-end programme-development process for SHIFT Africa, translating approved strategic and technical direction and inputs from relevant IOM functions into a coherent, technically sound and investment-ready programme package.
The Programme Development Officer (SHIFT Africa) will support the integration of relevant private-sector, market and partnership considerations into programme design, drawing on PSP expertise on private-sector engagement, partnership structuring, investment and co-investment, while working with regional and country teams to translate opportunity-system and market intelligence into viable programme pathways.
