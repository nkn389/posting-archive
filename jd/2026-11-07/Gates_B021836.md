The Team
Education is one of the strongest drivers of economic opportunity, yet millions of learners in the United States encounter barriers that prevent them from reaching their full potential. The Gates Foundation's U.S. Program (USP) is focused on helping millions more learners earn credentials of value that lead to economic mobility by improving outcomes at the critical milestones that shape long-term success.

The K-12 Teaching and Learning team works collaboratively across USP to deliver on the foundation's efforts to help more students build strong mathematical foundations and successfully complete Algebra I by 9th grade—an important milestone that opens pathways to advanced learning, postsecondary success, and future careers. Building on decades of partnership with educators, researchers, school systems, and developers, the team invests in evidence-based instructional approaches, high-quality instructional materials, professional learning, tutoring, responsible AI-enabled tools, and implementation supports that help teachers accelerate learning and enable students to catch up, keep pace, and get ahead. The team works across the education ecosystem to identify, develop, evaluate, and scale an integrated and coherent set of these solutions to improve mathematics teaching and learning for millions of students.

Your Role
The Deputy Director, Solutions provides strategic leadership for the Foundation's portfolio of investments that develop, validate, and scale AI-enabled solutions to improve K-12 mathematics learning. The role is accountable for advancing four core objectives of the foundation's K-12 strategy: expanding the availability of AI-enabled products, enabling instructional systems that are coherent, strengthening the field’s focus on curriculum and product quality and performance, and increasing access to modern high school mathematics courses.

This leader will bring deep edtech product, technology, and market expertise to refine and evolve the foundation’s strategy and work with the team to shape and evaluate investments that enable the development, improvement, and scaling of partner solutions. This includes assessment of technology architectures, product roadmaps, evidence plans, implementation and partnership models, and business and go-to-market strategies – ensuring that these are aligned with the foundation’s student-learning goals.

Reporting to the Director of K-12 Teaching and Learning, the Deputy Director leads and develops a team with expertise spanning product development, market dynamics, AI-enabled learning, instructional design, and partnerships – managing a portfolio of strategic investments, and building partnerships across education, technology, philanthropy, and research. Working closely with colleagues across the K-12 team, the AI & Data Infrastructure team, the Postsecondary Learning & Navigation team, the Advocacy & System Improvement team, and others, the Deputy Director helps ensure that innovative products evolve into scalable, evidence-based solutions that accelerate student learning and prepare the field for broad adoption of AI-enabled instructional systems. The role requires both the technical credibility to engage product and technology leaders and the people leadership to build trust, develop team capabilities, and lead through a significant evolution in strategy in an environment of rapid change and uncertainty.

What You'll Do

- Lead the foundation's K-12 Solutions strategy and manage a team of investors (program and strategy staff) with portfolios focused on increasing the availability of high-quality AI-enabled instructional products for K-12 mathematics that deliver learning acceleration. Support the continued improvement of that strategy as a reflection of field context, data and evidence on progress and impact, and foundation competitive advantage.

- Build field alignment around quality and performance by advancing common quality signals, implementation frameworks, procurement approaches, and other conditions that enable districts to confidently adopt AI-enabled instructional solutions that drive measurable outcomes for students.

- Identify emerging technologies, market opportunities, and strategic partnerships that validate and strengthen the K-12 AI ecosystem and improve student learning outcomes.

- Collaborate across K-12 Teaching & Learning, Postsecondary Learning & Navigation, AI & Data Infrastructure, and Advocacy & System Improvement to align product development, implementation, policy, and market adoption strategies.
- Continuously attend to contextual, progress, and impact data and evidence to inform tradeoffs for resource allocation and investment management.
