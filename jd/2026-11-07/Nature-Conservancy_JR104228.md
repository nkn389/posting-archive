The Advisor may also contribute to policy and government relations priorities related to grassland conservation and stewardship as this work evolves.&lt;/p&gt;&lt;p&gt; &lt;/p&gt;&lt;p&gt;The Policy Advisor reports to the Director of Government Relations and works closely with the Agriculture and Communications &amp; Marketing teams.
