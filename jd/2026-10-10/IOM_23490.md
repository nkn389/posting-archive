Project Context and Scope
Established in 1951, the International Organization for Migration (IOM) is the leading intergovernmental organisation in the field of migration and is committed to the principle that humane and orderly migration benefits migrants and society. IOM is part of the United Nations system as a related organisation and supports migrants and governments to manage migration in a rights-based manner, with a focus on protecting and saving lives, driving solutions to displacement and facilitating pathways for regular migration.
IOM Ireland implements the Mental Health and Psychosocial Support (MHPSS) Project for International Protection Applicants residing in Ireland. The project provides counselling, case management, cultural mediation, psychosocial support, information and referral assistance, community-based activities, capacity building and follow-up through the MHPSS Infoline.
The project has developed a range of SOPs, guidelines, workflows, forms and operational tools. To strengthen consistency, accountability, safeguarding and service quality, IOM Ireland requires an external consultant to systematically review existing documentation, identify gaps and duplication, harmonise terminology and processes, and develop a practical SOP package for MHPSS project operations.
The objective of the consultancy is to develop a coherent, standardised and implementation-ready package of SOPs that reflects current MHPSS project operations and supports safe, ethical, consistent and accountable service delivery across International Protection Applicant centres.
The consultant will coordinate closely with designated MHPSS focal points while remaining accountable to the Hiring Manager for the timely submission, revision and finalisation of all deliverables. The SOP package will be aligned with relevant IOM standards, policies and principles, including safeguarding, PSEA, protection, accountability to affected populations, data protection and confidentiality requirements.

Tasks to be performed under this contract
Under the direct supervision of the Head of the MHPSS Unit at IOM Ireland, the consultant will:

- Review and revise the current draft and existing Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), guidelines, workflows, templates and tools for the IOM Ireland MHPSS Project, ensuring they define the minimum procedures required for safe, ethical, consistent and accountable service delivery.
- Establish and facilitate consultations with the SOP Working Group, coordinated by the Head of the MHPSS Unit, and collect inputs from designated MHPSS focal points and relevant multidisciplinary team members to ensure that SOPs reflect operational realities, roles, responsibilities and service delivery requirements.
- Map, review and strengthen referral pathways and coordination mechanisms within Citywest and with external service providers, ensuring that referral procedures are practical, context-specific, client-centred and aligned with national systems and available services in Ireland.
- Ensure that SOPs clearly define the minimum actions and responsibilities required of MHPSS staff and focal points, including identification of needs and vulnerabilities, triage, immediate response, safeguarding measures, informed consent, confidentiality, data protection, referrals, follow-up, case documentation, escalation procedures and case closure.
- Coordinate with relevant internal IOM colleagues and external stakeholders, where appropriate, to ensure alignment with IOM policies, protection standards, existing referral mechanisms, interagency coordination processes and operational arrangements in Ireland.
- Validate draft SOPs through consultation with the SOP Working Group and key operational focal points, testing the feasibility, practicality and applicability of procedures within the Citywest context and incorporating feedback as required.
- Compile and present the revised SOP package, including SOPs, referral pathways, workflows, tools and templates, to IOM Ireland management and technical focal points for review, validation and final endorsement.
- Facilitate the finalisation of the SOP package, incorporating consolidated feedback and submitting editable final versions together with implementation recommendations and a practical roll-out plan.
- Develop and deliver capacity-building sessions and practical training workshops for MHPSS staff, focal points and relevant partner organizations on the finalised SOPs, referral pathways, roles and responsibilities, and key procedures to support consistent implementation.
- Provide post-training guidance and recommendations to support the operationalization, monitoring and continuous improvement of the SOPs and referral pathways.

Performance indicators for the evaluation of results

All deliverables are submitted by the agreed dates and accepted in writing by the Hiring Manager, or by a delegated IOM focal point where applicable. The document inventory and gap analysis comprehensively cover the materials made available by IOM. SOPs clearly define purpose, scope, responsibilities, procedures, documentation, escalation and quality-control requirements.
The final package uses consistent terminology, numbering, formatting, cross-references and document-control elements. Procedures are practical, accessible and aligned with current MHPSS operations. Relevant requirements on safeguarding, PSEA, confidentiality, informed consent, data protection, accountability to affected populations and protection principles are appropriately integrated. Stakeholder consultations and validation sessions are completed and documented, and all final documents are supplied in editable Microsoft Word format.
