About the job
The mission of Global Affairs (GA) is to help people around the world by promoting responsible innovation and building trust in the potential of technology. GA operates at the forefront of many of Google's issues and includes the organizations that are responsible for the company’s public policy, legal, trust and safety, compliance, and charitable functions. This team drives key cross-functional initiatives and priorities across GA and Google.
As the Director of Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) Solutions Enablement, you will lead the strategic discipline of identifying, deploying, and supporting integrated GRC solutions. Reporting directly to Senior Director of Solutions Enablement and Business Intelligence, you will be responsible for scaling value-driven methodologies across the organization and ensuring that technical implementations align with legal and regulatory requirements and business goals.
In this role, you will lead a high-performing organization of enablement squads that perform the technical program management, design and delivery of end-to-end GRC solutions, bridging the gap between complex digital transformations and sustainable run-state outcomes.

Responsibilities

- Co-define the GRC strategy and outcomes with GA leadership across Regulatory Affairs, Legal and Risk, Compliance, and Integrity.

- Set the AI-first GRC technology outlook and strategic roadmap with Technical Risk Management (TRM) Product and Engineering teams and secure stakeholder buy-in.

- Lead GRC tech-enablement through all phases of implementation with TRM, owning the requirements and detailed design completion for template objects and ensuring blueprint engagement and traceability.

- Oversee a cohesive portfolio of interoperable GRC technology focused on legal and regulatory compliance and risk management to support GA and Google’s products and services.

- Act as key thought partner to senior executives and collaborate with cross-functional teams.
