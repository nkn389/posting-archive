Introducción
Established in 1951, the International Organization for Migration (IOM) is the leading intergovernmental organization in the field of migration and works closely with governmental, intergovernmental, and non-governmental partners. IOM is dedicated to promoting humane and orderly migration for the benefit of all. It does so by providing services and advice to governments and migrants.
IOM is committed to fostering a workplace where all employees can thrive professionally. Learn more about IOM’s workplace culture on the IOM Workplace Culture webpage.
