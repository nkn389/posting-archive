Purpose of the Consultancy
The consultant will support the WHO Country Office to strengthen donor visibility, health communications, and knowledge management, as well as enhance the CO’s capacity for translation and content adaptation.

Background

Strategic communication for public health is a high priority in the Western Pacific, as recognized in the regional vision, Weaving Health for Families, Communities and Societies in the Western Pacific Region (2025-2029): Working together to improve health, well-being, and save lives. The vision’s “weaving” analogy symbolizes collaboration between WHO, governments, and partners to improve health and well-being in the Region. It consists of five vertical strands of action led by governments, and three horizontal strands of action driven by WHO. The inclusion of communications as one of the three horizontal strands highlights the critical role communication plays in achieving key health outcomes, from expanding access to health services, increasing vaccination rates, reducing stigma around mental health, and addressing the risk factors leading to communicable diseases, among others. Different types of communication mechanisms, such as risk communications, community engagement, community protection, and social and behaviour change are key pillars in the Communication for Health (C4H) approach that support the strengthening of these capacities.
In Cambodia, the Cambodia-WHO Country Cooperation Strategy 2024-2028 aligns with this regional vision by emphasizing integrated, sustainable, and multi-sectoral solutions to advance universal health coverage and strengthen health systems. Strategic communication serves as a vital enabler across all three strategic priorities: advancing quality integrated systems reoriented toward primary health care; improving the health of individuals, families, and communities through action within and beyond the health sector; and strengthening health security systems to increase public health emergency preparedness, response, and resilience. Effective communication ensures that health interventions reach diverse stakeholders, mobilizes communities for health action, enhances visibility of WHO’s partnerships and technical support, and builds public trust in health systems.

Deliverables

Under the direct supervision of the Senior Health Advisor, the Strategic Communications Consultant will work in a hybrid modality, both remotely and in the WHO Cambodia office, with the following outputs and deliverables:
Output/s
Output 1: Develop and implement communications and partner visibility plans for 2026-2027
Deliverable 1.1: Support the development of overall communication plans, including evergreen content, health information, global campaigns, partner visibility and other relevant events.
Deliverable 1.2: Collaborate with technical teams to identify opportunities and develop partner visibility plans for 2026–2027 and support the development of related content.
Deliverable 1.3: Ensure opportunities to raise partner visibility during technical work - workshops, events, seminars, guidelines, protocols, etc. - are taken up and implemented according to partner and WHO branding guidelines.
Output 2: Implement WHO corporate communications to inform the public about global health issues, maintain and enhance WHO Cambodia’s reputation of credibility and reliability, and support policy initiatives and resource mobilization efforts
Deliverable 2.1: Proactively identify opportunities for visibility and lead the development, design, and delivery of content (web stories, social posts, media release, infographics, etc.).
Deliverable 2.2: Conduct interviews, draft, and publish web stories on WHO’s work across Cambodia. Travel within Cambodia may be required.
Deliverable 2.3: Adapt WHO global campaigns to the local context and work with technical teams to organize and support campaign-related events in coordination with the Ministry of Health.
Deliverable 2.4: Manage the WHO Cambodia website, including uploading and publishing health information, web stories, media releases and speeches to ensure all content is accurate and up to date.
Deliverable 2.5: Compile and publish bi-weekly media monitoring updates to stay updated on emerging health trends and issues in Cambodia.
Deliverable 2.6: Maintain and regularly update media contact lists; manage media inquiries in coordination with relevant technical teams; and monitor media coverage and mentions across relevant platforms.
Deliverable 2.7: Actively participate in the UN Communications Group and ensure joint visibility opportunities are taken up and implemented according to WHO Cambodia branding guidelines.
Deliverable 2.8: Ensure all external communications products meeting WHO Cambodia branding guidelines before they are disseminated or sent for country-level approvals.
Output 3: Produce multimedia content aligned with WHO branding and communication standards across platforms.
Deliverable 3.1: Support the creation of multimedia content (photos, videos and graphics) in line with WHO branding and communication standards across platforms.
Deliverable 3.2: Work closely with WHO Cambodia Country Office and the WHO Western Pacific Regional Office to develop social media content packages, including basic graphic design and video editing using Canva.
Deliverable 3.3: Photograph and video-record field activities and subjects as needed, ensuring all required consent forms are obtained and archived in compliance with WHO guidelines.
Deliverable 3.4: Maintain the WHO Multimedia Library by uploading materials, adding captions and ensuring that consent forms are properly linked and stored.
Deliverable 3.5: Ensure that sub-contractors – photographers, videographers, agencies, graphic designers etc. – are adequately briefed when required to deliver on content and products for the country office.
Other Deliverables: Monthly report and end of mission report.
