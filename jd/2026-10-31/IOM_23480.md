Introduction
Established in 1951, IOM is a Related Organization of the United Nations and the leading UN agency in the field of migration. Working closely with governmental, intergovernmental and non-governmental partners, IOM promotes humane and orderly migration for the benefit of all. It saves lives and protects people on the move, drives solutions to displacement, and facilitates pathways for regular migration, while providing services and advice to governments and migrants.
IOM is committed to fostering a respectful, inclusive and supportive workplace where all employees can thrive professionally and feel valued. By creating such an environment, IOM aims to better harness the full potential of migration and strengthen its support to people on the move.
IOM invites candidates from diverse backgrounds to apply and provides reasonable accommodation throughout the recruitment process when required. Learn more about IOM’s workplace culture at IOM workplace culture | International Organization for Migration

Organizational Context and Scope
Under the overall supervision of the Supply Chain Officer -Logistics in Afghanistan and the direct supervision of the senior Logistics Associate, the incumbent will be responsible and accountable for assisting the Asset management activities for IOM Mission in Kabul, Afghanistan measured by the below objectives:

- Support the management of office property, plant, equipment and other organisational assets in accordance with IOM policies, procedures and established asset management standards.

- Maintain an accurate and up-to-date asset register and ensure that all assets are properly identified, tagged, recorded and assigned to the responsible custodian and/or location.

- Coordinate the receipt, inspection, recording and allocation of newly acquired assets, verifying that assets received correspond with approved purchase orders, specifications and delivery documentation.

- Coordinate the transfer, relocation and reassignment of assets between staff members, units and offices, verifying that all movements are properly authorised, documented and reflected in the asset management system.

- Conduct periodic physical verification and inventory counts of assets, reconcile physical assets with system records and investigate and follow up on discrepancies, missing assets and other irregularities.

- Monitor the condition, utilisation and operational status of assets and coordinate with relevant units and service providers for maintenance, repair, replacement and warranty services as required.

- Monitor asset-related insurance coverage, warranties and service agreements and coordinate the timely renewal, reporting of losses or damages and follow-up of insurance claims and other contractual obligations.

- Coordinate the reporting and documentation of lost, stolen, damaged or destroyed assets and ensure that appropriate administrative, investigation and follow-up actions are undertaken in accordance with IOM procedures.

- Support the identification and processing of obsolete, surplus, damaged or uneconomical assets for disposal, including preparation of relevant documentation and coordination of authorised disposal activities.

- Coordinate with Finance and relevant units to reconcile asset records with financial records and ensure that acquisitions, transfers, disposals and other asset transactions are accurately recorded and supported by appropriate documentation.

- Maintain complete physical and electronic asset records and retrieve, analyse and report asset information, including asset holdings, movements, condition, utilisation, losses, maintenance and disposal status.

- Provide briefing, technical guidance and training to staff, asset custodians and junior SCM personnel on asset management procedures, safeguarding and accountability of organisational property, inventory control and reporting requirements.

- Perform other relevant asset management and supply chain duties as may be assigned.
