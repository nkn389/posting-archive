Result of Service
The TNC will contribute to strengthening the CT-GSP support in the Anglophone Caribbean Network fostering and nurturing a network community of countries, enhancing peer learning, and ensuring efficient country-level engagement. Through proactive outreach, regular bilateral meetings, and maintaining up-to-date transparency data, the coordinator will enable timely and effective country support. Additionally, network engagement will facilitate experience-sharing, reducing the learning curve for countries implementing new procedures and best practices. The programme envisages Transparency Networks not only as delivery mechanisms but also as mechanisms for articulating country demand and feeding lessons from national implementation back into global programme activities.

Duties and Responsibilities
The United Nations Environment Programme (UNEP) is the leading global environmental authority that sets the global environmental agenda, promotes the coherent implementation of the environmental dimension of sustainable development within the United Nations system and serves as an authoritative advocate for the global environment. UNEP-CCC is a leading international advisory institution on energy, climate, and sustainable development. Its work focuses on helping developing countries and emerging economies transition to low-carbon development pathways and supporting the integration of climate resilience into national development. UNEP-CCC is actively involved in the implementation of the UN Environment Climate Change Strategy and Energy Programme. The Climate Transparency Global Support Programme (CT-GSP)) supports developing countries in strengthening their capacities to implement the Enhanced Transparency Framework (ETF) under the Paris Agreement. The programme builds on the experience, partnerships and transparency networks established under previous phases of the Global Support Programme. A key pillar of CT-GSP's work will be the strengthening of 10 regional and linguistic transparency networks to foster sustained capacity-building, peer-to-peer learning, knowledge sharing, identification of country needs, and targeted technical support. By connecting global transparency guidance and expertise with national implementation, these networks will facilitate the effective transfer of knowledge while providing a critical feedback loop through which country and regional experiences inform the development of global tools, knowledge products, and programme priorities. The Transparency Network Coordinators (TNCs) are an integral part of the CT-GSP Project Technical Units (PTUs). Each TNC is responsible for one of the transparency networks and serves as the principal operational focal point between the programme, national transparency focal points and practitioners, regional institutions and relevant transparency support partners. The TNC is responsible for ensuring continuous engagement with countries, facilitating peer learning and regional cooperation, coordinating capacity-building and direct country support, organizing regional activities, maintaining regional transparency information, and systematically capturing results, lessons and evidence from implementation. The objective of the assignment is to ensure the effective operation, coordination and delivery of CT-GSP activities within the respective transparency network, with particular emphasis on: 1. Maintaining continuous and substantive engagement with participating countries; 2. Identifying and monitoring country and regional transparency capacity needs; 3. Translating identified needs into regional activities and targeted country support; 4. Providing technical, needs-based support to countries in relevant ETF areas; 5. Facilitating peer-to-peer learning and South–South cooperation; 6. Coordinating regional capacity-building, technical assistance and quality reviews; 7. Strengthening coordination with regional and international transparency support providers; 8. Maintaining accurate and up-to-date regional transparency, stakeholder and support information; and 9. Systematically documenting activities, results, lessons learned and supporting evidence for project management, monitoring and GEF reporting. The TNC shall ensure that network activities are implemented in accordance with the approved CT-GSP workplan, project procedures, technical standards, monitoring framework and relevant communication and knowledge management requirements.
