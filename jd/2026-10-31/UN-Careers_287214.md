Org. Setting and Reporting
The United Nations Secretary-General is responsible for the investment of the assets of the United Nations Joint Staff Pension Fund (UNJSPF). The Secretary-General has delegated this responsibility to the Representative of the Secretary-General for the investment of the assets of the UNJSPF (RSG). The RSG heads the Office of Investment Management (OIM), which manages a $100+ billion multi-asset class, global investment portfolio, about 85% of which is actively managed in-house. Asset classes under management comprise global equities, fixed income, foreign exchange, private equity, real estate, infrastructure, timber and commodities. OIM's staff are all based in New York but come from over 40 countries. For more information, please visit OIM's website at https://oim.unjspf.org/. Following the TPA Feasibility Study, OIM identified a sequenced programme to move the Fund towards a best-practice total portfolio model under Project Blue Print 2030. The One-Portfolio Investment Model Enhancement workstream seeks to strengthen enterprise-wide investment decision-making by improving coordination across investment teams, operations and governance functions; creating common investment language and aligned performance objectives; and establishing integrated governance, reporting and delegated-authority arrangements. This position is located in the Office of Investment Management (OIM) of the United Nations Joint Staff Pension Fund (UNJSPF). OIM is responsible for the investment of the assets of the fund. The position supports organizational development at OIM to facilitate the implementation of Total Portfolio Approach (TPA) best practices at the Fund. The Programme Management Officer reports to the Chief Investment Officer (CIO).

Review and analysis of issues and trends, preparation of evaluations or other research activities and studies. • Generates survey initiatives; designs data collection tools; reviews, analyzes and interprets responses, supports the review and standardization of performance measurement practices, identify problems/issues and prepares conclusions. • Organizes and prepares written outputs, e.g. draft background papers, analysis, sections of reports and studies, governance records, audit-ready documentation and decision trails for project deliverables. • Provides substantive backstopping to consultative and other meetings, conferences, etc., to include proposing agenda topics, identifying participants, preparation of documents and presentations and reporting materials for governance bodies like the Investments Committee and Pension Board. • Initiates and coordinates outreach activities; conducts training workshops, seminars, etc. on investment beliefs, TPA mindset and governance processes; makes presentations on assigned topics/activities. • Leads and/or participates in large, complex missions, including provision of guidance to external consultants, stakeholders and other parties and drafting mission summaries, etc. • Coordinates activities related to budget funding (programme/project preparation and submissions, progress reports, financial statements, etc.) and prepares related documents/reports (pledging, work programme, programme budget, etc.). • Collects and analyzes data to identify trends or patterns and provide insights through graphs, charts, tables and reports using data visualization methods to enable data-driven planning, decision-making, presentation and reporting, including executive reporting on organizational readiness, accountability, delegated authorities and change management. • Performs other duties as required.
