Context and why we are recruiting?

The World Economic Forum, committed to improving the state of the world, is the international organization for public-private cooperation. The Forum engages the foremost political, business and other leaders of society to shape global, regional and industry agendas.

The Forum's Centre for Nature and Climate (CNC) works towards a global transition to a nature-positive economy and healthy, resilient ecosystems. It aims to make nature and climate action a driver of growth and resilience by helping partners identify and scale effective solutions and mobilize collaboration where markets alone cannot deliver the necessary transitions.

The Forest Future Alliance (FFA) is an initiative of the Centre for Nature and Climate that brings together private-sector and philanthropic capital providers alongside for-profit innovators to support investment in forest conservation, restoration, stewardship and resilience. Through its thematic communities, partner-led Solutions Labs and Living Landscapes work, FFA enables collaboration, supports innovation and amplifies credible examples of impact.

We are seeking a Senior Associate, Communications to coordinate and deliver FFA's internal and external communications activities, helping strengthen the visibility of the alliance and communicate its priorities, initiatives and impact.
Working closely with FFA leadership, communications colleagues across the Centre for Nature and Climate and external partners, the Senior Associate will translate agreed communications priorities into engaging content, campaigns and digital communications activities. The role will independently manage defined communications workstreams, coordinate contributions from colleagues and external providers, and ensure timely, consistent and high-quality delivery.

Reporting line and how is this role embedded in the wider team?

The Senior Associate, Communications reports to the Director, Forest Future Alliance, with a matrix reporting line to the Manager, Strategic Communications in the Centre for Nature and Climate.

The role works closely with an Associate supporting FFA communications activities (approximately 50% FTE), particularly social media, newsletters and other digital communications. The Senior Associate coordinates agreed deliverables, reviews content and provides guidance to support effective delivery.

The role collaborates with FFA colleagues and other teams across the Centre for Nature and Climate, including the Tropical Forest Alliance, ACT Ocean and the Earth Systems Agenda, as well as the Forum's Global Communications Group and marketing teams.
Externally, the Senior Associate engages with communications representatives from FFA members and partners, donors, media contacts and communications agencies to coordinate content and communications activities.
