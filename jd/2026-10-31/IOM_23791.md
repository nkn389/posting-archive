Introduction

Working closely with governmental, intergovernmental and non-governmental partners, IOM promotes humane and orderly migration for the benefit of all. It saves lives and protects people on the move, drives solutions to displacement, and facilitates pathways for regular migration, while providing services and advice to governments and migrants.

Project Context and Scope
Under the overall supervision of the Deputy Chief of Mission and the direct supervision of the Project Manager, the Senior Engineer Consultant will serve as the project's lead technical focal point for the planning, design, procurement, implementation and close-out of construction and infrastructure interventions.
The Consultant will provide senior technical advice to project management; lead or coordinate engineering assessments and the preparation and review of technical documentation; oversee contractors and specialist engineers; support technically sound procurement and contract-administration decisions; and ensure that infrastructure interventions comply with approved designs, contractual requirements, applicable legislation in BiH, relevant technical standards, environmental and social requirements, occupational health and safety provisions and approved budgets and schedules.
The Consultant will coordinate technical inputs across relevant disciplines and maintain effective liaison with municipal authorities, utility providers, designers, supervision bodies, contractors and other stakeholders involved in project delivery, ensuring that all project activities are carried out in accordance with applicable technical standards, local regulations, approved project documentation and IOM procedures.

Organizational Department / Unit to which the Consultant is contributing
Solidarity Fund, IOM Bosnia and Herzegovina — Sarajevo

Tasks to be performed under this contract

- Lead technical assessments and feasibility reviews of proposed infrastructure interventions, including site inspections, condition assessments, technical options analysis, constructability reviews, preliminary cost estimates, implementation risks and recommendations to project management.

- Lead and coordinate the preparation, review and consolidation of engineering documentation, including surveys, conceptual and detailed designs, drawings, technical specifications, scopes of work, bills of quantities, cost estimates, implementation schedules, material requirements and photographic records; in accordance with industry standards, local regulations and good engineering practices; consolidate basic and detailed designs into complete bidding and construction packages.

- Review and validate technical deliverables prepared by consultants, designers and specialist engineers, identifying errors, omissions, inconsistencies, constructability concerns and potential cost or schedule implications before documents are approved for procurement or construction.

- Provide senior technical support to procurement and tendering, including preparation of technical procurement documentation, definition of proportionate technical and qualification criteria, participation in site visits and pre-bid meetings, preparation of clarifications, and technical evaluation of bids.

- Oversee construction and rehabilitation works through regular site inspections and review of supervision records, verifying compliance with approved drawings, specifications, bills of quantities, work programmes, permits, material standards, quality requirements and contractual obligations.

- Establish and monitor quality-assurance and quality-control arrangements, including inspection and test plans, material submittals, samples, certificates, testing results, non-conformance reports, corrective actions and as-built documentation.

- Review contractor progress submissions, measurements and supporting technical records, and provide documented technical recommendations concerning completed quantities, milestone achievement and payment certification, without assuming financial approval authority unless formally delegated.

- Assess proposed design changes, substitutions, additional works and variations, confirming technical necessity, reasonableness of quantities and cost implications and consistency with the project scope before submitting recommendations through the appropriate approval process.

- Coordinate technical engagement with authorities and utilities, including matters relating to permits, approvals, connections, inspections, applicable building requirements and acceptance of completed works.

- Perform other related duties as assigned.

- Regular travel to construction sites, project locations and relevant authorities across Bosnia and Herzegovina is required as part of the assignment.

Performance indicators for the evaluation of results

- Timely and accurate preparation, review and submission of technical documentation (assessments, designs, BoQs, bidding packages, supervision records).

- Quality and completeness of engineering deliverables reviewed and validated prior to procurement or construction.

- Effective oversight of construction works with documented site inspection and quality-control records.

- Adherence to approved budgets, schedules, contractual requirements and applicable technical standards.

- Quality and timeliness of technical recommendations on variations, payment, and contract administration matters.

- Effective liaison and coordination with authorities, utilities, contractors and other stakeholders.
