Org. Setting and Reporting
The International Court of Justice is the principal judicial organ of the United Nations. It is composed of 15 judges, elected by the General Assembly and the Security Council. The Court has a twofold role: first, to settle, in accordance with international law, legal disputes submitted to it by States; and, second, to give advisory opinions on legal questions referred to it by duly authorized United Nations organs and specialized agencies. The Court is the only principal organ of the United Nations not serviced by the Secretariat. The Court has its own administration, the Registry, which provides judicial support to the Court and acts as a permanent administrative organ. The Registry is headed by a Registrar, elected by the Court. The Registry of the International Court of Justice is seeking to fill a temporary vacancy for a maximum period of 364 days starting on 2 November 2026. The position is located in the Information and Communications Technologies Division (ICT Division) of the Registry.

Responsibilities
The incumbent reports directly to the Senior Information Technology Assistant. Within delegated authority, the IT Service Management Assistant will be responsible for the following duties: - Contributes to providing end users with first-line support and technical assistance. - Receives and logs service requests in the automated request management system. - Performs quality assurance procedures to ensure client satisfaction; addresses or escalates problems and monitors their resolution. Detects problems from incident patterns and recommends solutions. - Keeps abreast of the latest IT service management standards and technologies; drafts documentation and reports. - Conducts and manages customer satisfaction reviews and analysis, including the preparation and maintenance of the business customer survey questionnaire. Conducts periodic meetings with business units, business partners, IT management, and stores operations. - Understands key stakeholders and patterns of influence within the organization, and targets the right stakeholders at the right time. Maintains detailed knowledge of key business processes and how they are supported by technology. Ensures alignment between the IT strategy and the Court’s practices. Leads project prioritization, particularly through cost-benefit analysis, to proactively manage business demand. - Keeps contract and vendor information up to date in a specialized database. Plans, organizes, directs and/or performs field contracting and/or purchasing activities. Serves as a role model for the team by reinforcing organizational goals and demonstrating respect for others. - Conducts business analysis, outlining problems, opportunities and solutions for the business. Determines operational objectives by studying business functions, gathering information and evaluating output requirements and formats. Creates workflow charts and diagrams, assesses system capabilities and draws up specifications. Improves systems by reviewing current practices and designing modifications. - Recommends controls by identifying problems and drafting improved procedures. Contributes to team efforts by carrying out related tasks as needed. Conducts and co-ordinates financial, product, market, operational and other relevant research to support strategic and business planning within the various departments and programmes of the client group. Interprets, evaluates and correlates research data and develops integrated business analyses and projections for incorporation into strategic decision-making. - Performs daily, weekly and monthly reviews and analyses of current processes using operational metrics and reports. Reviews a variety of areas including operations, purchasing, inventory, distribution and facilities. Understands and communicates the financial and operational impact of any changes. Defines business requirements and communicates them to stakeholders. Ensures on-site IT support. - Acts as an escalation point for problem resolution and prioritization. - Organizes and provides technical training for end users. - Performs other duties as required.

Commitment to continuous learning: Keeps abreast of new developments in own occupation/profession; actively seeks to develop oneself professionally and personally; contributes to the learning of colleagues and subordinates; shows willingness to learn from others; seeks feedback to learn and improve.

Education
High school diploma or equivalent is required. A university degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Software Engineering, or related areas is desirable.

Job - Specific Qualification
ITIL 4 certification is required Knowledge and/or training in agile frameworks and methodologies, and how they are used to define the scope of solutions through functional requirements is desirable.

Work Experience
At least seven (7) years of experience in IT service management or related area is required. The minimum years of relevant experience is reduced to five (5) years for candidates who possess a first-level university degree or higher. Experience with the implementation, optimization and standardization of IT processes (ITIL) is required. Experience in performing a project management role to improve IT processes, procedures, documentation and tooling (based on ITIL) is required. Experience working under pressure (escalations, major incidents), setting priorities and managing time effectively is desirable. Experience in supporting multiple IT solutions (Microsoft knowledge) and devices (laptops, mobiles, video equipment) is desirable.
