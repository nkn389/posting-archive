Unit Context:
The Social Policy (SP) Global Practice delivers operational approaches and evidence-based solutions to help individuals and families manage risk, cope with chronic/transitional poverty, and access better livelihoods and jobs. These include:
• Strategy, analysis, financing and design of social programs delivering social safety nets/social assistance in the form of cash transfers, public works programs, and fee waivers.
• Advisory and analytical services on design and delivery of contributory transfers (including old-age pensions, unemployment insurance, disability and survivor benefits), and their fiscal sustainability (including budget support for transitions).
• Analysis, design and delivery of labor market and youth employment programs (with a focus on supply side interventions and intermediation policies), as well as policy advice on labor market regulations and interventions.
• Strategy, analysis, financing and design of integrated social protection delivery systems (e.g., payments, identification, registries, systems assessments) as underpinnings of effective social programs that can help cope with climatic shocks or underpin fiscal reforms.
• Strategy, analysis, policy dialogue, financing and design of social development and social risk management dimensions of operations considering impacts on vulnerable groups, labor and working conditions, community health and safety, stakeholder engagement, and the prevention of, and response to sexual exploitation, abuse and harassment

SP has a large portfolio in Nigeria and comprises of IDA-funded projects amounting to over US$ 3 billion. There are four major operations currently under supervision, one pipeline project, as well as technical work.

• National Social Safety Nets Program Scale Up (NASSP-SU) project. A US$800 million credit from IDA. The project development objective (PDO) for NASSP-SU is to expand coverage of shock responsive safety net support among the poor and vulnerable and strengthen the national safety net delivery system. The project supports government in meeting the urgent needs of Nigerian households and mitigate shocks while building a shock responsive safety net system that can help adapt to future climate and other shocks. The project currently supports the Nigerian government’s Household Prosperity and Empowerment (HOPE) program that is providing time-bound cash transfers to poor and vulnerable households adversely affected by the macro-economic reforms implemented from May 2023. The NASSP-SU project supports the strengthening of safety-net delivery systems including the Nigeria Social Register and the digital payments system. The project is national in coverage, with beneficiary households selected from all States and the Federal Capital Territory (FCT).
• NIGERIA: Community Action (for) Resilience and Economic Stimulus (NG-CARES). A US$1.25 billion program-for-results, including an Additional Financing of US$500 million approved in March 2025. NG-CARES was initially designed to support households who were affected by the shocks brought on by the COVID-19 pandemic. The program has evolved into a sub-national shock responsive platform that provides multisectoral interventions to the poor and vulnerable. The Program is being implemented at subnational level across all 36 states and the Federal Capital Territory (FCT), stimulating the local economy through social transfers, labor-intensive public works, livelihood grants, basic community services, agriculture and food security interventions. NG-CARES 2.0 is also supporting the expansion and updating of State Social Registry, which aggregates into the Nigeria Social Registry at federal level.
• Nigeria for Women Program Scale-Up (NFWP-SU). NFWP-SU is a US$500 million IDA-financed Investment Project Financing operation approved by the Board on June 22, 2023. Building directly on the six-state Nigeria for Women Project, its development objective is to institutionalize Women Affinity Groups (WAGs) and other platforms for women's economic empowerment and enhance the economic opportunities of unbanked women, taking the WAG model to national scale. The project is organized around three components: Building Community Institutions, which mobilizes and strengthens WAGs and their federated structures; a Livelihood Program, which provides community investment and livelihoods financing alongside training in savings, financial literacy, enterprise planning and value chain participation; and Project Management, Monitoring and Evaluation, and Learning.
• Household Prosperity and Empowerment – Social Protection Project (HOPE-SP, pipeline). This is a pipeline project with the objective to institutionalize regular cash transfer support to extreme poor and vulnerable households in Nigeria, financed increasingly from federal and state budgets and delivered through strengthened state and local government systems. It is scheduled for delivery in FY27.
• Social Protection Programmatic Advisory Services and Analytics (SP-PASA). The SP-PASA seeks to provide support to the Nigerian government through analytics and hands-on technical assistance for building robust SP system. Building on the existing engagements, the goal of the SP-PASA is to help the Government of Nigeria build institutionally and financially sustainable social protection systems to reduce poverty and inequality, enhance opportunities, and build resilience. The SP-PASA has three pillars (i) generate evidence; (ii) support operations and engagements (iii) advance SP policy dialogue.

DUTIES AND ACCOUNTABILITIES

As a core member of the Social Policy team in Nigeria, the Senior Social Protection Specialist will play a key role in advancing the implementation of the World Bank’s current and future operations. The selected candidate will contribute to and lead policy dialogue on and support other operational and ASA tasks that contribute to the Government of Nigeria’s social protection and labor agenda. This will, overtime, entail a combination of operational team leadership, technical assistance and analytical activities, contributing with hands-on, international best practice experience in areas relevant to the country’s social protection systems. The position will require close coordination with the Government, development partners, and other stakeholders.

Major responsibilities would include:

• Leadership / co-leadership of social policy tasks in Nigeria.
• Contribute to and lead aspects of the policy dialogue on Social Policy with key government institutions, including the Federal Ministry of Humanitarian Affairs and Poverty Reduction, Federal Ministry of Budget and Economic Planning, Federal Ministry of Women Affairs, and Project Implementation Units relevant to NASSP-SU, NG-CARES, NFWP-SU, and other operations and others as relevant to technical and policy dialogue.
• Support current and future social policy operational and analytical engagements at the federal and states level in various capacities as team member, co-lead, and lead.
• In coordination with colleagues from Health and Education and working with colleagues from other Verticals and units, support cross-sectoral approaches to promote investments in human capital and social policy in Nigeria.
• Participate and organize missions to provide implementation support, technical inputs and guidance to counterparts, including improving the monitoring and evaluation of project activities. The candidate will also be expected to participate in developing reports and evaluations on World Bank operations as may be relevant.
• Strengthen the World Bank’s services to clients and donor partners by supporting donor coordination and liaising with NGOs and Government counterparts.
• Work closely with the World Bank’s financial management, procurement, environmental and social teams, and assist the government in matters of project implementation.
•Carry out any other related technical assessments (including the preparation of analytical pieces and/or technical reports on various aspects of ongoing and pipeline Bank projects) and administrative activities as guided and required by the Practice Manager and Country Director.
• Work closely with other relevant stakeholders involved in strengthening the social protection systems including UN Agencies, GIZ, Gates Foundation, etc.
• Keep abreast of and provide technical assistance solutions based on international best practice experience in social protection, particularly with regards to adaptive social protection systems, jobs and economic inclusion, and productive social assistance support to vulnerable households.
