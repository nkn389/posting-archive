What we do

Since 1863, the International Committee of the Red Cross (ICRC) has worked to preserve human dignity and relieve suffering caused by war and armed violence.

In collaboration with our Red Cross and Red Crescent partners around the world, we do everything we can to deliver life-saving aid, reconnect families and locate missing people – helping those who need it most, regardless of who they are and what side of the front line they are on.

We engage with authorities and armed forces on all sides, often confidentially, pressing for access to detainees to improve their living conditions and urging compliance with international humanitarian law to protect non-combatants, including from digital threats.

Purpose

The Forensic Data Administrator processes, verifies, censors and consolidates assigned information on missing persons and their families, the circumstances of disappearance, grave sites and unidentified human remains. Using Resolve, Prot6 and related tools, the Administrator applies established procedures to maintain accurate, complete and traceable forensic data that supports search and forensic human identification.

The Forensic Data Administrator contributes to preparing approved records for the accelerated Responsible Handover of Missing Persons Data to the National Commission for the Missing and Forcibly Disappeared (NCMFD). The role focuses on accurate, timely processing, case-level analytical checks, systematic review and adjustment of own work, documented corrections and prompt referral of complex or ambiguous cases, under the priorities, guidance and quality-review arrangements established by the Forensic Data Manager.

Accountabilities & functional responsibilities

- Enters, updates, checks and organizes assigned forensic case information and supporting documents in the designated databases, files and tracking tools.

- Examines and crosschecks Ante Disappearance Data (ADD) questionnaires, attachments and linked records to understand individual case histories, identify discrepancies or missing information.

- Applies approved censorship rules to direct and indirect identifiers in the designated records and document copies, records interventions and escalates ambiguous cases with a clear explanation.

- Consolidates information from designated and approved sources according to agreed procedures, maintains references to the sources used and flags conflicting or unclear entries for reviewer guidance.

- Checks the completeness, consistency, source traceability, relationships and attachments of assigned censored and consolidated files before submitting them for review or acceptance.

- Processes assigned ADD, consent biological reference sample (BRS) and other forensic data updates in related systems accurately and within agreed priorities.

- Applies internal procedures, confidentiality requirements, authorized access arrangements and data protection safeguards throughout the handling of sensitive personal data.

- Prepares basic reports and accurate figures on processed cases, outstanding corrections and workflow progress, and proactively highlights relevant case level findings and recurring discrepancies.

- Maintains secure custody, filing and documented movement of physical files and documents in accordance with applicable procedures.

- Contributes to the protection and security of individuals’ personal data by following applicable rules on document access, storage, handling and sharing.
