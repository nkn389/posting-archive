East Asia and the Pacific (EAP) Region:

EAP is a large and diverse region, ranging from Timor-Leste and the Small Island Developing States of the Pacific to Indonesia, The Philippines, and China. The region has achieved remarkable economic growth and poverty reduction, but development outcomes remain uneven and significant challenges persist. Longer-term growth will depend increasingly on reversing the slowdown in productivity, creating more productive jobs, strengthening institutions and resilience, and adapting to climate change and population aging. At the same time, rapid technological change, including artificial intelligence, is creating new opportunities for trade, investment and productivity, although gaps in connectivity and skills risk limiting their benefits. The World Bank Group has a major role in the region through financing, analytical and advisory services, and support across infrastructure, human development, private sector development, climate and natural resources, and institutional reform. The World Bank Group is seen as a premier development institution in the Region, with a leadership role on analytical and advisory services as well as financing.

East Asia and the Pacific Region: https://www.worldbank.org/ext/en/region/eap

Digital and AI Vertical:

The Digital and AI Vertical was established on July 1, 2024, and is guiding World Bank Group efforts to establish the foundations of a digital economy. Central to the VPU's mandate is the WBG Digital and AI Strategy. The strategy is built around four pillars: Access, Affordability, Ecosystem, and AI Readiness and Adaptation. The Digital and AI Vice Presidency delivers on this agenda through the @A Billion Implementation Plan (FY26-30), anchored in ten scalable solutions spanning connectivity, device affordability, digital public infrastructure, local digital industry development, digital skills, and AI adoption.

Digital and AI Vertical: https://www.worldbank.org/en/topic/digital

Duties and accountabilities:

The Digital and AI EAP Unit is seeking an ET Consultant (Digital Specialist) based in Dili, Timor-Leste to support the Unit’s work program in Timor-Leste.

The ETC will be responsible for:

• Leading and contributing to the preparation and implementation of World Bank advisory, analytical, and operational engagements related to digital transformation, particularly on digital identity and other digital public infrastructure, broadband networks and data centers, digital government, and digital economy.

• Supporting ongoing technical assistance and policy dialogue with government counterparts, including coordination of meetings, workshops, consultations, missions, and follow-up activities.

• Building and maintaining strong working relationships with government agencies, development partners, private sector stakeholders, academia, and civil society organizations involved in digital transformation.

• Conducting analytical work, research, data collection, and assessments to support policy advice, technical assistance, and project preparation activities.

• Preparing technical notes, briefing materials, presentations, reports, aide-mémoires, and other knowledge products for government counterparts and World Bank teams.

• Supporting and coordinating with digital transformation initiatives across sectors, including land administration, health, social protection, financial inclusion and digital financial services, education, agriculture, and public services.

• Monitoring implementation progress, identifying risks and bottlenecks, and supporting problem-solving and coordination among stakeholders.

• Supporting stakeholder engagement and communications, including preparation of briefings, summaries, and knowledge products for internal and external audiences.

• Contributing to internal planning processes, such as work program updates, portfolio reviews, and the preparation of inputs for country strategies (e.g., SCDs, CPFs).

Selection Criteria
