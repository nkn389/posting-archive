Job description

Operations Analyst, NOB, Sarajevo, Bosnia and Herzegovina

Job Description

National Professional Officers (NPOs) must be citizens of the country where the office is located.

The Position:

The Operations Analyst (OA) delivers effective financial, administrative, procurement, and operational processing for the Bosnia and Herzegovina Country Office and provides day-to-day operational coordination and compliance support across the Western Balkans cluster (Bosnia and Herzegovina, Serbia, North Macedonia, Albania, and Kosovo (UNSCR 1244). Under the direct supervision of the Representative and in technical alignment with the Regional International Operations Manager (RIOM), the position ensures adherence to UNFPA operational rules, supports budget tracking, coordinates procurement workflows, and directly supervises local administrative staff in Sarajevo.

How you can make a difference:

UNFPA is the lead United Nations agency for delivering a world where every pregnancy is intended, every childbirth is safe, and every young person's potential is fulfilled. The UNFPA Strategic Plan for 2026-2029 articulates the organization’s response to a complex global environment, providing a roadmap for resilience and renewal. It is designed to accelerate the implementation of the Programme of Action of the International Conference on Population and Development (ICPD) and the achievement of the Sustainable Development Goals by 2030. This mandate is pursued through a focus on four interconnected outcomes: ending the unmet need for family planning; ending preventable maternal deaths; ending gender-based violence and harmful practices; and adapting to demographic change through evidence and rights-based policies.

In a world where fundamental human rights are at risk, we need principled and ethical staff who embody these international norms and standards and will defend them courageously and with full conviction.

UNFPA is seeking candidates who transform, inspire, and deliver high-impact sustained results, ensuring effective external relations, communications, partnership-building and resource mobilization in a rapidly changing development and funding landscape. We need staff who are transparent, exceptional in how they manage the resources entrusted to them, and who commit to delivering excellence in programme results

Job Purpose:

The Operations Analyst ensures fully accountable, well-managed, and results-oriented offices in the Western Balkans. You will contribute your operational expertise to facilitate effective delivery of five UNFPA country programmes, which aim to realize the 2030 Development Agenda in order to make a positive difference in the lives of millions of people. Participating in inter-agency coordination, you will foster a collaborative, client-oriented approach towards UNFPA teams, UNFPA Implementing Partners and the UN community, supporting effective communication and cooperation between Western Balkans offices, with the Regional Office (RO) and Headquarters (HQ).

The Operations Analyst will:

- Under the guidance of the Representative and in consultation with the Regional International Operations Manager, implement and apply established corporate SOPs and standardized workflows to support the effective functioning of the Western Balkans Operations cluster

- In close collaboration with programme teams and management in Western Balkans country cluster, provides operational and analytical input to support effective planning, implementation, tracking, and reporting on country programmes and project results.

- Coordinate day-to-day office operations in Bosnia and Herzegovina and support Heads of Offices in other locations to ensure smooth running of all offices and provision of high quality and timely service delivery by the operations team in a client-oriented manner.

- Identify operational bottlenecks and escalate complex compliance issues to the RIOM.

- Monitor and facilitate execution of financial, administrative, HR, ICT, logistics and travel services, and support procurement and vendor management, asset and inventory management, premises and common services; maximizing efficiency and effectiveness, streamlining and simplifying internal processes.

- Assess operational and staffing requirements for implementation of the country programmes, supporting optimal staffing of the offices and projects through executing timely recruitments, induction and training of personnel.

- Support operational execution of UNFPA activities, monitoring and contribute to building capacity of UNFPA Implementing Partners, conducting assurance activities in line with the HACT framework.

- Process procurement and vendor transactions in line with corporate procedures, ensuring timely provision of goods and services for the CO; securing required clearances and undertaking negotiations and effectively performing contract management processes.

- Directly supervise local operations support staff in the Bosnia and Herzegovina Country Office while exercise matrix technical supervision, task coordination, and functional guidance to operations focal points in Western Balkans cluster.

- Participate in technical-level UN Business Operations Strategy (BOS) working groups and support shared service activities as assigned.

- Maintain a continuous information flow between the COs and the Regional Office and HQ

- Perform other duties as assigned by the supervisor.

Qualifications and Experience:

Education:

Advanced degree in business administration, public administration, finance, accounting, social science or a related discipline.

Knowledge and Experience:

- A minimum of two years of relevant experience in administration, finance, procurement, human or office management is required.

- Progressively responsible experience in operations with the UN is desirable.

- Ability to supervise and lead a team is required. Experience of managing a remote team is an asset.

- Ability to work in a multicultural environment; strong interpersonal, coordination and communication skills are required.

- Ability to build effective relationships is required

- High integrity standards, strong capacity for sound judgement, and capability to work with limited supervision.

- Proficiency in current office software applications, ERP and corporate ICT systems is desirable.

Languages:
