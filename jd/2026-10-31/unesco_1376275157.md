OVERVIEW

UNESCO and UNICEF are supporting the Government of Somalia through the Peacebuilding Fund project Building Peace Through Religious Education in Somalia. The project promotes peacebuilding, social cohesion, inclusive teaching and safeguarding in traditional and Integrated Quranic Schools. The teacher-training component will build on the Peace Curriculum developed with the ulema network, resources from the Ministry of Education, Culture and Higher Education (MoECHE) and the Ministry of Endowments and Islamic Affairs (MEISA), UNESCO's Transformative Pedagogy for Peacebuilding and UNICEF safeguarding expertise.

Long Description

Objective of the consultancy

The consultant will support the preparation and implementation of a needs assessment and focus group discussions, and lead the analysis and reporting of findings. The assignment will identify teachers' capacities, community expectations and practical requirements for the subsequent teacher-training programme. It does not include developing the full training package or delivering Master Trainer training.

ASSIGNMENTS

Scope of work

The consultant will work under the overall authority of the Director of UNESCO Regional Office for Eastern Africa and the direct supervision of the UNESCO Project Manager in Mogadishu, with technical guidance from the Education team of the Regional Office and UNESCO International Institute for Capacity Building in Africa (IICBA) and in coordination with the UNESCO project team, UNICEF and relevant government counterparts.

1. Review existing evidence. Review available Peace Curriculum, MoECHE and MEISA materials, previous consultations, Quranic-school mapping and relevant UNESCO/IICBA/UNICEF resources. Identify existing evidence and gaps to guide the assessment, without undertaking a comprehensive curriculum revision.

Long Description

2. Refine the methodology and tools. Develop and refine the research questions, sampling approach, questionnaires and discussion guides with the project team. Cover teaching practices, peacebuilding, safeguarding, gender and inclusion, community expectations and conditions for training. Ensure clear Somali wording, cultural appropriateness and suitability for analysis.

3. Support testing and data collection. Support pre-testing and revision of the tools; prepare facilitator guidance and brief designated field personnel on the methodology and agreed safeguards, including informed consent, confidentiality, secure data handling, Do No Harm and referral of protection concerns through agreed channels. Provide methodological support during implementation, check coverage and data quality, and document constraints and deviations from the agreed approach. Organization and conduct of focus group discussions in Jubaland and Banadir may be included in the assignment (to be decided in consultation with relevant government authorities).

Long Description

4. Process and analyse findings. Consolidate, clean and code survey data and qualitative records from both locations. Analyse data by school type, location and relevant inclusion dimensions; triangulate with existing evidence and explain limitations. Maintain a documented dataset and coding framework.

5. Report and support the use of findings. Prepare an integrated report, presentation and matrix linking findings to teacher-training requirements. Contribute to the stakeholder consultation, incorporate consolidated feedback and hand over final reports, instruments and data to UNESCO.

Long Description

Working arrangements and safeguards

Planning assumptions are 40 assessment sites and focus group discussions in 12 schools, subject to confirmation. UNESCO will coordinate counterpart engagement, field access, logistics and field facilitation. Direct fieldwork and travel assigned to this consultant must be specified before contracting; separate Jubaland facilitation is not automatically included. The methodology, sample and instruments require UNESCO technical clearance before use. UNICEF will be consulted on safeguarding; any IICBA review will follow the agreed support arrangement.

Apply informed consent, confidentiality, secure data handling and Do No Harm throughout. Ensure voluntary participation and safe opportunities for female teachers and other participants to contribute. Any direct engagement with children requires prior approval, age-appropriate methods and agreed consent, assent and safeguarding arrangements. Refer protection concerns through agreed channels.
