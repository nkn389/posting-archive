Under the overall supervision of the Chief of Mission (CoM), Programme Support Unit Coordinator and the direct supervision of the M&E officer the successful candidate will be responsible for providing technical support and coordinating the implementation monitoring and evaluation activities across different program.

Required Qualifications and Experience
Education

- Bachelor’s degree in Development, Economics, Management and, or related Social or Political Science fields from an accredited academic institution with 5 years of relevant experience; or
- School diploma with seven years of relevant professional experience
Accredited Universities are those listed in the UNESCO World Higher Education Database.
Experience

- Minimum of seven years ( five years for degree holders) relevant experience in monitoring and evaluation of programmes related to migration management, particularly counter human trafficking, return and reintegration, direct assistance to vulnerable migrants including victims of trafficking.
Skills

- Demonstrated experience in monitoring and evaluation – with specific experience in project development, data analysis, knowledge management, monitoring, and evaluation in the non-profit sector;
- Demonstrated proficiency with MS office and advanced data science and/or statistical skills including knowledge of database management, statistical and project management software and data visualization skills is a strong advantage;
- Excellent data visualization, analysis and writing skills
- Proven ability to establish and maintain strong working relations with relevant Government counterparts, international organizations, and private sector entities.

Note: Strong preference for female candidates.
Languages
For all Applicants, fluency in English is required (oral and written).
Working knowledge of Pashto or Dari is required.
Fluency in all national language(s) are highly advantages.
Proficiency of language(s) required will be specifically evaluated during the selection process, which may include written and/or oral assessments.
