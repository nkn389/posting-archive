Introduction
Established in 1951, IOM is a Related Organization of the United Nations and the leading UN agency in the field of migration. Working closely with governmental, intergovernmental and non-governmental partners, IOM promotes humane and orderly migration for the benefit of all. It saves lives and protects people on the move, drives solutions to displacement, and facilitates pathways for regular migration, while providing services and advice to governments and migrants.
IOM is committed to fostering a respectful, inclusive and supportive workplace where all employees can thrive professionally and feel valued. By creating such an environment, IOM aims to better harness the full potential of migration and strengthen its support to people on the move.

Project Context and Scope
The consultancy contributes to the the ‘Awda Amina Phase II: Strengthening Miration Governance in Iraq’ project funded by the European Union and to the ‘Strengthening Data Systems to Improve the Managament of Internally Displaced Persons Referral Systems in Iraq’ project funded by the United Nations Internal Displacement Solutions Fund.
Under the overall guidance of the Head of Sub-Office (Erbil) and the direct supervision of the Programme Officer (DTM and Research), The consultant will provide specialized technical support and policy-development support to IOM staff working on migration and displacement data governance initiatives under the project portfolio. The consultant will draft technical and policy documents under the supervision of the Programme Officer (DTM and Research) and support technical consultations, workshops, framework development, implementation tracking, and reporting activities, identify technical and policy challenges, and provide recommendations for consideration by the Programme Officer (DTM and Research). All managerial, representational, and decision-making functions shall remain the responsibility of IOM staff.

Organizational Department / Unit to which the Consultant is contributing

Tasks to be performed under this contract

Data ownership and access

i. Support the development a structured framework that outlines roles, responsibilities and processes for managing migration and displacement data across government ministries and agencies, in both Federal Iraq and KRI.
ii. Provide technical support and draft clear guidelines for data ownership, access, sharing and utilisation, complying with national policies and promoting adherence with international best practices in terms of data protection.
iii. Under the supervision of the Programme Officer (DTM and Research) and in consultation with relevant IOM staff, the consultant will provide technical input and draft documentation for consideration by IOM in relation to:
i) Memorandums of Understanding (MoUs) between relevant ministries and the Central Statistical Office; and
ii) data-sharing protocols between Federal and KRG authorities aimed at enhancing collaboration and access to migration-related data.
iv. Draft a Data Integration Framework and provide technical recommendations regarding its implementation under the supervision of the Programme Officer (DTM and Research), to establish links with the National Referral Mechanism and enable seamless data flow by connecting the digital infrastructure for migration management.
Data standards

v. Draft a national framework for migration and displacement-related statistical definitions and concepts aligned with international/UN recommendations and standards, integrating inputs from consultations led by IOM staff with relevant national and international partners.
vi. Develop technical data guidelines and standardized indicators to ensure consistency and comparability across different governmental agencies in the operationalization of agreed definitions.
Data analysis and use

vii. Utilize secondary data analysis to inform and enhance policy development and facilitate Iraq's participation in international migration data forums.
viii. Develop recommendations for improved data analysis and reporting on migration and displacement by relevant Ministries, supporting evidence-based policy formulation.
ix. Prepare briefing materials and provide technical support to IOM staff for intra- and inter-ministerial workshops led by IOM staff.Draft recommendations, publication schedules, and other technical documents to facilitate the publishing of periodic statistics on government-owned websites to ensure public availability of migration data.
Other
x. Perform any other duties as may be assigned.
