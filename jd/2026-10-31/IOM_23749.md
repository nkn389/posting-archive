Introduction
Established in 1951, IOM is a Related Organization of the United Nations and the leading UN agency in the field of migration. Working closely with governmental, intergovernmental and non-governmental partners, IOM promotes humane and orderly migration for the benefit of all. It saves lives and protects people on the move, drives solutions to displacement, and facilitates pathways for regular migration, while providing services and advice to governments and migrants.
IOM is committed to fostering a respectful, inclusive and supportive workplace where all employees can thrive professionally and feel valued. By creating such an environment, IOM aims to better harness the full potential of migration and strengthen its support to people on the move.

Organizational Context and Scope
Following the rapid political shift in Damascus on 8 December 2024, Syria is undergoing a critical period of transformation. These developments have further intensified displacement across the country and complicated the dynamics of return, placing increased pressure on already vulnerable communities.
In response to the evolving situation, the International Organization for Migration (IOM) has re-established its presence in Damascus and is rapidly scaling up emergency operations across the country, while maintaining cross-border assistance in northwest Syria. To meet urgent humanitarian needs, this response aims to assist 1.1 million people with life -saving support, including shelter, winterisation, health care, protection services, camp coordination and management, and early recovery assistance.
Under the overall supervision of the Program Officer (Durable Solutions and direct supervision of the National Officer (Livelihoods), the Programme Associate – Livelihoods will be responsible for the implementation of livelihoods project activities supporting the Country Office (CO) and field operations.
