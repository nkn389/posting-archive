Purpose of Job

The Independent Project Accountability Mechanism (IPAM) is the EBRD’s Accountability Mechanism. IPAM independently reviews environmental, social and public disclosure-related complaints raised by project-affected people and civil society organisations concerning Bank projects. IPAM is an independent function, governed outside the Bank’s investment operations, reporting directly to the EBRD Board of Directors via the Audit and Risk Committee. The Project Accountability Policy (PAP) (2019) establishes the mandate, structure and operation of IPAM and regulates the way cases are processed.
The Problem-Solving function, one of the two functions through which IPAM addresses concerns, supports dialogue between Requesters and Clients to resolve the environmental, social and public disclosure issues underlying a Request, without attributing blame or fault. IPAM engages with Project-affected People, Clients, and other stakeholders as a neutral third party, to help find mutually satisfactory resolutions through flexible, consensus-based problem-solving approaches
Within this context, the Principal Problem Solving, reporting directly to the Associate Director Intake and Problem Solving, is responsible for implementing the Policy provisions for cases in the Problem Solving function in line with IPAM standards and best practices. As part of a multidisciplinary, collaborative working environment, the Principal shall contribute to the overall IPAM goals and strategy, supporting the Associate Director, providing inputs to the institutional learning and outreach efforts, as well as development and preparation of internal administrative procedures.

Accountabilities & Responsibilities
Under limited direction of the Associate Director, the Principal, Problem Solving is IPAM’s main dispute resolution expert and responsible for:

• Applying principles, approaches, methods, tools, and general provisions of the Project Accountability Policy, for complex Assessment and Problem Solving cases.
• Leading complex assessment processes and Problem Solving initiatives which include, among others, independently researching the context of the requests; mapping stakeholders and identifying and analysing key issues to be addressed, such as risks of reprisals to requesters.
• Providing expert level contributions to Project analysis and other case-related documents.
• Leading the design of robust Problem Solving processes, defining consensus building approaches, promoting scenario building, developing case-specific terms of reference, resolution options, ground rules and drafting Problem Solving Agreements.
• Conducting efficient, timely and effective project management of cases from start to finish independently, managing case progress, schedules, budgets and stakeholder engagement

• Developing targeted strategies to build rapport, engage with and maintain regular communication with EBRD Management, Clients, public officials, civil society representative and complainants.
• Leading facilitation, dialogue, technical briefings, conversations with experts, and any other process-related events, as needed.
• Identifying, hiring and managing (both technically and contractually) external facilitators and dispute resolution experts.
• Contributing to organization and logistical aspects of field missions, as well as any other process-related activities.
• Monitoring any geo-political and project landscape during assessment, dialogue or monitoring stages.
• Ensuring proper handling of information in accordance with both the PAP and the Access to Information Policy.
• Ensuring consistent use of Bank systems and regularly updating the case registry with relevant information
• Independently drafting robust, high-quality reports (e.g., Assessment Reports, Terms of Reference, Problem Solving Reports, Monitoring Reports, Back to Office Reports and others as needed) which can withstand high levels of internal and external scrutiny.
• Independently monitoring the implementation commitments established through Project Accountability Policy case processes, engaging with all parties to determine when all actions have been satisfactorily implemented.
• Engaging with other IPAM team members and provide expert inputs for Board materials, institutional learning, and outreach. Contributing to the ongoing development of professional practices within IPAM.
• Performing other assignments or tasks as delegated.
