VPU Context:

The WBG Infrastructure Vertical provides public and private solutions to operational teams and clients across the WBG to produce scalable impact. Its mandate is to deliver knowledge for impact to support the WBG to achieve its goals in support of our mission. The Infrastructure VPU’s objectives in driving outcomes include replicating and scaling effective solutions, enhancing thought leadership and innovation, and delivering timely knowledge to client teams. For more information: https://www.worldbank.org/en/topic/infrastructure

Transport and Logistics Global Department

Within the Infrastructure VPU, the WBG Transport and Logistics (T&L) Department seeks to connect people to jobs, social services and income-generating opportunities, as well as to move goods between local, regional, and international markets, using affordable, safer, cleaner and more resilient transport solutions, with a particular focus on private capital mobilization.

The Department is composed of 65 staff and 3 units: (1) Policy and Regulations; (2) Solutions and Impact; and (3) Transport Trust Funds.

Duties and accountabilities:

The WBG Global Director will be accountable for modeling WBG leadership values and managerial behavior and ensuring that the department delivers on its commitments. Key responsibilities include:

Strategic Leadership:

• Provide vision and direction for the T&L department to deliver “thought leadership” (policy and regulations) and “scale and replication” (solutions and impact) for both public and private sector clients.
• Lead new WBG approach to thought leadership and scaling for the Transport and Logistics department, including driving organizational change and the design & roll-out of Solutions.
• Ensure a focus on Corporate Priorities, in particular Private Sector mobilization, Jobs and Sustainable Impact, as well as the delivery of the Economic Corridors strategy under preparation.
• Collaborate with WBG Directors of other sectors, verticals, and, Regions, to deliver multi-sectoral solutions in Transport and Logistics.
• Represent the VPU on WBG corporate strategic issues.

Operational Delivery

• Coordinate pipeline development across Regions, ensuring that the most promising WBG development solutions get scaled and replicated.

Relationship Management

Global Engagement

• Lead global engagement efforts, including participation in global education fora, G7/G20, COP, UNGA, and other advocacy and partnership initiatives.
• Position the WBG as a global thought leader by understanding and influencing major directional trends.
• Nurture the success and relevance of our flagship “Transforming Transportation” event, aiming in particular to boost the participation from the private sector

• Engage with operational counterparts to support a culture of WBG knowledge and delivery of joint public and private sector solutions to clients. Build internal understanding of WBG Transport and Logistics strategy, solutions and accountability framework.

Knowledge Management and Communications

People/Talent Management
