Job description

The Position:

The Democratic Republic of Congo (DRC) is undertaking its second General Population and Housing Census (RGPH). Managing the logistics for a digital census across the DRC—spanning 2.3 million square kilometers and 26 provinces—presents extraordinary operational and supply chain challenges. Hundreds of thousands of items, including mobile devices (tablets), solar chargers, power banks, uniforms, training materials, and IT infrastructure, must be secured, stored, and deployed to thousands of field personnel across urban, riverine, and extremely remote rural areas.

Under the direct supervision of the Census Programme Coordinator and overall guidance of the Census Chief Technical Adviser (CTA), the Logistics Specialist will oversee end-to-end supply chain management, distribution logistics, warehousing, customs clearance, asset tracking, and reverse logistics across all phases of the census (mapping, pilot, and main enumeration).

Job Purpose:

The Logistics Specialist provides technical and operational support to the Bureau Central du Recensement (BCR) and the Institut National de la Statistique (INS) to ensure the timely, secure, cost-effective, and fully accountable movement of census equipment, materials, and personnel across the DRC. The incumbent will design, execute, and monitor the census logistics operational plan, while building the capacity of the Bureau Central du Recensement (BCR) and the Institut National de la Statistique (INS) to handle large-scale, tech-driven fieldwork operations.

You would be responsible for:

1. Supply Chain Planning & Distribution Strategy

- Lead the operational development and follow up - in coordination with INS/BCR - of the Census National Logistics Master Plan, including detailed distribution and retrieval schedules for all 26 provinces.

- Standardize transport workflows across complex multimodal transit routes (air, road, river, and foot) to ensure high-value IT equipment reaches remote field hubs safely and on schedule.

-
Collaborate with the UNFPA Procurement Unit to establish procurement timelines, packaging standards, and delivery terms (INCOTERMS) for all domestic and international census goods.

2. Custom Clearance, Freight & Inventory Management

- Oversee international shipments, managing customs clearance procedures (exonération/attestation de destination) in coordination with DRC government ministries, port authorities, and airport custom units.

- Design and oversee central (Kinshasa) and regional/provincial warehouse operations, ensuring security, climate control, fire safety, and strict inventory controls for sensitive electronic devices.

- Design and implement in collaboration with INS/BCR a robust electronic inventory tracking system (using barcodes/QR codes) to all goods deployed for the census including the tracking of hardware movements from central receiving down to field supervision areas.

3. Asset Control & Reverse Logistics

- Support INS/BCR to implement strict asset management protocols to secure high-value items, including tens of thousands of mobile CAPI tablets, solar kits, and generators.

- Formulate and execute the Reverse Logistics Strategy to collect, inspect, reconcile, and re-warehouse all non-consumable technology assets and equipment post-enumeration.

- Conduct inventory audits, manage damage and loss reporting mechanisms, and oversee proper decommissioning or re-allocation of materials in accordance with UNFPA and government regulations.

4. Operational Coordination & Field Support

- Coordinate field transport logistics by negotiating agreements and operating frameworks with local transport providers, commercial carriers, UN logistics partners, and government fleets.

- Conduct risk assessments on fragile supply lines, establishing contingency protocols for difficult-to-reach or security-compromised territory.

- Provide real-time logistical support during field mapping, pre-tests, pilot census, and main enumeration to immediately resolve local bottlenecks or hardware shortfalls.

5. Capacity Building & Partner Support

- Provide technical assistance and training to logistics personnel at the INS/BCR and provincial census offices (BPR).

- Train national logistics teams on modern inventory tools, field asset tracking, warehouse safety standards, and fleet management protocols.
