United Nations University (UNU)

The United Nations University (UNU) is a global think tank and postgraduate teaching organization within the United Nations System. UNU engages in policy-relevant research, capacity development, and knowledge dissemination in furtherance of the purposes and principles of the United Nations. The work of UNU contributes to solving pressing global problems that are the concern of the United Nations and its Member States.
For the past five decades, UNU has been a source of impartial research on sustainability, human security, and economic development. With hundreds of researchers working at institutes in 12 countries, UNU's expertise spans the full breadth of the 17 Sustainable Development Goals, generating policy-relevant knowledge to effect positive global change. UNU collaborates closely with other UN entities and leading research institutions across the globe. For more information, please visit http://unu.edu
UNU-EHS is one of 13 research and training institutes that comprise the think tanks in the UNU system.

UNU-EHS
The United Nations University’s Institute for Environment and Human Security (UNU-EHS) was established in 2003 in Bonn, Germany. The institute conducts research work focusing on advancing human security and well-being by addressing present and future risks arising from environmental hazards and climate change. Its main areas of work are risk & adaptation and transformation. In addition to its research work, UNU-EHS offers education opportunities at the master’s level and hosts a number of international PhD projects and capacity-development courses on global issues of environmental risks and sustainable development. More information about UNU-EHS can be found here: https://unu.edu/ehs.

Urban Futures and Sustainability Transformation (FAST) Division
The FAST division researches systemic transformation toward sustainability to inform and enhance policy. The division seeks to understand complex realities by researching social, environmental and technological sub-systems, such as infrastructure provision, social security mechanisms and related good governance approaches, with a focus on their interlinkages and dependencies. Thereby the division aims to advance system understanding and identify key leverage points for triggering change. The work is rooted in the understanding that sustainable futures require technological conversion coupled with social and environmental justice. This includes research into what lifestyles are desirable, affordable and just in a changing global environment, which in turn advances conceptual approaches to future well-being. The current core focus is on urban areas, which already see the effects of global change and are among its key drivers at the same time. Based on its own primary and transformative research, FAST will provide science-based advice for inclusive urban policy action.

Well-being and Equity (WE) Division
“Well-being and Equity (WE)” is a pioneering science-to-policy research area of the United Nations University – Institute for Environment and Human Security (UNU-EHS). WE aspires towards a holistic understanding of the preconditions for human and planetary well-being and equity, including by leveraging on different knowledge systems that go beyond current predominant knowledge system. To transform towards a WE society, this Division intends to bring together a broad range of interdisciplinary scientific expertise and diverse knowledge necessary to understand the interface between well-being, sustainability and equity. The division aims to develop holistic data and policy models to weigh all dimensions of well-being, sustainability and equity for decision makers; leverage change towards a sustainable and equitable society focusing on well-being and to develop a compelling narrative for a holistic framework.

Responsibilities
Reporting to the Vice-Rector in Europe (ViE) /Director of UNU-EHS, the Academic Officer, Head of the Urban Futures and Sustainability Transformation (FAST) and interim Head of Well-being & Equity (WE) Divisions is responsible for the following duties:

1. Strategic Programme Leadership and Management

- Contribute to the development and implementation of the strategic direction of the Transformation Department and, in coordination with the Director and relevant colleagues, identify and pursue diversified funding opportunities for the FAST and WE portfolios;

- Provide overall leadership for the Urban Futures and Sustainability Transformation (FAST) Division, ensuring scientific and policy relevance, sound financial and human resources management, and alignment with the strategic objectives of UNU-EHS and UNU;

- Support the development of the Well-being and Equity (WE) Division and establish strategic partnerships to strengthen its networks;

- Acquire new projects and develop new Division-level initiatives to expand UNU-EHS portfolio in the aforementioned two research areas;

- Develop innovative research and convening methodologies, including the use of art and other creative formats.

2. Team Leadership and Capacity Development

- Recruit, lead and manage a diverse, high-performing team, including direct supervision of FAST and WE personnel;

- Provide scientific guidance, mentoring and performance management to personnel at different career stages;

- Contribute to the Joint Master’s programme on “The Geography of Environmental Risks and Human Security” with the University of Bonn;

- Support institutional capacity development by supervising MSc and PhD students and mentoring junior researchers;

- Contribute to the implementation of a UNU-EHS PhD programme with the University of Bonn;

- Foster an inclusive, collaborative, and innovative team culture.

3. Research Excellence and Knowledge Innovation

- Lead and co-author high-quality, peer-reviewed publications and knowledge products in collaboration with other UNU colleagues and international partners;

- Together with institute leadership, and where appropriate, in cooperation with Heads of Divisions in the Risk and Adaptation Department, initiate and develop new research areas aligned with UNU’s strategic priorities and emerging global challenges;

- Ensure that scientific outputs meet international standards and contribute to both academic excellence and policy relevance.

4. Policy Impact, Representation and Outreach

- Represent UNU-EHS, within delegated authority, at high-level international conferences, policy dialogues and strategic forums;

- Translate and communicate research findings for diverse audiences, including policy makers, donors, media and civil society using tailored products such as Op-Eds, policy briefs, interviews, and presentations;

- Strengthen UNU’s visibility and credibility within global sustainability and transformation agendas;

- Serve as the focal point for engagement with international actors and networks relevant to the FAST and WE divisions;

- Serve as the institute’s media spokesperson on FAST and WE related matters, as authorized by the Director and in coordination with the institute’s communications function.

5. Perform other duties as assigned by supervisor·

- Serve as an alternate certifying officer for UNU-EHS at the request of the Director;

- Serve as a management team member, advising the Director on institutional matters and contributing to office task forces or any other related activities;

- Perform other duties as assigned by the supervisor.
