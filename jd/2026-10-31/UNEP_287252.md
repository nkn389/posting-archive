Duties and Responsibilities
The United Nations Environment Programme UNEP is the leading global environmental authority that sets the global environmental agenda, promotes the coherent implementation of the environmental dimension of sustainable development within the United Nations system and serves as an authoritative advocate for the global environment. Its mandate is to coordinate the development of environmental policy consensus by keeping the global environment under review and bringing emerging issues to the attention of governments and the international community for action. UNEP’s Ecosystems Division works with international and national partners, providing technical assistance and capacity development for the implementation of environmental policy, and strengthening the environmental management capacity of developing countries and countries with economies in transition. This consultancy assignment will support the work of the GEF Biodiversity and Land Degradation Unit in the Biodiversity and Land Branch, Ecosystems Division. A.INTRODUCTION AND BACKGROUND The Governments of Lesotho and Malawi, have secured funding from the Global Environment Facility (GEF) through UNEP for biodiversity-related initiatives projects titled: “Strengthening Lake Malawi National Park management and reducing human wildlife conflict to enhance conservation and improve community livelihoods" GEFID 12113” and “Reducing human wildlife conflict and applying integrated management approaches to enhance protected areas management in Matšeng Biosphere Reserve GEFID 12158” These proposed projects have received a Project Preparation Grant (PPG) to prepare a Medium-Sized Project (MSP) document, based on the approved project proposal concept (PIF). The project will be executed by the Ministry of Environment and Forestry (MOEF) and the Malawi Department of National Parks and Wildlife (DNPW) respectfully. The PPG phase is considered as a preparatory phase and will need to support various data collection efforts and undertake validation of the key sections contained in the PIFs. The final output of the PPG phases will be a UNEP GEF project document and a GEF CEO Endorsement Request submitted to and approved by UNEP and GEF, with all required supporting annexes, UNEP's Safeguard Risk Identification Form (SRIF) and background information. In this regard, UNEP would like to recruit a consultant to undertake the assignment as described in this TORs. This consultancy post is located in UNEP / Ecosystems Division / GEF Biodiversity and Land Degradation (BD/LD) unit.
