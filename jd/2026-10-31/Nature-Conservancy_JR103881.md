&lt;h3&gt;&lt;span class="emphasis-2"&gt;&lt;b&gt;&lt;u&gt;What We Can Achieve Together:&lt;/u&gt;&lt;/b&gt;&lt;/span&gt;&lt;/h3&gt;&lt;p&gt;&lt;/p&gt;&lt;p&gt;The Finance Specialist for the Kentucky, Tennessee, and Virginia provides advanced financial management support across a shared services unit (pod), partnering with the Finance Manager, Finance Director, and business unit staff to deliver high-quality financial services. This role supports one or more states or organizational areas within the pod, providing shared finance expertise that flexes to meet business priorities and operational needs. The Finance Specialist serves as a trusted partner by delivering financial analysis, reporting, budgeting, forecasting, and other financial management activities while ensuring compliance with organizational policies, procedures, and best practices.&lt;/p&gt;&lt;p&gt;&lt;/p&gt;&lt;h3&gt;&lt;span class="emphasis-2"&gt;&lt;u&gt;&lt;b&gt;We&#39;re Looking for You:&lt;/b&gt;&lt;/u&gt;&lt;/span&gt;&lt;/h3&gt;&lt;p&gt;&lt;/p&gt;&lt;p&gt;The Finance Specialist provides advanced financial support across assigned states, programs, or business units within a shared services pod. Working collaboratively with the Finance Manager and the Finance Director, the position supports budgeting, forecasting, financial reporting, month- and year-end close activities, financial analysis, and ongoing financial management. The role develops a strong understanding of assigned business operations and provides timely financial guidance to support informed decision-making.&lt;/p&gt;&lt;p&gt;&lt;/p&gt;&lt;p&gt;The Finance Specialist administers financial activities in accordance with TNC policies, procedures, and financial management best practices. The role responds to moderately complex financial questions and serves as a resource to staff on finance processes, systems, and reporting.&lt;/p&gt;&lt;p&gt;&lt;/p&gt;&lt;p&gt;&lt;b&gt;Work
