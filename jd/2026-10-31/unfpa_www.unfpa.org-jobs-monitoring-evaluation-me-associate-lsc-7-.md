Job description

The Position:

Situated within the Country Office M&E function, the M&E Associate directly supports overall monitoring and evaluation operations. The Associate plays a pivotal role in reviewing partner workplans and quarterly progress reports, maintaining monitoring platforms, tracking last-mile supply distributions, supporting evaluation activities, and strengthening partner capacities to ensure evidence-based decision-making across all intervention areas.

How you can make a difference:

UNFPA is the lead United Nations agency for delivering a world where every pregnancy is intended, every childbirth is safe, and every young person's potential is fulfilled. The UNFPA Strategic Plan for 2026-2029 articulates the organization’s response to a complex global environment, providing a roadmap for resilience and renewal. It is designed to accelerate the implementation of the Programme of Action of the International Conference on Population and Development (ICPD) and the achievement of the Sustainable Development Goals by 2030. This mandate is pursued through a focus on four interconnected outcomes: ending the unmet need for family planning; ending preventable maternal deaths; ending gender-based violence and harmful practices; and adapting to demographic change through evidence and rights-based policies.

In a world where fundamental human rights are at risk, we need principled and ethical staff who embody these international norms and standards and will defend them courageously and with full conviction.

UNFPA is seeking candidates who transform, inspire, and deliver high-impact sustained results, ensuring effective external relations, communications, partnership-building and resource mobilization in a rapidly changing development and funding landscape. We need staff who are transparent, exceptional in how they manage the resources entrusted to them, and who commit to delivering excellence in programme results.

Job Purpose:

Under the direct supervision of the M&E Analyst and working closely with the programme team, the M&E Associate is responsible for the following core key functions:

1. Partners’ Workplans and Progress Reports

- Review partners’ annual workplans results planning, ensuring alignment with UNFPA Results and Resources Plan (RRP) milestones and results frameworks and quality assurance criteria.

- Verify and revise partners’ quarterly progress reports, ensuring data verification and alignment with planned results, as well as UNFPA quality assurance criteria.

- Follow up partners’ submission of workplan progress reports on UNFPA Quantum+ reporting system to complete reports and address gaps, as well as provide technical support to complete quarterly reports on the system when needed.

2. Partners’ Monitoring Platforms and Survey management

- Revise indicators on the monthly monitoring platforms to adhere to any annual workplan revision undertaken by the programme team.

- Monthly monitoring and verification of partners’ Excel Monthly Monitoring platforms, ensuring data accuracy and consistency with UNFPA reporting requirements. This also includes following up and supporting the work of UNFPA’s M&E focal points in Gaza in this process.

- Provide technical support to partners for data entry and quality assurance on the monthly monitoring platforms.

- Provide support in survey designs on Kobo, googleforms, etc. as well as adapting localized M&E tools, data collection templates, forms, and surveys suited to the operating realities of the West Bank and Gaza, as well as donor requirements.

3. Monitoring of Supplies Last-Mile Distribution to Beneficiaries

- In close cooperation with the programme team, follow up the last-mile monitoring of supplies distribution (e.g., SRH supplies, dignity kits, postpartum kits, MHM Kits, AY kits etc.) by partners and non-partners.

- Track supplies beneficiary reach and data verification collected from partners and non-partners.

- Conduct focus group discussions with beneficiaries when needed.

- Follow up, sampling and analysis of post distribution monitoring surveys with stakeholders.

4. Capacity Building and Reporting

- Organize orientation and refresher sessions for partners and staff on M&E tools, data collection, and reporting procedures when needed.

- Provide support in preparing quarterly monitoring reports, organizing semi-annual and annual review workshops, and reporting to donors and UNFPA internal reports.

5. Undertake any other relevant tasks assigned by the Representative and supervisor

Institutional Arrangement

The M&E Associate reports directly to the UNFPA Monitoring & Evaluation Analyst. The position works in close coordination with Country Office programme teams,Gaza M&E focal points, and local implementing partners across the occupied Palestinian territory.
