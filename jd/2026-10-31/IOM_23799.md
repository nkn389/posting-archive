Project Context and Scope
IOM and UNHCR in Albania are implementing the EU4Migration in Albania project, which supports the Government of Albania in strengthening effective, efficient and EU-aligned migration and asylum governance systems. Under the project, IOM supports national authorities in improving protection-sensitive reception and assistance arrangements for migrants in vulnerable situations, with a specific focus on unaccompanied and separated children (UASC).
As part of this project, IOM is supporting the establishment and the operationalization of a dedicated center for UASC. This includes technical support related to infrastructure, equipment, protection-sensitive reception standards, standard operating procedures, referral pathways, case management, guardianship procedures, and coordination among relevant institutions. At this stage, additional technical work is needed to help translate these preparations into a clear and workable model for how the UASC Center will be managed, staffed, financed and linked to existing child protection, social care, migration and asylum systems.
The consultancy will therefore be requested to develop a practical model for the operationalization of the Center. The model will look at the legislative, financial, professional and operational aspects that need to be in place for the Center to function effectively. It will also set out realistic options for its management, staff and professional profiles required, guardianship arrangements, including the possible use of professional guardians, service standards, institutional responsibilities, coordination mechanisms and longer-term sustainability. The technical model and options will be discussed with MoHSW and other relevant child protection and social care authorities.

Tasks to be performed under this contract

- Objective of the Consultancy
• Develop a comprehensive and practical model for the operationalization of the Center for UASC, covering legislative, financial, professional and operational dimensions.
• Prepare clear options for UASC Center management, staffing, guardianship arrangements and inter-institutional coordination, to support structured discussions with MoIA and MoHSW.
- The local consultant / technical expert will be responsible to:
• Review the main legal, policy and procedural documents related to child protection, social care services, migration and asylum governance, UASC protection, guardianship, reception arrangements, licensing, service standards and institutional mandates.
• Carry out consultations with relevant central institutions and stakeholders, including the Ministry of Internal Affairs (MoIA), the Ministry of Health and Social Welfare (MoHSW), the State Agency for Child Rights and Protection (SACRP), social care and child protection structures, and other relevant actors, as agreed with IOM.
• Map institutional roles and responsibilities: Clarify the roles and responsibilities of the institutions and service providers that would be involved in the establishment and day-to-day functioning of the UASC Center, including MoIA, MoHSP, SACRP, State Social Service, municipalities, Child Protection Units and possible non-public or civil society providers.
• Identify the legal and administrative steps needed for the UASC Center to become operational, including possible amendments, secondary legislation, ministerial orders, cooperation agreements, internal regulations, licensing requirements, referral procedures or operational guidance.
• Develop practical options for the UASC Center’s management model, including state-led, delegated, mixed or partnership-based arrangements, with a clear explanation of their feasibility, advantages, limitations, accountability lines and sustainability implications.
• Propose the professional model needed for the UASC Center, including the staffing structure, required profiles, qualifications, roles and responsibilities, minimum service standards, safeguarding requirements, case management functions, training needs and supervision arrangements.
• Assess guardianship options for UASC, including professional guardians, legal representation, safeguards, and the roles of Child Protection Units, SACRP and other competent authorities, in line with Law No. 92/2025, relevant bylaws and the child protection, migration and asylum framework, and the best interests of the child.
• Prepare a realistic financial model for the UASC Center’s functioning, including key recurrent cost categories, staffing and service costs, maintenance and running costs, possible state budget implications, temporary project-supported costs and sustainability considerations.
• Consult relevant institutions and stakeholders to test the proposed options, confirm what is feasible in practice and collect technical input for the final model.
• Prepare a document with technical options and a presentation package that can be used to guide discussions with MoIA, MoHSW and other relevant stakeholders.
• Revise and finalize the model based on counterparts’ feedback, making sure the final product is practical, coherent and ready to be shared with MoIA.
