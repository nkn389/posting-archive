CONTEXT AND BACKGROUND

The World Food Programme (WFP) Legal Office (LEG) provides legal advice and support to Headquarters, Regional Offices and Country Offices on all aspects of WFP operations, helping ensure that activities are conducted in accordance with the applicable legal framework and that legal risks are appropriately identified and managed. LEG supports WFP's global humanitarian mandate through the provision of practical, solution-oriented legal services across a broad spectrum of institutional, operational and corporate matters. The Legal Office is composed of three specialized branches: the Administrative and Employment Law Branch (LEGA), the Contractual and Constitutional Law Branch (LEGC), and the Maritime, Transport and Insurance Law Branch (LEGM).

The Administrative and Employment Law Branch (LEGA) provides advice to management on all aspects of the employment relationship between WFP and its employees, including the development, interpretation and application of WFP's employment framework, appropriate contractual modalities and conditions of service, conduct and discipline matters, contentious employment matters, claims and dispute settlement, and privileges and immunities of WFP personnel.

The Contractual and Constitutional Law Branch (LEGC) provides legal advice on governance and constitutional matters, including the interpretation and application of WFP's constitutional and regulatory framework. LEGC also advises on public international law, privileges and immunities, Basic Agreements, partnerships with governments, United Nations entities, non-governmental organizations and private-sector partners, fundraising, data protection, information technology, intellectual property, treasury and finance operations, cash-based transfer programmes, innovation initiatives and related dispute resolution proceedings.

The Maritime, Transport and Insurance Law Branch (LEGM) provides legal support to WFP's global supply chain and common services operations. Its work includes legal advice on procurement, logistics, shipping, aviation, overland transport, construction, engineering, leasing, real estate and insurance matters. LEGM also supports the management of claims, commodity losses and operational disputes, provides legal risk mitigation advice, and represents WFP in dispute resolution proceedings relating to matters within its area of responsibility.

The Legal Consultant will be assigned to the Contractual and Constitutional Law Branch (LEGC). The role will be diverse and will involve providing legal advice and working on a variety of matters covered by LEGC, including related to private-sector partnerships.

The Legal Consultant may, however, work on assignments in other branches and areas of activity within LEG, based on business needs and expressed personal interests.

KEY ACCOUNTABILITIES (not all-inclusive)
Under the general supervision of a Legal Officer, the Legal Consultant will be responsible for the following key duties:

- Undertake extensive legal research and analysis; handle a range of multi-disciplinary complex legal matters, including interpretation and application of various legal instruments, to facilitate the provision of quality legal services.
- Review legal documents, identify and analyze important issues, presenting findings and recommendations for internal review to contribute to the decision-making process.
- Prepare or assist in the preparation of legal opinions or advice on a wide range of legal issues, background papers, studies, memoranda, reports and other legal documents.
- Negotiate and prepare agreements and contracts with public and private entities including governments, other UN organisations, non-governmental organizations and private sector partners, financial institutions, digital payment providers and other commercial entities, and assisting with contractual interpretation and disputes to meet unique client needs.
- Identify potential liabilities, particularly vis-à-vis third parties, and assist in the management of risks in WFP’s activities to facilitate well-informed decisions.
- Prepare and assess briefs of evidence to contribute to legal analysis and solutions.
- Service and/or assist senior legal officers in servicing committees, task forces, expert working groups and other bodies with legal advice and preparation/review of legal documents to contribute to the formulation of WFP’s position.
- Take responsibility for incorporating gender perspectives in all areas of work; and
- Other, as required.
