Result of Service
The ultimate result of the service will be strengthened results-based management, monitoring, reporting and evaluation across the Source-to-Sea Pollution Unit (SSPU), including the Wastewater Surveillance Initiative for Africa (WWS4Africa), through reliable performance data, systematic tracking of results and timely, evidence-based reporting. The Individual Contractor will enable SSPU, WWS4Africa and Marine and Freshwater Branch management to better demonstrate progress, results and impact, identify implementation challenges and opportunities for improvement, meet UNEP and donor accountability requirements, and use monitoring evidence to inform programme planning, decision-making, institutional learning and the effective delivery and scale-up of the Unit’s work.

Duties and Responsibilities
The United Nations Environment Programme (UNEP) is the leading global environmental authority that sets the global environmental agenda, promotes the coherent implementation of the environmental dimension of sustainable development within the United Nations system and serves as an authoritative advocate for the global environment. Its mandate is to coordinate the development of environmental policy consensus by keeping the global environment under review and bringing emerging issues to the attention of government and the international community for action. UNEP’s Ecosystem Division drives conservation, restoration and sustainable use of ecosystems to achieve benefits for nature and people: today and future generations. The division works with Member States and partners to strengthen resilience by providing policy and technical support to catalyze sustainable social and economic transformation for biodiversity, people and peace. This role is required in the Ecosystem Division to support the Source to Sea Pollution Unit to maintain project monitoring and reporting systems, consolidate and analyze implementation data and evidence, prepare timely and high-quality reports, and document results, lessons learned and good practices of the Unit, particularly for activities under the Wastewater Surveillance Initiative for Africa (WWS4Africa), funded by the European Union's Health Emergency Preparedness and Response Authority (HERA). Strengthen the collection, management and analysis of performance data and evidence across SSPU activities and WWS4Africa, working with country counterparts, regional offices, Regional Seas Conventions and Action Plans, implementing partners and other stakeholders to ensure data quality, consistency and completeness. c. Coordinate and deliver timely, high-quality reporting for SSPU projects and activities, including WWS4Africa, through IPMR updates, donor and UNEA reports, corporate and thematic reporting, progress reports, briefing notes and other reporting requirements for UNEP leadership. d. Strengthen monitoring and reporting capacity and coordination among SSPU staff and implementing partners by providing technical guidance, tools, templates and support, while facilitating harmonized monitoring and reporting practices across the Marine and Freshwater Branch. f.
