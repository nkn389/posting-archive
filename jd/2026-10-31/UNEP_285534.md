Org. Setting and Reporting
The United Nations Environment Programme (UN Environment) is the leading global environmental authority that sets the global environmental agenda, promotes the coherent implementation of the environmental dimension of sustainable development within the United Nations system and serves as an authoritative advocate for the global environment. The overall objective of UNEP’s Industry and Economy Division is to encourage decision makers in government, local authorities and industry to develop and adopt policies, strategies and practices and technologies that promote sustainable patterns of consumption and production, make efficient use of natural resources, ensure safe management of chemicals and contribute to making trade and environment policies mutually supportive. As part of the Industry and Economy Division, the International Resource Panel (IRP) was launched by UNEP in 2007 to build and share the knowledge needed to improve the use of natural resources worldwide. The IRP aims to provide decision makers and other interested parties with independent and authoritative scientific assessments of policy relevance on the sustainable use of natural resources and in particular the environmental impacts over the full life cycle. The IRP is located within the Materials and Systems Innovation Unit, which brings together data, science, and knowledge networks in the area of sustainable materials management, circular economy and life cycle thinking to support governments and engage industry and finance actors to develop relevant policy and practices. This position is located in the International Resource Panel secretariat, within the Materials and Systems Innovation unit of the Industry Innovation Branch, Industry and Economy Division, at the Paris duty station. The Programme Officer reports to the senior Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) advisor under the overall supervision of Head of the Materials and Systems Innovation Unit and has an additional reporting line to the Programme Management Officer (Statistician) in the Office of Science. The incumbent will support the delivery of the IRP’s work programme. Within delegated authority, the Programme Officer will be responsible for the following duties:

Responsibilities
• Organizes, designs, plans and carries out the collection, estimation, evaluation, analysis, compilation and dissemination of statistical data on Economy-Wide Material Flow Accounts (EW-MFA) and other relevant data by selecting methods of data collection, selecting and implementing methods for checking collected data, and selecting and implementing appropriate methods for data processing for incorporation into databases. • Improves existing and develops new statistical quantitative and qualitative models, and projections to support EW-MFA or other relevant analysis. Participates in the management of EW-MFA database and other statistical database(s). • Provides training and technical support to National Statistical Offices, Ministries of Environment and other national stakeholders on data collection programmes, country practices and other information related to EW-MFA . • Provides feedback and advice to National Statistical Offices, Ministries of Environment and other stakeholders at the national, regional and global levels on data published in IRP’s Global Material Flows Database (GMFD), Sustainable Consumption and Production Hotspot Analysis Tool (SCP-HAT) and data obtained through the Global Footprint Tool. • Prepares various written outputs, e.g. draft background papers, analysis, sections of reports and studies, inputs to publications. • Participates in the development or revision of standards on statistical concepts, definitions, and classifications by performing methodological research on sustainable use of materials and environmental impacts throughout the full life cycle. • Organizes and participates in seminars, working groups and expert meetings as a technical resource person. • Undertakes outreach activities on EW-MFA and other relevant data and tools. • Guides, trains and supervises junior staff. • Coordinates activities related to budget and funding (programme/project preparation and submissions, progress reports, financial statements) and prepares related documents/reports (pledging, work programme, programme budget). • Collects and analyzes data to identify trends or patterns and provide insights through graphs, charts, tables and reports using data visualization methods to enable data-driven planning, decision-making, presentation and reporting. • Performs other duties as required.
