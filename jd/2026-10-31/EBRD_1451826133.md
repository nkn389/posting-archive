Purpose of Job

Under the guidance of the Principal Manager, the Associate will lead and contribute to the implementation and growth of Star Venture in Türkiye. This includes sourcing and selecting startups, delivering tailored advisory and ecosystem-building activities, and building links with investors, accelerators and other ecosystem stakeholders. The Associate will also contribute to wider Advice for Small Businesses activities as needed, including the initiation, structuring, execution, monitoring and completion of advisory projects and non-project activities.

EBRD Star Venture is the Bank’s flagship venture-building programme, designed to support and scale high-potential startups in emerging markets. Operating in over 30 countries, Star Venture provides tailored, hands-on support to early-stage companies through a combination of advisory services, international mentorship, corporate partnerships, blended finance instruments, and market access initiatives. In parallel, the programme works closely with local accelerators, investors, corporates, and other stakeholders to strengthen startup ecosystems and improve access to finance and growth opportunities for innovative firms. To date, Star Venture has directly supported more than 400 startups that have collectively raised over EUR 800 million in commercial investment.

The Associate will proactively build a high-quality pipeline of startups and advisory projects, undertake due diligence including alignment with Star Venture and SME F&D strategic priorities, and coordinate stakeholder outreach and other non-financial services. They will work directly with founders of high-growth-potential startups to identify their business, advisory and financing needs and support their investment readiness, fundraising and growth.

The Associate will coordinate closely with banking, policy and sector teams in EBRD Resident Offices and HQ and, where relevant and under the supervision of the Principal Manager, liaise with external partners, stakeholders and donors.

Accountabilities & Responsibilities

Under the Principal Manager’s supervision and, where applicable, supporting the Regional Associate Director, the Associate will deliver Star Venture in Türkiye and other delegated ASB activities, aligned with Bank and SME F&D priorities and coordinated with relevant Equity, Corporate, Financial Institutions and Policy teams.

- Structuring and Execution

- Ensure all Programme activities comply with Bank policies, procedures, donor requirements, internal controls and compliance standards, upholding integrity and ethical conduct and working with credible counterparts.

- Manage Star Venture programme’s day-to-day delivery and growth in Türkiye and the design, implementation, monitoring and evaluation of advisory activities against agreed objectives, operational plans, quality standards and donor commitments. Maintain sourcing and referral channels and screen start-ups for selection, eligibility and integrity.

- Work with banking colleagues to identify and structure suitable advisory support for high-growth SMEs and start-ups.

- Assess clients’ financials and growth prospects to tailor advisory support and prepare them for investment and financing.

- In close collaboration with the Principal Manager, lead business development to build a high-quality client and advisory pipeline in priority areas.

- Coordinate with the Principal Manager and external partners to identify SME and entrepreneurship needs and design initiatives. Partners include venture capital and angel networks, financial institutions, government bodies, business associations, accelerators, incubators, corporates, universities and other support organisations.

- Portfolio Monitoring, Value Creation and Reporting

- Strengthen links between advisory and banking activities, including Star Venture and other financial and non-financial initiatives.

- Monitor supported businesses’ performance, growth and investment readiness, identifying follow-on support and financing opportunities.

- Contribute to banking project approval documents and implementation as required.

- Track market, start-up ecosystem and venture capital trends in strategic priority areas to develop innovative financial and non-financial products for SMEs and start-ups.

- Prepare internal and donor reports with HQ on progress, KPIs and funding utilisation, and contribute to new donor proposals.

- Where relevant, deliver marketing and visibility activities promoting ASB and its donors in Türkiye, including start-up success stories.
