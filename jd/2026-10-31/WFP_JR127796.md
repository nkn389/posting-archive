Aviation Service Consultancy roster for Short-Term assignments

Purpose of this roster

The WFP Aviation Service is establishing this roster to support short-term assignments as operational requirements arise on a regular basis.
These assignments may vary in duration and are intended to address temporary staffing needs across a broad range of aviation‑related functions. Candidates placed on the roster may be deployed to fulfil different roles and responsibilities depending on operational needs, and in certain cases may also be requested to act as Chief Air Transport Officers or Aviation Safety Officers to ensure continuity of leadership and oversight in critical duty stations.
By establishing this roster, the Aviation Service will be better positioned to maintain a pool of qualified candidates and enhance workforce planning and profiling.

We invite candidates with relevant aviation profiles (as listed below) to submit an Expression of Interest. Candidates should be willing and available to undertake temporary assignments in support of the Aviation Service's activities and or the Aviation Safety unit, either remotely, at Headquarter, or where required, in countries where WFP operates, including hardship duty stations.
Remote assignments may be possible depending on operational requirements and business needs.

Profiles needed

Ideal candidates will possess experience in one or more areas of aviation operations, management, aviation safety and risk management, quality assurance, regulatory compliance, or aviation support services. Experience in humanitarian aviation and the application of aviation safety management principles would be considered an asset.

Experience gained across different operational environments, including field-based assignments, Headquarters functions, and emergency response contexts, is highly desirable. Applications from both experienced and early-career professionals are encouraged.
We invite candidates to apply to the roster who have expertise in any of the following areas:

- Air Operations
- Aviation Quality Assurance
- Aviation Safety and Risk Management
- Aviation Security
- Aircraft procurement and contract management
- Aviation Data Analytics, Planning, Optimization and Management

Candidates may demonstrate expertise in operational, analytical, assurance, compliance, or coordination functions within the aviation sector.

General requirements:

Work experience

For Junior consultancy assignments: From less than 1 year of experience to maximum 3 years of postgraduate relevant work experience in the area of Aviation and Air Operations.
For level 1 consultancy assignments: Between 3 to 5 years of postgraduate relevant work experience in the area of Aviation and Air Operations
For level 2 consultancy assignments: Between 5 to 10 years of postgraduate relevant work experience in the area of Aviation and Air Operations
Please note that the level of the consultancy assignment will be defined by the complexity of the Terms of Reference and not based on the candidate’s experience.

Education

Advanced University degree preferably Aviation Management & Operations (Airline Financial Management, Airport Planning & Design, Corporate Aviation Operations, Aviation Security Law), Aeronautical Sciences, Aviation Safety, Air Transport, Data Analytics, Business Intelligence, Quality Management, Aeronautical/Aerospace Engineering, Operations Management, Aviation Business Administration, Economics, Statistics, Information Management, risk management, or other related field, or First University degree with additional years of related work experience and/or training/courses.

Certification and Licenses

The following additional certifications or licenses are desirable:

- Commercial Pilot License (CPL)
- Airline Transport Pilot License
- Air Traffic Control / Air Traffic Controller (ATC) certification
- Air Dispatch / Flight Dispatch

For candidates who want to be considered for Aviation Quality Assurance Positions: IATA IOSA Certification is an advantage.
For candidates who want to be considered for Aviation Security Positions: ICAO Aviation Security Professional Management Certification is mandatory.
For candidates who want to be considered for Aviation Safety Positions: Professional qualifications or formal training in Aviation Safety Management Systems (SMS), aviation risk management, accident and incident investigation, aviation safety auditing, or ICAO aviation safety programmes are highly desirable. Possession of a recognized aviation safety certification would be considered a strong advantage.

Language

Fluency (level C) in English language is required. Intermediate knowledge (level B) of a second official UN language is highly desirable: Arabic, Chinese, French, Russian, Spanish, and/or WFP’s working language, Portuguese.
