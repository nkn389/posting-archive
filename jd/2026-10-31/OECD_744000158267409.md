Company Description

The Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD) is an international organisation comprised of 38 member countries, that works to build better policies for better lives. Our mission is to promote policies that will improve the economic and social well-being of people around the world. Together with governments, policy makers and citizens, we work on establishing evidence-based international standards, and finding solutions to a range of social, economic and environmental challenges. From improving economic performance and creating jobs to fostering strong education and fighting international tax evasion, we provide a unique forum and knowledge hub for data and analysis, exchange of experiences, best-practice sharing, and advice on public policies and international standard-setting.
The International Energy Agency (IEA) is an intergovernmental organisation committed to advancing security of energy supply, economic growth and environmental sustainability through energy policy co-operation. The IEA operates as an autonomous body within the framework of the OECD. Both the IEA and the OECD seek opportunities to work with an increasing number of non-members of growing importance to the world economy. The Agency aims to foster the contribution of the energy sector to economic development and environmental sustainability.
Mission Innovation is a global, member-driven intergovernmental platform dedicated to accelerating clean energy innovation through voluntary collaboration among governments, international organisations and industry. Launched at COP21 in Paris in 2015, MI brings together 22 countries and the European Commission, representing the European Union, with the shared objective of advancing research, development and demonstration to make clean energy solutions affordable, attractive and accessible worldwide.
The OECD and the IEA are jointly recruiting a Programme Manager for the Mission Innovation (MI) Secretariat. Based at the IEA and reporting to the Head of the MI Secretariat, the Programme Manager will play a key role in supporting the development, coordination and delivery of Mission Innovation's programme portfolio, including its Innovation Communities, Missions, Funders Dialogue and cross-cutting initiatives.
The Programme Manager will work closely with MI members, workstream leadership teams, partner organisations and Secretariat colleagues to strengthen collaboration, support implementation of work programmes and contribute to the continued evolution of Mission Innovation as a global platform for clean energy innovation.

Job Description

Coordination of MI’s Programme Portfolio

- Coordinate and support the implementation of Mission Innovation's workstreams, including Innovation Communities, Missions, the Funders Dialogue and other member-led initiatives.
- Serve as a focal point for assigned workstreams, supporting governance processes, work planning, member engagement and delivery of agreed activities.
- Facilitate collaboration and knowledge exchange across workstreams, identifying opportunities for synergies, joint activities and cross-cutting outputs.
- Support the development, monitoring and reporting of workstream objectives, deliverables and impact.
- Draft briefing materials, reports, presentations and strategic papers for Secretariat leadership, members and external stakeholders.
- Synthesize technical, policy and programme information from across the MI community into clear and accessible materials for diverse audiences.
- Provide organisational and content support for meetings, workshops, consultations and expert exchanges relating to energy innovation policy, technology development and innovation ecosystems.
- Contribute to the design and implementation of new initiatives and programme activities in response to member priorities and evolving global energy innovation challenges.
Stakeholder Engagement and Outreach

- Support engagement with existing and prospective Mission Innovation members, helping to strengthen participation and collaboration across the MI community.
- Develop and implement outreach and stakeholder engagement activities that raise awareness of Mission Innovation's work and impact.
- Build and maintain productive relationships with colleagues in the IEA, governments, international organisations, research institutions, industry representatives, philanthropic organisations and other key stakeholders.
- Support dissemination strategies and communications activities that promote workstream outputs, events and policy insights.
- Contribute to the development of strategic partnerships that enhance the effectiveness and reach of Mission Innovation initiatives.
Secretariat and Strategic Support

- Work closely with Secretariat colleagues to support the planning and delivery of major Mission Innovation meetings and events, including the Ministerial and Annual Gathering.
- Contribute to broader Secretariat activities through written analysis, policy briefings, presentations and strategic advice.
- Collaborate closely with the IEA, OECD and other partner organisations to ensure alignment and maximise the value of Mission Innovation activities.
