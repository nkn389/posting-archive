Introduction
Established in 1951, IOM is a Related Organization of the United Nations and the leading UN agency in the field of migration. Working closely with governmental, intergovernmental and non-governmental partners, IOM promotes humane and orderly migration for the benefit of all. It saves lives and protects people on the move, drives solutions to displacement, and facilitates pathways for regular migration, while providing services and advice to governments and migrants.
IOM is committed to fostering a respectful, inclusive and supportive workplace where all employees can thrive professionally and feel valued. By creating such an environment, IOM aims to better harness the full potential of migration and strengthen its support to people on the move.
IOM invites candidates from diverse backgrounds to apply and provides reasonable accommodation throughout the recruitment process when required. Learn more about IOM’s workplace culture at IOM workplace culture | International Organization for Migration

Project Context and Scope:
Under the overall supervision of the Senior Programme Coordinator in Amman and direct supervision of the Programme Coordinator, Needs and Population Monitoring (NPM) and, in collaboration with relevant units the Information Management (IM) and Geographic Information System (GIS) Consultant will be mainly responsible and accountable for developing GIS systems, data collection tools, databases and statistical reports and maps.

Organizational Department / Unit to which the Consultant is contributing is Needs and Population Monitoring

Required Qualifications and Experience
Education

- Bachelor’s degree in computer science, Information Management and related engineering programmes or related fields from an accredited academic institution with three years of relevant professional experience in GIS and mapping systems., or

- High School diploma with five years of relevant professional experience

Experience

- Experience in supporting the management and coordination of information flows, data management including collection, storing, processing, and analyzing data to generate information products.

- Knowledge of the latest technological developments in information technology and information system;

- Prior work experience with international humanitarian organizations, non-government or government institutions/organization in a multi-cultural setting is an advantage.

- Computer knowledge use including SQL database, Microsoft Office, QGIS, ArcGIS

Skills

- Proven skills to analyze statistical information

- Demonstrated information management skills and understanding of different data collection methodologies; and, knowledge of KoboCollect applications.

Tasks to be performed under this contract

- Assist in the implementation and routine monitoring of project activities in line with approved workplans and under the supervision of senior staff.

- Support the maintenance, updating, and routine operation of programme databases and data collection systems.

- Contribute to the implementation of established data gathering and information management mechanisms to ensure timely, accurate, and reliable data collection, storage, and reporting.

- Support the monitoring of data collection processes, including data encoding, validation, storage, and transfer to IOM data repositories and data warehouses, in accordance with established procedures.

- Conduct routine data processing, cleaning, and basic analysis of collected data to support programme reporting and operational decision-making.

- Assist programme staff in the adaptation and use of data collection tools, databases, and standard statistical outputs, including tables, charts, and maps.

- Support the routine use and operation of existing GIS systems to produce maps and spatial outputs based on approved datasets and methodologies.

- Perform data visualization and mapping using GIS software and other approved data visualization platforms, following established standards and templates.

- Promote the consistent application of GIS coding and geographic referencing in data collection activities, in coordination with senior IM/GIS staff.

- Provide user support to programme staff and implementing partners on the use of IM and GIS tools, including contributing to user guides, training materials, and basic training sessions under supervision.

- Assist in providing guidance and hands-on support to colleagues within the unit on standard IM tools and workflows, as required.

- Carry out data entry, verification, and updates when necessary to ensure completeness and accuracy of programme datasets.

- Perform other related duties as assigned.

Performance indicators for the evaluation of results

- Timely maintenance and updating of programme databases, data collection tools, and information management systems.

- Accuracy, completeness, and reliability of datasets through regular data entry, validation, cleaning, and quality control.

- Timely production of statistical reports, data visualizations, maps, and GIS outputs in accordance with established standards and requirements.

- Effective application of GIS tools, geographic referencing, and mapping methodologies to support programme implementation and operational decision-making.

- Timely processing, storage, and transfer of data to designated IOM data warehouses in accordance with established procedures.

- Effective technical support provided to programme staff and implementing partners on the use of information management and GIS tools.

- Preparation and updating of user guides, training materials, and standard data collection tools, as required.

- Consistent application of IOM data management, confidentiality, and data protection standards across all assigned activities.
