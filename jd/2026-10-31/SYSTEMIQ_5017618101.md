&lt;p&gt;We are looking to grow our global network of talented independent consultants with experience in consulting, professional services, and project management. &lt;p&gt;With advanced problem-solving, analytical and relationship skills, you will be experienced working on consulting projects to deliver breakthrough insights, action and results.
