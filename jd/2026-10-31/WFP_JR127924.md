BACKGROUND AND PURPOSE OF THE ASSIGNMENT :

In line with the direction to strengthening employee wellness and enhancing Duty of Care, there is an increasing need to leverage data more systematically to inform decision-making, monitor trends, and assess the effectiveness of interventions.

Existing tools, such as the sick leave data and wellness intervention data, provide valuable insights ; however, these require continuous management, analysis, and translation into actionable recommendations to support global, regional, and country-level decision-making.

At the same time, upcoming activities, such as the Global Health Survey, present an opportunity to deepen understanding of staff health risks and priorities, enabling more targeted and cost-effective interventions. This project aims to strengthen the use of data visualization and analytics to move from descriptive reporting to predictive and action-oriented wellness initiatives, ultimately improving staff health and wellbeing and optimizing resource allocation.
