VPU Context:

EAP is a large and diverse region, ranging from Small Island States of the Pacific to the Philippines and China. Despite substantial economic growth and poverty reduction, the EAP region faces huge development challenges. Growth has been uneven across and within countries, institutions still need strengthening, and most countries are heavily affected by climate change. The Bank plays a significant role on global public goods, including climate resilient infrastructure, electricity access and renewable energy, rural and urban development, biodiversity, water pollution, and health. For more information, visit www.worldbank.org/en/region/eap

Duties and accountabilities:

• Transportation of staff/delegates to/from meetings and site visits, primarily within Honiara city and outside boundaries.

• Keep accurate and up to date records via a logbook, including record of mileage, gas/oil consumption

• Conduct regular maintenance checks & manage vehicle renewals in compliance of Solomon Islands law (i.e. annual registration, annual road work checks, annual insurance)

• Assist with customs clearance procedures for WB office shipments

• Under guidance of World Bank staff, help to follow up with visa applications for IRS staff/visiting missions

• Assist with Procurement and provide logistical support for the office as well as during organized events/meetings

• Help to facilitate new vehicle registration and new driver’s license for newly arrived international staff.

• Provide administrative support to the office under direction of the ACS team – to include media monitoring of press and digital, following up on transactions with vendors and other duties to be discussed and agreed.

• Provide a vetted list of taxis and drivers who are available for hire to visiting mission teams.
