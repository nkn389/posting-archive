Unit Context:

The Risk and Analytics team has embarked on a journey to streamline data management which is cloud based. The target is to achieve a sing source of truth w.r.t. data for all Finance complex needs. This data platform will be used for many different provisioning in terms of data usage.

Our IT strategy is designed to align IT initiatives with the WBG business strategy and with the overarching objective of ensuring that users have uniform and easy access to information and systems, both from Washington and country offices. This position has to be a technically sound candidate around cloud technology and data management principles.

The candidate will be expected to:

• As a multinational, multicultural institution, success at WBG requires the ability to negotiate and facilitate trade-offs and build consensus and commitment to a recommended direction among multiple stakeholders, with broadly divergent frames of reference. The candidate must also embrace and foster respect for all, including diversity in age, gender, and national origin.
• Successful candidate must possess the highest personal and professional integrity, as demonstrated through strong personal values, as well as consistent words and actions. He or she must build and sustain strong collegial working relationships with other units of the ITS and the World Bank Group, and be comfortable approaching others assertively, responsibly, and supportively.
• Technically very strong in cloud technologies, data management principles and able to individually deliver developed and tested components
• Hands-on experience in ADLS, Parquet, Python and Dremio is an absolute must (Certifications are highly advantageous)
• Exposure to Fixed income treasury business functions specifically around portfolio risk is required
