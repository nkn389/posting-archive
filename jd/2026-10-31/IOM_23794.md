Introduction
Established in 1951, IOM is a Related Organization of the United Nations and the leading UN agency in the field of migration. Working closely with governmental, intergovernmental and non-governmental partners, IOM promotes humane and orderly migration for the benefit of all. It saves lives and protects people on the move, drives solutions to displacement, and facilitates pathways for regular migration, while providing services and advice to governments and migrants.
IOM is committed to fostering a respectful, inclusive and supportive workplace where all employees can thrive professionally and feel valued. By creating such an environment, IOM aims to better harness the full potential of migration and strengthen its support to people on the move.

Project Context and Scope
Under the overall supervision of the Deputy Chief of Mission and the direct supervision of the Project Manager, the Field Engineer Consultant will be responsible and accountable for supporting the planning and implementation of engineering, construction and infrastructure-related activities.
The Consultant will undertake site assessments; prepare and review technical designs, specifications, Bills of Quantities and other technical documentation; provide technical support to procurement and tendering processes; monitor and supervise construction and rehabilitation works; support quality assurance and quality control; and coordinate with contractors, consultants, suppliers, relevant authorities and other technical stakeholders.
The Consultant will ensure that assigned works are implemented in accordance with approved technical documentation, contractual requirements, applicable technical standards and regulations in Bosnia and Herzegovina, approved project budgets and schedules, and relevant IOM rules and procedures.

Organizational Department / Unit to which the Consultant is contributing
Solidarity Fund, IOM Bosnia and Herzegovina — Sarajevo

Required Qualifications and Experience

- University degree in Civil Engineering or Architecture from an accredited academic institution with five years of relevant professional experience.

- Successful completion of the Professional Engineering Exam is a mandatory requirement.

Experience

- Proven professional experience in project design and in a civil engineering or civil construction related field.

- Demonstrated knowledge and experience with the planning, organization, monitoring and enforcement of safe work practices and procedures, with strong knowledge of BiH regulations within the construction sector.

- Working knowledge and experience with project planning and implementation, including knowledge of all phases of a construction project.

- Demonstrated experience in the supervision, construction, rehabilitation, or maintenance of water supply, wastewater, drainage, environmental, utility, or other public infrastructure projects is considered an advantage.

- Experience in the preparation of tender documentation and ability to participate in and support the tendering and procurement process.

- Knowledge of construction project planning, implementation and site supervision.

- Knowledge of procurement and tendering procedures in line with organizational rules.

- Knowledge of budgeting, cost estimation and preparation of Bills of Quantities (BoQ).

- Familiarity with water supply, wastewater, drainage, utility, environmental, or municipal infrastructure systems is an advantage.

- Proficiency in Microsoft Office applications; knowledge of AutoCAD or similar engineering software is an asset.

- Knowledge of contract management and coordination with contractors and suppliers.

- Knowledge of quality assurance, regulatory compliance and construction standards.

- Driving licence: B category and five years of active driving experience.

Tasks to be performed under this contract

- Conduct site assessments and technical surveys and prepare conceptual analyses, scopes of work, technical specifications, Bills of Quantities (BoQs), cost estimates, drawings, schedules, photographic records and other technical documentation required for assigned infrastructure interventions.

- Prepare and/or review engineering designs and technical documentation in accordance with applicable legislation, technical standards, approved project requirements and good engineering practice.

- Support the technical evaluation of bids and proposals by assessing compliance with technical specifications and providing documented technical findings and recommendations through the appropriate procurement process.

- Assess site conditions and prepare conceptual analyses and complete technical documentation, including scope of works, bills of quantities, material specifications, and photographic records.

- Monitor construction, rehabilitation and infrastructure works through regular site inspections, verifying implementation against approved drawings, specifications, Bills of Quantities, work schedules, applicable standards and contractual requirements.

- Identify risks, delays, or variations and provide technical input for contract amendments or additional works, ensuring alignment with approved budgets and schedules.

- Assess proposed technical changes, substitutions, variations and additional works, including their justification and implications for quantities, cost, quality and schedule, and submit technical recommendations to the Project Manager for consideration through the appropriate approval process.

- Monitor compliance with applicable occupational health and safety requirements at construction sites, document identified technical or safety-related concerns and follow up on corrective measures within the Consultant's area of responsibility.

- Maintain accurate technical project records, including site inspection reports, photographic documentation, drawings, technical correspondence, progress records, measurement documentation, testing records, variation documentation and other relevant engineering records.

- Support technical inspections, testing, commissioning, completion and handover of works, including the identification and follow-up of outstanding works or defects and review of relevant completion and as-built documentation.

- Perform such other related duties as may be assigned.

- Regular travel to construction sites and project locations across Bosnia and Herzegovina is required as part of the assignment.

Performance indicators for the evaluation of results

- Timely and accurate preparation and submission of technical documentation (site reports, BoQs, drawings, specifications).

- Quality and completeness of technical records and project files maintained throughout the assignment.

- Effective monitoring of construction works with documented site inspection reports.

- Adherence to approved budgets, schedules and contractual requirements.

- Compliance of works with applicable technical standards, local regulations and IOM procedures.
