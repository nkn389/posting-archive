For every child, the right to a Future

Generation Unlimited (GenU) brings together governments, UN agencies, the private sector, academia, civil society organizations and young people to co-create and scale innovative solutions that expand opportunities for young people. In line with the UNICEF Strategic Plan 2026–2029 and the GenU Strategy 2026–2029, GenU is advancing opportunities for young people to access job-relevant skills, quality employment and entrepreneurship, with the economic empowerment of adolescent girls and young women as a key priority.

This position offers an opportunity to contribute directly to this agenda by supporting the closure and learning agenda of the Girls Education and Skills Partnership (GESP), contributing to the design and development of EmPowerHer Asia, and providing technical support to EmPowerHer Africa. The role will also contribute to GenU’s broader governance, advocacy, knowledge-sharing and thought leadership on livelihoods for adolescent girls and young women.

How can you make a difference?

Under the supervision of the Senior Advisor, the Programme Specialist will support Generation Unlimited’s work to advance livelihoods and economic opportunities for adolescent girls and young women. The role will support the closure of the Girls Education and Skills Partnership (GESP), including programme implementation close-out, evidence and learning, dissemination and final donor reporting. The Specialist will contribute to the design and development of EmPowerHer Asia, including its theory of change, programme model, results framework, partnerships and early implementation planning, while providing technical support to EmPowerHer Africa. The role will also contribute to GenU’s governance, advocacy and thought leadership on youth livelihoods, strengthen integrated programming across UNICEF, and support evidence generation, knowledge-sharing and resource mobilization. Working with UNICEF offices, governments, private-sector partners, civil society, youth and women’s organizations, the Specialist will help translate evidence and learning into stronger programming for adolescent girls and young women.
