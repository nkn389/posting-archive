OVERVIEW

The National Consultant will provide technical leadership and expertise to support the School Education Department (SED) of Balochistan and Elementary and Secondary Education Department (E&SED), Khyber Pakhtunkhwa (KP) in strengthening the integration of climate change, environmental sustainability and disaster risk reduction (DRR) into provincial learning assessment frameworks. The consultant will undertake technical reviews and mapping, identify priority competencies and learning outcomes, develop evidence-based recommendations and assessment approaches, and facilitate stakeholder consultation, co-creation, capacity development and validation processes. The assignment will be undertaken in close coordination with UNESCO, the respective Education Departments, relevant provincial institutions and the international expert team supporting CSESI. While working closely with the provincial Education Departments for the assignment, the consultant will be contracted, supervised, and managed by UNESCO and will report directly to UNESCO.

Long Description

B. BACKGROUND
Funded by the Global Partnership for Education (GPE), CSESI seeks to enhance countries’ capacities to mainstream climate change adaptation and environmental sustainability into education sector plans, budgets and strategies as well as to enhance education ministry capacity for cross-sectoral coordination on climate and environment-related policy and programming. The CSESI initiative in Pakistan will be structured around 7 component areas outlined further in this work program which has been developed by CSESI partners (Save the Children, UNESCO-ESD and UNESCO-IIEP) based on consultations with the School Education Departments and partners held in Islamabad in July 2025.

The UNESCO Section of Education for Sustainable Development (ESD) supports ministries of education specifically with integrating learning of climate change and environmental sustainability into formal and non-formal education. This entails reviewing and, where required, adjusting and revising national curricula and syllabi for pre-primary, primary and secondary schools as well as teaching and learning materials (TLMs) based on each country’s needs and opportunities.

UNESCO uses a holistic learning approach of ESD to address climate change from economic, social and environmental perspectives, and supports the acquisition of knowledge, values and attitudes to underpin practical actions by learners to contribute to climate mitigation, adaptation, resilience and sustainable development. In addition to the resources available in the countries, UNESCO will use a set of global reference documents including the Greening Curriculum Guidance (UNESCO, 2024) and ESD for Sustainable Development Goals: Learning Objectives (UNESCO, 2017) as well as other guidance on curriculum alignment produced by UNESCO International Bureau of Education (IBE).

Led by UNESCO in close collaboration with SED, Government of Balochistan and E&SED, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, one of the objectives of CSESI in Pakistan is to support the integration of Sustainable Development themes into education, with an emphasis on climate change. It will involve reviewing the current learning assessment framework to identify gaps and opportunities for integration of climate/ environment/ DRR-related content. The review will inform the identification of priority topics and learning outcomes, drawing on the Greening Curriculum Guidance and applying a gender-sensitive lens. Based on this review, a set of recommendations will be developed for the integration of these priority topics and learning outcomes into the provincial learning assessment framework.

To this end, the UNESCO seeks to engage an expert individual as a consultant to support the integration of climate and environment into learning assessments in Balochistan and KP.

ASSIGNMENTS

C. OBJECTIVE AND SCOPE OF THE ASSIGNMENT
OBJECTIVE: The objective of the assignment is to strengthen the capacity of the education systems in Balochistan and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa to assess learners’ knowledge, competencies, attitudes and behaviours related to climate change, environmental sustainability and DRR. Specifically, the assignment will review existing learning assessment frameworks, identify gaps and priority learning outcomes, and develop contextually relevant, gender-responsive and evidence-based recommendations for their integration into provincial assessment systems, aligned with UNESCO’s Greening Curriculum Guidance and ongoing national and provincial curriculum reforms.

SCOPE: The assignment will cover Balochistan and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and will encompass the review and mapping of existing learning assessment frameworks, curricula, policies, teaching and learning materials and other relevant education documents against climate change, environmental sustainability and DRR competencies and learning outcomes. It will include identification of priority topics and learning outcomes; development of competency-based, gender-responsive and inclusive assessment approaches; formulation of recommendations for revising and strengthening provincial learning assessment frameworks; and preparation of an implementation and scaling strategy. The consultant will also facilitate provincial consultations, co-creation and validation workshops, undertake capacity development of relevant stakeholders, and ensure alignment with ongoing national curriculum reforms and relevant provincial policies and priorities.

ACTIVITIES: Under the overall authority of the Head of UNESCO Islamabad Office in Pakistan and the direct supervision of the Head of the Education, the national consultant will perform the following duties:

The consultant will be responsible for supporting the integration of climate and environment into learning assessment by reviewing the status, identifying priorities and developing recommendations for the integration of these priority topics and learning outcomes into the learning assessment framework.

Based on the global reference documents produced by UNESCO ESD Section, including the Greening Curriculum Guidance and other national or regional resources to be identified in consultation with the SED, and in concertation with the CSESI project team and the international experts, the consultant will deliver the following tasks:

Long Description

1.Conduct a review of the current learning assessment frameworks and learning objectives for climate/ environment/ DRR-related content in Balochistan and KP to identify gaps and opportunities for integration.
a. Review and map the existing learning assessment frameworks and other relevant curriculum and policy documents of Balochistan and KP against climate change, environmental sustainability, and DRR competencies and learning outcomes outlined in UNESCO's Greening Curriculum Guidance, considering the environmental, socio-economic, and cultural context of Balochistan. Learning assessment framework documents reviewed may include relevant policy and curriculum materials, including the Provincial Education Policy, Education Sector Plan, curriculum documents (including textbooks, syllabi, and lesson plans), as well as relevant provincial environmental protection policies.
b. Identify a set of contextually relevant priority topics and learning outcomes to be incorporated into the learning assessment framework.
c. Integrate local knowledge and ensure policy alignment by incorporating evidence from provincial stakeholder consultations and indigenous knowledge, while ensuring that the proposed learning outcomes and assessment priorities are aligned with the curriculum reform process led by the Ministry of Federal Education and Professional Training (MoFE&PT) and can inform broader national curriculum reforms.
d. Apply a gender-responsive approach to assess the extent to which existing learning objectives and assessment practices address the differentiated needs, experiences, and vulnerabilities of girls and boys, and recommend gender-responsive learning outcomes and assessment approaches that promote equality, inclusion, and resilience.

Long Description

2. Provide recommendations for the integration of these priority topics and learning outcomes into the learning assessment framework by incorporating priority themes, competencies, and learning outcomes identified through the review process and UNESCO's Greening Curriculum Guidance.

a. Develop evidence-based recommendations for strengthening the integration of climate change, environmental sustainability, and DRR into the provincial learning assessment frameworks of Balochistan and KP, including proposed learning outcomes, assessment methodologies, implementation considerations, and options for scaling across additional grades and subject areas.
b. Develop an implementation and scaling strategy for the phased integration of the revised learning assessment framework, including key implementation considerations, institutional responsibilities, capacity development needs, and options for expansion across additional grades, subjects, and assessment levels.
c. Ensure alignment with national curriculum reforms by harmonizing the proposed recommendations with the ongoing curriculum review led by the National Curriculum Council (NCC), MoFE&PT, and incorporating evidence and local knowledge generated through multi-stakeholder consultation workshops.
d. Strengthen assessment approaches by recommending assessment methodologies that move beyond knowledge-based (cognitive) evaluation to include socio-emotional and Behavioural learning domains. Recommendations should include practical and competency-based assessment methods, such as performance tasks, observation checklists, practical assignments, portfolios, project journals, eco-action projects, and community engagement logs, as appropriate.
e. Apply a gender-responsive and inclusive approach throughout the assignment by ensuring that recommendations reflect the principles of gender equality and inclusion outlined in UNESCO's Greening Curriculum Guidance. The consultant shall promote gender-balanced stakeholder participation during consultations and ensure that the proposed assessment framework addresses the diverse needs of learners, including considerations related to gender, disability, socio-economic background, language, and other dimensions of diversity. Recommendations should also address gender-specific climate vulnerabilities and promote equitable representation of women and men in climate-related learning and assessment materials.

Long Description

Support the School Education Department and UNESCO in organising and delivering at least 3 inter-ministerial, multi-stakeholder workshops per province (Quetta and Peshawar) for consultation, co-creation, capacity building and validation of outputs, totalling 6 workshops overall.

These workshops will be critical for collecting meaningful inputs and feedback from key provincial stakeholders on the learning assessment review, as well as for securing consensus of identified priorities and validation of findings and recommendations.
a. Facilitate the following 3 workshops in each province:
 Workshop 1: Consultation workshop to review the findings from the learning assessment review, identify priority learning outcomes, agree on the approach for revising the learning assessment framework and provide a technical review of the draft recommendations for further inputs.
 Workshop 2: Co-creation workshop to further refine learning assessment framework recommendations.
 Workshop 3: Validation and endorsement of the final recommendations and revised learning assessment framework, including presentation of the final outputs to key provincial stakeholders.
b. Facilitate stakeholder consultations both during the workshops and through bilateral meetings to collect technical input and feedback from relevant government institutions, academia, development partners, and civil society, and build consensus on priority climate-related learning outcomes, assessment approaches, and recommendations.
c. Design and deliver capacity-building sessions during the workshops to strengthen stakeholders' understanding of climate change education, climate literacy, and approaches for integrating climate change, environmental sustainability, and DRR into learning assessments across different grade levels.

D.COORDINATION AND COLLABORATUION
The consultant will be jointly supervised by the UNESCO Islamabad with technical backstopping provided by a team of international expert consultants working with UNESCO HQ. The consultant will work in close collaboration with SED and E&SED, as well as in close consultation with the wider UNESCO CSESI team, other provincial technical departments, relevant government authorities and key partners including Save the Children.

Activities need to be planned, developed, and implemented in close consultation and coordination with technical teams in the provincial education departments, including Bureau of Curriculum, Provincial Institute for Teacher Education (PITE), Textbooks Borad, Directorate of Schools, Climate Change and Environment Department, other relevant Provincial Departments and the UNESCO CSESI team.

The consultant will ensure coordination with other partners delivering programmes in Climate Education, to ensure complementarity and alignment of initiatives, and avoid duplication. The consultant shall actively participate in the coordination mechanisms established under the CSESI and engage in regular technical consultations and bilateral meetings to ensure complementarity, alignment with provincial and national priorities, and effective knowledge sharing.

The consultant will adopt a co-creation methodology to ensure the recommendations for revision of the learning assessments are co-developed jointly with key provincial stakeholders.

The consultant will aim to achieve gender parity with project participants and apply an intersectional approach as outlined in the Greening Curriculum Guidance to ensure the learning assessment recommendations consider the range of diversities of learners including gender, class, language background and ability levels.
