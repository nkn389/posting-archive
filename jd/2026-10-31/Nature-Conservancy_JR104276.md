&lt;h3&gt;&lt;span class="emphasis-2"&gt;&lt;b&gt;&lt;u&gt;What We Can Achieve Together:&lt;/u&gt;&lt;/b&gt;&lt;/span&gt;&lt;/h3&gt;&lt;br /&gt;&lt;p&gt;The Development Program Specialist will be an integral part of the Virginia Development team, whose goal is to secure funding and ensure financial sustainability for the conservation priorities of the Virginia Business Unit and beyond. This is a remote position in the Eastern Standard Time zone and expected to work 9am – 5pm EST.&lt;/p&gt;&lt;p&gt;The Specialist will be responsible for all aspects of gift processing to ensure that donor gifts are received and acknowledged as per TNC’s best practices and compliance. The Specialist will prepare and update fundraising dashboards and track philanthropic contributions. &lt;span style="color:#000000"&gt; &lt;/span&gt;&lt;/p&gt;&lt;p&gt; &lt;/p&gt;&lt;p&gt;The Specialist, with the Development Operations Manager, will serve as subject matter expert for data management in the donor database (Blackbaud CRM). They will produce reports and queries from the donor database in support of fundraising efforts including events, donor mailings, and pledge reminder letters. They may assist with special initiatives.&lt;/p&gt;&lt;p&gt; &lt;/p&gt;&lt;p&gt;The Specialist will conduct research on prospective and current donors and support the writing of strategic internal and external facing documents.&lt;/p&gt;&lt;p&gt; &lt;/p&gt;&lt;p&gt;The Specialist must maintain advanced skills with the systems in order to execute tasks and provide support to team members.
