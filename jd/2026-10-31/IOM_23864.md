Introduction
Established in 1951, IOM is a Related Organization of the United Nations and the leading UN agency in the field of migration. Working closely with governmental, intergovernmental and non-governmental partners, IOM promotes humane and orderly migration for the benefit of all. It saves lives and protects people on the move, drives solutions to displacement, and facilitates pathways for regular migration, while providing services and advice to governments and migrants.
IOM is committed to fostering a respectful, inclusive and supportive workplace where all employees can thrive professionally and feel valued. By creating such an environment, IOM aims to better harness the full potential of migration and strengthen its support to people on the move.

Organizational Context and Scope
In Lebanon, IOM’s Labour Mobility Unit leads projects and provides thematic oversight related to labour mobility and labour migration governance. Current projects include Abeona, funded by the Government of Italy, and DAR, funded by the European Union, both of which aim to upskill Syrians in Lebanon and facilitate their access to employment opportunities in destination countries through four integrated pillars of intervention: (1) skills development, including language and vocational training; (2) employer engagement and job matching; (3) migration planning; and (4) post-arrival assistance and integration support. Looking ahead, IOM seeks to expand labour mobility programming to include other refugee populations in Lebanon, as well as circular labour mobility opportunities for Lebanese nationals.
​Under the overall supervision of the Head of Programmes in Beirut, Lebanon, and direct supervision of the Programme Officer (Labour Mobility), and in collaboration with relevant units in the Lebanon country office and other country offices in destination countries, the successful candidate will be responsible and accountable for leading candidate outreach and skills profiling activities for IOM’s labour mobility projects. This includes contributing to the development of candidate outreach strategies, creating and managing referral mechanisms and data collection systems, developing and facilitating informational webinars, conducting candidate assessment calls, tracking candidate participation in programme activities, analyzing and preparing snapshots of candidate data as requested by employers and other stakeholders, and training others on candidate outreach and profiling procedures, among other activities.
