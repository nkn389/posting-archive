Organizational Context and Scope
The International Organization for Migration (IOM) is the UN Migration Agency. With 175 member states it is committed to the principle that humane and orderly migration benefits migrants and society. Established in 1951 and now active in over 400 field locations worldwide, IOM works with partners, government and civil society to: 1. Assist in meeting the operational challenges of migration and human mobility; 2. Advance understanding of migration issues; 3. Encourage social and economic development through migration; and 4. Uphold the human dignity and well-being of migrants and mobile populations.
Burundi continues to experience complex displacement dynamics driven by returns of refugees, internal displacement, climate-related shocks, land pressures and limited absorption capacity at community level. In this context, IOM supports government-led and community-based efforts to advance durable solutions for returnees, internally displaced persons and host communities, with a strong focus on social cohesion, access to basic services, livelihoods and local governance. IOM’s Durable Solutions portfolio in Burundi contributes to the humanitarian–development–peace nexus by strengthening local systems, supporting communal planning processes and promoting inclusive recovery in displacement-affected areas.
Under the overall supervision of the Chief of Mission, the supervision of the Programme Officer (Durable Solutions) and the direct supervision of the Senior Project Associate (Durable Solutions), the incumbent will support the implementation, coordination and monitoring of durable solutions interventions under IOM Burundi’s programme portfolio.
The incumbent will contribute to the effective delivery of Durable Solutions activities in line with IOM’s strategic priorities in Burundi, with a focus on community-based approaches supporting return, local integration and resilience of displacement-affected communities. The position provides programme support across planning, implementation, monitoring and reporting processes, including coordination with government counterparts, implementing partners and local communities.
