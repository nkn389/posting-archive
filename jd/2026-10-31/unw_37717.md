Background:

UN Women, grounded in the vision of equality enshrined in the Charter of the United Nations, works for the elimination of discrimination against women and girls; the empowerment of women; and the achievement of equality between women and men as partners and beneficiaries of development, human rights, humanitarian action and peace and security. UN Women has been in Afghanistan for two decades. UN Women's programming approach is informed by analysis of the political, economic and humanitarian situation, risks to and capacities of partner organizations, and needs of Afghan women and girls. UN Women Afghanistan currently has five key programme priorities: (1) Integrated Programming and Localization, (2) Women's movement building through core grant making; 3) Policy and Research; 4) Economic Resilience and Women's Empowerment and 5) Coordination, Access and Humanitarian Response. UN Women Afghanistan operates through its main office in Kabul and five sub-offices located in the provinces of Balkh, Bamyan, Herat, Kandahar and Nangarhar.

UN Women is currently implementing the Protection of women’s rights in Afghanistan and delivery of essential services to address gender-based violence project, which is situated across two program pillars, namely, Integrated Programming and Policy and Research. The project’s dual focus integrates service delivery, with gender-responsive research and advocacy, to deliver both immediate and long-term change in protecting the rights of Afghan women and girls to live free from gender-based violence. Through the project, UN Women supports community-based initiatives that expand access to quality essential services for women, girls, and their families, while engaging communities, leaders, men, and boys to promote positive social norms and respect for women’s rights. In parallel, the project generates high-quality gender analysis, including through the collection and analysis of quantitative and qualitative data, to inform the work of the UN system and international stakeholders.​

​Reporting to the Deputy Country Representative – Programmes, the Project Coordinator is responsible for coordinating the overall management of the Protection of women’s rights in Afghanistan and delivery of essential services to address gender-based violence project, working in tandem with colleagues across the Country Office, including Programme Management Support Unit, Partnerships and Resource Mobilization Unit, and Operations Unit.

III. Scope of Work:

1.Coordinate the overall management and implementation of the project

- Lead the overall planning, coordination and management of the project in accordance with the approved Project Document, Results Framework, workplan and budget, UN Women policies and procedures, and donor requirements, with inputs from respective programme pillar.

- Coordinate project implementation across relevant ACO programme units, sub-offices and corporate functions, ensuring coherence across the project’s service delivery, research and advocacy components.

- In coordination with the relevant ACO units, develop, review and update and finalize project-related documents, including workplans, risk matrices, briefs, presentations, etc. and implementation arrangements, ensuring alignment between planned activities, results, budgets and timelines.

- Track implementation progress and identify and document emerging challenges, bottlenecks and corrective actions to support timely and quality delivery.

- Lead regular internal monthly project coordination meetings and quarterly progress reviews, ensuring agreed actions are documented and followed up.

- Coordinate Project Board meetings and follow up on decisions and recommendations.

- Provide guidance and support to all project-related units on donor rules and procedures to ensure compliance.

2.Lead project monitoring, reporting and risk management of the project

- Oversee monitoring of the project Results Framework, including progress against outputs, outcomes and performance indicators, in close coordination with relevant programme teams and the Programme Management Support Unit (PMSU).

- Regularly review programmatic and financial performance and provide an analysis of progress, challenges, risks and recommended corrective actions, building on inputs from relevant ACO units.

- Coordinate the preparation and quality assurance of, and directly support drafting and generation of, timely and high-quality donor narrative and financial reports, technical briefing notes, situation updates and other project documentation.

- Coordinate regular updates of the project risk register/risk management matrix and follow up on mitigation measures with responsible units.

- Ensure that project data, evidence and documentation are systematically captured and available to support reporting, learning and decision-making.

- Promote the application of relevant evidence, lessons learned and good practices throughout project implementation.

3.Support coordination of project partnerships and stakeholder engagement of the project

- Where relevant, under the guidance of the responsible programme units, support the coordination of project relationships with partners, service providers, and other relevant stakeholders.

- Maintain an overview of project partners, service providers, and implementation arrangements across the project.

- Facilitate effective communication and coordination across programme units, sub-offices, Operations, Finance, PMSU, Partnerships & Relational Management unit (PRM) and other relevant UN Women offices and units.

- Serve as the primary project focal point for the donor, working closely with the PRM, the Deputy Representative and relevant colleagues.

4.Oversee and guide financial and resource management of the project

- Monitor project budgets, expenditures, financial delivery and resource utilization, ensuring alignment with approved workplans, Results Framework, and agreed targets.

- Work closely with Finance, Operations and programme teams to identify and address financial or operational issues affecting project implementation.

- Support forward planning and forecasting to ensure the effective and timely utilization of project resources.

5.Support strategic partnerships and resource mobilization

- Contribute to the development, coordination and review of donor proposals, concept notes and other resource mobilization materials related to the project and/or its areas of work.

6.Manage knowledge building efforts and support project evaluation

- Identify, document and synthesize good practices, lessons learned, innovations and evidence emerging from project implementation.

- Develop knowledge products that strengthen programming, policy engagement and resource mobilization efforts.

- Facilitate the dissemination and application of project learning within the Country Office and, as appropriate, with partners, donors and other UN Women offices.

- Coordinate the final project evaluation if applicable.
