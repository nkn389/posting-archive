We currently offer paid internships and short-term roles in a range of disciplines across our Virginia Teams. With a focus on recruiting and developing a diverse staff, we strive to create an environment where people of all backgrounds, experiences, and identities feel welcome to participate in conservation. Wherever you are on your educational journey; community college, technical school, four-year program, or graduate school, STEP may have an opportunity for you to consider! &lt;/p&gt;&lt;p&gt; &lt;/p&gt;&lt;p&gt;All STEP hires will receive an orientation, professional development webinars, and have chances to visit TNC sites. This will be accomplished by supporting three areas of our program: (1) stewardship and prescribed fire, (2) fee land and easement monitoring, and (3) volunteer/community outreach. This position will work closely with several TNC staff members and be supervised by our Prescribed Fire and Stewardship Specialist.&lt;/p&gt;&lt;br /&gt;&lt;p&gt;The 6-month position will involve working with TNC’s Central Stewardship Program team to learn more about our work in Virginia and assist our staff with a variety of essential activities. The internship will focus on field-based stewardship on preserves across the state. This includes participating in prescribed burns, easement monitoring, and supporting volunteer programs. The intern will have the opportunity to work with the entire Stewardship Team and members of our Landscape Programs. The position will spend approximately 2/3 time in the field and 1/3 time in the office working both with the team and independently. Some overnight or weekend travel should be expected.&lt;/p&gt;&lt;br /&gt;&lt;p&gt;Specific work tasks may include but not be limited to:&lt;/p&gt;&lt;ul&gt;&lt;li&gt;Participating in prescribed fire tools&lt;/li&gt;&lt;li&gt;Recording data with field iPad, GPS, ESRI Survey 123 or Field Maps software&lt;/li&gt;&lt;li&gt;Operating, loading, and unloading a side-by-side all-terrain vehicle, truck and trailer according to established safety protocols&lt;/li&gt;&lt;li&gt;Assisting with the planning, implementation, and reporting associated with volunteer land stewardship and community outreach
