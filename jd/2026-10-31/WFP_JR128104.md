JOB PURPOSE
The Travel Focal Point will support the GHQ Travel Hub by providing end‑to‑end air ticket processing services, with a primary responsibility for handling flight bookings through a Global Distribution System (GDS). The incumbent will identify and share suitable flight options with requestors, and upon confirmation, submit electronic ticketing requests to the BCD Automated Ticket Issuance system for issuance.

The role requires strong technical expertise in airline reservations, ticketing rules, and fare construction, ensuring accurate, timely, and compliant ticketing in line with WFP travel policies and corporate efficiency principles.

Organizational Context
The United Nations World Food Programme (WFP) is the world’s largest humanitarian organization, saving lives in emergencies and using food assistance to build pathways to peace, stability, and prosperity.

Under the One Global HQ model, WFP has established the GHQ Travel Hub to consolidate and streamline global travel services, ensuring policy compliance, cost efficiency, and consistent service delivery across headquarters, regional offices, and country operations. The GHQ Travel Hub plays a critical role in supporting WFP’s operational readiness by providing timely and accurate travel services worldwide

KEY ACCOUNTABILITIES (Not all inclusive)

GDS Ticketing and Travel Processing

- Process air travel requests received through WFP travel workflows and systems.
- Perform flight searches and reservations using a Global Distribution System (GDS), ensuring optimal routing, fare selection, and compliance with WFP travel policies.
- Prepare and share proposed flight options with travellers and travel focal points, clearly outlining fare conditions and key constraints.
- Coordinate closely with travellers/travel focal points to confirm itineraries and travel preferences.
- Submit finalized itineraries through the BCD Automated Ticket Issuance system for ticket issuance.
- Ensure timely issuance of e‑tickets and accurate transmission of travel documentation to travellers.

Compliance and Quality Assurance

- Ensure all bookings comply with WFP travel policies, financial rules, security requirements, and corporate efficiency measures.
- Apply cost‑containment principles, including use of lowest logical fares, approved routes, and preferred carriers when applicable.
- Conduct accuracy checks on itineraries, ticketing data, and fare rules prior to ticket issuance.
- Maintain proper records of bookings and ticketing actions for audit and reporting purposes.

Coordination and Client Support

- Serve as a key operational contact for travellers and GHQ Travel Hub colleagues on ticketing‑related matters.
- Liaise with BCD Travel and internal stakeholders to resolve ticketing issues, re‑issues, exchanges, cancellations, and disruptions.
- Support travellers during disruptions by proposing alternative routing options and coordinating corrective ticketing actions.
- Maintain clear, timely, and professional communication with all stakeholders.

Process Improvement and Support

- Contribute to the continuous improvement of ticketing workflows and SOPs within the GHQ Travel Hub.
- Support team knowledge‑sharing on GDS functionality, fare rules, and ticketing best practices.
- Flag recurring issues, system constraints, or policy gaps and propose practical solutions.

Perform any other related duties as required.
