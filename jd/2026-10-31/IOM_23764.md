Project Context and Scope
The 24-month project “Enhancing National Pandemic Preparedness and Cross-Border Health Security in Lao PDR” (PRIMA Code LA10P0566; Project Code MP0745), starting 01 October 2026, supports national and provincial authorities to strengthen pandemic preparedness and cross-border health security. Outcome 2 focuses on strengthening legislation and policy frameworks for priority pathogens. Output 2.1 commits to expanding endorsement and operationalization of bilateral coordination SOPs and cross-border data-sharing protocols with Viet Nam, Thailand, and China.
Organizational Department / Unit to which the Consultant is contributing

Tasks to be performed under this contract
Scope of responsibilities
Under the project’s health component, the consultant will:

- Review existing public health surveillance, event notification, verification, risk assessment, response coordination, referral, and information-sharing arrangements for the Lao PDR–Thailand, Lao PDR–Viet Nam, and Lao PDR–China corridors.

- Recommend 1 Lao-side priority Point of Entry (PoE) for each corridor, with a rationale. IOM Lao PDR and the relevant authorities will confirm the sites and scope during inception.

- Identify operational gaps and clarify the roles, triggers, communication channels, timelines, safeguards, escalation, and follow-up needed for practical bilateral coordination.

- Develop 3 bilateral SOP packages, tailored to the confirmed corridor and PoE, and consult relevant authorities on the drafts.

- Align the review with applicable national procedures and bilateral arrangements; relevant IHR (2005) provisions—including Articles 19, 21 and 22 and Annex 1, Part B, as applicable—and WHO guidance on PoE core capacities and public health emergency contingency planning.

- Support technical validation, document feedback and unresolved decisions, and provide handover guidance for the Lao project team.

The NO-A will coordinate government and technical engagement; the G5 Project Assistant will support administrative arrangements; and IOM Thailand MHU will provide budgeted technical support. Competent authorities retain decision-making and formal endorsement authority. The assignment is limited to SOP-related procedures; it is not a nationwide or full IHR capacity assessment and does not determine PoE designation. After handover, the IOM Lao PDR project team will coordinate follow-up toward endorsement and operationalization. DRCMU flow-monitoring work is outside this consultancy.
Category B Consultants: Tangible and measurable outputs of the work assignment (with realistic delivery dates)
Deliverable 1 – Inception report | Due within 1 months of contract start | Payment: 30% upon written acceptance. The report will set out the methodology, document review, consultation plan, assessment framework, and workplan for all 3 corridors. It will recommend 1 Lao-side priority PoE for each corridor with rationale; identify preliminary priority events/pathogens and stakeholders; provide the common SOP outline, drafting approach, validation and travel schedule; and state assumptions, risks, mitigation measures, and decisions required. IOM Lao PDR and relevant authorities will confirm the 3 sites and scope before detailed assessment begins. The inception report will explain how relevant IHR provisions and WHO PoE guidance will inform the assessment and drafting.
Deliverable 2 – Consolidated assessment and draft SOP packages | Due by Month 4 | Payment: 60% upon written acceptance. Submit a consolidated assessment of the 3 confirmed corridors and PoEs, including current process maps, roles and responsibilities, operational gaps, and recommendations, together with 3 draft bilateral SOP packages using a common structure and country- and PoE-specific procedures. Each draft will cover event triggers, notification channels and timelines, verification and risk assessment, coordination and response, information-sharing safeguards, referral and follow-up where applicable, escalation and after-hours contacts, records, closure, and review. Include consultation records, draft forms or job aids, a comment log, and validation-session materials; provide editable files and clean PDFs. Include a concise IHR/WHO alignment and gap matrix for each package, mapping relevant IHR provisions and WHO guidance to the draft procedures, current arrangements, identified gaps, and recommendations.
Deliverable 3 – Final validated SOP package and handover | Due by Month 6 | Payment: 10% upon written acceptance. Submit the final assessment and recommendations and 3 complete SOP packages incorporating consolidated technical comments and documented validation for each package. Include relevant forms, concise job aids, an updated contact-list template, a response-to-comments matrix, unresolved decisions and endorsement steps, and a handover/implementation plan identifying sequence, proposed institutional responsibilities, resource and coordination needs, monitoring indicators, contact updates, and periodic review. Brief the Lao project team and designated government counterparts and provide editable source files. Formal government endorsement is not guaranteed by the consultant and is not a condition of payment where it depends on government decisions outside the consultant’s control; endorsement steps and decisions must be documented. Include the updated IHR/WHO alignment and gap matrix for each package, noting remaining gaps and any policy or institutional decisions for the competent authorities.
