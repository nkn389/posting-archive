Unit Context:

The IT Transformation Management (TM) function enables ITS to deliver measurable business impact by strengthening how strategy, execution, and performance measurement connect across the organization. TM serves as an integrator across operating model priorities, ensuring ITS modernization efforts contribute to the World Bank Group’s Evolution Roadmap and are supported by clear outcomes, disciplined execution, and transparent performance reporting.

The Transformation Office plays a central role in elevating how ITS delivers value by evolving the IT operating model toward greater clarity of ownership, improved value flow, stronger governance, and measurable performance management. This work requires coordinated progress across nine interdependent operating model components: Performance, Organizational Structure, Ways of Working, Tools/Assets, Places, Talent, Sourcing & Alliances, Decision Rights, AI, Data and Financials.

This role drives staff understanding and engagement with ITS transformation through data-informed, AI-enabled communications and knowledge management, strengthening workforce readiness and supporting operating model maturity over time.

Roles and Responsibilities:

Scope of Work

• Design and manage Power BI dashboards that convert data into clear, compelling visual narratives tailored for leadership and stakeholders, including implementation of Row-Level Security (RLS) to ensure data access is appropriately restricted by user identity, region, department, or role.

• Use statistical techniques to uncover trends, measure impact, and generate evidence-based recommendations related to change adoption.

• Translate survey and feedback data—quantitative and qualitative—into clear, actionable insights for reporting and continuous improvement.

• Work within Agile frameworks (e.g., SAFe, Scrum, and Kanban), understanding and applying Agile principles in transformation contexts.

• Ensure consistency, accuracy, and usability of data across transformation efforts.

• Communicate findings effectively through concise summaries, visual reports, and strategic recommendations tailored for both technical and non-technical stakeholders.

• Demonstrate a strong understanding of AI tools and their practical application to drive innovation, improve workflows, and contribute to digital transformation efforts.

• Use digital collaboration tools such as Mural, Confluence, Microsoft Teams, and SharePoint for virtual coordination.

Selection Criteria
• Master’s degree in data science, statistics, information systems, or a related field with with 5 years of experience or a Bachelors Degree with a minimum of 7 years of relevant experience, or equivalent combination of education and experience.

• Demonstrated expertise in data and statistical analysis, with a focus on generating actionable outcomes.

• Working knowledge of analytical languages such as SQL or Python.

• Proficiency in Power BI and Excel for dashboard development and reporting, including experience designing and implementing RLS roles within Power BI datasets or semantic models using DAX filter expressions (e.g., USERPRINCIPALNAME() or USERNAME()) and validating roles prior to deployment.

• Proven ability to translate content, metrics, and feedback into actionable outcomes and practical insights.

• Excellent verbal and written communication skills, with the ability to communicate clearly across all organizational levels and with both technical and non-technical audiences.

• SAFe Agile certification is a plus.

• Ability to manage multiple tasks independently and deliver results in a dynamic, Agile setting.
