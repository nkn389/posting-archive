About this position

The Technology and Transformation Department (TTD) provides the information and technology tools, solutions, and services that enable the ongoing operation and management of the Bank. The department is the focal point for the application and governance of information technology.

We are looking for an analytical, collaborative, and results-oriented IT Compliance Controls and Automation Testing Consultant. The consultant will work directly with the Technology, Data and Assurance Unit (TTD/TDA) and Product Family Owners in a dual role: identifying risks and testing controls for in-scope IT systems during self-assessment processes, and conducting automation testing for software applications to support quality.

The consultant will report to the TDA Compliance Team Leader in the Technology, Data and Assurance Unit.

What you’ll do

- Participating with members of TTD/TDA in the design and execution of the internal control testing exercise. The Bank performs annual testing for each IT General Control (ITGC).

- Working with TTD/TDA to identify which IT internal controls apply to specific new system implementations and assessing the related downstream implications.

- Performing continuous monitoring of highly privileged accounts across infrastructure components, including databases, operating systems, cloud components, privileged identity management solutions, and applications, and advising Product Owners and Technical Leaders on the implementation of monitoring controls.

- Participating in the review, update, and creation of documentation related to ITGCs, including procedures, operational documents, monitoring documents, inventories, and other supporting documentation.

- Meeting with stakeholders across the Technology and Transformation Department to understand how each control is performed and identify control-testing automation needs.

- Coordinating activities with outsourced automation testing providers to ensure the unit responds to the needs of Product Owners and Technical Leads. Activities include functional, regression, performance, and security testing.

- Conducting research and providing technical recommendations on how controls can be designed and deployed to meet testing and CI/CD requirements and presenting recommendations to the corresponding stakeholders.

What you'll need

Education: Master degree (or equivalent advanced degree) in in Computer Science or other fields relevant to the responsibilities of the role.

Experience: At least 5 years of progressive relevant experience in Information Systems, IT Audit, IT Risk Management, or automation testing, including:

- IT risk assessment, controls, compliance, remediation, and relevant frameworks (e.g., ISO 27001/27002, SOC 2, SOX, NIST, COBIT, COSO).

- Cloud technologies, DevSecOps, version control, Jira, ServiceNow, and Power BI.

- Effective documentation, presentation, and cross-functional stakeholder collaboration.

- Controls automation in Microsoft Azure is a plus.

Additional knowledge/certifications:

- Certifications in information security, IT risk, automation testing, or cloud security are desirable, including CISA, CISM, CRISC, CISSP, ISTQB, or Selenium-related credentials.

- Proficiency in AI and generative AI technologies, along with their potential applications; and

- Extensive understanding of ethical considerations and responsible use of AI.

Languages: Proficiency in Spanish and English, spoken and written, is required. Additional knowledge of French and Portuguese is preferable.
