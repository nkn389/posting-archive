INTRODUCTION TO GGGI

The Global Green Growth Institute (GGGI) is a treaty-based international, inter-governmental organization dedicated to supporting and promoting strong, inclusive and sustainable economic growth in developing countries and emerging economies. To learn more please visit about GGGI web page.

Project background
Peru has made notable progress in preparing for cooperative approaches under Article 6 of the Paris Agreement, including the establishment of regulatory instruments such as the National Registry for Mitigation Measures (RENAMI) and the signing of bilateral agreements with Switzerland and Singapore. However, important challenges remain to fully operationalize participation in international carbon markets, including the absence of a comprehensive Article 6 implementation framework to guide coordination and implementation, limited technical capacity at the sectoral level to identify and prioritize mitigation activities, and the need for practical tools and methodologies to develop a robust pipeline of Article 6-eligible activities.

In this context, the project “Strengthening Peru’s Readiness for Article 6 Carbon Market Participation” aims to enhance Peru’s institutional, technical, and operational readiness to participate effectively in international carbon markets. It will provide capacity-building support to government stakeholders and enhance awareness among public and private actors on Article 6 requirements and opportunities. The project will also support the development of regulatory and technical instruments, strengthen institutional framework and refine criteria and tools for identifying and prioritizing mitigation activities in key sectors such as energy, transport, and industry. In addition, it will support the preparation of Mitigation Activity Identification Notes (MAINs) aligned with national priorities and Peru’s Nationally Determined Contribution (NDC).

To ensure the effective coordination and implementation of project activities and maximize stakeholder engagement and outreach, this technical assistance will engage a Coordination and Outreach Consultant with proven experience in stakeholder engagement, institutional coordination, and communications related to climate action and/or carbon markets. The consultant will support the coordination and delivery of project activities, facilitate engagement and collaboration among relevant public and private stakeholders, and contribute to effective outreach and dissemination of project outputs and key Article 6 messages. The consultant will also support the organization of capacity-building and awareness-raising activities, maintain effective communication with project stakeholders, and help strengthen linkages and coordination across the different activities and actors involved in the project.

Objectives of the assignment

The objective of this consultancy is to support the effective coordination, stakeholder engagement, and outreach required for the implementation of project activities aimed at strengthening Peru’s readiness to participate in cooperative approaches under Article 6 of the Paris Agreement. The consultant will facilitate engagement among government institutions, private sector actors, and other relevant stakeholders; support awareness-raising and knowledge-sharing activities; and contribute to effective communication and coordination around key project outputs and processes, helping to strengthen stakeholder participation, institutional ownership, and uptake of Article 6-related frameworks, tools, and opportunities.

Scope of work

The consultant will be responsible for a series of general and project-specific activities.

General Responsibilities:

-
Support the planning, coordination, and implementation of stakeholder engagement, capacity-building, consultation, and outreach activities under the project.

-
Facilitate communication and coordination among MINAM, relevant government institutions, private sector actors, development partners, and other stakeholders involved in Article 6-related activities.

-
Support the preparation, dissemination, and communication of project information, knowledge products, and outreach materials, ensuring that content is appropriately tailored to relevant audiences, based on technical content developed or validated by the project team.

-
Support MINAM and the project team in documenting stakeholder engagement processes, capturing feedback and inputs, and communicating project results and key messages at national and international events, workshops, and other relevant forums, as required.

Project-specific activities

1: Raised awareness and enhanced capacity of stakeholders to engage in Article 6 cooperative approaches on an equitable basis

-
Support the implementation of the Article 6 Awareness-Raising and Outreach Plan, including stakeholder mapping, engagement activities, and dissemination of relevant information.

-
Support the organization and delivery of capacity-building, awareness-raising, and knowledge-sharing activities for relevant public and private sector stakeholders.

-
Coordinate the dissemination of Article 6 knowledge products and other communication materials through appropriate stakeholder and outreach channels.

-
Gather and document stakeholder feedback and inputs to inform the refinement of capacity-building and awareness-raising activities.

2: Governance frameworks and institutional capabilities for Article 6 developed

-
Provide technical, coordination, and facilitation support for stakeholder consultations contributing to the development of Peru’s Article 6 Strategy and governance frameworks, including preparation of materials, coordination of inputs, and follow-up.

-
Facilitate engagement with relevant government institutions and other stakeholders to gather inputs, communicate project developments, and support institutional ownership of Article 6-related outputs.

-
Support the organization of consultation and validation processes for governance instruments, technical guidance, and other project outputs.

-
Document stakeholder inputs, consultation outcomes, and agreed actions, and support their effective communication with the project team and relevant counterparts.

3: Mitigation activities originated and MAINs (Mitigation Activity Idea Notes) prepared

-
Support outreach to potential mitigation activity developers and relevant sectoral stakeholders to identify and communicate opportunities for participation under Article 6.

-
Support the organization of consultations and engagement activities with potential mitigation activity developers and relevant government institutions throughout the activity identification and assessment process.

-
Facilitate communication and coordination among mitigation activity developers, MINAM, relevant government institutions, and technical consultants, including coordination of inputs and follow-up during the development and validation of mitigation activities and MAINs.

-
Support the organization of dissemination and matchmaking activities related to prioritized mitigation activities and MAINs.

Reporting Arrangements
The consultant will report to the Project Manager in Peru, who will be responsible for the overall supervision and coordination of the consultancy. The consultant will work in close coordination with the Project Manager, who will facilitate coordination with the General Directorate of Climate Change and Desertification (DGCCD) of the Ministry of Environment (MINAM), the project's main technical counterpart. The consultant will carry out the foreseen activities mainly in person, as agreed in the workplan and during its progress. The consultant shall consider the inputs and coordination agreed with the DGCCD in the implementation of the assignment.

The reporting and coordination arrangements will be agreed during the first week of the assignment. Progress will be monitored through ongoing coordination with the Project Manager, complemented by periodic coordination meetings, as required, to review progress, prioritize activities, and agree on the next steps.

Upon MINAM's request, and pursuant to the regulations established by the Comptroller General of the Republic of Peru, the consultant shall submit a sworn statement of interest via the official platform of the Sworn Statement System for the Management of Conflicts of Interest, within a maximum period of fifteen (15) business days from the contract execution date.
