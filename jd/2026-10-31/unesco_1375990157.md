BACKGROUND

The Women for Bees initiative, implemented by UNESCO's MAB Programme, empowers women beekeepers in biosphere reserves globally through training, networking, and research. Its first phase trained women across nine countries (Bulgaria, Cambodia, China, France, India, Peru, the Russian Federation, Rwanda and Slovenia) and yielded knowledge products as well as a consolidated network of beekeepers. A second phase of the project is now being launched.

PURPOSE OF THE ASSIGNMENT

We are seeking a Community Manager Consultant to carry the project's knowledge products through their final stages and to maintain the momentum of the women beekeepers' network during the second phase. The assignment is structured around two flagship deliverables: voluntary guidelines on sustainable beekeeping in biosphere reserves, and international beekeeping masterclass for project participants. It also includes follow-up with local partners and continuity of community engagement across project sites.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES

Key responsibilities:
