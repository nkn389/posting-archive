Inspired by the dynamics of sailboat racing and the urgency for climate action, 11th Hour Racing is committed to the health and resilience of ocean systems. Supporting innovative, sustainable solutions in the maritime industry and beyond through sport, storytelling and grant-making, 11th Hour Racing works to build healthy planetary systems and strong communities.

About the Role

As Grant Program Associate, you’ll help shape how 11th Hour Racing opens doors to the ocean, from expanding gender equity and leadership in sailing to building more equitable pathways from education into ocean science, marine trades, and related careers. You’ll work closely with grantees and partners across our focus areas, help turn strategy into funded projects, and see that work up close through site visits and events. This is a hands-on role with real opportunity to influence our impact on ocean health.

Key responsibilities for this role include the following:

\n

Strategy

-
Help develop and refine 11th Hour Racing’s Grant Program strategy with the President and Senior Grant Program Manager, with a particular focus on Equity & Access and Experiential Education & Ocean Career Pathways. This includes articulating and evolving a theory of change, logic model, tactics, measurable outcomes, and learning agendas.

-
Stay current on and share developments in gender equity in sailing and ocean education-to-career pathways, as well as other focus areas such as ocean literacy, coastal restoration, marine industry sustainability, and soil and water health.

-
Present grantmaking recommendations and strategy to 11th Hour Racing leadership and the Schmidt Family Foundation Board.

Grantmaking

-
Identify grantmaking opportunities with potential for transformational change and develop recommendations to achieve our strategic objectives, such as expanding gender equity and leadership in sailing and building stronger, more equitable education-to-career pathways into ocean science, marine trades, and related fields.

-
Solicit, review, and analyze grant proposals and evaluate final reports.

-
Evaluate partner and grantee organizations, including through site visits.

-
Work closely with grants administration and finance to ensure grants are processed in a timely, accurate, and compliant way.

-
Maintain grant files and manage grant information in our online database.

Partnerships & Representation

-
Represent 11th Hour Racing at conferences, events, and meetings.

-
Build strategic relationships with other foundations and organizations.

-
Collaborate across Sponsorships, Ambassadors, and Communications teams to connect grantees’ work with the organization’s broader initiatives.

Communications

-
Develop and coordinate grantee stories and program content for our website, blog, and social media.

Other projects and activities as assigned by the Senior Grant Program Manager.
