The Consultant services are required to support implementation of activities within variety of projects focused on the repairs, reconstruction, overhaul or construction of the selected infrastructure objects.
The project “Hromada Reconstruction and Empowerment Facility” (HREF) is implemented on the basis of the Agreement concluded between the International Organization for Migration (IOM) and the Credit Institution for Reconstruction (KfW) on 19 December 2023 (BMZ-No 2023 679 69) with the aim to improve the economic independence and thus the living conditions of internally displaced persons and population groups affected by conflict with high economic vulnerability.
In order to achieve greater effectiveness by interlinking the various components of the programme, activities are implemented focusing on two oblasts: Mykolaiv and Vinnytsia, which are benefitting also from other projects funded by German government through KfW. These components include improving the infrastructure within businesses, investing in economically relevant local infrastructure and setting up a network of regional advisory centers (Hubs and sub-Hubs).
The Consultant will be responsible for technical assessments, preparing scope of required works, monitoring repairs and rehabilitations of selected buildings, in coordination with relevant colleagues, partners and municipalities.
Also, if required, the consultant might support activities related to repairs and refurbishment in collective centres or damaged individual residencies and multi-apartment residential buildings.

Tasks to be performed under this contract

- Conduct field visits as necessary to assess and document repair requirements, including but not limited to, for communal infrastructure, social institutions, collective centers, individual residencies and multi-apartment residential buildings.

- Draft Bills of Quantities and Bills of Materials and Equipment, necessary engineering drawings taking into consideration national legislation and following IOM standards.

- Assist the Senior Project Associate with the coordination with Logistics and Procurement Unit on the procurement process by reviewing or preparing construction-related procurement documents.

- Conduct quality, timeline and cost control and document progress of works on-site. Navigate changes and correction where necessary and needed. Document any changes from original designs and plans and coordinate necessary actions closely with the programme management and procurement units.

- Monitor implementation of construction activities at field, ensuring compliance with safety measures and donor visibility requirements, in order to track progress and report potential gaps/delays in the implementation to the Project Officer (Shelter).

- Work closely with relevant internal units to support that Housing Land and Property issues are considered and integrated in the unit’s response during all phases of the project.

- Support administrative coordination of project implementation, involving liaison with diverse organizational units and external parties to initiate requests, obtain necessary clearances, process and follow-up on administrative actions, etc.
