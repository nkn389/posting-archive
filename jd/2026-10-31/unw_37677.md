Background:

UN Women, grounded in the vision of equality enshrined in the Charter of the United Nations, works for the elimination of discrimination against women and girls; the empowerment of women; and the achievement of equality between women and men as partners and beneficiaries of development, human rights, humanitarian action and peace and security. UN Women has been in Afghanistan for two decades. UN Women’s programming approach is informed by analysis of the political, economic and humanitarian situation, risks to and capacities of partner organizations, and needs of Afghan women and girls. UN Women Afghanistan currently has five key programme priorities: (1) Integrated Programming and Localization, (2) Women’s movement building through core grant making; 3) Policy and Research; 4) Economic Resilience and Private Sector and 5) Coordination, Access and Humanitarian Response. UN Women Afghanistan operates through its main office in Kabul and five sub-offices located in the provinces of Balkh, Bamyan, Herat, Kandahar and Nangarhar.

UN Women Afghanistan Country Office operates in a highly constrained and rapidly evolving context. The Country Office’s programming is shaped by political, economic, humanitarian, and protection-related developments, as well as by the capacities and risks facing women’s organizations and implementing partners. Within this setting, the Integrated services portfolio plays a critical role in supporting women and girls through EVAW programming, essential protection services and humanitarian programming, coordination with civil society organizations, and quality oversight of implementing partners.

Following recent organizational restructuring and funding reductions, the Integrated Services team requires short-term coordination support for six months. The assignment will prioritize closure of partner agreements, onboarding and implementation readiness of new partners, coordination of key thematic initiatives including the 16 Days of Activism, technical follow-up on gender-based violence (GBV) and psychosocial support (PSS), and programme quality assessments. The support will help maintain business continuity and ensure timely, compliant, and results-focused delivery. The objective of the consultancy is to provide time-bound programme coordination and technical support to the Integrated Programming and Localization Unit, with particular attention to partner closure and onboarding, thematic coordination, programme quality assurance, and completion of priority programme management actions.

Description of Responsibilities /Scope of Work

Under the direct supervision of the Integrated Programming and Localization Programme Manager, and in close coordination with relevant programme, operations, Sub-Office, and partner personnel, the Programme Coordinator will:

1- Provide Advisory Support and Technical Programme Guidance for the Integrated Programme Unit

- Lead and coordinate effective project management and programme implementation, including technical advice to Sub-Offices for appropriate and efficient partner management to ensure smooth workflow and processes for results-based management;

- Manage the monitoring of project implementation, including coordination with Sub-Offices on all integrated programming initiatives to ensure adherence to implementation rates and utilization of resources to meet agreed upon targets and results;

- Lead the design of the Localization Workflow

- Analyze and ensure alignment of project documents and budgets with the Integrated Programme Unit’s workplan and overall objectives;

- Provide technical advice on project planning, implementation and management and ensure quality assurance at Sub-Office levels.

- Supports the development of the Integrated Programming Strategy

- Facilitate effective information flow, communication and coordination across the unit and between Sub-Offices and other ACO units and pillars; and

2- Manage capacity development to project/programme partners

- Provide technical support and advisory services to responsible parties and civil society partners on programme implementation, compliance, and quality standards.

- Design and deliver targeted coaching, mentoring, and capacity-building support to partners in areas such as results-based management, reporting, service quality, safeguarding, and survivor-centered programming.

- Support the design and rollout of calls for proposals, partner selection processes, partner onboarding, and implementation readiness.

- Conduct performance assessment of the current Programme partners to inform partner prioritization and selection for 2027

- Advise on engagement with women-led organizations, women activists, shelters, and relevant coordination platforms, including the Afghan Shelter Network where relevant.

3- Coordinate partner agreement closure and onboarding

- Lead the review of partner agreements due for closure and establish priorities, timelines and accountabilities for completing all 2026 outstanding actions.

- Oversee the completion of narrative, financial, programmatic, compliance, asset, refund and documentation requirements for partner agreement closure.

- Analyze closure bottlenecks and risks and recommend timely corrective or escalation measures.

- Coordinate closure actions across programme, finance, operations, Sub-Offices and responsible partners and ensure that decisions and follow-up actions are properly documented.

- Manage the programmatic onboarding of newly selected responsible partners and amendments, including orientation, risk review, workplan finalization, compliance readiness and implementation planning for Q4 2026 and Q1 2027.

4- Coordinate thematic programme priorities

- Lead the planning and coordination of the 16 Days of Activism across programme teams, Sub-Offices and responsible partners, ensuring coherence, timely implementation and consolidated reporting.

- Facilitate collaboration among UN Women teams, responsible partners and relevant stakeholders to strengthen coherence across thematic interventions.

- Provide technical oversight and coordination for GBV and PSS interventions, including essential services, referral pathways, safeguarding and survivor-centred approaches.

- Identify thematic implementation gaps, emerging risks and opportunities and recommend appropriate programmatic responses.

- Represent the Integrated Programming and Localization Unit in relevant technical discussions and coordination mechanisms, including GBV, protection and mental health and psychosocial support forums, as delegated by the Programme Manager.

5- Manage knowledge building efforts

- Manage the process of identifying and synthesizing of best practices and lessons learned that are directly linked to the implementation of the Programme and contribute to their global dissemination.

- Finalize lessons learned and communication products on ACO work in prisons, Drug Treatment and Male engagement efforts

6- Strengthen monitoring, reporting, and programme coordination

- Monitor priority actions, results, risks, and delivery timelines and recommend timely corrective action where required.

- Review and Finalise the EU Report and supporting documents

- Prepare and consolidate briefing notes, progress summaries, management updates, presentations, donor inputs, and other programme documentation.

- Coordinate regular follow-up with programme teams, Sub-Offices, operations colleagues, and partners to resolve implementation issues and maintain a clear record of decisions and actions.

- Document lessons learned, good practices, recurring gaps, and recommendations to support programme improvement and handover.

7- Build partnerships and provide support in developing resource mobilization strategies

- Develop and implement partnerships and resource mobilization strategies;

- Lead coordination with Sub-Offices to support the development of a standard Integrated Programme Proposal to be adapted per various funding opportunities

- Analyze and research information on donors, finalize substantive briefs on possible areas of cooperation, identification of opportunities for resource mobilization and cost sharing;

- Determine programmatic areas of cooperation, based on strategic goals of UN Women, country needs and donors’ priorities and develop the relevant partnerships.
