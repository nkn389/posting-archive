Introduction
Established in 1951, IOM is a Related Organization of the United Nations and the leading UN agency in the field of migration. Working closely with governmental, intergovernmental and non-governmental partners, IOM promotes humane and orderly migration for the benefit of all. It saves lives and protects people on the move, drives solutions to displacement, and facilitates pathways for regular migration, while providing services and advice to governments and migrants.
IOM is committed to fostering a respectful, inclusive and supportive workplace where all employees can thrive professionally and feel valued. By creating such an environment, IOM aims to better harness the full potential of migration and strengthen its support to people on the move.

Organizational Context and Scope
Under the overall supervision of the Head of Programme Support Unit and direct supervision of the Programme Support Officer, the Monitoring and Evaluation (M&E) Associate will be responsible and accountable for providing technical and administrative support to the Programme Support Unit (PSU) and assist in strengthening monitoring, reporting, and results-based management across the International Organization for Migration (IOM) Lebanon’s programme portfolio. The incumbent will support the implementation and quality control of M&E processes in accordance with IOM institutional frameworks, including Results-Based Management (RBM), Project Information and Management Application (PRIMA), and donor reporting requirements. The M&E Associate will also maintain the Country Office (CO)-wide M&E systems, tools, and processes, working closely with designated M&E focal points across programme units to strengthen monitoring practices, data quality, accountability, and results reporting. The M&E Associate will work in close coordination with Programme Managers (PMs), M&E focal points, and thematic units to support monitoring activities, reporting consolidation, indicator harmonisation, capacity-building efforts, and compliance with donor and institutional requirements.
