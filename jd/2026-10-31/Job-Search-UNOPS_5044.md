About the Region

The UNOPS Global Portfolios Office (GPO) brings together diverse expertise to help partners deliver impact worldwide. With hubs in New York, Geneva, and Vienna, and expert teams operating globally, GPO leads multi-regional initiatives that advance sustainable development, climate action, and peacebuilding - including in some of the world’s most challenging environments. By leveraging our collective expertise and global networks, GPO supports UNOPS’ strategic priorities and the Sustainable Development Goals across more than 130 countries. We work closely with major global partners - including governments, international financial institutions, and UN agencies - to deliver a wide range of services, such as project management, fund management, hosting services, and HR support.

About the Country/Multi-Country Office

The Water, Environment and Climate (WEC) portfolio, based in Vienna, and with offices based globally is part of the UNOPS Global Portfolios Office. WEC has built strong partnerships and is effectively managing a portfolio of over 500 million USD over the last 15 years to support key initiatives with fund management, project implementation and administrative support.

WEC effectively operationalizes partners' agendas with global approaches, as well as regional and country specific activities focused on climate action, protection and conservation of the environment. Partners profit from WEC’s ability to operationalize and/or scale up their important substantive agendas, including in support of key multilateral environmental and climate agreements, such as the Paris Agreement, the Cartagena Convention as well as the Sustainable Development Goals.

Role Purpose

The role of the Advisor, Portfolio Management for WEC is a home-based, retainer role. Under the guidance and supervision of the Head of WEC Portfolio, and in close collaboration with the WEC Portfolio Management Team, the Advisor, Portfolio Management will support WEC’s portfolio delivery performance and growth ambitions. The incumbent translates evolving climate action trends and partner needs into practical operational approaches, identifying synergies, replicable models, and new service lines to ensure WEC’s growth aligns with UNOPS, GPO, and WEC strategic objectives.

Functions / Key Results Expected

The Advisor, Portfolio Management is responsible for four key functional areas:

- Strategic Portfolio Development and Positioning

- Concept Note and Proposal Development

- Project Set-up and Operationalization

- Knowledge Management and Capacity Building

Strategic Portfolio Development and Positioning:

- Evaluate global climate finance, policy, and programming trends to identify business growth opportunities and strategic partnership avenues.

- Translate analytical insights into practical operational approaches, value propositions, and innovative service offerings for partner countries, cities, donors, and multilateral funds.

- Articulate WEC’s value proposition and strategic positioning within the broader climate action and environmental landscape.

- Support active promotion of WEC’s grant management, fund support, and implementation services to existing and prospective donors and partners.

Concept Note and Proposal Development:

- Identify and pursue high-potential opportunities with new and existing partners, leveraging WEC’s existing portfolio, partnerships, and track record.

- Lead and support the development of compelling, high-quality concept notes and proposals aligned with UNOPS, GPO and WEC strategic objectives.

- Ensure proposals adhere to UNOPS policy frameworks and standards while maintaining high feasibility, sustainability and operational standards.

- Collaborate across WEC and GPO portfolio and project teams to mobilize relevant technical expertise during project design.

Project Set-up and Operationalization:

- Guide project teams through the formal setup and initiation phase in full adherence to UNOPS operational policies and procedures.

- Provide advisory support on project setup, including governance structures, recruitment strategies, and procurement processes to ensure transparency and fairness.

- Support the development of implementation tools, including detailed work plans, budgets, risk logs, and monitoring and evaluation (M&E) frameworks.

- Assist in establishing strong working relationships with partners, donors, host governments, and key stakeholders to facilitate smooth implementation.

Knowledge Management and Capacity Building:

- Extract lessons learned, data, and insights from existing projects to identify cross-portfolio synergies and opportunities for scaling and replication.

- Contribute to continuous improvement by capturing operational feedback to refine UNOPS tools, guidelines, and corporate frameworks.

- Develop knowledge products and deliver advisory sessions/trainings to strengthen capacity across WEC portfolio teams.

- Connect project teams with broader UNOPS organizational networks, publications, and external climate sector best practices.
