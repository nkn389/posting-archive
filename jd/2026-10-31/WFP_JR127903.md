The Nutrition Coordinator will support the Resident Coordinator and the Government of Lesotho in accelerating implementation of the national nutrition agenda by strengthening coordination, resource mobilization, stakeholder engagement, and accountability mechanisms across sectors and levels of governance.The Nutrition Coordinator will be based in Maseru and under the supervision of the Resident Coordinator.
Responsibilities:

- Provide strategic advice to the Resident Coordinator on nutrition priorities, policies, and interventions to ensure nutrition is effectively integrated into national development processes, UN programming, and implementation of the Food and Nutrition Strategic Framework (2026-2031) and Lesotho's Nutrition for Growth (N4G) commitments.
- Strengthen multisectoral coordination under the Scaling Up Nutrition (SUN) Movement, including engagement with government coordination mechanisms, the UN Nutrition Network, development partners, civil society, academia, and private sector platforms.
- Support the integration and operationalization of the Chiefs' nutrition commitments within the Food and Nutrition Strategic Framework (2026-2031) and N4G commitments.
- Facilitate resource mobilization and strategic partnerships to advance implementation of nutrition priorities and commitments.
- Promote constructive engagement between public and private sector stakeholders to support nutrition-sensitive investments and partnerships.
- Support evidence-based advocacy by documenting, disseminating, and promoting good practices, lessons learned, and success stories, while developing strategic communication, advocacy, and resource mobilization materials.
Accountabilities/Responsibilities
The incumbent will:
Support coordination and Partnership Building

- Support the Resident Coordinator in reactivating and operationalizing SUN platforms, including the UN, civil society, donor, private sector, and academia networks.
- Strengthen multisectoral coordination by working closely with FNCO and facilitating collaboration among government institutions, traditional leadership structures, UN agencies, development partners, civil society organizations, academia, and the private sector.
- Establish and maintain effective coordination mechanisms between the Resident Coordinator's Office, FNCO, SUN platforms, and other key stakeholders to ensure alignment around national nutrition priorities.
- Develop and maintain a stakeholder coordination and monitoring system to track commitments, follow-up actions, partnership performance, and implementation progress.
Support to Traditional Leadership Engagement

- Support engagement between the Resident Coordinator, the College of Chiefs, and relevant stakeholders on nutrition priorities.
- Organize quarterly coordination meetings with the College of Chiefs, including preparation of agendas, briefing materials, meeting documentation, and follow-up actions.
- Facilitate linkages between Chiefs and SUN platforms to mobilize technical and financial support for implementation of Chiefs' commitments.
- Collaborate with UN agencies and government partners to document and disseminate successful examples of Chiefs serving as nutrition champions.
- Work with the UN Nutrition Network and relevant government technical working groups to ensure Chiefs receive appropriate technical guidance and advocacy support.
Resource Mobilization and Advocacy

- Support the Resident Coordinator in convening development partner and private sector forums to mobilize financial and technical resources for implementation of the Food and Nutrition Strategic Framework and N4G commitments.
- Prepare resource mobilization and advocacy materials, including concept notes, funding proposals, briefing notes, donor presentations, and partnership documents.
- Support strategic engagement with development partners, IFIs, philanthropic organizations, and private sector actors to increase investment in nutrition.
Communication, Learning and Youth Engagement

- Support the documentation and dissemination of lessons learned, success stories, and good practices related to nutrition leadership and community action.
- Support the Resident Coordinator in leveraging existing platforms, including Sebabatso, to promote youth engagement and participation in nutrition initiatives.
- Develop advocacy and communication materials that strengthen public awareness and promote nutrition-sensitive action.
Reporting and Advisory Support

- Provide periodic briefings and analytical updates to the Resident Coordinator on progress, opportunities, risks, and emerging issues related to nutrition coordination and stakeholder engagement.
- Produce regular coordination monitoring reports highlighting achievements, bottlenecks, stakeholder commitments, and recommendations for corrective action.
- Perform any other duties as assigned by the Resident Coordinator.
Deliverables

- A detailed annual workplan, including key performance indicators (KPIs), milestones, outputs, and expected outcomes, developed within the first month of assignment and approved by the Resident Coordinator.
- SUN platforms (UN, civil society, donor, private sector, and academia) reactivated and operational, with regular meetings convened and action points monitored.
- Effective coordination mechanism established and functioning between the Resident Coordinator's Office, FNCO, SUN platforms, and key nutrition stakeholders.
- Stakeholder consultation and validation processes facilitated, with feedback incorporated into implementation plans and stakeholder engagement strategies.
- Quarterly coordination meetings with the College of Chiefs convened, documented, and followed up.
- Development partner and private sector engagement forums convened to support nutrition financing and partnership development.
- Resource mobilization and advocacy materials, including concept notes, briefing papers, partnership proposals, and funding submissions, developed and disseminated.
- Advocacy and communication materials developed to support nutrition awareness, youth engagement, and stakeholder mobilization.
- Case studies, success stories, and knowledge products documenting nutrition leadership and community action produced and disseminated.
- Regular briefing notes and progress reports prepared for the Resident Coordinator.
- Coordination monitoring reports produced, highlighting implementation progress, stakeholder commitments, challenges, lessons learned, and recommendations.
QUALIFICATIONS & EXPERIENCE REQUIRED
Advanced degree in Business/Public Management, Social policy, Public Health, Nutrition, Economics, Development Studies, or related field or First University degree with additional years of related work experience and/or training/courses.
Knowledge & Skills: Minimum 7 years’ experience in multi-sector coordination, policy advocacy, or programme management with proven experience working with traditional leadership structures and government institutions. Good planning and project management skills coupled with strong facilitation, negotiation, and communication skills. Familiarity with UN coordination mechanisms and nutrition governance frameworks.
