Unit Context

IT People Engagement & Development (ITSPE), within ITS Business Administration, strengthens the workforce, skills, leadership, engagement, and Agile capabilities needed for ITS to deliver business priorities and digital transformation. The unit leads technology learning, workforce and skills planning, job and career architecture, employee engagement, and Agile culture enablement.

Duties and Accountabilities

The Extended Term Consultant will work as an established individual contributor. With guidance, the Analyst will conduct research and analysis on difficult but well-defined assignments, prepare written outputs, coordinate assigned workstreams, and recommend practical solutions.

• Analyze workforce, skills, learning, and engagement data to identify trends, gaps, risks, and opportunities, and translate findings into clear recommendations.
• Support the implementation and ongoing validation of the ITS job architecture, including role mapping, documentation, benchmarking, stakeholder input, and alignment with grade-level expectations.
• Maintain the ITS Job Architecture online presence and supporting digital resources, ensuring role profiles, frameworks, guidance, reference materials, and related webpages remain current, accurate, well-organized, and accessible. Coordinate updates and version control, identify content requiring refresh, and support improvements to the digital experience so managers and staff can easily navigate and use Job Architecture resources.
• Coordinate the IT Job Architecture Governance Group and related governance activities, including scheduling meetings, developing agendas and supporting materials, coordinating inputs from stakeholders, documenting decisions and actions, and tracking follow-ups through completion. Maintain governance records and decision logs, and support the timely review and resolution of Job Architecture questions, proposed changes, and implementation issues.
• Support the development, administration, and analysis of skills assessments and inventories, and connect findings to workforce planning and role-based learning priorities.
• Prepare Job Architecture and Skills Assessment related decision-ready reports, dashboards, presentations, and briefing materials as needed.
• Assist in the facilitation of workshops, training sessions, and programs to promote professional growth and skill enhancement.
• Implement opportunities for employee learning and development, supporting career growth and skill enhancement through innovative programs.
• Coordinate defined workforce development and engagement initiatives, including work plans, milestones, stakeholder consultations, workshops, communications, and action tracking.
• Analyze program metrics and employee feedback to assess effectiveness, identify improvements, and support employee engagement, retention, and development initiatives.
• Partner with HR and IT teams to optimize workforce structures and resourcing models.
• Apply Agile ways of working and digital tools, including Microsoft 365, Power BI, Azure DevOps, and Microsoft Copilot, to strengthen collaboration, transparency, analysis, and delivery.
• Other duties and responsibilities may be assigned based on business needs.

Selection Criteria
• Bachelor's or Master's degree in human resources, organizational development, business administration, IT management, data analytics, learning and development, or a related field with 2 years of experience or equivalent combination of education and experience (for example, in the IT field: Bachelor's Degree with a minimum of 1 year of related work experience).
• At least two years of relevant professional experience, or an equivalent combination of education and experience, in workforce development, talent, learning, engagement, organizational development, or workforce analytics.
• Demonstrated analytical reasoning, including the ability to structure research, interpret quantitative and qualitative data, identify issues, and recommend practical solutions.
• Experience preparing concise reports, dashboards, presentations, and other written outputs for management and stakeholder audiences.
• Working knowledge of job architecture, skills frameworks, learning and development, employee engagement, or organizational performance measurement.
• Proficiency in Microsoft Excel and PowerPoint; experience with Power BI, Azure DevOps, Microsoft Copilot, or comparable tools is desirable.
• Strong communication, project-support, collaboration, organization, and follow-through skills, with the ability to manage varied assignments in a geographically distributed environment.
