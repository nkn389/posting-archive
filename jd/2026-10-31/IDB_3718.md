About this position

The Transport Division (TSP) is seeking a professional with experience in the preparation and execution of projects and knowledge products, with particular emphasis on the transport sector.

You will work in the Transport Division, which is part of the Infrastructure Department (INE). The team leads the promotion of public policies, the financing of transport projects, and the provision of technical assistance across the region’s transport sector. The Division also contributes through its strategic areas of focus: (i) institutional transformation; (ii) user-centered public transport; (iii) resilience and green infrastructure; (iv) logistics and regional integration; and (v) enabling private investment.

These guidelines also take into account each country's specific characteristics, the importance of regional integration, gender dimensions, and the environmental, social, and fiscal sustainability of its interventions, while emphasizing the importance of increasing public investment and private-sector participation in infrastructure financing. This contributes to expanding available knowledge on transportation and to the preparation and execution of transportation projects in Latin America and the Caribbean (LAC). Research topics are related to the Division's strategic activities.

What you’ll do

- Support the preparation of sector working documents, including the Project Profile (PP), Proposal for Operation Development (POD), and Loan Proposal (LP), together with their respective annexes, and assist in monitoring and supporting the loan preparation process.

- Review the documents listed above to ensure the accuracy, completeness, and quality of the final product and the proper application of relevant guidelines. Update project-related corporate databases and their corresponding schedules.

- Support the preparation of and participation in missions, including their Terms of Reference, the preparation of memoranda, and the distribution of communications.

- Contribute to the preparation of technical cooperation documents and their corresponding annexes, as well as Project Completion Reports.

- Provide technical, logistical, and administrative support for the organization of seminars, conferences, and meetings.

- Conduct literature reviews in transport and related fields by searching for, analyzing, and selecting articles published in scientific journals, public- and private-sector reports, publications from international organizations, and other sources of knowledge in the sector.

- Prepare inputs and materials for knowledge activities and support their dissemination.

- Participate in a series of operations and activities to identify new business opportunities in the private sector and support the preparation of presentation materials.

- Contribute to the TSP Management Team on activities related with internal and external events, in coordination with other multilaterals and different teams.

What you'll need

- Education: Bachelor’s degree in civil engineering, architecture, finance, economics, or another related field relevant to the responsibilities of the role.

- Experience: Between 2 and 4 years of progressive experience.

- Languages: Proficiency in Spanish and English, both oral and written, is required.
