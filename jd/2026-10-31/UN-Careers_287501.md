Org. Setting and Reporting
The United Nations Office for Outer Space Affairs (UNOOSA) is the United Nations office responsible for promoting international cooperation in the peaceful uses of outer space. UNOOSA manages and implements the programme on the Peaceful Uses of Outer Space, which focusses on strengthening international cooperation in the exploration and peaceful uses of outer space and advancing the use of space science and technology for achieving sustainable development, including through the Programme on Space Applications and the United Nations Platform for Space-based Information for Disaster Management and Emergency Response (UN-SPIDER), both implemented by the Space Applications Section (SAS) within UNOOSA. This post is located within the Space Applications Section and based in Vienna. The Geospatial Information Officer (Earth observation and Water Monitoring) works under the direct supervision of the Chief of the Space Applications Section, and under the overall guidance of the Director of UNOOSA. For more information on UNOOSA, please visit the following website: www.unoosa.org

Responsibilities
Within delegated authority, the incumbent will be responsible for the following specific duties: - Support management and planning for the implementation of Component 2 of the Central Asia Water and Land Nexus (CAWLN) Programme, through the development and delivery of Earth Observation and geospatial services for water accounting, agricultural monitoring, land degradation, drought and flood risk, and related nexus analytics across the Amu Darya and Syr Darya basins. - Coordinate the Space4Water initiative on an interim basis, ensuring that methodologies, training resources, and case studies from CAWLN Component 2 are integrated into the Space4Water Portal, disseminated to the global community of practice, and represented in international fora. - Supervise and coordinate the work of staff and consultants under those projects, ensuring compliance with United Nations administrative and financial rules and procedures. - Provide expert advice to Member States, regional institutions (such as the Scientific-Information Center of the Interstate Commission for Water Coordination in Central Asia (SIC ICWC)), and international partners (including the Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO) and the United Nations Global Service Centre (UNGSC)) on the operational integration of Earth Observation (EO)-based services into policy frameworks and decision-making processes related to water and land management. - Ensure synergies and inter-agency coordination, working closely with other CAWLN components, UNOOSA sections, and relevant United Nations entities to mainstream EO applications into wider food security, disaster risk reduction, environmental monitoring, and sustainable development agendas. - Prepare and deliver written outputs, including analytical reports, donor submissions, inputs to CAWLN governance mechanisms, United Nations Committee on the Peaceful Uses of Outer Space (COPUOS) documentation, and other publications. - Represent UNOOSA at high-level meetings, expert groups, and conferences in Central Asia and internationally, liaising with government ministries, United Nations system entities, and international organizations on matters related to EO and sustainable natural resource management. - Collect and analyze data to identify trends or patterns and provide insights through graphs, charts, tables and reports using data visualization methods to enable data-driven planning, decision-making, presentation and reporting. - Perform other work-related duties as required.

A first-level university degree in similar fields in combination with two additional years of qualifying experience may be accepted in lieu of the advanced university degree.
