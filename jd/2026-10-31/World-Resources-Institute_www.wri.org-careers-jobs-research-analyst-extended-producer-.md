Program Overview
World Resources Institute (WRI) Indonesia is an independent, nonprofit research organization that turns big ideas into action at the nexus of environment, economic opportunity, and human well-being. WRI Indonesia works with government, businesses, civil society, research institutions, and development partners to advance evidence-based policies and solutions for sustainable development.

Through its work on plastic pollution and the circular economy, WRI Indonesia supports Indonesia and relevant stakeholders in strengthening policies, institutions, and implementation mechanisms to reduce plastic pollution and promote a more inclusive and sustainable packaging system. Mandatory Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) is an important policy instrument for strengthening producers' responsibility for the post-consumer management of packaging. Effective implementation requires an enabling ecosystem that connects packaging design, recycled-content markets, alternative materials, reuse and refill systems, financing mechanisms, waste management infrastructure, and inclusive participation across the value chain.

Job Highlight
The Research Analyst will support the delivery of a research package to strengthen mandatory Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) for packaging in Indonesia. The role will contribute to data collection, analysis, and drafting across three interconnected research workstreams:

- Sustainable packaging ecosystem and governance: Assessment of recycled-content markets, alternative packaging materials, and reuse/refill systems, including recommendations for policy interventions, implementation roadmaps, and traceability frameworks.
- Strengthening mandatory EPR for packaging: Assessment of policy scenarios and their implications for EPR costs, contributions from non-obliged companies, greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions, and eco-modulation mechanisms.
- Equity and just transition: Analysis of formal and informal sector value chains, socioeconomic and employment impacts, social protection, and gender equality, disability, and social inclusion (GEDSI), with recommendations for an inclusive transition (including GEDSI guideline for EPR implementation).

What You Will Do
Research, Data Analysis, and Writing (75%)

- Conduct literature reviews, policy and regulatory analysis on EPR for packaging, recycled-content policies, eco-modulation, traceability, reuse/refill, and just transition.
- Collect, clean, and manage primary and secondary data on packaging placed on the market, waste generation, collection and recycling rates, recycled-resin prices and offtake, and waste management costs. Maintain a clear log of data sources, assumptions, and limitations.
- Develop and run spreadsheet-based models and scenario calculations for EPR costs, recycled-content targets, and GHG emissions (e.g., material flow analysis, emission factors, cost-per-tonne estimates) under the direction of the Managers and Senior Research Lead.
- Map packaging and recycling value chains, including producers, producer responsibility organizations, recyclers, aggregators (lapak/pelapak), waste banks (bank sampah), and informal waste workers.
- Support the design and test data collection tools (e.g. interview guides, survey questionnaires, FGD protocols), and conduct interviews, field visits, and focus group discussions, collecting sex- and disability-disaggregated data where relevant.
- Analyze qualitative and quantitative data, and develop tables, charts, maps, and infographics to communicate findings clearly.
- Draft assigned sections, annexes, and summaries for three technical knowledge products, incorporating feedback from Senior Research Lead, peer reviewers, and donor representative.

Stakeholder Engagement, Coordination, and Administrative Support (25%)

- Coordinate logistic for technical consultations, interviews, workshops, and validation meetings with government ministries and agencies, private sector representatives, producer responsibility organizations, industry associations, recyclers, civil society organizations, research institutions, and informal sector representatives.
- Prepare background notes, meeting materials, and presentations, and documenting consultation findings and agreed follow-up actions.
- Maintain organized stakeholder contact list, consultation records, and research files in accordance with WRI’s data management and research ethics procedures.
- Support the preparation of policy briefs, slide decks, blogs, and other short products that share research findings with government, donors, and the public.
- Support work planning, progress tracking, and donor reporting for the research package.

Deliverables
The Research Analyst will contribute to the timely, high-quality delivery of the three technical knowledge products below, working under the technical lead of the Senior Research Lead.
1. Deliverable 1: Draft of Technical Report on Building the Ecosystem and Governance for Sustainable Packaging
The report will assess and recommend ways to build a sustainable packaging ecosystem in Indonesia, covering demand for recycled content, recycled-content targets, traceability, and ecomodulation framework. The Research Analyst will support it by:

- Supporting research and analysis on recycled content target-setting and scenario, traceability, and ecomodulation frameworks.
- General support in data collection and research writing
2. Deliverable 2: Draft of Technical Paper on Strengthening Mandatory EPR for Packaging.
The paper will assess policy scenarios for mandatory EPR and their effects on system costs, contributions from non-obliged companies, GHG emissions, and eco-modulation. The Research Analyst will support it by:

- Supporting EPR for packaging research through cost data collection and scenario analysis, profiling non-obliged companies and developing their contribution mechanisms, assessing GHG impacts and alignment with national and corporate climate targets.
3. Deliverable 3: Draft of Technical Paper on Equity and Just Transition in Mandatory EPR for Packaging
The paper will set out a pathway toward a just and inclusive EPR system, including GEDSI guidelines. The Research Analyst will support it by:

- Supporting interviews and focus group discussions with various stakeholders

For all deliverables, the Research Analyst will also provide the following supporting outputs:

- A shared, well-organised evidence base: literature review matrix, datasets, and model files with a documented assumptions log.
- Interview, consultation, and validation notes in an agreed format.
- Charts, tables, infographics, and slide decks for presentations and dissemination.
- Short monthly progress updates to the Research Lead.
