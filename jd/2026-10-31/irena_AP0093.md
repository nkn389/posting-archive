Background

The International Renewable Energy Agency (IRENA) is an inter-governmental organisation headquartered in Abu Dhabi, mandated to promote the widespread and increased adoption and sustainable use of all forms of renewable energy in the pursuit of sustainable development, energy access, energy security and low-carbon economic growth and prosperity. IRENA’s mission is to play a leading role in the ongoing transformation of the global energy systems as a centre of excellence for knowledge and innovation, a global voice of renewable energy, a network hub for all stakeholders and a source of advice and support for countries. At present, IRENA has 173 Members (172 States and the European Union) that acceded to its Statute.

Within this mandate, the focus of the Knowledge, Policy and Finance Centre (KPFC) is on collecting data, developing knowledge platforms and conducting analysis to support investment and growth in renewables including the creation of enabling environments for private investments. This entails analytical work on policy and finance, developing renewable energy statistics, giving input to advisory services that the Agency provides to its member states and at regional levels, and targeted engagement with the private sector and civil society.

The purpose of the Associate Professional Programme is to enable junior talented and promising university graduates and postgraduates to gain first-hand experience in the work of IRENA and a deeper insight into the role of the Agency in promoting the widespread and increased adoption and sustainable use of all forms of renewable energy. The programme offers valuable exposure to IRENA’s main activities and programmatic areas. The Agency will also benefit from gaining new and motivated members of staff, bringing new skills and perspectives.

Duties and Responsibilities

The post will be based in Abu Dhabi, in the Renewable Energy Markets Unit of KPFC. Under the supervision of the Team Lead, Renewable Energy Policy and Finance, the Associate Professional will:

- Support the unit in collecting information and analysing data on various aspects of energy transition finance covering renewables and other relevant technologies and infrastructure, including financing instruments and structures, financing trends across countries and regions and supply chain investments.

- Contribute data and analysis on energy transition finance for IRENA flagship publications such as the World Energy Transitions Outlook, Tracking the UAE consensus and SDG7 tracking;

- Assist in the organisation and execution of technical meetings, workshops, conferences and capacity building session, including major events like the IRENA General Assembly and Council meetings;

- Help prepare inputs for presentations and speeches by senior management;

- Help support other projects and workstreams in the division as required.
