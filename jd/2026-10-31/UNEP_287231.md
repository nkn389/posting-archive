Result of Service
The service will deliver a comprehensive communication strategy and production plan, and a set of audience-ready communication products, that effectively communicate the results and impact of the EAS project, the NBSAP/7NR project and the Unit’s wider portfolio under the Nature Action Thematic Programmes. In particular, the service will: (1) strengthen stakeholder engagement and messaging across countries, UNEP, UNDP, the CBD Secretariat, the GEF, donors and the public; (2) promote the dissemination of knowledge, best practices and project results in support of KMGBF implementation, including syntheses of country experience from NBSAP updates and national reports; (3) foster alignment, transparency and collaboration among implementing and executing partners; and (4) embed communication practice in how the Unit plans, implements and reports on its projects, so that results and stories are captured as they occur. Priorities across the EAS project, the NBSAP/7NR project and the wider Nature Action portfolio will be agreed with the supervisor and project teams in the communication strategy, based on communication needs, project milestones and available resources. For projects at or near completion, the emphasis will be on documenting results, lessons and country experience. The primary audiences are national focal points and project teams, the CBD Secretariat, the GEF Secretariat and implementing partners. The wider public is a secondary audience.

Duties and Responsibilities
Organizational Setting: The United Nations Environment Programme (UNEP) is the leading global environmental authority that sets the global environmental agenda, promotes the coherent implementation of the environmental dimension of sustainable development within the United Nations system and serves as an authoritative advocate for the global environment. Its mandate is to coordinate the development of environmental policy consensus by keeping the global environment under review and bringing emerging issues to the attention of governments and the international community for action. UNEP's Ecosystems Division works with international and national partners, providing technical assistance and capacity development for the implementation of environmental policy, and strengthening the environmental management capacity of developing countries and countries with economies in transition. UNEP’s Ecosystems Division drives conservation, restoration and sustainable use of ecosystems to achieve benefits for nature and people: today and future generations. The division works with Member States and partners to strengthen resilience by providing policy and technical support to catalyze sustainable social and economic transformation for biodiversity, people and peace. This position will support the work of the Nature Frameworks Support Unit (NFSU) in the Biodiversity and Land Branch, Ecosystems Division. NFSU supports countries in implementing the Kunming-Montreal Global Biodiversity Framework (KMGBF) through Global Environment Facility (GEF)-funded projects and manages a diverse portfolio under the Nature Action Thematic Programmes, and also supports countries in delivering their commitments under the Convention on Biological Diversity (CBD) and the UN Convention to Combat Desertification (UNCCD) as well as other Multilateral Environmental Agreements (e.g. CITES, CMS). The Unit serves as the technical/substantive lead in guiding UNEP delegations to relevant COPs and meetings. The Unit also liaises closely with UNEP’s Secretariat for the Kunming Biodiversity Fund, serving as the primary technical focal for in-house coordination of UNEP’s potential delivery under the fund.
