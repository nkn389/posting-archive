What we do

In collaboration with our Red Cross and Red Crescent partners around the world, we do everything we can to deliver life-saving aid, reconnect families and locate missing people – helping those who need it most, regardless of who they are and what side of the front line they are on.

Purpose

Warehouse Officer is responsible for the proper handling and storage of goods in the warehouse in accordance with Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs). S/he supervises and organizes the work of the daily workers when needed.

Accountabilities & functional responsibilities

Receives and checks incoming goods against the delivery note and reports any issues.

- Coordinates the replenishment of the stock with Zahle warehouse in a timely manner.

- Check with Beirut medical planner the replenishment of the medical items.

- Liaise with Beirut procurement team for any direct reception.

Ensures that goods are properly stored, picked, packed and loaded and reports any issues

- Report to the Logistician in charge any no conformity.

- Pick and pack according to the Supply Chain KPIs.

- Ensures that all the stored goods are properly labelled (expiry dates, batch numbers, manufacturer, etc.) and recorded.

- Weighs and tallies goods during loading and unloading. Generates waybill and packing list as per the best practices and SOPs.

Participates in inventory counts.

- Updates bin cards and stock cards when supervising an inventory transaction.

- Support the Supply Chain Coordinator and Logistician during the monthly and End of Year inventory count.

Keeps the facility's equipment and materials neat, clean and orderly and reports any issues.

- Daily check of the premises and report any issue to the Logistician in charge.
