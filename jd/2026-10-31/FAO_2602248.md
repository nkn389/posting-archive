Organizational Setting
The Agrifood Economics and Policy Division (ESA) conducts economic research and policy analysis to support the transformation to more efficient, inclusive, resilient, and sustainable agrifood systems for better production, better nutrition, a better environment, and a better life, leaving no one behind. ESA provides evidence-based support to national, regional, and global policy processes and initiatives related to monitoring and analysing food and agricultural policies, agribusiness and value chain development, rural transformation and poverty, food security and nutrition information and analysis, resilience, bioeconomy, and climate-smart agriculture. The division also leads the production of two FAO flagship publications: The State of Food and Agriculture (SOFA) and The State of Food Security and Nutrition in the World (SOFI) and provides core technical support for the FAO Global Roadmap.

Reporting Lines
The selected candidate will work under the direct supervision of the SOFI Technical Lead, and under overall supervision of the ESA Director.

Technical Focus
The incumbent will support the preparation of The State of Food Security and Nutrition in the World (SOFI) report through research, support to coordination, communication and data-management activities. The assignment will support dissemination and outreach for SOFI 2026 edition with contribution to the analytical groundwork for SOFI 2027 edition.

Tasks and responsibilities:
• Conduct systematic and targeted literature reviews relevant to the SOFI 2027 theme and analytical framework, covering food security, nutrition, healthy diets, agrifood systems, food prices, resilience and related topics.
• Prepare annotated bibliographies, literature summaries and evidence-synthesis notes, and help identify research gaps, emerging policy issues and potential analytical approaches for SOFI 2027.
• Support preparation of SOFI 2026 outreach and dissemination materials, including presentations, briefing notes, factsheets, talking points, web and social-media content.
• Compile feedback, media coverage and stakeholder-engagement information following the SOFI 2026 launch, and support coordination with FAO Communication Teams and partner agencies.
• Support SOFI Steering Committee and inter-agency coordination by preparing agendas, background notes, minutes and action trackers and following up on deadlines and deliverables.
• Assist in organizing virtual and in-person coordination meetings.
• Support compilation, cleaning, validation and management of datasets related to food security and nutrition indicators, including basic statistical processing and quality checks.
• Help prepare tables, figures, metadata and analytical supporting materials, and maintain organized and reproducible data repositories.
