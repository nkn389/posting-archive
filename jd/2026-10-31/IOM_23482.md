Organizational Context and Scope
Established in 1951, IOM is the leading inter-governmental organization in the field of migration and works closely with governmental, intergovernmental, and non-governmental partners. IOM is dedicated to promoting humane and orderly migration for the benefit of all. It does so by providing services and advice to governments and migrants.

Under the overall supervision of the Head of Supply Chain unit in Afghanistan and the direct supervision of the Supply Chain Officer -Logistics, the incumbent will be responsible and accountable for supporting the coordination of fleet management activities for IOM Mission in Kabul, Afghanistan measured by the below objectives

- Support safe and efficient fleet operations.

- Maintain accurate fleet records and reports.

- Coordinate vehicle maintenance and servicing.

- Monitor vehicle utilization, fuel, and compliance.

- Coordinate and monitor the day-to-day fleet operations of the office in accordance with IOM policies, procedures, security requirements and established fleet management standards.

- Receive, review and coordinate approved vehicle requests from programme and support units, prepare daily vehicle schedules, allocate vehicles and drivers, and ensure efficient utilisation of the fleet.

- Maintain and monitor vehicle logbooks, movement records, mileage, fuel consumption and other fleet-related documentation, ensuring accuracy, completeness and timely updating.

- Coordinate preventive and corrective vehicle maintenance, including servicing, inspections, repairs and follow-up with workshops and service providers to minimise vehicle downtime.

- Monitor the validity and renewal of vehicle registration, insurance, roadworthiness certificates and other statutory or organisational documentation and ensure that all vehicles remain compliant.

- Monitor fuel consumption and related costs, analyse consumption patterns and identify significant variances or irregularities for appropriate follow-up.

- Coordinate the reporting and documentation of vehicle accidents, breakdowns, damages and other fleet incidents and ensure that required follow-up actions are completed in accordance with established procedures.

- Assist with the procurement of vehicles, spare parts, repairs, maintenance and transportation services, including preparation of technical specifications, solicitation of quotations, bid analysis and provision of technical inputs during evaluations.

- Conduct market surveys and support the identification, evaluation and management of fleet-related suppliers, including workshops, vehicle rental companies, spare-parts suppliers and other service providers.

- Maintain complete and accurate physical and electronic fleet records and prepare periodic reports on vehicle utilisation, maintenance, fuel consumption, costs, availability and other fleet performance indicators.

- Support fleet planning and budgeting by providing information on vehicle requirements, operating and maintenance costs, replacement needs and disposal of vehicles in accordance with IOM procedures.

- Provide technical guidance and training to drivers, junior SCM staff and relevant office personnel on fleet management procedures, vehicle safety, documentation, proper vehicle use and compliance requirements.

- Perform other relevant supply chain duties as may be assigned.
