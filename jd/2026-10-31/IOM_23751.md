Introduction
Established in 1951, IOM is a Related Organization of the United Nations and the leading UN agency in the field of migration. Working closely with governmental, intergovernmental and non-governmental partners, IOM promotes humane and orderly migration for the benefit of all. It saves lives and protects people on the move, drives solutions to displacement, and facilitates pathways for regular migration, while providing services and advice to governments and migrants.
IOM is committed to fostering a respectful, inclusive and supportive workplace where all employees can thrive professionally and feel valued. By creating such an environment, IOM aims to better harness the full potential of migration and strengthen its support to people on the move.

Project Context and Scope
Under the overall supervision of the Chief of Mission (CoM) and the direct supervision of the Chief Migration Health Officer, the successful candidate will be responsible for the carrying out the following duties and responsibilities in relation to the Migration Health Assessment Centre (MHAC) in Banjul, The Gambia.

Migration Health Division

Tasks to be performed under this contract

- Conduct migration health assessments, including medical examinations, review of radiology and laboratory results, vaccinations, TB management, treatment, referrals, documentation, certification, and information transfer in accordance with IOM and resettlement-country requirements. Monthly activity reports submitted to CMHO.

- Assessed beneficiaries are correctly identified and have complete medical records entered into MiMOSA/eMedical and other applicable systems, with minimal errors requiring correction.

- Monitor and coordinate treatment and follow-up of TB and STI cases and provide support to HIV counselling activities. Monthly case management summary and treatment outcomes report submitted.

- Ensure implementation of vaccination and presumptive treatment programmes in accordance with technical instructions and protocols. Submit monthly monitoring report including coverage rates, stock utilization, and compliance observations.

- Conduct at least one quality review activity per month (e.g., audit, observation, case review, self-assessment). Develop, update, or implement MHAC SOPs and submit quarterly quality improvement recommendations to CMHO.

- Produce and submit monthly migration health statistics, trend analysis, and ad hoc reports as requested by the CMHO. Ensure accuracy and completeness of health assessment data.

- Ensure timely and accurate entry of migration health assessment data into MiMOSA and other required databases. Monthly data quality checks completed with corrective actions documented.

- Review expenditures against budget, identify variances, and submit recommendations for cost-effective solutions. Provide financial monitoring inputs and forecasts to support budget management.

- Coordinate procurement planning and monitor inventory levels for medical equipment, vaccines, medications, and medical supplies. Submit quarterly procurement and stock status reports and identify replenishment needs.

- Provide technical advice in close coordination with the direct technical supervisor and operational support to ensure efficient MHAC service delivery and contribute to special assignments or initiatives as requested by the CMHO. Summary of activities and outcomes included in periodic reports.

Performance indicators for the evaluation of results

- Timely migration health assessment cases completed within required service standards.

- Data accuracy and completeness in MiMOSA/eMedical records.

- 100% compliance with receiving-country technical instructions and IOM SOPs.

- Monthly reports submitted by agreed deadlines.

- TB treatment and referral cases monitored and documented according to protocol.

- Quality assurance reviews conducted according to agreed schedule with corrective actions tracked and documented.

- Timely submission of financial, procurement, and operational monitoring reports.
