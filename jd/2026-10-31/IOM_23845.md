Introduction
Established in 1951, IOM is a Related Organization of the United Nations and the leading UN agency in the field of migration. Working closely with governmental, intergovernmental and non-governmental partners, IOM promotes humane and orderly migration for the benefit of all. It saves lives and protects people on the move, drives solutions to displacement, and facilitates pathways for regular migration, while providing services and advice to governments and migrants.
IOM is committed to fostering a respectful, inclusive and supportive workplace where all employees can thrive professionally and feel valued. By creating such an environment, IOM aims to better harness the full potential of migration and strengthen its support to people on the move.

Project Context and Scope
IOM implements protection programming in Lebanon to strengthen access to protection services, address protection risks, and promote the rights and protection of vulnerable populations, including migrants and individuals at risk of exploitation, abuse, and trafficking. Engagement with governmental stakeholders is essential to strengthening policy dialogue, promoting evidence-based approaches, and supporting the development and implementation of protection-related policies and practices. Key thematic areas include counter-trafficking, regularization and access to legal status, social protection, and other migration and protection-related policy priorities. Under the overall supervision of the Protection Programme Manager, the consultancy will provide specialised, time-bound technical and policy support to agreed protection and migration governance priorities. The Consultant will provide analytical and technical inputs to government engagement processes, support defined policy consultations, develop policy and technical products, and deliver targeted capacity-development activities in agreed thematic areas.
