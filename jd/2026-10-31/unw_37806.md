II. Organizational Context

UN Women, grounded in the vision of equality enshrined in the Charter of the United Nations, works for the elimination of discrimination against women and girls; the empowerment of women; and the achievement of equality between women and men as partners and beneficiaries of development, human rights, humanitarian action and peace and security.

The Ecuador Country Office advances strategic partnerships and resource mobilization efforts to expand funding and strengthen collaboration with government institutions, bilateral and multilateral donors, UN entities, civil society and the private sector.

Reporting to the Representative or designated supervisor, the Partnerships & Resource Mobilization Analyst provides technical and operational support to partnership development, donor engagement, resource mobilization, donor reporting and knowledge management.

III. Scope of Work

1. Support the development and maintenance of strategic partnerships and resource mobilization initiatives

- Support the development and maintenance of partnerships with government institutions, development partners, UN entities, civil society organizations, academia and private sector stakeholders.

- Contribute to the implementation and monitoring of partnership engagement plans and annual resource mobilization priorities.

- Research and analyze donor priorities, funding trends and partnership opportunities to support resource mobilization efforts.

- Maintain updated information on donor profiles, stakeholder mapping and partnership databases.

- Support engagement with the Civil Society Advisory Group (CSAG) and other strategic stakeholders, as required.

- Contribute to advocacy and partnership initiatives aimed at strengthening UN Women’s positioning with donors and partners.

2. Support the implementation of resource mobilization strategies and initiatives

- Contribute to the implementation of resource mobilization strategies, fundraising initiatives and donor outreach activities.

- Support the preparation of resource mobilization products, donor briefs, presentations and other fundraising materials.

- Coordinate the collection and analysis of information on programme pipelines and funding gaps.

- Provide technical support for the development of funding proposals, concept notes and project documents aligned with UN Women priorities and donor requirements.

- Conduct donor research and funding opportunity mapping and maintain updated information on potential funding sources.

- Support the monitoring and updating of resource mobilization plans and funding pipelines.

3. Support donor relations and partnership management

- Prepare briefing notes, background documents, talking points and presentations for senior management engagement with donors and partners.

- Support the organization and follow-up of meetings, missions and consultations with development partners.

- Maintain donor databases, contact management systems and donor intelligence information.

- Track donor commitments, engagement opportunities and partnership actions.

- Support the preparation and review of partnership agreements, Framework Arrangements, Cost-Sharing Agreements and related documentation.

- Contribute to the preparation of concept notes, funding proposals and partnership documentation.

4. Support the preparation of quality and timely donor reports

- Maintain reporting trackers and monitoring tools to support compliance with donor agreements and reporting deadlines.

- Coordinate with programme teams to collect information required for donor reporting.

- Provide technical support for the preparation and quality review of donor reports and results-based reporting products.

- Support the consolidation of programme results, lessons learned and achievements for external reporting purposes.

- Monitor reporting schedules and ensure timely follow-up with relevant stakeholders.

5. Support strengthening internal capacity on partnerships and resource mobilization

- Provide guidance to programme personnel on resource mobilization processes, donor requirements and funding modalities.

- Support the organization of training sessions, workshops and knowledge-sharing activities related to partnerships and resource mobilization.

- Develop and maintain tools, templates and reference materials to facilitate resource mobilization efforts.

- Support the preparation and dissemination of communication and visibility materials for partners and donors.

- Contribute to the development of resource mobilization and partnership knowledge products.

6. Contribute to knowledge management, communication and advocacy

- Contribute to the development of knowledge products, briefing materials and communication content related to partnerships and resource mobilization.

- Support knowledge sharing across programme teams through the dissemination of tools, templates, lessons learned and good practices.

- Monitor emerging donor trends, partnership opportunities and potential risks that may affect resource mobilization efforts.

- Contribute to advocacy, visibility and external engagement initiatives that strengthen UN Women's strategic positioning.

- Maintain records and documentation of partnership and resource mobilization achievements and lessons learned.
