Introduction
Established in 1951, IOM is a Related Organization of the United Nations and the leading UN agency in the field of migration. Working closely with governmental, intergovernmental and non-governmental partners, IOM promotes humane and orderly migration for the benefit of all. It saves lives and protects people on the move, drives solutions to displacement, and facilitates pathways for regular migration, while providing services and advice to governments and migrants.
IOM is committed to fostering a respectful, inclusive and supportive workplace where all employees can thrive professionally and feel valued. By creating such an environment, IOM aims to better harness the full potential of migration and strengthen its support to people on the move.

Organizational Context and Scope
IOM Ethiopia’s Migration Management and Government (MMG) Unit works closely with the Government of Ethiopia, and various stakeholders to contribute to addressing the migration governnance needs, as well as to promote and uphold the rights of individuals and their communities. The Unit has three main areas of intervention: Coordination and Capacity Development support (CCD), Immigration and border governance (IBG), and Labor Mobility and Social Inclusion (LMI).
IOM’ Ethiopia Labour Mobility and Social Inclusion programme provides policy, regulatory and operational guidance in matters related to labour and human mobility facilitation, integration and social cohesion, and diaspora engagement as an enabler of sustainable development in line with the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development.
Under the overall supervision of the Senior Migration Management and Governance Officer, and the direct supervision of the National Programme Officer (LMI), the Senior Project Associate (Labor Migration and Social Inclusion – LMI) will support the technical, operational and administrative implementation of Labor Mobility and Social Inclusion projects within the LMI portfolio.
The incumbent will serve as the operational technical focal point for Youth Business Incubator (YBI) initiatives and will support other labor migration and social inclusion -related initiatives implemented under the LMI portfolio. The incumbent will provide technical coordination support under the guidance of the National Programme Officer LMI, in alignment with approved project documents, donor requirements and IOM standards.
