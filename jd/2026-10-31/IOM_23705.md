Organizational Context and Scope
IOM South Sudan implements a broad portfolio of programmes across humanitarian coordination and response, resilience, peacebuilding, transition and development, working closely with the Government of South Sudan, communities, development partners and other stakeholders. Within its community resilience portfolio, IOM implements programmes aimed at strengthening resilience to climate-related shocks, disaster risk and displacement, including the Regional Climate Resilience Program (RCRP), implemented in partnership with the Government of South Sudan with financing from the World Bank. The programme seeks to strengthen flood resilience and reduce the impacts of flooding in targeted areas of South Sudan through flood risk management, climate-resilient infrastructure and strengthened institutional capacity.
Under the overall supervision of the Chief of Mission (CoM) and the direct supervision of the Project Manager (Community Resilience) in Juba, and in close coordination with relevant IOM units and government counterparts, the Project Officer (Disaster Risk Management (DRM)) will be responsible and accountable for supporting the implementation of DRM and flood resilience activities under Regional Climate Resilience Program (RCRP) and across IOM South Sudan's community resilience portfolio. The Project Officer (DRM) will provide technical support on flood risk assessments, hydrological and hydraulic studies, infrastructure design and implementation of flood mitigation works, including dykes, drainage systems, culverts, embankments and other climate-resilient infrastructure. The Project Officer (DRM) will also support the technical review of designs and specifications, monitoring of construction works and quality assurance activities, and assist with the coordination of DRM governance and institutional capacity-building activities, and support the integration of disaster risk reduction and climate resilience into programme planning and implementation.
