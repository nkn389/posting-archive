Associate, M365 Engineer

Join EBRD as an M365 Engineer and help shape, operate and continuously improve the Microsoft 365 ecosystem that enables our people to work securely and effectively.

This is a hands-on engineering role covering Exchange Online, SharePoint Online, Teams, OneDrive, Entra ID and PowerShell automation. You’ll take ownership of complex technical issues, platform configuration and optimisation, while delivering configuration changes and technical improvements through testing, implementation and documentation across our digital workplace.

What you’ll be doing

- Engineer, administer and optimise Exchange Online, including mail flow, permissions, user and group management and platform health.

- Manage SharePoint Online, Teams and OneDrive, covering access, permissions, sharing, troubleshooting, recovery, monitoring and platform improvements.

- Engineer and troubleshoot identity and access across Microsoft Entra ID, including authentication, MFA, Conditional Access and enterprise applications.

- Use PowerShell for Microsoft 365 administration, automation and troubleshooting across M365 services.

- Manage and optimise Microsoft 365 E5 licensing.

- Investigate complex incidents, identify root causes and deliver M365 configuration changes and technical improvements through testing, implementation and documentation, contributing to continuous platform improvement.

Need to have

- Strong hands-on Microsoft 365 engineering/administration experience within a medium-to-large organisation.

- Strong technical knowledge across Exchange Online, SharePoint Online, Teams and OneDrive.

- Strong experience with Microsoft Entra ID and identity and access management, including authentication, MFA, Conditional Access, enterprise applications and identity-related troubleshooting.

- Hands-on PowerShell experience for Microsoft 365 administration, automation and troubleshooting.

- Proven ability to troubleshoot complex issues across multiple M365 workloads.

- Demonstrable experience independently implementing, configuring, troubleshooting and optimising Microsoft 365 services.

- Experience delivering M365 configuration changes and technical improvements through testing, implementation and documentation.

- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to translate technical concepts for both technical and non-technical stakeholders.

- Experience working collaboratively in an Agile, distributed environment.

- Fluent written and spoken English.

Nice to have

- Experience with Microsoft Azure and M365/Azure integration.

- Knowledge of Defender for Office 365 and Mimecast.

- Experience with Power Platform, including Power Apps, Power Automate and Power BI, and/or Microsoft Copilot or Copilot Chat.

- Experience deploying or supporting Microsoft Teams Rooms and AV integrations.

- Knowledge of M365 E5 licensing, security monitoring, auditing and reporting.

- Relevant Microsoft certifications or equivalent technical experience.

Why you’ll love this role

- Broad technical scope: Work across the core Microsoft 365 ecosystem rather than being limited to a single technology.

- Real engineering ownership: Troubleshoot complex problems, deliver technical changes and improve the platform.

- Modern technology: Build experience across M365, Entra ID, PowerShell automation and Azure-connected services, with exposure to Defender, Power Platform, Copilot and Microsoft Teams Rooms.

- More than BAU: Contribute to engineering changes, implementations, automation, optimisation and continuous platform improvement.

- Meaningful impact: Your work will enable colleagues across EBRD to collaborate securely and effectively.
