&lt;p&gt;&lt;b&gt;YOUR POSITION WITH TNC&lt;/b&gt;&lt;/p&gt;&lt;p&gt;The Director of Finance &amp; Operations, Europe serves as the region’s senior finance leader and operations integrator—the regional equivalent of a CFO with broader operations integration responsibilities. The role is a trusted partner to the Regional Managing Director, Europe and operates within a global matrix, with accountability to Global Finance for financial stewardship, standards, and alignment, and to the Regional Managing Director for regional strategy execution and organizational effectiveness.&lt;/p&gt;&lt;p&gt; &lt;/p&gt;&lt;p&gt;In this role, the Director partners closely with both the Senior Director of Finance &amp; Operations, Global Regions and the Regional Managing Director, Europe to support regional leadership, drive key priorities, and ensure effective execution across the Europe region. The role has broad Finance &amp; Operations responsibility across a region that operates in multiple countries, while combining strategic oversight with practical hands-on leadership appropriate to the size, structure, and complexity of the region.&lt;/p&gt;&lt;p&gt; &lt;/p&gt;&lt;p&gt;&lt;b&gt;ESSENTIAL FUNCTIONS&lt;/b&gt;&lt;/p&gt;&lt;p&gt;Reporting to the Senior Director of Finance &amp; Operations, Global Regions, with a dotted-line relationship to the Regional Managing Director, Europe, the Director serves on the Europe Leadership Team, leads the regional Finance &amp; Operations team, and provides leadership and coordination across countries, programs, and enabling functions. The role also serves as Managing Director of the related entity through which TNC operates in Germany, with responsibility for its effective management and governance. The Director helps shape and execute regional priorities while contributing Europe perspectives and learning to TNC-wide Finance &amp; Operations initiatives.&lt;/p&gt;&lt;p&gt;&lt;b&gt;
