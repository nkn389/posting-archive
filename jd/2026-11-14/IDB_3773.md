About this position

We are seeking a Knowledge Management Product Specialist Consultant to provide strategic advisory services and specialized expertise for the Knowledge Management product portfolio. The consultant will support the definition of the product vision and roadmap, recommend governance arrangements and investment priorities, and advise on the development and evolution of integrated digital solutions. The consultant will provide guidance throughout the product lifecycle, from opportunity identification and strategic design to delivery, adoption, and continuous improvement. Working closely with senior stakeholders and cross-functional teams, the consultant will develop recommendations and practical solutions to help ensure that the portfolio delivers measurable institutional value, responds to user needs, and contributes to the Bank’s strategic objectives. The assignment will also include assessing the capabilities, skills, resources, and delivery models required to implement the product strategy effectively. The consultant will recommend approaches for engaging external vendors and partners, support technical and strategic discussions, and provide guidance on the most appropriate combination of internal capabilities and external expertise needed to support the portfolio’s implementation and continued evolution. The consultant will work with Technology & Transformation for Operations, within the Technology & Transformation Department. The team is responsible for enhancing, improving, and digitally transforming the Bank’s operational work.

What you’ll do

- Advise product strategy: Provide specialized advice and analysis to support the definition and implementation of the Knowledge Management product strategy, in alignment with the Bank’s Knowledge Agenda and institutional priorities.

- Develop product roadmaps: Prepare and maintain integrated product roadmaps for IDB Learning, the Digital Library, Knowledge Management platforms, and related knowledge solutions across the IDB Group.

- Shape portfolio recommendations: Analyze product needs, dependencies, opportunities, costs, risks, and expected benefits, and prepare recommendations to support prioritization and investment decisions by authorized Bank staff.

- Facilitate stakeholder collaboration: Facilitate collaboration among business owners, operational units, product teams, user-experience specialists, data teams, and technology teams to support the delivery and continuous improvement of knowledge products.

- Support product delivery Monitoring: Monitor product activities, milestones, dependencies, and implementation risks, and provide recommendations to help teams deliver agreed products and outcomes.

- Promote user-centered solutions: Support user research and user-experience activities to ensure that knowledge products respond to user needs and provide accessible and effective experiences.

- Monitor adoption and performance: Develop proposed key performance indicators and use available analytics and user feedback to assess product adoption, effectiveness, and opportunities for improvement.

- Provide resource-planning analysis: Analyze capacity, budget, and technical resource needs and prepare recommendations for consideration and approval by the responsible Bank staff.

- Provide risk and innovation analysis: Identify emerging technologies, business opportunities, operational risks, adoption barriers, and innovation opportunities relevant to the Knowledge Management product portfolio.

- Provide technical input for staff-led vendor and partner engagement: Provide technical input, documentation, and analysis to support staff-led engagement with external vendors and institutional partners.

What you'll need

Education: Master’s degree (or equivalent advanced degree) in Digital transformation, Innovation and Technology, or other fields relevant to the responsibilities of the role.

Experience: At least 10 years of progressive experience in product management. Experience with knowledge and learning products is a plus.

Languages: Proficiency in English and Spanish, spoken and written, is required. Additional knowledge of French and Portuguese is preferable.

Additional education/experience:

• Proficiency in AI and generative AI technologies, along with their potential applications.

• Extensive understanding of ethical considerations and responsible use of AI.
