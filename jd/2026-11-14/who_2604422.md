OBJECTIVES OF THE PROGRAMME
The vision of the Division of Health Promotion, Disease Prevention and Care (PPC) is to advance health for all by anticipating change and integrating promotion, prevention and care across all health and disease areas to address today's greatest health burdens and tomorrow's challenges. Within PPC Division, the Department of Environment, Climate Change, One Health and Migration (ECO) aims to reduce the global disease burden caused by unhealthy environments by 25% within the next decade, by igniting health-centric decisions in pub ic policies, informing them with data, scientific evidence, norms & standards, and catalysing support to protect the most vulnerable. The Chemicals, Radiation and Health (CRH) Unit works to protect health from risks associated with hazardous chemicals and exposure to ionizing and non‑ionizing radiation. The Unit provides normative guidance, harmonized risk‑assessment approaches and technical support to Member States on prevention, risk management, and preparedness and response to chemical and radiological events, and promotes health in international governance processes.

DESCRIPTION OF DUTIES
Generic duties:

- Provides technical leadership, coordination and managerial support within the Department/Programme, in the Unit of Chemicals, Radiation and Health, ensuring that activities in this area are carried out efficiently and effectively.

- Develops and proposes technical health policies and strategies for implementation through collaboration with Regional and Country Offices and provides advice in developing further chemicals, radiation and health for strengthening and improving technical capacity of target clienteles.

- Oversees a unit of technical staff specialized in various domains within their area of expertise and facilitates the efficient flow of unit synergies.

- Manages the programme area in delivering its objectives through efficient management of a team of technical staff, financial resources and effective work planning to optimize the programme' s deliverables in line with the mandate of the Organization.

- Leads and conducts research on best practices in implementing programmes and activities and dissemination of information for capacity building in the respective areas of work.

- Manages the monitoring and evaluation and reporting of the subject area of work of key performance indicators and reporting requirements and mechanisms to optimize performance of Programme deliverables.

- Serves as technical reference among the peer community, UN Agencies and other stakeholders at the technical levels in the respective areas of expertise.

- Liaises with and utilizes partnerships in other UN Agencies, Donor, or research institutions in chemicals, radiation and health to position the technical area of work and implementation into Global and/or National policies.

- Represents the programme area of work at various meetings to advocate for the protection of health in the programmatic area of chemicals, radiation and health.

- Perform all other related duties as assigned.

Specific duties:

- Engages with Member States, relevant UN agencies and other international stakeholders to scale up the sound management of chemicals and radiation protection of the public, workers and patients.

- Leads and coordinates the provision of authoritative scientific and technical advice on chemicals, radiation and health, the development of WHO information products, norms, standards and guidelines, and related communication and advocacy campaigns.

- Represents the Organization at conferences to promote public health objectives in the development and implementation of international instruments on chemicals, radiation and health.

REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS
Education
Essential: An advanced university degree in medicine, medical physics, public health, biological or physical sciences or another field relevant to radiation and health.
Desirable: A doctoral degree in a field relevant to the functions of the position. Additional training or qualifications in public health, programme management, environmental health or chemicals and health.

Experience
Essential: A minimum of 10 years professional experience in radiation and health, including the management of health risks associated with ionizing and/or non-ionizing radiation and the development or implementation of health policies and strategies. Demonstrated experience managing human and financial resources and leading multidisciplinary teams. Demonstrated experience at the international level. Senior-level experience representing an organization and building partnerships in international or intergovernmental settings
Desirable: Experience in public health aspects of chemicals management or in supervising a chemicals and health programme. Experience in radiation protection in health care, environmental or public exposures, or radiological and nuclear emergency preparedness and response. Experience working in WHO, the UN Common System or another international organization. Experience in mobilizing resources and managing externally funded programmes.

Skills

- Concrete skills in the planning and coordination of multi-disciplinary public health programmes, including the implementation of public health/disease prevention programmes.

- Strong skills in developing and establishing polices and strategies.

- Excellent skills and demonstrated ability in managing diverse teams and financial resources.

- Good skills in mobilizing resources.

- Ability to engage and communicate effectively and diplomatically with external partners, including Ministries of Health, donor institutions, civil society organizations and other stakeholders.

- Commitment to collaborate effectively with key partners in chemical,radiation and health areas.

- Ability to work with expert networks and with other organizations, including through inter-institutional teams at international, regional and national levels, with the aim of promoting and strengthening policies related to chemicals, radiation and health.
