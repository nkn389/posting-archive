Duties and Responsibilities
The United Nations Environment Programme (UNEP) is the leading global environmental authority that sets the global environmental agenda, promotes the coherent implementation of the environmental dimension of sustainable development within the United Nations system, and serves as an authoritative advocate for the global environment. UNEP’s Ecosystems Division works with governments and partners to develop and promote ecosystem-based solutions that support sustainable development and biodiversity conservation. The NBSAP Accelerator Partnership (NBSAP-AP) is a country-led global initiative that supports the development and implementation of ambitious National Biodiversity Strategies and Action Plans (NBSAPs) aligned with the Kunming-Montreal Global Biodiversity Framework (KMGBF). Through technical assistance, facilitation, capacity development, and strategic coordination, the Partnership supports countries in accelerating implementation of national biodiversity commitments through whole-of-government and whole-of-society approaches. Mexico has established an ambitious policy framework for ecosystem restoration through ENBioMex 2.0, the National Environmental Restoration Programme (PNRA) 2025–2030, the Kunming-Montreal Global Biodiversity Framework (KMGBF), and its Nationally Determined Contribution (NDC 3.0). The National Environmental Restoration Programme (PNRA) has identified 80 priority restoration sites throughout the country, including 53 priority sites identified during 2025 and an additional 27 sites to be incorporated during 2026. These sites represent strategic opportunities for restoring priority ecosystems while contributing to biodiversity conservation, climate change mitigation and adaptation, water security, disaster risk reduction, sustainable livelihoods and ecosystem services. Although significant progress has been made in identifying restoration priorities, one of the principal barriers to implementation is the absence of standardized methodologies for structuring restoration projects that meet the technical, financial and institutional requirements expected by financing institutions. Existing project information is heterogeneous, prepared under different methodologies, and frequently lacks the technical, economic and financial elements necessary to attract investment or access national and international funding opportunities. Strengthening the investment readiness of restoration projects has therefore become a strategic priority for the Directorate General for Environmental Restoration (DGRA) of the Ministry of Environment and Natural Resources (SEMARNAT). Developing a standardized portfolio of investment-ready restoration projects will facilitate access to public budgets, international cooperation, biodiversity and climate finance, environmental compensation schemes, blended finance mechanisms and private sector investment. To address this challenge, the Directorate General for Environmental Restoration (DGRA of the Ministry of Environment and Natural Resources (SEMARNAT) requires specialized technical assistance to consolidate information from the 80 priority restoration sites, develop a standardized project preparation methodology, structure a national restoration investment portfolio, apply practical project preparation tools, and identify information and financing needs that can support subsequent project development. This consultancy contributes directly to the implementation of National Biodiversity Targets (NBTs) 2.1, 2.2 and 2.3 under ENBioMex 2.0, which collectively represent Mexico’s contribution to Global Target 2 of the Kunming-Montreal Global Biodiversity Framework. These targets focus on restoring degraded terrestrial, freshwater, coastal and marine ecosystems, increasing ecological functionality, strengthening restoration governance and planning mechanisms, and enhancing the long-term sustainability of restoration efforts. The consultant will report to the Programme Management Officer of the Nature Frameworks Support Unit, in close collaboration with the NBSAP Accelerator Partnership Facilitator for Mexico and the Directorate General for Environmental Restoration (DGRA), SEMARNAT.
