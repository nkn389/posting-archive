THE ROLE

To support effective delivery of Cash Based Transfer (CBT) finance, budget and administration support services to facilitate transparent and efficient utilization of financial resources.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES (not all-inclusive, nor exhaustive):

Under the overall supervision of the Head of Finance and the direct supervision of the Finance Officer, the Finance Assistant will perform the following duties and responsibilities, including but not limited to:

- Provide procedural and technical support to staff in the finance area to ensure compliance with WFP financial policies, rules, and regulations.
- Support the development and implementation of financial policies and procedures and assist in setting up and implementing proper accounting systems and internal controls.
- Coordinate with other teams to ensure that financial services are performed efficiently and in line with the needs of the wider team.
- Monitor monthly expenditure forecasts and provide expenditure reports in order to facilitate informed decision-making.
- Monitor account balances and process financial transactions accurately and promptly to avoid overdrafts.
- Monitor and analyze general ledger accounts to identify issues and take corrective actions.
- Compile information from various sources to prepare reports relating to budgets, accounting, finance and statistics, including Key Risk Indicators (KRI) monthly.
Functional Responsibilities:

- Act as the finance focal point for Cash-Based Transfers (CBT) activities.
- Process CBT disbursement on time to ensure that beneficiaries get their monthly entitlement with no delay.
- Liaise with CBT focal points in Area/Field Offices and Hubs to ensure timely replenishment of CBT bank accounts in line with operational requirements.
- Familiarize with corporate and country-specific guidelines and procedures and ensure that services have been rendered and properly authorized before processing payments.
- Perform relevant functions and roles within the beneficiary database system (SCOPE) including verification of SCOPE payment lists and generation of PIN codes upon receiving requests from client unit, and within the corporate enterprise system (WINGS) related to cash-based operations.
- Verify vendor claims, advances and other receivables and observe appropriate procurement procedures, to ensure that suppliers are paid in a timely manner and in compliance with WFP finance rules and regulations.
- Monitor and record all expenses in line with the approved budget in order to ensure that correct expenditure costs are charged and payments to external suppliers, cash grants and other supplier invoices are processed in a timely manner.
- Retrieve, format and validate information obtained from various financial information systems and bring any discrepancy or inaccuracy to the attention of the supervisor and follow corrective actions.
- Monitor the distribution reconciliation process and ensure records are kept updated.
- Perform manual uploads for CBT bank accounts and clear general ledger items.
- Ensure that open items are identified and cleared promptly by the respective Area/Field Offices and Hubs.
- Maintain the web-based register for all invoices and payment transfers and record paperwork, to ensure that all documents are maintained and filed in compliance with WFP standards and guidelines.
- Perform any other duties as requested by the supervisor.

QUALIFICATIONS AND EXPERIENCE:

Education: Completion of secondary school education. A post-secondary certificate in the related functional area is desirable.

Experience: At least five years of relevant work experience.

Language: Fluency (level C) in both oral and written communication in English and Arabic.

DESIRED EXPERIENCES FOR ENTRY INTO THE ROLE:
