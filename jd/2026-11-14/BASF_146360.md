As a Regulatory Label Specialist, you create chemistry by...

- Coordinating regulated pesticide label layouts for business-to-business products and reviewing label content for accuracy and compliance with applicable safety, legal, regulatory and BASF requirements.

- Performing high-volume document-control, technical-writing and quality-control activities that support regulatory data management, compliance documentation and timely label delivery.

- Managing mass label changes driven by Pesticide Registration Notices, BASF standards, regulatory developments and business requirements, while coordinating inputs and approvals across functions.

- Maintaining accurate label metadata, version histories, change records and database entries by analyzing, editing and reorganizing information in regulatory software applications.

- Coordinating change processes for unregulated labels and supporting QR-code and industry digital-labeling initiatives through project planning, stakeholder follow-up and delivery against critical deadlines.

- Supporting registration activities for supplemental distributor and other third-party labels, including coordination with Country Registration Managers and Operations to confirm correct product labeling.

- Communicating and negotiating with Regulatory, Operations, Manufacturing, Supply Chain and other technical and business partners to resolve labeling issues, obtain timely approvals and manage regulatory or operational risk.

If you...

- Hold a master’s or doctoral degree with at least three years of experience in a regulated industry, or a bachelor’s degree with at least five years of regulated-industry experience

- Bring experience in pesticide registration, regulatory affairs or a comparable regulated environment; knowledge of pesticide registration requirements and processes is strongly preferred.

- Apply knowledge of FIFRA labeling rules and requirements to technical writing, document management and quality control of pesticide labels.

- Manage multiple projects concurrently under critical deadlines while maintaining strong attention to detail, accuracy, organization and administrative discipline.

- Communicate and collaborate effectively across technical, business and operational functions and influence stakeholders when you do not have direct administrative authority.

- Have experience analyzing labeling content and metadata, resolve discrepancies and understand how regulatory decisions affect product registration, manufacturing, supply and commercial activities is preferred.

- Contribute to a collaborative team culture through strong written and verbal communication, interpersonal, analytical and negotiation skills; knowledge of the agricultural industry is preferred.

- Are able to travel up to 5%, domestically.
