Introduction
Established in 1951, IOM is a Related Organization of the United Nations and the leading UN agency in the field of migration. Working closely with governmental, intergovernmental and non-governmental partners, IOM promotes humane and orderly migration for the benefit of all. It saves lives and protects people on the move, drives solutions to displacement, and facilitates pathways for regular migration, while providing services and advice to governments and migrants.
IOM is committed to fostering a respectful, inclusive and supportive workplace where all employees can thrive professionally and feel valued. By creating such an environment, IOM aims to better harness the full potential of migration and strengthen its support to people on the move.

Project Context and Scope

The Republic of Congo remains vulnerable to the importation and spread of epidemic-prone diseases due to its significant population mobility, social, commercial and riverine connectivity with neighboring countries, particularly the Democratic Republic of Congo.
IOM is preparing US-funded Bundibugyo Ebola Virus Disease (BEVD) preparedness interventions. Its approach to outbreak preparedness and response is anchored in the Health, Border and Mobility Management (HBMM) Framework, which links population mobility with public health surveillance and supports mobility-sensitive interventions at Points of Entry (PoEs), Points of Control (PoCs), informal crossings, transport hubs, markets and other high-mobility locations.
The project will be implemented in close coordination with the Ministry of Health and other relevant Government authorities, national coordination mechanisms, UN agencies, technical partners, civil society and an implementing partner.
The consultant will support the timely operationalization and implementation of the project, ensuring that activities are aligned with the approved project document, national preparedness priorities, IOM procedures and donor requirements.
