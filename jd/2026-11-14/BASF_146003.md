WHAT YOU CAN EXPECT

We in Agricultural Solutions are preparing to be an independent, pure-play agricultural company within BASF group. To be ready for independence, we are looking for you to join us in our new Corporate roles (see here: Build Your Career on Our Standalone Transformation).

As Compliance Specialist, you support the global operationalization of selected key compliance topics of our Compliance Management System. Depending on business priorities, you will take ownership of one or more of these compliance topics, while working within the governance standards and methodologies defined by the central Agricultural Solutions Compliance department. The role combines global coordination with hands-on implementation across regions and functions.

- You operationalize selected CMS topics and program components globally, such as anti-corruption, human rights, business partner due diligence, whistleblowing or compliance case handling.

- You translate Agricultural Solutions policies, governance requirements and central methodologies into practical processes, guidance, implementation tools, controls and evidence requirements.

- Your responsibilities include supporting compliance risk assessments, mitigation measures, control documentation and testing, remediation tracking and effectiveness reviews.

- In addition, you coordinate risk-based compliance training and communication, from target-group analysis and rollout to completion tracking and follow-up.

- As a go-to user, you provide support for tools and applications used across the compliance area.

- You contribute to compliance case intake, documentation and follow-up and, where applicable, support investigation teams in line with defined governance responsibilities without displacing the designated case owner.

- To ensure transparency, you consolidate relevant KPIs, risks, implementation progress and open actions and prepare clear reporting for Agricultural Solutions Compliance management and governance bodies.

- Working closely with Legal, Internal Audit, Risk Management, Internal Controls, Information Security, HR, Procurement and the wider Agricultural Solutions Compliance Community, you drive agreed actions to completion and constructively address implementation gaps.

WHAT YOU OFFER

- university degree in law, compliance, business administration, finance, risk management or a comparable discipline

- relevant professional experience in compliance, legal, internal controls, risk management, audit, investigations or a related governance function, preferably in an international organization

- practical understanding of CMS elements such as risk assessment, controls, monitoring, training, case management, reporting and continuous improvement

- experience translating governance requirements into practical processes, tools and documentation and working with evidence in an audit-ready manner

- strong analytical judgment, reliable execution and confidence in communicating risks, controls and required actions to diverse stakeholders without relying on hierarchy

- excellent English, verbally and in writing; additional languages are an advantage

- digital mindset and confidence working with compliance, risk, reporting or workflow applications

HOW YOU CREATE IMPACT

- Own it: Take responsibility for reliable implementation, evidence, follow-through and timely escalation.

- Drive it: Turn identified risks, control weaknesses and regulatory or governance changes into clear actions and priorities.

- Excel in it: Combine analytical rigor with pragmatic execution, use KPIs and stakeholder feedback, and continuously improve the effectiveness of the Agricultural Solutions Compliance Management System.
