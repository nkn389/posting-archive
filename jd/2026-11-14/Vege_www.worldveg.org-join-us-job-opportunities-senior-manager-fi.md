Overview

The World Vegetable Center (WorldVeg) is a non-profit, autonomous international agricultural research center with headquarters in Taiwan and five regional offices around the globe. WorldVeg conducts research and development programs that contribute to realizing the potential of vegetables for healthier lives and more resilient livelihoods. For more information, please visit our website: worldveg.org

WorldVeg holds the world’s largest public collection of vegetable genetic resources. The Center’s vegetable breeding programs in Africa and Asia turn these resources into improved breeding lines and work with the public and private sectors to develop and scale new vegetable varieties for the benefit of smallholder farmers and consumers in lower-income countries. The Center also develops and promotes safer and more sustainable production and postharvest practices to make vegetable value chains more efficient with the ultimate aim of making vegetables more available, affordable, and accessible.

WorldVeg seeks to recruit a Senior Manager, Finance and Grants for an upcoming horticulture program operating in Senegal and The Gambia. Based in Thiès, Senegal, the position will provide senior financial leadership for a program that aims to establish Horticulture Business Networks (HBNs) and create employment opportunities for young women aged 18 to 35 in horticulture value chains across the two countries.

Key Responsibilities

- Financial Strategy, Planning and Budget Management

- Lead the program’s financial planning, annual budgets, forecasts, cash-flow projections and periodic budget revisions across Senegal and The Gambia.
- Provide financial analysis, expenditure tracking and management advice to support timely program decisions and effective resource allocation.

- Accounting, Treasury and Financial Reporting

- Oversee accounting, reconciliations, cash and treasury management, financial records and the consistent application of WorldVeg financial procedures.
- Prepare accurate, complete and timely financial reports for program leadership and authorized donor reporting channels, in line with grant agreements and applicable requirements.

- Grants and Partner Financial Management

- Oversee grant financial administration, partner and subrecipient financial monitoring, fund disbursements, financial acquittals and supporting documentation.
- Review partner budgets and financial reports, identify capacity gaps and provide guidance to strengthen partner financial stewardship and compliance.

- Internal Controls, Risk Management and Audit

- Maintain effective financial controls, segregation of duties, financial risk assessments and measures to prevent fraud, corruption and misuse of resources.
- Coordinate internal and external financial audits, respond to findings and monitor implementation of agreed corrective actions.

- Team Leadership and Cross-functional Coordination

- Supervise, coach and assess finance staff, promote consistent financial practices and strengthen financial management capacity across the program.
- Coordinate with the Program Management Unit, procurement, operations, grants and HQ Finance on budgets, payments, reporting and financial decision-making.

- Ethics, Safeguarding and Institutional Compliance

- Ensure compliance with WorldVeg policies, applicable grant conditions, financial regulations, confidentiality and records-management requirements.
- Promote safeguarding, prevention of sexual exploitation, abuse and harassment, anti-retaliation and safe reporting practices in financial operations and partner engagements; promptly escalate concerns through authorized channels.
