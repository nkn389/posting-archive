The
Development Economics Policy Indicators Group (DECIG) produces quantitative
indicators of business regulation through primary data collected by the
department on laws, regulations, and the experiences of firms operating in the
private sector.

Within
DECIG, the Enterprise Analysis Unit (DECEA) has developed the Enterprise
Surveys (WBES), a survey instrument that links investment climate conditions to
firm outcomes such as performance, firm growth, productivity, and employment in
client countries. To date, DECEA has assembled a critical mass of firm-level
data using WBES on more than 200,000 firms across all World Bank regions. WBES
also provides factual firm-experience data in support of the World Bank’s
Business Ready project. The Unit has developed additional products to meet
internal client demands in data collection, indicator generation, and data
analysis, including an informal sector survey, innovation modules, and WBES
follow-ups to measure the impacts of shocks. The team produces peer‑reviewed
research across private sector development using internal and external data.
DECEA
is seeking to hire 1 Extended Term Consultant (ETC) DECEA is seeking to hire
one Extended Term Consultant (ETC) to execute high-value, well-defined tasks
that strengthen communications, outreach, and dissemination for the World Bank
Enterprise Surveys (WBES). The consultant will develop social media and other
digital content, craft stories and research summaries, support partnerships and
stakeholder engagement, and help track the impact and use of WBES data. The
selected individuals will join a group of economists and will report to the
DECEA manager.
Duties
and Responsibilities:
Communications and Content
• Support the development of a social media communications strategy and editorial calendar, including post planning and scheduling.
• Conceptualize and develop effective written and visual social media content that supports the overall communications goals of the World Bank Enterprise Surveys (WBES).
• Contribute to growing the WBES user base through engagement on social and other digital media.
• Craft compelling stories for blogs, newsletters, feature stories, and other products to raise awareness of the work and research of the WBES.
• Prepare research summaries, key findings, publication announcements, and promotional materials that communicate evidence on private sector development and business environments.

Research and Impact
• Collaborate with technical teams to identify and communicate findings that contribute to policy discussions on firm productivity, innovation, employment, business regulation, and economic development.
• Support the collection of information on the impact of WBES data, such as citations and use in World Bank analytical products.
Partnerships and Outreach
• Support the identification and engagement of strategic partners, including universities, research institutions, think tanks, government agencies, chambers of commerce, and private sector organizations.
• Coordinate outreach initiatives to strengthen institutional relationships and expand awareness and use of Enterprise Surveys data.
• Support the planning, coordination, and implementation of international conferences, workshops, institutional visits, and high-level stakeholder engagement activities.

Monitoring
• Lead the team's media monitoring and reporting tasks.
