Under the direct supervision of the Head of WFP Port Sudan Sub-Office and the overall supervision of the Head of logistics WFP Sudan, or the officers in-charges in their absences, the incumbent should perform the following duties: -

ACCOUNTABILITIES/RESPONSIBILITIES:

- Support Port Sudan Port operations following standard processes and contributing, directly or indirectly, to the effective delivery of food assistance to beneficiaries.
- Engage actively with operating logistics partners to gather real-time intelligence on Port Operations ensuring WFP’s interests are proactively protected.
- Maintain proactive communication with local shipping agents and HQ regarding incoming shipments. Ensure the receipt of complete documentation and relevant exemption letters prior to vessel arrival, thereby optimizing the customs clearance process and securing timely deliveries.
- Coordinate with shipping agents, stevedores, and Sudanese government entities to ensure the timely, cost-effective discharge, customs clearance, and final delivery of all WFP bulk, break-bulk, and containerized shipments.
- Consolidate Port operations data and contribute to preparation of accurate and timely reporting, supporting a WFP wide view of logistics activities that enables informed decision making and consistency of information presented to stakeholders.
- Conduct market surveys for clearing services and other service providers to ensure competitive selection of efficient service providers.
- Coordinate and supervise the discharge of WFP bulk, break bulk and containers shipments, to proactively avoid or minimize demurrage/detention charges and ensure adhering to WFP standards.
- Monitor the performance of WFP service providers to ensure accurate and proper quality and quantity deliveries as per WFP standards, to protect WFP and donor’s interests.
- Act as the primary point of escalation and technical mentor for support staff, resolving complex queries and providing analytical oversight to ensure operational accuracy and team growth.
- Support the capacity building of WFP staff and partners for efficient and effective delivery of food assistance to beneficiaries.
- Identify and build productive relationships with logistics and other colleagues and staff within the area of assignment to support an integrated approach to food assistance.
- Follow up on emergency logistics preparedness practices to ensure WFP can quickly respond and deploy food and needed resources to affected areas at the onset of the crisis.
- Act in an assigned emergency response capacity as required to meet emergency food assistance needs.
- Perform any other related duties as maybe required.

DESIRED EXPERIENCES FOR ENTRY INTO THE ROLE:

- Contract Management: Experience in coordinating service providers to ensure seamless contract execution and compliance.
- Vessel & Port Operations: Experience in managing port actions, arranging supplier deliveries, and overseeing the discharge of WFP vessels.
- Supply Chain & Logistics: Experience managing end-to-end operations, including customs clearance, warehousing, and inland transport.

QUALIFICATIONS AND EXPERIENCE

Education: Advanced University degree in Economics, Supply Chain, Logistics, Engineering, Business Management or another related field, or First University degree with two additional years of related work experience and/or relevant training/courses.

Experience: Minimum of one year of postgraduate (or equivalent per Education requirements) professional experience in Supply Chain, Logistics, Business Management, or another related field.

Language: Fluency (level C) in English language and Arabic Language.
