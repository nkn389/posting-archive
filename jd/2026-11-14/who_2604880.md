Background
The Ministry of Health of Ukraine (MoH) coordinates cooperation with international development partners to ensure that international support contributes effectively to national health priorities, health system recovery and reconstruction, reform implementation and Ukraine’s European integration agenda.
The Sector Working Group “Health” serves as a key platform for strategic dialogue and coordination between the Ministry of Health, development partners, international organizations and other stakeholders supporting the health sector. The SWG facilitates information exchange, alignment of partner support with Government priorities, discussion of strategic and operational issues, and coordinated follow-up on agreed actions.
The WHO Country Office in Ukraine supports the Ministry of Health in strengthening health governance, aid effectiveness and development partner coordination, including through co-chairing of the Sector Working Group “Health” and resourcing its Secretariat.
To ensure continuous and effective functioning of the SWG Health and its related coordination mechanisms, WHO intends to engage a consultant to provide comprehensive technical, analytical, coordination and Secretariat support to the SWG Health.
Deliverables
Scope of work
The Consultant will:
• Support the planning, preparation, organization and follow-up of regular and ad hoc SWG meetings.
• Coordinate meeting schedules and substantive preparations with the MoH, SWG co-chairs, and participating development partners.
• Prepare draft agendas and coordinate proposed agenda items, presentations and interventions with relevant stakeholders.
• Prepare and circulate invitations, agendas, background materials and other meeting documentation.
• Coordinate contributions and inputs from SWG members and other relevant stakeholders ahead of meetings and consultations.
• Provide organizational support for in-person, online and hybrid SWG meetings.
• Prepare meeting minutes, summaries of discussions, decisions, recommendations and agreed follow-up actions.
• Maintain and regularly update an SWG action tracker and follow up with responsible stakeholders on implementation of agreed actions.
• Maintain up-to-date SWG membership, focal-point and distribution lists, including coordination of changes in organizational representation.
• Support onboarding and engagement of new SWG members.
• Maintain an organized repository of SWG documentation, including agendas, minutes, presentations, decisions, analytical materials and other relevant records.
• Support development, updating and monitoring of the SWG workplan and agreed coordination priorities.
• Maintain regular communication with development partners, UN agencies, international financial institutions, diplomatic missions and other SWG stakeholders.
• Coordinate written consultations and consolidation of partner comments or inputs requested by the MoH or SWG leadership.
• Prepare briefing notes, talking points, presentations, analytical summaries and other decision-support materials for SWG meetings and partner engagement.
• Collect, consolidate and analyze information from development partners on ongoing and planned support to the health sector.
• Support collection of information for the mapping of development partner activities against Government priorities and identify potential gaps, overlaps and opportunities for improved coordination.
• Support coordination between SWG Health and relevant thematic, technical or ad hoc coordination mechanisms where required.
• Support SWG-related preparation and coordination in connection with major international processes and events relevant to the health sector, including the Ukraine Recovery Conference and other development partner engagement processes.
• Perform other technical, analytical and coordination tasks directly related to the effective functioning of SWG Health, as requested by WHO and the SWG leadership.
Deliverables
• Monthly (December 2026 - November 2027) SWG Health coordination and secretariat support package, including, as applicable during the reporting period:
o preparation and follow-up of SWG Health meetings and consultations;
o agendas, invitations, meeting materials, minutes and action points;
o updated SWG membership/contact records and action tracker;
o coordination and follow-up with SWG members, co-chairs and relevant stakeholders;
o briefing, analytical and communication materials prepared for SWG purposes;
o consolidated partner inputs and relevant mapping/data updates;
o progress update on implementation of agreed SWG actions and priorities.
• Final (by 18 December 2027) SWG Health coordination and secretariat support package, including:
o documentation and follow-up from SWG meetings and consultations during the reporting period;
o updated SWG membership/contact records and action tracker;
o status of outstanding SWG follow-up actions;
o summary of key SWG activities and coordination developments during 2027;
o proposed priorities and coordination/workplan inputs for 2028;
o final handover of relevant SWG documentation and coordination records.
