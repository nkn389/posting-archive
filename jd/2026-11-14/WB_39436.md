Description
At the International Finance Corporation (IFC), a member of the World Bank Group (WBG), you’ll join a diverse, global community working across cultures, disciplines, and borders to address the world’s most pressing development challenges. With 1.2 billion young people reaching working age in the decade ahead, the challenge of job creation has never been greater. Through partnerships across 145 countries and more than 182 offices worldwide, we work with governments, private sector, development partners and other stakeholders to invest in people, strengthen markets, and deliver scalable, data-driven solutions that generate more and better jobs and improve lives. In fiscal year 2026, IFC committed a record $105.6 billion to private companies and financial institutions in developing countries. For more information, visit www.ifc.org.

IFC's Infrastructure Department for Latin America and the Caribbean (LAC) is recruiting an (Associate) Investment Officer to be based in Guatemala City, Guatemala. The candidate will help identify business opportunities and meet with clients; support the structuring and execution of infrastructure investment transactions (project finance, corporate finance, equity, and hybrid instruments); and actively manage portfolio investments in core infrastructure sectors, including water, energy, transport, waste, municipal infrastructure, and telecommunications. While the role engages across LAC, the primary geographic focus will be Central America.

The overall goal of the position is to perform the regular activities of an Associate within a project finance and infrastructure investment department. The candidate must demonstrate sound business and finance judgement, ability to meet with clients, strong financial modeling skills, with proficiency in Excel as a strict requirement, and must be able to conduct business fluently in both English and Spanish.
Duties and Accountabilities

Specific responsibilities include, but are not limited to:

• Map and screen business opportunities in infrastructure sectors across Central America; undertake market research and perform corresponding integrity checks.
• Support transaction teams during investment due diligence and execution. Prepare term sheets and investment committee materials; evaluate, structure, negotiate, and close new transactions.
• Draft and present memos on relevant early-stage investment opportunities for discussion with management, incorporating local market context and regional sector dynamics.
• Actively manage and supervise portfolio investments, including credit risk assessment, financial analysis, processing of waivers, disbursements, and covenant monitoring.
• Ensure that transactional requirements recorded in the legal documentation are monitored and complied with; process amendments and restructurings as needed, working with portfolio management, credit, legal, and E&S colleagues.
• Build complex financial models from scratch, perform financial analysis, and provide quality control over financial analysis and projections developed by analysts to evaluate the benefits and risks of investments.
• Produce country and sector analysis, marketing materials, and pitch books.
• Support the development of a strong pipeline of investment opportunities by anticipating local and regional market trends.
• Coordinate closely with social, environmental, legal, and technical specialists to add value to clients' businesses, find pragmatic solutions to sector challenges, and achieve sustainable development impact and private capital mobilization.
• Manage and support relationships with clients, regional private businesses, banking and multilateral partners, and government officials to develop the sector and advance specific investment opportunities.

Selection Criteria
Required Qualifications

• A master's degree in a related field, including business, finance, economics, or an area of expertise specific to a sector or industry specialty.
• Full professional proficiency in English and Spanish, both verbally and in writing; ability to engage clients and counterparts across diverse linguistic and cultural settings in the region.
• At least 6–8 years of relevant experience, which may include project finance, private equity, or investment and commercial banking.
• Strong knowledge of Excel and financial modeling, with the ability to analyze financial information and draw well-reasoned conclusions.
• Strong analytical and financial structuring skills and sound business judgment.

Highly Desirable

• Experience in infrastructure investment transactions in Latin America and the Caribbean, particularly in Central America.
• Experience or familiarity with diverse investment products for infrastructure projects, such as project finance debt, corporate finance debt, guarantees, and bonds.
• Demonstrated ability to carry business development meetings and screen opportunities aligned with IFC’s requirements and strategic priorities.
• Appreciation of legal, technical, environmental, social, and other considerations in project and corporate finance.
• Genuine interest in, and knowledge of, relevant infrastructure and regional sector trends.
• Strong interpersonal skills and the ability to interact effectively and independently with clients and colleagues from diverse cultural backgrounds.
• Highly motivated individual, committed to the highest ethical standards.
• Genuine commitment to sustainable development and IFC's mandate.
• Proactivity to lead and take accountability for specific workstreams in transaction processing.
