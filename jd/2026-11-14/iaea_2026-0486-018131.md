Organizational Setting
The Department of Safeguards carries out the IAEA’s duties and responsibilities as the world’s nuclear inspectorate, supporting global efforts to stop the spread of nuclear weapons. The primary role of the Department is to develop and implement IAEA safeguards to ensure that there is no diversion of declared nuclear material from peaceful activities and no indications of undeclared nuclear material or activities in a State as a whole.

The Department comprises nuclear safeguards inspectors, responsible for carrying out inspections and verifications of all-safeguards relevant information for nuclear facilities in over 180 States; and technical staff responsible for a wide range of activities including: developing concepts and approaches for implementing safeguards; developing and maintaining safeguards equipment; providing analytical and laboratory services for sample analysis; collecting, evaluating and analysing safeguards-relevant information; providing information and communication technology infrastructure and services; and providing programme coordination support.

The Division of Concepts and Planning (SGCP) is responsible for the development and promotion of safeguards concepts, approaches and policy at the facility and State level; the development and provision of safeguards training for staff and Member States; strategic planning and the development of resource mobilization priorities; the coordination of Member State Support Programmes and associated research and development activities; and the implementation of the quality management system of the Department.

Main Purpose
Reporting to the Director, the Senior Coordination Officer (SGCP) leads and coordinates divisional programme planning; monitoring and evaluation; financial resource management; governance processes and institutional reporting activities; and strategic and improvement initiatives to support the effective delivery of the Division’s mandate and priorities. The Senior Coordination Officer promotes cross-divisional and departmental collaboration and quality management activities, ensuring the coherent integration of divisional activities while promoting harmonized approaches, and supporting effective decision-making through analysis, coordination and oversight of key programme and management processes.

Role
The Senior Coordination Officer (SGCP) is: (1) an expert analyst, developing, reviewing and evaluating divisional procedures, frameworks, management processes and strategic documentation; (2) a coordinator, serving as the divisional focal point for programme delivery, strategic initiatives, improvement projects and cross-divisional actions; (3) a facilitator ensuring effective coordination, integration and follow-up of activities within the Division and across the Department; (4) an adviser to the Director, management, and divisional staff, providing expert guidance and direction on strategic initiatives and objectives; and (5) a collaborator with internal and external stakeholders on programme delivery, quality management, process improvements and good practices.

Functions / Key Results Expected
Act as the divisional focal point for the planning, coordination, oversight and effective delivery of the Division’s programme of work.

Lead and coordinate strategic initiatives and improvement projects involving SGCP and other Divisions within the Department, ensuring effective stakeholder engagement, implementation, tracking, and timely delivery of project objectives.

Coordinate, plan, analyse, evaluate and oversee reporting on programme and budget activities, including maintaining oversight of financial planning, budget implementation, expenditure monitoring and reporting processes.

Plan and monitor programme delivery and resource utilization to support effective implementation of divisional priorities and objectives.

Develop, implement and assess frameworks, indicators and evaluation processes, including coordinating reporting activities and promoting innovative approaches to streamline, harmonize and strengthen internal planning and execution processes.

Represent the Division on departmental and/or IAEA committees, coordinating divisional contributions and ensuring timely communication and follow-up of relevant actions and decisions.

In close collaboration with the Section for Safeguards Programme Coordination, develop and implement communication approaches and reporting strategies to enhance the understanding and visibility of the Division’s activities and strengthen alignment of SGCP priorities with departmental objectives.

Act as Divisional Quality Manager and ensure the consistent implementation and maintenance of quality management standards, processes and good practices across the Division.

Provide expert advice and guidance to colleagues in the Division regarding improvements in the effectiveness, efficiency and consistency of safeguards implementation.
