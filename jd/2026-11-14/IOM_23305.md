Introduction
Established in 1951, IOM is a Related Organization of the United Nations and the leading UN agency in the field of migration. Working closely with governmental, intergovernmental, and non-governmental partners, IOM promotes humane and orderly migration for the benefit of all. It saves lives and protects people on the move, drives solutions to displacement, and facilitates pathways for regular migration, while providing services and advice to governments and migrants.
IOM is committed to fostering a respectful, inclusive and supportive workplace where all employees can thrive professionally and feel valued. By creating such an environment, IOM aims to better harness the full potential of migration and strengthen its support to people on the move.

Organizational Context and Scope
Under the overall supervision of the Chief of Mission (CoM) in Abuja and the direct supervision of the Head of Migration Management Unit, the Programme Manager (Immigration and Border Governance, Migration Governance, and Labour Mobility) will be responsible and accountable for managing and providing professional technical and programme support to the International Organization for Migration (IOM) Nigeria’s work on immigration and border governance, migration governance, and labour mobility.

The Programme Manager will contribute to the development and implementation of programmes, policy engagement, stakeholder coordination, research and capacity-building activities within the assigned areas. The Programme Manager will work closely with relevant IOM units, field offices, the Regional Office (RO) and Headquarters (HQ), as well as Government counterparts, United Nations (UN) agencies, donors, development partners, civil society, private sector and other stakeholders.

The Programme Manager will also contribute to ensuring that relevant interventions are aligned with IOM policies and strategies, national priorities, and applicable regional and global frameworks.
