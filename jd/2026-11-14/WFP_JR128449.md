The Early Warning Expert position will be based within the Supply Chain Unit’s Emergency Preparedness and Response Unit of the WFP Malawi Country Office and Seconded to the SADC Humanitarian and Emergency Operations Centre (SHOC) in Nacala, Mozambique.

The position will report to the delegated authority of the Director, SHOC.

To strengthen regional multi-hazard early warning, anticipatory action, and disaster risk management capacities by working closely with Government institutions, humanitarian partners, and regional organizations to enhance preparedness, improve risk analysis, and support evidence-based decision-making for disaster preparedness and response.Under the delegated authority of the Executive Director, the Early Warning Expert shall perform the following functions:

1. Coordination and Partnerships

- Support the WFP Project Manager in planning, coordinating and monitoring project activities in collaboration with SHOC
- Maintain regular liaison with Project technical partners to ensure alignment of national and regional activities.
- Support the planning and facilitation of technical coordination meetings, trainings and joint missions among project stakeholders and partners
- Support the establishment and coordination of regional early warning and Information Management working groups and technical task teams.
- Promote knowledge sharing and visibility of project outcomes across SADC and partner institutions.

2. Hazard Monitoring & Early Warning Analysis

- Lead real-time monitoring and analysis of regional multi-hazards through the SHOC Multi-Hazard Situation Room, ensuring timely identiﬁcation of triggers and thresholds that inform Anticipatory Action protocols across the SADC region.
- Generate actionable early warning briefs, advisories and risk intelligence updates for SADC Member States, incorporating co-production approaches and regional contextualization to ensure that advisories directly feed into pre-agreed anticipatory actions.
- Deliver anticipatory analysis and forward-looking risk assessments—including scenario building and probability-based risk proﬁling—to support evidence-driven preparedness planning and enable early actions before hazards materialize.
- Enhance and maintain multi-hazard monitoring dashboards and analytical tools in collaboration with SHOC’s Information Management and GIS teams, ensuring they integrate risk indicators, triggers, and decision-support layers used for activating anticipatory action mechanisms.

3. Technical and Operational Support

- Support implementation of AI-enabled Early Warning Systems, geospatial analysis tools and risk modelling.
- Assist in the setup and operation of the Geospatial Information Management Service Desk, ensuring technical requests are tracked and addressed.
- Support SHOC and partners in integrating Early Warning geospatial data into decision-making, reporting, preparedness and emergency operations.
- Support the SADC Emergency Response Team (ERT) with early warning information before and during deployments

4. Data Integration and Analytics

- Collaborate with SHOC Information Management/GIS teams to develop integrated early warning datasets/information (Hazard, vulnerability, exposure and coping capacity).
- Continuously support the development and update of triggers for Anticipatory Action and monitor them for Early Action
- Support the establishment and update of the regional disaster loss database and monitoring tools Facilitate the co-production and interoperability between SHOC Early Warning products and systems and regional products platforms.

5. Capacity Development and Communication

- Assist in the development of training materials, guidance notes, and knowledge products for capacity building and dissemination.
- Support organization of training sessions and workshops for SHOC and NDMA staff on early warning, geospatial tools, and disaster preparedness.
- Liaise with SHOC and WFP’s communications teams for documentation of key milestones, success stories, and media materials.

7. Monitoring, Reporting and Documentation

- Compile monthly progress updates, quarterly reports, and ﬁnal project reports as per SHOC, WFP and donor requirements.
- Monitor performance indicators and document lessons learned, success stories, and best practices. Maintain an up-to-date project results matrix and ensure quality and accuracy of technical deliverables.

QUALIFICATIONS & EXPERIENCE REQUIRED:

Education:

- Advanced University degree in Meteorology, Climate Science, Disaster Risk Management, Environmental Science, Natural Resources Management, or another relevant discipline.

Experience

- At least five years of progressively responsible professional experience in early warning systems, disaster risk reduction, climate services, humanitarian preparedness, emergency management or related fields.
- Demonstrated experience in multi-hazard risk assessment, anticipatory action, climate and weather analysis, GIS and information management.
- Experience working with Government institutions, UN agencies or humanitarian organizations.
- Knowledge of disaster risk management frameworks and emergency preparedness.
- Experience developing technical reports, dashboards and analytical products.
- Strong coordination, facilitation and stakeholder engagement skills.
- Proficiency in Microsoft Office, GIS applications and data analysis software.
- Ability to work effectively in multidisciplinary and multicultural environments.

Languages:

- Fluency (Level C) in English, with excellent written and verbal communication skills, is required for effective coordination with internal and external stakeholders, reporting, and operational support activities.
