Introduction
Established in 1951, IOM is a Related Organization of the United Nations and the leading UN agency in the field of migration. Working closely with governmental, intergovernmental and non-governmental partners, IOM promotes humane and orderly migration for the benefit of all. It saves lives and protects people on the move, drives solutions to displacement, and facilitates pathways for regular migration, while providing services and advice to governments and migrants.
IOM is committed to fostering a respectful, inclusive and supportive workplace where all employees can thrive professionally and feel valued. By creating such an environment, IOM aims to better harness the full potential of migration and strengthen its support to people on the move.

Organizational Context and Scope
IOM Ecuador is a key partner in supporting the Government of Ecuador and other stakeholders in addressing migration challenges and harnessing the opportunities of human mobility for sustainable development. As part of the International Organization for Migration (IOM), the UN Migration Agency, IOM Ecuador works to advance safe, orderly and regular migration through comprehensive approaches that include protection and assistance, socioeconomic inclusion, regular pathways, institutional strengthening, and data and evidence. Aligned with its 2022–2026 strategy, IOM Ecuador emphasizes sustainable, community-based interventions and strong coordination with national and local authorities, communities and other stakeholders to strengthen migration governance, promote inclusive development, and uphold the rights and dignity of migrants.

Under the general supervision of the Chief of Mission, and direct supervision of the Head of the program Support Unit , the Monitoring and Evaluation Assistant is responsible for supporting the implementation of Monitoring, Evaluation, Accountability and Learning (MEAL) processes established by IOM.
