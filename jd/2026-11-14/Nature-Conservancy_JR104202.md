&lt;h3&gt;&lt;span class="emphasis-2"&gt;&lt;b&gt;&lt;u&gt;What We Can Achieve Together:&lt;/u&gt;&lt;/b&gt;&lt;/span&gt;&lt;/h3&gt;&lt;br /&gt;&lt;p&gt;As the SE MN Preserve Management Assistant (PMA,) you will work throughout the driftless region of southeast Minnesota and serve on a 3-5-person crew participating in stewardship activities on both public and private lands and work closely with other non-profits and state and federal government employees. &lt;/p&gt;&lt;br /&gt;&lt;p&gt;You will be expected to coordinate, lead, and manage other TNC employees, volunteers, and partners. This position requires considerable physical exertion and/or muscular strain.&lt;/p&gt;&lt;br /&gt;&lt;p&gt;The position is based at our Weaver Dunes Preserve near Kellogg, MN.
