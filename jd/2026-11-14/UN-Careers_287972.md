Org. Setting and Reporting
This position is located in the Regional Office for Central America and the Caribbean (ROPAN), Division for Operations (DO) of the United Nations Office on Drugs and Crime (UNODC) in Panama City, Panama. As head of the Latin America and the Caribbean Procurement Hub, the Procurement Officer works under the direct supervision of the Chief Procurement Officer (UNOV/UNODC Procurement Unit, Vienna), and the overall supervision of the Regional Representative, ROPAN. The incumbent will execute his/her functions as part of the ROPAN Administrative Team and work in close coordination with the UNODC Deputy Representatives and Operations Managers in the Latin America and the Caribbean (LAC) region. For more information on UNODC, please visit: www.unodc.org

Responsibilities
The incumbent coordinates, supports and provides supervisory oversight of the procurement function across the entire LAC region, including the UNODC Regional Office for Central America and the Caribbean (ROPAN), the UNODC Regional Office for the Andrean Region and the Southern Cone (ROCOL), the UNODC Country Office in Mexico, and the UNODC Country Office in the Federative Republic of Brazil (COFRB). The incumbent is expected, upon request, to support other UNODC offices and Global Programmes in the procurement hub where UNODC operates. Within the assigned authority, the Procurement Officer will provide the following specific duties: • Serve as a senior procurement and contracting expert with responsibility for the procurement of a wide variety of supplies and services associated with technically and legally complex contracts and procurement activities; lead and support the procurement work of UNODC Offices in the LAC region. • Oversee other staff in the Procurement Hub, and provide advice to Buyer teams across the different offices covered by the Hub • Plan, develop and manage all procurement and contractual aspects of projects of significant complexity related to diverse services and commodities (e.g., information technology, specialized electronic equipment and instruments, medicines, safety and security equipment including Personal Protective Equipment (PPE), foodstuffs, architectural services and construction requirements, refurbishment works, event-related services, office supplies and consumables, furniture, etc.), taking into account local economic and other conditions. • Plan, develop and manage procurement activities related to infrastructure and major construction projects, including high-value projects, in consultation with and with the support of the UNOV/UNODC Procurement Unit, and in close cooperation with relevant UNODC requisitioning units and local authorities, to ensure compliance with all applicable building codes, regulations and safety requirements. • Ensure procurement activities comply with the United Nations Secretariat Financial Regulations and Rules; foster collaborative procurement with other entities of the UN system through active participation in established common working groups and coordination mechanisms, including Operations Management Team, Business Operations Strategy, and Common Back Office, to support common requirements and improve efficiency in line with the Mutual Recognition principle. • Coordinate procurement functions between ROPAN, ROCOL, COMEX, and COFRB under the Procurement Hub structure, including harmonizing delegation of authority thresholds, ensuring equitable workload distribution among buyers across offices in the region, and formalizing arrangements for ad-hoc mutual support with the UNOV/UNODC Procurement Unit. • Liaise closely with all UNODC requisitioning programmes and project teams in the LAC Region to foster effective procurement planning, including the early identification and consolidation of requirements, and ensure efficient and continuous communication and exchange of information throughout the procurement process. • Provide technical and strategic advice on procurement-related matters to UNODC officials, buyers, and requisitioning units during all phases of the procurement cycle; review procurement requests and technical specifications prior to publication to ensure they are fit-to-bid and in line with established quality standards. • Develop and prepare complex contracts for procurement of non-routine and technologically advanced commodities or long-range services; establish long-term agreements; issue and manage international tender invitations; evaluate responses to tenders; make recommendations for finalization of purchases and award of contracts; sign procurement orders up to the authorized limit and prepare submissions to the Contracts Committee for higher-value approvals. • Formulate strategies and design innovative solutions to resolve issues or conflicts in complex procurement projects, particularly construction, liaising closely with national authorities to ensure compliance with local laws. • Establish and maintain work programmes and schedules for ongoing and newly planned contracts; develop tactical approaches to contracts and new methods for their application based on evolving market trends. • Contribute to the development of regional procurement guidance guidelines, procedures, workflows, standards, templates, innovation initiatives and criteria for the evaluation of goods, services, and works and develop supplier capacity; streamline procurement processes and support implementation of UNOV/UNODC and Secretariat-wide initiatives. • Maintain constant examination of production patterns in exporting countries in the LAC region; evaluate productive capacities and performance of manufacturers to identify reliable supply sources. • Establish approaches for negotiations and conduct or coordinate all phases of negotiations with concerned parties, including resolution of disputes arising from contracts. • Prepare, review, clear and/or approve a variety of procurement-related documents, note to file, contracts, communications, guidelines, and instructions. • Develop and deliver procurement-related training programmes, and provide guidance to UNODC personnel, including buyers, managers, and project teams; develop and implement capacity development activities. • Prepare accurate and timely reports of procurement activities; monitor and manage auditing of procurement activities, ensuring audit observations are implemented in a timely manner and in conformity with applicable UN regulations and rules. • Monitor various corporate dashboards to ensure compliance with procurement-related key performance indicators related and coordinate with counterparts to ensure compliance with established procurement standards. • Lead the implementation of Enterprise and Corporate tools mandated by the Organization to enhance productivity and improve overall operations. These tools may include the Procurement Tracker, the e-tendering tool (Ariba), the Instant Feedback System (IFS) and the Contract Performance Reporting Tool (CPRT). Following deployment, ensure their effective utilization through user support, guidance, monitoring, and continuous improvement initiatives to maximize organizational benefits and compliance. • Collect and analyze data to identify trends or patterns and provide insights through graphs, charts, tables and reports using data visualization methods to enable data-driven planning, decision-making, presentation and reporting. • Monitor and oversee the use of Low Value Acquisition (LVA) within the Procurement Hub, with the aim to identify opportunities for consolidation, reducing reliance on LVA, and promoting the establishment and use of Long-Term Agreements (LTAs) for the Hub. • Conduct procurement assistance visits to UNODC offices in the region, as and when needed. • Provide guidance to and supervise procurement staff. • Perform other work-related duties as required.

Competencies
Professionalism: Has knowledge of internationally recognized procurement standards and of all phases
