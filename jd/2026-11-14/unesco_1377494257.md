OVERVIEW

The UNESCO Office in Uzbekistan’s Education Unit supports the Government of Uzbekistan in strengthening teacher quality and professional development across both pre-service and in-service education systems. Working closely with the Ministry of Education and Science, pedagogical universities, and teacher training institutions, UNESCO contributes to the development of teacher professional standards, competency frameworks, curricula, practicum models, and continuous professional development systems. The Unit supports reforms aimed at modernizing teacher education through competency-based approaches, digital and ICT skills development, inclusive education, STEM education, and innovative pedagogies. Through technical assistance, capacity-building programmes, policy dialogue, and international knowledge exchange, UNESCO seeks to ensure that prospective and practicing teachers are equipped with the skills and competencies required to deliver quality, equitable, and future-oriented education for all learners.

ASSIGNMENTS

National expert will support the effective implementation of project activities by ensuring timely planning, monitoring, reporting, and stakeholder coordination. The expert will track progress against approved workplans, results frameworks, and implementation timelines; monitor the achievement of outputs, outcomes, and key performance indicators; and identify implementation challenges and propose corrective actions as needed.

The role includes coordinating communication and collaboration among government counterparts, educational institutions, experts, development partners, and project teams to ensure alignment and timely delivery of activities.

The expert will compile and analyze project data, and monitoring updates, and provide the strategic guidance during coordination meetings, technical consultations, workshops, and field missions.

Furthermore, the expert will contribute to quality assurance processes, maintain project documentation and records, support risk management and follow-up on agreed actions, and ensure that project implementation remains consistent with donor requirements, national priorities, and UNESCO's programme and reporting standards.

Long Description

DELIVERABLES

Deliverable 1: Strategic Monitoring and Performance Analysis

Develop and implement a strategic monitoring framework to assess progress toward project outcomes and objectives. Analyze implementation performance against key indicators, identify emerging risks and bottlenecks, provide evidence-based recommendations for adaptive management, and prepare strategic progress reports to support decision-making by UNESCO and national stakeholders.

Deliverable 2: Strategic Coordination and Stakeholder Engagement

Lead the coordination and alignment of project implementation among government counterparts, development partners, implementing institutions, and technical experts. Facilitate strategic review meetings, monitor the implementation of agreed actions, and support high-level stakeholder engagement to ensure coherence, partnership effectiveness, and achievement of project results.

Deliverable 3: Results Reporting, Knowledge Management, and Quality Assurance

Produce consolidated analytical reports on project performance, results achievement, lessons learned, and good practices. Maintain oversight of project documentation and reporting processes, support quality assurance of deliverables and activities, and provide strategic insights to inform programme planning, resource allocation, and future interventions.
