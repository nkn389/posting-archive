Org. Setting and Reporting
This position is located within the Investigation and Analysis Section (IAS) of the International, Impartial and Independent Mechanism to assist in the Investigation and Prosecution of Persons Responsible for the Most Serious Crimes under International Law Committed in the Syrian Arab Republic since March 2011 (IIIM). IAS is responsible for gathering evidence within the context of the IIIM's structural investigation and generating analytical products that can be shared with competent jurisdictions to advance ongoing and/or future investigations and prosecutions. The incumbent will function under the supervision of the Senior Legal Officer (IAS). The incumbent will work within a multi-disciplinary team of legal officers, analysts and investigators, and in this context be responsible for preparing analytical products that can be shared with competent jurisdictions.

Responsibilities
Within delegated authority, the Legal Officer may be responsible for the following duties: - Prepares strategic recommendations to advance the development of the IIIM's structural investigation, strategic lines of inquiry, case files, evidence modules and other analytical products with the objective of advancing long-term justice initiatives and/or accountability efforts of national jurisdictions investigating and prosecuting crimes in relation to the Syrian context; - Drafts instructions, manuals and procedures to advance the large-scale review and analysis of evidence relating to serious breaches of international humanitarian law and violations of international criminal law and the responsibility of individuals for such breaches and violations and oversees the work of junior analytical staff, junior lawyers and legal interns conducting these reviews; - Conducts extensive legal research, evidence review and other related analysis, and prepares briefs and other analytical products which form part of the IIIM's case files and evidence modules; - Handles, and conducts research on diverse substantive and procedural legal issues pertaining to international and domestic criminal law as well as complex issues of fact relevant to the IIIM’s structural investigation, its strategic lines of inquiry and analytical blocks. including by overseeing work performed on these matters by junior lawyers, junior analytical staff and legal interns, and identifying and managing relationships with external consultants retained as subject-matter experts by the IIIM; - Participates in investigative activities, including by working in collaboration with IIIM investigative staff to identify and assess potential sources of evidence and ensure the collection and preservation of evidence from these sources, in collaboration with the IIIM’s Information Systems Management Section, and participates in interviews of witnesses and suspects together with IIIM investigative staff; - Participates in IIIM working groups as required and participates in conferences and workshops with other members of the IIIM, as well as actors external to the IIIM, including governmental officials, civil society actors, and members of other UN entities and international organizations; - Delivers legal capacity building to Syrian transitional justice authorities as required.

Competencies
• PROFESSIONALISM: Knowledge of, and ability to apply, legal principles, concepts and procedures and terminology for review, examination, and processing of a range of legal documents. Knowledge of various legal research sources, including electronic, and ability to conduct research and analyze information on a wide range of legal issues. Knowledge of the civil law and/or common law legal systems and principles. Ability to interpret and apply legislative instruments, develop and present results, recommendations, and opinions clearly and concisely. Ability to draft legal papers and work under pressure. Ability to apply good legal judgment in the context of assignments given. Shows pride in work and in achievements; demonstrates professional competence and mastery of subject matter; is conscientious and efficient in meeting commitments, observing deadlines and achieving results; is motivated by professional rather than personal concerns; shows persistence when faced with difficult problems or challenges; remains calm in stressful situations. Takes responsibility for incorporating gender perspectives and ensuring the equal participation of women and men in all areas of work. • COMMUNICATION: Speaks and writes clearly and effectively; listens to others, correctly interprets messages from others and responds appropriately; asks questions to clarify, and exhibits interest in having two-way communication; tailors language, tone, style and format to match audience; demonstrates openness in sharing information and keeping people informed. • TEAMWORK: Works collaboratively with colleagues to achieve organizational goals; solicits input by genuinely valuing others' ideas and expertise; is willing to learn from others; places team agenda before personal agenda; supports and acts in accordance with final group decision, even when such decisions may not entirely reflect own position; shares credit for team accomplishments and accepts joint responsibility for team shortcomings.

Education
Advanced university degree (Master's degree or equivalent degree) in law or related field. A first-level university degree in combination with two additional years of qualifying experience may be accepted in lieu of the advanced university degree.

Job - Specific Qualification
Not available.

Work Experience
A minimum of five years of progressively responsible experience in law, including legal analysis, research and writing relating to core international crimes obtained at the domestic or international level, is required. Prior work experience as a prosecutor with an international court or tribunal, or as a national prosecutor dealing with core international crimes, is desirable. Prior work experience using Relativity to review evidence is desirable. Prior work experience relating to international criminal accountability in the Syrian context is desirable.
