SUMMARY OF THE ASSIGNED DUTIES, INCLUDING GOALS AND OBJECTIVES OF THE POST

The Senior Coordinator Cybercrime, under the supervision of the Director Cybercrime Directorate (CD), is responsible for supporting CD Assistant Directors in developing and coordinating current and new initiatives and projects in line with INTERPOL policies and procedures. The officer will coordinate and monitor the development and implementation of Action Plans and roadmaps across the Cybercrime Directorate and report progress to the Director.
and the wider strategic and operational aims and objectives of the organization.

The post holder will be responsible for the effective coordination of CD activity across the Organization, identifying and realizing opportunities for cross Directorate fusion and collaboration. This will include identifying and proposing strategic and operational partnerships with other international organizations who share the same aims and objectives as INTERPOL. This is to maximize the resources available to tackle cybercrime threats facing member countries.

PRINCIPAL DUTIES AND ACTIVITIES

DUTY 1: Plan, monitor and coordinate initiatives and projects

- Coordination with all involved units and directorates across CD and the organization.

- Ensure compliance with INTERPOL policy and procedures.

- Ensure the CD calendar of operations is continually updated working with AD Cybercrime Ops.

- Assist with the budget and human resources planning to deliver the CD strategy.

- Ensure Strategic Actions Plans are in place and regularly updated for each CD initiative as defined by the Director CD.

- Identify and realize opportunities for cross directorate coordination, collaboration and fusion on strategic and operational matters.

- Liaise with INTERPOL units and prioritize support for CD activity. Ensure CD activities are well reasoned and under-pinned by comprehensive threat assessments and appropriate intelligence and analysis.

- Ensure all activity reflects the priorities of the CD strategy, making recommendations where necessary.

DUTY 2: Support and advise the Director Cybercrime

- Serve as a point of contact for EDIS-EO, Cabinet and other Directorates concerning CD operations, initiatives and projects.

- Maintain a forward-looking diary for operations and significant activity and report these to the Director weekly.

- Assist sub-directorates in CD to implement an effective performance regime, including debriefings and reporting.

- Make recommendations to Director CD for improvements in the efficiency and effectiveness of CD activity.

DUTY 3: Define, update policies and SOPs for operations, initiatives and projects

- Develop and maintain proposed criteria and guidance for INTERPOL-facilitated operations, initiatives and projects for management approval; and under which conditions INTERPOL can or should participate with partner organizations and member countries.

- Define details SOPs that cover both operational and administrative aspects of CD activity.

- Share best practice across CD and the organization.

Perform any other duties as required by the hierarchy.
