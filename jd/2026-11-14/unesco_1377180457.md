ORGANIZATIONAL CONTEXT

Under the overall authority of the Assistant Director-General for Sciences (SCS) and the direct supervision of the Director of UNESCO Regional Office in Dakar, the incumbent will work in close collaboration with the Sciences Sector at Headquarters, and in close coordination with Programme/Project Officers across different SCS areas and other Programme Sectors within the Dakar Office, as well as National Offices within the Region, to advance the implementation of SCS activities in West Africa.

The incumbent will be responsible for promoting and implementing international and regional agendas and action plans within UNESCO’s areas of competence in Sciences, including Water Sciences, Ecological and Earth Sciences, Local and Indigenous Knowledge Systems, Climate Action and Disaster Risk Reduction and Preparedness. Furthermore, the incumbent will ensure the promotion and implementation of Science, Technology and Innovation (STI) policy and cooperation, Science, Technology, Engineering and Mathematics (STEM) education as well as Capacity Building in the Basic and Engineering Sciences, through the development of research networks, knowledge sharing initiatives and training programmes.

OVERVIEW OF THE FUNCTIONS OF THE POST

The incumbent will work within the frameworks of UNESCO's Medium-Term Strategy, the approved Programme and Budget (C/5) and relevant global and regional development agendas including Agenda 2030 and Agenda 2063.

In particular, the incumbent shall:

- Conceptualize, design, plan, develop, coordinate, implement and monitor work plans for major areas of the Sciences Sector Strategies and priorities in line with countries priorities contribute to resource mobilization and promote intersectionality and synergies across sciences.

- Design and implement activities that foster scientific research and innovation, support Member States in identifying needs and priorities, and facilitate cooperation among regional and international centres. This includes coordinating studies, assessments, data collection and analysis, information dissemination, and peer-review processes

- Strengthen collaborative relationships with National Commissions and Member States while promoting Science for Sustainable Development within the framework of the Sciences programmes and in the Western Africa region

- Develop, coordinate and manage national, regional and international capacity building activities related to sciences. Provide expert assistance and technical support to Member States in West Africa.

- Advise and support Governments in the identification, formulation and implementation of UNESCO’s Sciences initiatives, and contribute to enhancing regional governance and cooperation with the purpose of strengthening and expanding related agendas and projects.

- Ensure the effective integration of UNESCO's priority areas into United Nations Common Country Analyses (CCA) and United Nations Sustainable Development Cooperation Frameworks (UNSDCFs) by providing technical input, supporting the preparation and review of strategic documents, facilitating engagement with development partners and donors, and promoting multi-stakeholder partnerships.

- Strengthen networks and their activity as well as performance of relevant regional institutions for improved services and knowledge sharing, increased partnerships, joint programming and integration into regional and international infrastructures.

- Strengthen regional scientific networks and institutions by enhancing their capacities, performance and collaboration, thereby improving knowledge exchange, service delivery, joint programming and integration into regional and international scientific frameworks.

- Mobilize and coordinate national, regional and international stakeholders active in UNESCO's priority science domains to support Member States in addressing development challenges. Establish and maintain strategic partnerships with governmental authorities, research and academic institutions, intergovernmental and non-governmental organizations, and other relevant stakeholders.

- Develop and strengthen partnerships with United Nations agencies, development partners, international financial institutions, regional organizations, research institutions and the private sector to design, fund and implement science-related programmes and projects, including those addressing resilience, emergency preparedness and response.
