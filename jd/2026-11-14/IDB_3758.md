About this position

The Research Department (RES) develops economic research and analytical tools that inform policy dialogue and the Bank’s work in Latin America and the Caribbean. Its macroeconomic research examines regional and country-level developments and the implications of external shocks, macroeconomic policies, and structural change. The research assistant will contribute to the design and implementation of applied research in macroeconomics.

What you’ll do

- Compile, clean, document, and update macroeconomic datasets, including country-level and time-series indicators from official and other recognized sources.

- Conduct descriptive, statistical, and econometric analysis of macroeconomic questions relevant to Latin America and the Caribbean, including growth, inflation, fiscal policy, external adjustment, commodity shocks, or international finance, as assigned.

- Develop reproducible code, charts, tables, and technical documentation for research projects, policy notes, presentations, and departmental outputs.

- Review relevant macroeconomic literature and summarize methods and findings that inform the research design.

- Contribute to the preparation and revision of research papers and presentations, and discuss results and data limitations with the research team.

- Track agreed research tasks and deliverables and coordinate input with economists and other collaborators as needed.

What you'll need

Education: Bachelor’s degree in economics or a related quantitative field. A master’s degree in economics or a related field is preferred.

Experience: A minimum of 2 years of related experience.

Languages: Proficiency in English, proficiency in Spanish is a plus.
Strong grounding in macroeconomic theory and applied quantitative research, with interest or experience in Latin American and Caribbean macroeconomic issues.

Experience working with macroeconomic time-series or panel data and applying statistical or econometric methods. Familiarity with methods such as forecasting, vector autoregressions, or panel-data analysis is an advantage.
Proficiency in at least one statistical programming environment such as Stata, R, Python, MATLAB, or EViews, with the ability to produce well-documented and reproducible work.
Ability to communicate research findings clearly in English. Spanish or another official language of the Bank is an advantage.
Ability to manage assigned research tasks independently while collaborating closely with economists and other researchers.
