Objectives of the Position

To sustain and grow BASF AP’s business in the Asia Pacific region, it is essential to strengthen regional regulatory science expertise in toxicology, consumer safety, environmental fate and ecotoxicology. This expertise is crucial for safeguarding our License-To-Operate in a changing regulatory environment, driven by an increasing number of dossiers submitted to support our commercial growth and more stringent data requirements across the region. Furthermore, developing new active ingredients tailored for the Asia market requires the involvement of regional experts in global development teams. There is also a rising demand for local risk assessments and customized mitigation proposals as part of our stewardship and sustainability initiatives.

This regional ecotoxicology function will provide support for environmental risk assessments, coordinate and monitor local ecotoxicological studies as needed, assist with dossier preparation, and deliver technical training to local regulatory teams across Asia-Pacific. As local studies can have an impact on global level the candidate will be embedded also in the global ecotox team to facilitate discussions and raise awareness for requirements of the region. Regulatory ecotox requirements are emerging across different countries in the APA region, thus the position will include scientific advocacy. The regional ecotoxicology scientist will report operationally to the corresponding Global Function in Limburgerhof, Germany and administratively to the regional Regulatory Science Group Leader in Beijing.

Main Tasks

- As part of a global community of ecotoxicologists, develop and implement regional ecotoxicology strategies to facilitate successful registrations in APA countries.

- Conduct environmental risk assessments according to local requirements for all APA countries as needed.

- Collaborate with global ecotoxicology team to design, plan and monitor locally conducted ecotoxicology studies, with a focus on China.

- Track, analyze and communicate regional government practices, policies and future trends regarding ecotoxicological data requirements and risk assessment approaches, advising the organization on potential impacts.

- Represent the APA region in BASF's global Ecotoxicology network, contributing to knowledge exchange and global responsibilities.

- Engage with authorities to enhance understanding of international ecotoxicology data requirements and advocate sound approaches to environmental risk assessment.

- Influential participation in industry associations and regulatory expert groups, advocating to develop robust local pesticide evaluation schemes in the region.

- Provide recommendations on safe use practices, mitigation options and appropriate stewardship measures related to environmental concerns; contribute to the development of stewardship programs, offering technical advice in relevant areas (i.e. bees, fish, birds)

- Deliver training to local staff to enhance their understanding of ecotoxicology and risk assessment principles

Minimum Education and Qualification Required for the Position

- A scientific background in biology, ecotoxicology, environmental sciences, or a related field is essential.

- Candidates must hold a PhD with a minimum of 3 years of experience, or a Master of Science degree with at least 8 years of experience in a relevant scientific discipline.

- Proven experience in designing, conducting, and interpreting ecotoxicological studies and/or conducting environmental risk assessments is required.

- International experience is preferred.

- A relevant industry background with substantial experience in the registration of chemicals or agrochemicals is advantageous.

- The ideal candidate is a creative, motivated team player with excellent communication skills and experience interacting with regulatory agencies and multidisciplinary teams. They should demonstrate a mature, self-confident demeanor and possess a high standard of ethical and intellectual integrity.

- Fluency in both written and spoken English is essential.
