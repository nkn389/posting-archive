It ensures end-to-end visibility, data integrity, and alignment between operational processes and corporate systems such as LESS and CP Stock.ABOUT LOGISTICS OFFICER P3 – GLOBAL COMMODITY TRACKING AT WFP

The position is based in the Supply Chain and Delivery Division (SCD) at Headquarters (Rome) within the Global Commodity Tracking and Fund Management Unit.

THE ROLE

The Logistics Officer (P3) is responsible for global coordination, governance, and system ownership of commodity tracking processes and platforms, ensuring standardization, data integrity, and effective system implementation across all operations.

The role supports the mainstreaming of CP Stock, strengthens operational performance through data-driven insights, and ensures consistent application of corporate policies through technical guidance, capacity development, and cross-functional coordination.
