responsibilities, including coordination of technical work, consultants and partners, tracking of implementation progress and deliverables, and maintenance of project information and supporting documentation in accordance with TNC requirements.&lt;/li&gt;&lt;li&gt;Monitoring and Evaluation Support and contribute to the monitoring, evaluation, and learning initiatives related to watershed and water resource assessment and analysis, coordinating achievements across multiple TNC projects in Mongolia while providing training and consulting services as necessary.&lt;/li&gt;&lt;li&gt;May work
