Organizational Context and Scope
The International Organization for Migration (IOM) is the UN Migration Agency. With 175 member states, it is committed to the principle that humane and orderly migration benefits migrants and society. Established in 1951 and now active in over 400 field locations worldwide, IOM works with partners, governments, and civil society to: 1. Assist in meeting the operational challenges of migration and human mobility; 2. Advance understanding of migration issues; 3. Encourage social and economic development through migration; and 4. Uphold the human dignity and well-being of migrants and mobile populations.
Burundi continues to experience complex displacement dynamics driven by returns of refugees, internal displacement, climate-related shocks, land pressures, and limited absorption capacity at community level. In this context, IOM supports government-led and community-based efforts to advance durable solutions for returnees, internally displaced persons, and host communities, with a strong focus on social cohesion, access to basic services, livelihoods, and local governance. IOM’s Durable Solutions portfolio in Burundi contributes to the humanitarian–development–peace nexus by strengthening local systems, supporting communal planning processes, and promoting inclusive recovery in displacement-affected areas.
Under the overall supervision of the Chief of Mission (CoM) Burundi and direct supervision of the Programme Coordinator, and in close coordination with the Regional Thematic Specialist (RTS) at the Regional Office (RO) for East, Horn and Southern Africa in Nairobi and relevant Divisions/Departments/Units at Headquarters (HQ), the Programme Officer (Durable Solutions) will support and coordinate the development, implementation, monitoring and reporting activities of the Durable Solutions Programme in the Burundi Country Office (CO).
In this role, the Programme Officer (Durable Solutions) will provide technical leadership and operational oversight for the implementation of Durable Solutions activities, ensuring strategic coherence across livelihoods, social cohesion and community engagement components. The Programme Officer (Durable Solutions) will lead day-to-day programme coordination, guide and supervise programme and field-based teams, and support operational planning, quality assurance and risk management. The Programme Officer (Durable Solutions) will play a key role in steering engagement with government counterparts, implementing partners and community stakeholders, contributing to the adoption of inclusive, evidence-based and government-led approaches to durable solutions, while ensuring timely delivery of results in compliance with IOM standards and donor requirements.
