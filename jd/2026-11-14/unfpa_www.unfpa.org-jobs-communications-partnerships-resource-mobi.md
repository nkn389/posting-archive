Job description

The Position:

The Programme Analyst, Partnerships, Resource Mobilization and Communications position is located in the UNFPA Lesotho Country Office and reports to the Head of Office. This position is responsible for providing routine operational and technical support tocommunication, advocacy, and resource mobilization strategy and processes for the Country Office.

How you can make a difference:

UNFPA is the lead United Nations agency for delivering a world where every pregnancy is intended, every childbirth is safe, and every young person's potential is fulfilled. The UNFPA Strategic Plan for 2026-2029 articulates the organization’s response to a complex global environment, providing a roadmap for resilience and renewal. It is designed to accelerate the implementation of the Programme of Action of the International Conference on Population and Development (ICPD) and the achievement of the Sustainable Development Goals by 2030. This mandate is pursued through a focus on four interconnected outcomes: ending the unmet need for family planning; ending preventable maternal deaths; ending gender-based violence and harmful practices; and adapting to demographic change through evidence and rights-based policies.

In a world where fundamental human rights are at risk, we need principled and ethical staff who embody these international norms and standards and will defend them courageously and with full conviction.

UNFPA is seeking candidates who transform, inspire, and deliver high-impact sustained results, ensuring effective external relations, communications, partnership-building and resource mobilization in a rapidly changing development and funding landscape. We need staff who are transparent, exceptional in how they manage the resources entrusted to them, and who commit to delivering excellence in programme results

Job Purpose:

The purpose of the role is to provide foundational support to communications, partnerships, and resource mobilization functionsn. The position willassist the Country Office’s capacity to strategically position itself, expand and diversify its partnership base, and effectively engage both traditional and non-traditional donors.

The Programme Analyst supports the day-to-day execution of communication and resource mobilization work plans. S/he assists in maintaining databases of partners and donors, tracking resource mobilization tools, and supporting the drafting of visibility materials under the guidance of senior office staff.

The Communications, Partnerships and RM Analyst supports Country Office visibility by executing social media updates, compiling press monitoring materials, maintaining the website, and handling logistics for media and partnership events.

You would be responsible for:

- Programme & operational support effectiveness:

- Support the implementation of visibility activities aligned with the priorities of the Lesotho Country Programme, in accordance with UNFPA guidance and under the supervision of office leadership.

- Provide logistical and administrative support for strategic partnership meetings, inter-agency working groups (e.g., UN Communications Group), and network interactions with local media and civil society.

- Assist in organizing logistics, workshops, and sensitization sessions for implementing partners and local journalists regarding UNFPA's mandate in Lesotho.

- Track compliance and maintain records for the Country Office communication work plan, ensuring routine updates and monitoring timelines.

- Content Support, Visibility & Knowledge Sharing:

- Support the rollout of existing communication and advocacy plans by drafting routine materials (e.g., social media posts, basic press releases, event summaries, and human-interest story drafts).

- Assist senior staff by gathering background information and routine media monitoring on local political, social, and economic trends relevant to UNFPA's work in Lesotho.

- Assist with logistics for media visits, project site photography, and maintaining the Country Office digital photo/video library.

- Maintain and update the UNFPA Lesotho Country Office website and social media channels with approved content.

- Participate in and provide administrative support to the UN Communications Group in Lesotho.

-

- Partnerships & Resource mobilization:

- Gather and assemble background data, donor profiles, and routine information regarding SRHR, GBV, and population development to support senior staff in partner discussions.

- Compile and track updates on donor policies, national plans, and funding announcements, organizing files for team review.

- Support the compilation of due-diligence screening materials and background documentation on potential partners in accordance with UN Secretariat and UNFPA policies.

- Assist in preparing routine documentation (e.g., standard briefing packs, standard programme summaries, slide decks, and meeting minutes) for donor and stakeholder engagementss

- General Support:

- Stand-in for other team members on selected functions as may be required.

-
Carry out any other duties as may be required by UNFPA leadership.
