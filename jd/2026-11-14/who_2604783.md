Purpose of the consultancy
The purpose of this vacancy is to establish a roster of qualified consultants who may be considered for assignments supporting the Asia-Pacific Hub of the Alliance for Transformative Action on Climate and Health (ATACH). Consultants will contribute to WHO regional and country support for climate-resilient, low-carbon and environmentally sustainable health systems.
The consultant will support practical country and regional action to strengthen health-system resilience to climate and environmental risks; reduce greenhouse gas emissions and environmental impacts from health-care delivery, including infrastructure, procurement, supply chains and health-care waste; and strengthen access to technical expertise, evidence, partnerships and financing.
Based within WHO ACE, the consultant will support implementation of regional and country priorities, facilitate knowledge exchange and peer learning, and document practical approaches, results and lessons to inform WHO policy and technical cooperation, investment and scale-up across the Region.
Successful candidates may subsequently be selected for consultancy assignments in one or more areas of work described below. Inclusion in the roster does not guarantee selection for a consultancy contract and creates no commitment on either side. Each assignment will be governed by separate terms of reference specifying the scope, deliverables, duration, location, travel and remuneration.
Background
Climate change is increasingly affecting health and placing additional pressure on health systems across the Western Pacific Region. Extreme weather, rising temperatures and other climate and environmental hazards threaten health infrastructure, supply chains, the health workforce and continuity of essential services. Health systems must therefore become more resilient while reducing greenhouse gas emissions and wider environmental impacts.
The WHO Asia-Pacific Centre for Environment and Health (WHO ACE) supports Member States to translate global climate and health commitments into practical action. In line with the WHO ACE Strategic Plan 2025-2030, this includes technical cooperation, evidence and guidance, capacity building, partnerships and investment for climate-resilient, low-carbon and environmentally sustainable health systems.
ATACH is a WHO-hosted, Member State-led platform supporting implementation of climate and health commitments. The Asia-Pacific ATACH Hub, based within WHO ACE in Seoul, connects Member State priorities with technical expertise, tools, partners and financing opportunities; facilitates regional cooperation and peer learning; and captures implementation experience to inform policy, investment and scale-up.
There are three main workstreams:

- Building climate resilient and sustainable health systems and health care facilities

- Building low-carbon health systems

- Foresight and Intelligence for decision making

Deliverables
Output 1: Implement the ATACH-hub workplan

- Work with WHO country offices and technical teams to identify Member State priorities, implementation challenges and opportunities for technical cooperation related to climate-resilient, low-carbon and environmentally sustainable health systems.
- Support Member States through WHO country offices in the design and implementation of priority actions.
- Map relevant ATACH, WHO and partner initiatives, tools and examples related to climate resilient and low carbon health systems to country priorities.
- Support regional and country-level dialogue on practical pathways to reduce emissions across procurement, production, packaging, transport, storage, use and end-of-life management of health products.
- Coordinate technical contributions to guidance, policy briefs, webinars and other knowledge products.
Output 2: Compile and develop country and partner case studies

- Identify and prioritize examples of health system initiatives, and support publishing case studies that describe the context, intervention, implementation process, partners, results, enabling factors, challenges, lessons learned and potential for replication or scale-up.
- Engage Member States, WHO teams and partners to collect, verify and synthesize quantitative and qualitative information.
- Maintain an organized case study repository and prepare cross-case synthesis of common lessons, gaps and recommendations.
- Ensure documented claims are evidence-based, appropriately attributed and cleared by contributing countries and partners before publication or dissemination.
Output 3: Support communication, knowledge exchange and uptake

- Prepare presentations, briefing notes, web content and other communication materials to share ATACH-hub activities and case study findings.
- Organize or support webinars, peer-learning sessions and technical discussions on all areas of ATACH.
- Contribute regional evidence and examples to ATACH events, global publications and major climate and health milestones.
- Support dissemination and use of case studies in country dialogue, technical cooperation, partnership development and resource mobilization.
- Develop key publications for delivery each year.
Output 4: Strengthen ATACH Hub coordination and country support

- Participating in relevant global ATACH working groups and initiatives.
- Coordinate Western Pacific regional and country inputs to ATACH processes, events, communities of practice and knowledge-exchange activities.
- Support Member States to engage with ATACH partners and members, identify priority implementation needs, and access relevant technical resources.
- Maintain a regional database of country participation, priorities, technical assistance needs, planned activities and progress.
- Coordinate with WHO headquarters, regional offices and technical teams to promote coherent support, consistent messaging and two-way exchange between global and regional levels.
