Overview

The World Vegetable Center (WorldVeg) is a non-profit, autonomous international agricultural research center headquartered in Taiwan and with five regional offices worldwide. WorldVeg delivers science and innovation for enhanced vegetable production and consumption in a rapidly changing world. For more information, please visit our website: www.worldveg.org and our new global strategy.
WorldVeg seeks a dynamic, innovative, and impact-driven leader to serve as Program Director for the recently launched program “HortiFuture: Seed Systems Supporting the Growth of West Africa’s Horticultural Sector”. The program is aimed at creating an inclusive and competitive vegetable and potato seed sector that provides smallholder farmers with access to affordable, quality seeds of a wide range of adapted varieties. Funded by the government of the Netherlands, the five-year program operates in Benin, Côte d’Ivoire, Ghana, and Senegal. Using an Integrated Seed Sector Development (ISSD) approach, HortiFuture strengthens the seed sector in the partner countries along four intervention domains: 1. seed market development; 2. biodiversity, varieties, and seed supply; 3. seed sector regulation; and 4. seed sector governance. The program supports international, domestic, and community-based seed producers through business development, variety testing, biodiversity conservation, improved seed production, regulatory reform, and enhanced sector coordination.
This senior position requires technical expertise in seed sector development, thought leadership, and excellent management skills. The Program Director will take full accountability for the delivery of the program against its approved results framework. She/He will lead a multi-country project implementation team of international and local partners, each contributing unique expertise and experience, ensuring smooth collaboration, communication, and continuous progress toward achieving the program objectives.
The Program Director will be based at the WorldVeg regional office for West and Central Africa in Cotonou, Benin. The position will report to the Regional Director for West and Central Africa and the Research Director for Social Sciences and Nutrition, with strategic oversight from a WorldVeg Program Committee chaired by the Director General. The respective reporting responsibilities and delegated decision-making authorities will be confirmed before appointment. The Director will be supported by program staff responsible for finance, procurement, administration, communications and monitoring, evaluation and learning (MEL), alongside Country Coordinators and country teams.

- Direct HortiFuture as an integrated five-year program across four countries and multiple implementing partners, with overall accountability for delivery against approved results, budgets and contractual commitments
- Lead the project team and partners to produce annual and multi-year workplans, ensuring plans are realistic, adequately resourced, and have clear, achievable milestones.
- Ensure that the program has the right level of staffing and delivery arrangements.
- Resolve or escalate significant implementation challenges to supervisors.
- Exercise day-to-day program management authority within approved workplans, budgets and delegated limits; seek appropriate approval for major financial, contractual and strategic changes.

- Team leadership and organizational effectiveness

- Build and lead a high-performing and delivery-focused team.
- Ensure that individual responsibilities and supervisory relationships are known and effective.
- Ensure alignment and effective communication between the program coordination office in Benin, country teams, and partners.
- Identify training needs and design a capacity development plan to ensure that team members have requisite skills.
- Conduct performance evaluations based on agreed metrics in line with the WorldVeg performance management framework.

- Build strong working relationships with WorldVeg business units, such as finance, procurement, R&D Support, and human resources, to ensure systems, subgrant agreements, and service contracts support effective program delivery.
- Identify financial risks and develop and manage the mitigation plan.
- Track program budgets, monitor burn rates, and manage fluctuations caused by external factors.
- Ensure audit readiness by communicating clearly with the team and preparing auditable documents within parameters agreed upon with the funder.
- Oversee the preparation of periodic annual financial reports for submission to the funder on a timely basis.
- Oversee partner agreements, service contracts and procurement planning, ensuring compliance with donor requirements and WorldVeg procedures.

- Partnership management

- Serve as a day-to-day program contact with the funder in coordination with the program management team and WorldVeg senior management.
- Coordinate implementation partners, country teams, seed companies, governments, and other stakeholders around shared goals and priorities, with clear responsibilities and accountability.

- Technical oversight

- Ensure the program implementation is technically sound and that all outputs are of high quality and in line with the program’ results commitments.
- Ensure that the program team, comprising WorldVeg staff and local and international partners, has all relevant technical and socioeconomic expertise, applies interdisciplinary thinking, and builds on state-of-the-art knowledge of seed systems development.

- At least an MSc or MA in business management, agronomy, plant science, agricultural economics, or another relevant field.
- Strong track record in contract management, financial oversight, procurement, donor reporting, risk management and results-based delivery.
- Sound understanding of the seed sector in West Africa, including the vegetable seed sector, particularly regarding regulations and governance issues.
- Full professional proficiency in written and spoken English and French.
- Culturally agile team builder comfortable with extensive regional travel and able to work independently.

Required Experience

- Proven experience coordinating complex programs across countries and implementing partners, including leadership of multidisciplinary and remotely distributed teams.
