Duties and Responsibilities
The United Nations Environment Programme (UNEP) is the leading global environmental authority that sets the global environmental agenda, promotes the coherent implementation of the environmental dimension of sustainable development within the United Nations system and serves as an authoritative advocate for the global environment.UNEP-CCC is a leading international advisory institution on energy, climate and sustainable development. Its work focuses on helping developing countries and emerging economies transition to low-carbon development pathways and supporting the integration of climate resilience into national development. UNEP-CCC is actively involved in the implementation of the UN Environment Climate Change Strategy and Energy Programme. The NDC Partnership Action Fund (PAF) implementation team in Moldova requires a National Consultant for Air Pollutant Emission Projections with a focus on energy related emissions. Moldova is preparing its 2026–2029 National Air Pollution Control Programme to meet national emission reduction targets for key air pollutants by 2030 and beyond. Under the NDC Partnership project, the consultant will develop and update relevant sectoral air pollutant inventory and emission projections to 2040 using international reporting standards and national energy data. The role is critical to supporting the Government of Moldova in delivering its air quality and climate commitments. The Republic of Moldova is developing the National Air Pollution Control Programme (NAPCP) for 2026–2029 in accordance with Article 8(d) of Law No. 98/2022 on Atmospheric Air Quality. The NAPCP addresses the national Emission Reduction Commitments (ERCs) established by Government Decision No. HG593/2024 of 28 August 2024 for nitrogen oxides (NOₓ), non-methane volatile organic compounds (NMVOCs), sulphur dioxide (SO₂), ammonia (NH₃) and fine particulate matter (PM₂.₅), with reduction targets for 2030–2039 and from 2040 onwards, relative to 2020 emission levels. Under the NDC Partnership Action Fund (PAF) Moldova Project, the Consultant will prepare and update the Energy sector air pollutant emission inventory in the relevant Nomenclature for Reporting (NFR) tables and develop emission projections through 2040, in accordance with the UNECE Convention on Long-range Transboundary Air Pollution (CLRTAP) reporting requirements, Annex I to the 2023 CLRTAP Reporting Guidelines and the applicable EMEP/EEA air pollutant emission inventory guidebook methodology. The work will cover relevant stationary combustion and fugitive emission source categories allocated to the Energy sector, excluding residential combustion and transport sources assigned to the respective sectoral consultants. The assessment will address the five pollutants subject to national ERCs, as applicable to the sources concerned, as well as other pollutants included in the national inventory where relevant. Historical emission inventory data, national energy balances, fuel consumption statistics, energy demand projections, emission factors and documented assumptions will be used to prepare consistent and transparent estimates. Based on the historical emission inventory and the effects of policies and measures already implemented or adopted, the Consultant will prepare With Measures (WM) emission projections for the Energy sector for the relevant NAPCP target years, including 2030, 2035 and 2040. The WM projections will be assessed in the context of national ERCs to identify the Energy sector’s contribution to national emission trends and potential risks of non-compliance. With Additional Measures (WAM) emission projections will be prepared, where necessary, to assess the potential impact of additional policies and measures, including energy efficiency improvements, fuel switching, renewable energy deployment, modernisation of combustion technologies and measures to reduce fugitive emissions, where applicable. The assignment will support CLRTAP inventory reporting, implementation of the NAPCP 2026–2029, national air pollution control planning, assessment of progress towards the ERCs and identification of priorities for reducing emissions from energy production and use. The objective is to prepare transparent and technically sound air pollutant emission projections for the Energy sector for 2030, 2035 and 2040, disaggregated by pollutant and relevant NFR source category, under the With Measures (WM) scenario and, where necessary, the With Additional Measures (WAM) scenario.
