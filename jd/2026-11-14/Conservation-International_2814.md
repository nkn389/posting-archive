Climate and Rangeland Science Manager
Conservation International protects nature for the benefit of humanity. Through science, policy, fieldwork, and finance, we spotlight and secure the most important places in nature for the climate, biodiversity, and for people. With offices in 30 countries and projects in more than 100 countries, Conservation International partners with governments, companies, civil society, Indigenous peoples, and local communities to help people and nature thrive together.
Conservation South Africa (CSA), as a local affiliate of Conservation International (CI), is committed to helping societies adopt a more sustainable approach to development—one that considers and values nature at every turn and improves human well-being through the conservation of healthy ecosystems and the goods and services they provide. CSA seeks to influence policy, develop markets, engage the private sector, and support communities to develop and implement conservation- based economic growth models and long-term human well-being.
POSITION SUMMARY - This position is based in Matatiele, Eastern Cape. It is a fixed-term contract, renewable based on donor funding and performance. The contract will commence on or before 01 February 2027 and end 31 December 2028
Conservation South Africa is seeking an experienced and passionate Climate and Rangeland Science Manager to provide scientific and programme leadership across the Umzimvubu Landscape. This role offers an exciting opportunity to apply science, innovation and evidence-based decision-making to support large-scale landscape restoration, sustainable livestock production, climate resilience and community wellbeing.
Working at the intersection of science, implementation and policy, you will lead a technical team and work closely with communities, grazing associations, researchers, government stakeholders and implementation partners to ensure that restoration investments deliver measurable environmental and social impact.
RESPONSIBILITIES

- Provide scientific leadership on climate-resilient rangeland management and restoration initiatives.
- Lead ecological monitoring, climate adaptation and evidence-based decision-making processes
- Support sustainable grazing management, ecosystem restoration and landscape resilience interventions.
- Translate scientific and climate information into practical solutions for communities and programme teams.
- Build and manage relationships with research institutions, government partners, donors and other stakeholders.
- Lead, coach and develop a multidisciplinary technical team.
- Contribute to programme planning, budgeting, reporting and adaptive management processes.
WORKING CONDITIONS

- Typical office environment in a rural landscape.
- Travel to programme delivery sites across the landscape is required.
- Ability to work extended hours or travel as needed.

QUALIFICATIONS
Required

- Bachelor of Science degree in environmental science, Ecology, Rangeland Ecology, Natural Resource Management, Agricultural Science, Climate Science, or a related field.
- Minimum 5-6 years of relevant professional experience in management of conservation, restoration, climate adaptation, rangeland management, or related fields.
- At least 2 years’ experience leading projects, teams, consultants or multidisciplinary initiatives.
- Experience in ecological monitoring, data analysis, landscape restoration, or applies environmental science.
- Strong stakeholder engagement, partnership development and communication skills.
- Valid South African driver’s license.
Preferred

- Master's degree in a relevant discipline.
- Experience working within donor-funded conservation, climate resilience, or landscape restoration programmes.
- GIS, climate adaptation, or ecosystem restoration expertise.
