The Middle East and North Africa, Afghanistan, and Pakistan Region serves 22 countries across different income groups, of which 14 are active/potentially active IBRD/IDA borrowers (Afghanistan, Algeria, Djibouti, Egypt, Iran, Iraq, Jordan, Lebanon, Morocco, Pakistan, Syria, West Bank and Gaza, Tunisia, and Yemen), with a total of 251 projects and over US$51 bn in total commitment. Eight are users of the Region's non-lending services, mostly on a reimbursable basis (Bahrain, Kuwait, Libya, Malta, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, and the United Arab Emirates). The mission of the region is to foster sustainable growth with a focus on job creation and poverty reduction, economic and social inclusion, and good governance. The region attaches importance to creating a supportive work environment based on the values of teamwork, transparency, trust, and client service, underlined by professional excellence. The region places high value on diversity. The region is organized into seven Country Departments (CMUs), the Office of the Regional Chief Economist, and the Development Effectiveness Unit (MAPDE).

With an expanded regional strategy, the World Bank aims to help the region weather the current crises, move from stabilization to transformation, unlock its vast economic potential of youth and women, develop its human capital to adapt to global megatrends, and foster innovation. For more information, please visit www.worldbank.org/en/region/mena

People Vice Presidency
The People Vertical at the World Bank Group supports countries with their human development – to protect and invest in their people, enabling them to reach their full potential and enjoy a good quality life. Better lives and better futures are only possible when all people have equal access to quality health care, education and social protection, and when all people have the opportunity to build and draw on their human capital throughout life to learn, work, create businesses, have dignity and quality of life, even amid challenges.
When Countries strategically invest in people and the systems designed to protect and build human capital at scale, they unlock the wealth of nations and the potential of everyone. Entire societies are more resilient against economic downturns, climate change, and conflicts. When girls and women are given equal opportunities to develop their full potential, everyone benefits. And when people migrate, whether by choice or not, their human capital can make the critical difference between surviving and thriving.

The Regional Practice Director for People in MENAAP is based in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia and oversees the Education and Skills, Gender, Health, Social program in the region, which in turn are each led by Practice Managers.

The Regional Practice Director for People is looking for a Senior Executive Assistant to be based in Riyadh, KSA. The selected candidate will need to have broader regional experience, proven ability to work in a matrix environment, and will work with the RPD as well as the broader group of administrative and client services team (ACS) and country management units in the region and at headquarters.

Duties and Responsibilities:

• Responsible for highly proactive time, travel and calendar management for the RPD, including effective prioritization and resolution of related conflicts and competing demands. Initiates dialogue with the RPD to proactively resolve scheduling complexities and conflicts.
• Responsible for the day-to-day supervision and coordination of the RPD’s workflow in close coordination with the Program Assistant for the Portfolio and Operations teams, the Program Assistants for regional Practice Managers, and key staff members who contribute to the RPD’s front office.
• In consultation with other colleagues, screens and prioritizes invitations received for the RPD and senior management (e.g., for speaking engagements) and make recommendations to the RPD.
• Ensures quality assurance and operational accuracy of all documents requiring the RPD’s signature. Guides staff on official formatting and standard protocol in processing executive correspondence and briefing materials. Works closely with the Program Assistant for the Portfolio and Operations teams to ensure all operations-related correspondence conforms with operational guidelines.
• Provides general research support on a range of topics on behalf of RPD utilizing all relevant software programs. Participates in regular Management Team meetings as requested by the RPD.
• Where requested by the RPD, liaises with senior officials both inside and outside the World Bank Group including a wide variety of high-level contacts in government offices, other international organizations, NGOs and the public sector.
• Establishes and maintains contact with executive assistants of all relevant clients of the RPDs and with the executive assistants of the MENAAP RVP and DSO.
• Independently responds to diverse inquiries and makes decisions when multiple courses of action are possible. Keeps relevant parties informed of actions taken by the RPD by providing pertinent information, reports or status updates.
• Supports the RPD in ensuring that management decisions are properly communicated, carried out and delivered in a timely manner.
• Stays informed on technological developments as introduced by the World Bank Group; makes sure all staff in the RPD’s office are informed.
• Contributes to office administration, maintains and updates contact lists, the filing system, and ensures the general efficient functioning of the RPD’s office, including ordering supplies and materials needed for the smooth running of the Office and any other necessary tasks.
• Assists the RPD with the preparation of mission materials, including through the solicitation of preparatory materials from relevant colleagues.
• Assists the RPD with mission planning, scheduling and booking, including mission meetings and liaising with the program assistants of Country/Division Directors and other colleagues required for mission planning.
• Takes the lead in setting up relevant departmental and managerial meetings for the RPD.
• Takes responsibility for maintaining a high degree of organization and efficiency in the RPD’s front offices.

• Ability and willingness to work with a diverse set of colleagues, cooperating and working seamlessly with all key members of the MENAAP VPU, People VPU, and the CMU management teams.
