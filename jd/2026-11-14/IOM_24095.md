Project Context and Scope
The BRMM Remittances Phase III Project, financed by the UK’s Foreign Commonwealth & Development Office (FCDO) and implemented by IOM, builds on previous phases of IOM’s remittance programming and adopts a whole-of-corridor approach, linking interventions in countries of origin and destination. The project seeks to strengthen migrant and household financial capabilities, improve access to safe and affordable remittance channels, generate evidence to inform programming and policy, and leverage diaspora financing for productive community investments.
In Qatar, the project will focus on three complementary areas: remittance corridor research, mapping and knowledge products; digital financial literacy for Kenyan and Somali migrant workers; and diaspora outreach for clean investment. The consultant will design and conduct the evidence collection component through focus group discussion (FGDs) with the targeted migrant communities, analyze and prepare its findings, and subsequently design and facilitate the digital financial literacy training sessions for migrant workers. This integrated role will help ensure continuity between evidence generation and capacity building, with findings from the research directly informing the content, examples and areas of emphasis used in the workshop.

Tasks to be performed under this contract

- Design and conduct the research component through focus group discussions (FGDs) with Kenyan and Somali migrant workers in Qatar. The research shall explore remittance behaviours, financial practices, information needs, use of remittance channels, and challenges related to financial and digital inclusion. Develop a structured survey questionnaire, as appropriate, to support the research and ensure alignment with the objectives of the assignment.

- Analyse the research findings and prepare the research output. Analyse and document the findings from the FGDs and any related survey activities, identifying key trends, needs, challenges, and recommendations. The findings shall inform the design, content, and facilitation approach of the digital financial literacy training sessions.

- Design and conduct two separate digital financial literacy training sessions, targeting 40 Kenyan and 40 Somali migrant workers, respectively. The sessions shall be informed by the research findings and tailored to the identified needs and contexts of the respective participant groups.

- Ensure appropriate monitoring, documentation, and reporting throughout the assignment, including maintaining participant attendance records, collecting participant feedback, documenting key activities and outcomes, and submitting the required deliverables to IOM.
