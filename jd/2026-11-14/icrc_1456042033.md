Buyer 2 (800475)

What we do

Since 1863, the International Committee of the Red Cross (ICRC) has worked to preserve human dignity and relieve suffering caused by war and armed violence.

In collaboration with our Red Cross and Red Crescent partners around the world, we do everything we can to deliver life-saving aid, reconnect families and locate missing people – helping those who need it most, regardless of who they are and what side of the front line they are on.

We engage with authorities and armed forces on all sides, often confidentially, pressing for access to detainees to improve their living conditions and urging compliance with international humanitarian law to protect non-combatants, including from digital threats.

Purpose

The Buyer is to implement the category strategies in South Sudan and support the Country Procurement Manager to manage product assigned category management for the country.

S/He should assure that local implementation plans for the regional/global strategies as well as supplier panels are established, with the target to create best value for money for the ICRC.

The Buyer is responsible for the procurement of Engineering & Wathab Services, Vehicle Fleet related goods and services, Logistics 3PL services, Administration and Premises related supply and services (independent of the requesting department) and the ICRC Delegation in Juba as well as for the ICRC Sub Delegations and offices in South Sudan securing the best correlation of quality, price and delivery time.

Accountabilities & functional responsibilities

Procurement Planning and Coordination

- Plan and coordinate procurement and logistics files at the national level.

- Maintain contact with logistics focal points in sub-delegations for follow-up, guidance and quality assurance.

- Contribute to procurement and logistics strategy by anticipating needs and implementing national-level actions.

- Implement strategic purchasing orientations, with emphasis on quality, social and environmental (QSE) requirements.

- Report on national procurement and logistics files to the CPM and propose process improvements.

Purchasing and Supplier Management

- Source, assess and evaluate suppliers and update supplier master data.

- Anticipate needs and research the availability of goods and services.

- Follow up on quotations, tenders, supplier selection, negotiations, contracts and deliveries.

- Monitor supplier performance, conduct annual evaluations and maintain the supplier database.

- Conduct market surveys, price and quality analyses, and negotiate purchasing terms and conditions.

Order Management and Supply-Chain Coordination

- Participate in collaborative demand planning and integrate decisions into the purchase plan.

- Receive and analyse approved SDs or SPs and review procurement requests and technical specifications.

- Determine product specifications and sources of supply with Supply Chain Planners and SCOs.

- Follow up on procurement orders, ensuring timely delivery and approval of received goods and services.

- Liaise with supply-chain units on financial arrangements, imports, exports, transfers and warehousing.

Quotations, Tenders and Contract Management

- Obtain pro forma invoices and price offers and prepare requests for quotation (RFQs).

- Organise tenders, prepare supplier selection tables and ensure compliance with procurement procedures.

- Prepare purchase orders and contracts, ensure legal validity and obtain required approvals from Nairobi and/or Geneva (GVA).

Finance, Administration and Reporting

- Ensure compliance with financial procedures and expenditure authorisation requirements.

- Check supplier invoices and supporting documents for approval and payment.

- Ensure working advances are settled accurately and on time.

- Resolve quality claims before validating supplier invoices.

- Maintain accurate and auditable procurement files, databases, tender registers and follow-up sheets.

- Report market changes, product availability, shortages, price increases and procurement challenges.
