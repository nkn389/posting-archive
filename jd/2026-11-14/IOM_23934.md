Project Context and Scope
The International Organization for Migration (IOM), through the Japan International Cooperation Agency (JICA)-funded Resilient and Equitable Access to Care and Health (REACH-BaSulTa) Project, aims to improve equitable access to quality maternal, newborn, and child health (MNCH) services among vulnerable and underserved populations across Basilan, Sulu, and Tawi-Tawi. The project supports strengthening health systems, workforce capacity development, and improved access to PhilHealth benefits.
Despite progress in expanding PhilHealth coverage, significant gaps remain in BARMM, where only 41.4% of the household population was covered by PhilHealth according to the 2022 National Demographic and Health Survey (NDHS). Vulnerable populations continue to face barriers to enrolment and benefit utilization, including limited awareness, documentation requirements, and geographical inaccessibility.
As of 2026, all six target health facilities have obtained or maintained PhilHealth accreditation, including for applicable Maternity Care Package (MCP) and Newborn Care Package (NCP) benefits. However, continued technical support is needed to strengthen accreditation compliance, claims management, reimbursement processes, and effective utilization of PhilHealth benefits. Under Outcome 2, the project supports improved MNCH practices and health-seeking behaviors through strengthened access to financial protection and community-based PhilHealth awareness and enrolment initiatives.
Under the overall guidance of the Head of Sub-Office in Cotabato City and direct supervision of the National Migration Health Project Officer for MNCH, the Consultant will provide technical assistance in PhilHealth gap analysis, facility-specific capacity strengthening, accreditation compliance, technical training, and community awareness and enrolment support. The Consultant will also review existing beneficiary profiling and monitoring tools, support implementation of community registration activities, and document results and recommendations in coordination with relevant government counterparts.

Tasks to be performed under this contract
Consultant will provide technical advice and assistance to improve PhilHealth coverage, profiling, enrolment, service utilization, reimbursement, and claims management in the target areas. The assignment will include a review of existing assessments and data, technical support for community mapping and beneficiary profiling, support for community awareness and enrolment activities, capacity building, stakeholder coordination, monitoring and documentation of results. The Consultant shall complete the tasks and submit the deliverables specified below. The consultancy fee will be paid in three instalments, subject to IOM’s satisfactory review and acceptance of the deliverables associated with each instalment.

A. FIRST DELIVERABLES: Inception, PhilHealth Gap Analysis and Capacity Strengthening Preparation and Facility Readiness.

- Prepare and submit an Inception Report, outlining the detailed implementation work plan, proposed methodology, deliverables, coordination and stakeholder engagement, and risk management.
- Review and analyze existing assessments, baseline findings, profiling, and available PhilHealth-related data to identify key gaps affecting enrolment, utilization, claims management, reimbursement, accreditation compliance, and BEMONC and MNCH service delivery.
- Design comprehensive, health facility-specific PhilHealth training/capacity strengthening plans in each of the six selected health facilities that respond to gaps in technical, operational, and service delivery competencies. The result of this activity will be actionable, facility-level training blueprints that guide the project’s investments in workforce capacity development on PhilHealth. This covers the facility-level orientations and Training on PhilHealth Administrative System.
- Draft the Situational Analysis report and develop facility-specific PhilHealth accreditation and compliance strengthening roadmaps that outline priority actions, technical recommendations, capacity-building needs, and compliance measures to improve PhilHealth enrolment, service utilization, claims management, and sustainability of MCP and NCP in the six priority municipalities
- Prepare Standardized training and orientation package developed, including PowerPoint presentations, facilitator guides, and learning materials on BEmONC, PhilHealth accreditation requirements, and PhilHealth administrative systems for use in capacity-building activities.
- Prepare facility level orientation materials based on the identified needs, including proposed training topics, target participants, learning objectives, and delivery modalities to guide the subsequent capacity-building activities.
Activities shall be completed with all required deliverable reports submitted by email to IOM no later than 30 December 2026 (25 % of overall contract value), upon satisfactory completion and acceptance of the deliverable.
B. SECOND DELIVERABLES: Capacity Strengthening and Training Delivery

- Conduct six (6) facility-level orientation sessions across the six target health facilities on PhilHealth Maternity Care Package (MCP) and Newborn Care Package (NCP) certification standards, requirements, and procedures.
- Provide technical guidance and assistance to the target facilities in preparing for MCP and NCP certification, including compliance with relevant policies, clinical protocols, referral systems, documentation requirements, and patient safety standards.
- Organize and facilitate one (1) training session on PhilHealth administrative systems for at least 30 participants from the six target health facilities, based on the identified needs and priorities outlined in the facility-level assessments and capacity-strengthening plans.
- Utilize and, where necessary, adapt the training materials developed under the first deliverable to address facility-specific learning needs, without duplicating previously completed outputs.
- Review and refine, as necessary, existing community mapping, beneficiary profiling, tracking tools, and operational processes to strengthen the identification, enrolment, service utilization monitoring, and follow-up of PhilHealth-eligible and vulnerable populations, particularly women of reproductive age (WRA) in the target areas.
- Training Documentation and Reporting activities are required to prepare submit the following:
- Submit six (6) facility-level orientation reports and one (1) consolidated PhilHealth administrative systems training report, documenting the sessions conducted, participant attendance, key discussions, identified gaps, agreed action points, and recommendations.
- Include pre- and post-training assessment results, where applicable, to document knowledge improvement and inform subsequent capacity-strengthening activities.
- Submit revised community mapping, profiling, or beneficiary tracking tools, where modifications have been made, together with a brief summary of the changes and their intended application.

Activities shall be completed with all required deliverable reports submitted by email to IOM no later than 15 March 2027 (35% of overall contract value), upon satisfactory completion and acceptance of the deliverable.
C. THIRD DELIVERABLES: Community PhilHealth Implementation and Final Reporting Package

- Review existing PhilHealth information and materials, job aids, enrolment guides, orientation modules, and technical reference materials for technical accuracy, consistency with applicable PhilHealth policies, clarity, and suitability for their intended audiences. Identify gaps and prepare recommended revisions and adapted materials, distinguishing community-facing guidance on enrolment and benefit entitlements from health-worker guidance on documentation, benefit utilization, and claims processes.
- Prepare PhilHealth Community Awareness and Registration Implementation Package, comprising site-specific campaign mission plans, orientation materials adapted from existing resources, registration and documentation-support guidance, and monitoring templates. The package shall define implementation schedules, roles and responsibilities, logistical requirements, and arrangements for tracking registration outcomes, informed by the validated beneficiary priority list.
- Provide technical support in planning and implementation during the conduct of community-based PhilHealth awareness, education, and enrolment initiatives to improve understanding, access, and registration among target populations. 12 community-based registration drives will then be rolled out in strategic locations such as RHUs, barangay health stations, evacuation and transit centers, or community halls across the 6 target Health Facilities.
- Prepare and submit Consultancy Report Package documenting all completed deliverables, community profiling results, enrolment outcomes, reach of awareness activities, results of PhilHealth enrolment caravans, capacity-building activities, service utilization, challenges, good practices, lessons learned, and recommendations.
Activities shall be completed with all required deliverable reports submitted by email to IOM no later than 15 May 2027 (40% of overall contract value), upon satisfactory completion and acceptance of the deliverable.
