Housekeeper (800540)

What we do

Since 1863, the International Committee of the Red Cross (ICRC) has worked to preserve human dignity and relieve suffering caused by war and armed violence.

In collaboration with our Red Cross and Red Crescent partners around the world, we do everything we can to deliver life-saving aid, reconnect families and locate missing people – helping those who need it most, regardless of who they are and what side of the front line they are on.

We engage with authorities and armed forces on all sides, often confidentially, pressing for access to detainees to improve their living conditions and urging compliance with international humanitarian law to protect non-combatants, including from digital threats.

Purpose

The Housekeeper ensures that ICRC premises, including offices and residences, are maintained in a clean, hygienic and orderly condition, providing a comfortable and safe working and living environment for staff.

Accountabilities & functional responsibilities

- Washes bathrooms (floor, wall, mirror, sink and toilet), offices and residences with water and cleaning liquid.

- Checks frequently if the bathrooms are still clean.

- Empties paper basket in all rooms.

- Prepares coffee/tea and makes it available on the "coffee-break corner".

- Washes the cups and all cutlery used in the office.

- Checks and replenishes, when necessary, toilet paper, liquid hand soap, insecticide and air fresheners in all bathrooms.

- Washes towels from all bathrooms/residences.

- Wipes and washes all floors with water and cleaning liquid.

- Cleans desks, furniture, copy machines, scanners and printers.

- Cleans windows, doors and electric switches.

- Cleans fridges (inside and outside).

- Washes all curtains.

- Ensures that the outside of the Offices/Residences is always maintained in good condition and informs Supervisor when work is necessary.

- Reports any maintenance need (electricity, plumbing, lights) to Premises department.

- Provides cleaning/housekeeping/laundry services at ICRC premises.

- Informs Premises department about any issues regarding the premises (materials needed to carry out duties, malfunctioning equipment, damaged furniture or premises, etc.).

- Supports maintenance, logistics and other colleagues in a variety of manual tasks when assigned to the office.
