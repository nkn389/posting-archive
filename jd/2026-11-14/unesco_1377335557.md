OVERVIEW

Within the framework of the ITALEEM programme, jointly implemented with UNICEF and funded by EU, UNESCO Office in Iraq will support the Ministry of Education (MOE) to strengthen and implement institutionalized In-Service Training (INSET) for General Education teachers to enhance professional skills and competencies. In recognition of the rapidly changing world, reskilling and upskilling are critical for teachers and school directors to remain relevant and effective, MOE aim to ensure that teachers and school directors have the opportunity of continuous professional development (CPD) through several models and approaches - a balanced approach. This will enable them to upgrade their expertise (knowledge, skills, attitudes, behaviour) and qualifications, and move along their preferred career path, as well as strengthening education quality to align with the SDG 4 targets.

Teachers at all levels and across all subject areas need relevant inputs into their CPD trajectory, on a regular basis. Thus, the in-service teacher training and other forms of CPD in Iraq must be adapted to the range of professional contributions that a teacher is called upon to make in the classroom, for instance:

- Pedagogical practices and skills, including classroom management, participatory and experiential learning, continuous/formative assessment of learning, psycho-social support/Social Emotional Learning, use of digital/ICT/EdTech as an integrated teaching/learning resource.

- Cross-cutting areas, such as climate change and Education for Sustainable Development (ESD).

- Up-to-date subject knowledge for teachers whose focus is one or two subjects, mostly at intermediate and preparatory levels.

ASSIGNMENTS

Under the overall authority of the Director of UNESCO Iraq Office and the Chief of Education Unit, overall supervision of the ITALEEM Project Officer and day-to-day guidance from the Associate Project Officer (ITALEEM Outcome 3 Lead), the consultant will perform the following duties in close consultation with the TCPD Technical Working group:

- Support the review and development of in-service teacher training packages on the four priority areas: Classroom Management, Climate Change Education, Social Emotional Learning (SEL), and ICT integration in teaching and learning.

- Liaise with relevant stakeholders, plan and manage consultative meetings as well as training package review workshops.

- Plan, organize and coordinate Training of Trainers (ToT) activities related to the in-service teacher training packages, in close collaboration with the Outcome lead, partners, project Assistant and the M&E Officer.

- Plan, organize and coordinate cascade training for teachers, in close collaboration with the Programme Assistant and the M&E Officer, and follow up on the implementation of the training activities.

- Support the preparation and development of Calls for Partnerships (CfPs) to engage Civil Society Organizations (CSOs) and Non-Governmental Organizations (NGOs) as implementing partners for the delivery of Training of Trainers (ToT) and cascade training on the developed in-service teacher training packages.

- Develop the technical Terms of Reference for the CfPs, including the scope of work, expected outputs, implementation arrangements, timelines and technical requirements, in close coordination with the ITALEEM Outcome 3 Lead and relevant stakeholders.

- Provide technical input to UNESCO's evaluation of proposals submitted by prospective implementing partners, including assessments of their technical capacity, proposed training methodologies, and implementation plans. The consultant's role shall be strictly advisory, without voting rights or decision-making authority in the selection process. The consultant shall sign a conflict-of-interest declaration prior to reviewing any proposals.

- Coordinate and follow up with selected implementing partners, the Ministry of Education and relevant stakeholders to ensure the effective planning, implementation, monitoring and reporting of ToT and cascade training activities, in line with the approved training packages and project objectives

- Contribute and support the technical committee on TCPD and implementation of ongoing activities related to teacher education and training.

- Work in an integrated manner with ITALEEM team and liaise with the lead on the teacher’s component to collaborate on teacher-related issues within the programme.

- Contribute to the analysis of policies related to teachers, their education, and professional development.

- Prepare progress reports and follow up actions related to the assignment.

- Provide technical and administrative support for the publication process of the training packages according to UNESCO guidelines.

Contribute to the writing of reports, materials, and communication resources

DELIVERABLES

The consultant shall prepare a six-month workplan covering the review and rollout of INSET training packages, preparation of Calls for Partnerships (CfPs), and coordination and monitoring of Training of Trainers (ToT) and cascade training.

The assignment covers four INSET areas: Classroom Management, Climate Change Education, Social Emotional Learning (SEL) and ICT in teaching and learning. As the packages are at different stages, the consultant shall coordinate their review and rollout progressively, in close collaboration with the technical experts, Ministry of Education (MoE), UNESCO and relevant stakeholders.

Deliverables:

D1 – End of Month 1 – 10%
Inception report and six-month workplan, including the status of the four INSET packages, activities, timelines and milestones for their review and rollout, CfP preparation, and ToT and cascade training.

D2 – End of Month 2 – 20%
Technical Terms of Reference for the CfPs to engage implementing partners for ToT and cascade training, including scope of work, outputs, implementation arrangements, timelines and technical requirements.

D3 – End of Month 3 – 15%
Package review coordination report documenting consultations and technical review workshops with the experts, MoE and stakeholders for packages available up to Month 3, with consolidated feedback, agreed recommendations and follow-up actions.

D4 – End of Month 4 – 20%
ToT planning and coordination package for packages ready for rollout, including training arrangements, schedules, coordination with MoE and stakeholders, monitoring requirements, and documentation of additional package reviews in Month 4.

D5 – End of Month 5 – 20%
ToT and cascade training coordination and monitoring package, including implementation arrangements, coordination with MoE and implementing partners, monitoring tools, documentation of trainings held, and feedback and follow-up actions from additional package reviews in Month 5.

D6 – End of Month 6 – 15%
Final technical report covering package review coordination, CfP preparation, ToT and cascade training implementation and monitoring, completed outputs, achievements, outstanding activities, lessons learned and recommendations.

Acceptance of deliverables:
All deliverables shall be submitted in English to the ITALEEM Project Officer. UNESCO will review each deliverable within 10 working days and either accept it in writing or send comments. The consultant shall address comments within 5 working days. Payment will be released only after written acceptance by UNESCO.
