Org. Setting and Reporting
This position is located in the Office of the Director, Office of Intergovernmental Support and Coordination for Sustainable Development (OISC), Department of Economic and Social Affairs (DESA), OISC supports the work of the General Assembly, the Economic and Social Council, the High-level Political Forum on Sustainable Development (HLPF) and the Committee on Non-Governmental Organizations. More information can be found on our website: at https://www.un.org/ecosoc/en. The Information Systems Officer reports to the Programme Management Officer in the Office of the Director. The Office of the Director (OD) provides strategic leadership, management, and oversight of the Office of Intergovernmental Support and Coordination for Sustainable Development (OISC), ensuring effective support to the Economic and Social Council (ECOSOC) and the President of ECOSOC, the General Assembly, and the High-level Political Forum on Sustainable Development (HLPF). The Office oversees the coordination and delivery of substantive, policy, and organizational support for intergovernmental processes, promotes coherent follow-up and review of the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development, and facilitates engagement with Member States, the United Nations system, civil society, and other stakeholders. The Department of Economic and Social Affairs (DESA) supports the development pillar of the United Nations Secretariat by fostering international cooperation for sustainable development for all. Its work spans three mutually reinforcing areas: (a) providing substantive support on development issues to the General Assembly, the Economic and Social Council, its functional commissions and expert bodies; (b) monitoring and analysing global development trends, prospects and policy issues to inform decision-making; and (c) assisting Member States in translating commitments and policy frameworks agreed at United Nations conferences and summits into national policies and programmes, and strengthening their capacities to implement them. More information is available at www.un.org/desa

Responsibilities
Within limits of delegated authority and under the supervision of the Programme Management Officer, the Information Systems Officer carries out the following duties: • Manages projects involving feasibility studies, computer information, systems analysis, knowledge assessments, design, development and implementation of new, complex systems. • Assesses functionality of existing systems and proposes and implements tailored and considered solutions. • Takes into account user experience while implementing technical fixes and upgrades to existing systems. • Develops detailed computer information system and other functional specifications and user documentation for major systems. • Provides specialized advice to users, analyzing users’ requirements and translating these into new applications; determines application systems integration and linkage issues. • Develops and maintains, upgrades or enhances existing websites and computer information systems (mainly in Drupal); troubleshoots and provides continuing user support, including by resolving difficult problems, advising on the use of new techniques, monitoring transactions to measure performance and continued effectiveness of assigned systems, etc. • Develops and maintains computer programs that require integration of many interrelated systems and program elements; ensures appropriate data security and access controls considering both local and wide area issues. • Organizes and performs unit and integrated testing, designing and utilizing test bases; assists users in acceptance testing. • Develops training materials, operating and user manuals; trains others in assigned systems. • Coordinates with focal points in UN DESA, other UN offices, Agencies, Funds, and Programmes, and other stakeholders to maximize collaboration in the area of knowledge sharing, integration of systems and data flows, automation and information processing and analysis. • Provides guidance to, and may supervise, new/junior staff, consultants, etc. • Performs other duties as required.

Competencies
PROFESSIONALISM: Knowledge of information technology and knowledge management, particularly in systems analysis, database design and programming. Knowledge of several high-level programming languages and Content Management Systems including Drupal, and significant exposure to and demonstrated proficiency in all aspects of programming and analysis, including structured/object-oriented design, relational systems, scripting and query languages, document design and management, hardware and software requirements, systems facilities and execution protocols. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, to include proficiency in the development and implementation of systems of moderate size/complexity. Knowledge of interactive systems; good knowledge of organization’s information infrastructure and IT strategy as it relates to user area(s); independently maintains assigned systems and develops innovative approaches to resolve a wide range of issues/problems. Shows pride in work and in achievements; demonstrates professional competence and mastery of subject matter; is conscientious and efficient in meeting commitments, observing deadlines and achieving results; is motivated by professional rather than personal concerns; shows persistence when faced with difficult problems or challenges; remains calm in stressful situations. Takes responsibility for incorporating gender perspectives and ensuring the equal participation of women and men in all areas of work. Able to analyze and interpret data in support of decision-making and convey resulting information to management. TEAMWORK: Works collaboratively with colleagues to achieve organizational goals; solicits input by genuinely valuing others’ ideas and expertise; is willing to learn from others; places team agenda before personal agenda; supports and acts in accordance with final group decision, even when such decisions may not entirely reflect own position; shares credit for team accomplishments and accepts joint responsibility for team shortcomings. COMMITMENT TO CONTINUOUS LEARNING: Keeps abreast of new developments in own occupation/profession; actively seeks to develop oneself professionally and personally; contributes to the learning of colleagues and subordinates; shows willingness to learn from others; seeks feedback to learn and improve.

Education
An Advanced university degree (Master’s degree or equivalent degree) in computer science, information systems, mathematics, statistics or related field is required. A first-level university degree in combination with two additional years of qualifying work experience may be accepted in lieu of the advanced university degree.

Not available.

Work Experience
A minimum of five years of progressively responsible experience in planning, design, development, implementation and maintenance of computer information systems or related area is required. Experience in HTML, CSS, Javascript, PHP, mySQL database and experience working with Drupal 9/10 are required. Experience working with Drupal 11 is desirable. Experience in graphic design and editing is desirable. Experience building and maintaining Drupal themes using Twig is desirable. Experience with drush and AWS/Azure is desirable. Experience updating Drupal core and Drupal modules via Composer is desirable. Experience using Git and CI/CD deployment pipelines is desirable. Experience using Nightwatch.js to write and run automated tests is desirable. Experience in assessing and implementing strategies and computer systems for knowledge management, stakeholder communities of practice or partnerships in the field of sustainable development, is desirable.

Languages
English and French are the working languages of the United Nations Secretariat.
