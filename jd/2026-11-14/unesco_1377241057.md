ORGANIZATIONAL CONTEXT

The United Nations in Kuwait is implementing a joint programme entitled “Promoting Inclusive Employment and Empowerment” under the One UN approach and in alignment with Kuwait’s national development priorities.
The project brings together two complementary programme components implemented by multiple UN agencies:
• A joint UNESCO–ILO component focused on strengthening the inclusion of persons with disabilities in the world of work through improved policies, inclusive education and training, employer engagement, media inclusion, and data systems.
• A WHO–UNODC component focused on strengthening Kuwait’s national response to substance use through rehabilitation and reintegration services.
The UNESCO–ILO component will be implemented in partnership with the Ministry of Social Affairs (MoSA), the Public Authority for Disability Affairs (PADA), the Human Capacity Building Society for Social Development (HCBSSD) as well as other civil society organizations, employers’ organizations, training institutions, media institutions, and other national stakeholders.
The project aims to strengthen institutional capacities, improve enabling environments for inclusive employment, and enhance evidence-based policymaking through improved disability-inclusive data systems.
To support the implementation of the project, UNESCO seeks to recruit an Administrative Assistant based in Doha-Qatar, to provide administrative, operational, logistical, financial, and coordination support to the project team for the implementation of project activities in Kuwait.

MAIN TASKS AND SERVICES

Under the overall authority of the Director of the UNESCO Regional Office for the Gulf States and Yemen, direct supervision of the Social and Human Sciences (SHS) Programme Specialist, and in collaboration with the Project Coordinator, the incumbent shall support the implementation of the joint project on disability inclusion and empowerment in Kuwait.
In particular, the incumbent will undertake the following responsibilities:

• Provide administrative and operational support for the effective implementation of project activities and workplans.
• Assist in budget monitoring, expenditure tracking, and preparation of financial information related to project implementation.
• Support procurement and contractual processes, including preparation and follow-up of contracts, purchase requests, consultants’ documentation, and payment requests in compliance with UNESCO rules and procedures.
• Assist in organizing meetings, consultations, workshops, trainings, missions, and public events related to project implementation.
• Coordinate logistical arrangements for project activities, including venue arrangements, participant coordination, travel arrangements, accessibility requirements, and communication with stakeholders.
• Support the preparation and maintenance of project files, records, databases, and documentation systems.
• Assist in monitoring project timelines, deliverables, and administrative follow-up actions to support timely implementation.
• Draft routine correspondence, meeting notes, emails, and administrative documents in English and, where applicable, Arabic.
• Support communication and coordination with government counterparts, UN agencies, civil society organizations, experts, and implementing partners.
• Assist in the preparation of narrative and financial reports, presentations, briefing notes, and visibility materials related to project implementation.
• Support mission planning and travel arrangements for project personnel and consultants, including preparation and follow-up of travel authorizations and mission documentation.
• Ensure proper filing and archiving of administrative, procurement, contractual, and financial documentation.
• Provide support for monitoring and evaluation activities and project visibility initiatives.
• Perform any other related duties as required by the supervisor for the successful implementation of the project.
