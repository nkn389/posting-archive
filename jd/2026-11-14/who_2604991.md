OBJECTIVES OF THE PROGRAMME
The GMC Cluster supports WHO’s regional and country operations through coherent administrative, logistical, and operational frameworks, and application of the best practices in the areas of the Cluster's overall responsibility.
Through an integrated service delivery model, including a dedicated support hub, the Cluster supports country offices and the regional office, enhancing operational efficiency, consistency and responsiveness across the Region.
The objective of ITM is to carry out in an efficient and reliable manner all activities related to informatics, telecommunications and communication systems and technologies, at both strategic and operational levels for the African region of WHO.
DESCRIPTION OF DUTIES
The incumbent will be expected to perform the following duties:
1. Lead, manage and motivate a team of Professional and support ITM staff responsible for the design and delivery of all the ITM-related activities in the WHO African Region;
2. Provide leadership and oversight for the planning, development, implementation, and maintenance of ICT systems, policies, and strategies in the WHO African Region;
3. Lead the transformation of ICT into a driver of business innovation, promoting automation, real-time data applications, artificial intelligence, and standardization across all operations. Oversee the roll-out and optimization of cloud services and tailored connectivity solutions, enhancing service quality and fostering greater autonomy at country office level.
4. Guide the design and implementation of modern, flexible workplace solutions to support secure, seamless remote work and a digitally enabled workforce.
5. Strengthen capacities of local IT teams to support the gradual decentralization of IT services, empowering country offices to effectively manage and troubleshoot ICT challenges.
6. Oversee and coordinate Information and Communication Technology activities, procedures and activities within the region;
7. Ensure the streamlined operation of the ITM unit in alignment with WHO and AFRO strategic objectives;
8. Identify, recommend, develop, implement, and support cost-effective ITM solutions for all aspects of the organization;
9. Define and implement ITM standards, policies, procedures and best practices to ensure high quality of services across the region;
10. Coordinate the design, development, maintenance and upgrade of all ICT services and systems in the region. This includes the: Global Management System (GSM) and Business Management Services (BMS), WHO Identity System (WIMS), Global Private Network (GPN), Users' IT devices; wiring, switching and telecommunication infrastructure, data centres, web-based systems and corporate and health database applications and systems;
11. Coordinate the regional ICT steering committee and systematic technical review of ICT initiatives, in collaboration with WHO global IT, technical clusters and country offices, to reduce duplication and ensure alignment with organizational priorities, architecture and security standards;
12. Establish consolidated reporting of ICT expenditure and consumption, including cloud financial management (FinOps), in collaboration with finance and procurement, and identify opportunities to optimize cloud resources, applications and licences;
13. Develop and maintain a regional ICT service catalogue with standardized cloud and connectivity offerings, transparent costs and agreed service levels, adapting solutions to country operating conditions and monitoring user satisfaction and operational benefits;
14. Design, develop, implement and manage IT Business Continuity and Emergency plans for the Regional and Country offices;
15. Ensure ICT systems security and redundancy region wide.
