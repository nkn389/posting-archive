Purpose of Job

The Director, Banking, Finance and HR Technology is a key leadership role and is accountable for the technology implementation, operation and support within their defined business supporting capabilities including oversight of any outsourced provision including the solution architecture steer, IT Risk, and cyber security oversight of the allocated portfolio. The Director, Banking, Finance and HR Technology is responsible for the effective and efficient functioning of their capability areas, ensuring adherence to the principles of autonomy, self-management, a product led mind set, iterative delivery, adequate resourcing, and high technical standards and always operating within the practices and procedures of the department. This individual will have outstanding hands-on technology leadership and communication skills, a desire to help others learn and develop a calm and pragmatic manner, excellent vendor management skill, plus a superior, user-oriented approach to process and operations.

The role is also responsible for both implementation and operational processes to ensure quality, efficiency and agility goals are achieved. This role is a member of the Technology leadership team. This role contributes to the development and execution of the enterprise-wide IT strategy and ensures its alignment with the enterprise's business strategy and objectives.

Background

This role is a part of the Technology leadership team of the Bank and is influential both within the Department and wider. The role maintains their relevant Capabilities in as robust, efficient, and compliant way as possible, leads the development and deployment of new technologies, services, and operations in their capabilities, represents IT on major projects and initiatives. This role also oversees and steers the capability budget the Capability budget and seeks savings where appropriate
