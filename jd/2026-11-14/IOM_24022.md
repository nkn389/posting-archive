Project Context and Scope
The Sudan Integrated Social Sector Infrastructure Rehabilitation Project (SISSIRP) will rehabilitate and equip 10 skills development facilities at technical schools and vocational training centres in Aj Jazira, River Nile, White Nile and Sennar states. The investment may include rehabilitated learning and workshop spaces, workshop equipment, solar systems, computers and digital equipment. These assets will improve services only if school managers establish clear accountabilities, protect and maintain equipment, supervise teaching quality, manage risks and connect training to employment and enterprise opportunities.
The assignment supports SISSIRP Output Indicator 1.1.3 on vocational training centres that incorporate climate change, green growth and gender approaches and are restored, upgraded and equipped. It also contributes to gender sensitive capacity development of education and skills development personnel. Training attendance is supporting evidence; the required result is improved management practice demonstrated through verifiable school level systems, records and actions.
Delivery must respond to Sudan's conflict affected operating environment. The programme shall be practical, bilingual where required, accessible, offline-ready and realistic about constrained staffing, power, connectivity, transport, finance and institutional authority.
