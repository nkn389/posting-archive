Context:
Under the overall supervision of the Senior Programme Coordinator -DTM and the direct supervision of DTM Database Officer, the Senior Database Associate DTM will be responsible for supporting all aspects of the development and implementation of DTM databases and application systems, providing data analysis, and disseminating information that can translate into evidence for decision making. As well as on the following activities.

Required Qualifications and Experience

Education

- Bachelor’s degree, equivalent or higher in Information Technology, Computer Science, or related course with four years of relevant experience in data/information management, preferably in crisis and humanitarian situations.

Or

- High School diploma with six years of relevant experience in data/information management, preferably in crisis and humanitarian situations.

Experience

Experience with International Organizations, INGO or LNGOs is an advantage.

SKILLS

- Good database design and management skills (familiarity with SQL Server, MS Access, Excel)

- Solid knowledge of MS Office software products is required (especially MS Access, MS Excel, MS Power-point, MS Word).

- Backend programming skills (familiarity with one or more of Python, Java, JavaScript, Ruby, C#, and PHP, etc.) is required.

- Knowledge of interactive visualization software is required (Power BI, Tableau) is an advantage.

- The understanding of humanitarian indicators and their development is an advantage

- Good knowledge of GIS systems and supporting software (QGIS, ESRI ArcMap10.2 are required), and experience in the management of spatial/geographic data layers is an advantage.

- Knowledge/familiarity with: Google Earth, In-Design, Adobe Photoshop, and Illustrator is an advantage.

Languages

- For this position, fluency in English is required (oral and written).
- Working knowledge of Pashto or Dari is required.

Note: Qualified female candidates are highly encouraged to apply.
