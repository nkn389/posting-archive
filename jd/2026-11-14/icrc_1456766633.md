Trainee/Associate (800789)

What we do

Since 1863, the International Committee of the Red Cross (ICRC) has worked to preserve human dignity and relieve suffering caused by war and armed violence.

In collaboration with our Red Cross and Red Crescent partners around the world, we do everything we can to deliver life-saving aid, reconnect families and locate missing people – helping those who need it most, regardless of who they are and what side of the front line they are on.

We engage with authorities and armed forces on all sides, often confidentially, pressing for access to detainees to improve their living conditions and urging compliance with international humanitarian law to protect non-combatants, including from digital threats.

Purpose

Representing the ICRC to the EU, NATO and the Kingdom of Belgium, the ICRC Brussels Delegation aims to, among others, ensure appropriate consideration for International Humanitarian Law (IHL) and discuss IHL implementation, clarification, and possible developments with these interlocutors. It also disseminates IHL towards relevant audiences. The ICRC Brussels Delegation is therefore recruiting a Legal Associate to support the IHL team in the accomplishment of these objectives.

Main duties and responsibilities

- Supports the IHL team in developing briefings/speaking notes on IHL-related matters for high-level meetings;

- Prepares and organises the Bruges Colloquium (IHL Colloquium co-organised by the College of Europe and the ICRC), and drafts the Colloquium proceedings;

- Supports the organisation and participates in meetings, conferences, trainings and events (including drafting concept notes, contacting speakers, drafting reports, delivering training, etc.) and reports internally;

- Monitors, conducts research and drafts analysis on IHL practice and developments at EU, NATO and Belgium level;

- Interacts with other delegation colleagues of the EU and NATO teams and provides IHL input where needed;

- Supports the Senior Legal Advisor and the Legal Advisor in the preparation, participation and reporting of external engagements;

- Other administrative duties as required.
