Introduction
Established in 1951, IOM is a Related Organization of the United Nations and the leading UN agency in the field of migration. Working closely with governmental, intergovernmental, and non-governmental partners, IOM promotes humane and orderly migration for the benefit of all. It saves lives and protects people on the move, drives solutions to displacement, and facilitates pathways for regular migration, while providing services and advice to governments and migrants.

Organizational Context and Scope
Under the direct supervision of the Head of Programmes (HoP), the overall supervision of the Chief of Mission (CoM), and the overall guidance and technical support of the Regional Thematic Specialist (RTS) on Protection (PxD), the Protection Coordinator coordinates and support the implementation of protection institutional policy in the Rwanda Country Office (CO). The Global Assisted Voluntary Return (AVR) program aims to provide Assisted Voluntary Return (AVR) to migrants in Rwanda, ensuring that returns are safe, dignified, and based on the migrants’ free, prior, and informed consent. Additionally, the program offers independent, needs-based post-arrival assistance at the request of countries of origin or receiving countries, always with the explicit consent of the migrants.

Specifically, the Protection Coordinator provides oversight, quality control, and thematic coordination and support to promote consistent, cohesive, and quality evidence-based programming while fostering opportunities and risks as well as knowledge sharing.
