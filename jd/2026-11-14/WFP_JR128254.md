ORGANIZATIONAL CONTEXT:
This job is found in Harare, WFP Zimbabwe Country Office. The job holder reports to the Head of Supply Chain, or the designate. Job holder operates with a high degree of independence, and guide staff to ensure effective procurement services including planning, organization and coordination, and setting objectives.

JOB PURPOSE

To lead transparent, timely and cost-effective procurement of food, non-food items and services for WFP Zimbabwe, while strengthening planning, market intelligence, compliance, risk management and cross-functional collaboration. The role supports programme delivery and emergency preparedness through an integrated supply chain approach.

ACCOUNTABILITIES/RESPONSIBILITIES:

- Lead and manage the Country Office Supplier Relationship Management (SRM), Vendor Management and Donor Vetting functions.
- Lead supplier registration, due diligence, donor vetting, re-vetting, exemption and extension processes
- Develop and implement the Country Office Supplier Relationship Management Strategy, Roadmap and SOPs
- Lead supplier performance management activities, including supplier segmentation, evaluations, performance reviews, corrective action plans and supplier development initiatives.
- Manage supplier databases, vetting records, reporting tools and documentation repositories, ensuring data integrity, accuracy and audit readiness.
- Conduct market research, supplier mapping and supplier risk assessments to strengthen the supplier base, improve market intelligence and support procurement planning.
- Develop supplier performance dashboards, compliance reports and analytical products, providing recommendations to management on supplier performance, risks and opportunities.
- Serve as the focal point for supplier management and donor vetting matters, providing technical advice and guidance to Procurement, Programme, Finance, Partnerships and other stakeholders.
- Lead supplier engagement and capacity-building initiatives to improve supplier performance, compliance and understanding of WFP requirements.
- Support audits, donor reviews and compliance exercises, ensuring timely provision of information and implementation of agreed corrective actions.
- Perform other related tasks as required.
QUALIFICATIONS & EXPERIENCE REQUIRED:

Experience:

- Experience working in the international development/humanitarian sectors an advantage.
