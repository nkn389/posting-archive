&lt;p&gt;&lt;b&gt;YOUR POSITION WITH TNC&lt;/b&gt;&lt;/p&gt;&lt;p&gt;The Land Management Fellow performs and participates in preserve operations including the maintenance, management, development and coordination of conservation programs. As we gain more insight into the evolving and complex threats to the lands and waters on which all life depends, the responsibilities we carry in the act of stewardship must evolve as well. Across the 30,000&#43; acres that TNC manages in Vermont, it is critical to expand our understanding, learn from the landscape, and adapt to necessary changes to honor the conservation legacy of our home. The Land Management Fellow (2-year limited term position) will be in service of this goal as we further our capacity to manage lands and waters with attention and care.&lt;/p&gt;&lt;br /&gt;&lt;p&gt;The Nature Conservancy in Vermont has created this fellowship position with the goal of fostering graduates who are pursuing careers in conservation. Early career and first-generation college students are strongly encouraged to apply. This is a two-year position, with priority given to recent graduates with a Bachelor’s degree. &lt;/p&gt;&lt;br /&gt;&lt;p&gt;&lt;b&gt;ESSENTIAL FUNCTIONS&lt;/b&gt;&lt;/p&gt;&lt;p&gt;The Fellow’s role will focus on advancing our strategic goals and improving the conditions of our land management. They will ensure the coordination, implementation, and revision of the management plans that underlie stewardship activities. The Fellow will participate in essential field operations and conduct follow-up office work to organize and maintain data and records. The Fellow will be based and supervised in our Poultney, Vermont office, work in a hybrid office environment with several days per week spent working out of the office and several days working in the field in southwestern VT and execute the following responsibilities:&lt;/p&gt;&lt;br /&gt;&lt;p&gt;&lt;b&gt;RESPONSIBILITIES &amp; SCOPE&lt;/b&gt;&lt;/p&gt;&lt;ul&gt;&lt;li&gt;Lead or monitor work groups consisting of staff, interns, and volunteers covering a wide geographic area.&lt;/li&gt;&lt;li&gt;Support natural area management planning and projects designed to improve on-site conditions.&lt;/li&gt;&lt;li&gt;Coordinate the effort to standardize the information and data that comprises our management plans. &lt;/li&gt;&lt;li&gt;Gather and maintain reports and data. &lt;/li&gt;&lt;li&gt;Learn and share the stories of our natural areas to deepen public understanding.&lt;/li&gt;&lt;li&gt;Network with diverse groups, including land owners, conservation partners, government officials, donors, board members and the general public to recruit support for the Conservancy and publicize Conservancy programs/preserves. &lt;/li&gt;&lt;li&gt;May work under minimal and/or distant supervision; may supervise interns or volunteers; may make some independent decisions based on analysis, experience and context. &lt;/li&gt;&lt;li&gt;May work in variable weather conditions, at remote locations, on difficult and hazardous terrain and under physically demanding circumstances.&lt;/li&gt;&lt;li&gt;Must have valid driver’s license and good driving record. &lt;/li&gt;&lt;/ul&gt;&lt;br /&gt;&lt;p&gt;&lt;b&gt;MINIMUM QUALIFICATIONS&lt;/b&gt;&lt;/p&gt;&lt;ul&gt;&lt;li&gt;BA/BS degree in science-related field and 1 year related experience in custodial and ecological land management or similar field or equivalent combination of education and experience.&lt;/li&gt;&lt;li&gt;Experience with natural systems. &lt;/li&gt;&lt;li&gt;Experience in ecological land management principles.&lt;/li&gt;&lt;/ul&gt;&lt;br /&gt;&lt;p&gt;&lt;b&gt;DESIRED QUALIFICATIONS&lt;/b&gt;&lt;/p&gt;&lt;ul&gt;&lt;li&gt;Multi-lingual skills and multi-cultural or cross-cultural experience appreciated.&lt;/li&gt;&lt;li&gt;Recent Bachelor ‘s degree or equivalent within the last two years.&lt;/li&gt;&lt;li&gt;Experience and comfort working outside in a variety of conditions, some of which may be variable, remote, on hazardous terrain, and under physically demanding circumstances.&lt;/li&gt;&lt;li&gt;Demonstrated ability to work independently and collaboratively with respect to your and others’ schedules.&lt;/li&gt;&lt;li&gt;Strong commitment to organizing information, attention to detail, and adaptability. &lt;/li&gt;&lt;li&gt;Working knowledge of common software applications (e.g.; Word, Excel, Web browsers). &lt;/li&gt;&lt;li&gt;Communicating clearly via written, spoken and graphical means in English and other relevant languages.&lt;/li&gt;&lt;/ul&gt;&lt;br /&gt;&lt;p&gt;&lt;i&gt;This description is not designed to be a complete list of all duties and responsibilities required for this job.&lt;/i&gt;&lt;/p&gt;&lt;br /&gt;&lt;p&gt;&lt;i&gt;We encourage applicants who believe in our mission, even if you don’t meet every qualification listed within this job description.&lt;/i&gt;&lt;/p&gt;&lt;p style="text-align:inherit !important"&gt;&lt;/p&gt;&lt;p style="text-align:left !important"&gt;This position requires a valid driver&#39;s license and compliance with &lt;b&gt;TNC’s Auto Safety Program&lt;/b&gt;.
