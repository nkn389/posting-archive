About the job
As Director of Service Delivery, Quality, and Training, you will set the vision and strategy for Service Delivery, Quality Management, Training, Knowledge Management (KM), and Process Effectiveness across YouTube's Global Vendor Operations—helping keep YouTube safe while delivering great support to billions of viewers, creators, and partners. Sitting at the intersection of policy, product operations, and global vendor operations execution, you'll pair strategic vision with operational discipline to turn complex policy, safety, and regulatory goals into clear, scalable operations and build a culture of continuous improvement.
You will lead a globally distributed team of full-time Googlers. Through this team, you will also oversee our external vendor partners, who run scaled operations across dozens of Trust & Safety, Creator Support, and Consumer Support verticals. You will be accountable for protecting the platform, delivering high-precision quality, running efficient operations, and safeguarding the well-being of the extended workforce—all while hiring, coaching, and growing an exceptional leadership team. You will also partner closely with Risk, Compliance, Legal, Policy, and Engineering teams to uphold regulatory and operational governance, while supporting targeted AI/ML evaluation workflows across YouTube surfaces.
We're looking for an empathetic leader who thrives in fast-moving environments. You're just as comfortable diving into the details of quality metrics, capacity models, and vendor economics as you are partnering with VPs across Operations, Policy, Risk, Product, and Finance to work through tough trade-offs and lead lasting organizational change.

Responsibilities

- Set the global vision and multi-year roadmap for service delivery across regions and vendor partners. Guide SLA delivery, global capacity planning, multi-year OpEx budgets, and vendor footprint strategy.

- Build and run rigorous quality programs, statistical sampling, and regular policy calibration across content moderation, creator and consumer support, copyright, and scaled abuse—holding vendors accountable and fixing root causes fast when quality slips.

- Build modern training and KM programs that help new reviewers ramp faster, do their best work, and deliver consistent decisions.

- Foster a data-informed culture that balances quality, speed, cost, and reviewer well-being.

- Grow, mentor, and support a strong global leadership bench—helping your managers prioritize ruthlessly and develop their teams.
