Organizational Context and Scope
Within this framework, the Senior Communications Assistant (Multimedia) will play a key role in enhancing the programme’s visibility and storytelling impact. Under the overall supervision of the Senior Regional Programme Coordinator and the direct supervision of the Regional Media and Communications Lead, the incumbent will support the design, production, dissemination, and archiving of multimedia content that reflects the programme’s impact and aligns with IOM’s institutional standards and donor visibility requirements.
The incumbent will collaborate closely with country offices, implementing partners, and regional stakeholders to ensure that all multimedia outputs are technically sound, ethically produced, and strategically aligned with MPRR-SSA’s communications and visibility goals. The role is instrumental in reinforcing the programme’s regional narrative, supporting donor engagement, and amplifying the voices of migrants and returnees through high-quality visual storytelling.

Required Qualifications and Experience
Education

- University degree in Multimedia Production, Digital Media, Visual Communication, Journalism, Graphic Design, Communication Arts, or a related field from an accredited academic institution, with four years of relevant professional experience; or

- High school diploma with six years of relevant professional experience in photography, video production, graphic design, and multimedia software (e.g., Adobe Creative Suite).

Accredited Universities are those listed in the UNESCO World Higher Education Database.
Experience

- Proven experience in multimedia production (video shooting/editing, photography, design).

- Proficiency with Adobe Creative Suite (Premiere Pro, After Effects, Illustrator, Photoshop), Canva, and other editing tools.

- Experience working in humanitarian or development contexts.

- Familiarity with consent protocols, ethical storytelling, and digital asset management.

Skills

- Strong visual storytelling and post-production skills.

- Ability to work under pressure and meet deadlines independently.

- Capacity to coordinate with remote teams across countries.

- Knowledge of IOM branding and donor visibility requirements is a strong asset.
