Project Context and Scope
IOM in the Republic of Moldova is implementing the “Reducing community-level vulnerabilities to TIP in Moldova” project, which supports national efforts to strengthen the protection of and assistance to victims of crime, including victims of trafficking in human beings.
Access to identity, civil-status, travel, residence and other relevant documents can be essential for victims of trafficking to exercise their rights and access protection, assistance, justice and longer-term recovery support. In practice, documentation may involve several institutions and may be complicated by missing or expired documents, uncertainty regarding identity or residence status, procedural requirements, unclear institutional responsibilities or the specific protection circumstances of a victim.
MLSP, as the main beneficiary of this assignment and a key institution in the protection and referral system, requires a concise evidence base on the main documentation bottlenecks and possible avenues for resolving them. The assignment will combine a targeted legal and procedural review with analysis of selected anonymised cases and interviews with relevant stakeholders. It will document priority gaps and propose realistic next steps and a practical roadmap for MLSP-led dialogue and advocacy with the Public Services Agency, the Ministry of Justice and other competent institutions.
Two separate but complementary consultancy positions will be issued: one Lead Legal Expert and one Supporting Legal Expert. Both experts will work as a team, while remaining individually contracted for up to 10 working days each. The scope and division of work will be agreed with MLSP and IOM at inception and may be adapted within the available working days.

Tasks to be performed under this contract
The overall objective is to support MLSP in identifying and addressing priority legal and practical barriers related to the documentation of victims of trafficking in persons.
Under the overall supervision of the IOM Program Coordinator and in close coordination with the other Lead exper , MLSP, the expert will undertake the following:
• Agree on the priority documentation issues, methodology, institutions to be consulted, selected cases or scenarios and division of responsibilities.
• Conduct a targeted review of relevant legislation, regulations, administrative procedures, guidance and available materials concerning identity, civil status, residence, migration, travel and other documentation relevant to victims of trafficking.
• Analyse a limited selection of anonymised trafficking cases or case scenarios to identify recurring legal and procedural barriers and their consequences for victims.
• Conduct targeted interviews or consultations with relevant stakeholders, which may include MLSP, the Public Services Agency, the Ministry of Justice, migration and law-enforcement authorities, prosecutors, courts, civil-status bodies, consular actors, legal aid providers, specialised services and civil society organisations.
• Document priority legal, administrative, coordination and implementation gaps, including issues affecting Moldovan nationals, foreign victims and persons unable to obtain documents through ordinary procedures.
• Help distinguish issues that may be addressed through coordination, administrative practice or guidance from those that may require regulatory or legislative action.
• Contribute to concise, prioritised recommendations and a practical roadmap for MLSP, indicating suggested follow-up actions, institutions to be engaged and an indicative sequence for inter-institutional dialogue and advocacy.
• Present and discuss preliminary findings with MLSP, IOM and selected stakeholders through a focused validation meeting or workshop and incorporate relevant feedback.
• Provide the Lead Legal Expert with practical legal analysis, case-based observations, stakeholder interview inputs and drafting contributions according to the agreed division of work.
• Review and comment on the consolidated draft outputs and support the Lead Legal Expert in addressing practical, procedural and case-related gaps before final submission
