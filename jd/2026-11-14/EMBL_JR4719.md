Help shape a campus where world-class science can thrive.

EMBL-EBI is one of the world’s leading centres for biological data and bioinformatics, supporting scientists across the globe. Behind that work is a campus environment that enables people to do their best.

As we grow, expand and modernise, we are looking for a Campus Operations Manager to help shape and deliver safe, welcoming, sustainable and efficient workplace services.

Your role

Working closely with the Head of Campus Operations, you will combine strategic planning with operational leadership to ensure our services meet the needs of our people today and support future campus developments.

Your remit will span facilities management, reception, hospitality, catering, workplace experience and space utilisation. You will lead our Facilities and Reception teams, with five direct reports, and build effective partnerships with Wellcome Genome Campus Limited, EMBL colleagues across Europe and external service providers.

You will have the opportunity to:

- Help develop and deliver the Campus Operations Strategy, supporting organisational growth and workplace transformation.
- Improve workplace services through staff feedback, service reviews, data analysis and clear performance measures.
- Shape policies and standards for space utilisation, meeting rooms, access management and the wider workplace.
- Strengthen relationships with campus partners and suppliers, setting clear service expectations and monitoring delivery.
- Support sustainability objectives through the Green Committee and coordination of agreed actions.
- Lead business continuity planning and coordinate incident management preparations, working closely with the Head of Campus Operations and Health & Safety Manager.
- Develop your teams through effective delegation, professional development and succession planning.
- Represent EMBL-EBI in campus working groups and governance forums, deputising for the Head of Campus Operations where appropriate.

About you

You will be an experienced operational leader who can translate organisational priorities into practical improvements. Comfortable working across a complex organisation, you will bring sound judgement, a collaborative approach and a clear focus on the people who use our services.

You have

- A degree or equivalent professional experience in facilities management, workplace management, business management or a related discipline.
- Experience leading and developing teams, managing budgets and delivering operational change.
- A track record of managing outsourced services and building productive relationships with a range of stakeholders.
- Strong operational planning, contract management and continuous improvement skills.
- The ability to analyse data and produce clear reports that inform decisions.
- Excellent communication, organisational and IT skills, with the confidence to influence senior stakeholders and balance competing priorities.
- A commitment to integrity, accountability, respect and fairness.

You may also have

- Experience in a scientific, higher education or international environment
- Experience in business continuity, hospitality or workplace services.
- Relevant IWFM, project management or health and safety qualifications

Make an impact beyond day-to-day operations

This is an opportunity to shape how a leading scientific institute works: developing people, strengthening services and creating an environment that supports international collaboration and discovery.

If you bring a strategic outlook, practical leadership and a commitment to excellent workplace services, we would welcome your application.

Contract length: 3 years, renewable to a maximum of 9 years

Salary: Grade 6 or 7 depending on experience. Monthly salary from £3,861.91 (grade 6) or £4,395.66 (grade 7) after tax plus generous benefits and financial allowances based on individual circumstances. Excluding 12.99% personal pension and insurance contributions.
