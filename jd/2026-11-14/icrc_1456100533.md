Treasury Business Analyst

What we do

Since 1863, the International Committee of the Red Cross (ICRC) has worked to preserve human dignity and relieve suffering caused by war and armed violence.

In collaboration with our Red Cross and Red Crescent partners around the world, we do everything we can to deliver life-saving aid, reconnect families and locate missing people – helping those who need it most, regardless of who they are and what side of the front line they are on.

We engage with authorities and armed forces on all sides, often confidentially, pressing for access to detainees to improve their living conditions and urging compliance with international humanitarian law to protect non-combatants, including from digital threats.

Treasury at ICRC

Geneva Treasury is responsible for liquidity management including appropriate funding of ICRC operations, and manages foreign currency risk, liquidity risk, bank relationship risk, counterparty risk, management and monitoring of ICRC long term financial investments, insurance policies and global set up for operations, premises and general liability in Switzerland and the field, ad hoc financial topics managed jointly with other parts of the organization, back office monitoring of Fondation du CICR.

Purpose of the position

Under the supervision of the Head of Treasury, the Treasury Business Analyst provides analytical and operational support across the full range of Treasury activities.
The position holder will actively work on the ongoing transformation projects linked to the revamping of the insurance system and the monitoring of financial assets and support financial risk management, more specifically liquidity, FX and cash management, as part of the back-up arrangements within the team. The position holder works within the policies, limits and priorities set by the Head of Treasury and has no line management responsibility.

Main duties and responsibilities

General

- Contribute to the implementation of Treasury projects, within the scope, timelines and decisions set by the Head of Treasury

- Act as an operational point of contact for internal stakeholders (FAD, other departments, delegations) and external counterparts (banks, insurance brokers, insurers) on assigned topics

- Prepare clear analyses, briefing notes and recommendations for the Head of Treasury, and explain Treasury processes and requirements to internal users

Treasury focus

- Support the roll-out of the revamped insurance set-up across the organization: collect and consolidate insurance data from headquarters and delegations, follow up on policy renewals and claims with brokers and insurers, and contribute to user guidance

- Prepare the periodic monitoring and reporting of the ICRC’s long-term financial investments, check positions and performance data, and flag deviations to the Head of Treasury

- Contribute to daily liquidity, FX and cash management (cash positioning, liquidity forecasting, preparation of funding and FX operations) and act as back-up within approved limits and delegations of authority

- Act as a key user of the treasury management system: contribute to its deployment, document related processes and improve data quality and reporting

- Contribute to other Treasury projects and ad hoc analyses as requested by the Head of Treasury

Experience and Education background

- Master’s degree in finance, economics, business administration or a related quantitative field

- First professional experience, including internships or traineeships, in Treasury/Financial Services Industry/Banking/Insurance sector in an international environment

- Strong analytical and data skills (advanced Excel) and interest in technology and transformation projects; knowledge of treasury management systems or data analytics tools is an asset

- Fluency in English and working proficiency in French with excellent writing skills in both languages

Desired profile

- Strong interest in humanitarian activities and not-for-profit international organizations

- Excellent conceptual and organisational skills, analysis and robust planning discipline, ability to analyse and understand complex information flow

- Excellent interpersonal and communication skills and mental agility

- Strong team player and capacity for collaborative teamwork across disciplines and in a fast-moving environment

- Strong problem-solving skills, including innovative thinking

- Intellectual curiosity and learning agility, with the ability to quickly master new technical subjects (treasury, insurance, investments)

- Display of a strong sense of initiative, whilst being results-oriented and proactive
Versatile, pragmatic, practical, flexible and resilient, able to move between operational tasks and project work

- Unquestionable ethics, integrity and values

ICRC is a humanitarian organization, which works to protect and assist victims of armed conflicts. This position requires external contacts with counterparts outside of the ICRC. The holder of this position shall factor into their position a role of representative of the ICRC and is expected to behave appropriately at all times and in all places.

All members of staff should ensure that their attitude corresponds to ICRC principles, both during and after working hours.
