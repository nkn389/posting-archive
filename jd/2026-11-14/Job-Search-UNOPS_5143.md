Our vision is a world where everyone, everywhere, can lead a dignified life on a healthy planet, powered by sustainable energy. Job Specific Context

The main objectives of the Sustainable Cooling programme are: Provide advice, convene leaders and support policy progress on access to sustainable cooling; Develop tools and resources that enable partners to have improved decision making on access to sustainable cooling; Track and analyze access to sustainable cooling through Chilling Prospects; Mobilize new investment in sustainable cooling to meet the needs of the most vulnerable; Provide and lead communications efforts that improve understanding of access to sustainable cooling through #ThisIsCool.

Role Purpose

The Advisor Sustainable Cooling Investment plays a critical role within the Sustainable Cooling programme by leading the design, operationalization, and scaling of innovative financing mechanisms and investment frameworks. Working closely with governments, development finance institutions, private sector entities, and project developers, the Advisor Sustainable Cooling Investment directly supports:

- Market Enablement & Matchmaking: Driving the Sustainable Cooling Marketplace and disseminating investment guidance to address market barriers and connect solutions with investors in priority countries.
- Innovative Financing Instruments: Supporting the design, management, and operationalization of the Cool Finance Fund, focusing on results-based financing and revolving funds.
- Nature-Based Solutions: Developing financial incentive guidelines and investment frameworks for the Nature for Cooling Challenge (NCC) to assist target countries in creating bankable sustainable cooling projects.
- Targeted Project Support: Designing tailored financing mechanisms, such as local currency instruments, density bonuses, and blended finance structures to address urban heat risks and vulnerable populations.
- Strategic Partnerships: Contributing high-level technical expertise to SEforALL’s Energy Transition department, knowledge products, and multi-stakeholder investment dialogues.

This is a part-time retainer role, with an expected commitment of up to a total of 25 days over 12 months.

Functions / Key Results Expected

Under the supervision of the Director, Energy Solutions, the Advisor Sustainable Cooling Investment role will focus on the following activities:
Sustainable Cooling Marketplace

- Support the launch and dissemination of the ‘Investment Prospectus Guide’ for sustainable cooling in countries supported by the programme.
- Support the delivery of the Sustainable Cooling programme’s ‘Sustainable Cooling Marketplace’ in target countries, by working with the programme team to address market barriers to investment with data evidence, advising on project design and prioritization, and supporting matchmaking between solutions with investors in supported countries.
- Advise the Sustainable Cooling programme and Communities of Practice for Sustainable Cooling in supported countries on available sources of finance and innovation opportunities that can increase investment in sustainable cooling at the global and national level.
Financing Mechanisms for Sustainable Cooling

- Support the Sustainable Cooling programme’s efforts to leverage financial and business model innovation to increase investment in sustainable cooling technologies, with a particular focus on innovation benefiting vulnerable groups such as the rural and urban poor, women, and youth.
- Support the design, management, and operationalization of the Cool Finance Fund in an advisory capacity, with a focus on results-based financing, revolving funds and other innovative financing instruments for sustainable cooling.
- Advise the Sustainable Cooling programme and relevant multi-stakeholder platforms on capital sources, financial innovation, and market mechanisms to mobilize investment in sustainable cooling globally and nationally.
Financing Nature for Cooling

- Lead the development and implementation of financial incentive guidelines and investment frameworks for the Nature for Cooling Challenge (NCC), assisting target countries and developers in creating bankable sustainable cooling projects.
- Advise the Sustainable Cooling programme and relevant multi-stakeholder platforms on capital sources, financial innovation, and market mechanisms to mobilize investment in nature-based solutions for cooling globally and nationally.
Strategic Support and Partnerships

- Collaborate within the SEforALL Energy Transition department to expand organizational engagement in financing energy efficiency and cooling solutions across broader initiatives.
- Provide high-level technical and advisory input to internal and external partners, contributing to knowledge products, strategic proposals, and investment dialogues.
