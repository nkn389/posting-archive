The Team
We are looking for a Senior Business Systems Analyst to join our Enterprise Business Applications – Finance, Travel, Events, and Legal Applications team within IT. The mission of our team is to unleash the power of digital platforms to better support the core work of the foundation, accelerate the achievement of our goals and amplify the impact Gates Foundation can have on the world. This specific position will focus on supporting the foundation’s Strategic Sourcing and Procurement and Finance business capabilities and related business applications.

This role will be posted 11/12/2026 to 11/30/2026.
This is a full-time position based in Seattle, WA.

Your Role
As the Senior Business Systems Analyst, you will report to the Senior Manager, Information Technology, Enterprise Business Applications and be able to work in a hybrid remote work arrangement from our Seattle, WA office. You are a curious, analytical problem solver and trusted business partner who brings hands-on Coupa expertise to design and configure solutions to support finance and procurement solutions. You will partner with Global Payments, Strategic Sourcing, and Procurement Operations to turn business needs into end-to-end solutions across Coupa and connected applications, while also serving as service desk support for our finance applications. By documenting common issues and acting on the trends you see, you will prevent problems before they start and continuously improve how our finance platforms serve the business, supporting IT’s Technology Innovation and Operational Excellence objectives in a dynamic environment.

What You'll Do

- Partner with Global Payments, Strategic Sourcing, and Procurement Operations to understand their processes, pain points, and goals, and turn them into clear requirements and end-to-end solutions that span Coupa, Microsoft D365 Finance & Operations, and Salesforce.
- Design and configure Coupa across the source-to-pay lifecycle ([requisitions, purchase orders, invoicing, supplier management, sourcing events, contracts, and payments]), including workflows, approval chains, custom fields, forms, reports/analytics and roles and permissions.
- Architect how data and transactions flow between Coupa and upstream and downstream applications. This includes data mapping, master data alignment (such as suppliers, accounts, and chart of accounts values), error handling, and reconciliation.
- Own solutions from start to finish: requirements, design, configuration, integration testing across systems, user acceptance testing, release, and post-launch support.
- Serve as service desk support for our finance applications, triaging and resolving requests and incidents in Service Now, answering user questions, and escalating to vendors or other teams when needed, with a focus on timely, clear communication to the business.
- Act as the escalation point for complex issues that cross application boundaries, tracing a problem through the full transaction path to find the root cause and drive a durable fix rather than a workaround.
- Document recurring issues, common questions, and known errors in a searchable knowledge base, and turn them into user guides, FAQs, and runbooks that reduce repeat tickets.
- Analyze support trends to spot patterns early, then proactively recommend and deliver fixes such as configuration changes, workflow improvements, data cleanup, training, or process updates before small issues become big ones.
- Evaluate the impact of Coupa release cycles on connected processes, and recommend what to adopt and when, including emerging automation and AI capabilities.
- Help keep our controls strong by supporting segregation of duties, access reviews, and audit evidence across the connected applications.
- Create and maintain clear documentation, including configuration records, integration designs, data flow diagrams, test scripts, and runbooks.
- Mentor teammates and share Coupa, integration, support, and business analysis best practices across the team.

Your Experience

- Bachelor’s degree in information systems, finance, business, or a related field, or equivalent experience, plus 10+ years of experience as a business systems analyst or similar role supporting finance or procurement platforms.
- Hands-on technical experience with Coupa. You’ve configured and designed solutions, not just administered them.
- Experience designing solutions across integrated enterprise applications alongside Coupa, and an understanding of how a change in one system affects the others.
- Experience providing application or service desk support for business users, including triaging requests, troubleshooting, and working with ticketing tools.
- Communicates clearly with partners from different backgrounds, translating business needs into technical designs and technical tradeoffs into plain language.
- Strong working knowledge of source-to-pay and payment processes and how they connect to financial accounting, and to customer and account data, along with the controls that go with them.
- Technical depth in integrations and data (APIs, file-based interfaces, middleware, data mapping, and troubleshooting data issues across systems).
- Analytical and proactive. Notices patterns in support requests and data, documents what you find, and acts on it to prevent problems rather than only reacting to them.
- Strong initiative and organizational skills. Works autonomously and cross-functionally while staying flexible as priorities change.
- Models professional courage by engaging in trust-based debate, asking hard questions, and being willing to say no.
- Skilled at writing clear, testable user stories with well-defined acceptance criteria that capture business intent and gives the technical team what they need to deliver. Uses Jira to break down, prioritize, and track work, keeping backlogs, statuses, and dependencies current and visible to stakeholders.
- Preferred but not required: Coupa certifications; experience administering or configuring D365 Finance & Operations or Salesforce; familiarity with integration platforms such as Boomi, global payments or bank connectivity, and audit or internal control requirements.
