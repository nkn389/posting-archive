Background

This position is located in the Regional Office for Asia and the Pacific Section of the Regional Coordination Office Department. The Assistant Project Communication Officer will report to the Project Officer, Regional Office for Asia and Pacific. The focus of this position is to support the planning, development, and implementation of communication strategies to enhance visibility, strengthen stakeholder engagement, and knowledge sharing for the CREWS, ESCAP and Adaptation Fund projects in South Asia and South-East Asia.

This includes, but is not limited to, compliance with the WMO Visual Identity Guidelines, Social Media Guidelines, Media Engagement Guidelines, Editorial Standards, Branding Requirements, and any other applicable communications policies and procedures issued by WMO. The incumbent will be responsible for maintaining consistency in messaging, branding, and communications practices across all project-related outputs and activities, and coordinate with the Global Communications and Engagement team in WMO HQ.

Duties and Responsibilities

Within the delegated authority / under the direct supervision of the RAP Project Officer, the Assistant Project Communication Officer will be responsible for the following duties:

a. Assist in the planning and implementation of project activities and communication activities in support of project objectives.

b. Collaborate with the corporate WMO Global Communications and Engagement Team to support alignment of project communications with organizational messaging, branding, and engagement strategies. The role will contribute to broader WMO corporate communications efforts while supporting project-specific communication needs within Asia and the Pacific.

c. Support creation of key messaging and communication materials (including drafting reports, developing stories, flyers, brochures, and videos etc.) for internal and external audiences.

d. Draft stories and source multimedia content, including photos and videos from projects to be published globally to highlight impact of WMO projects in Asia and the Pacific.

e. Draft and edit communication materials such as press releases, newsletters, reports, and social media content.

f. Support preparation of presentations, speeches, and talking points.

g. Maintain and update digital platforms including websites and social media channels.

h. Assist in stakeholder engagement activities and coordination with partners.

i. Support organization of events, workshops, and campaigns.

j. Track and report on communication activities and outputs.

k. Document lessons learned, success stories, and best practices.

l. Perform other duties as required.

Experience:

A minimum of 1 year of progressively responsible experience related to project communications or support related roles.
