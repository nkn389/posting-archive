About this position

We are looking for a rigorous, innovative, and driven Economist Specialist . As an Economist, you will conduct cutting-edge applied economic research, generate high-impact empirical evidence, and provide technical guidance to shape public policies across Latin America and the Caribbean.

You will work in the Institutions for Development Sector (IFD) in the Vice Presidency for Sectors and Knowledge. This team is responsible for designing, supporting, and executing analytical and operational agendas that strengthen public sector governance, state capacity, digital transformation, citizen security and crime prevention, and efficient financial markets.

What you’ll do

- Conduct high-level empirical research in public economics, applied microeconomics, and development economics, focusing on citizen security, state capacity, digitalization, and financial markets.

- Lead and contribute to flagship institutional publications, research papers, and policy notes produced by the sector, devoting at least 60% of professional time to research activities.

- Support the Sectoral Economic Advisor (SEA) in managing, coordinating, and ensuring the quality of research and analytical products across IFD divisions, acting as a key technical resource to support the broader department in knowledge matters under the SEA’s direction.

- Work closely with the network of knowledge coordinators across divisions to review, comment on, and provide continuous technical feedback on analytical outputs and departmental knowledge initiatives.

- Assist in preparing responses to Vice Presidency for Sectors (VPS) and Vice Presidency for Countries (VPC) requests, ensuring rigorous technical and economic backing.

- Provide technical assistance and economic analysis for regional and country operations, technical cooperations, and policy dialogues related to state modernization and financial development.

- Design and execute empirical evaluations, randomized controlled trials, and econometric modeling using administrative and survey data to measure the impact of public interventions.

- Collaborate with internal colleagues, external academic networks, and multilateral partners to advance the sector's strategic research agenda.

- Disseminate research findings through academic seminars, policy forums, workshops, and peer-reviewed international journals.

- Support institutional knowledge management initiatives, training sessions, and technical workshops organized by the sector.

- Perform other institutional and analytical tasks assigned by the Sectoral Economic Advisor to support the production, management, and dissemination of knowledge.

What you'll need

• Education: Masters degree required, PhD in Economics, Public Policy, or a closely related field strongly preferred

• Experience: At least 4 years (5 preferably) of progressive professional experience in empirical research, policy analysis, or economic development, with a strong publication record or pipeline in applied microeconomics or public economics.

• Languages: Proficiency in Spanish and English, spoken and written, is required. Additional knowledge of French and Portuguese is preferable.

Requirements

• Citizenship: You are a citizen of one of our 48-member countries. We may offer assistance with relocation and visa applications for you and your eligible dependents.

• Consanguinity: You have no family members (up to the fourth degree of consanguinity and second degree of affinity, including spouse) working at the IDB, IDB Invest, or IDB Lab.

Type of contract and duration

• Staff: International staff contract, 36 months initially, renewable upon mutual agreement.
