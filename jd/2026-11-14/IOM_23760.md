Introduction
Established in 1951, IOM is a Related Organization of the United Nations and the leading UN agency in the field of migration. Working closely with governmental, intergovernmental and non-governmental partners, IOM promotes humane and orderly migration for the benefit of all. It saves lives and protects people on the move, drives solutions to displacement, and facilitates pathways for regular migration, while providing services and advice to governments and migrants.
IOM is committed to fostering a respectful, inclusive and supportive workplace where all employees can thrive professionally and feel valued. By creating such an environment, IOM aims to better harness the full potential of migration and strengthen its support to people on the move.
IOM invites candidates from diverse backgrounds to apply and provides reasonable accommodation throughout the recruitment process when required. Learn more about IOM’s workplace culture at IOM workplace culture | International Organization for Migration

Organizational Context and Scope
Under the overall and direct supervison of the Senior Information Security Officer (SISO), the
Information Security Officer ensures that IT systems, services, and data in the IOM-ICT ecosystem
meet NIST cybersecurity standards. Key responsibilities include developing and improving information
and data security policies and controls, ensuring regulatory compliance, and collaborating with business
units to maintain security requirements throughout system lifecycles. The role requires collaboration,
governance and technical leadership, and translating security needs into solutions, with frequent
support of other teams.

This position reports to the Senior Information Security Officer within the Information Security &
Risk Management Unit. All IOM staff must perform duties according to job descriptions, delegated
authorities, UN and IOM rules, and uphold IOM’s values of professionalism, integrity, and respect for diversity.

Key Governance Responsibilities
1. Plan and conduct internal security audits, as well as support external audit activities.
2. Identify, assess, evaluate, document, and report cybersecurity risks, maintain a centralized risk register, and track mitigation actions.
3. Support organizational objectives by ensuring that business needs are met with secure, compliant, and cost-effective solutions.
4. Ensure data protection and privacy compliance, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and other relevant regulations for both applications and data, through the implementation of technical and organizational measures.
5. Support the design and implementation of security controls for data-in-transit and data-at-rest, including encryption, access controls, and monitoring.
6. Advise business units and management on information security requirements and strategic security issues.
7. Support the SISO in developing and executing a comprehensive cybersecurity strategy that aligns with organizational goals and ensures robust risk and compliance management frameworks that address current and emerging threats.
8. Support the SISO in establishing and implementing an effective information security management system and program.
9. Oversee vendor, contract, and change management activities related to cybersecurity, ensuring alignment with strategic objectives and compliance standards.
10. Design and implement targeted phishing campaigns and user awareness programs in collaboration with business functions, fostering a culture of cybersecurity and risk consciousness.
11. Collaborate with cross-functional teams to integrate cybersecurity into business processes
and initiatives across all projects and programs.
12. Continuously assess and enhance IOM’s global security posture, including the evaluation of
cybersecurity maturity across systems and processes.
13. Develop and maintain cybersecurity roadmaps, including objectives, milestones, and
performance indicators for cybersecurity initiatives to guide the execution of strategic
initiatives and track progress. Develop and maintain security policies and procedures based on international standards (e.g., ISO 27001, National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST), Center for Internet Security (CIS)) and produce essential documentation to support project and operational needs.
14. Provide strategic guidance on the implementation of security controls to protect organizational assets and ensure resilience against emerging threats.
15. Perform additional duties as assigned by the Senior Information Security Officer, contributing to cross-functional initiatives and organizational priorities.
Key Technical Responsibilities
16. Monitor and analyze security incidents and respond to breaches or vulnerabilities.
17. Respond to security breaches and vulnerabilities.
18. Perform hands-on security monitoring and risk assessments.
19. Support penetration testing and vulnerability management activities.
20. Oversee the security of systems, data, and infrastructure.
21. Conduct comprehensive security assessments of on-premises and cloud environments, and manage third-party penetration testing engagements, including drafting terms of reference and performing quality assurance on deliverables.
