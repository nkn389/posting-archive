Description
At the World Bank, a member of the World Bank Group (WBG), you’ll join a diverse, global community working across cultures, disciplines, and borders to address the world’s most pressing development challenges. With 1.2 billion young people reaching working age in the decade ahead, the challenge of job creation has never been greater. Through partnerships across 145 countries and more than 182 offices worldwide, we work with governments, private sector, development partners and other stakeholders to invest in people, strengthen markets, and deliver scalable, data-driven solutions that generate more and better jobs and improve lives. For more information, visit https://www.worldbank.org/en/who-we-are/ibrd
The World Bank Group has maintained a longstanding and active development partnership with the Kingdom of Morocco, one of the largest IBRD borrowers in the Middle East and North Africa, Afghanistan and Pakistan (MNAAP) region. The Morocco program is managed within the broader Maghreb and Malta Country Management Unit (CMU), which operates under the MNAAP Vice Presidency and covers Algeria, Libya, Morocco, Tunisia, and Malta.

The World Bank Group's engagement in Morocco is guided by the FY2026-2035 Country Partnership Framework (CPF) which promotes a focused and jobs-centered program structured around three mutually reinforcing outcomes: (i) improved productivity and competitiveness of firms for job creation; (ii) inclusive, connected and resilient territories; and (iii) strengthened human capital. The CPF reflects close collaboration across IBRD, IFC, and MIGA to maximize development impact through a One World Bank Group approach.

Morocco's portfolio spans a broad range of sectors and instruments spanning infrastructure, financial inclusion, digital economy, education, health, social protection, agriculture, and water, and involves sustained policy dialogue with the Government at the highest levels, including the Ministry of Economy and Finance, line ministries, and sector agencies.

The locally recruited Senior Operations Officer (SOO) is a critical member of the CMU team based in Rabat. The SOO draws on Morocco's institutional landscape, policy environment, and development priorities to serve as the primary in-country interface between the World Bank country office and the Moroccan government, local stakeholders, and development partners. The position works under the supervision of the Operations Manager (OM) and collaborates closely with Task Team Leaders across the portfolio. Under the supervision of the OM, support the CMU in the strategic oversight of the Morocco IBRD portfolio, with a strong focus on implementation performance, disbursement, achievement of development outcomes, timely restructuring and disciplined management of project closings and agreed actions of D-readiness.

While the position would be primarily focused on the Morocco World Bank program, there will be opportunities to broaden the scope of the day-to-day work to other countries in the CMU, and to expand the scope to a One World Bank Group approach strongly pursued by the CMU.

Role and Responsibilities

• Assist the OM in monitoring the development impact and implementation progress of the portfolio, draw lessons and resolve implementation issues.
• Maintain an integrated view of the lending, pipeline and ASA portfolios; identify delivery risks, strategic gaps, duplication and opportunities for consolidation, sequencing and scale-up; and prepare concise, decision-oriented portfolio analytics and management briefings.
• Track agreed actions and ensure timely escalation and follow-up with clients and Bank management, with clear line of sight to implementing the CPF as One WBG with measurable development impact.
• Review ASA concepts, deliverables and other knowledge products on behalf of the CMU, ensuring analytical rigor, operational relevance, complementarity across Global Practices, and effective plans for dissemination, uptake and use.
• As a core member of the Operations team, review and provide quality enhancement advice on all operations throughout the project cycle, ensuring alignment with CPF providing advisory support in all phases of operations. Facilitate resolution of cross-department and cross-sectorial problems.
• Participate in operational missions and meetings and assist teams in resolution of difficult operational issues.
• Work closely with the Operations and Implementation Support Teams to address key fiduciary and E&S issues that are generic to the portfolio.
• Play a key role in quality enhancement, risk management and implementation issues, under the guidance of the OM.
• Play a key role on behalf of the CMU management in the review of projects from the concept stage to final approval. Review of appraisal documentation prior to Board submission.
• Advise the country teams on operational policies, instruments, and procedures and on complex operational issues.
• Support the Division Director, the OM, CM and RRs in the preparation of work programs and budgets, and monitor budget execution, and the fulfilling of WPA agreement.

Country Strategy Formulation and Implementation

• Support the DD and OM in monitoring implementation of the CPF and related products as relevant.
• Support the OM in managing relations with clients and partners, including monitoring trust funds and harmonization of development assistance/policy dialogue with multilateral and bilateral partners.
