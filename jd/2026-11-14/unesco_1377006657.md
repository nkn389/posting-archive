ORGANIZATIONAL CONTEXT

The United Nations in Kuwait is implementing a joint programme entitled “Promoting Inclusive Employment and Empowerment” under the One UN approach and in alignment with Kuwait’s national development priorities.
• The project brings together two complementary programme components implemented by multiple UN agencies:
A joint UNESCO–ILO component focused on strengthening the inclusion of persons with disabilities in the world of work through improved policies, inclusive education and training, employer engagement, media inclusion, and data systems.
• A WHO–UNODC component focused on strengthening Kuwait’s national response to substance use through rehabilitation and reintegration services.

The UNESCO–ILO component is implemented in partnership with the Ministry of Social Affairs (MoSA), the Public Authority for Persons with Disabilities (PADA), the Human Capacity Building Society for Social Development (HCBSSD) and other relevant civil society organizations, employers’ organizations, training institutions, media institutions, and other national stakeholders.

The project aims to strengthen institutional capacities, improve enabling environments for inclusive employment, and enhance evidence-based policymaking through improved disability-inclusive data systems.
To support the implementation of the project, UNESCO seeks to recruit a Project Coordinator to provide strategic, technical, operational, coordination, communication, and donor liaison support to ensure effective implementation of the UNESCO component in Kuwait.

MAIN TASKS AND SERVICES

Under the overall authority of the Director of the UNESCO Regional Office for the Gulf States and Yemen, the general supervision of the Social and Human Sciences (SHS) Programme Specialist, and in close coordination with the joint UNESCO–ILO project management team, the incumbent shall support the implementation of the joint project on disability inclusion and empowerment in Kuwait.

In particular, the incumbent will undertake the following responsibilities:

Provide strategic and operational coordination for the effective implementation of UNESCO-led project activities and workplans:
• Develop, update, and monitor annual workplans, implementation schedules, and results frameworks for UNESCO activities.
• Ensure timely and quality delivery of all project outputs under UNESCO responsibility.
• Coordinate closely with the ILO project team to ensure coherence, complementarity, and joint delivery of results.

Provide technical oversight of project implementation across all UNESCO-led components, including:
• Strengthening the capacity of the National CRPD Committee in substantively contributing to the workshops content and facilitation
• Supporting development of TVET-to-work transition tools and guidelines.
• Overseeing establishment of the disability inclusion clinic.
• Coordinating employability skills curriculum development.
• Supervising disability-inclusive media and communication interventions.
• Supporting OPD-led awareness campaigns.
• Coordinating employer engagement initiatives and partnerships.
• Overseeing situational analysis studies and research outputs.
• Coordinating development of AI-enabled disability inclusion data systems.

Coordinate stakeholder engagement and partnership building, including:
• Liaising with government entities (including MoSA and PADA) and coordinating with HCBSSD, in accordance with its role within the project governance and partnership framework.
• Engaging organizations of persons with disabilities (OPDs).
• Coordinating with TVET institutions, media organizations, employers, and business associations.
• Maintaining close collaboration with UN agencies and the UN Resident Coordinator system.
• Supporting the establishment and maintenance of multi-stakeholder coordination mechanisms, including facilitating effective participation of national partners and relevant governance bodies.

Lead donor coordination, reporting, and strategic communication, including:
• Serve as focal point for donor communication and coordination.
• Prepare consolidated donor reports, briefing notes, and presentations in view of the Steering committee meetings.
• Ensure timely submission of narrative and technical inputs from implementing partners and national partner organizations, as appropriate.
• Support organization of donor missions, review meetings, and steering committee sessions.
• Ensure alignment of reporting with donor requirements and UN standards.
• Reflect the technical contributions and participation of national partners in the monitoring and evaluation reports to submit to the Steering committees, in line with agreed project governance arrangements.

Lead communication, visibility, and knowledge management activities, including:

• Develop and implement communication and visibility plans for UNESCO project activities.
• Produce newsletters, success stories, human-interest stories, and web-based content.
• Ensure visibility of UNESCO and donor contributions in line with branding guidelines.
• Support media engagement and public communication activities.
• Document lessons learned, best practices, and project achievements.
• Support communication outputs for national, regional, and international events.
• Maintain a structured repository of communication materials and visibility assets.

Support monitoring, reporting, and documentation, including:
• Prepare quarterly progress reports, technical briefs, and analytical updates.
• Track project implementation against indicators, outputs, and budgets.
• Consolidate inputs received from implementing and national partners, ensuring that the contributions of all participating national stakeholders are appropriately reflected in project reporting and documentation, in accordance with UNESCO and donor reporting requirements.
• Prepare monitoring frameworks and results tracking matrices.
• Support preparation of steering committee meetings and technical working groups.
• Produce analytical summaries, charts, and visualization of results.

Support financial and administrative coordination, including:
• Work closely with UNESCO finance and operations teams on budget tracking.
• Support procurement processes, recruitment of consultants, and contract management.
• Assist in organization of workshops, missions, and events.
• Ensure compliance with UNESCO financial and administrative procedures.
