Introduction
Established in 1951, IOM is a Related Organization of the United Nations and the leading UN agency in the field of migration. Working closely with governmental, intergovernmental and non-governmental partners, IOM promotes humane and orderly migration for the benefit of all. It saves lives and protects people on the move, drives solutions to displacement, and facilitates pathways for regular migration, while providing services and advice to governments and migrants.
IOM is committed to fostering a respectful, inclusive and supportive workplace where all employees can thrive professionally and feel valued. By creating such an environment, IOM aims to better harness the full potential of migration and strengthen its support to people on the move.

Organizational Context and Scope
Under the overall supervision of the Director of RMD and the direct guidance of the Head of Financing and Multilateral Development Banks (MDB) Engagement, the Resource Mobilization Officer (Asia) will contribute to strengthening the organization’s resource mobilization efforts by supporting donor engagement and facilitating strong donor relationships.

Required Qualifications and Experience
Education

- Master’s degree in international relations, Partnership, Development Studies, International Affairs, Social Sciences, Law, or any related discipline from an accredited academic institution with at least two years of relevant professional experience; or,
- Bachelor’s degree in the above fields with at least four years of relevant professional experience.

Accredited Universities are those listed in the UNESCO World Higher Education Database.
Experience

- Experience in humanitarian, resource mobilisation, programme support or donor relations and liaison;
- Demonstrated experience supporting engagement with governmental or institutional donors from Japan, Republic of Korea, or China, including contributing to the preparation of funding proposals, donor briefs, or formal communications;
- Excellent writing and communication skills; experience in preparing analytical briefs, talking points or donor engagement materials is an asset;
- Experience coordinating large Calls for Proposal and liaising with both donors and internal stakeholders is an asset.

Skills

- Organizational skills, with experience in multi-tasking, prioritizing, and working independently to meet tight deadlines;
- Demonstrated understanding of humanitarian architecture, funding mechanisms;
- Knowledge of donor priorities, funding trends, and partnership approaches, particularly in the Asia region, is an asset;
- Excellent communication, drafting, and interpersonal skills;
- Demonstrated attention to detail;
- Ability to work effectively within multicultural teams.

Languages
IOM’s official languages are English, French, and Spanish. All staff members are required to be fluent in one of the three languages.

For this position, fluency in English is required (oral and written). Fluency in at least one of the following Asian languages: Korean, Japanese or Chinese is highly desirable.

Working knowledge of another official UN language (Arabic, French, Russian, and Spanish) is an advantage.
Proficiency of language(s) required will be specifically evaluated during the selection process, which may include written and/or oral assessments.
