ORGANIZATIONAL CONTEXT
The United Nations World Food Programme (WFP) is the 2020 Nobel Peace Prize Laureate. WFP is the world’s largest humanitarian organization, saving lives in emergencies and using food assistance to build a pathway to peace, stability and prosperity for people recovering from conflict, disasters, and the impact of climate change. Around 20,000 people work for the organization, most of them in remote areas, directly serving the hungry poor.

To reach a world with zero hunger, WFP is investing in and growing private sector fundraising and partnerships. ShareTheMeal is WFP’s fundraising and awareness-raising app. Free to download, the award-winning app is a pioneering way for people to join our efforts in creating a world with zero hunger.

ShareTheMeal currently has more than 6 million users worldwide, who have shared more than 330 million meals. WFP is pursuing an ambitious growth strategy for individual giving, and ShareTheMeal is a key driver for this strategy going forward. The team, located in Berlin, is focused on further developing the app to include new and innovative features, and on increasing the engagement of existing users and growing the user base through marketing and communication initiatives. The Head of ShareTheMeal Product is a member of the ShareTheMeal leadership team and leads the team responsible for the product experience used by millions of supporters worldwide.

ACCOUNTABILITIES / RESPONSIBILITIES
Reporting directly to, and working very closely with, the Head of ShareTheMeal, the Head of ShareTheMeal Product is responsible for leading the Product team and for the product strategy, roadmap and performance of ShareTheMeal’s products on app and web. Under the guidance and supervision of the Head of ShareTheMeal, the Head of ShareTheMeal Product will:
Own the product strategy and roadmap for ShareTheMeal products:

- Lead and develop the product vision and strategy together with the Head of ShareTheMeal and the leadership team, to deliver on the agreed upon growth and business results.

- Build hypotheses and data-based scenarios, goals and forecasts for product features and experiences both on web and app.

- Carry out discussions with relevant external counterparts and WFP staff to derive options on the product strategy.

- Own the product roadmap and, together with the Product Managers, UX/UI and Tech, plan and structure epics and prioritize across competing needs.

- Oversee the creation, prioritization and delivery of features and experiences, conduct user testing for an agile test-and-learn approach, as well as ensure relevant documentation is kept up to date.

- Continuously improve working processes within the Product team and with partner teams (incl. tools).

- Track, analyze and monitor product performance metrics and surface them in the proper forums.

- Collaborate with product vendors and partners.
Lead, manage and develop the Product team:

- Lead the team of Product Managers, setting clear goals, priorities and expectations, and oversee and provide direction to the team’s work.

- Manage the performance of direct reports, including regular feedback, performance reviews and development plans.

- Mentor and coach direct reports and their reports as needed, to ensure performance in the team is sustained and continuously improved.

- Support team members with training and development activities.

- Define recruiting needs, lead the selection process including interviewing, and ensure a smooth onboarding of new team members.
Other activities:

- Work closely with the Design / UX/UI, Engineering, Data and Marketing teams to ensure alignment on priorities and delivery.

- As a member of the leadership team, contribute to quarterly and annual planning of team-wide objectives and provide regular updates to management.

- Instill a culture of customer centricity and experimentation, based on data and insights.
