&lt;h3&gt;&lt;span class="emphasis-2"&gt;&lt;b&gt;&lt;u&gt;What We Can Achieve Together:&lt;/u&gt;&lt;/b&gt;&lt;/span&gt;&lt;/h3&gt;&lt;br /&gt;&lt;p&gt;The Midwest Division Development Writer reports to the Donor Communications Manager and plays a key role in the region&#39;s Development Operations Team. This position works closely with state-based development teams in Illinois, Indiana, Michigan, Ohio, and Wisconsin to produce donor communications materials for individual, foundation, and corporate donors with major and/or principal gift capacity. The most common deliverables include letters of intent, fundraising proposals, and impact reports for The Nature Conservancy&#39;s work in each state, the Midwest Division, and around the world. Other projects may include donor acknowledgement letters, annual appeals, donor profiles, outreach emails, talking points, presentations, and customizable proposal/report templates. The ideal candidate has experience writing and designing successful private grant proposals and/or impact reports within strict funder guidelines. While the team makes every effort to identify anticipated writing requests at least 4-6 weeks in advance, this position may also work under tight time constraints to meet corporate and principal gift deadlines. &lt;/p&gt;&lt;br /&gt;&lt;p&gt;The Development Writer helps implement the strategic priorities of the Midwest Division Donor Communications team by collaborating closely with other departments, including Conservation, Legal, Finance, and Marketing &amp; Communications. Successful candidates in this role will stay informed of TNC&#39;s conservation work within the Midwest Division and become familiar with our highest priority work across the globe, too. This position functions as an essential bridge between TNC&#39;s conservation and development staff, so they may also provide editorial advice and expertise to translate TNC&#39;s complex bodies of work for donor audiences. Although the Development Writer develops communications materials for external audiences, this is an internal-facing position that supports out-the-door fundraising staff. However, there may be occasional opportunities to interact with individual and institutional donors. &lt;/p&gt;&lt;br /&gt;&lt;p&gt;This is a hybrid position open to candidates living within a reasonable driving distance from The Nature Conservancy&#39;s office in Madison, Wisconsin. Candidates within the greater Madison and Milwaukee markets are strongly preferred.
