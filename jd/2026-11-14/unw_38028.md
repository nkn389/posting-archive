Background:

UN Women, grounded in the vision of equality enshrined in the Charter of the United Nations, works for the elimination of discrimination against women and girls; the empowerment of women; and the achievement of equality between women and men as partners and beneficiaries of development, human rights, humanitarian action and peace and security.

UN Women supports state and non-state partners towards the achievement of substantive gender equality in Georgia and the South Caucasus region. In line with international and national commitments, UN Women works on the levels of policies and legislation, institutions and communities, to achieve transformative results for increased gender equality and greater protection of the rights of women and girls. UN Women is providing technical and financial support to partners worldwide, working in several thematic areas: Women, Peace and Security (WPS) is one of them.

This work includes supporting the implementation and localization of national WPS commitments through the participation of grassroots women’s organizations, internally displaced and conflict-affected women in local planning and budgeting, alongside capacity development for local authorities. Since 2024, the portfolio has also expanded its support for women’s leadership and participation in climate action and disaster risk reduction.

In September 2026, with support from the United Kingdom’s Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office (FCDO), UN Women commenced two projects on women for sustainable peace and WPS leadership and defence capacity. The projects support women’s meaningful participation and leadership in peacebuilding, dialogue and recovery, and strengthen institutional capacities to implement WPS commitments within partner institutions across the South Caucasus region. Activities under these projects are tailored to national priorities, institutional mandates and opportunities for engagement.

Reporting to UN Women WPS Programme Specialist Georgia Country Office, under the overall guidance of the UN Women Country Representative in Georgia and Liaison for the South Caucasus, the International Consultants will provide technical and coordination support during inception phase of the two projects and its early implementation. The assignment builds on already conducted WPS scoping and stakeholder mapping exercises and supports the project team in the preparation and testing of selected capacity-building initiatives, as well as provision of expert support to regional learning events. It will help connect women’s expertise with opportunities for meaningful participation in peace and security processes and support women’s leadership and professional development within defence institutions.

Description of Responsibilities/ Scope of Work

The Consultants will provide technical and coordination support during the inception and early implementation of the two projects, including stakeholder capacity needs assessments and further scoping consultations with relevant institutions, women peacebuilders, women-led organizations and technical experts. The assignment will include supporting the design, organization, delivery and refinement of selected pilot initiatives and capacity-building activities to advance women’s meaningful participation in peacebuilding and recovery, women’s leadership within defence institutions, and institutional capacities to implement WPS commitments. This may include adapting selected institutional tools, in cooperation with specialised experts. The Consultants will also provide substantive and organizational support to regional learning events and technical exchanges, prepare concept notes, agendas and learning materials, support facilitation and participant coordination, and document feedback, lessons and recommendations. Findings will inform project workplans, inception reporting and follow-up activities. All activities will reflect national priorities, institutional mandates and context-specific opportunities for engagement.
