Org. Setting and Reporting
In its Resolution A/RES/72/279, the United Nations General Assembly endorsed the transformation of the Development Coordination Office (DCO) as a stand-alone coordination office within the United Nations Secretariat. DCO exercises managerial and oversight functions of the resident coordinator system under the oversight of the Chair of United Nations Sustainable Development Group (UNSDG). In 2026, DCO undertook a recalibration to ensure the RC system remains optimally configured to support the acceleration of the 2030 Agenda and embodies the UN 2.0 shift, anchored in data, digital, innovation, behavioral science, strategic foresight, and a mission-driven organizational culture. The position is located in the United Nations Development Coordination Office (UNDCO) Regional Office for Arab States in Amman and reports to the Regional Team Leader/Senior Programme Management Officer as First Reporting Officer, with the Regional Director as Second Reporting Officer. The Chief of Policy and SDG Financing serves as an additional reporting officer to enable matrixed collaboration.

Responsibilities
Within delegated authority, the Sustainable Development Officer (Regional Policy Specialist) will be responsible for the following duties: • Monitors and analyzes regional and sub-regional SDG acceleration bottlenecks, identifies emerging policy, financing and implementation risks, and initiates or supports corrective and follow-up actions in coordination with relevant stakeholders. • Performs policy advisory assignments in collaboration with Regional Offices, UN Country Teams, Member States and external partners by facilitating strategic policy dialogue and supporting the development of integrated and actionable responses to sustainable development and SDG-related challenges. • Researches and analyzes sustainable development issues, including integrated policy, cross-border and political economy dimensions, and prepares strategic assessments and analysis informed by regional and country-level realities. • Contributes to the development of DCO policy recommendations and analytical products through reviews of regional and sub-regional trends, institutional frameworks, financing dynamics and policy developments affecting SDG implementation and economic transformation. • Supports Resident Coordinators, UN Country Teams and Member States in the development and implementation of Integrated National Financing Frameworks (INFFs), SDG financing strategies, fiscal space analysis and financing prioritization aligned with Cooperation Frameworks and SDG acceleration pathways. • Engages and coordinates with International Financial Institutions (IFIs), Multilateral Development Banks (MDBs), regional financing institutions and development partners on issues related to SDG financing, debt vulnerabilities, financing reform, economic resilience and innovative financing mechanisms. • Provides analytical and advisory support to RCs, RCOs and UNCTs in identifying SDG priorities, sequencing policy interventions and assessing financing trade-offs under constrained fiscal and development environments. • Provides analytical support to countries in special situations, including SIDS, LLDCs and MICs, as relevant, in coordination with relevant UN entities and partners. • Designs and undertakes surveys, analytical scans and data collection exercises; reviews and interprets findings; and prepares evidence-based conclusions and recommendations to support data-driven planning and decision-making. • Prepares high-quality written outputs on sustainable development and SDG financing issues, including policy briefs, thematic analysis, reports, speeches, presentations, background papers and inputs to regional and corporate analytical products. • Provides substantive support to regional consultative processes, inter-agency coordination mechanisms, Regional Platform for Integration and other policy and financing-related fora, including through agenda development, preparation of analytical materials and stakeholder engagement. • Provides analytical inputs to Common Country Analyses, Cooperation Frameworks and regional horizon scans, and supports integrated policy approaches, strategic foresight and anticipatory analysis in line with the UN 2.0 agenda. • Participates in and contributes to inter-agency task forces, expert groups, policy networks and communities of practice at regional and global levels, including support to the regional community of practice of RC economists. • Develops and sustains engagement with regional organizations, development partners, academia, think tanks, private sector actors and financing coalitions to strengthen regional policy dialogue, partnerships and SDG financing cooperation. • Initiates and coordinates outreach, training workshops, seminars and knowledge-sharing activities, and contributes to the strategic communication and positioning of regional policy and SDG financing priorities. • Leads and/or participates in complex field missions, including the provision of guidance to consultants, government counterparts, RCOs and UN Country Teams, and prepares related mission reports and recommendations. • Collects and analyzes data to identify trends and generate insights through data visualization and analytical tools to support evidence-based planning, decision-making and reporting. • Initiates and coordinates outreach activities, conducts training, seminars, and presents on assigned topics. • Prepares speeches and other inputs for presentations by senior staff on sustainable development and other issues within UN working streams, including cross-pillar, as relevant. • Performs other duties as required.

Competencies
PROFESSIONALISM: Ability to apply sustainable development theories, concepts and approaches. Ability to identify issues, analyze and participate in the resolution of issues/problems. Ability to conduct data collection using various methods. Conceptual analytical and evaluative skills to conduct independent research and analysis, including familiarity with and experience in the use of various research sources, including electronic resources on the internet, intranet and other databases. Shows pride in work and in achievements; demonstrates professional competence and mastery of subject matter; is conscientious and efficient in meeting commitments, observing deadlines and achieving results; is motivated by professional rather than personal concerns; shows persistence when faced with difficult problems or challenges; remains calm in stressful situations. Takes responsibility for incorporating gender perspectives and ensuring the equal participation of women and men in all areas of work. Able to analyze and interpret data in support of decision-making and convey resulting information to management. PLANNING AND ORGANIZING: Develops clear goals that are consistent with agreed strategies; identifies priority activities and assignments; adjusts priorities as required; allocates appropriate amount of time and resources for completing work; foresees risks and allows for contingencies when planning; monitors and adjusts plans and actions as necessary; uses time efficiently. COMMUNICATION: Speaks and writes clearly and effectively; listens to others, correctly interprets messages from others and responds appropriately; asks questions to clarify, and exhibits interest in having two-way communication; tailors language, tone, style and format to match audience; demonstrates openness in sharing information and keeping people informed.

Education
Advanced university degree (Master’s degree or equivalent) in sustainable development, political science, economics, public policy, public administration, finance, social sciences or related field. A first level university degree in combination with qualifying experience may be accepted in lieu of the advanced university degree.
