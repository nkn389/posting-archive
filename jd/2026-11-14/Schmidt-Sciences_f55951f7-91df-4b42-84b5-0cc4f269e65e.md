We’re agog. That’s not just our name. It’s how we feel about a whole new form of communication that is quickly emerging. Agog: The Immersive Media Institute is helping creators and nonprofit leaders harness the power of extended reality (XR) technologies, like virtual reality and augmented reality, to open new avenues for empathy, understanding, and activism.

We believe these new tools can help put humanity on a trajectory to a better future. Our initial areas of focus include social justice and equity; high-impact storytelling; research, education, and outreach; and policy and ethics. Founded by Wendy Schmidt and Chip Giller and guided by an accomplished advisory board, Agog is poised for big impact as an ecosystem builder across the XR industry, the nonprofit sector, philanthropy, academia, and beyond.

The Role

The Labs and Residencies Manager will build, scale, and oversee our labs, residencies, and future educational initiatives, acting as a key ambassador to build meaningful relationships with partner organizations and deliver exceptional, high-touch experiences for all lab attendees.

Reporting to the Director of Programs, this person will lead curriculum development and manage participant and partner experience. They will also synthesize program insights into recommendations that strengthen Agog's future educational initiatives.

This person will manage Agog's relationships with current and prospective residency and fellowship partners, ensuring funded programs align with Agog's mission and learning goals. They will work cross-functionally with Programs, Production, and Marketing & Communications teams to align strategy, operations, and storytelling efforts, ensuring immersive educational experiences are delivered seamlessly from design through execution.
