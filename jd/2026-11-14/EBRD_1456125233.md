Purpose of Job

As part of the Corporate & Project Finance Credit Team, work alongside more experienced Risk Officers to independently analyse and appraise risks of EBRD’s new and existing portfolio corporate and project finance debt transactions. As part of the credit review, identify required due diligence, recommend appropriate risk mitigation measures and transaction structures, including security, covenants, and pricing. Advise bankers and the Bank’s Committees to ensure that banking projects comply with the Bank’s sound banking mandate and are in line with risk appetite guidelines. Under supervision of a Senior/Principal Risk Officer, the Associate Risk Officer reviews portfolio projects, assigns credit ratings and communicates trends in the portfolio and lessons learned to management and peers for their broader strategic decision-making and bottom-up stress-testing.

Accountabilities & Responsibilities

The Associate Risk Officer will work under supervision of the Senior/Principal Risk Officer focusing on:

- Establishing an independent and unbiased view on projects proposed to the Operations Committee / SBIC / under Delegated Authority and make clear recommendations, or approvals on each proposal.
- Analysing the key risks of each proposal and advise on risk mitigation measures, structures, tenor, security, covenants and condition precedents, to the project teams and, via credit notes. Review integrity and anti-money laundering issues to ensure compliance with internal guidelines and advise on referral to the Office of the Chief Compliance Officer as required. Be versed in climate risk issues that have an effect on new and existing operations, including risk mitigation and reputational issues. Appraising the validity and reliability of key assumptions of each proposal, including via involvement in the commissioning and review of independent due diligence and travel.
- In liaison with Risk Policy and Reporting, Climate Risk and other Credit teams, supporting sector portfolio reviews and providing input to portfolio stress-tests.
- Ensuring projects comply with any relevant risk guidelines, prudential limits set by the Bank, assesses the climate risk as per the procedures and that appropriate ratings are assigned and updated for probability of default and loss given default. Ensure accuracy of Risk data in projects involved.
- Exercising independent judgement regarding portfolio actions (including certain waivers and amendments), and recommendations to senior management on matters requiring higher level approval (including transfers to corporate recovery, provisions and more significant restructuring, etc.).
- Carrying out proactive and regular monitoring of the assigned portfolio counterparties, countries and market developments. Actively and constructively engaging with Banking and other relevant staff to ensure adequacy of front-line monitoring.
- Collaborating with other members of staff; especially in the Banking Department, Finance, the Office of the General Counsel and the Office of the Chief Economist to ensure effective cross-departmental and business functioning.
- Contributing to various executive Bank committees - including the Operations Committee, SBIC, RiskCom etc.
- Participating and contributing positively to various initiatives within Risk Management or bank-wide to support and develop engagement in the team.
- Among peers, being an agent of credit culture dissemination.

Knowledge, Skills, Experience & Qualifications

- The Associate Risk Officer is expected to be, commensurate to their experience, authoritative and influential and support Principal and Senior Risk Officers in providing well-judged advice and guidance across the entire business.
- Proven experience of analysing high yield loans, structured finance and other financial instruments, in both corporate and project finance.
- Deliver clear authoritative conclusions in the context of large volumes and tight timescales.
- Knowledge of emerging markets, including CIS, CEE, SEMED and SSA.
- Strong written and spoken communication skills and excellent Excel knowledge.
- Advanced degree in finance, business, economics or equivalent on the job training.
- Relevant experience, of which [at least] some in a risk management role.
