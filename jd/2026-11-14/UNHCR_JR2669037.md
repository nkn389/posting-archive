Operational Context:

UNHCR has maintained a presence in Bosnia and Herzegovina since 1992. The UNHCR Representation for Central and South-East Europe covers nine operations, including Bosnia and Herzegovina (BiH). In BiH, UNHCR works with national authorities, civil society, UN agencies and other partners to strengthen a sustainable and protection-sensitive asylum and solutions system and to promote effective access to rights for refugees, asylum-seekers, stateless persons and other persons under UNHCR's mandate.

BiH has established an asylum legal framework broadly aligned with international standards and the EU acquis and has made important progress in developing its national asylum system. The current context offers opportunities to consolidate these achievements, including by further strengthening access to asylum procedures, the quality and efficiency of refugee status determination, access to free legal aid, and the effective enjoyment of rights by persons granted international protection, while advancing sustainable approaches to refugee inclusion and integration. BiH’s multi-level institutional structure also creates an important space for strategic legal engagement, advocacy and coordination with authorities at different levels of government.

UNHCR's engagement in BiH focuses on strengthening national ownership and sustainable protection systems, including through institutional capacity development, strengthened public free legal aid, refugee inclusion and integration, and the prevention and reduction of statelessness. This work is also closely linked to BiH's EU accession process and continued alignment of national legislation and practice with international protection standards and the EU acquis.

Against this background, the Legal Associate will be part of the Protection team and will support legal and policy analysis, monitoring of legislative and jurisprudential developments, preparation of legal comments and advocacy materials, organization of capacity building/advocacy events, monitor and follow-up on individual cases involving legal or protection issues.

Particular attention will be given to asylum procedures and procedural safeguards, access to justice and free legal aid, access to rights and integration of persons granted international protection, civil registration, nationality and statelessness. The incumbent will liaise with relevant authorities, legal aid institutions, civil society organizations and other partners and contribute to capacity-development activities aimed at strengthening sustainable, nationally owned protection systems.

The position requires strong legal research, analytical and drafting skills, sound knowledge of the legal and institutional framework in BiH, and the ability to work effectively with a wide range of national and international stakeholders.

Purpose and Scope of Assignment:

The Legal Associate is a member of the protection team. S/he normally will report to the Assistant Protection Officer.

The Legal Associate assists the supervisor to prepare legal analysis required for quality, timely and effective protection responses to the needs of populations of concern. Another important function of the position is to contribute to the involvement of the persons of concern with an office in making decisions that affect them, whether in accessing their rights or in identifying appropriate solutions to their problems. To achieve this, the Legal Associate will need to support involvement of communities of concern and relevant authorities in continuous cooperation. Additionally, the Legal Associate is expected to contribute to the capacity building activities for local and central state authorities in the area of protection of persons of concern either directly or in cooperation with partners.

The Legal Associate assists an office in providing quality, timely and effective protection responses to the needs of populations of concern. S/he is also required to assist an office in ensuring that persons of concern are involved with the office in making decisions that affect them, whether in accessing their rights or in identifying appropriate solutions to their problems. In addition, the Legal Associate is in charge of the office’s corporate legal work and advises on all matters of Labour Law affecting refugees.
