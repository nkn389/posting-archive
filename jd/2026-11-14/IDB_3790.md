About this position

Review Mechanism under the Access to Information Policy

IDB Invest’s Access to Information Policy provides for a two-stage review process by which parties requesting information can request a review of the decision to deny access to information pursuant to the terms of the Policy. The requester may first request that IDB Invest’s Administration Committee review a denial of information to confirm the correct application of the Policy and its exceptions. In a second stage, an external panel (the “Panel”) independent of IDB Invest Management may review any denial of information by the Administration Committee. The Panel will verify the correct application of the Policy and its exceptions and may confirm or reverse the Administration Committee’s denial.

Role of the Access to Information External Review Panel

The Panel, which is composed of three external members independent of IDB Invest, conducts second-stage reviews in response to requests from interested parties in cases where the Administration Committee, in conducting first-stage reviews, upholds Management decisions to deny a requester access to information. The Panel has the authority to uphold or reverse the Administration Committee’s first-stage review decisions to deny access to information. If the Panel reverses the Administration Committee’s decision to deny access to information, the Panel will grant the requester the information requested, which is the sole remedy available. Decisions of the Panel are final and not subject to further review. Decisions will be published on IDB Invest website within five days of the Panel issuing a decision.

What you’ll do

The primary responsibilities of the members of the Panel include the following:

- Reviewing and deciding on any request for a second-stage review, following the decision of the Administration Committee to uphold IDB Invest Management’s decision to deny access to information, as indicated above and in accordance with any rules of procedure governing the review mechanism and the Panel. Each member of the Panel will provide input leading to decisions to either uphold or reverse the decision of the Administration Committee, for each request for review that is presented to the Panel;

- Preparing for and participating in the sessions of the Panel. Members will meet virtually and in person as may be required;

- Participating in training sessions provided to them by IDB Invest;

- Submitting conclusions of the Panel’s review to IDB Invest; and

- Providing all necessary input for the preparation of reports to the IDB Invest’s Board of Executive Directors and publications concerning the Panel’s activities and decisions.
