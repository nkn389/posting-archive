They will develop documentation, modify processes, and participate in the enhancement of systems and tools used by the team. They will also support the team in strategic planning.&lt;/li&gt;&lt;/ul&gt;&lt;p&gt; &lt;/p&gt;&lt;p&gt;The Program Manager works in close collaboration with any or all of the following: TNC staff, donors, volunteers, vendors, financial institutions, legal professionals, and contractors.
