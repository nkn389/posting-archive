Result of Service
The consultant will act as UNEP’s national coordinator for the Nature for Cooling Challenge in Côte d’Ivoire, under the supervision of the NCC project team and the Head of the UNEP West Africa Sub-regional Office. The consultant will ensure that the national-level activities of the project are delivered on time, with strong government engagement and national ownership. Duties and Responsibilities
UNEP is accelerating the global transition to sustainable cooling. Getting the right solutions in place for cooling is essential for improving the quality of life and health of people without undue impacts on the planet. Established in 2019 as a transformative initiative of the UN Secretary-General’s Climate Action Summit, the Cool Coalition is the global multistakeholder platform supporting countries to take comprehensive and integrated action on sustainable cooling and thereby deliver on the Paris Agreement, the Kigali Amendment and the SDGs. Comprehensive, integrated action is defined as taking joint action on passive cooling, energy efficiency and refrigerant phase-down, in line with a pathway to cut 60% of emissions from the sector while increasing access to life-saving cooling (UNEP Cool Coalition Global Cooling Watch 2023). The Coalition convenes expertise across the cooling value chain to collaborate on science, policy and advocacy that underpin political commitments and their implementation, and to develop and test methodologies that require demonstration to be later scaled by partners. Since 2024, it has served as the secretariat to the Global Cooling Pledge. Cities are warming twice as fast as the global average, and today 815 million urban residents face critical risks of heat-related deaths, illness and productivity losses, caught between rising temperatures and limited access to affordable, sustainable cooling. Nature holds a powerful solution: urban forests, green façades and roofs, and water bodies can reduce peak summer temperatures by 1–5°C, while reducing energy demand for air conditioning and the associated greenhouse gas emissions. Yet investment in standardized methodologies and monitoring systems remains insufficient, preventing nature-based solutions (NbS) for cooling from being scaled. The GEF-funded and UNEP-led Nature for Cooling Challenge (NCC) responds to this gap. Funded with USD 3 million from the GEF-8 Climate Change Mitigation set-aside, supplemented by co-financing, the NCC is a three-year project (2026–2029) implemented by UNEP with Sustainable Energy for All (SEforALL). It works with national governments to establish policy frameworks and guidance, and with subnational governments, developers and design practitioners to accelerate higher-quality landscape design and strengthen markets for nature-based cooling solutions. Pilot activities take place in Brazil, Côte d’Ivoire and Cambodia. In each country, a competitive Call for Proposals selects NbS projects proposed by developers (or developers partnering with cities). UNEP is recruiting a locally based consultant in Abidjan to coordinate the day-to-day implementation of the Nature for Cooling Challenge in Côte d’Ivoire. The consultant will be the main liaison between UNEP and the Government. They will work closely with the government focal points to keep project activities aligned with national priorities and to secure the Government’s participation and ownership. The consultant will also convene and engage national stakeholders, coordinate with project partners, and ensure the timely delivery of national activities and products.
