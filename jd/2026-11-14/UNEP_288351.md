Duties and Responsibilities
The United Nations Environment Programme (UNEP) is the leading global environmental authority that sets the global environmental agenda, promotes the coherent implementation of the environmental dimension of sustainable development within the United Nations system and serves as an authoritative advocate for the global environment. UNEP-CCC is a leading international advisory institution on energy, climate and sustainable development. Its work focuses on helping developing countries and emerging economies transition to low-carbon development pathways and supporting the integration of climate resilience into national development. UNEP-CCC is actively involved in the implementation of the UN Environment Climate Change Strategy and Energy Programme. The NDC Partnership Action Fund (PAF) implementation team in Moldova requires a National Consultant for Air Pollutant Emission Projections with a focus on Industrial Production and Product Use (IPPU) related emissions. Moldova is preparing its 2026–2029 National Air Pollution Control Programme to meet national emission reduction targets for key air pollutants by 2030 and beyond. Under the NDC Partnership project, the consultant will develop and update relevant sectoral air pollutant inventory and emission projections to 2040 using international reporting standards and national energy data. The role is critical to supporting the Government of Moldova in delivering its air quality and climate commitments. The Republic of Moldova is developing the National Air Pollution Control Programme (NAPCP) for 2026–2029 in accordance with Article 8(d) of Law No. 98/2022 on Atmospheric Air Quality. The NAPCP includes the national Emission Reduction Commitments (ERCs) established by Government Decision No. HG593/2024 of 28 August 2024 for nitrogen oxides (NOx), non-methane volatile organic compounds (NMVOCs), sulphur dioxide (SO₂), ammonia (NH₃) and fine particulate matter (PM₂.₅). The commitments establish emission reduction targets for 2030–2039 and from 2040 onwards, relative to 2020 emission levels. Under the NDC Partnership Action Fund (PAF) Moldova Project, the Consultant will collect, verify and update the air pollutant emission inventory data for the Industrial Processes and Solvent Use sector in the relevant Nomenclature for Reporting (NFR) tables and prepare emission projections through 2040. The work shall follow the reporting requirements of the UNECE Convention on Long-range Transboundary Air Pollution (CLRTAP), Annex I to the 2023 CLRTAP Reporting Guidelines and the applicable EMEP/EEA air pollutant emission inventory guidebook methodologies. The assignment will cover relevant industrial process, solvent use and other product-use source categories, using historical inventory data, industrial production statistics, solvent and product consumption data, emission factors and documented assumptions. Industrial fuel combustion shall be addressed under the relevant Energy sector NFR categories to avoid double counting. Based on the historical emission inventory and the effects of policies and measures already implemented or adopted, the Consultant will prepare With Measures (WM) emission projections for 2030, 2035 and 2040. The projections will support the assessment of national ERC compliance and the identification of potential emission reduction gaps. With Additional Measures (WAM) projections will be prepared where necessary, to assess the potential impact of additional technically feasible and cost-effective measures. The assignment will contribute to CLRTAP inventory reporting, implementation of the NAPCP 2026–2029, assessment of progress towards the national ERCs and identification of priorities for reducing emissions from industrial processes, solvent use and other relevant product-use activities. Objectives: To collect, verify and update the air pollutant emission inventory for the Industrial Processes and Solvent Use sector and prepare emission projections for 2030, 2035 and 2040, disaggregated by pollutant and relevant NFR source category, under the WM scenario and, where necessary, the WAM scenario. Task 1. Review of historical emissions and industrial activity data Review historical emissions and relevant NFR inventory data for the Industrial Processes and Solvent Use sector; Collect and analyse available data on industrial production volumes, relevant industrial subsectors, process technologies, raw materials and production methods; Collect and review available information on solvent and other product consumption, solvent content, product composition and emission-control technologies; Review relevant industrial emissions legislation, permitting requirements, applicable Best Available Techniques (BAT) conclusions, environmental standards and adopted policies and measures affecting air pollutant emissions; Identify data gaps, methodological limitations and uncertainties affecting the inventory and emission projections. Task 2. Preparation of the WM scenario Establish the baseline year and document assumptions concerning future industrial production, process technologies and solvent and product use; Prepare activity projections for 2030, 2035 and 2040 by relevant NFR source category; Account for the expected effects of adopted policies and measures, including industrial process modernisation, cleaner production, changes in production volumes, substitution of products containing solvents, reduced solvent content, improved emission capture and treatment, and implementation of applicable industrial emission-control requirements; Apply the appropriate EMEP/EEA methodologies and emission factors, taking into account the relevant source-specific calculation methods and available activity data; Calculate projected emissions by pollutant, NFR category and projection year; Document the activity projections, assumptions, emission factors and methods used in the calculations.
