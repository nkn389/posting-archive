Company Description

The Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD) is an international organisation comprised of 38 member countries, that works to build better policies for better lives. Our mission is to promote policies that will improve the economic and social well-being of people around the world. Together with governments, policy makers and citizens, we work on establishing evidence-based international standards, and finding solutions to a range of social, economic and environmental challenges. From improving economic performance and creating jobs to fostering strong education and fighting international tax evasion, we provide a unique forum and knowledge hub for data and analysis, exchange of experiences, best-practice sharing, and advice on public policies and international standard-setting.
The OECD Environment Directorate (ENV), in line with the strategic objectives of the Secretary-General, provides relevant and timely information, analysis and advice to support governments in identifying and implementing the environmental policies needed to support a cleaner, more resource-efficient and low-carbon green growth path. The Directorate currently comprises approximately 200 staff members working across a broad range of issues such as: green growth, climate change, biodiversity, water, eco-innovation, resource productivity and waste, environmental policy tools and evaluation, safety of chemicals, biotechnology products and nanomaterials, and linkages between environment and tax, agriculture, energy, transport, development assistance, trade and investment policies. For more information, please refer to http://www.oecd.org/env/.
ENV is looking for a scientific administrator to work with the Environment, Health and Safety Division in support of the activities on the testing and risk assessment of chemicals (including nanomaterials and advanced materials) overseen by the Chemicals and Biotechnology Committee. The selected candidate will report to the Head of the Environment, Health and Safety Division within the Environment Directorate and work under the general supervision of a Principal Scientific Administrator.

Job Description

Main Responsibilities

- Assist in preparation of drafts of technical and policy documents, revise the drafts based on comments submitted by member countries and facilitate the consensus building;
- Facilitate the country commenting procedures and revisions of documents developed by intellectual service providers;
- Assist in the organisation of meetings, workshops and teleconferences of national experts, and prepare documentation for these events;
- Maintain an awareness of scientific developments relevant for the projects in order to take initiatives with respect to proposals for new work.
- Contribute to related safety activities of OECD’s Environment, Health and Safety Programme as a whole, especially with respect to risk assessment and management of chemicals (including nanomaterials, advanced materials, pesticides, biocides) and products of modern biotechnology.

Qualifications

Ideal Candidate Profile
Academic background

- An advanced university degree in chemistry, toxicology, ecotoxicology or biological sciences, environmental science, engineering, scientific policy development or other related topics.
Professional background

- At least two years of professional experience working on various aspects of the development or implementation of chemical risk assessment methodologies or related environmental assessment methodologies, acquired in national governments, agencies, research institutes, intergovernmental organisations or a combination of the above.
- Experience in health and environment policy related to management of chemical safety;
- Good knowledge of current methods for managing the risks of chemicals;
- Experience in working with committees and groups of experts, particularly in an international context, involving industry and non-governmental organisations, and in international consultations and negotiations would be an advantage.
