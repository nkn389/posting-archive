BACKGROUND AND PURPOSE OF ASSIGNMENT:

WFP Cambodia is implementing its Country Strategic Plan (CSP) through integrated programme and operational support functions that enable efficient, accountable and sustainable delivery of assistance. Within this context, the Business Support Assistant will provide business and administrative support across programme, focusing on partnership documentation, procurement coordination, and donor reporting. This role is critical to ensuring smooth operations and compliance with WFP standards, enabling technical teams to focus on programme delivery and impact.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES:

1. Partnership Agreement Support
· Provide administrative support in preparing, reviewing, and finalizing Partnership Agreements, Field Level Agreements (FLAs)..
· Ensure agreements comply with WFP’s rules, donor requirements, and internal procedures.
· Track timelines, deliverables, and clearance processes for partnership documentation.

2. Finance, Procurement & Administration
· Support procurement processes, including requests, purchase orders, deliveries, invoices and payment follow-up.
· Maintain accurate financial, procurement and administrative records and supporting documentation.
· Liaise with Finance, Logistics and other teams to resolve pending transactions and administrative actions.
· Support financial checks, reconciliations and preparation of documentation for audits and reviews.

3. Reporting, Monitoring & Documentation
· Track partner financial and narrative reports, deliverables and other requirements under agreements.
· Consolidate partner submissions and support preparation of reports, briefs and management updates.
· Maintain organized files and trackers, and flag missing information, discrepancies or overdue actions.
· Support monitoring of unit workplans and follow up on agreed deliverables.

4. Programme & Team Support
· Organize workshops, missions, and coordination meetings, including logistical arrangements.
· Maintain filing systems for agreements, procurement records, and programme documentation.
· Provide day‑to‑day administrative support to the programme team, ensuring smooth internal workflows.
· Provide support in consolidating the initial draft of the joint unit workplan, monitor deliverables, and update as required

5. Other Required Responsibilities
· Collaborate with WFP Country Office communication focal points to support any events, including donor and government joint field visits. This includes coordinating with the Area Office to schedule field visits and manage related logistical arrangements.

· Perform other activities as required.

DESIRED EXPERIENCES FOR ENTRY INTO THE ROLE:

- Has a minimum of five years relevant experience in Administration, procurement, managing partner, or related operational functions.

- Has experience utilizing computers, including Microsoft Office package (e.g., Word, Excel, Power Point) and other standard software packages and systems.

- Has familiarity working with field operations , reporting, programme management, monitoring and evaluation processes.

- Has experience working with different types of external stakeholders at national and sub-national levels.

- Experience in budget monitoring, data analysis, and preparation of management reports to support decision-making and performance improvement.

- Knowledge of the UN rules, procedures and regulations would be an asset.
