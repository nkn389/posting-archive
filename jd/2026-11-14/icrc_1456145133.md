Protection of Family Links Field Officer - Odesa - 810505

WHAT WE DO

The International Committee of the Red Cross (ICRC) is an impartial, neutral, and independent organization whose exclusively humanitarian mission is to protect the lives and dignity of victims of armed conflict and other situations of violence and to provide them with assistance. The ICRC is mandated to work in countries affected by armed conflict or other situations of violence. The ICRC is present in over 100 countries and has 18,000 staff worldwide. In 2014, the ICRC expanded its presence in Ukraine. Over 900 team members work across the county in various professional fields.

ABOUT THE JOB

The ICRC Protection Unit is seeking a skilled and dedicated Protection of Family Links Field Officer (PFL Field Officer) to join our team in Odesa. In this role, you will be responsible for implementing and monitoring ICRC PFL activities in Odesa`s area of responsibility. The PFL Field Officer will handle tracing inquiries from families of missing persons, collect, and process tracing requests across its different stages, and ensure timely follow-up of the missing cases and with family enquirers under his/her responsibility. Additionally, you will support multidisciplinary assessments of the needs of families of missing persons and assist in providing appropriate responses.

As a key member of the team, the PFL Field Officer in Odesa will also be expected to raise awareness about the ICRC’s role and procedures related to the Missing file during meetings with the families of the missing or authorities involved in the case.

Start date: December 2026

YOUR TASKS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

- Develop and maintain contacts with the families of missing and captured persons

- Collect new tracing enquiries and regularly follow-up on existing enquiries

- Keep families informed of any developments or news in their case

- Ensure proper documentation and data management

- Understand and analyze the existing cases to inform the ICRC’s actions in favor of the families

- Organize and conduct awareness raising sessions with families of missing persons and authorities to disseminate information about ICRC PFL action, and orient families in the search process

- Proactively understand the needs of families of missing individually or more collectively (trends), beyond the right to know: mental health needs, legal and administrative needs, etc., through the organization of focus groups discussions with families of missing

- Proactively identify referrals to other ICRC departments

- Actively map and assess available external services for families of missing, in the area of responsibility, and promote the internal and external services available among families of Missing

- Support families’ gathering in Odesa area of responsibility and together with the team, maintain regular contact with families’ associations
