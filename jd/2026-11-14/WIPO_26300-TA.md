1. Organizational Context

a. Organizational Setting

The post is located in the WIPO Academy and supports Academy-wide educational technology (EdTech) initiatives, services and projects.
The WIPO Academy is WIPO's capacity-building arm, delivering intellectual property (IP) education globally through online, hybrid and face-to-face programs, supported by an integrated and evolving digital learning ecosystem.
The Academy plays a central role in strengthening the capacity of countries to use the IP system effectively. It adopts an interdisciplinary approach to IP education, exploring its links with trade, innovation, health, applied sciences, information technology and the creative industries.

b. Purpose Statement

The purpose of the position is to strengthen the WIPO Academy's data, analytics and AI-enabled capabilities to support effective digital learning services, evidence-based decision-making and continuous service improvement. This role contributes to the development of reliable, secure and responsible data and AI solutions across the Academy's EdTech ecosystem, enabling greater operational efficiency, actionable insights and innovation in support of the Academy's strategic objectives.

c. Reporting Lines

The incumbent works under the supervision of the eLearning IT Solutions Manager

2. Duties and Responsibilities

The incumbent will perform the following principal duties:

a. Support the design, development and maintenance of data engineering, reporting, learning analytics, dashboards, and AI-solutions for WIPO Academy EdTech platforms and initiatives.

b. Analyze data and reporting requirements across Academy EdTech services and propose improvements to data models as well as technical specifications and rules.

c. Contribute to the review and assessment of existing Academy data sources, reports, dashboards, feedback mechanisms and related initiatives.

d. Develop and maintain Data Dictionary and related documentation, in coordination with the relevant Academy and technical stakeholders.

e. Support data extraction, transformation, integration and automation across relevant Academy digital services and platforms.

f. Develop, maintain and improve dashboards, reports and analytical tools related to performance indicators.

g. Monitor and analyze data quality, identify and solve inconsistencies and gaps.

h. Apply practical AI methods and tools, as appropriate, to support data extraction, analytical and output tools, and contribute to the design, testing, and validation of AI use cases and pilots.

i. Perform other related duties as required.
