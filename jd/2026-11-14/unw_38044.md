Background:

UN Women Nigeria supports women’s economic empowerment through improved skills, productive livelihoods, market access and more resilient value chains. In Cross River State, women play important roles in the fisheries and seafood value chain, including handling, processing, preservation, packaging and marketing. However, limited access to improved processing techniques, food-safety practices, appropriate equipment, business skills and higher-value markets can constrain productivity and incomes.

UN Women therefore seeks a qualified local trainer to deliver practical, hands-on training for women on safe and hygienic seafood handling, processing, preservation, value addition, packaging and basic enterprise practices. The assignment should prioritize locally available seafood and technologies that are affordable, energy-efficient and appropriate for women-led micro and small enterprises.

The trainer will work under the overall guidance of UN Women Nigeria and the designated programme focal point, in coordination with relevant state/local authorities, community leadership and participating women’s groups as applicable. All deliverables are subject to UN Women review and acceptance.

Objective of the Assignment

The overall objective is to strengthen the technical and enterprise capacities of participating women to process seafood safely, reduce post-harvest losses, improve product quality and shelf life, diversify value-added products, and enhance their competitiveness and incomes.

Specific Objectives

- Assess participants’ existing practices, skills gaps and locally available seafood products.

- Provide practical training on hygienic handling, cleaning, sorting, preparation and preservation of seafood.

- Demonstrate improved processing techniques suited to local products, including smoking, drying and other safe value-addition methods where appropriate.

- Build practical skills in food safety, quality control, packaging, labelling, storage and waste minimization.

- Strengthen basic costing, pricing, record-keeping, customer orientation and market-readiness skills.

- Equip participants to operate and maintain relevant small-scale processing equipment safely.

Scope of Work and Responsibilities

- Conduct an inception meeting with UN Women and relevant local stakeholders and submit a concise training plan.

- Carry out a rapid pre-training needs/skills assessment and adapt the curriculum to participants’ literacy levels, local seafood species and market context.

- Develop simple participant-friendly training materials, practical demonstrations and job aids in English and, where useful, locally understandable language.

- Deliver competency-focused sessions combining demonstration, supervised practice and peer learning.

- Cover good hygiene practices, prevention of cross-contamination, safe water use, temperature/time control, clean work surfaces, appropriate protective clothing and safe storage.

- Demonstrate seafood processing and preservation methods applicable to the target products, such as improved smoking, drying, chilling/freezing or other methods agreed with UN Women.

- Train participants in packaging and labelling fundamentals, product presentation, basic quality assurance and compliance awareness for market access.

- Integrate occupational health and safety, energy efficiency, responsible use of fuel and water, by-product utilization and environmentally sound waste disposal.

- Introduce simple business modules covering product costing, pricing, production records, stock control, customer service and market linkages.

- Administer post-training competency checks, document lessons learned and provide coaching/mentoring during practical application.

- Submit complete training documentation and a final assignment report with recommendations for sustainability and follow-up support.

Indicative Training Modules

Module 1: Seafood value chain, participant assessment and good hygiene practices

Module 2: Raw material selection, handling, cleaning and preparation

Module 3: Improved processing and preservation: smoking, drying and context-appropriate cold preservation

Module 4: Food safety, contamination prevention, quality control and safe storage

Module 5: Processing equipment: safe operation, routine cleaning and basic maintenance

Module 6: Value-added product development, portioning, packaging and labelling

Module 7: By-product utilization, waste management, energy/water efficiency and workplace safety

Module 8: Costing, pricing, record-keeping, customer requirements and market readiness

Module 9: Practical production exercises, competency assessment and coaching

Training Approach

The trainer will use an adult-learning and learning-by-doing approach. Training should be predominantly practical, with demonstrations followed by supervised participant practice. Examples, measurements and job aids should be simple and locally relevant. The trainer will ensure safe use of knives, heat/smoking equipment, cold-storage equipment and other tools; promote hygienic workstations; and make reasonable adaptations that enable equitable participation by women.

Expected Deliverables

- Inception report and training plan: Methodology, schedule, stakeholder engagement approach and materials/equipment list.

- Needs assessment and adapted training package: Baseline findings, session plans, participant materials and practical exercises.

- Practical training delivered: Attendance records, photos where consent and UN Women protocols permit, practice logs and competency observations.

- Coaching and post-training assessment: Follow-up coaching, post-assessment results and identified support needs.

- Final report: Activities, participant profile, pre/post results, achievements, challenges, lessons learned and recommendations, with annexes.

Performance Indicators

- Training delivered according to the approved plan and within 20 working days.

- Participants demonstrate improved knowledge and practical competence based on pre/post assessment and observation.

- Participants can apply key hygiene, processing, preservation, packaging and basic costing practices.

- Complete, accurate and timely training records and reports submitted.

- Positive participant feedback and clear recommendations for post-training coaching and market support.
