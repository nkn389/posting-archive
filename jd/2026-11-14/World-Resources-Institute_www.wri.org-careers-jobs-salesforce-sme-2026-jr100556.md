About us:
WRI works to improve people’s lives, protect and restore nature, and stabilize the climate. As an independent research organization, we leverage our data, expertise and global reach to influence policy and catalyze change across systems like food, land and water; energy; and cities.
Founded in 1982, our 2,000+ staff work on the ground in more than a dozen focus countries and with partners in over 50 nations

About the Program:
The CRM Team sits within the WRI IT Department. The WRI IT Department is responsible for managing the procurement, use and maintenance of all electronic equipment by the Institute. It is responsible for providing the infrastructure and technology for automation and operational efficiencies across the enterprise. It implements the governance for the use of network and operating systems, and it assists the Business units by providing the required tools and functionality. It is a part of Global Operations Team which ensures efficiency of operations and consistency of practices throughout the WRI network.
The CRM Team is responsible for administering WRI's Salesforce instance and supporting WRI staff with its use for tracking of constituent engagement, fundraising, and similar activities.

Job Highlight:
Reporting to the CRM Associate Director, the Salesforce SME will serve as a senior business and technical analysis resource for the Salesforce ecosystem.
The Salesforce SME serves as the strategic owner and subject matter expert for WRI's Salesforce ecosystem, driving platform governance, business process optimization, data integrity, user adoption, and technology-enabled impact across global programs. This role partners with stakeholders across Development, Programs, Finance, Operations, Communications, and IT to ensure Salesforce effectively supports fundraising, donor management, grants management, program delivery, reporting, and organizational decision making.

What will you do:
This role combines business analysis, process improvement, Salesforce administration, reporting, data management, user support, and platform governance to ensure Salesforce effectively supports organizational objectives.

Salesforce Solution Design & Process Development (60%)

- Translate business requirements into scalable Salesforce solution approaches in partnership with the CRM team and technical resources.
- Evaluate solution options based on business requirements, Salesforce capabilities, usability, scalability, maintainability, and organizational best practices, prioritizing existing functionality before introducing new configuration or custom development.
- Analyze impacts and dependencies across Salesforce objects, fields, automation, security, reporting, integrations, and existing functionality.
- Design, develop, test, and deploy custom Salesforce solutions using Apex, Visualforce, Lightning Web Components (LWC), and Flows.
- Oversee Salesforce integrations with enterprise platforms including ERP, Finance, HR, Marketing, Collaboration, and Data Management systems.

- Partner with integration teams and vendors to ensure reliable, secure, and scalable data exchange.
- Manage integration, governance, monitoring, issue resolution, and system dependencies.

- Partner with CRM leadership to align NPSP capabilities and solutions with organizational strategy.

Salesforce Strategy, Governance & Platform Ownership (20%)

- Serve as the organization's primary Salesforce Subject Matter Expert and trusted advisor for CRM and related business systems.
- Establish and maintain platform governance, standards, security controls, data quality frameworks, and release management processes.
- Partner with business leaders to identify opportunities for process optimization, automation, and improved data-driven decision making.
- Ensure platform scalability, sustainability, compliance, and alignment with enterprise architecture principles.
- Identify opportunities to leverage AI to improve program delivery, constituent engagement, fundraising effectiveness, and operational efficiency.
- Support innovation initiatives that improve organizational effectiveness and user experience.
- Support and optimize nonprofit business processes including:
- Donor and constituent management
- Grant management and grant lifecycle tracking
- Program and project monitoring
- Impact measurement and reporting

- Leverage Salesforce Nonprofit Success Pack (NPSP), Nonprofit Cloud, and related solutions, where applicable.

Data Management, Reporting & Analytics (20%)

- Establish data governance standards, data quality processes, and data practices.Drive improvements in reporting, dashboarding, KPI tracking, and executive analytics.
- Enable consistent reporting across fundraising, programmatic, operational, and financial activities.
- Support data migration, cleansing, deduplication, and ongoing system optimization initiatives.
- Ensure alignment between fundraising, program delivery, and reporting processes across the organization.
- Support data structures and workflows required for philanthropic reporting and donor stewardship.
