BACKGROUND AND PURPOSE OF THE ASSIGNMENT:

WFP’s Partnerships Department's Country Office Support Hub (PPDR) helps Country Offices protect, grow and diversify their partnerships and resourcing across public and private donors.

Based on Country Office needs, PPDR provides operational, strategic and business development support to strengthen resource mobilization and partnership management across a range of partners.

PPDR assists Country Offices in navigating main corporate reporting and partnership planning processes. PPDR also promotes Knowledge Management and Learning (KM&L) across WFP to ensure teams have the information and tools they need to enhance partnerships. PPDR works closely with Partnerships divisions, Global Offices, Regional Director Offices and other Headquarters divisions to deliver timely, practical and high quality support.

The consultant will provide day-to-day coordination and production support for KM&L activities, with a focus on learning events, communications, participation tracking and timely follow-up. The consultant will work under the overall supervision of the KM&L Coordinator and collaborate with Partnerships & Innovation (PI) focal points, content owners, subject matter experts, Regional Offices, Country Offices and other contributors. ACCOUNTABILITIES/RESPONSIBILITIES:

- Support the preparation and delivery of Campfires and other online and in-person learning activities, including agendas, presentations, exercises, participant materials, speaker coordination and follow-up communications.
- Provide support for the PI One-Stop Shop/PI Manual and PI Learning Centre, including organizing, formatting and uploading validated content and learning resources in line with agreed standards.
- Support bootcamp preparation and delivery through participant communications, learning materials, session resources and coordination and logistical requirements.
- Maintain content inventories and support content mapping, migration, quality checks and routine updates, escalating gaps or inconsistencies to the KM&L Coordinator.
- Compile and organize participation data, survey results, feedback and lessons learned, and prepare clear summaries for review.
- Maintain accurate learning calendars, workplans, trackers, mailing lists and content repositories, ensuring records are current and accessible.
- Support the Resource Mobilization Learning Community through practical coordination, communications and dissemination of learning opportunities and resources.
- Provide general coordination, research and support across the KM&L workstream, as required by the supervisor.

DELIVERABLES AT THE END OF THE CONTRACT:

- Support the delivery of Campfires and other assigned learning activities, including the preparation of agendas, participant communications, presentations, exercises, resources and follow-up materials.
- Validated and well-organized content packages uploaded to the PI One-Stop Shop/PI Manual and PI Learning Centre, with associated inventories and update records maintained.
- Bootcamp support, including participant communications, session materials, resource folders and logistical documentation.
- Up-to-date content inventories, migration trackers, learning calendars, workplans, mailing lists and repositories.
- Consolidated participation and feedback records, with periodic summaries of attendance, Country Office representation, survey findings and lessons learned.
- Communication and engagement materials for the Resource Mobilization Learning Community and other KM&L initiatives.

QUALIFICATIONS AND EXPERIENCE REQUIRED:

EDUCATION:
Bachelor’s degree in communications, knowledge management, learning and development, public administration, international affairs, social sciences, development studies, public health, business administration or another field relevant to the assignment. An advanced university degree in a relevant field is an advantage.

EXPERIENCE:
One to two years of work experience in learning or event coordination, knowledge or content management, communications, programme support or capacity strengthening. Exposure to an international organization, the UN system, development cooperation or partnerships work is an asset.

KNOWLEDGE & SKILLS:
Strong organizational skills and attention to detail, with the ability to manage routine tasks, records and deadlines reliably. Clear written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to format and adapt content for different audiences. Ability to support the preparation of presentations, learning materials, communications and simple reports using established guidance and templates. Proficiency in standard office productivity tools, including word processing, presentations and spreadsheets. Familiarity with Microsoft 365, SharePoint, content-management systems or online learning platforms is desirable. Basic ability to compile, organize and present participation, survey and feedback data accurately. Initiative, service orientation and sound judgement, including knowing when to seek guidance or escalate issues. Ability to work collaboratively and respectfully with colleagues from diverse cultural and professional backgrounds.

LANGUAGES
Fluency, level C, in English is required. Intermediate or advanced knowledge of another WFP working language is an advantage.
