Project Context and Scope
IOM in the Republic of Moldova is implementing the “Reducing community-level vulnerabilities to TIP in Moldova” project, which supports national efforts to strengthen the protection of and assistance to victims of crime, including victims of trafficking in human beings.
MLSP has a central role in the development and coordination of the NRMV. Effective implementation requires a clear legal and institutional framework, practical referral and case-management arrangements, and coordinated action by central and local authorities, multidisciplinary teams, service providers and civil society organisations.
In this context, the consultancy will provide targeted support to MLSP to review selected aspects of NRMV implementation and identify feasible measures for improvement. The review will consider, as relevant, the functioning of the multidisciplinary approach, referral and case management for victims of trafficking and other victims of crime, the roles of specialists at local level, coordination between central and local actors, access to specialised services, and consistency with applicable EU, regional and international standards.
The assignment is designed as a focused 20-working-day exercise. Its scope and priorities will be agreed with MLSP and IOM at inception and may be adjusted within the available working days to respond to the most relevant needs and available evidence.

Tasks to be performed under this contract
Under the overall supervision of the IOM Program Coordinator and in close coordination with MLSP, the expert will undertake the following:
• Agree with MLSP and IOM on the priority areas, methodology, documents, stakeholders and work plan for the assignment.
• Review relevant legislation, policies, regulations, methodologies and available implementation information concerning the NRMV, victims’ rights, protection and assistance.
• Assess selected legal, institutional and operational aspects of NRMV implementation against applicable EU, regional and international standards, distinguishing between issues requiring legal amendments and those that may be addressed through regulatory, methodological or operational measures.
• Analyse, to the extent relevant and feasible, the functioning of multidisciplinary cooperation; referral and case-management arrangements for victims of trafficking and other victims of crime; the roles and responsibilities of relevant specialists at central and local levels; and coordination and access to specialised services.
• Consult MLSP and selected relevant stakeholders to clarify implementation challenges, practical needs and possible solutions.
• Prepare preliminary findings and practical, prioritised recommendations for strengthening NRMV implementation, including proposed legal or operational measures where appropriate.
• Revise the findings and recommendations based on relevant feedback and prepare a concise final report, including suggested next steps for MLSP.
• Prepare and facilitate a final workshop to present and validate the final findings, recommendations and proposed follow-up measures.
• Finalise the outputs following the final workshop and submit all materials in editable format.
