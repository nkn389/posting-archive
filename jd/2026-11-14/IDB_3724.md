About this position

The Environmental and Social Solutions Division (VPS/ESG) is responsible for ensuring that IDB-financed projects and other activities are environmentally and socially sustainable and comply with the Bank’s environmental and social policies.

VPS/ESG has developed an Information Management System, ESG Compass, a customized platform developed in .NET that supports ESG operational processes and information management. We are looking for a highly motivated professional to join the VPS/ESG Innovation and Technology Group to support the maintenance, enhancement, reporting, and continuous improvement of ESG Compass and related information management solutions.

As an Information Management and Reporting Consultant, you will work closely with VPS/ESG users, Information Technology (IT) teams, and external providers to ensure the effective functioning of ESG Compass, support data-driven decision-making, and contribute to the implementation of innovation and technology initiatives across the Division.

What you’ll do

- Innovation and AI integration

Identify opportunities to improve processes, system functionalities, integrations, and support the adoption and implementation of Artificial Intelligence (AI) solutions that enhance operational effectiveness.

- Project Coordination and Collaboration

Coordinate activities with VPS/ESG users, IT teams, and service providers, track ongoing initiatives, and support the planning, development, testing and delivery of ESG Compass enhancements and related projects.

- System Support and Maintenance

Support the maintenance and development of ESG Compass, including user support, issue resolution, functional analysis and validation of system enhancements, and coordination with internal and external stakeholders.

- Data Management and Reporting

Manage data quality and information management activities, develop reports and dashboards, and provide analytical insights to support ESG operations and decision-making.

- Knowledge Management and Capacity Building

Update training materials, user documentation, communication resources, and support knowledge-sharing initiatives to promote the effective use of ESG Compass and related tools.

What you'll need

Education: Master’s degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Information Systems, Data Analytics, Engineering, or other fields relevant to the responsibilities of the role.

Experience: At least 5 years of progressive experience in information management systems, application support and maintenance, data analysis and reporting, system implementations or integrations, and business process improvement and technology solutions. Proficiency in SQL, Power BI, SharePoint, and Microsoft Office is required. Familiarity with Microsoft Copilot and AI-enabled solutions, ServiceNow, Azure DevOps, Jira, data integration processes, and APIs is preferred.

Languages: Proficiency in English and Spanish, spoken and written, is required. Additional knowledge of French and Portuguese is preferable.
