Introduction
Established in 1951, IOM is a Related Organization of the United Nations and the leading UN agency in the field of migration. Working closely with governmental, intergovernmental and non-governmental partners, IOM promotes humane and orderly migration for the benefit of all. It saves lives and protects people on the move, drives solutions to displacement, and facilitates pathways for regular migration, while providing services and advice to governments and migrants.

Project Context and Scope
To support rights-based migration governance, the consultant will consolidate and harmonize existing guidance on return, readmission and reintegration including the readmission manual and the technical guidance on return and reintegration, into a single integrated guidance document on Return, Readmission and Reintegration (RRR). The final document will be primarily targeted at government practitioners and authorities responsible for the development, implementation and management of return, readmission and reintegration processes, while also serving other relevant stakeholders. The guidance will aim to clarify the applicable standards and frameworks for rights-based RRR management and provide concrete operational recommendations to help practitioners establish or strengthen RRR systems including through effective inter-State cooperation.

Tasks to be performed under this contract
Produce a coherent and comprehensive integrated guidance document on Return, Readmission and Reintegration (RRR) based on the consolidation and harmonization of the existing draft of the Technical Guidance on Return and Reintegration, developed by IOM’s Regional Office in Vienna, and the draft Readmission Manual, developed by the Immigration, Visa, and Readmission Unit in IOM’s Headquarters. More precisely the consultant will:

- Identify overlaps and complementarities, and propose the appropriate structure and lay out of a coherent document for optimal user experience.

- Consult appropriate teams and partners as needed.

- Integrate the concepts and guidance from both documents into a single coherent narrative.

- Ensure the final document is primarily targeted at government practitioners and authorities responsible for the development, implementation and management of return, readmission and reintegration processes, while also serving other relevant stakeholders.

- Prepare dissemination material for external use.
