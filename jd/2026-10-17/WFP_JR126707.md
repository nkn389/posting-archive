ABOUT CLIMATE RESILIENCE AT WFP
The World Food Programme (WFP) is the leading humanitarian organization fighting hunger worldwide, delivering food assistance in emergencies and working with communities to improve nutrition and build resilience. WFP's Strategic Plan is guided by the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) set forth in the 2030 Agenda, in particular SDG 2 on ending hunger and SDG 17 on revitalizing global partnerships for implementation of the SDGs.

The most vulnerable and food insecure people live in fragile, resource-scarce, and degraded
environments, in areas that are prone to climate disasters and exposed to frequent shocks. WFP Tanzania's climate resilience programme is under its country strategic plan (CSP 2022-2028) and works with communities and local institutions to support integrated resilience building that enables them to mitigate and prevent environmental degradation and promote climate change adaptation. WFP offers opportunities to food-insecure individuals and households to participate in creating productive community assets and regenerative interventions while covering their immediate food and nutrition needs. Moreover, the programmes strengthen the capacity of households, institutions, and other stakeholders to adopt approaches that promote climate adaptation.

THE ROLE
The Programme Unit within the Tanzania Country Office (CO) is looking for a highly experienced technical candidate to support the climate resilience portfolio. Overall, this role will support existing landscape rehabilitation and natural resources management initiatives, coordinate climate-focused initiatives across the Country Office, provide programme management and technical support to the teams and partners, and support in fostering strategic partnerships. This position is based in Dar Es Salaam and reports to the Activity 7 Manager.

ACCOUNTABILITIES/RESPONSIBILITIES:

Working under the supervision of the Activity 7 Manager, the Climate Resilience National Officer will carry out the following tasks:

- Provide project monitoring and implementation support for integrated resilience activities, undertake field visits, review implementation of project activities to ensure progress towards objectives and identify opportunities of further collaboration.

- Design and develop proposals in the climate resilience portfolio under the guidance of the Activity Manager and WFP SO3 Manager.

- Provide technical expertise and guidance, including natural resources management, asset creation, regenerative agriculture, livelihood development, natural resource management, among other integrated activities.

- Contribute to the preparation of accurate and timely reporting on programmes and activities that enable informed decision making and consistency of information presented to stakeholders.

- Represent WFP in public fora and maintain relationships with the government, development partners, and other organizations involved in natural resource management.

- Support the capacity building of WFP staff, partners and national government to integrate climate resilience concepts and approaches, targeting a variety of sectors (i.e. social protection, anticipatory action, school feeding, and agriculture market development)

- Produce internal and external operating procedures, knowledge products (e.g., briefs, case studies), and lessons learned, among others.

- Any other tasks as may be assigned.
