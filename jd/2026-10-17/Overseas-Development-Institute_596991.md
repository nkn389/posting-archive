Description
The People Advisor plays a central role in the day-to-day operation of the People Function at ODI Global, a global affairs think tank.

With a new strategy and a strong digital transformation agenda, we are building our People Team, to take ODI Global forward to become an employer of choice.

The People Advisor will support the implementation of organisational change, enable employee wellbeing and continuous improvement while ensuring services are delivered efficiently, accurately, consistently and in accordance with organisational policies, employment legislation and best practice.

You will provide high-quality, responsive and customer-focused operational and advisory support across the full employee lifecycle, ensuring the effective delivery of People services to managers and employees across ODI Global.

Working closely with the People Partner (Operations), People Partner (Employee Experience) and the Data and Systems Manager, the postholder will coordinate recruitment, onboarding, contractual changes, employee records, payroll administration and People operations, while acting as the first point of contact for a wide range of People enquiries. The role will provide practical advice and guidance to managers and employees on the application of People policies, procedures and employment practices, resolving routine matters independently and escalating more complex issues where appropriate.

Holding a level 5 CIPD qualification (or equivalent), knowledge of employment law and best practice and excellent administrative skills, you will have experience of effectively providing HR advice and supporting managers in processes in relation to conduct, performance, absence management and recruitment.

ODI Global welcomes applicants from diverse backgrounds and supports equality of opportunity.

Main Duties and Responsibilities
The role is responsible for providing high-quality operational, administrative and advisory support across the People Function, ensuring an efficient, professional and customer-focused service.

Employee Advice and Manager Support

- Provide first-line advice and guidance to managers and employees on People policies, procedures and good employment practice.

- Support managers in the application of attendance, probation, performance management, flexible working, family leave and other routine employee relations processes.

- Respond to employee enquiries, providing clear, consistent and customer-focused advice, escalating complex or high-risk matters where appropriate.

- Support managers in resolving routine people issues, ensuring organisational policies are applied fairly and consistently.

- Signpost managers to appropriate policies, guidance, learning resources and specialist support where required.

Employee Lifecycle Administration

- Coordinate administrative processes throughout the employee lifecycle, including recruitment, onboarding, contractual changes and leavers.

- Prepare contracts of employment, offer letters and employment-related correspondence.

- Process changes to terms and conditions, salary amendments and contractual variations.

- Ensure all employee records are accurate, complete and maintained in accordance with organisational procedures and legislative requirements.

- Support probation, confirmation, contract renewal and exit processes.

Recruitment and Onboarding

- Coordinate recruitment activity from advertisement through to appointment, acting as the main contact for recruiting managers and candidates.

- Provide advice to managers on recruitment procedures, safer recruitment requirements and recruitment best practice.

- Arrange interviews and assessment activities, supporting recruitment panels with administration and logistics.

- Coordinate pre-employment checks, references, right to work documentation and employment offers.

- Support the process for obtaining and renewing staff visas as appropriate.

- Support the delivery of onboarding and induction activities, ensuring new colleagues have a positive employee experience.

People Operations, Payroll and Administration

- Provide day-to-day operational support across the wider People team, working flexibly to meet changing priorities.

- Maintain accurate electronic employee records and personnel files.

- Prepare standard correspondence, reports and employment documentation.

- Coordinate monthly payroll changes, ensuring accurate and timely submission of information relating to salary, contractual changes, allowances and employee benefits.

- Support the administration of pensions, benefits and other employee reward arrangements.

- Respond to routine payroll and benefits enquiries and liaise with payroll providers where required.

- Ensure confidential information is handled appropriately and in accordance with GDPR and organisational policies.

HR Systems, Data and Reporting

- Maintain accurate employee information within the HR Information System.

- Support managers and employees with routine HR systems queries.

- Produce routine workforce reports, management information and people metrics.

- Carry out data validation, system audits and quality assurance activities to maintain data integrity.

- Assist with maintaining organisational structures, reporting lines and workflows within People systems.

- Support the implementation of new technologies, digital processes and AI-enabled solutions that improve efficiency and enhance the employee experience.

Employee Relations Support

- Provide operational and administrative support for employee relations casework, including preparing documentation, arranging meetings and maintaining confidential records.

- Monitor absence, probation, fixed-term contracts and other employee-related processes, identifying issues that require follow-up.

- Support consultation exercises, organisational change projects and policy implementation.

- Maintain accurate records relating to formal People procedures and contribute to ensuring legal compliance.

Learning, Development and Employee Experience

- Coordinate learning and development activities, including booking training, maintaining learning records and supporting organisational development initiatives.

- Support employee engagement, wellbeing, equality, diversity and inclusion and other employee experience initiatives.

- Assist with internal communications relating to People activities and organisational change.

- Support organisation-wide People events and initiatives.

- Provide organisational and administrative support in relation to Trade Union and staff liaison

Continuous Improvement

- Identify opportunities to improve People processes, systems and ways of working.

- Contribute to the development of policies, procedures, guidance notes and standard operating procedures.

- Support continuous improvement initiatives that enhance customer service, operational efficiency and the effective use of technology and AI-enabled processes.

- Promote a culture of continuous improvement, collaboration and excellent customer service across the People Function.

General

- Maintain the highest standards of confidentiality and professionalism in relation to employee and organisational information.

- Build effective working relationships with managers, employees and colleagues across ODI Global.

- Contribute to departmental planning and the delivery of the People Strategy.

- Keep knowledge of employment legislation, HR best practice and organisational policies up to date.

- Undertake other duties commensurate with the grade and responsibilities of the post.

- Work collaboratively as required across the Team to ensure an appropriate People Service is maintained

Person Specification Essential

- Hold CIPD Level 5 qualification or equivalent qualification / experience.

- A knowledge of UK employment law and best practice

- Experience of effectively providing HR advice to line managers on HR, procedure and practice

- Experience of supporting managers with HR processes, including in relation to conduct, performance, absence management and recruitment

- Experience of providing high-quality administrative support within an HR or People environment.

- Good understanding of employee lifecycle processes.

- Excellent organisational skills with the ability to manage multiple priorities and meet deadlines.

- Strong attention to detail and commitment to maintaining accurate records.

- Excellent written and verbal communication skills.

- Ability to provide professional and customer-focused support to managers and employees.

- Experience of maintaining confidential information appropriately.

- Good IT skills including Microsoft Office and experience of using HR systems and databases.

- Ability to work independently whilst contributing effectively as part of a team.

- Strong interpersonal skills with the ability to build positive working relationships.

- Ability to identify opportunities to improve processes and service delivery.

Desirable

- Experience working within the charity, research or international development sectors.

- Experience of working in a unionized enviroment

- Experience supporting organisational change or consultation processes.
