1. Organizational Context

a. Organizational Setting

The position is located at the Diplomatic Engagement and Assemblies Affairs Division, Sector of the Director General. The Division is responsible for all engagements with the diplomatic community in Geneva through various avenues such as events and meetings, as well as overseeing the administrative, logistical, and other aspects of governing body meetings such as the Assemblies of the Member States of WIPO (the Assemblies).

b. Purpose Statement

The Assemblies Affairs and Meetings Planner contributes to the effective planning and delivery of the work of the Diplomatic Engagement and Assemblies Affairs Division in support of WIPO’s governing body meetings. The incumbent also facilitates coordination and collaboration with relevant internal and external stakeholders, supports the optimization of related business processes and the effective use of digital tools, and helps ensure that organizational, procedural and operational requirements are addressed and that services for the governing bodies are delivered efficiently and to a high standard.

c. Reporting Lines

The incumbent works under the supervision of and reports directly to the Senior Counsellor, Diplomatic Engagement and Assemblies Affairs Division.

2. Duties and Responsibilities

The incumbent will perform the following principal duties:

a. Support the planning, preparation and management of the documentation workflow for the WIPO Assemblies, under the guidance of the Director, Diplomatic Engagement and Assemblies Affairs Division. Ensure the timely preparation, translation and online publication of official documentation in all UN languages, including the drafting and editing of reports and other Assembly-related documents.

b. Review, edit and proofread official Assemblies documentation in English, ensuring accuracy and compliance with WIPO editorial standards, style guidelines, and established procedures.

c. Coordinate and monitor the delegate registration process through WIPO Meet, including the review and approval of registrations and liaison with relevant internal stakeholders. Maintain and regularly update relevant sections of WIPO Meet, ensuring data accuracy, completeness and content integrity.

d. Contribute to the rollout and integration of new functionalities within the Digital Secretariat platform, including modules related to financed participants.

e. Facilitate the planning and organization of side events held in the framework of the WIPO Assemblies and subsidiary bodies, including Working Groups and Standing Committees. Maintain and update the side events calendar and liaise closely with the Division’s Events team and other relevant stakeholders to ensure effective coordination and delivery.

f. Support in the organization and delivery of high-level meetings, governing body sessions, and subsidiary body meetings. Monitor, coordinate, and disseminate up-to-date information on procedural and administrative matters relating to governing and subsidiary bodies across the Organization.

g. Perform other related duties as required.
