Project Context and Scope:
Effective community engagement and policing are central to building trust, enhancing public safety, and promoting accountability within law enforcement systems. In Nigeria, ongoing border security trends highlight the need for a structured, inclusive, and context-specific Framework to guide interactions between border officials, law enforcement and communities.
To support improved national response mechanisms, the International Organization for Migration (IOM) seeks to support the efforts of the Federal Government of Nigeria, to review, strengthen, and finalize the National Community Engagement and Policing Framework for Nigeria, ensuring it is practical, inclusive, gender-responsive, conflict-sensitive, and aligned with national policies and international best practices. A zero draft of the same has already been developed but further technical support is required to refine, strengthen, and finalize the document. To achieve this, IOM is looking for a reputable consultant to support the finalisation of the Framework, through a whole-of-government and whole-of-society approach. The consultant will also provide capacity building support to facilitate effective implementation of the Framework.
This intervention is implemented under the EU National Migration Governance Programme (2024–2029) which aims to support Nigeria in enhancing migration governance through strengthened border management systems, improved identity management frameworks, and targeted institutional capacity-building interventions.

Tasks to be performed under this contract
The tasks listed below will be subject to ongoing review, and as may be necessary, in consultations between the incumbent and IOM:

- Conduct a desk review of existing zero draft Framework, national, regional and international policy/legal frameworks, on border and migration management in Nigeria, ensuring integration of human rights, gender and youth inclusivity, rights-based approach and conflict sensitivity.

- Facilitate consultative workshops/meetings and stakeholder engagements to gather technical and operational insights to inform the Framework development.

- Develop an inception report detailing proposed methodology, workplan, and the draft outline of the Framework.

- Collaborate closely with Nigeria Police Force (NPF), Nigeria Security and Civil Defence Corps (NSCDC), Department of State Services (DSS), Nigeria Immigration Service (NIS), Federal Road Safety Corps (FRSC), Nigeria Customs Service (NCS), Nigeria Correctional Service (NCoS), and other relevant stakeholder including community leaders, border officials, law enforcement agencies, CSOs, border communities, to conduct perception studies of interactions, interdependence and mutual relationships within selected border areas. The objective is to establish baseline data on community engagement and awareness of policing initiative, trust dynamics and the socio-economic effects of strengthened border and migration management on local livelihoods.

- Facilitate a review workshop with relevant stakeholders to present and validate findings from the perception studies and assessments.

- Incorporate findings from the desk review and field assessments to develop a comprehensive, rights-based, and operational Framework aligned with relevant national, regional, and international standards.

- Conduct a stakeholder mapping exercise of all relevant institutions and actors involved in community engagement and policing outlining a clear coordination mechanism, roles and responsibilities as well as information-sharing arrangements to support implementation of the Framework

- Develop user‑friendly implementation tools, including flowcharts, checklists, monitoring and evaluation tools and quick reference guides, to support practical application of the Framework.

- Present the draft Framework for review, collect stakeholder feedback, and integrate comments into an improved version.

- Facilitate a national validation workshop with relevant stakeholders and finalise the final Framework.

- Design and deliver training sessions on the Framework to relevant border management officials to enhance sustainability and knowledge transfer.

- Prepare and submit both training report and a comprehensive final consultancy report that outline the process undertaken, highlight key findings, and provide clear, actionable recommendations to strengthen capacity-building efforts and enhance community engagement.

- Undertake duty travel to the communities and as is necessary to complete the above-mentioned tasks.
