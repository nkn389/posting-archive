Org. Setting and Reporting
In its Resolution A/RES/72/279, the United Nations General Assembly endorsed the transformation of the United Nations Development Coordination Office (DCO) as a stand-alone coordination office within the United Nations Secretariat. DCO exercises managerial and oversight functions of the resident coordinator system under the oversight of the Chair of the United Nations Sustainable Development Group (UNSDG). In 2026, DCO undertook a recalibration to ensure the RC system remains optimally configured to support the acceleration of the 2030 Agenda and embodies the UN 2.0 shift- anchored in data, digital, innovation, behavioral science, strategic foresight, and a mission-driven organizational culture. The position is located in the United Nations Development Coordination Office (UNDCO) in the Regional Office and reports to the Senior Programme Management Officer, Regional Coordination. The Administrative Officer, Regional Management and Efficiencies works closely with the RC System desk in the OneEO, Department of Operational Support.

Responsibilities
Within delegated authority, the Administrative Officer, Regional Management and Efficiencies, will be responsible for the following duties: Human Resource Management • Effectively coordinate with Regional human resources (HR) focal points, Headquarters and service providers as applicable, on any advisory actions relative to the administration of human resource activities, ensuring consistency in the application of UN rules and procedures. • Capacity building and HR capability support to the administrative and executive assistants in the region, for skills development and effective execution of their functions. • Support the onboarding and integration of administrative staff and Heads of Resident Coordinator Offices (HRCOs), ensuring facilitation of access to the required systems, roles and resources needed to execute their functions effectively. • Provide input on staffing related resources and operational impacts as may be needed, while flagging the operational impact of any HR gaps, constraints or functional overlaps as appropriate. Budget and Finance • Act as certifying officer for all transactions of the Regional Office in the ERP system and provide and/or ensure backup as required to the Heads of RCOs across the region. • Monitor budget performance and provides advisory guidance to RCOs to ensure that resources are utilised in accordance with the planned budgets and allotments issued. • Guide the region in the commitment and review of the budget of the individual offices, conducting regular reviews to assess progress of the expenditures and needs for revisions or reallocations. • Supervise and/or provide guidance on financial administration and management information issues and practices to colleagues. • Ensure that accounting and financial management controls and actions are consistent with UN policy, rules and regulations. • Perform other related duties as required. General Administration • Oversee work related to low value acquisitions, procurement, travel certification and service delivery to facilitate payments to vendors and individual contractors for services delivered to the regional office and accordingly guide the RCOs as requested. • Oversee the management of the Regional Office premises/facilities management and services and provide guidance to the RCOs as requested. • Oversee the identification of office technology needs and maintenance of equipment, software and systems, coordinating enhancements as necessary in liaison with HQ and/or service providers as applicable. • Perform other related duties as required. System-wide Efficiencies • Support progress oversight of Common Premises (CP) projects and the coordination of the annual UN premises data review exercise, providing substantive support and capacity development to countries in their assigned regions. • Provides substantive support and guidance to Resident Coordinator offices and Operations Management Teams (OMT) on the development of Common Back Offices (CBOs), including facilitation of capacity development and report production activities. • Supports the implementation and scaling of Global Shared Services (GSS) and Far-Reaching Efficiency Initiatives (FREI) across the region, including support to the operationalization of Mutual Recognition and other efficiency enablers at the country level. • Analyses and presents information gathered from diverse sources; perform regional analysis and modelling; promotes common data sets at country and regional levels. • Works closely with the DCO New York to align regional practices on efficiency initiatives with corporate guidance, facilitate knowledge sharing, and ensure coherent adoption of harmonized approaches by RCOs and OMTs. • Coordinates webinars and training with lead experts, in support of BOS, CP and CBO as required.
