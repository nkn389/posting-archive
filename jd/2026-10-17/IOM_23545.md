Infrastructure is an operational unit within IOM Bangladesh. The unit is integral to the Rohingya Refugee Response implemented by the International Organization for Migration (IOM) with funding from various international donors. The Infrastructure Unit activities include planning, designing, and construction of small to medium-complexity projects, including vulnerable communities enhancement work, health and community facilities and various types of site rehabilitation works, including drainage, canal rehabilitation, pedestrian access, slope stabilisation and installation of solar energy throughout the camps and host communities sites, mainly in Cox’s Bazar District, Bangladesh, as well as in other vulnerable areas of the country when required.
