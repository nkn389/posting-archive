Unit Context:
With about 135.9 million people in 2025,
Ethiopia is Africa’s second most populous country after Nigeria. Its economy
grew by 9.2% in FY2024/25, among the region’s fastest rates, yet it remains
one of the poorest countries, with per capita GDP of $979.
Ethiopia’s state-led model
improved infrastructure and living standards, extending nearby potable water access
to 60 million more people, doubling electricity access, and increasing child
vaccinations by 64%. Poverty fell from 39% to 24% between 2004 and 2016.
However, an overvalued currency, unsustainable debt, and restrictions on
private investment weakened competitiveness, fueled inflation, and constrained
productivity and job creation for 1.8 million new job seekers annually;
poverty rose from 27% to 32% between 2016 and 2021.
The World Bank’s longstanding
partnership with Ethiopia supports sustainable, inclusive growth. Priorities
include creating jobs, improving governance, sustaining macroeconomic and
structural reforms, reducing state dominance, deepening trade integration,
and expanding private-sector opportunities. Reducing conflict is also
critical because displacement and loss of livelihoods and human capital heighten
vulnerability to climate and other shocks. Food insecurity is worsening due
to extreme weather, reliance on rainfed agriculture, locusts, conflict, and
global food-price inflation. With roughly 2 million people reaching working
age each year, Ethiopia must improve existing jobs and create enough new
ones.
https://www.worldbank.org/en/country/ethiopia
Duties and accountabilities:
The Team Assistant will:
• Provide administrative support to the unit’s work program, including task management, procurement, databases, IT coordination, and editorial support.
• Draft and format correspondence and other documents in accordance with institutional standards, proper grammar, punctuation and style. Format and edit large documents.
• Conduct routine analytical tasks, compile information, and support Task Team Leaders (TTLs) and team members with project-related deliverables.
• Maintain accurate project data, status reports, contact lists, distribution lists, databases, and electronic and physical records.
• Monitor team activities, deadlines, and deliverables; follow up on outstanding actions and provide timely updates.
• Apply relevant procedures and exercise sound judgment to resolve routine administrative issues, escalating complex matters as appropriate.
• Attend meetings, draft minutes, and ensure timely clearance and distribution.
• Support even preparation and logistics for meetings and events, including high-level visits, conferences, seminars, and workshops; coordinate logistics, prepare materials, record minutes, and support timely clearance and distribution.
• Respond to internal and external inquiries or route them promptly to the appropriate staff.
• Promote efficient work practices and ensure that documents, records, and processes comply with departmental, VPU, and institutional requirements.
• Provide backup support to team members and Team/Program Assistants on project and administrative matters, as needed.
