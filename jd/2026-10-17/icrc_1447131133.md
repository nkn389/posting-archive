Global Process Owner - Institutional Planning & Budgeting

About the ICRC

Since 1863, the International Committee of the Red Cross (ICRC) has worked to preserve human dignity and relieve suffering caused by war and armed violence.

In collaboration with our Red Cross and Red Crescent partners around the world, we do everything we can to deliver life-saving aid, reconnect families and locate missing people - helping those who need it most, regardless of who they are and what side of the front line they are on.

We engage with authorities and armed forces on all sides, often confidentially, pressing for access to detainees to improve their living conditions and urging compliance with international humanitarian law to protect non-combatants, including from digital threats.

Purpose of the Role

As Global Process Owner (GPO), you will lead the design, optimization, governance and implementation of the ICRC’s Institutional Planning & Budgeting process.

You will be an integral member of the End-to-End Transformation Program team, contributing to the transformation of relevant processes, systems and data flows over the next two to four years.

Your key objective is to connect the dots between the ICRC’s Institutional Strategy, operational needs and the underlying planning and budgeting processes. Throughout the redesign, you will need to strike a balance between a longer-term planning horizon (2 x 2 years) and short-term flexibility and agility, reflecting the operational reality of delegations. The goal is a simpler, more integrated planning process, supported by a streamlined architecture of systems, tools and data.

The role requires effective coordination between the Office of the Director-General (ODG) and key stakeholders, including but not limited to Operations, Finance, Logistics, Human Resources, Resource Mobilization, and Support & Digital Transformation.

In addition to your GPO responsibilities, you will play a key part in the Organizational Management & Planning team, supporting the preparation of analyses on topics such as strategic portfolio management, organizational design and others.

Main Duties & Responsibilities

Global Process Owner (60%)

- Lead the design of an integrated planning framework that connects Institutional Strategy and operational needs to multiyear planning and budgeting, as well as quarterly forecasting and reporting

- Drive improved integration between all underlying layers of planning, budgeting and forecasting, including supply chain planning, workforce planning, operational/program planning and financial planning

- Act as a strategic liaison and partner to Operations, Finance, Logistics, Resource Mobilization, Data and Technology teams to capture planning and budgeting needs

- Ensure seamless integration across relevant systems and data flows: ERP, PMT, PAM, Anaplan, RedLoop and Master Data

- Ensure process coherence, reduce inefficiencies, and embed lean, scalable solutions to drive operational excellence

- Define and monitor performance metrics to support continuous process improvement and optimization

- Drive awareness and adoption of best practices

Other responsibilities (40%)

- Support the Head of Organizational Management & Planning in other activities, most notably:

- Supporting specific files in the implementation of the ICRC’s organizational model

- Supporting the oversight of the ICRC’s Strategic Portfolio of Programs

- Providing ad-hoc support and other analyses, where relevant and needed

Note: the balance of focus and workload may shift between the two areas of responsibility and will not always correspond to the 60/40 split.

- Experience in the design, implementation and coordination of planning and monitoring processes

- Experience in the use and design of planning and reporting technologies

- Experience in managing projects and multi-stakeholder environments

- Ideally, experience working for the ICRC or a humanitarian and/or development organization

- Proficiency in data analytics and reporting tools (e.g. Tableau, Microsoft Power BI)
