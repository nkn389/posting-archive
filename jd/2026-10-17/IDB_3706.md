About this position

The Office of Protocol is responsible for planning, organizing, and executing events and activities at the IDB Headquarters in Washington, D.C., and in other member countries of the Bank. It advises Bank departments and units on protocol and ceremonial matters, ensuring the proper application of international protocol rules. It also administers policies related to the use of Bank symbols and corporate identity, Representation Expenses, and the Executive Dining Rooms.

Professionals included in the Talent Pool may provide proactive support and guidance to the Office of Protocol in its daily operations and in activities related to high-level events, with the objective of maintaining and enhancing the quality of services provided by the Office.

What you’ll do

- Provide support in the planning, organization, and execution of ceremonial aspects of official events and meetings, including the IDB Group Annual Meetings, regional meetings of Governors, bilateral meetings, signing ceremonies, National Day celebrations, and other high-level activities.

- Ensure the proper application of protocol and ceremonial standards, including order of precedence, seating arrangements, place cards, official courtesies, and the display and use of Bank symbols.

- Coordinate and support official visits by Heads of State and Government, ministers, governors, ambassadors, Board members, senior executives, and other high-level guests and delegations.

- Prepare briefing materials, executive summaries, biographies, minute-by-minute programs, scripts, agendas, and talking points for high-level events.

- Draft, edit, and revise invitations, verbal notes, diplomatic correspondence, and general protocol-related communications with member countries, embassies, and other stakeholders.

- Greet and escort VIP guests and coordinate with IDB Security and other relevant areas to facilitate their access to Bank facilities and provide administrative support for the scheduling, monitoring, and execution of daily events in the Executive Dining Rooms.

- Execute and monitor internal administrative procedures, including updating protocol databases, diplomatic directories, precedence lists, inventories, and mission calendars.

- Review and update key protocol documents, including frequently asked questions, diplomatic lists, lists of Heads of State and Government, and the Protocol Handbook.

- Support the implementation and updating of policies related to Representation Expenses, the use of Bank symbols, and related inventories.

- Collaborate with team members, share relevant information and technical knowledge, and perform other protocol-related activities within the scope of the assignment, as requested by the supervisor.

What you'll need

Education: Master's degree in International Relations, Political Science, Diplomacy, Public Administration, Hospitality, Event Management, Social Sciences, or another related field. The specific degree requirement will depend on the level of the assignment.

Experience: Atleast 5 years of relevant professional experience in diplomatic protocol, ceremonial activities, high-level event management, government institutions, international organizations, hospitality, or related fields. Assignments may require profiles ranging from at least two years of experience to five or more years of progressively responsible experience. Experience interacting with senior stakeholders and coordinating complex official events is highly desirable.

Languages: Proficiency in English and Spanish. Working knowledge of French and/or Portuguese is a plus.
