Background:

UN Women, grounded in the vision of equality enshrined in the Charter of the United Nations, works for the elimination of discrimination against women and girls; the empowerment of women; and the achievement of equality between women and men as partners and beneficiaries of development, human rights, humanitarian action and peace and security.

Description of Responsibilities /Scope of Work

The National Consultant will provide technical support to update, test and deploy revised content and functionality for the My Journey Mobile App, based on content reviewed and approved by UN Women.

Duties and Responsibilities

In close coordination with the Programme Analyst, Communications Specialist and relevant UN Women programme team, who will provide substantive guidance and review and approve content, the consultant will undertake the following technical tasks:

- Update application branding and digital store content as required under PROTECT.

- Review and consolidate into an editable document the application’s existing content for UN Women’s review, and incorporate the content approved by UN Women into the application.

- Incorporate UN Women-approved updates to service information, content and multimedia resources into the application.

- Verify and improve the application’s functionality by testing and validating all phone numbers, hyperlinks, and other interactive features to ensure they are functional and up to date. UN Women will coordinate the review of the service directory and technical information and provide the updated content to the consultant, who will be responsible for incorporating it into the application.

- Develop and implement the approved enhancements.

- Conduct testing, quality assurance, and deployment.

- Review and address the App compliance with the Google Play Store and other technical requirement to ensure a smooth operation.

- Establish and configure a privacy-sensitive analytics framework to track application uptake and usage, including downloads, active users, user retention, navigation patterns, feature use and relevant technical performance indicators. Ensure that data collection complies with applicable privacy, data protection and safeguarding requirements.

- Develop and implement a user experience feedback and testing approach, including appropriate methods for collecting and analysing user feedback.

- Prepare a concise report summarising user experience findings, uptake and usage data, key challenges, and actionable recommendations for further improvement of the application.

- Develop and hand over a sustainable system for ongoing monitoring of application uptake, usage and user experience after the consultancy ends. This should include access to analytics platforms and dashboards, clear documentation of indicators and data-collection methods, and practical guidance and training for designated UN Women personnel on accessing, interpreting and reporting the data.
