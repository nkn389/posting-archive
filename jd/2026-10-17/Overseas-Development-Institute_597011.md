Description
ODI Global a global affairs think tank, seeks a People Partner: Operations, to lead the practical delivery of People services across our organisation. You will give managers confident, proportionate advice while building a reliable employee experience.

With a new strategy and a strong digital transformation agenda, we are building our People Team, to take ODI Global forward to become an employer of choice.

You will lead employee relations case management, advise on disciplinary, grievance, capability, absence and performance matters, and guide organisational change, consultation and workforce planning. You will oversee fair recruitment and onboarding, including visa process coordination where relevant, improve policies and workflows, and use workforce information to spot risks. You will set priorities, quality check delivery and coach the People Advisor. You will work with the Director of People, managers across the organisation, trade union representatives, the People Partner (Employee Experience) and the People Systems and Data Manager.

We seek significant operational HR partnering and case management experience in a unionised environment, sound UK employment law knowledge and judgement, change and consultation experience, and evidence of supervising an HR professional. You will be able to challenge senior stakeholders constructively, make defensible recommendations, handle confidential information and turn policy into workable practice. A CIPD Level 7 qualification or equivalent , is sought. International workforce, charity or research, immigration, data and digital improvement experience would add value.

ODI Global welcomes applicants from diverse backgrounds and supports equality of opportunity.

Purpose of the Job

To support the operational delivery of ODI Global's People services, ensuring the organisation receives high-quality, timely and pragmatic HR advice and support across the employee lifecycle. The role provides expert guidance to managers on employee relations, organisational change, workforce planning and employment policy, whilst ensuring operational processes are delivered efficiently and consistently.

Working closely with the People Director, the postholder will translate organisational priorities into effective operational delivery, providing business partnering support across the organisation and helping managers build confident, high-performing and inclusive teams.

The postholder will be the primary task manager of the People Advisor, providing clear direction and support ensuring that operational People activities are delivered accurately, consistently and within agreed timescales.

The role also acts as the operational lead for employee relations activity, organisational change programmes and workforce planning, ensuring legal compliance, effective risk management and an excellent employee experience.

Main Duties and Responsibilities
This role is responsible for leading the operational delivery of the People Function and ensuring that managers and colleagues receive responsive, consistent and professional support across the full employee lifecycle.

Operational HR

- Deliver the day-to-day operational People services across ODI Global.

- Provide professional HR advice to managers on the interpretation and application of employment policies and procedures.

- Ensure consistent application of employment legislation, organisational policies and best practice.

- Develop effective working relationships with managers to provide proactive business partnering support.

- Support managers to address workforce issues in a timely, constructive and legally compliant manner.

- Promote continuous improvement in People processes and service delivery.

- Provide effective supervision and support to the People Advisor as primary task manager.

- Set clear objectives, priorities and performance expectations for the People Advisor.

- Monitor the quality, accuracy and timeliness of work undertaken by the People Advisor.

Employee Relations

- Lead on employee relations case management across the organisation.

- Provide advice and guidance on disciplinary, grievance, capability, absence management, performance, probation and conduct matters.

- Support managers in resolving workplace issues through early intervention and informal resolution where appropriate.

- Commission and manage external employment law or specialist HR advice where complex or high-risk cases require additional expertise.

- Monitor employee relations trends and identify opportunities to reduce organisational risk.

- Provide guidance and oversight to the People Advisor where they are supporting employee relations casework or administration.

Organisational Change

- Support organisational restructuring, consultation exercises and change programmes.

- Provide professional advice on restructuring, redundancy, redeployment and organisational design.

- Support managers throughout consultation processes and implementation of organisational change.

- Ensure organisational change activity complies with employment legislation and organisational policy.

- Coordinate and oversee administrative support provided by the People Advisor during consultation and change processes.

Workforce Planning and Recruitment

- Support workforce planning activity across the organisation.

- Work with managers and oversee the People Advisor in coordinating recruitment and onboarding activity, including the application process for staff requiring visas in line with UKVI requirements.

- Advise on workforce planning, resource requirements and organisational capability.

- Ensure recruitment processes are delivered fairly, efficiently and in accordance with organisational policy.

- Allocate recruitment and onboarding activity to the People Advisor and provide advice, support and quality assurance as required.

- Support the People Advisor to develop their recruitment, onboarding and wider employee lifecycle capability.

Policy Development

- Support the review, development and implementation of People policies and procedures.

- Ensure policies remain legally compliant and aligned with organisational priorities.

- Develop guidance and briefing materials to support managers and employees.

- Ensure the People Advisor is appropriately briefed and trained to support the consistent administration of People policies and procedures.

Service Improvement

- Identify opportunities to improve operational efficiency through process redesign and digital solutions.

- Work collaboratively with the People Systems & Data Manager to improve workflows and employee self-service.

- Contribute to continuous improvement initiatives across the People Function.

- Involve and support the People Advisor in identifying service improvements and implementing revised processes and ways of working.

Relationship Manager

- Build effective relationships with managers, employees, Trade Union representatives and external advisers.

- Represent the People Function on organisational projects and working groups as required.

- Support the People Director in delivering strategic People initiatives.

- Support the People Advisor to build effective working relationships with managers, employees and colleagues across the organisation.

General

- Contribute to departmental planning and delivery of the People Strategy.

- Maintain accurate records and ensure confidentiality of employee information.

- Prepare reports, briefing papers and management information as required, including providing data and analysis to support Board reporting.

- Manage the workload, priorities and development needs of the People Advisor.

- Ensure appropriate cover and resilience arrangements are in place for operational People services.

- Undertake other duties appropriate to the grade and responsibilities of the post.

- Work collaboratively as required across the Team to ensure an appropriate People Service is maintained.
