The Economics and Financial Markets for Asia and the Pacific (EFMAP) Unit of the Bank for International Settlements (BIS) is seeking a talented Data Scientists / Research Analysts.

This role is based in Hong Kong SAR. However, thanks to our status as an international organization, we can hire globally and welcome applications from candidates of all nationalities and locations worldwide. Relocation support is available for the successful candidate and their dependent family members.

Application deadline: Please note that the deadline for applications is Tuesday 3 November at the end of day.

Please note that this role is offered as a three-year fixed term contract.

Purpose of the job:

Under the supervision of a line manager or senior colleagues, you will:

- Provide research support to EFMAP economists.

- Bridge the gap between economics, data, and technology.

- Enhance our analytical capabilities through your expertise.

By joining the EFMAP team, you will:

- Contribute to our mission of supporting central banks in their pursuit of monetary and financial stability.

- Engage with emerging issues in the global economy, offering diverse challenges and learning opportunities.

- Enjoy a team-oriented culture with opportunities for collaboration in a diverse environment.

- Benefit from working in a unique, international setting that offers flexible, hybrid working options with a blend of onsite work in Hong Kong and home office.

Principal Accountabilities:

- Supporting economists in preparing key publications, meeting notes for central bank governors, research papers, and senior management presentations.

- Conducting data analysis, including collection, quality checks, methodology review, and data presentation.

- Developing and maintaining databases to streamline internal workflows.

- Seeking out new data sources and advanced analytic methods.

- Sharing knowledge and expertise in data and technology.

- Engaging in cross-functional collaborative innovation projects.

Qualifications, skills and experience:

- Education: Master’s degree in Economics, Finance, Data Science, Statistics, Computer Science, or a related field.

- Experience: Professional experience in monetary economics, macroeconomics, financial markets, or data science with a focus on Asia would be an advantage.

- Technical Skills: Proficiency in data analysis or programming tools (e.g., Python, R, MATLAB, Stata). Familiarity with big data technologies and AI techniques would be an advantage.

- Economic Knowledge: Background in economics or financial markets, or a strong interest in applying technical skills in these areas.

- Teamwork: Ability to collaborate in a multicultural environment.

- Communication: Good verbal and written communication skills in English, with the ability to explain complex concepts to a non-expert audience.

The ideal candidate is a fast learner, eager to work across various topics with a passion for innovation.

Please note that the BIS's corporate language is English, which is used for all internal and external communication.

Who we are:

Internationalism is at the core of our identity and the best representation of this is our workforce. With employees from over 66 countries and offices in nine locations, the BIS is a global organisation with a truly international workforce. By joining us, you will work in a unique, highly rewarding, and international work environment. We are committed to equal opportunities at the BIS and aim to build a workforce that reflects our global membership. We strive to attract the best talent and foster an inclusive environment. We welcome applications from all qualified candidates, including those with a breadth of professional experience. You can discover more about our inclusive working environment here.

What the BIS offers:

We want your time at BIS to be a rewarding and career-enriching experience. We offer an agile and flexible working environment with hybrid working opportunities including home office and working from abroad days. To support our international applicants, we offer relocation support that extends to your dependent family members. In addition, we offer a competitive compensation and benefits package, including support for working families including childcare and education allowances (where applicable). Finally, we offer a genuinely unique international working environment, which will give you exposure to the global financial system and the opportunity to collaborate with passionate experts from all over the world.
