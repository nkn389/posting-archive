Program Overview
World Resources Institute (WRI) is an independent global research organization that turns big ideas into action at the nexus of environment, economic opportunity, and human well-being. The Energy and Sustainable Business (ESB) program works to advance decarbonization among corporates and accelerate a just transition towards net zero emission in Indonesia, while balancing the need for economic development. In achieving that, WRI introduces two of its flagship products, which are: Greenhouse Gas Protocol (GHGP) for emission accounting and Science-based Targets initiative (SBTi) for ambitious and science-aligned emission reduction target setting, within and beyond corporate value chains. By adapting the tools to the national context and pairing them with rigorous policy and economic analysis, WRI Indonesia guides both government and corporates towards a complete net zero journey, aiming to increase Indonesian industry's competitiveness through strengthened standards and climate mitigation/adaptation measures which ultimately contribute significantly to Indonesia's emission reduction.

WRI Indonesia believes that sustainable development highlights the need for integrated approaches to finding solutions that are aligned with the challenge of achieving economic, social, and environmental goals that are often interlinked. This can be a daunting task, as the interlinkages are complex and context specific and depend on the issues under consideration, the geographic and temporal scales, sector of the industry, and the existing technologies. Therefore, the ESB program aims to work across disciplines with the climate, cities, and forest and land use teams to combine decarbonization frameworks, quantitative and economic models, low-carbon technology solutions, and other policy-making decision tools to support and achieve key national commitments, such as Indonesia's net zero ambition.

Green Industrial Policy Package to Enable Industrial Net Zero in Indonesia by 2050
Indonesia's Ministry of Industry, supported by WRI Indonesia and partners, has formulated a roadmap towards a clean and net zero industry sector by 2050 covering nine priority subsectors: cement, iron and steel, fertilizers, chemicals, pulp and paper, textiles, glass and ceramics, automotive, and food and beverages. The roadmap sets out an ambition to reduce emissions by around 66.5 million tCO2e by 2035 and 289.7 million tCO2e by 2050 — a decade ahead of Indonesia's national net zero target — through energy efficiency, fuel substitution, electrification, process and technology upgrades, and carbon capture. Delivering that trajectory, however, depends on an enabling policy and economic environment that does not yet exist in coherent form.

This research develops a Green Industrial Policy Package to Enable Industrial Net Zero in Indonesia by 2050: an integrated set of policy and economic instruments that makes low-carbon production the commercially rational choice for Indonesian industry. The work examines the design and sequencing of three policy design stage mainly: strategic case building, policy package design, and macroeconomic impact analysis. The policy package design will center around three key areas: clean input infrastructure (clean energy, sustainable material, and low carbon technology), market, and financing.

Job Highlight
The Senior Economist will provide technical leadership in conducting and coordinating the economic analysis underpinning WRI Indonesia's clean industry transition work and to raise the analytical standard of the ESB program's policy products. The consultant will contribute substantive economic expertise to the study on “Green Industrial Policy Package to Support Industrial Net Zero by 2050 in Indonesia” including contributing to the development of evidence-based analysis and policy recommendation.

The work spans across empirical trend and cross-country comparative analysis, green demand signal mapping and market sizing, trade and investment exposure analysis including CBAM cost calculations, barrier and market failure diagnosis, industrial and trade policy design, as well as co-development and interpretation of CGD-based macroeconomic scenarios. The work is also highly collaborative, demanding coordination with government, industries, and other think tanks.

The Senior Economist must stay abreast of climate and industrial policy developments, energy transition economics, and macroeconomic trends relevant to Indonesia's industrial landscape. Quantitative rigor, sound economic judgment, critical thinking, stakeholder collaboration, and the ability to translate complex analysis into decision-ready advice are expected and highly rewarded skills for this position. The Senior Economist will work with analysts within WRI Indonesia's ESB team and other teams across the institution.

This position will be based in Jakarta with an office-flex working arrangement. This position will be reporting directly to the Senior Manager for Energy and Sustainable Business, working closely and collaboratively with the Net Zero Senior Analyst and the wider research team within the Energy and Sustainable Business portfolio. In support of WRI's DEI commitment, the role is expected to actively contribute to these collaborative efforts by integrating equity into the work, workplace, and organizational culture.

What You Will Need

- Demonstrated proficiency in applied quantitative methods, including econometrics and at least one type of economy-wide or sectoral modelling (e.g., input-output, CGE, partial equilibrium, system dynamics, or techno-economic modelling), with proficiency in relevant analytical software (e.g., R, Python, Stata, GAMS, or equivalent).
- Strong working knowledge of carbon pricing design and Indonesia's Nilai Ekonomi Karbon (NEK) framework, and of fiscal and industrial policy instruments used to support decarbonization.

- Ability to communicate research approach and findings effectively to technical and non-technical audiences, both in Bahasa Indonesia and English.
