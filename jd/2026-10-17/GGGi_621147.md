INTRODUCTION TO GGGI

The Global Green Growth Institute (GGGI) is a treaty-based international, inter-governmental organization dedicated to supporting and promoting strong, inclusive and sustainable economic growth in developing countries and emerging economies. To learn more please visit about GGGI web page.

The Global Green Growth Institute (GGGI) is implementing the Green Coupon Subsidy Facility (GCSF), Pillar III of the European Union’s Global Green Bond Initiative (GGBI). The Facility is designed to expand access of low- and middle-income countries to international and local green capital markets by lowering the cost of capital associated with eligible green and sustainability bond issuances.

The GCSF provides targeted grant subsidies to eligible sovereign, sub-sovereign and corporate issuers and complements the GGBI Fund and Technical Assistance pillar. GGGI is responsible for the assessment, approval, grant management, monitoring and reporting functions of the Facility, working closely with the European Commission, EU Delegations, the GGBI Fund and Pillar II Implementing Partners.

The Facility is implemented through a Facility Management Unit and a Transaction Management Unit. The Facility Management Unit is responsible for the overall strategic, operational and financial management of the GCSF, including portfolio oversight, governance, legal and contractual matters, grant administration, financial management, results monitoring, audit coordination and Facility-level reporting.

POSITION SUMMARY

The GCSF Facility Manager will lead the implementation of the Green Coupon Subsidy Facility and have overall accountability for its strategic, operational and financial management. The position will lead the GCSF team, oversee the Facility’s transaction pipeline and grant portfolio, and serve as GGGI’s primary operational interface with the European Commission.

Based in Luxembourg, the Facility Manager will work closely with GGGI teams in Sub-Saharan Africa and other eligible regions and with European Commission counterparts in Brussels. The position requires approximately 20% travel to Brussels, as well as periodic travel to GCSF partner countries and other locations as required.
