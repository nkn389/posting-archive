Org. Setting and Reporting
This position is based in the Office of Legal Affairs (OLA) of the International Seabed Authority in Kingston, Jamaica. The International Seabed Authority is an international organization established under the United Nations Convention on the Law of the Sea (UNCLOS) and the 1994 Agreement relating to the Implementation of Part XI of UNCLOS. Pursuant to these instruments, the Authority is the organization through which States Parties organize and control activities in the Area, namely the seabed, ocean floor and subsoil thereof beyond the limits of national jurisdiction, particularly with a view to administering the mineral resources of the Area. The Office of Legal Affairs provides legal advice and support to the Authority's organs and the Secretariat on matters arising from the interpretation and application of the Convention, the 1994 Agreement and the rules, regulations and procedures adopted by the Authority.

Responsibilities
Under the overall guidance of the Secretary-General and the direct supervision of the Director of the Office of Legal Affairs/Legal Counsel, and within delegated authority, the Senior Legal Officer (Regulatory Affairs) will be responsible for the following duties: 1. Regulatory development and legal leadership i) Leads and coordinates the development of the Mining Code and associated standards and guidelines, providing expert legal analysis and advice on complex, multidisciplinary and sensitive regulatory matters, including issues involving competing legal interpretations, institutional considerations and policy objectives. ii) Coordinates and directs the work of legal officers and other assigned personnel engaged in regulatory development; establishes priorities, reviews legal work, provides guidance and supervision, and ensures the consistency, quality and timely delivery of assigned outputs. iii) Advises on the legal coherence of proposed regulatory provisions and their relationship to UNCLOS, the 1994 Agreement, applicable decisions of the Authority and other relevant rules and procedures, identifying legal gaps, ambiguities and implementation issues and proposing appropriate solutions. 2. Legal advice, research and analysis i) Provides authoritative legal advice to the Secretary-General, the Director of the Office of Legal Affairs/Legal Counsel, the Council and the Legal and Technical Commission, as appropriate, on complex substantive and procedural questions relating to the development and application of the regulatory framework for activities in the Area, including exploration and exploitation. ii) Conducts and supervises extensive legal research and analysis on complex, novel or sensitive questions of international law, the law of the sea and the law of international organizations relevant to the Authority's regulatory mandate; prepares and reviews legal opinions, studies, briefs, reports, memoranda and correspondence, and develops recommendations for consideration by the appropriate decision-making bodies. iii) Advises senior officials and relevant units of the Secretariat on legal and regulatory matters within the incumbent's area of responsibility, including the legal implications of proposed measures and the options available to address identified issues. 3. Management, negotiation and coordination i) Assists the Director of the Office of Legal Affairs/Legal Counsel in the management and administration of the relevant legal work programme, including the planning, prioritization, allocation, coordination and review of assignments, and the monitoring of progress against agreed priorities and deadlines. ii) Advises on and supervises the review, negotiation and drafting of complex contracts and other legal instruments related to the regulatory work programme, including agreements with consultants, experts and researchers; identifies appropriate legal and institutional arrangements and develops solutions to address novel legal requirements and operational circumstances. iii) Coordinates, as appropriate, with relevant offices and units of the Secretariat, Member States, experts and other stakeholders to facilitate the effective preparation, review and implementation of regulatory proposals, ensuring that legal considerations are appropriately reflected in the relevant processes. 4. Support to intergovernmental and technical bodies i) Services meetings of the Council, the Legal and Technical Commission and relevant intersessional working groups, expert groups, task forces and other subsidiary or ad hoc bodies, as assigned; prepares and reviews legal and background materials, analytical summaries of issues and positions expressed by delegations, draft texts, road maps, meeting reports and other documentation required to facilitate deliberations. ii) Provides legal and procedural guidance during relevant meetings and negotiations, as appropriate, and advises on drafting options, outstanding legal questions and possible approaches to resolving differences, while respecting the respective mandates and decision-making authority of the bodies concerned. 5. Representation, reporting and other duties i) Represents the Office of Legal Affairs and, as authorized, the Authority at meetings, conferences, seminars and other relevant fora; contributes to the presentation and dissemination of the Authority's work on regulatory affairs and prepares substantive written and oral briefings for senior officials. ii) Prepares and reviews reports, recommendations and other submissions for senior management and the relevant organs of the Authority on progress, emerging legal issues, risks and priorities relating to the development of the Mining Code and associated standards and guidelines. iii) Performs other duties within the scope of the post, as requested by the Director of the Office of Legal Affairs/Legal Counsel or the Secretary-General.
