ORGANIZATIONAL CONTEXT:

WFP is the world’s largest humanitarian organization fighting hunger and supporting progress towards Zero Hunger. Through its global operations, WFP works to ensure that vulnerable people can access the food and nutrition they need.
The Programme Operations Staffing Coordination and Capacity Service (POS), reporting to the Assistant Executive Director for Programme Operations, supports staffing coordination, workforce planning, talent management and capacity development across Programme Operations. POS helps align people, roles and skills to strengthen programme delivery and organizational effectiveness. POS also coordinates cross-functional learning initiatives within the Programme Operations Department to strengthen staff capabilities, support mobility across functions, and ensure staff have the skills and knowledge required to perform effectively. To support this work, WFP is seeking a Learning and Development Specialist to lead and coordinate the learning and capacity development advisory services for WFP Programme Operations functions. The specialist will work as part of the POS team and collaborate with functional and staffing coordination colleagues to ensure learning activities respond to identified needs and gaps and align with operational priorities.

The position leads a team and reports to the Staffing Coordinator (or delegate) within POS and works closely with other L&D colleagues, based at WFP Headquarters in Rome, Italy.

JOB PURPOSE

The position will provide specialized support and lead global Programme Operations learning initiatives, assure effective learning experiences and continuous enhancement of the Programme Operations Learning & Development approach.

KEY ACCOUNTABILITIES (not all-inclusive, within delegated authority):

Under the direct supervision of the Staffing Coordinator (or delegate) within POS the Staff Development Officer will work in close collaboration and coordination with the HRMTC branch and will have responsibilities as outlined below:

- Leveraging available learning resources – lead the design, development and implementation of appropriate learning solutions so that Programme Operations staff can perform their work optimally to deliver WFP’s mandate and adjust to industry best-practice developments relevant for the humanitarian sector.
- Oversight on technical, cross-cutting and soft skills trainings to enable a cohesive approach.
- Facilitate the coordination between key stakeholders such as internal functional experts, trainers and instructional designer and/or vendor, staffing coordinator.
- Develop guidelines for streamlining functional learning and development processes applicable to both Global HQ and field colleagues.
- Support the development of strategic learning structures that would adapt to evolving needs.
- Continuously monitor progress and provide feedback and evaluation of the different learning activities to the Staffing Coordination team.
- Measure and evaluate effectiveness of learning events and products which feed into Programme Operations’ integrated workforce management and informed decision-making processes.
- Provide inputs to the L&D component of the workforce planning and update information accordingly.
- Support the development of a comprehensive annual learning plan for Programme Operations to ensure that training requirements, needs, and expectations are met.
- Drive learning and develop innovation ideas and projects with a strategic focus on enhanced and improved performance.
- Research the viability of new programme offerings, market potential, and competitive pricing models, developing unique programming in response to wider humanitarian community needs and interests in collaboration with the corporate learning and development team in HR.
- Design and lead the roll-out of cross-functional learning pathways and learning journeys to support mobility and capability development across Programme Operations functions.
- Maintain and update the L&D dashboards, contributing and leading relevant improvements, recommendations, and change in order to effectively communicate the strategic relevance of L&D on the Programme Operations mandate.
- Provide strategic advice to Programme Operations functions on learning and development priorities, capability gaps and appropriate interventions.
- Develop the terms of reference, coordinate and moderate a network of regional training focal points for the design and coordination of regional training plans for Programme Operations.
- Manage and guide the work of the learning and development team, ensuring delivery of agreed objectives and outputs.
- Establish and maintain partnerships with internal and external stakeholders to enhance learning and development approaches and initiatives.
- Promote a culture of continuous learning and knowledge sharing across Programme Operations.
- Perform other duties as required.

DELIVERABLES AT THE END OF THE CONTRACT:

- Professional L&D activities are rolled out at global level in a harmonized way.
- Enhanced visibility of L&D within Programme Operations.
- Improved communication and relevance of L&D being incorporated into Staffing Coordinator actions, contributing to talent management.
- An established network of Programme Operations focal points.
- An established comprehensive annual training plan that is renewable and adaptable to future needs.
