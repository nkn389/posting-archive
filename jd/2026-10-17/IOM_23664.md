Introduction
Established in 1951, IOM is a Related Organization of the United Nations and the leading UN agency in the field of migration. Working closely with governmental, intergovernmental and non-governmental partners, IOM promotes humane and orderly migration for the benefit of all. It saves lives and protects people on the move, drives solutions to displacement, and facilitates pathways for regular migration, while providing services and advice to governments and migrants.
IOM is committed to fostering a respectful, inclusive and supportive workplace where all employees can thrive professionally and feel valued. By creating such an environment, IOM aims to better harness the full potential of migration and strengthen its support to people on the move.

Organizational Context and Scope
The International Organization for Migration (IOM) Lao People’s Democratic Republic supports safe, orderly, and humane migration in alignment with national priorities and international standards. Established in Lao PDR in 2002 and becoming a full member state in 2018, IOM works closely with government institutions, civil society, and international partners to strengthen migration governance, assist vulnerable migrants including victims of trafficking, and promote sustainable development. The mission emphasizes building government capacity, providing humanitarian assistance when needed, and creating opportunities for migrants to contribute positively to society. Through collaboration with national institutions, communities, and international partners, IOM Lao PDR advances effective migration management and fosters resilience for both migrants and host communities.
Under the overall supervision of the Head of Programmes and direct supervision of the National Health Project Officer, the successful candidate will provide full support on the implementation of Migration Health activities in IOM Lao PDR.
