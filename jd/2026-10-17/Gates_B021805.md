The Team
The Global Development (GD) Division includes a diverse range of program areas focused on finding creative ways to ensure solutions and products get into the hands of people in low-income countries. We focus on areas with the potential for high-impact, sustainable solutions that can reach millions of people. Our strategies include Emergency Response, Immunization, and Polio Eradication. A common theme across these programs is focus on innovative delivery, including an emphasis on strengthening primary health care systems. Our teams work in close partnership with Regional and Country Office Teams to align the foundation's health and development equity agenda with the government's broader priorities.

Within GD, the Exemplars in Global Health (EGH) program equips decision-makers with the evidence, insights, and practical “know-how” needed to accelerate and scale interventions that improve the health of the world's most vulnerable populations. Working with a global network of partners, EGH identifies and studies exemplars and best practices across health outcomes, primary health care systems, and service delivery strategies.

Through rigorous research, evidence synthesis, and timely analysis, EGH translates lessons from what works into actionable insights that help policymakers, practitioners, and funders strengthen systems, improve service delivery, and address pressing global health challenges.

We pursue this through:
1. The study of “positive outliers”—identifying countries or regions that have achieved exceptional progress in health outcomes and analyzing the drivers behind their success;
2. The study of emerging practices with global learning value, using prospective research and real-time analytics alongside implementation to meet the ambition of 'learning by doing';
3. The provision of internal advisory services to support teams in pursuit of the Foundation's three goals toward 2045; and
4. External partnerships to scale exemplary practices with governments, policymakers, global health initiatives, and multilateral development banks.

Your Role
The Senior Manager, Portfolio Planning & Team Operations, will provide strategic leadership of business and team operations for the EGH team, including management of Program Coordinator (PC) and Program Assistant (PA) teams. You will be a key thought partner for the Deputy Director, Strategy, Planning and Management (DDSPM) to develop and monitor portfolio allocation strategy and assess results against resourcing spent. You will own core business processes for the team, including developing a research project pipeline management approach, overseeing investment making, and leading the EGH team’s annual planning process.

What You'll Do

- Provide strategic leadership of business and team operations for the EGH team.

- Own the team's operating rhythm, including budget planning, resourcing, and portfolio-wide tracking against goals.

- Develop a rigorous approach to research project pipeline management and execution.

- Lead processes to develop and oversee team investment of dollars and people time, as well as financial and operational processes, which may include the full investment development process, annual planning, and budget management and forecasting.

- Manage and develop the SPC and PA teams, setting priorities and ensuring high-quality operational support across EGH.

- Serve as a thought partner to EGH leadership on organizational design, workflow, and resource allocation questions.

- Develop and monitor portfolio allocation strategy and execution.

- Build systems and processes that improve efficiency, consistency, and decision-making across the team's research and investment portfolio.

- Develop analyses and second-order insights to assess portfolio progress and guide resource allocation decisions.

- Provide critical support to program leadership in translating strategy goals into coherent portfolio level theory of action and plans that align staff time and investments to strategic goals.

- Monitor research project pipeline and investment portfolio to facilitate management decision-making, including providing guidance to deputy directors to ensure portfolio resource use is strategically aligned, with clearly articulated outcomes that link to key strategy goals.

- Monitor research, grant and contract pipeline to produce accurate forecasts vs. budget reporting and performance against key deliverables and outcomes to facilitate management decision-making.

- Identify strategic and portfolio execution risks, as well as develop and support implementation of risk mitigation.

- Lead team operations to drive overall team efficiency, effectiveness, and positive experience.

- Expertly facilitate the shaping of people processes and team dynamics to champion culture and incentives that best enable achieving program goals, in partnership with program leadership, SPA, and Culture Working Group.

- Collaborate with SPA to develop and run team rhythm of business.

- Foster enabling conditions for talent to thrive and be accountable for inclusive team culture.
