Introduction
Established in 1951, IOM is a Related Organization of the United Nations and the leading UN agency in the field of migration. Working closely with governmental, intergovernmental and non-governmental partners, IOM promotes humane and orderly migration for the benefit of all. It saves lives and protects people on the move, drives solutions to displacement, and facilitates pathways for regular migration, while providing services and advice to governments and migrants.
IOM is committed to fostering a respectful, inclusive and supportive workplace where all employees can thrive professionally and feel valued. By creating such an environment, IOM aims to better harness the full potential of migration and strengthen its support to people on the move.

Organizational Context and Scope
Under the overall supervision of the Programme Coordinator (Global Health Assessment Programme) and direct supervision of the Senior Health Operations and Compliance Officer, the Programme Officer (Health Assessment Programme – (HAP)) will coordinate and support global HAP continuing professional development initiatives and continuous programme monitoring activities, including virtual Migration Health Assessment Centres (MHAC) monitoring visits, follow-up of corrective actions, and analysis of programme performance trends.

1. Continuing Professional Development (CPD)

- Coordinate the planning, implementation, and evaluation of HAP continuing professional development programmes for Chief Migration Health Officers (CMHOs), Senior Migration Health Physicians, and other HAP personnel.
- Support the development of annual learning strategies, competency frameworks, training plans, and professional development pathways for HAP managers and key personnel.
- Coordinate webinars, workshops, leadership development initiatives, mentorship programmes, communities of practice, and other learning activities.
- Liaise with technical focal points, regional offices, and external subject matter experts to develop and update training materials, guidance documents, case studies, and learning resources.
- Monitor participation, learning outcomes, and effectiveness of CPD activities and prepare reports and recommendations for programme improvement.
- Support the development, periodic review, and dissemination of operational guidance, handbooks, standard operating procedures, and best-practice resources arising from CPD initiatives.
- Maintain learning records and contribute to tracking professional development requirements and completion rates across the global HAP network.
2. Continuous Programme Monitoring

- Support the design and implementation of a global continuous monitoring framework for Health Assessment Programmes.
- Monitor programme performance indicators, quality assurance findings, compliance metrics, operational risks, and corrective action plans across MHACs.
- Analyze data and programme trends and prepare dashboards, briefing notes, management reports, and recommendations for continuous improvement.
- Support the identification of operational, compliance, and quality risks and coordinate follow-up actions with country offices, regional offices, and technical focal points.
- Monitor implementation of recommendations arising from audits, monitoring visits, evaluations, and compliance reviews.
- Contribute to the development and refinement of monitoring tools, assessment checklists, performance indicators, and reporting systems.
- Coordinate regular programme review meetings and maintain tracking systems for findings, recommendations, and management actions.
3. Virtual MHAC Monitoring Visits

- Coordinate and participate in virtual monitoring visits to MHACs globally, in collaboration with regional coordinators and relevant technical units.
- Review pre-assessment documentation, self-assessment reports, monitoring tools, programme data, and supporting evidence prior to monitoring visits.
- Conduct virtual assessments of MHAC operations, workflows, service delivery processes, facility readiness, quality assurance systems, and compliance with HAP requirements.
- Facilitate virtual interviews, focus group discussions, and technical consultations with MHAC management and operational staff.
- Document findings, good practices, risks, gaps, and recommendations in monitoring reports.
- Support the development of corrective and preventive action plans and monitor implementation of agreed actions.
- Maintain a repository of monitoring findings and contribute to the identification of recurrent global trends requiring policy, operational, or training interventions.
4. Programme Support and Coordination

- Provide technical and administrative support for global HAP projects and strategic initiatives related to quality assurance, compliance, operational excellence, and capacity building.
- Draft reports, briefing notes, presentations, concept notes, and management updates for senior leadership.
- Coordinate collaboration between headquarters, regional offices, country offices, external partners, and technical focal points.
- Contribute to the preparation of workplans, budgets, donor reports, and project documentation related to HAP quality improvement initiatives.
- Perform such other duties as may be assigned.
