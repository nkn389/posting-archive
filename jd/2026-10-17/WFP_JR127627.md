BACKGROUND AND PURPOSE OF THE ASSIGNMENT:
WFP delivers food, cash and voucher assistance to people in need around the world. It is a corporate priority to increase the level of assurance for food delivery in WFP’s country operations and to record the expense recognition of food once it is distributed to households.
This assignment supports the continued development, deployment and adoption of corporate Identity Management (IDM) and delivery solutions for in-kind and cash operations, including SCOPE and related corporate systems. HQ SCD and TEC teams are responsible for ensuring that corporate solutions meet corporate standards and remain responsive to field-level business needs.
The Business Analyst will support business requirements gathering, process design, field-facing analysis and implementation support for IDM and delivery services. The assignment contributes to implementation of the UN80 CCI provisions and supports a strengthened, field-oriented service model for Country Offices.
