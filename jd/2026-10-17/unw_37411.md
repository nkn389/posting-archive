Background:

UN Women, grounded in the vision of equality enshrined in the Charter of the United Nations, works for the elimination of discrimination against women and girls; the empowerment of women; and the achievement of equality between women and men as partners and beneficiaries of development, human rights, humanitarian action and peace and security.

In Indonesia, UN Women works with government, civil society organizations, academia, the media, the public and private sectors to address national priorities of gender equality and women’s empowerment. Placing the advancement of women’s rights at the centre of our work, UN Women Indonesia focuses on (1) ending violence against women (2) women’s economic empowerment (3) governance and women’s peace and security.

To support the effective implementation of the communication plans and activities of the programmes, UN Women Indonesia Office continuously requires the production of communication, advocacy, and visibility materials. To maximize its reach, these materials will be disseminated through various channels, including social media, articles and publications, external media networks, partner networks, and reports. In view of responding to the needs of the office for the above-mentioned purposes, UN Women seeks to hire qualified individuals for photography, videography and video editing services to contribute to a pool of photos and produce quality video contents that capture key gender equality and women's empowerment issues and UN Women's responses, particularly in project sites such as Aceh and East Nusa Tenggara.

The consultant will be working closely with the Advocacy and Communication Analyst and Programme Officers to support communications and dissemination of information about programme events, stories and results through UN Women’s channels, as well as sharing for use by UN agencies. The photographer/videographer should be based in the country for which they are submitting proposals.

Description of Responsibilities /Scope of Work

Objective of the Assignment

The consultant will be responsible for photography, videography, and video editing services related for the purpose of UN Women programme or communication needs, for both internal and external audience, including coverage of key targeted events, and UN Women-related local activities and initiatives. The consultant will be required to have her/his own technical and software-based equipment to deliver the services specified in this ToR.

Scope of Work

The consultant will work under supervision from the Advocacy and Communication Analyst of UN Women Indonesia and will perform the following tasks:

- Photography

- Provide professional photographic coverage in digital original for UN Women; image acquisition includes unconditional non-exclusive and perpetual image rights (see section VI below);

- Successfully capture and emotionally express the core elements of the situation assigned for coverage;

- Cover a range of related subjects (different components of events/activities, as well as general photographs that illustrate the real, positive and negative, situation of women and girls);

- Provide different visual perspectives (close-up, medium range, long distance), in both horizontal and vertical formats;

- Make sure pictures are technically good (properly exposed, processed and framed);

- Cover all assignments in digital original with images shot at the highest possible resolution that the camera permits;

- Provide raw full take upon request as well as edited, optimized selection of best images from each assignment;

- Undertake field trips to ensure the coverage of special events/activities at the country level. UN Women will undertake all the necessary travel arrangements;

- Consultant have the right to be credited, implying that their name should accompany all originals and reproductions of their work;

- Abide by UN Women’s Photo Policy and Guidelines document, as provided.

2. Videography

- Record and produce HD videos to document events, activities, statements, documentaries, and interview key staff/partners/beneficiaries per request of UN Women to ensure capturing of UN Women’s activities in video format;

- Provide different visual perspectives (close-up, medium range, long distance videos, multi exposure, etc.);

- Export and deliver audio track of videos as per request of UN Women;

- Export final version of a video in formats optimized for HD web distribution and TV broadcast and deliver to UN Women;

- Final approved videos shall be provided in raw format and optimized resolution to UN Women.

3. Video Editing

- Edit short-form and long-form quality videos for various communication purposes.

- Develop engaging narratives in collaboration with UN Women Indonesia team that aligns with project objectives and target audiences.

- Utilize advanced video editing software (e.g., Adobe Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro) to create polished and engaging videos.

- Incorporate graphics, lower thirds, subtitles, special effects, music, and sound effects as required to enhance video content.

- Perform color correction, color grading, and audio enhancement to ensure professional quality outputs.

- Ensure ethical use of music tracks.

- Ensure all video content meets high-quality standards and is consistent with UN Women’s Audio-Visual Guideline.

Final Product and Deliverables:

Specific requirements for photo submission:

- Resolution must be minimum 4000 pixels on the long side in the original file

- For every event photographed throughout the contract period, on the same day, a minimum of 50 colour digital images in high resolution shot as a RAW file and highest quality JPEG files will be delivered to UN Women Indonesia.

- Each image from the optimized edit as well as the full take must include in the IPTC metadata: the full name of the photographer for photo credit, the name of the event, the date, the venue, and the city/country. If possible and relevant, names and titles should be included as well. IPTC metadata can be embedded within the photo through the use of image editing software such as Photoshop, Light room, or Photo Mechanic. In addition to required info, a descriptive caption written in complete sentences is preferred.

- Deliver raw and edited photos via file transfer links for downloads.

Specific requirements for video footage submission:

- Deliverables must meet minimum video technical standards for HD recording.

- Frame rate and codec: 1080/30p (29.97p)

- Pixel aspect ratio: 1920 x 1080

- Picture framing: 16x9, please protect action within 4:3 center-cut picture safety

- Audio: 48K sample rate, 24 or 16 bit, peak up to -6db

- Deliver raw video files via hard drive or file transfer links for downloads.

Specific requirements for edited video submission:

- Deliverables must be in MP4 format.

- Deliverable submission package includes:

- Final edited video with High-Res CODEC (such as Apple Hi-Res 422 HQ or LT) with platform-specific resolution exports, if required.

- Clean versions of the edited video, if required, such as:

- Final video with no background music.

- Final video with no background music, no subtitles.

- Final video with no background music, no subtitles, no texts or typography.

- Final video with no background music, no subtitles, no texts or typography, and no lowerthirds.

- SRT file of the subtitles.

- Music tracks and copyright certificates or proof of eligibility of use for non-profit purposes.
