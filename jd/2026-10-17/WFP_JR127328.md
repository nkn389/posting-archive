Indonesia has established the necessary regulatory framework for fortified rice, including national standards for Fortified Rice Kernels (FRK) and Fortified Rice (FR). However, translating these standards into large-scale implementation requires sustained technical coordination, cross-ministerial alignment, and day-to-day engagement with key government stakeholders.
To support this process, WFP seeks to engage a Programme Policy Officer to be seconded to the Coordinating Ministry of Food to provide embedded technical expertise on rice fortification, food safety and nutrition. The incumbent will play a critical role in facilitating coordination across ministries, advancing pilot implementation, and supporting evidence-based planning for scale-up.BACKGROUND AND PURPOSE OF THE ASSIGNMENT:

The Government of Indonesia is advancing the integration of nutrition-sensitive approaches within national social protection and food systems programmes, including the Makan Bergizi Gratis (MBG) programme. Given the centrality of rice in national diets and public food programmes, rice fortification presents a high-impact opportunity to address micronutrient deficiencies at scale.
To support this process, WFP seeks to engage a Programme Policy Officer to be seconded to the Coordinating Ministry of Food to provide embedded technical expertise on rice fortification, food safety and nutrition. The incumbent will play a critical role in facilitating coordination across ministries, advancing pilot implementation, and supporting evidence-based planning for scale-up.

OBJECTIVE OF THE ASSIGNMENT:
To deliver measurable progress toward the operationalization of rice fortification within national programmes, specifically:
1. Development and Government validation of a pilot implementation model for integration of fortified rice into MBG
2. Establishment of a functional inter-ministerial coordination mechanism
3. Provision of evidence-based recommendations to inform Government decision-making and scale-up on rice fortification, food safety and nutrition

SCOPE OF WORK AND KEY RESPONSIBILITIES:

The person will:

A. Technical Leadership & Delivery
· Lead the development of a pilot design package for fortified rice integration into MBG
· Translate policy and technical priorities into actionable implementation pathways
· Provide targeted technical advice to Government counterparts

B. Coordination & Institutional Alignment
· Facilitate structured coordination across relevant ministries and agencies (e.g., BAPPENAS, MoH, MoA, BULOG, NNA, NFA) at the national and subnational levels
· Ensure alignment between policy, technical, and operational inputs

C. Evidence & Decision Support
· Develop decision-support materials (briefs, presentations, recommendations)
· Support Government in identifying operational options and trade-offs
· Consolidate inputs into clear recommendations for leadership decision-making

D. Partnerships & Resource Mobilization
· Mobilize and coordinate technical expertise from WFP, partners, and private sector
· Facilitate technical exchanges and knowledge sharing
· Identify opportunities for scaling and future collaboration

E. Strategic Liaison Function
· Act as a strategic interface between WFP and Government, ensuring:
o Smooth communication between WFP and Government
o Early identification of risks, bottlenecks, and opportunities
Alignment of Government processes with agreed programme objectives

DELIVERABLES:

The person will be accountable for the following:
1. Pilot Design Package
o Government-endorsed pilot for fortified rice under MBG
o Includes targeting, supply chain model, costing, and implementation roadmap
o Submitted for Government validation
2. Inter-Ministerial Coordination Mechanism
o Regular meetings held with documented decisions and follow-up
3. Decision-Support Briefing Pack
o Consolidated evidence and recommendations for Government and WFP

4. Partnerships Mobilized
o Technical contributions from key stakeholders secured and integrated into programme work
5. Final Assignment Report
o Summary of results achieved
o Key bottlenecks and recommendations for scale-up
