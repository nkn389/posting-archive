ORGANIZATIONAL CONTEXT

The mission of the Office of the Inspector General (OIG) is to provide WFP’s stakeholders assurance on the adequacy and effectiveness of WFP’s governance, risk management and control processes aimed at detecting and deterring fraud, waste and abuse through independent and objective oversight activities; to conduct inspections and investigations into allegations of wrongdoing and misconduct; and to facilitate the adoption and implementation of United Nations, humanitarian agency and private-sector best practices related to assurance and oversight.

OIG consists of three functional branches:

- The Office of Internal Audit (OIGA), which provides independent assurance and advisory services on governance, risk management and internal controls.
- The Office of Inspections and Investigations (OIGI), which investigates allegations of misconduct, including fraud, corruption and sexual misconduct by WFP employees and partners and conducts inspections into areas of heightened risk.
- Professional Support Services (PSS), which provides shared analytical, technology, digital forensics, intelligence, administrative and business support services, enabling OIG to operate as an integrated, modern and data-driven oversight function.

This position is located in OIGI’s Intake and Case Development (ICD) unit. ICD is the strategic decision point determining the most appropriate course of action for allegations or concerns reported to OIGI. The Head of Intake serves as the first-level supervisor of the ICD team and reports to the Chief, ICD. Some travel, including to remote field locations, may be required.

THE ROLE

The Head of Intake plays a critical strategic role within OIGI, coordinating the ICD team and overseeing the full assessment workflow – from the receipt and early triage of allegations through case prioritisation, preliminary fact-finding, quality assurance and timely submission for management decision. The incumbent deputises for the Chief, ICD and, in close coordination with the Chief, provides strategic and technical direction on complex, sensitive and high-risk matters; oversees clear, high-quality analysis and recommendations; and ensures outcomes are communicated effectively to relevant stakeholders. The role promotes a timely, consistent and risk-informed intake function, while supporting staff development, sound practice and continuous improvement.

KEY ACCOUNTABILITIES:
Under the supervision of the Chief, ICD, the Head of Intake, P4, will undertake the following tasks:

A. Intake workflow, assessment and case development:

- Lead and oversee the OIGI intake assessment workflow, including the receipt and triage of allegations and complaints, risk-based case prioritisation and adherence to applicable OIGI and WFP frameworks.
- Direct and oversee timely, risk-based and data-informed preliminary fact-finding, ensuring that available information is assessed, information gaps are identified and appropriate avenues for case development, referral or other action are pursued.
- Provide strategic direction on particularly complex, sensitive, urgent or high-risk matters, including timely notification to OIGI management with a clear assessment and proposed course of action.
- Coordinate the escalation of intake matters for management decision and ensure adequate and timely follow-up and communication of outcomes to relevant stakeholders.
B. Written products, quality assurance and records:

- Coordinate the preparation and review of intake assessments, briefing notes, correspondence and other written outputs arising from intake work.
- Establish and maintain effective quality assurance arrangements to ensure that intake outputs are clear, concise, well-reasoned and consistent with applicable standards and practice.
- Ensure that information and evidence are handled confidentially and in accordance with applicable organisational requirements.
- Ensure complete, accurate and confidential records of intake activities, assessment decisions and outcomes.
C. Team leadership, workload management and operational continuity:

- Provide overall management and direction of the intake team, including workload distribution, prioritisation, performance support and day-to-day operational continuity.
- Set clear work priorities and oversee delivery within established timeframes, taking account of risk, urgency, available resources and organisational priorities.
- Provide expert guidance and technical advice to intake staff, promote best practices and foster on-the-job learning through collaborative fact-finding and case development work.
- Support an equitable, inclusive and collaborative workplace, provide timely and constructive feedback, and identify appropriate staff development opportunities.
D. Coordination and stakeholder engagement:

- Establish, maintain and facilitate effective coordination with Global Headquarters, regional offices, country offices, local officials, governments and external organisations to secure timely and appropriate support for intake fact-finding activities.
- Ensure effective collaboration with other OIGI and OIG functions to obtain specialist support, including digital forensics, analysis, audit input and strategic advice.
- Maintain strategic collaboration with internal and external control, monitoring, risk, compliance and oversight functions, as necessary to facilitate effective intake activities.
- Provide expert guidance and advice to WFP employees on complex matters pertaining to allegations of misconduct or wrongdoing.
E. Policy, practice and knowledge sharing:

- Contribute actively to the development and implementation of policies, procedures and practices related to OIGI’s mandate and intake function.
- Promote the sharing of lessons learned and best practices to strengthen the quality, consistency and effectiveness of intake work.
- Identify and lead practical improvements to intake systems, processes and ways of working, including opportunities to strengthen risk-informed decision-making and operational efficiency.
- Leverage technology, including artificial intelligence, to improve intake work; enable staff to use these tools effectively and responsibly; and maintain a digitally capable, technology-savvy intake team.
- Act as Officer-in-Charge, when required, and perform other responsibilities as assigned.
