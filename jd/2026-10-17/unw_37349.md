Background:

UN Women, grounded in the vision of equality enshrined in the Charter of the United Nations, works for the elimination of discrimination against women and girls; the empowerment of women; and the achievement of equality between women and men as partners and beneficiaries of development, human rights, humanitarian action and peace and security.

UN Women continues to advance its digital transformation agenda through the implementation, enhancement and integration of enterprise platforms and digital solutions that support organizational effectiveness, business process simplification, data-driven decision-making, and improved user experience. These initiatives require coordinated project management across business stakeholders, as well as effective governance, planning, risk management, quality assurance, change management, and stakeholder engagement.

PRISM, UN Women’s new corporate platform to support strategic planning, results management, and monitoring and reporting of Strategic Plan, Strategic Notes, workplans and projects, has been successfully deployed during 2025-2026 across the organization, with ensuring its integration with Quantum, UN Women’s ERP system. The priority in 2027 will be to support system stabilization and sustained adoption, deliver prioritized enhancements, optimize existing functionalities and business processes, and strengthen integrations and reporting capabilities.

The Project Manager will be responsible for leading these corporate technology and digital transformation projects in accordance with organizational priorities. The role is therefore not limited to PRISM and is expected to develop strategies, manage project planning, and implement change management initiatives to transform business processes and improve efficiency in the scope of the projects assigned. This role involves managing resources and risks, and ensuring a cohesive, cross-functional approach.

Under the overall guidance of the Digital Governance Board and direct supervision by Chief Information Systems and Telecommunications (IST), the Project Manager will primarily be responsible for the effective management of assigned projects. This includes ensuring that project objectives, scope, timelines, resources, budgets, risks, dependencies, and governance arrangements are effectively managed and that decisions and escalations are brought to the appropriate governance bodies in a timely manner.

The Project Manager will work in close consultation and collaboration with a wide range of clients and stakeholders across the UN Women, such as key Business Process Owners (including Strategic Planning Unit and Programme Support Management Unit in SPRED), Information Technology teams, key end-users in the country, regional and corporate levels, as well as external partners, as applicable. The goal is to promote critical thinking and innovation to design simplified and streamlined processes that will support the delivery of practical, sustainable, and user-centered solutions and will enhance organizational performance and efficiency.

Note: Prior to starting their assignment, home based international PPSA are responsible for holding a work permit valid for the homebased location during the entire IPPSA duration.

Scope of Work

1. Lead the strategic direction and management of assigned digital transformation projects, including PRISM

• Provide strategic vision and project management leadership for the assigned projects in collaboration with the Chief, IST, relevant Business Process Owner, and Digital Governance Board.

• Coordinate the technical implementation of PRISM enhancements and stabilization roadmap during 2027, supporting prioritization of improvements, resolution of post-go-live issues, platform optimization, and adoption in collaboration with the Chief, IST and Chief, SPU.

• Act as a trusted and neutral project manager across all business processes and workstreams; oversee and facilitate process improvement discussions between business owners, build project momentum, and manage escalations to the Senior leadership and governance bodies;

• Engage and coordinate business owners and focal points across the Organization globally and provide them with updates on the project;

• Ensure that project objectives and proposed solutions remain aligned with Organization’s goals, priorities, policies and capitalize on UN System and industry best practices;

• Provide high-level analysis and advice on project options, priorities, trade-offs, implementation approaches, risks, dependencies, and resource implications to support timely decision-making.

• Monitor, track, and control technical outcomes to resolve issues, conflicts, dependencies, and critical path deliverables.

2. Plan and coordinate project delivery across business and technical teams

• Develop, maintain and monitor integrated project plans, including scope, deliverables, timelines, milestones, and resource allocation.

• Coordinate with various departments to ensure that all aspects of the project are aligned and progressing as planned.

• Monitor project delivery, identify variances and constraints, and coordinate corrective actions and recovery plans where required.

• Provide input to and review implementation plans, component plans, contingency plans, milestones, dependencies etc. attached to the project’s high-level deliverables;

• Provide input to and review project budgets to ensure they are based on outputs and deliverables and clearly linked to milestones and project phases.

3. Engage and manage stakeholders at all levels

• Engage with stakeholders at all levels, including senior management, business process owners, technical teams, regional and country offices, end-users, vendors, and external partners.

• Provide regular progress reports to Digital Governance Board and other stakeholders as needed

• Ensure that stakeholders are appropriately informed, involved, consulted, and supportive of the project's goals and progress.

• Coordinate cross-functional discussions to resolve competing priorities, clarify ownership, and facilitate timely decisions.

4. Manage Project Scope, Changes, Risks and Dependencies

• Establish and maintain effective mechanisms for managing project scope, requirements, changes, risks, issues, assumptions,and dependencies.

• Ensure proposed changes are assessed for business value, cost, schedule, and dependency impacts and are submitted to the appropriate governance body for decision.

• Identify potential risks and develop mitigation strategies related to the implementation of the projects.

• Monitor risks throughout the project lifecycle and take proactive measures to address any issues that arise.

• Monitor and report on effectiveness of project risk framework, related controls, and indicators to the Digital Governance Board.

• Identify and manage dependencies across business streams and other corporate initiatives.

5. Plan and Monitor Project Resources and Budgets

• Support the development and monitoring of project budgets, ensuring costs are linked to approved scope, outputs, milestones, and project phases.

• Manage these resources efficiently to avoid delays and cost overruns.

• Plan and coordinate the efficient and effective deployment of human and financial resources required for project delivery including necessary recruitment and procurement of additional resources.

• Monitor resource constraints and capacity risks and recommend to governance bodies on resource allocation and priorities.

• Identify opportunities for efficiency and cost optimization wherever possible.

6. Ensure Quality Assurance and Testing of Project Deliverables

• Ensure appropriate quality assurance processes are integrated into project deliverables to meet the required standards and specifications.

• Coordinate with business and technical teams on requirements validation, UAT, defect resolution, sign-off and release readiness, as applicable.

• Monitor critical defects and issues through resolution, including post-go-live stabilization activities.

7. Coordinate Project Communication and Change Management

• Develop and coordinate regular communication with the project team, Digital Governance Board, and stakeholders during project implementation.

• Provide updates on project status, progress, and any issues that need attention.

• Support change management activities required to facilitate adoption of new or enhanced systems, business processes, and ways of working.

• Establish and coordinate necessary collaboration with other UN System Organizations and UN Women partners.

8. Coordinate Knowledge Management and End-User Training and Support

• Lead and coordinate the organization and facilitation of workshops and training to ensure ongoing business transformation, process changes, and system evolution is understood and appropriately used by stakeholders

• Ensure that end-users are trained and supported during the rollout of the new system.

• Coordinate training sessions and provide support to ensure a smooth transition.

• Coordinate the maintenance of a dedicated site of UN Women Intranet to ensure all the relevant documents and training materials are available and accessible to all UN Women personnel in user-friendly manner.

• Promote reuse of lessons learned, templates and project management practices across future initiatives.

9. Coordinate Project Closure, Transition and Continuous Improvement

• Coordinate PRISM closure, including confirmation of deliverables, outstanding actions, financial status, documentation, lessons learned and transition arrangements.

• Support post-implementation reviews and audits

• Identify opportunities and lessons learned to strengthen project management governance and practices within IST and across UN Women.
