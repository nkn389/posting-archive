II. Consultancy Assignment

Background/Context

UN Women promotes integrated approaches to ensure that all women and girls live a life free from all forms of violence. Gender-based violence (GBV) exists in various settings, including domestic, public, private, online, and workplaces and affects all genders, women and marginalised genders bear a disproportionate burden of its impact. Violence against women (VAW) is a grave violation of human rights with far-reaching consequences for women, their families and communities worldwide. It has tremendous costs from greater strains on health care to legal expenses and losses in productivity. The violence cuts across boundaries of age, class, caste, sexuality, sexual expression, education, marital status, race, culture, and geography, among others.

UN Women in India has played a pivotal role in supporting governments in eliminating violence and discrimination against women by demonstrating models of that work to prevent and respond to violence. Efforts towards promoting gender equality are multiplied through partnerships with governments, women’s organisations, civil society, academia, private sector, and the larger UN ecosystem. Initiatives range from strengthening legal frameworks and specific national and sub-national actions, including enhancing service provisioning for survivors of violence, and supporting prevention at the grassroots level. UN Women India supports strengthening data collection and knowledge generation on VAW, facilitating new learning on the issue. Through advocacy and communication campaigns, UN Women India works to transform gender norms, behaviours and attitudes that perpetuate gender inequality.

UN Women’s Safe Cities and Safe Public Spaces approach addresses sexual harassment and other forms of violence against women and girls in public spaces through locally owned, evidence-based and multisectoral action. In Madhya Pradesh, UN Women works with state and city institutions to strengthen gender-responsive urban governance by linking women’s lived experiences and safety evidence with coordinated planning, budgeting, infrastructure and service improvements, institutional capacity, community participation, and stronger prevention and response systems. Within this broader state-level focus, Jabalpur provides an important city-level platform to translate these principles into sustained institutional practice and a model that can inform learning and replication across Madhya Pradesh.

Jabalpur launched its Safe Cities and Safe Public Spaces initiative in July 2025, building on the Bhopal Declaration and city leadership. The first year established the foundation for city-level action through institutional coordination, safety assessments, public-space improvements, capacity development, community engagement and public awareness.

The next phase will focus on consolidation and institutionalization rather than expansion of standalone activities. This includes strengthening monitoring, evaluation and learning systems; embedding gender-responsive approaches within the routine functioning of Jabalpur Municipal Corporation, Police, Women and Child Development, Transport, Railways, Education, Tourism, Health and allied departments; and ensuring that women’s safety, accessibility and inclusion are reflected in planning, budgeting, maintenance, service delivery and review mechanisms.

By the end of the assignment, the consultant is expected to help UN Women and city partners achieve a clearer institutional model for safe and enabling public spaces in Jabalpur: validated evidence and trackers in use; costed and owned departmental action plans; visible and monitored improvements in priority locations; strengthened referral and response linkages; and practical documentation that supports sustainability and replication in other cities of Madhya Pradesh.

Within this context, UN Women seeks a Gender Coordinator to support the State Lead and Policy Specialist, Madhya Pradesh, in providing city-levelclose coordination, technical assistance, monitoring with the State Lead on, documentation and learning support for the Safe Cities and Safe Public Spaces initiative in Jabalpur. The role will prioritize consolidation of existing work, institutionalization of gender-responsive approaches across relevant departments, and evidence-based planning for sustained improvements in public spaces.

2. Roles and Responsibilities

Under the direct supervision of the State Lead and Policy Specialist, Madhya Pradesh, with technical guidance from UN Women’s EVAW team, the consultant will support implementation of a focused annual workplan for Jabalpur. The role will emphasize coordination, evidence consolidation, Monitoring, Evaluation, Accountability and Learning (MEAL), institutionalization, documentation and follow-up on agreed city priorities, aligned with UN Women’s safe cities and safe public spaces framework. The consultant will work in close coordination with designated government nodal officers and relevant departments.

3. Priority Areas of Work

3.1 Institutional Coordination and Gender-Responsive Governance

• A functional city coordination mechanism operates with clear roles for key government departments, civil society, academia and community institutions.

• City priorities, departmental commitments, bottlenecks and follow-up actions are tracked and reviewed.

• Women’s safety, accessibility and inclusion are integrated into departmental plans, budgets, inspections, services and maintenance systems.

• Departmental focal points, ownership arrangements and review mechanisms support sustainability.

3.2 Safety Evidence, Planning and Follow-up

• Safety audits, mobility mapping, assessments and consultations are consolidated into one evidence repository.

• City priorities are validated with women and girls, ward representatives, service providers and marginalized groups.

• Validated findings inform prioritized, costed and owned departmental and ward-level action plans.

• Corrective actions are reassessed through user feedback and functionality checks.

3.3 Gender-Responsive Infrastructure and Services

• Public-space improvements reflect gender and inclusion inputs on lighting, sanitation, signage, accessibility, transport, CCTV and reporting information.

• Improvements are field-veri fied for functionality, maintenance gaps, user feedback and departmental follow-up.

• A Safe and Enabling Public Spaces checklist supports routine inspections and follow-up.

• Strengthening prevention and response mechanisms with focus on women help desks at police stations, Gender resource centers and One Stop Centres.

3.4 Capacity Development and Institutional Practice

• A needs-based capacity-development plan covers municipal staff, police, transport, railways, educators, ward representatives and service providers.

• Priority stakeholders strengthen practice on gender-responsive planning, harassment prevention, survivor-centred response, accessibility and accountability.

• Training results are captured through participation data, pre/post assessment, feedback and application evidence.

• Departmental focal persons or trainers support continuity beyond one-off sessions.

3.5 Community Participation and Social Norm Change

• Women’s groups, youth, vendors, transport associations, men and boys, civil society and community institutions participate through structured platforms.

• Safe feedback channels enable women and girls to share concerns without exposing personal or sensitive information.

• Bilingual materials communicate respectful public-space norms, services, reporting pathways and shared responsibility.

• Community feedback informs departmental action and is communicated back accessibly.

3.6 Monitoring, Documentation and Learning

• Prepare a results and monitoring framework in consultation with UN Women’s MEL specialist, EVAW team and State Lead, with sex-, age- and disability-disaggregated indicators wherever feasible and appropriate.

• Maintain trackers for audit recommendations, infrastructure and service actions, capacity development, community engagement, referrals and institutional commitments.

• Prepare monthly progress updates, quarterly analytical reports, briefing notes, presentations, case studies, human-interest stories and inputs to institutional and donor reports.

• Document implementation challenges, adaptations, good practices, costs and enabling factors, and prepare an operational Jabalpur model or guidance note for replication and inter-city learning.

• Ensure informed consent, confidentiality, safeguarding, ethical documentation and responsible data management across all activities.
