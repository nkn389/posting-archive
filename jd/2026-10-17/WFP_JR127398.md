BACKGROUND AND PURPOSE OF THE ASSIGNMENT:
What will you do in this position?
The World Food Programme (WFP) is the United Nations frontline humanitarian agency addressing hunger and malnutrition worldwide. Due to its expertise in the field of humanitarian logistics, WFP was chosen to be the lead agency for the Logistics Cluster.

Comprised of a diverse, multi-skilled group of logisticians, the Logistics Cluster, led by WFP, is mandated by the Inter-Agency Standing Committee (IASC) to complement and co-ordinate the logistics capabilities of co-operating humanitarian agencies during large-scale complex emergencies and natural disasters.
In response to the request made by the Palestine Humanitarian Country Team, the Palestine Logistics Cluster was officially activated on 16 October 2023.
The escalation of the conflict which began on 7 October in Gaza, resulted in the disruption of electricity, food, water, and fuel supplies, as well as restricted access to and movement within Gaza. This has caused a significant worsening of the humanitarian crisis and a large-scale displacement of the population within Gaza. Due to the magnitude of the humanitarian crisis, the significant impact on the supply chain and the access challenges to and inside Gaza, humanitarian organizations face significant logistics challenges for the delivery of relief supplies and life-saving cargo. Logistics coordination cells, part of the Palestine Logistics Cluster have also been set up in Cairo and Amman to support the Gaza response.

Why work with WFP Palestine?
For decades, Palestine has been facing a protracted and complex protection and humanitarian crisis. The humanitarian situation worsened alarmingly after an upsurge in violence from October 2023.
WFP, through our dual mandate in humanitarian and development, provides life-saving food assistance to the most vulnerable and food insecure non-refugee Palestinians.
WFP provides logistics support to humanitarian and development partners, allowing them to reach more people faster, and at a lower cost to donors and the environment. By offering our cash-based transfer platform to the wider humanitarian and development community, coordinating logistics, and developing an inter-agency common feedback mechanism, WFP contributes to wider humanitarian efforts.

How can you make a difference?
The position is based in East Jerusalem, and reports to the Palestine Logistics Cluster Coordinator. The Operations Coordination and Planning Officer works closely with the Gaza team, Logistics Cluster teams across the different corridors (Egypt, Jordan, Ashdod, West Bank) and closely coordinates with partners, to design and support daily planning and coordination of Logistics Cluster common logistics services. This position aims to provide additional expert capacity to:

- Lead a "Logistics Operations Coordination and Planning" role to consolidate humanitarian truck movement coordination to and in Gaza.
- Facilitate discussion and agreement on the use of common standards and tools among humanitarian organizations and UN agencies; and promote awareness of and adherence to relevant policy guidelines, codes of conduct and examples of good practice.
- Provide Technical Guidance on logistics best practices and operational improvements to partners
1. Logistics Coordination System Setup

- Establish an Integrated Logistics Operations Coordination System: develop and manage a centralised system to consolidate partners’ truck movement requests across humanitarian corridors.
- Design Efficient Routing Strategies: identify and prioritize optimal convoy routes based on commodity types and urgency levels.
- Implement Real-Time Tracking and Monitoring: deploy tools to provide accurate, live updates on truck movements for stakeholders, including partners, humanitarian organisations, and authorities.
- On-Site Oversight: Supervise and oversee operations at transshipment platforms (if granted authorization), ensuring system efficiency and seamless coordination.
2. Cross-Functional Collaboration with Humanitarian Military Interaction & Access (HMIA) and Supply Chain

- Coordinate Across Teams: act as the primary liaison between the Logistics Cluster, HMIA, Supply Chain teams, and partners to ensure alignment with operational objectives.
- Lead Joint Planning Sessions: organise regular meetings to address priorities, share challenges, and refine logistics strategies.
3. Technical Expertise and Oversight

- Provide Technical Guidance: offer expert advice on logistics best practices and operational improvements.
- Lead Recruitment and Onboarding: identify staffing gaps and lead the recruitment and the onboarding of new personnel to ensure smooth system setup and continuity.
- Evaluate System Efficiency: monitor logistics processes, identify bottlenecks, and propose solutions for optimisation.
4. Partner Engagement and Reporting

- Maintain Partner Coordination: build and sustain relationships with local and international partners, keeping them informed about logistics updates and plans.
- Ensure Accurate Data Tracking: maintain high-quality records and real-time data to support informed decision-making.
- Document Lessons Learned: compile insights from operations for future reference and continuous improvement.
