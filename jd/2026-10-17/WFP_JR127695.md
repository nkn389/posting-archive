ORGANIZATIONAL CONTEXT:

The Aviation Quality Assurance Officer is based in Rome, Italy, and reports to the Head of Aviation Quality Assurance within the Aviation Quality Assurance Unit (QAU). The position contributes to the implementation and continuous improvement of quality assurance, compliance monitoring, and environmental sustainability activities across WFP Aviation operations.
The role supports the application of aviation quality standards, regulatory requirements, and organizational policies throughout Headquarters, Regional and Country Offices. Working closely with operational and technical stakeholders, the incumbent assists in monitoring compliance, analyzing performance information, identifying improvement opportunities, and promoting consistency in aviation governance and assurance processes.
The position also supports the implementation of WFP Aviation's Environmental and Sustainability Programme by contributing to initiatives that strengthen environmental performance and ensure alignment with relevant international standards and organizational objectives.

JOB PURPOSE:

To support the implementation and continuous improvement of WFP Aviation's quality assurance, compliance monitoring, and environmental sustainability programme, contributing to safe, efficient, accountable, and compliant aviation operations worldwide.
The position provides technical support in the monitoring of aviation quality standards, regulatory compliance, operational performance, and continuous improvement initiatives across WFP Aviation. It also serves as a focal point for environmental sustainability activities within the Aviation Service, supporting the implementation of environmental management initiatives, performance monitoring, reporting, and the integration of sustainability principles into aviation processes and practices.
Through these efforts, the role contributes to strengthening organizational effectiveness, environmental responsibility, and operational excellence across WFP Aviation operations.

ACCOUNTABILITIES:

- Support the implementation and continuous improvement of WFP Aviation's Quality Assurance Framework, contributing to the consistent application of aviation standards, compliance requirements, and assurance processes across Headquarters, Regional, and Country Offices.

- Conduct and coordinate quality assurance and compliance monitoring activities, including the analysis of audits, assessments, inspections, investigations, and compliance reviews; monitor the implementation of corrective and preventive actions and support continuous improvement initiatives across aviation operations.

- Contribute to the development, review, harmonization, and maintenance of aviation policies, procedures, standards, guidance materials, and quality assurance documentation, ensuring alignment with WFP requirements, ICAO Standards and Recommended Practices (SARPs), applicable regulations, and industry best practices.

- Analyze operational, safety, compliance, quality, and environmental performance data to identify trends, emerging risks, systemic issues, and opportunities for improvement, and provide recommendations, reports, and management information to support evidence-based decision making.

- Serve as a technical focal point for the implementation and continuous improvement of WFP Aviation's Environmental Management Programme, supporting the integration of environmental sustainability principles into aviation activities, monitoring environmental performance indicators, and contributing to the achievement of organizational environmental objectives.

- Support the development and maintenance of environmental sustainability initiatives, tools, guidance materials, reporting mechanisms, awareness activities, and performance monitoring processes, promoting compliance with relevant environmental standards and the adoption of industry best practices across WFP Aviation operations.

- Provide technical support and guidance to Headquarters, Regional, and Country Offices on quality assurance, compliance monitoring, environmental management, and continuous improvement practices, promoting consistency, standardization, and knowledge sharing across the organization.

- Maintain effective collaboration with internal and external stakeholders and contribute to reporting, training, knowledge management, and programme support activities related to aviation quality assurance, compliance, and environmental sustainability, and perform other related duties as required.

MINIMUM QUALIFICATIONS AND EXPERIENCE:

Education: Advanced University Degree in Aviation Management, Aeronautical Engineering, Aviation Safety, Air Transport Management, Logistics, or a related field.
Alternatively, a First University Degree combined with additional years of relevant professional experience and recognized aviation qualifications, such as an Airline Transport Pilot License (ATPL), Commercial Pilot License (CPL), Air Traffic Control License, Flight Operations Officer/Flight Dispatcher License, Aeronautical Engineering qualification, Remote Pilot License, or equivalent professional aviation certification.

Experience: A minimum of five (5) years of progressively responsible professional experience in aviation operations, aviation safety, aviation quality assurance, compliance monitoring, environmental management, operational support, or other related aviation functions.

Experience in one or more of the following areas is desirable:

- Aviation quality assurance, compliance monitoring, audits, inspections, assessments, investigations, or safety oversight activities.

- Supporting the implementation and continuous improvement of aviation procedures, standards, guidance materials, management systems, or operational frameworks.

- Monitoring operational, safety, quality, compliance, or environmental performance and preparing analyses and reports to support decision making.

- Supporting environmental sustainability initiatives, environmental management systems, performance monitoring programmes, or related compliance activities within aviation or transport operations.

- Working in humanitarian, international, regulatory, commercial aviation, or multi-country operating environments.

- Coordinating and collaborating with aviation operators, Civil Aviation Authorities, UN agencies, governmental organizations, or other aviation stakeholders.

- Supporting the delivery of training, awareness, knowledge-sharing, or capacity-strengthening initiatives related to aviation quality assurance, compliance, safety, or environmental management.

Knowledge & Skills:

- Good knowledge of ICAO Standards and Recommended Practices (SARPs), aviation regulatory frameworks, quality assurance principles and compliance monitoring methodologies.

- Knowledge of aviation safety management, operational risk management and continuous improvement practices.

- Understanding of humanitarian aviation operations and aviation governance frameworks.

- Familiarity with environmental sustainability principles and their application within aviation operations.

- Ability to analyze operational and performance information and prepare clear technical recommendations and reports.

- Ability to support audits, assessments, inspections and follow up activities.

- Strong analytical, organizational and problem-solving skills.

- Good communication and stakeholder engagement skills, including the ability to communicate technical information clearly to different audiences.

- Ability to work collaboratively in multicultural and multidisciplinary environments.

- Proficiency in Microsoft Office applications and data analysis/reporting tools.

Language: Full proficiency (level C) in English. Knowledge of other UN languages
would be desirable.
