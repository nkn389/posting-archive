PROJECT BACKGROUND
GGGI works in partnership with countries around the world to strengthen their capacity and advance green growth policies and investments that can positively impact the lives of millions of people. The organization collaborates with national governments, multilateral institutions, public agencies, and the private sector to support the development of economies that are robust, resource-efficient, low-carbon, and resilient to climate change.

GGGI implements a diverse portfolio of programs across developing countries. Through these in-country initiatives, complemented by global products and services, the organization delivers results through an integrated approach that combines evidence-based policy planning, technical assistance, and investment facilitation, aligned with national development priorities. GGGI also promotes knowledge generation and management activities that strengthen the theoretical and empirical foundations of green growth, provide practical guidance for policymakers, and help create enabling conditions for both public and private green investments.

In Colombia, GGGI has been working closely with the national government since 2014 to advance green growth policies and investments. The organization has established strategic partnerships with institutions such as the Ministry of Environment and Sustainable Development, the Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Development, the Ministry of Finance and Public Credit, and the National Planning Department (DNP), among others.

Within the framework of the UK PACT (Partnering for Accelerated Climate Transitions) Bio Rural Project (2024–2027), the Global Green Growth Institute (GGGI), the Government of Antioquia, and the Caja de Compensación Familiar de Antioquia – COMFAMA signed a Special Cooperation Agreement to implement the initiative "Antioquia BioCompetitiva." The project seeks to accelerate the scaling of innovative bioeconomy solutions in the Department of Antioquia through two strategic components: (I) promoting knowledge transfer and capacity-building processes, with a focus on circular bioeconomy and the internationalization of bioproducts; and (II) strengthening productive, technological, and commercial capacities within the department through an integrated value-chain development approach.

Under the second component, three calls for proposals will be implemented to accelerate the development of the bioeconomy ecosystem while strengthening strategic value chains in the department. These calls include:

-
Bioeconomy acceleration support for microenterprises affiliated with COMFAMA.

-
Investment scaling support for four companies linked to bioeconomy value chains.

OBJECTIVES OF THE ASSIGNMENT
The objective of this consultancy is to implement of an integrated B2B/B2C marketing strengthening and commercial acceleration plan for Biotropical's BioTrichum product under guidance of the GGGI Colombia team — encompassing the implementation of the commercial and digital diagnostic, a high-conversion B2B landing page, an AI-assisted multimedia content kit, and the alignment of digital advertising campaigns (Meta Ads Manager and Google Ads) — overseeing the quality and consistency of each component, incorporating the technical inputs provided by Biotropical, and transferring the resulting digital assets, tools, and knowledge so that Biotropical can manage its digital commercial channels autonomously and streamline the methodology across other products in its portfolio.

DELIVERABLES AND PAYMENT SCHEDULE
Under the overall supervision of the GGGI Colombia team, and in direct coordination with the beneficiary company, Biotropical, the Consultant will provide marketing, communications, and support, consolidated for monitoring and payment purposes into three (3) deliverable packages. The assignment includes, the following activities:

1. Work plan and methodology: A detailed work plan incorporating the consultancy methodology, implementation schedule, information requirements, and data collection process. This deliverable must include the basic implementation plan for a B2B/B2C digital acquisition campaign ecosystem and commercial acceleration.

2. B2B/B2C digital acquisition campaign ecosystem and commercial acceleration

-
Basic B2B/B2C marketing and commercial acceleration plan: review of Biotropical's current commercial and digital situation; alignment of B2B and B2C segments with commercial potential and their target audiences; review of commercial opportunities and potential markets; optimization of acquisition, follow-up, and conversion routes and of priority digital channels; supporting the execution of demand-generation and opportunity-recovery actions, alongside repurchase, cross-selling, and client-development strategies by deploying automated triggers and workflows; and assisting in the operational deployment of a commercial acceleration process for BioTrichum, articulated through the synchronized rollout of the landing page, content, campaign, and WhatsApp components described below.

-
High-conversion B2B landing page: support the design, development and implementation of a responsive B2B and technical landing page for BioTrichum (optimized for desktop, tablet, and mobile devices), including an integrated dosage calculator, a technical data-sheet download section, a sample-request form, a WhatsApp contact button, links to social media channels, strategically placed calls to action, connection to Biotropical's existing website, and basic tracking alignment.

-
AI-Assisted multimedia content kit: assisting in thedevelopment and editing of three (3) technical videos supported by artificial-intelligence tools, one (1) brand-voice/sound piece of up to 30 seconds, and ten (10) promotional images, each aligned in coordination with Biotropical regarding objective, audience, context, copy, and required scenes and images, and adapted to the 1:1, 4:5, and 9:16 formats.

-
Digital campaign alignment and knowledge transfer: Reviewing and configuring of Biotropical's advertising accounts and assets in Meta Ads Manager and Google Ads, prioritizing the B2B market; initial management of campaigns and audiences and basic tracking alignment; and knowledge transfer to the Biotropical team through a practical explanation of the processes used, hands-on training on campaign administration and coordination on the interpretation of results, in addition to three (3) recorded training sessions for future reference.

-
B2B Digital acquisition campaign: management, alignment, and preparation for launch of a Meta Ads commercial campaign using the assets developed under this consultancy, including audience and segmentation alignment, distribution of the advertising budget, use of the developed content, implementation of calls to action, campaign tracking, and routing toward the BioTrichum landing page and WhatsApp; and hands-on training for the Biotropical team on the process and set-up of the campaign.

3. Final report: The final deliverable shall include

a) High-Conversion B2B landing page: BioTrichum landing page implemented, integrated with existing commercial channels, and ready for operation;

b) AI-Assisted multimedia content kit: technical videos, brand voice/sound piece, and promotional concepts delivered;

c) Digital campaign alignment and knowledge transfer;

d) One (1) integrated Meta & Google Ads B2B commercial campaign configured and ready for activation, subject to Biotropical’s approval.;

e) Consolidation of digital assets, documentation, and training recordings; final knowledge transfer and closure of the consultancy.
