Applications are welcome from internal and external candidates. For all IOM vacancies, applications from qualified and eligible first-tier candidates are considered before those of qualified and eligible second-tier candidates in the selection process. For the purpose of this vacancy, internal candidates are considered first-tier candidates.

Under the direct supervision of the Resource Management Officer and the overall supervision of the Head of Office in IOM Bulgaria, the successful candidate will be accountable for the financial and administrative implementation of IOM Sofia programmes.

This includes but is not limited to the financial and administrative services for the successful mastering of all relevant rules, guidelines, processes and procedures in the implementation of AMIF projects, funded by the Bulgarian Shared Management Funds.

Please be advised that this is a local position and as such only qualified candidates with suitable residence/ working permit in Bulgaria will be considered.

Education

- High School diploma with seven years of relevant experience; or,
- Bachelor’s degree in Business Administration, Accounting, Finances, or related field with five years of relevant professional experience.

Accredited Universities are those listed in the UNESCO World Higher Education Database.

Experience

- Fluency in Bulgarian (oral and written) is mandatory and essential for coordinating with different stakeholders.
- Knowledge of Bulgarian tax and accounting legislation
- Experience with the National Revenue Agency (NRA), banking institutions, and other Bulgarian state institutions.
- Experience with ERP systems (e.g., SAP, Oracle) and proficiency in MS Excel.
- Prior experience working on EU-funded programs/projects, including those financed through national programs.
- Proficient in Microsoft Office applications.
- Prior work experience with international humanitarian organizations, non-governmental organizations, or Bulgarian government institutions in a multicultural setting is an advantage.
- Attention to detail, ability to organize paperwork in a methodical way;
- Discreet, details and clients-oriented, patient and willingness to learn new things;

Skills

- Knowledge of financial rules and regulations.
- Knowledge of International Public Sector Accounting Standards (IPSAS)

Languages

- Fluency in Bulgarian and English (oral and written).

IOM’s official languages are English, French and Spanish.
