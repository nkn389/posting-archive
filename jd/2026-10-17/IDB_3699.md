About this position

Your responsibilities will span the full software development lifecycle including application design frontend and backend development database integration API development testing deployment and ongoing maintenance You will develop interactive dashboards and web based interfaces connect applications to internal and external data sources and integrate analytical models and AI capabilities into automated workflows You will also troubleshoot technical issues optimize application performance and maintain technical documentation to ensure that the tools are reliable maintainable and responsive to users needs Additionally you will contribute to the development and optimization of automated data pipelines AI enabled workflows and user friendly dashboards and web based applications that translate complex findings into clear actionable insights

The role requires strong software engineering skills and hands on experience with frontend technologies such as JavaScript or TypeScript HTML CSS and React backend development using Python or comparable technologies and database management using SQL Experience with version control automated testing application deployment and the integration of AI models or services is also required alongside an understanding of application security and user centered design Your work will help transform the teams analytical capabilities into accessible dependable applications that support timely well informed decision making within the Office of Risk Management

You will work in Systemic Risk Management SRM part of Office of Risk Management RMG This team is responsible for is responsible for identifying monitoring assessing and communicating financial and non financial systemic risks that may affect the IDBs financial standing development strategy and operations SRM plays an active role in safeguarding the IDBs development delivery and financial strength by providing early warnings supporting contingency planning and informing management actions SRM also leads RMGs risk management research agenda and works with internal and external stakeholders to design risk frameworks that are anticipatory actionable and decision useful In addition SRM leads efforts to digitize and automate risk related processes across RMG

What you’ll do

- Work closely with systemic risk analysts, researchers, and end users to translate business requirements, analytical models, and research prototypes into technical specifications and production-ready applications.

- Design, develop, and maintain full-stack web applications and interactive dashboards using React, JavaScript or TypeScript, and Python, combining user-friendly interfaces with reliable back-end services to support systemic risk monitoring and senior management decision-making.

- Develop and maintain application programming interfaces (APIs) and automated integration workflows to connect applications with internal and external data sources, databases, and analytical platforms.

- Integrate analytical models and AI capabilities into applications and automated workflows, including tools for news processing, systemic risk monitoring, and scenario analysis, in collaboration with the team’s analysts and researchers.

- Design, implement, and optimize databases, data schemas, and storage solutions to support application functionality and the efficient handling of structured and unstructured information.

- Develop and maintain automated unit, integration, and end-to-end tests to verify application functionality, data handling, and reliability, and identify and resolve software defects.

- Use version control, code reviews, and automated build and deployment workflows to support consistent software delivery, coordinating application deployment and maintenance with relevant IDB technology teams.

- Monitor application performance, troubleshoot technical issues, and implement enhancements to improve reliability, scalability, usability, and accessibility in response to user feedback and evolving requirements.

- Prepare and maintain technical documentation covering application architecture, source code, APIs, database structures, and deployment procedures, alongside user guides and knowledge-transfer materials.

- Implement appropriate authentication, access controls, and secure data-handling practices in accordance with RMG’s data governance policies and applicable IDB technology, security, and confidentiality requirements.

What you'll need

Education: A master’s degree or Ph.D. in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Systems Engineering, Information Technology, or a related technical field from an accredited university.

Experience: A minimum of 3 years of relevant professional experience in software engineering, full-stack development, or related roles. Demonstrated experience designing, developing, deploying, and maintaining web-based applications and interactive dashboards, including front-end interfaces, back-end services, databases, and APIs. The role requires practical experience integrating AI models or services into software applications and automated workflows, as well as translating business requirements and analytical prototypes into user-friendly decision-support tools for senior management. Candidates should have experience collaborating with analysts, researchers, and end users throughout the software development lifecycle, including requirements gathering, testing, deployment, maintenance, and technical documentation. Strong proficiency in front-end development using JavaScript or TypeScript, HTML, CSS, and React or comparable technologies, together with back-end development skills in Python or a comparable language. Proficiency in SQL, database design, and API development and integration is required, along with experience using version control systems such as Git, conducting code reviews, implementing automated tests, and managing build and deployment workflows. Candidates should also have a working understanding of application security, authentication, access controls, performance optimization, and accessible, user-centered design.

Languages: Proficiency in English.
