Org. Setting and Reporting
This position is located in the Cybercrime Convention and Intergovernmental Services Team, Cybercrime and Technology Section (CTS), Organized Crime and Illicit Trafficking Branch (OCB), Division for Treaty Affairs (DTA), of the United Nations Office on Drugs and Crime (UNODC). The incumbent will report to the Crime Prevention and Criminal Justice Officer responsible for supporting the Secretariat of the Ad Hoc Committee to Elaborate a Comprehensive International Convention on Countering the Use of Information and Communications Technologies for Criminal Purposes (AHC) and the future Conference of the States Parties (CoSP), and under the overall supervision of the Chief of Section. For more information on UNODC, please visit the following website: www.unodc.org

Responsibilities
Within assigned authority, the incumbent will carry out a range of functions related to the advancement and implementation of the UN Convention against Cybercrime. In particular, the incumbent will perform the following specific duties: • Contribute with substantive and technical support to the AHC and future CoSP for the UN Convention against Cybercrime and any related body; • Contribute to the servicing of other formal or informal meetings, as required. • Draft pre-, in- and post session documents for the meetings of the AHC and the CoSP, as applicable, and contribute to substantive servicing of formal or informal meetings; • Prepare substantive inputs for the maintenance of various databases and directories of competent authorities, including collecting relevant laws, regulations and policy documents from States parties, as appropriate; • Draft a variety of written outputs, such as background papers, correspondence with governments, working papers and presentations in support of activities related to the entry into force of the Convention, as well as future work of the CoSP; • Provide substantive inputs on matters related to the ratification and implementation of the Convention, including on legislative and policy matters, assist in the review of relevant legal documents, instruments, or other material; • Compile background information and documentation, including on legislative and policy developments, on the implementation of the Convention and of resolutions and decisions of the CoSP; • Contribute with the drafting of the Travaux Préparatoires and legislative guide and other relevant tools on the implementation of the Convention; • Undertake legal analysis and research, including on evolving legal issues on the use of information and communication technologies for committing crimes to ensure that the AHC and CoSP are kept abreast of such developments; • Collect and analyze data to identify trends or patterns and provide draft insights through graphs, charts, tables and reports using data visualization methods for data-driven planning, decision-making, presentation and reporting; • Provide, as required, guidance to consultants and other staff members supporting implementation of the Convention and its processes; • Perform any other work-related duties as may be required.

Competencies
Professionalism: Has substantive knowledge and understanding of international law, policies, legislative measures and practices in crime prevention and criminal justice, in particular preventing and combatting transnational organized crime or cybercrime, as well as the mandates of the United Nations Office on Drugs and Crime; has knowledge of the work of intergovernmental bodies, processes, procedures and rules; is able to identify and analyse a diverse range of required substantive and procedural issues and challenges and recommend innovative and creative solutions; is able to expeditiously prepare comprehensive analysis, reports and summaries; has excellent writing skills; is able to analyze and interpret data in support of decision-making and convey resulting information to management; shows pride in work and in achievements; demonstrates professional competence and mastery of subject matter; is conscientious and efficient in meeting commitments, observing deadlines and achieving results; is motivated by professional rather than personal concerns; shows persistence when faced with difficult problems or challenges; remains calm in stressful situations. Takes responsibility for incorporating gender perspectives and ensuring the equal participation of women and men in all areas of work. Communication: Speaks and writes clearly and effectively; listens to others, correctly interprets messages from others and responds appropriately; asks questions to clarify, and exhibits interest in having two-way communication; tailors language, tone, style and format to match audience; demonstrates openness in sharing information and keeping people informed. Teamwork: Works collaboratively with colleagues to achieve organizational goals; solicits input by genuinely valuing others’ ideas and expertise; is willing to learn from others; places team agenda before personal agenda; supports and acts in accordance with final group decision, even when such decisions may not entirely reflect own position; shares credit for team accomplishments and accepts joint responsibility for team shortcomings.

Education
An advanced university degree (Master’s degree or equivalent) in law, political science, international relations, criminal justice, criminology, or related discipline is required. A first-level university degree in similar fields in combination with two additional years of qualifying experience may be accepted in lieu of the advanced university degree.

Job - Specific Qualification
Not available.

Work Experience
A minimum of five years of progressively responsible work experience in criminal justice matters, including on organized crime, cybercrime, or a related field is required. Within the minimum years of experience, at least two years in the substantive servicing of intergovernmental bodies on crime prevention and criminal justice issues is required. Within the minimum years of experience, at least two years in conducting legal or policy research and analysis on organized crime, or cybercrime, or similar legal matters is required. Work experience in drafting substantive documentation, such as reports, background papers, or other types of documentation for intergovernmental meetings, is desirable. Work experience in the collection and analysis of legislative or other substantive information, including through databases or similar information-management tools, is desirable. Work experience in the United Nations system or similar international organization dealing with crime prevention and criminal justice issues is desirable. Work experience in data analytics or related area is desirable.
