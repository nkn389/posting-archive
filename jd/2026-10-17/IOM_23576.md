Project Context and Scope
The International Organization for Migration (IOM), in cooperation with Asian Development Bank (ADB) and the governments of Uzbekistan and Tajikistan, is implementing the project “Cross-border Security Partnership between the Republic of Tajikistan and the Republic of Uzbekistan”, launched in September 2026 under the ADB Technical Assistance entitled: Addressing Health Threats in Central Asia Regional Economic Cooperation Countries and the Caucasus. The project responds to persistent public health risks, including insufficient coordination between countries, gaps in disease surveillance and data exchange, and limited understanding of the health vulnerabilities of migrants and mobile populations.
The project will support the development of a situational analysis of health workforce migration and related policies in Uzbekistan and Tajikistan and other selected Central Asia Regional Economic Cooperation (CAREC) countries . The analysis involves the in-migration and out-migration flows and exchanges of health workers (nurses and doctors) and any mutual recognition of skills of health workforce amongst selected countries. This analysis will help identify how health workforce mobility may affect health system capacity and preparedness, particularly in border and high-mobility areas where adequate health personnel are important for preventing, detecting and responding to public health threats.

Under the overall supervision of the Project Managers at IOM Uzbekistan and IOM Tajikistan the Consultant will perform the following tasks:
1. Develop an inception report and work plan outlining the scope (selection of countries by IOM and ADB), methodology, approach, and timeline for the assignment, in coordination with IOM Project Managers at IOM Uzbekistan and IOM Tajikistan.
2. Conduct a desk review of relevant literature, policies, legal frameworks, agreements, and existing studies related to health workforce migration, skills recognition in selected CAREC countries (Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, Tajikistan, Kyrgyzstan, Mongolia, Pakistan, Afghanistan, Turkmenistan, China, Georgia, Azerbaijan).
3. Identify opportunities, challenges, and barriers related to health workforce mobility and skills recognition across selected CAREC countries.
4. Conduct online consultations and key informant interviews with relevant government institutions, professional associations, academic institutions, development partners, and other stakeholders, as required.
5. Develop practical recommendations for strengthening mutual recognition of skills, facilitating professional exchanges, improving regional cooperation, and supporting health workforce development across CAREC countries.
6. Prepare and submit a situational analysis report, in accordance with IOM House Style and IOM guidelines for research reports.
7. Prepare and deliver presentations of the key findings to IOM, government counterparts, WHO and relevant stakeholders, facilitate discussion of key findings and recommendations, and incorporate relevant feedback into the final assessment report.
8. Participate in coordination meetings and provide periodic progress updates throughout the assignment.
