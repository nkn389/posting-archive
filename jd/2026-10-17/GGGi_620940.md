PROJECT BACKGROUND

The Global Green Growth Institute (GGGI) is a treaty-based intergovernmental organization that supports strong, inclusive, and sustainable economic growth in developing countries and emerging economies. Learn more on the About GGGI webpage.

GGGI supports the Government of Indonesia (GoI) through the joint GoI–GGGI Green Indonesia Future Initiative (GIFT) Program. GIFT seeks to transform Indonesia’s natural capital into green jobs and increased productivity, with a focus on green energy, sustainable landscapes, investment and financial markets, cities, and green industry.

Redirecting Investments for an Inclusive, Just Energy Transition (REDI4JET) is a five-year project (2026–2030) funded by the International Climate Initiative (IKI) of the German Federal Ministry for the Environment, Climate Action, Nature Conservation and Nuclear Safety (BMUKN). GGGI leads the consortium, with Perspectives Climate Research (PCR) and Yayasan Climate Policy Initiative (CPI) as consortium members. REDI4JET aims to accelerate Indonesia’s just energy transition (JET) by strengthening policy frameworks, carbon markets, and climate finance; mobilizing climate-friendly and just energy investments; and supporting knowledge sharing on JET policy development and climate finance under Output V.

Output V focuses on knowledge sharing that strengthens understanding of—and capacity to implement—a just energy transition in Indonesia. It will disseminate Indonesian lessons learned, facilitate knowledge exchange, and promote the use of evidence and practical experience in JET implementation.

The key indicators for Output V include:

- Indicator V.1: Attendees of knowledge-sharing events report using information on Indonesian lessons learned in their decision-making regarding JET by Q4 2030; and

- Indicator V.2: Successfully implemented knowledge-exchange events report on lessons learned in JET policy development and climate finance to inform JET implementation in Indonesia by Q3 2030.

OBJECTIVES OF THE ASSIGNMENT

The communications and knowledge-sharing consultant position is expected to provide strategic communications, knowledge management, and editorial support to REDI4JET during its first year of implementation. This includes strengthening the project’s ability to communicate key messages, disseminate lessons learned, and support knowledge exchange on JET policy development and climate finance.

The consultant will also help shape the technical output (including policy frameworks, carbon markets, climate financing, and investment insights) into clear, evidence-based narratives and accessible knowledge products for government, development partners, the private sector, and other key targeted audiences.

The consultant will work closely with the REDI4JET project team, GGGI, Perspectives Climate Research (PCR), Yayasan Climate Policy Initiative (CPI), and relevant government partners to develop a coherent communications and visibility plan, strengthen the project’s positioning within Indonesia’s broader JET agenda, and ensure consistent messaging and visibility across project outputs. The consultant will also support establishing the systems, workflows, content architecture, and knowledge-sharing mechanisms needed to support communications throughout the five-year implementation period.

To contribute to Output V, the consultant will support the Communications and Knowledge Sharing Senior Associate (COMKS Sr. Associate) to identify and package relevant knowledge for different audiences, develop communications products that facilitate learning and uptake, and support knowledge-sharing activities that strengthen understanding and capacity to implement a JET in Indonesia. The consultant will also work closely with the Monitoring, Evaluation and Learning (MEL) function to ensure that results, lessons learned, and impact stories are supported by quality and verified data.

DELIVERABLES AND PAYMENT SCHEDULE

The consultant will be accountable for deliverables and responsibilities listed below:

Component 1: Communications Collateral Production and Dissemination

- Map technical outputs, policy studies, and project milestones to maintain a centralized REDI4JET content pipeline.

- Coordinate the editorial and communications work plan and manage day-to-day production with the project team, service providers, and relevant stakeholders. Tasks include preparing production and dissemination briefs and drafting presentations, explainers, stories, and other communications collateral.

- Provide strategic communications support for arranging digital and print collateral used in project workshops and events.

- Coordinate with external service providers (including communications firms, event organizers, and graphic designers) to ensure timely, high-quality delivery of agreed products and initiatives.

Component 2: Knowledge Dissemination and Media Engagement

- In coordination with the COMKS Sr. Associate and MEL function, synthesize data and capture project knowledge, including technical findings and policy insights translated to accessible knowledge products, such as policy briefs and toolkits.

- Support in designing and handling knowledge-sharing vents, working alongside with service providers.

- Support COMKS Sr. Associate to prepare media kits, assist in pitches, and building relationships with journalists through appropriate outreach activities.

Component 3: Planning, Coordination, and Monitoring

- Maintain and integrate project calendar covering publication releases, dissemination activities, potential media engagements, workshops, and events.

- Support the implementation of editorial and communications workplan, including to execute any recommended and advised improvement for communications workplan.

- Coordinate communications inputs across consortium members, including CPI and PCR.

- Coordinate, collect, and monitor content, media, and outreach performance across REDI4JET consortium members’ owned and earned channels, including content analytics, web analytics, and media monitoring. Support to measure the progress of agreed communications workplan and provide insights to make improvements.

Component 4: Quality Assurance

- Work with the COMKS Sr. Associate as the first-line quality-control checkpoint across consortium communications, ensuring compliance with REDI4JET communications protocols, branding standards, and donor visibility requirements.

- In its responsibility to provide high quality products, including technical and editorial reviews of project outputs and knowledge products. Provide actionable feedback on clarity, accuracy, narrative coherence, and alignment with project goals prior to management clearance. The exact arrangement of clearance workflow of any outputs or deliverables will be agreed with the team once the consultant is on board.

- Recommend practical, streamlined communications workflows, tools, and systems to the COMKS Sr Associate, project team, and consortium members to support effective content coordination throughout the first-year implementation

Component 5: Communications Strategy, Positioning, and Visibility

- Support the COMK Sr. Associate to prepare and facilitate a validation workshop for REDI4JET Communications and Visibility Strategy with consortium partners and key government stakeholders.

- Support to develop strategic key messages for targeted audiences, including but not limited to government counterparts, resource partners, private sectors, targeted community, investors.

- Proactively identify and suggest strategic communications and visibility opportunities to strengthen REDI4JET’s positioning and relevance within Indonesia’s broader JET agenda.

Working and Reporting Arrangements

- The consultant will be working directly with COMKS Sr. Associate on day-to-day strategic guidance, task management and workflow. All communications product/output will be reviewed and approved by the COMKS Lead, with formal reporting and accountability for project governance will be directed to REDI4JET Project Manager.

- The consultant will coordinate closely with the MEL function to ensure that knowledge-sharing, results communication, and dissemination activities are aligned with the project’s results framework and contribute to Output V indicators.

- The consultant will work closely with Perspectives Climate Research (PCR) and Yayasan Climate Policy Initiative (CPI) as well as relevant government partners to coordinate communications inputs, ensure consistency across consortium outputs, and support to facilitate the timely development and dissemination of project knowledge.

- All major communications and knowledge products will be subject to review and approval through the relevant GGGI and project processes. The consultant will provide strategic and editorial recommendations but will not independently approve project deliverables on behalf of GGGI.

During this contract period, the Consultant performance will be assessed against the monthly timesheet detailing list of tasks and project results done and delivered in the month.
