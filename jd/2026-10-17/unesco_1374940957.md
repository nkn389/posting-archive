ORGANIZATIONAL CONTEXT

Founded in 1964 by the late Nobel Laureate Abdus Salam and located in Trieste (Italy), the Abdus Salam International Centre for Theoretical Physics (ICTP) seeks to accomplish its mandate by providing scientists from developing countries with the continuing education and skills that they need to enjoy long and productive careers.

OVERVIEW OF THE FUNCTIONS OF THE POST

Under the overall authority of the Director and the direct supervision of the Head of Unit, Information and Communication Technology (ICT), the incumbent leads the IT Infrastructure Team, which is responsible for the hardware and software network and server infrastructure of the Centre. This includes the physical IT infrastructure (cabling, servers, data centres, computing laboratories, wireless access points, laptops and desktop computers), the software required to operate it, in particular email servers, the hardware and software of the phone system and of the security system, and cybersecurity. KEY ROLE AND RESPONSIBILITIES

Team Leadership:

- Supervise the infrastructure IT staff in their day-to-day activities, including network monitoring and the management of PCs, laptops, data centres/server rooms, storage, servers, network and communications equipment, and the help-desk service.

- Set the work priorities of the members of the infrastructure team.

- Work closely with the Head of Unit, ICT and the leader of the systems/applications development team on overall priorities and synergies across the Unit; participate in the meetings of the Institute IT Committee and in the monthly meetings of the ICT Section.

Infrastructure Planning and Operations:

- Plan, design, implement and maintain the hardware and software configuration of the Centre’s computer system, including UNIX workstations and servers, LAN and LAN devices, PCs, Macs and the Internet connection.

- Implement hardware and software projects to maintain and enhance the ICT infrastructure, including the comprehensive review and improvement of the infrastructure linked to the ongoing renovation of the Centre.

- Coordinate with colleagues in Procurement, Technical Services and Maintenance on the purchase and deployment of IT equipment, and with the University of Trieste Network Manager on shared services.

Long Description

Security and Scientific Computing Support:

- Ensure a safe and secure computing environment (network, data, etc.), including cybersecurity and compliance with data protection regulations.

- Support the High-Performance Computing (HPC) activities of the scientific sections, the International Consortium for Scientific Computing (ICOMP) and the Master in High Performance Computing programme, and provide technical guidance to the scientific sections, the Library and Multimedia Services on projects involving data management and computing technology.

- Represent the Centre in international IT projects and in its IT-related training activities.
