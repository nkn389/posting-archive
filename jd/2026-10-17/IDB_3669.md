About this position

We are seeking a consultant to assess the feasibility of a structured subsidy mechanism and develop an initial concept to support premium affordability of the Parametric Insurance Program under the Caribbean Water Utilities Insurance Collective Segregated Portfolio (CWUIC SP). CWUIC SP is managed by the Water and Sanitation Division within IDB’s Infrastructure and Energy Sector (INE/WSA), in collaboration with other partner organizations.

As the consultant, you will be responsible for (i) assessing the feasibility of a structured subsidization mechanism to enhance premium affordability under the CWUIC Parametric Insurance Program, including a review of relevant financing approaches and constraints; and (ii) developing an initial concept for a subsidy mechanism for CWUIC SP member utilities, including its design principles, potential funding pathways, stakeholder landscape, and associated risks.

You will work in the Water and Sanitation Division within the Infrastructure and Energy (INE) department. The Water and Sanitation Division (WSA) seeks to ensure universal and sustainable access to high-quality water, sanitation, and solid waste management services to contribute to sustainable economic growth in IDB member countries in Latin America and the Caribbean and to improving the quality of life of their citizens. Priority areas include: (i) expanding access to quality services for low-income and vulnerable populations, and promoting comprehensive and sustainable solutions; (ii) improve sector governance and financial sustainability and (iii) incorporate the concept of water security in the sector, particularly by increasing coverage of wastewater treatment, protecting supply basins and reducing flood risks.

This consultancy will be financed with the resources of the technical cooperation RG-T4777 “Strengthening and Expansion of CWUIC to Enhance Resiliency in the Water and Sanitation Sector in the Caribbean”.

What you’ll do

As CWUIC SP subsidization mechanism Consultant, you will:

- Conduct a targeted review of existing sustainable subsidy mechanisms and relevant financing approaches on similar facilities across the region and world.

- Develop a framework for the design and implementation of a subsidy mechanism tailored to the CWUIC context, including what aspects need to be considered for a subsidization mechanism (e.g., eligibility criteria, targeting approach, sustainability, alignment with CCRIF and CWUIC structures, donor requirements).

- Facilitate a discussion process with CWUIC stakeholders, including CCRIF, to co-develop an initial subsidy mechanism proposal. The facilitation should include a high-level feasibility and risk assessment of the different subsidy mechanism options, including financial, operational, and institutional considerations.

- Initiate early-stage engagement with potential funding sources to assess interest and alignment (e.g. GCF, Loss and Damage Fund).

- Prepare an initial concept note outlining the proposed subsidy mechanism, including its rationale, structure and potential funding pathways.

- Develop presentation materials to communicate the proposed approach to key stakeholders and potential funding partners.
