Unit Context:

The WBG Corporate Solutions Department (ITSCO) is focused on the digital transformation of essential corporate services to ensure WBG operations are seamless, resilient, and future ready. Uniting digitalization roadmaps, experience design, process optimization, application delivery, systems management, and technology modernization across some 70 products and services, the department links business ambition with operational excellence—from intuitive employee experiences and precision payroll to data-driven decision making, intelligent automation, and AI enablement.

Duties and accountabilities:

• Work with Director/CIO to ensure key strategic initiatives are well formulated and planned, follow-up persistently on execution with teams, and prepare stakeholder updates.

• Proactively track and follow up on strategic priorities for the department with urgency, clarity, and consistency.

• Prepare clear, compelling, and visually-appealing executive-level presentations and reporting for leadership and stakeholders.

• Support the department’s front office with ad hoc reporting needs, transformation initiatives, knowledge sharing, and more.

• Embrace an entrepreneurial and change mind set; willingness to challenge the status quo; and digital and AI native ways of working, including proficiency with AI tools such as Claude, Copilot, Gemini to work faster and more effectively.

Selection Criteria
• Master’s degree with 5 years of relevant experience, or bachelor’s degree with a minimum of 7 years of relevant experience, or equivalent combination of education and experience.

• Experience reporting to senior management and executives with a focus on clear and impactful presentations, conveying data-driven recommendations, metrics, and program health transparency.

• Excellent communication, stakeholder management, and organizational abilities. Able to relentlessly move priorities forward without direct authority, and to manage conflict constructively.

• Proven ability to prioritize effectively, act decisively, and maintain momentum under pressure. Proactively resolve bottlenecks and consistently deliver without sacrificing quality.

• Demonstrated ability to champion new ways of working and constructively challenge the status quo. Build support for change and help teams navigate transitions with clarity and confidence.

• Proven ability to connect day-to-day decisions to strategic goals and engage stakeholders with credibility. Apply sound judgment in light of organizational priorities and the broader operating environment.
