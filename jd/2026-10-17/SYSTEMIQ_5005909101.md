&lt;div class=&quot;content-intro&quot;&gt;&lt;p&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Company Description:&lt;/strong&gt; Systemiq is a system-change company committed to achieving the Sustainable Development Goals and the Paris Agreement by transforming markets and business models across five key systems: nature and food, materials and circularity, energy, urban areas, and sustainable finance.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;As a certified B Corp, we combine strategic advisory with high-impact, on-the-ground work, partnering with business, finance, policymakers and civil society to deliver system change. With offices across Africa, Asia, Europe and the Americas, we are dedicated exclusively to sustainability-focused work.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/p&gt;&lt;/div&gt;&lt;h2&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Do you want to be part of the change?&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/h2&gt;
&lt;p&gt;Systemiq is a fast-growing and constantly evolving organisation that has business operations in 7 countries. We are hiring a &lt;strong&gt;Junior Legal Counsel&lt;/strong&gt; (1-2 years post-qualification experience (PQE)) to join our global legal team based in London and Jakarta. We are looking for a lawyer who wants to work for a purpose-driven company. We offer the opportunity to be part of a thriving team committed to sustainability and environmental projects that have a lasting impact. You will have a high degree of flexibility to manage your workload and working pattern and a genuine interest in impact projects. Working alongside our high- performing team of 4 in Legal, you will be instrumental in shaping the structure and advising our business across our 3 legal pillars (i) &lt;strong&gt;Commercial Transactions&lt;/strong&gt; (ii) &lt;strong&gt;Corporate and Company Secretarial&lt;/strong&gt;; and (iii) &lt;strong&gt;Grants and Philanthropy&lt;/strong&gt;. You will work closely with our joint Heads of Legal and Legal Counsel team (based in London &amp;amp; Jakarta) on key commercial, strategic and transactional business projects.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;This is a hands-on role for a junior lawyer who wants early responsibility, close exposure to senior legal stakeholders and the opportunity to help build legal processes in a growing international organisation.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;As part of a small and growing legal team, you will have significant scope to develop your expertise, broaden your responsibilities and progress your legal career as the organisation grows.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;h3&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Primary Responsibilities&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/h3&gt;
&lt;p&gt;From day one, you will support a broad range of legal, operational and governance workstreams as our Junior Legal Counsel.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;ul&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Supporting Legal and Operations leads across corporate, commercial, grants and HR workstreams.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Independently manage routine and lower-complexity legal matters, with guidance from senior counsel on higher-risk or more complex issues.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Owning legal operations processes, including FAQs, playbooks, decision trees, checklists and training materials and workflow improvements.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Carrying out legal research and managing legal knowledge, data and AI-enabled workflows.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Preparing legal training materials for Systemiq consultants, partners and internal teams.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;/ul&gt;
&lt;p&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Commercial&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;ul&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Support the Head of Legal (Commercial) on client contracts, NDAs and procurement contracts.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Maintain commercial contract templates and support contract lifecycle management.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Manage contractor, advisor and project expert onboarding, including template contracts and HR engagement documentation.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;/ul&gt;
&lt;p&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Corporate and Company Secretarial&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;ul&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Support the Head of Legal (Corporate) on corporate templates, share and asset transactions, spin-offs, entity set-up and board materials.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Manage the relationship with the outsourced company secretarial function and track corporate filings and administration.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Support international intra-group entity management and corporate governance processes.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Prepare, track and coordinate powers of attorney, delegations of authority and execution formalities.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;/ul&gt;
&lt;p&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Grants, Philanthropy and Partnerships&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;ul&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Coordinate vendor onboarding with Finance, including KYC, due diligence, sanctions screening and reputational checks.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Develop and maintain templates for grants and philanthropic projects.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Support annual compliance and reporting for B Corp, UN Global Compact, EU Transparency Register and other partnership and membership obligations.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;/ul&gt;
