Duties and Responsibilities
Background: Established in Osaka in 1992, UNEP’s International Environmental Technology Centre (IETC) is part of the Industry and Economy Division and serves as UNEP’s hub for the waste and technology dimensions of the circular economy. Guided by the vision “Innovation Towards Zero Waste,” the Centre supports developing countries and countries with economies in transition in adopting environmentally sound technologies, policies and practices. IETC leads on electronics across the full value chain and addresses the waste dimensions of plastics, textiles and food, with expertise spanning circularity, innovation, critical minerals, and sustainable value chains; with special attention to the social, gender and youth dimensions of zero-waste and circular economy agendas. The Centre works closely with UNEP teams, governments, businesses and other stakeholders, including its key strategic partners Institute for Global Environmental Strategies and Osaka City’s Global Environment Centre Foundation. Through policy and technical advice, knowledge development, technology cooperation, partnerships, and demonstration initiatives, IETC aims to accelerate an inclusive, technology-driven transition towards zero-waste and circular economy systems. Recognizing waste reduction and the avoidance of waste generation as priority policy actions to prevent waste mismanagement, particularly in low- and middle-income countries, IETC provides technical and policy advice, develops knowledge and partnerships and implements demonstration projects. Working across UNEP and with key partners, including the Institute for Global Environmental Strategies and Osaka City’s Global Environment Centre Foundation, IETC advances inclusive, technology-driven solutions that accelerate progress towards zero waste and circular economy societies. This advertisement is seeking two interns to work in the UNEP-IETC headquarters, in Osaka, Japan during a 6-month period. The Internship is UNPAID and full-time. Interns work five days per week (35 hours) under the supervision of a staff member in the department or office to which they are assigned. Interns are not financially remunerated by the United Nations. Costs and arrangements for travel, visas, accommodation and living expenses are the responsibility of interns or their sponsoring institutions. Tasks: Under the direct supervision of IETC Officer, the Intern will: 1. Research and summarize scientific information on zero waste and circular economy solutions, including social and economic aspects such as informal sector integration, gender mainstreaming , behaviour insights and zero-waste job creation potential. 2. Analyse waste-related and circular economy policies, such as national waste management strategies and regulatory frameworks, from the perspectives of environmental sustainability and resource circulation. 3. Draft analytical briefs, policy reviews, research summaries, and technical reports that support evidence-based decision-making and knowledge generation addressing zero waste and circular economy matters 4. Organize and otherwise support meetings (e.g., notetaking, assisting in the management of participant travel, visitor management) and events organized by IETC or in which the Centre is involved. 5. Map out business opportunities and technology-based innovations that advance circularity, resource recovery and zero-waste objectives, particularly in high-impact sectors. 6. Assist in drafting or assessing documents related to UNEP’s programme of work, and develop technical documentation, guidance or tools related to IETC’s work. 7. Assist in populating digital platforms and knowledge-sharing platforms related to IETC’s work. 8. Support the collection, analysis and visualization of data and indicators of zero waste and circular economy. 9. Draft outreach, communication and educational materials related to IETC’s work. 10. Assist in stakeholder mapping and the development of partnership and networks. 11. Carry out other duties relevant to the Intern’s educational background and professional aspirations.
