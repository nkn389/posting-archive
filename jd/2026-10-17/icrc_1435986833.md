Data & Analytics Officer (801051)

What we do

Since 1863, the International Committee of the Red Cross (ICRC) has worked to preserve human dignity and relieve suffering caused by war and armed violence.

In collaboration with our Red Cross and Red Crescent partners around the world, we do everything we can to deliver life-saving aid, reconnect families and locate missing people – helping those who need it most, regardless of who they are and what side of the front line they are on.

We engage with authorities and armed forces on all sides, often confidentially, pressing for access to detainees to improve their living conditions and urging compliance with international humanitarian law to protect non-combatants, including from digital threats.

Purpose

Within the HRSS, the IRSP Data & Analytics Officer is responsible to carry out data analytics transactions and administrative work. S/he handles transactions related to enrolment, contributions, payment of benefits, and providing reports and analysis. S/he will also provide support and guidance on issues within their area of expertise to IRSP Officers.

Accountabilities & functional responsibilities

Core activities

- Keeps Data Management processes and documentation updated.

- Provides insight to the métier in reflecting on the team’s performance measures, improvements in the functional area of expertise and provides ongoing support with data and analysis.

- Contributes to institutional reporting and dashboards by supporting colleagues in delivering reports, revisions, and optimization.

- Ensures continuity and smooth transition of data entered into various system applications.

- Contributes to the development of the data model and usage of the referential data that supports analysis and decision making.

- Focus on the data validation of the monthly IRSP contribution cycle, payment process, and applicable retro adjustments.

- Contribute to the post go-live optimization of IntelliPen within the scope of the IRSP data and analysis.

- Ownership of year-end reporting activities provided to Finance, Accounting, and actuarial services.

- Support in process walkthrough, submission of audit samples, and answering to questions during internal and/or external audit activities.

Process management

- Perform First Time Right (FTR) analysis for the transactions and administrative work of IRSP processes.

- Oversee and monitor accuracy and timeliness of IRSP processes, in line with the Service Level Agreement.

- Proactively communicate with the stakeholders concerned (Finance, HRSS, or HQ Counterparts) for any changes pertaining to ways of working, policy, or system updates/downtime.

Accountabilities & functional responsibilities

Quality Control

- Uphold an audit-ready mindset to support any future internal/external audit activities.

- Assists in improving, developing & applying data quality monitoring processes & tools (dashboards etc.) and contributes where appropriate in DQ controls automation.

- Monitors data quality controls and consolidates, data clean-up and implements corrective actions.

- Performs four-eye/buddy check to the tasks performed by IRSP Officers.

Other tasks

- If necessary and upon request, provide ad hoc administrative support of a data and analytics officer within the scope of IRSP Administration services.

- Being proactive with continuous improvement propositions for the team’s management processes and / or more generally the HR Shared Services
