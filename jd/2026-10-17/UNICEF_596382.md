For every child, the right to Commitment

Join UNICEF's Global Programme Division (GPD), which plays a central role in driving programme excellence across the organization by providing global strategic leadership, policy direction, and technical expertise. GPD develops and monitors frameworks and standards, promotes coherence across sectors, regions, and partners, and generates evidence-based solutions that help improve outcomes for children worldwide.

As part of UNICEF's efforts to accelerate results for children, the Financing Child Nutrition & Development Unit works within a network of Centres of Excellence that provide high-quality, demand-driven technical assistance to countries. Through collaboration across regions and programme areas, the team supports policy reform, institutional strengthening, workforce development, and scalable programming that advances child development outcomes at scale. Good nutrition is essential for child survival, growth, learning, and lifelong productivity, yet significant financing gaps remain, creating an urgent need for innovative, sustainable financing solutions for children.

How can you make a difference?

In this role, you will contribute to UNICEF's efforts to strengthen financing for child nutrition and development by supporting evidence-based policies, scalable programmes, and country-led solutions. Working within a global team of experts, you will help advance innovative approaches to financing, provide technical support to governments and partners, and contribute to strengthening systems that improve outcomes for children and adolescents. Your work will support UNICEF's mission to translate global policy into practical action and help countries deliver sustainable, large-scale results for children.
