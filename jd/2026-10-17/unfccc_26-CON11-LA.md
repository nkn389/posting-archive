The United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change (UNFCCC) is the focus of the political
process to address Climate Change. The Convention secretariat supports the Convention and its Kyoto
Protocol by a range of activities, including substantive and organizational support to meetings of the Parties.

The Legal Affairs division of the UNFCCC secretariat in Bonn, Germany, provides legal and procedural
advice and services to support (1) the intergovernmental negotiation process and the implementation of the
Convention, the Kyoto Protocol and the Paris Agr eement, and (2) the administration and operations of the
UNFCCC secretariat.

The consultancy is situated within the Policy Review Team of the Legal Affairs division and supports the
implementation of the secretariat's Policy Review Project. The project seeks to strengthen and modernize
the secretariat's institutional regulatory framework through the systematic review, revision, consolidation and
development of administrative instructions, continued operationalization of the delegation of authority
framework, and enhancement of the Policies Portal and Delegation of Authority Portal.

Purpose

The consultant will provide specialized legal and policy expertise to support the implementation of the Policy
Review Project. The consultant will undertake the substantive review, development, revision and
consolidation of administrative instructions and related documents, support the continued implementation
and refinement of the Delegation of Authority framework, and assist with the content management and
enhancement of the Policies Portal and Delegation of Authority Portal.

The consultant will work under the supervision of the Director of the Legal Affairs division and in close
consultation with policy owners and other relevant stakeholders across the secretariat. The work will
contribute to ensuring that the secretariat's re gulatory framework remains legally sound, operationally
relevant, aligned with the secretariat's institutional linkage to the United Nations, and responsive to evolving
organizational needs.

Tasks

Under the supervision of the Director of the Legal Affairs division, or her designated representative, the
consultant will:

1. Review, revise, consolidate and develop administrative instructions and related policy instruments
identified under the Policy Review Project, with particular focus on administrative instructions
requiring substantial revision or modernization.

2. Conduct legal and policy analysis of existing administrative instructions and associated instruments
to ensure consistency with the secretariat's legal framework, organizational practice, delegations of
authority, and relevant United Nations standards and guidance.

3. Coordinate consultations with policy owners and relevant stakeholders, consolidate comments
received during review processes, and support resolution of legal and policy issues arising during
policy development and revision exercises.

4. Support the continued implementation, refinement, and operationalization of the secretariat's
delegation of authority framework, including review of delegation instruments and associated
procedures.

5. Support the maintenance, content management, and enhancement of the Policies Portal and
Delegation of Authority Portal, as needed, including quality assurance of content, identification of
functional improvements and support for implementation of updates.

6. Support the development of guidance materials, communication products, and training resources to
facilitate understanding and implementation of administrative instructions and delegation instruments
by secretariat staff.

7. Perform any other job -related activity required to meet the mandates, goals, and objectives of the
Legal Affairs division and the secretariat.

Timeframe

The duration of the assignment is for seven (7) months, from 2 November 2026 to 30 April 2027. The total
number of working days is 150.5 days.

Duty station

The consultant will work remotely. Travel may be required for meetings as requested by the Legal Affairs
Division.

Requirements

Educational Background:

Advanced university degree (Master's degree or equivalent) in law, with specialization in international law,
administrative law, or a related field.
A first level university degree in combination with an additional two years of qualifying experience may be
accepted in lieu of the advanced university degree.

Experience:

A minimum of seven (7) to ten (10) years of progressively responsible experience in law, including legal
analysis, research, and drafting is required.
At least five years (5) in an international environment, preferably in a United Nations common system
organization or other intergovernmental organization, is desirable.
Experience in reviewing, developing, drafting, and providing advice on the implementation of policies in a
United Nations common system or other intergovernmental organization is desirable.

Language requirements:

Fluency in English (both oral and written) is required. Working knowledge of another UN language is an
asset.
