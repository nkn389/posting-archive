Project Context and Scope
The Green Skills Corridor (GSC) has evolved through collaboration between Labour Mobility and Climate Action and Mobility Regional Thematic Units at IOM Regional Office for Middle East and North Africa (MENA), beginning with policy research on the links between skills-based labour mobility and climate change in MENA, followed by a green-skills needs assessment in Tunisia and analysis of how Skills Mobility Partnerships can support skills development and climate adaptation in the Gulf Cooperation Council. Three regional dialogues convened since 2024 have further advanced the transition from policy foundations to practical, scalable implementation. These dialogues were supported by the Danish-funded IOM Climate Change and Migration Data (CCMD) Programme.
The GSC and accompanying Human Capital Investment Framework (HCIF) provide an integrated mechanism for operationalizing the Route-Based Approach (RBA). They connect green-skills development, labour-market matching, industry-recognized certification, fair recruitment, legal mobility pathways and sustainable investment. The initiative is initially focused on Egypt, Morocco and Tunisia, with priority sectors including renewable energy, controlled-environment and climate-smart agriculture, waste management, circular economy and green hydrogen.
The GSC advances the RBA through two mutually reinforcing pillars. First, it expands safe, regular and demand-driven labour mobility pathways by linking certified workers with verified employment opportunities in countries of origin and destination. Second, it addresses structural drivers of irregular migration by investing in Technical and Vocational Education and Training (TVET) modernization, employability, green jobs, labour-market institutions and human capital in origin communities. The HCIF aims to mobilize blended finance from governments, development finance institutions, climate finance, employers and private investors, moving beyond stand-alone donor-funded pilots toward a scalable investment model that supports coordinated interventions across countries of origin and destination and contributes to reducing the structural drivers of irregular migration.
The consultancy builds on activities under CCMD, particularly resilience building to climate-related mobility risks, expanded access to climate-adapted and environmentally sustainable livelihoods, and support for safe, regular migration options along key migration routes. Through the GSC and HCIF, the consultancy will help translate these objectives into scalable programme and investment opportunities linking countries of origin and destination. The mobilization of blended finance through the HCIF will also support the sustainability of activities under CCMD following programme closure.
Guided by outputs from the CCMD funded workshops on Regular Pathways for Climate Action, this consultancy will support development of technical guidelines and recommendations. It will also support implementation of these same guidelines and recommendations through proposal development, investment preparation, partnership engagement and resource mobilization for the GSC and HCIF. The senior consultant will undertake the work under the supervision of Senior Regional Thematic Specialist (RTS) for Labour Mobility (LMD) and in close coordination with the RTS for Climate Action and Mobility, the Regional Project Development Officer, and IOM Country Offices, and other relevant regional, country and HQ colleagues.
