Introduction
Established in 1951, IOM is a Related Organization of the United Nations and the leading UN agency in the field of migration. Working closely with governmental, intergovernmental and non-governmental partners, IOM promotes humane and orderly migration for the benefit of all. It saves lives and protects people on the move, drives solutions to displacement, and facilitates pathways for regular migration, while providing services and advice to governments and migrants.
IOM is committed to fostering a respectful, inclusive and supportive workplace where all employees can thrive professionally and feel valued. By creating such an environment, IOM aims to better harness the full potential of migration and strengthen its support to people on the move.

Organizational Context and Scope
Under the direct supervision of the Comptroller/Director of the Department of Financial and Administrative Management (DFAM), the Chief of Global Accounting leads IOM’s global accounting, statutory financial reporting and project financial report certification functions. They oversees the preparation of IOM’s statutory financial statements ensuring IPSAS compliance, management reporting on the Organization’s financial performance and position, project financial report certification and global accounting operations.

Required Qualifications and Experience
Education

- Master's degree in Accounting, Finance or a related field from an accredited academic institution with ten years of relevant professional experience; or,
- University degree in the above fields with twelve years of relevant professional experience; and,
- Professional certification as chartered accountant (CA) or certified public accountant (CPA) or membership at the Association of Chartered Certified Accountants (ACCA) or another recognized professional accountancy body is required.

Accredited Universities are those listed in the UNESCO World Higher Education Database.
Experience

- A minimum of ten years (twelve years for bachelor's degree holders) of progressively evolving leadership experience in financial management with responsibilities for accounting, financial reporting and financial analysis, including at corporate level, in a multinational / international organization environment;
- Demonstrated experience defining accounting requirements for, implementing or substantially improving enterprise resource planning systems and related financial reporting solutions, including financial master data, controls, data flows and process integration and,
- Demonstrated experience with internal control assessments, financial risk management and external audit engagement, including the resolution of material accounting issues and implementation of corrective actions in a complex organizational environment.

Skills

- Extensive professional knowledge of financial accounting as applied to international public organizations with experience with International Public Sector Accounting Standards (IPSAS) and/or International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS);
- Knowledge of IOM and/or UN financial regulations, policies, procedures and practices is desired;
- Successful track record in a lead role played in the consolidation and production of corporate accounts and statutory financial statements under internationally recognized accounting standards for external audit;
- Successful track record in remotely managing teams and functions, including highly decentralized financial and accounting practices;
- Advanced ability to use enterprise resource planning systems, financial consolidation and reporting tools, business intelligence platforms and data analytics to oversee accounting quality, investigate issues and support management decision-making.

- Manage the development and updating of IOM’s accounting protocols and procedures in response to emerging operational requirements and changes in IPSAS, while pursuing alignment with United Nations (UN) accounting practices where appropriate and feasible within IOM’s financial context.

- Ensure that the structure and content of IOM’s annual statutory reports and financial management reports are regularly updated to reflect the evolving operational context and stakeholder requirements.
- Manage IOM’s project financial report certification process, ensuring the timely and consistent review and certification of reports in accordance with applicable organizational requirements and donor reporting obligations, and advance standardised, digitised and scalable reporting solutions.
- Oversee accounts consolidation, periodic closure and the global accounting control framework, including reconciliations, balance-sheet reviews, account ownership, review and approval arrangements, and the timely identification and resolution of material accounting issues, financial risks and accounts-quality concerns.
- Safeguard the integrity and quality of IOM’s accounting records, ensuring the timely and accurate recognition, measurement, presentation and disclosure of assets, liabilities, revenue and expenses across offices worldwide, and directing corrective action where weaknesses or errors are identified.
- Oversee the accounting dimensions of IOM’s enterprise resource planning system and related financial management tools, financial master data and data flows. Ensure that accounting requirements, controls and reporting needs are appropriately incorporated into system design and process changes for all accounting processes, including accounts receivable, accounts payable, fixed assets and project accounting.
- Provide functional leadership, guidance and oversight to the relevant accounting and financial reporting functions at IOM Headquarters and within the delocalised structures of Department of Financial and Administrative Management (DFAM) and ensure appropriate guidance and oversight of field accounting functions in collaboration with Regional Offices and the delocalised Finance and Accounting Division (FAD) functions.
- Lead the continuous improvement and transformation of accounting and financial reporting processes, including standardisation, simplification, automation, data analytics, the appropriate use of emerging technologies, and the implementation of process improvements in coordination with interdependent functions across IOM.
- Ensure IOM’s readiness to adopt new accounting standards, including new IPSAS requirements, and lead their timely implementation across the Organization.
- Lead initiatives to strengthen the technical accounting and financial reporting capabilities of IOM’s global finance community. Establish priorities based on recurring accounting issues, audit findings, new IPSAS requirements and system or process changes; promote practical knowledge products and professional development; and contribute accounting expertise to DFAM-wide finance capacity-building and workforce initiatives
- Coordinate the implementation of DFAM’s annual work plan from the FAD perspective and facilitate the work of IOM’s External Auditors appointed by the Council in relation to IOM’s annual financial statements.
- Lead, manage and develop the staff under the incumbent’s supervision, establish work priorities and performance expectations, ensure the effective allocation of resources, and foster collaboration across the accounting and financial reporting functions.
