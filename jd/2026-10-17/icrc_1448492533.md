Functional Quality Assurance and Data Migration Officer

What we do

Since 1863, the International Committee of the Red Cross (ICRC) has worked to preserve human dignity and relieve suffering caused by war and armed violence.

In collaboration with our Red Cross and Red Crescent partners around the world, we do everything we can to deliver life-saving aid, reconnect families and locate missing people – helping those who need it most, regardless of who they are and what side of the front line they are on.

We engage with authorities and armed forces on all sides, often confidentially, pressing for access to detainees to improve their living conditions and urging compliance with international humanitarian law to protect non-combatants, including from digital threats.

Purpose

The Protection & CTA Division aims to protect people affected by war and armed conflict by advocating for their rights under the Geneva Conventions and providing life-saving assistance at all stages of hostilities. It also strives to minimize the vulnerabilities of people at risk and ensure that those involved in the fighting comply with their obligations under international humanitarian law. To support its programs across the globe, it relies on its ability to translate data into usable insights to better respond to the needs of the people affected.

The Functional Quality Assurance and Data Migration Officer is a technical quality specialist for the Protection Digital Ecosystem and other digital solutions managed by the Protection Data team, with a focus on Data Migration and Quality Assurance. They are responsible for preparing data through global and delegation-specific data cleaning, mapping, and data transformation. At the same time, they are responsible for data loading, reconciliation, data integrity, and post-migration validations.

They are also responsible for executing the functional quality and operational tests from a user and business perspective, as well as proposing and executing test automation, all related to the new Protection digital ecosystem. Simply put, the position is responsible to support the Functional Quality Assurance & Testing Officer based in Geneva, in ensuring that the new digital ecosystem works the way users and protection programs expect it to work, i.e. whether it is practical, functional and fit for Protection’s use and purpose - not on the underlying technical code or infrastructure.

Accountabilities & Functional Responsibilities

Data Migration

- Contributes to the implementation of the data migration strategy.

- Assists in analysing and mapping existing data structures to the Protection Digital Ecosystem requirements.

- Conducts analysis to identify global or delegation-specific inconsistencies in data that require cleaning.

- Prepares, adapts and executes data cleaning jobs to correct identified global or delegation-specific inconsistencies.

- Supports loads and imports data into the Protection Digital Ecosystem.

- Performs data quality and migration testing to validate the accuracy, completeness, and integrity of data and the outcome of the data migration on test and production.

- Participates and contributes to the improvement of data migration scripts; reports identified bugs and problems.

- Supports parallel running of both systems during the transition period and documents the migration process and outcomes.

Quality Assurance and Testing

- Follows functional testing strategy, planning, schedules, dependencies and milestones and supports their improvement.

- Directly conducts functional testing of the Protection digital ecosystem, including prototype and configuration testing, component and functional testing, workflow and end-to-end testing, integration and interface testing, role, access and permission testing, regression testing, usability testing, user acceptance testing (UAT) and pilot testing.

- Clearly reports feedback. Suggests improvements and test automatization if possible and appropriate.

- Supports functional testing coordination, onboarding, preparation, provision of guidance, test scenarios, instructions, training as well as consolidation and analysis of feedback from testers to provide a coherent view of functional testing outcomes.

- Assists in issues, results and test reporting, with activities such as regular documentation and testing issues/results classification to maintain an evidence base to support functional acceptance and deployment decisions.

- Assists in functional readiness and continuous improvement by documenting and highlighting outstanding issues, gaps and risks and suggesting improvements.
