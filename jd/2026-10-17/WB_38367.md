Unit Context

The ITS Operations Products unit (ITSOP) enables the Bank's business processes through modern, flexible, and user-friendly digital platforms that integrate and optimize critical functions for front-line staff. ITSOP also drives AI-powered business solutions that support operational priorities and accelerate trusted, data-informed decision-making.

ITSOP is seeking an Associate Senior AI Solution Architect to lead the unit’s AI strategy and translate it into secure, scalable, and production-ready solutions. The successful candidate will personally design and prototype AI components, develop reference implementations, evaluate technical approaches, and guide engineering teams through deployment and operationalization. This is not a purely advisory role. It requires recent, demonstrable experience building and operating Generative AI and agentic solutions in enterprise environments.

Key Responsibilities

AI Strategy and Architecture

• Develop the ITSOP AI strategy, capability roadmap, reference architectures, standards, and reusable patterns, aligned with enterprise direction and measurable operational outcomes.
• Translate business opportunities into prioritized AI use cases, target architectures, delivery approaches, and investment recommendations.
• Design end-to-end solutions spanning user experience, agents, models, retrieval, enterprise data, APIs, identity, security, observability, and operational support.
• Evaluate technology choices and architecture trade-offs across models, platforms, orchestration frameworks, retrieval approaches, and integration patterns.
Hands-on Prototyping and Engineering

• Design, code, test, and demonstrate working prototypes and reference implementations using Python and modern software engineering practices.
• Graduate selected solutions from discovery and proof of concept through technical validation, production design, deployment, and operational readiness.
• Create reusable SDKs, templates, components, and implementation guidance that enable delivery teams to build consistently and securely.
• Conduct design and code reviews, troubleshoot complex cross-layer issues, and provide practical technical direction to engineering teams.
Agentic AI, Enterprise Knowledge, and Integration

• Design and implement agentic solutions using tool calling, structured outputs, orchestration, state and memory management, routing, retries, fallbacks, and human approval patterns.
• Design safe single-agent and multi-agent workflows with clear task boundaries, controlled permissions, escalation paths, and failure handling.
• Build Retrieval-Augmented Generation and enterprise knowledge solutions using semantic search, vector retrieval, knowledge graphs, ontologies, and structured and unstructured data.
• Integrate agents with enterprise applications, APIs, event-driven services, data platforms, content repositories, and business workflows.
• Apply open agent interoperability approaches such as Model Context Protocol (MCP), Agent2Agent (A2A), or equivalent standards where appropriate.

Production Quality, Security, and AI Operations

• Define and implement evaluation frameworks covering groundedness, relevance, completeness, task success, safety, latency, reliability, and cost.
• Create automated regression tests, representative evaluation datasets, and human-review processes for prompts, retrieval, tools, models, and agent behavior.
• Implement end-to-end tracing, prompt and configuration versioning, model and tool telemetry, cost monitoring, feedback capture, release controls, and rollback approaches.
• Apply AI-specific security controls for prompt injection, data leakage, unsafe tool execution, sensitive information, identity, authorization, auditability, and workload isolation.
• Embed Responsible AI, privacy, records management, compliance, and human oversight requirements throughout the solution lifecycle.

Technical Leadership and Adoption

• Act as a trusted technical advisor while remaining directly involved in prototyping, implementation decisions, and production problem-solving.
• Partner with business, product, architecture, data, security, platform, and engineering teams to deliver measurable value and sustainable adoption.
• Mentor architects and engineers, share implementation practices, and raise the unit’s capability to deliver governed AI solutions.
• Monitor emerging AI technologies and recommend adoption only where they provide clear business, engineering, or operational value.

• Demonstrated experience designing, developing, deploying, and supporting enterprise-scale Generative AI and/or Agentic AI solutions in production environments.
• Proven experience translating business requirements into scalable technology solutions and delivering measurable business outcomes.
• Experience working with enterprise data, APIs, applications, workflow platforms, and knowledge management systems.
• Strong software engineering skills, including Python development and modern software delivery practices.

• Demonstrated ability to communicate complex technical concepts and architectural decisions to both technical and non-technical stakeholders.
