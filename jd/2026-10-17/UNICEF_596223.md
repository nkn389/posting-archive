Organizational Context:

The Division of Global Communication and Advocacy (DGCA) works to place children and child rights at the heart of global, regional and national agendas. Through strategic advocacy, communication, engagement and brand leadership, DGCA strengthens UNICEF’s influence, mobilizes support and drives positive change for children worldwide. The Division provides leadership and support across headquarters, regional and country offices to amplify UNICEF’s voice and advance child rights, humanitarian action and sustainable development outcomes.

The Website, Editorial and Governance Section leads UNICEF’s global web editorial, platforms and digital governance functions. The Section stewards UNICEF’s digital voice and develops authoritative, audience-focused content for the global UNICEF website and other priority editorial products across languages. It works to strengthen UNICEF’s visibility, reach and engagement through editorial excellence, audience insights, analytics and evolving search and digital discoverability practices, while reinforcing content governance, brand integrity and UNICEF’s position as a trusted source of information on children’s rights. The Section also provides platforms, standards, guidance and capacity-building support to regional and country offices across the organization.

Purpose of the Job:

Under the general guidance of the Digital Communication Manager, the Web Editor is responsible for overseeing key sections of the UNICEF global website, including evergreen content related to programmes, institutional information and humanitarian action and emergencies, as well as other assigned thematic portfolios in line with annual priorities, with the goal of advancing and communicating UNICEF’s mandate and highlighting the impact of UNICEF’s work, as well as the voices of children and young people across the globe.

Responsibilities include writing, editing, producing and distributing compelling narratives, web features and stories for the general public, partners, opinion leaders, the media and a variety of key audiences, in line with the UNICEF Strategic Plan.

The incumbent will work to strengthen the discoverability, reach, engagement and performance of UNICEF web content across search engines and evolving AI-mediated discovery by applying audience insights, analytics and search best practices.

Key functions, accountabilities and related duties/tasks:

- Overall accountability

- Writing, editing and production of web content

- Global priorities and campaigns

- Web engagement, search discoverability and performance

- Web standards, best practices and capacity building
