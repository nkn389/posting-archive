Finance ERP Engineer

What we do

Since 1863, the International Committee of the Red Cross (ICRC) has worked to preserve human dignity and relieve suffering caused by war and armed violence.

In collaboration with our Red Cross and Red Crescent partners around the world, we do everything we can to deliver life-saving aid, reconnect families and locate missing people – helping those who need it most, regardless of who they are and what side of the front line they are on.

We engage with authorities and armed forces on all sides, often confidentially, pressing for access to detainees to improve their living conditions and urging compliance with international humanitarian law to protect non-combatants, including from digital threats.

Purpose

The Finance ERP Engineer ensures the smooth running of financial operations in accordance with the defined SLA and takes actions to correct in an independent manner.

S/he liaises with various stakeholders (product owners, team coach or external actors) to integrate and support solutions. S/he contributes to platforms' evolution.

Accountabilities & functional responsibilities

- Administers, maintains, and supports the technological landscape (Financial ERP and satellites), ensuring seamless integration within the ICRC application ecosystem

- Supporst the implementation of the relevant project or programs inside the product line.

- Performs and supports functional, integration and regression testing.

- Maintains technical documentation, system knowledge, and operational procedures.

- Performs regular system updates, patching, and maintenance to ensure security and reliability.

- Monitors the ERP environments for performance, security, and availability issues.

- Resolves assigned incidents and requests while ensuring resolution time commitments are met according to the service and operational level agreements (SLAs and OLAs).

- Ensures that the tickets are managed and followed pro-actively / contribute to the monitoring of all product tickets and requests proactively (and management of backlog).

- Provides round-the-clock product support, adhering to the procedures and shifts / on call schedules outlined in the staff regulations.

Professional experience required
