Introduction
Established in 1951, IOM is a Related Organization of the United Nations and the leading UN agency in the field of migration. Working closely with governmental, intergovernmental and non-governmental partners, IOM promotes humane and orderly migration for the benefit of all. It saves lives and protects people on the move, drives solutions to displacement, and facilitates pathways for regular migration, while providing services and advice to governments and migrants.
IOM is committed to fostering a respectful, inclusive and supportive workplace where all employees can thrive professionally and feel valued. By creating such an environment, IOM aims to better harness the full potential of migration and strengthen its support to people on the move.
IOM invites candidates from diverse backgrounds to apply and provides reasonable accommodation throughout the recruitment process when required. Learn more about IOM’s workplace culture at IOM workplace culture | International Organization for Migration

Project Context and Scope
The IOM Coordination Office for the South Pacific in Suva, Fiji supports programme development, resource mobilization, donor relations, and programme oversight across multiple Pacific Island Countries. As the Pacific portfolio continues to expand in areas including climate mobility, disaster risk reduction, labour mobility, border governance, protection, and humanitarian preparedness and response, additional surge support is required to strengthen project development and donor engagement efforts.
Under the overall supervision of the Head of Programme Support Unit and direct supervision of the Programme Development Officer, the consultant will support the Programme Support Unit in the development of concept notes, project proposals, resource mobilization, partnership engagement activities and programme information products. The consultant will work closely with programme teams and relevant IOM offices to ensure timely and high-quality project development processes. This consultancy will contribute to strengthening the Mission's project pipeline, resource mobilization, and programme quality assurance mechanisms across the South Pacific region.
