Introduction
Established in 1951, IOM is a Related Organization of the United Nations and the leading UN agency in the field of migration. Working closely with governmental, intergovernmental and non-governmental partners, IOM promotes humane and orderly migration for the benefit of all. It saves lives and protects people on the move, drives solutions to displacement, and facilitates pathways for regular migration, while providing services and advice to governments and migrants.
IOM is committed to fostering a respectful, inclusive and supportive workplace where all employees can thrive professionally and feel valued. By creating such an environment, IOM aims to better harness the full potential of migration and strengthen its support to people on the move.
IOM invites candidates from diverse backgrounds to apply and provides reasonable accommodation throughout the recruitment process when required. Learn more about IOM’s workplace culture at IOM workplace culture | International Organization for Migration

Project Context and Scope
Under the direct supervision of the MECR National Officer, technical guidance of the designated MECR Project Associates and coordination with the wider RE2CLID team, the District Coordinator will provide day-to-day field support, maintain regular engagement with district authorities and communities, monitor implementation and keep the RE2CLID District Dossier up to date.

Organizational Department / Unit to which the Consultant is contributing

Migration, Environment, Climate Change and Risk Reduction (MECR) / RE2CLID Programme

Required Qualifications and Experience

- University degree in development studies, environmental sciences, geography, disaster risk management, climate change, agriculture, natural-resource management, social sciences, public administration, community development or another relevant field, with at least two years of relevant professional experience; or
- Secondary education with at least four years of relevant professional experience.

Experience

- Work at district or community level in Mozambique, preferably in Sofala or Tete Province.
- Engagement with district Government authorities, community leadership, civil society organizations, or development and humanitarian actors.

- Ability to work with limited day-to-day supervision while maintaining close communication with a geographically dispersed team.

- Experience driving a motorbike and possession of a valid motorbike driving licence are advantages.

Tasks to be performed under this contract

District context and planning
• Maintain an up-to-date understanding of district institutions, plans, coordination mechanisms, projects, climate and disaster risks, displacement dynamics, livelihoods and natural-resource issues relevant to RE2CLID.
• Gather, review and compile information from Government institutions, partners, existing data and other relevant sources, and flag important gaps or developments.
• Maintain and regularly update the RE2CLID District Dossier and related mapping and planning tools.

Government, partner and community engagement
• Maintain regular contact with the District Administration Office, district technical services, partners and other relevant stakeholders.
• Participate in relevant district coordination mechanisms and support follow-up on agreed actions.
• Maintain an updated overview of actors, projects, Government priorities and plans, identifying complementarities, gaps and opportunities for collaboration.

Climate Ambassadors support
• Maintain regular communication with Climate Ambassadors assigned to RE2CLID target communities.
• Consolidate and summarize information and community feedback received from Climate Ambassadors.
• Flag operational or implementation issues to the Geographic Lead.

Implementation and monitoring
• Follow up on activities implemented directly by IOM and through Government counterparts, implementing partners, consultants, CTGs and other delivery actors.
• Monitor implementation progress through stakeholder engagement and field follow-up, reporting delays, challenges, risks and opportunities.
• Support district-level meetings, consultations, missions, assessments, training and other programme activities.
• Provide contextual information and practical support to RE2CLID staff, specialists and consultants working in the district.

Reporting and information management
• Maintain records of meetings, stakeholder engagements, field follow-up, project developments and agreed actions.
• Provide regular updates, concise field notes, meeting summaries and briefing inputs.
• Promptly escalate significant contextual, operational or stakeholder issues requiring action.
