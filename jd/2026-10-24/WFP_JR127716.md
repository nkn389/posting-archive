BUSINESS SUPPORT ASSOCIATE (COMMUNICATIONS)
Deadline for Application: 14 days from day of publication

The Office of the UN World Food Programme (WFP) Accra invites applications from suitable candidates to fill the vacancy below.

Job Title : Business Support Associate (Communications)
Duration : 1 year (Renewable)
Post Grade : SC 6
Duty Station : Accra

JOB PURPOSE:
To coordinate and control a full range of business support services for a specific professional area of work, to ensure that services are delivered
effectively.

MAJOR DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES
The Business Support Assistant (Communications) will be under the supervision of the Project Officer, Accra. The incumbent’s duties shall include but are not limited to the following:

- Provide dedicated administrative and executive support to the TTC team and management, including responding to queries and providing timely and accurate guidance to internal stakeholders.

- Prepare, consolidate and proofread reports, correspondence, briefing notes, presentations and other documentation, ensuring compliance with corporate standards and quality requirements.

- Collect, compile and perform basic analysis of information and data to support reporting, business information management, decision-making and project implementation.

- Maintain records, databases and filing systems to ensure information is accurate, organised, readily accessible and compliant with relevant policies and procedures.

- Assist in building and monitoring budgets and administrative expenditures to support the effective use of project resources.

- Identify opportunities to improve business processes and administrative procedures and contribute to the implementation of efficiency measures.

- Support the planning, coordination and delivery of TTC activities, including missions, trainings, meetings, workshops and events, by preparing administrative documentation, invitation letters and briefing materials, coordinating participants, and following up on travel and visa arrangements where required.

- Support the implementation of TTC communication and visibility activities, including the development, management, storage and distribution of communication, audio-visual and promotional materials, ensuring compliance with corporate branding, messaging and relevant policies.

- Support the planning and organisation of TTC promotional events, including activities related to the official inauguration of the centre.

- Perform any other duties and responsibilities as assigned by the supervisor

Minimum Academic Qualifications
Completion of secondary school education. A post-secondary certificate in the related functional area.

Desired Experiences for entry into the role:
1. The candidate should have six or more years of progressively responsible work experience in the relative business stream with experience in general administrative work.
2. Specialised knowledge of the application of common business processes, practical business support methods, procedures and systems used in area of work, gained through technical training and work experience.
3. Good communication skills in order to provide specialised business support services to a wide range of individuals.
4. Ability to analyse and interpret financial data and monitor budgets.
5. Ability to proactively identify and recommend areas for improvement to the design and delivery of specialised services.
6. Ability to build relationships with a variety of individuals across functions.
7. Experience in coordinating the work of others and self and training and supporting others.
8. Experience in preparing communications content (articles, video script etc)
9. Ability to maintain confidentiality.

Language:
Fluency in both oral and written communication in the UN language in use at the duty station and in the duty station’s language, if different.
