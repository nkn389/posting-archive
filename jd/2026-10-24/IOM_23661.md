The Manila Supply Chain Unit (MSCU), being part of Supply Chain Division of IOM, based at the Global Shared Services Centre in Manila, provides advisory, back-office, global warehouse operations and field procurement support services for IOM projects implemented worldwide. MSCU plays an important role by performing Procurement Process Review function before contracts are awarded, ensuring that IOM regulations are applied and adhered to in procurements conducted by Missions and Offices around the world. Among others, MSCU provides guidance and advice on IOM’s procurement policy, e.g. it assists missions in developing procurement strategies, reviewing solicitation documents, proposals and contracts, manage risks, etc.
This position is located within the Procurement Compliance and Training Team of MSCU. Under the overall supervision of the Senior Supply Chain and Procurement Officer, MSCU Head and the direct supervision of the National Procurement Officer (Team Leader), the Senior Supply Chain Associate will be responsible and accountable for conducting complex procurement compliance reviews, providing technical analyses and recommendations, supporting procurement risk management and quality assurance activities, and serving as a senior technical resource on procurement compliance matters to support the consistent application of IOM procurement policies and procedures globally

EDUCATION

- Bachelor’s degree in Business Administration, Logistics, Management or a related field from an accredited academic institution with five (5) years of relevant professional experience; or,
- High School diploma with seven (7) years of relevant professional experience;
- Certification in procurement, such as CIPS or an equivalent qualification is desirable.
Accredited Universities are those listed in the UNESCO World Higher Education Database.
EXPERIENCE

- A minimum of five years of progressively responsible experience in procurement, contract management, supply chain management, administrative services or related areas;

- Experience working with an enterprise resource planning (ERP) system in the area of purchasing and/or supply chain is an advantage.

- Team player; ability to work effectively with different levels within and outside the organisation and with colleagues from varied cultures and professional backgrounds.
