Organizational Context and Scope
The International Organization for Migration (IOM) is the leading inter-governmental organization in the field of migration and works closely with governmental, intergovernmental, and non-governmental partners. IOM is dedicated to promoting humane and orderly migration for the benefit of all and does so by providing services and advice to governments and migrants.
IOM is a major actor in post-conflict recovery and reconstruction, and as such is heavily involved in livelihoods development and job creation programmes. IOM’s strengths lie in its field presence around the country and operational capacity to rebuild community infrastructure, conduct vocational and other trainings, provide grant funding at local level to support business development, and support a variety of other areas key to recovery.
Under the overall supervision of Head of Livelihoods Technical team and the direct supervision of the National Project Officer – Livelihoods, the incumbent will be responsible for supporting programmes related to livelihoods portfolio under the area of responsibility. The post will be based in Anbar, Iraq, with travel/roving to various regions/governorates within Iraq as needed to support livelihoods activities and teams.
