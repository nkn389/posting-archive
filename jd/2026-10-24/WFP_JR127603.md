BACKGROUND AND PURPOSE OF THE ASSIGNMENT:
The United Nations World Food Programme is the world’s largest humanitarian agency fighting hunger worldwide. WFP's mission is to help the world achieve Zero Hunger in our lifetimes. Every day, WFP works globally to ensure that no child goes to bed hungry and that the poorest and most vulnerable—particularly women and children—have access to the nutritious food they need.

The food insecurity situation in Somalia remains fragile due to the combined effects of below-average and poorly distributed rainfall, limited household access to income opportunities, elevated and stagnant high food prices, ongoing insecurity, and conflict. WFP Somalia’s four-year Country Strategic Plan (CSP) 2022–2025 emphasizes flexible modalities of food assistance (e-vouchers and mobile money), conflict sensitivity, gender mainstreaming, and referral linkages across its programme activities. WFP Somalia is currently in the process of transition to its CSP for 2026–2030.

This position, based in Mogadishu, will provide robust food security analysis and strategic guidance to support both the implementation of the CSP. The incumbent will serve as the focal point for assessing and monitoring food security, informing preparedness and response activities, and providing oversight and technical advice on assistance planning and targeting. Within the Country Office VAM team, the incumbent will also lead the market and economic analysis pillar.

ACCOUNTABILITIES/RESPONSIBILITIES:

Under the supervision of the head of the Vulnerability Analysis and Mapping unit, the VAM officer will be responsible for delivering following outputs;

- Guide and supervise the relevant teams in food security assessments, survey design, sampling methodology and statistical data analysis.
- Strengthen the current WFP Somalia market and economic monitoring system, addressing gaps related to coverage, reporting and key market and economic monitoring indicators.
- Provide oversight and advice to the implementation of data driven beneficiary targeting, including application of statistical models and scoring algorithms for general food assistance programmes and review and update the targeting manuals, guidelines and SOPs.
- Provide technical assistance, trainings and capacity strengthening to improve the implementation of data driven targeting in Somalia.
- Manage cross-sectional, longitudinal food security assessments, experimental research and statistical analysis informing the food assistance policies, duration, targeting models and, algorithms.
- Update current data quality assurance systems and develop capacity and skills for national and area office data collection teams, including WFP partners and roll out the use of revised tools using WFP mobile data collection platforms such as MoDA (Mobile operational Data Acquisition).
- Run data quality checks and develop algorithms to automate data cleaning, data quality checks and reporting error detection.
- Manage and analyse market price database at the country level by ensuring data quality, integrating new parameters (commodities, markets, etc.), time series analysis and automating data collection and visualization using the Mobile Operational Data Acquisition (MODA) platform, Tableau applications or dashboards.
- Provide technical guidance, coordinate with technical agencies to monitor and update the Minimum Expenditure Basket (MEB) and adjust food/MPCA assistance transfer values as appropriately using household essential needs assessment findings and market monitoring data by applying quantitative analysis.
- In collaboration with the supply chain team, update SOPs for market price monitoring and enhance current Market Functionality Monitoring (MFI) and update food prices, biannual trends of exchange rates and fuel prices.
- In consultation with the head of VAM, plan capacity-strengthening training programmes for national staff at the country office level and area offices and provide coaching and guidance as required to improve the skills in markets, food security and economic monitoring.
- Participate and contribute to the Cash working group, food security working group and Operational Information Management (OIM).
- Participate and contribute to VAM food security assessments, including training programmes, IPC analysis workshops and drafting of reports and highlights.
- Participate and lead technical discussions on the establishment of single registration system for referrals and referral scoring system in Somalia.
- Collaborate with relevant entities, including key ministries, agencies and relevant stakeholders, to address issues relating to market, commodity flows, and price commodities for Somalia and the region, especially on cross-border trade monitoring.
