Job description

The Position:

The consultant will work closely with UNFPA Ukraine, the Youth Sectoral Working Group (SWG), relevant government and youth stakeholders, and the selected Implementing Partner (IP) to establish a practical, inclusive and sustainable model for the Youth Advisory Board (YAB). Consultant will report to the Deputy Representative of UNFPA Ukraine

The consultant will undertake the following:

1. YAB Participation, Governance and Inclusion Framework

- Develop the overall YAB Participation and Governance Framework (defining its purpose, added value, membership and tenure, roles and responsibilities, decision making and agenda setting, relationship with UNFPA, the Youth SWG, government and existing youth structures, wider youth engagement, safeguarding, confidentiality, accessibility, conflict of interest, communication, feedback and accountability mechanisms) based on the initial concept note

- Develop a practical Recruitment and Inclusion Framework to guide transparent and diverse selection, including targeted outreach to underrepresented groups such as IDPs, young veterans, young people with disabilities, youth from war affected communities, youth living outside Ukraine and young people not already connected to established NGO/youth networks.

- The consultant will provide technical guidance and quality assurance to the IP during recruitment and onboarding but will not directly select YAB members.

2. YAB Learning and Leadership Package

- Develop a structured YAB Learning and Leadership Package providing a 12 month learning pathway rather than one off training. This should cover youth leadership, policy engagement and advocacy, communication and facilitation, evidence-informed recommendations, meaningful participation and safeguarding and relevant UNFPA technical areas such as SRHR, GBV, MHPSS and gender equality.

- The package should also incorporate mentoring, peer learning, exposure to policy processes and selected national/international forums, recognition of learning and leadership and opportunities for continued engagement and alumni support.

- The consultant will work with the IP to translate the package into an achievable 12 months learning and engagement calendar.

3. Youth Participation, MEL and Accountability Framework

- Establish a practical Youth Participation,MEL Framework to assess not only participation numbers, but also the diversity, quality and influence of youth participation.

- The framework should track representation, quality of engagement, youth-led agenda setting, recommendations and responses, participation in policy processes, leadership and skills development, wider youth engagement, youth led initiatives, member experience and changes in institutional practice if any.

4. First-Year Implementation Roadmap and IP Delivery Framework

- Translate the above frameworks into a practical 1-year YAB Implementation Roadmap covering establishment, recruitment and onboarding, meetings and youth led agenda-setting, wider youth consultations, learning and mentoring, policy engagement, youth led initiatives, communications, feedback and MEL.

5. Technical Guidance, Establishment Support and Learning

- Provide technical guidance and quality assurance to UNFPA and the IP during the initial establishment and implementation period, including review of implementation plans, recruitment and outreach approaches, learning activities, participation tools and MEL data.

- Support the initial YAB orientation, participatory priority setting and development of its first workplan and ways of working.

- Conduct periodic learning reviews, identify inclusion or implementation gaps, recommend adaptations and document emerging good practices and lessons.

At the end of the consultancy, provide recommendations to enable UNFPA and the IP to sustain the mechanism without continued dependence on the consultant.

Academic/professional Qualification:

- Advanced university degree (Master's or equivalent) in social sciences, international development, public policy, youth development, public health, development studies, education, sociology, psychology, human rights or a related field

- Additional qualifications or demonstrated expertise in youth participation, youth leadership, programme design, monitoring and evaluation, or social inclusion would be an advantage.

Experience:

- At least 7 years of relevant professional experience in youth participation, youth development, youth leadership, social inclusion, policy engagement, programme design or a related field.

- Demonstrated experience in designing or strengthening youth advisory, youth leadership, youth participation or youth governance mechanisms.

- Demonstrated experience in developing participation frameworks, governance arrangements, selection processes or youth engagement strategies.

- Experience in developing monitoring, evaluation and learning frameworks, particularly for participation, empowerment or governance programmes.

- Demonstrated experience working with diverse and/or marginalised groups of young people.

- Experience working with government, UN agencies, international organisations, development partners or civil society organisations in Ukraine is strongly desirable.

- Knowledge of the Ukrainian youth policy and participation ecosystem, including youth councils, youth organisations, youth centres and other youth participation mechanisms, is highly desirable.

- Experience working in humanitarian, recovery or conflict-affected contexts is an advantage.
