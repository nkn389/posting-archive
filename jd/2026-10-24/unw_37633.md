Background:

UN Women, grounded in the vision of equality enshrined in the Charter of the United Nations, works for the elimination of discrimination against women and girls; the empowerment of women; and the achievement of equality between women and men as partners and beneficiaries of development, human rights, humanitarian action and peace and security.

Placing women’s rights at the center of all its efforts, UN Women leads and coordinates United Nations system efforts to ensure that commitments on gender equality and gender mainstreaming translate into action throughout the world. It provides strong and coherent leadership in support of Member States’ priorities and efforts, building effective partnerships with civil society and other relevant actors.

In Viet Nam, UN Women contributes to the development objectives of Viet Nam’s Socio-Economic Development Plan and the National Strategy for Gender Equality. Its Country Strategy Note for 2022-2026 is aligned to the UN Sustainable Development Cooperation Framework (CF) 2022-2026 and upcoming CF 2027-2031 and is focused on supporting Viet Nam’s fulfilment of Gender Equality commitments under The Convention on the Elimination of All Forms of Discrimination against Women (CEDAW), and the promise of the 2030 Sustainable Development Agenda to “leave no one behind”.

UN Women upcoming SN 2027-2030’s priorities are:

SN Outcome 1 (CF Outcome 2): By 2031, people in Viet Nam, especially those at risk of being left behind, are empowered to shape and benefit from a more inclusive, sustainable and gender-responsive economy driven by green transformation, industrialisation, innovation, productivity and decent work.

SN Outcome 2 (CF Outcome 4): By 2031, people in Viet Nam, especially those at risk of being left behind, benefit from and contribute to a more just, safe and inclusive society based on good governance, more responsive institutions, strengthened rule of law, and the protection of and respect for human rights, gender equality and freedom from all forms of violence and discrimination, in line with Viet Nam’s international commitments.

In Viet Nam, UN Women works with government institutions, non-governmental organizations, women’s organizations, development partners, United Nations agencies, academia, the media, youth networks and private sector partners to advance gender equality and women’s empowerment.

UN Women Viet Nam produces evidence and knowledge products to support policy dialogue, advocacy and evidence-based decision-making on gender equality. A key flagship knowledge product is the Country Gender Equality Profile (CGEP), which provides evidence and analysis on the situation of gender equality in Viet Nam and can support policy dialogue and advocacy with government and other stakeholders.

In parallel, the National Action Month on Gender Equality and Prevention and Response to Gender-Based Violence 2026 provides an important opportunity to strengthen public awareness, stakeholder engagement and coordinated advocacy on gender equality and ending violence against women and girls.

Against this background, UN Women Viet Nam seeks to recruit a National Consultant to Support CGEP Dissemination and National Action Month Coordination. The consultant will provide short-term, hands-on coordination and communications support to translate CGEP findings into accessible advocacy materials and support UN Women’s participation in and contribution to activities under the National Action Month.

Description of Responsibilities/ Scope of Work

The objective of the consultancy is to provide short-term implementation and coordination support for:

- dissemination and advocacy of the Country Gender Equality Profile (CGEP); and

- UN Women’s engagement in the National Action Month on Gender Equality and Prevention and Response to GBV.

Under the overall guidance of the UN Women Viet Nam Country Representative and direct supervision of the Communications and Advocacy Analyst, the consultant will undertake the following tasks.

Task 1. CGEP Dissemination and Advocacy

The consultant will:

- Develop and maintain a practical dissemination and advocacy plan for the CGEP based on UN Women’s approved priorities;

- Extract and package approved CGEP findings, data and recommendations into accessible communications and advocacy materials for different audiences;

- Draft and coordinate production of key messages, factsheets, presentations, social media content and partner dissemination materials;

- Support preparation and coordination of CGEP dissemination/launch activities, including invitations, participant coordination, event materials and follow-up;

- Coordinate inputs and comments from relevant UN Women colleagues and partners and facilitate revision and finalization of materials; and

- Track dissemination through UN Women and partner channels and consolidate available results and documentation.

Task 2. National Action Month Coordination and Communications

The consultant will:

- Develop and maintain an implementation and coordination plan for UN Women-supported activities under the National Action Month;

- Coordinate UN Women inputs and follow up with relevant partners and stakeholders on agreed activities and communication products;

- Draft and adapt key messages, social media content, event materials, invitations, talking points and other advocacy materials based on approved UN Women positions;

- Support localization and dissemination of relevant UN Women/UNiTE materials in the Viet Nam context;

- Support the organization and documentation of agreed events and public engagement activities;

- Maintain activity, partner and content trackers to monitor implementation;

- Collect and organize approved photographs, quotations, communication materials and relevant documentation from activities; and

- Prepare a concise results summary documenting activities, participation, dissemination results, lessons learned and follow-up actions.

Task 3. Coordination and Final Documentation

The consultant will:

- Maintain regular coordination with the Communications and Advocacy Analyst and relevant programme colleagues;

- Follow up on production and delivery of approved communications products and event materials;

- Maintain an organized electronic repository of final approved products and supporting documentation; and

- Prepare a final summary of outputs, dissemination results, lessons learned and recommendations for follow-up.
