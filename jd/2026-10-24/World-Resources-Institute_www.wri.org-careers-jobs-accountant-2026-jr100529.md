Job Highlight:

In this role, you will be responsible for executing the day-to-day accounting and bookkeeping functions for WRI Rwanda, ensuring accurate and timely recording of financial transactions in accordance with WRI internal controls, donor requirements, and applicable statutory and regulatory frameworks. You will support the full accounting cycle, including transaction processing, reconciliations, journal entries, and month-end close activities, while ensuring completeness, accuracy, and proper documentation of all financial records.
You will work closely with the Africa Finance and Accounting team, as well as Operations and Program teams, to support efficient financial operations, consistent application of policies and procedures, and effective coordination across systems and processes. You will also collaborate with the global accounting team to ensure alignment with organizational standards and reporting requirements.
You will report to WRI Rwanda Finance and Operations Manager.

What you will do:

Finance and Accounting Responsibilities (50%)

- Conduct the first level review of invoices submitted in Workday ERP to ensure they are adequately supported and coded correctly.
- Create invoices in Workday ERP for the Finance and Operations departments as may be required.
- Perform the initial review of accounting adjustments and journal entries submitted, including reclassifications, accruals, and other manual journals.
- Prepare and upload payroll, fringe benefit, and other accounting journal entries in the ERP system, ensuring completeness and accuracy of supporting documentation.
- Manage the supplier onboarding process for WRI Rwanda.
- Support internal, statutory and donor audits by preparing schedules and providing the required supporting documentation.
- Manage staff advances and receivables, ensuring timely reconciliation and follow-up.
- Support the monthly reconciliation of assigned general ledger accounts.
- Support the monthly bank statements reconciliation for WRI Rwanda Designated bank accounts
- Assist with monthly and year-end financial close processes.
- Maintain an organized and efficient archiving system for Finance and Accounting documents within SharePoint.
- Monitor WRI Rwanda's cash position, support the preparation of funding requests, and coordinate banking and cash management activities to ensure adequate liquidity for operational and program needs.

Payment Processing Responsibilities (40%)

- Process and settle vendor and sub awardees payments.
- Process staff advances and staff expense reimbursements in a timely and accurate manner.
- Collaborate closely with program teams to facilitate mobile money disbursements to participants.
- Ensure timely remittance of payroll related statutory deductions, including PAYE to RRA, Pension, Maternity and CBHI to RSSB and other applicable taxes such as withholding tax, to the relevant government authorities in accordance with statutory deadlines.

Payroll & Administration Support (10%)

- Support the WRI Rwanda Finance and Operations Manager in the preparation of monthly payroll, ensuring accuracy and compliance.
- Ensure the timely distribution of monthly pay slips to staff.
- Prepare and file all payroll related statutory returns in compliance with applicable regulations.
- Collaborate with HR to maintain up-to-date and accurate staff leave balances.
- Support the review, update, and implementation of financial policies and procedures.
- Work closely with the IT Team, Finance and Operations Manager, to maintain an accurate and up-to-date fixed asset register.
- Provide support to the Finance and operations Manager in procurement, logistics, and contract administration activities for the Finance and operations department.
- Provide guidance to both existing and new employees on time sheet completion and compliance requirements.
- Collaborate with program and core function staff to ensure timely submission and approval of timesheets.
- Perform any additional duties as may be assigned by the Finance and operations Manager

What you will need:

Education: You have completed a bachelor’s degree in business administration, Accounting, or Finance.

Professional Qualification: Possession of an accounting certification is an added advantage either CPA or ACCA.

Experience: You have 3+ years’ full-time relevant work experience in accounting and Finance Department, preferably in the NGO sector

- Experience applying accounting principles in the processing and recording of financial transactions
- Experience processing accounting transactions in an ERP environment
- Experience handling high volume transactional accounting work with accuracy and attention to detail.
- Proficiency in Microsoft Office applications, including Word, PowerPoint, and advanced Excel skills.

Languages: Languages Proficiency in written and spoken Kinyarwanda and English is required, with knowledge of French considered an added advantage.

Requirements: Existing work authorization is required where this position is based. WRI is unable to support visa applications.
