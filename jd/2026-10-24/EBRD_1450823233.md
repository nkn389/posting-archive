Help us maximise the value of our software investments while maintaining compliance across a global technology environment.

We are seeking an Associate, Software Asset Management (SAM) to support the management and optimisation of our enterprise software estate. This is an excellent opportunity for someone with a passion for technology, data analysis and governance who is looking to develop their expertise in Software Asset Management within a complex and dynamic organisation.

You will work closely with Technology, Procurement, Finance, Vendor Management and external software providers to ensure software assets are accurately managed, compliant and cost-effective throughout their lifecycle.

What you'll be doing

- Support Software Asset Management activities across the full software lifecycle, including software discovery, inventory management, licence allocation, entitlement management, compliance monitoring and optimisation.

- Perform software licence reconciliation and Effective Licence Position (ELP) analysis to identify compliance gaps, licensing risks, underutilised software and cost-saving opportunities.

- Maintain accurate and complete software asset records within ServiceNow SAM Pro, including software models, entitlements, contracts, licence metrics, publisher information and usage data.

- Support software publisher onboarding, software normalisation and the integration of entitlement and consumption data to improve the quality and reliability of SAM information.

- Assist with software licence renewals, vendor audits, compliance reviews and optimisation initiatives.

- Partner with Procurement, Contract & Vendor Management, Finance and Technology teams to support effective software governance and licence management.

- Contribute to dashboards, reporting, data quality improvements and process documentation that enable informed decision-making on software compliance, utilisation, costs and risk management.

What we're looking for

Must have

- Understanding of Software Asset Management (SAM), IT Asset Management (ITAM) principles and software asset lifecycle processes.

- Experience with ServiceNow SAM Pro or a comparable Software Asset Management platform.

- Knowledge of software licensing concepts, entitlement management, compliance controls and licence reconciliation.

- Familiarity with major software publishers and licensing models, such as Microsoft, Oracle, IBM, Adobe and other enterprise vendors.

- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to interpret inventory, entitlement, contract and usage data.

- Good Excel, reporting and data analysis skills, with the ability to generate meaningful insights and recommendations.

- Strong communication and stakeholder management skills, with the ability to collaborate effectively across technical and business teams.

Nice to have

- Experience supporting software audits, compliance assessments or optimisation programmes.

- Knowledge of ITIL practices, Agile ways of working and continuous improvement methodologies.

- Relevant ServiceNow, SAM, ITAM or ITIL certifications.

Why join us?

This role offers the opportunity to build expertise in one of the fastest-growing disciplines within IT governance and technology operations. You'll gain exposure to enterprise-scale software asset management, work with leading technology vendors and help drive optimisation initiatives that deliver measurable business value.
