Under the overall supervision of the Senior Programme Coordinator (DTM), the direct supervision of the Data Analysis and Visualisation Officer and in close coordination with the Database and Information Management Officer, the Head of Programmes and Programme Managers, the Senior Visualisation Associate – ACDMS will be responsible for supporting the development and maintenance of a unified dashboard ecosystem across IOM Afghanistan programmes, applying approved analytical, visualisation and information-management standards. The position will support the reduction of duplication and standardisation of visualisation approaches; develop and maintain ACDMS dashboards; and support the implementation and maintenance of approved Country Office (CO)-wide dashboard visual identity standards, in alignment with information products produced by Displacement Tracking Matirx (DTM) and the wider Information Management Unit.

ACDMS is a Country Office (CO)-wide institutional data management initiative intended to establish a centrally governed, secure, scalable and interoperable platform that harmonises programme data
standards and supports consistent data collection, validation, case and referral workflows, reporting, analysis, coordination, oversight and evidence-informed decision-making. The system is designed to reduce fragmentation and duplication, strengthen data quality and accountability, and enable programme-specific modules within a common architecture and governance framework.

EDUCATION
• Bachelor’s degree in Data Visualisation, Information Management, Statistics, Data Science, Computer Science, Information Systems, GIS, Economics or related field from an accredited academic institution with five years of relevant professional experience;
or
• High School diploma with seven years of relevant professional experience.

EXPERIENCE

• Experience with Power BI, dashboard development, data modelling, business intelligence, visualisation standards and information products;
• Experience translating programme requirements into dashboards and visualisation products, including quality assurance and maintenance;
• Experience with reporting automation and data integration is an advantage; and,
• Experience supporting humanitarian or development programmes is an advantage.
