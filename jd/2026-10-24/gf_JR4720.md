Advisor, Strategy and Policy - GL D - Defined Duration until December 2029

Reporting to the Manager, Strategy, Policy & Partnerships, the Advisor, Strategy & Policy supports the development and evolution of the Global Fund’s corporate Strategy and policy framework, helping ensure they are optimally positioned to maximize the impact of resources and build the sustainability of Global Fund-supported programs.

The Advisor works with senior members of the Department and Secretariat to develop and evolve relevant strategic areas of work, policy and core operational aspects of the Global Fund model. This involves developing strategic options and policy guidance, undertaking research, and developing evidence-based recommendations to inform the strategic and policy direction of the organization.

The Advisor also supports the Manager in other relevant functions or workstreams including horizon scanning and strategic intelligence, translating complex information into clear implications for organizational priorities and decision-making.

Key Responsibilities

Under the guidance of senior members of the Department, the Advisor will:

- Lead the proactive development of strategic positions and policy options to guide the evolution of the Global Fund’s work and implementation of its Strategy.
- Lead presentations and consultations to solicit and incorporate input from internal Secretariat and external stakeholders on operational model revisions and associated strategy and policy updates, synthesizing key conclusions, strategic implications, risks and mitigation measures.
- Conduct horizon scanning and strategic analysis on emerging global health, financing, geopolitical and other developments, translating evidence and external trends into implications and recommendations for the Global Fund’s strategic direction.
- Develop talking points, briefings, presentations, long-form policy papers and other materials to inform senior management decision-making on strategic and policy matters.
- Lead development of Secretariat position papers responding to independent audits, evaluations and reviews, including coordinating input from across the Secretariat and developing common agreed positions.
- Lead drafting of Committee and Board documents and presentations to support decision-making by the Global Fund’s governance bodies.
- Develop and maintain relationships with internal and external stakeholders to collaborate effectively, innovate and resolve challenges on joint areas of work.

Subject to change by the Executive Director at any time at their sole discretion.

Qualifications

Essential:
Advanced university degree (in public health, international development, development economics, public policy or a related field; or a combination of relevant academic/professional qualifications and experience.

Experience

Essential:

- Relevant work experience within an international management consultancy, public health or development organization, major multilateral or bilateral organization, not-for-profit or related setting.
- Proven writing skills, with experience distilling and synthesizing key strategic points from research, analysis, written input and extended discussions, and drafting longer papers to inform decision-making.
- Experience in the development of strategies and policies in global health.
- Experience developing high-quality presentations to communicate key strategic messages based on analysis or stakeholder input.
- Experience fostering relationships with internal and external stakeholders to collaborate, innovate, and resolve common challenges.
- Strong attention to detail.

Desirable:

- Over 4 years relevant work experience
- Demonstrated understanding of topics and innovations at the forefront of international development, particularly HIV, TB, Malaria, resilient and sustainable systems for health, equity, gender equality, human rights, sustainability and the global health architecture.
- Experience and familiarity with the Global Fund, its funding model and operations.
- Knowledge of the Global Fund Strategy
