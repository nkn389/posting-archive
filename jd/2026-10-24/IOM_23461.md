Under the overall supervision of the Head of Office (HoO) in Portugal and the direct supervision of the Senior Project Associate (G7), the Project Associate (G5) will support the implementation, coordination and monitoring of the PONTES project, a capacity-building initiative aimed at strengthening the preparedness of Portuguese institutions for the implementation of the European Pact on Migration and Asylum. The incumbent will contribute to the coordination, planning and delivery of project activities, liaise with project partners and stakeholders, support monitoring and reporting processes, and ensure the timely implementation of activities in line with IOM procedures, donor requirements and project objectives. The position will play a key role in fostering institutional cooperation and supporting capacity-building efforts related to asylum, reception, integration and fundamental rights safeguards.

EDUCATION

- Bachelor’s degree in Law, International Relations, Political Science, Sociology, Human Rights Studies, Public Administation or a related field from an accredited academic institution with three years of relevant professional experience; or,

- High School diploma with five years of relevant professional experience.

EXPERIENCE

- Experience in liaising with governmental authorities, national/international institutions, United Nations agencies and non-governmental organisations;

- Experience in working with migrants, refugees, internally displaced persons, victims of trafficking and other vulnerable groups; and,

- Prior work experience with international humanitarian organisations, non-government or government institutions/organisation in a multi-cultural setting is an advantage.

SKILLS

- Knowledge of the European Pact on Migration and Asylum and the Common European Asylum System (CEAS).

- Knowledge of international protection, refugee protection, and fundamental rights safeguards.

- Ability to analyse legal, policy, and operational documents and translate complex information into practical guidance, training content, and user-friendly materials.

- Experience in developing training materials, presentations, toolkits, manuals, and learning resources for diverse audiences.

- Experience in supporting the design and development of e-learning courses and digital learning content, including coordination with service providers and online learning platforms.

- Experience in coordinating capacity-building activities, workshops, and inter-institutional learning initiatives.

- Experience in project implementation, capacity-building, and stakeholder coordination.

- Strong analytical, drafting, monitoring, and reporting skills.

- Ability to liaise effectively with public authorities, civil society organizations, and international partners.

- Excellent communication and organizational skills.

- Proficiency in Microsoft Office applications.
