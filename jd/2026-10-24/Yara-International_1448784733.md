Environmental Technician (12 Month Fixed Term)

We at Yara are part of a global network, collaborating to profitably and responsibly solve some of the world's key challenges - resource scarcity, food insecurity and environmental change.
About the role

This 12-month fixed-term full-time role at Yara Pilbara Fertilisers reports to the Environmental Superintendent, and assists in environmental monitoring required for compliance with Yara Pilbara’s operational environmental licences and approvals.

The Environmental Technician supports the environment team in managing all aspects of environmental monitoring, sample handling, and associated data management.

The role is a residential Monday to Friday roster (with Rostered Days Off also available).

What you’ll do day-to-day

- Sample collection, preservation and handling - including groundwater, surface water and air

- Monitoring equipment calibration

- Logistics for sampling, sample handling and dispatching to laboratories

- Maintenance and downloading of loggers and deployed environmental monitoring equipment

- Laboratory certificate and result checks in ESDAT

Your work-life balance

- 5 weeks paid annual leave

- Optional RDO roster delivering 16 additional paid days off per year

- Residential role based in Karratha - no FIFO

- $46,000 per year Karratha housing allowance

- $35,000 per year regional location allowance

- $1,000 per year Health and Wellness allowance

- $3,000 per year utilities reimbursement (electricity/water)

- Relocation assistance

- Salary packaging and active Social Club

Why Yara?

About you

- Demonstrated experience in environmental monitoring and field work

- Familiar with environmental management and monitoring requirements of industries in Western Australia

- Bachelor’s degree in a related field (e.g. Environmental Science) or a Certificate IV in Environmental Monitoring and Technology, or equivalent (highly regarded)

- Senior First Aid Certificate desirable.

- 4X4 training desirable.

- Proficient in use of Microsoft Office

- Ability to work independently and also in a team.

- Fluent English (verbal and written) is essential.

About Yara Pilbara

Yara Pilbara is part of Yara International, a global crop nutrition company. The Pilbara site is one of the world’s largest ammonia production facilities, with downstream processing through the Yara Pilbara Nitrates Technical Ammonium Nitrate plant, a joint venture with Orica. Together with ENGIE, Yara Pilbara is also developing a renewable hydrogen plant to produce renewable ammonia.
