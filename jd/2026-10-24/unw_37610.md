Background:

UN Women, grounded in the vision of equality enshrined in the Charter of the United Nations, works for the elimination of discrimination against women and girls; the empowerment of women; and the achievement of equality between women and men as partners and beneficiaries of development, human rights, humanitarian action and peace and security.

UN Women has been providing continues support to partners in the South Caucasus to increase meaningful participation of women in peace and security processes. Under the current country program and in line with UN Sustainable Development Cooperation Framework (UNSDCF) 2026 – 2030 in Armenia, UN Women supports the Government of Armenia to develop and implement National Action Plans for the implementation of UN Security Council Resolutions on Women, Peace, and Security #1325 (2000) et al. and to achieve targets of the nationalized SDG 5 and 16.

UN Women in Armenia, with funding support from the UK Government, the Swedish International Development Cooperation Agency, and the Government of Denmark, is implementing initiatives aimed at enhancing women’s full, equal, direct and meaningful participation in peace and security processes and strengthening the capacities of state and non-state actors to advance the Women, Peace and Security (WPS) agenda in Armenia and fulfil commitments undertaken at the international, regional and national levels.

Reporting to the Programme Specialists based in Yerevan, Armenia, under the overall guidance of the UN Women Country Representative in Georgia and Liaison for the South Caucasus, the Programme Analyst contributes to the effective management and implementation of UN Women’s WPS interventions in Armenia by providing substantive technical support to programme design, implementation, monitoring, evaluation, and reporting.

The Programme Analyst will lead policy dialogue, advocacy, capacity development, and inter-agency coordination to advance the implementation of the WPS agenda in Armenia. The position develops and maintains strategic partnerships with government institutions, civil society organizations, academia, think tanks, development partners, donors, and the United Nations system, while contributing to resource mobilization and the expansion of the WPS work in Armenia.

Working in close collaboration with programme and operations teams in Armenia, the Georgia Country Office, and the Regional Office, the Programme Analyst ensures the effective delivery of the WPS portfolio in alignment with the UN Women Strategic Plan, the Armenia Biannual Work Plan, and national commitments under the WPS agenda.
