About the Group

The NDC Partnership
The NDC Partnership is a global coalition advancing the Paris Agreement by strengthening capacity and collaboration for country-owned climate and development action.
With over 270 members, including over 140 countries and more than 120 institutions, the NDC Partnership aligns partner support behind country priorities, builds shared knowledge and generates evidence to inform effective NDC implementation, sustained investment and higher ambition that drives sustainable development. Job Specific Context

The Kingdom of Eswatini is considered one of the most climatically vulnerable and exposed countries in the Southern African region. Some of the key climatic challenges include significant variations in precipitation patterns, higher temperatures, and increases in the frequency and intensity of severe weather events such as droughts, floods and cyclones. Consequently, the country has been facing increasing intensity and frequency of climate-induced disasters, in particular the sustained and pressing drought conditions, with direct impacts on rain-fed agriculture and other sectors of the economy. Climate change is considered a priority concern for Eswatini and all climate actions are undertaken within the context of the country’s national and regional development priorities, objectives and circumstances.
The country is well aware of its prevailing and future climate challenges and has identified agriculture and food security, water, tourism, and the ecosystem as the key vulnerable sectors. Over time, several key policy developments and institutional strengthening measures, such as, the National Climate Change Policy, the National Development Plan, the National Development Strategy and Nationally Determined Contributions have been developed and implemented through the ministries, departments and divisions. Similarly, the establishment of the multi-stakeholder National Climate Change Committee, National Disaster Management Authority, National Trust Commission and Eswatini Environmental Authority, along with the Ministry of Tourism and Environmental Affairs, are some of the noted institutions responsible for climate action in the country. It is noted that despite these considerable policy and institutional achievements, the country remains increasingly vulnerable and is not yet adequately prepared to face future challenges.

Eswatini's NDC 3.0 enhances the country's mitigation ambition by committing to an economy-wide reduction of 2.24 million tonnes of CO₂ equivalent (MtCO₂eq) by 2035 relative to the Business-as-Usual (BAU) scenario. Of this total, approximately 695 ktCO₂eq (31%) will be achieved through domestic resources, while the remaining 1.55 MtCO₂eq (69%) is conditional upon international support in the form of finance, technology transfer, investments, and capacity building. This represents a 115% increase in mitigation ambition compared to the emission reduction target contained in NDC 2.0.

Role Purpose

To provide technical advisory to the Kingdom of Eswatini to mobilise climate finance, strengthen the enabling environment and national capacity, and translate NDC 3.0 priorities into coordinated, bankable, and implementable programmes that enhance climate-resilient and low-carbon development pathways.

Functions / Key Results Expected

1. Climate Finance Mobilization and Investment Pipeline Development

- Lead the technical screening, appraisal, and prioritization of complex climate-resilient proposals, applying established multilateral fund criteria (e.g., GCF, GEF, AF) to ensure bankability and alignment with Eswatini's NDC and climate secor partners.
- Prepare and refine high-quality concept notes, project proposals, investment cases, and financing packages that mainstream gender, youth and health in consultation with ministries and government agencies for multilateral climate funds, development banks, bilateral partners, philanthropic organizations, and private investors.
- Coordinate bankable project documentation, including problem analysis, theory of change, results and monitoring frameworks, budgets, financing plans, implementation arrangements, environmental and social safeguards, gender and social-inclusion measures, and risk-management plans.
- Facilitate the development and regular update of a simplified Excel Roster of potential funding sources, donor and investor engagement plan, and resource-mobilization pipeline for priority sectoral interventions.

2. Strengthening climate change related policies, plans and strategies

- Provide operational and technical support to integrate climate change into national planning and budgeting processes through engagement with the Ministry of Economic Planning and Development, Ministry of Finance and sector ministries on climate-responsive planning.
- Provide technical expertise to adapt and operationalize existing national policy frameworks into the NDC Investment Plan, Climate Finance Strategies, and Long Term Low Emission Development Strategies in line with national priorities and established multilateral guidelines
- Provide technical support on the operationalization of Article 6 of the Paris Agreement in Eswatini.
- Coordinate the integration of the Climate finance landscape assessment into the Biennial Transparency Report (BTR).
- Provide inputs into policies, frameworks, strategies as requested by Government

3. Institutional Capacity Building and Knowledge Transfer

- Assess institutional capacity gaps and needs assessment in climate finance, investment planning, gender mainstreaming, health-climate nexus, project preparation, donor engagement, and NDC 3.0 implementation.
- Coordinate the design and implement a climate finance capacity building program incorporating gender mainstreaming and the health-climate nexus and based on the country plan.
- Coordinate the delivery of practical training for identified coaching needs, including one-to-one mentoring for Ministry staff, sectors, government agencies, and other implementing partners.
- Coordinate the development and institutionalizing training methodology and approaches on climate finance, project-development templates, investment-planning tools, guidance notes, standard operating procedures, and knowledge products that strengthen the quality and consistency of resource mobilization and programme implementation.
- Support information sharing and knowledge exchange with national and sub-national government, civil society and private sector stakeholders.
- Contribute to strengthening opportunities for sharing knowledge, experiences and best practices at national and global level and across local, regional and international partners.
- Strengthen internal arrangements for knowledge management, lesson capture, and sustained use of skills and tools.

4. Coordination, Partnerships, Advisory Support and Reporting

- Facilitate collaboration among ministries, government agencies, research institutions, development partners, civil-society organizations, banks and private-sector actors.
- Work closely with the NDC Partnership In-Country Facilitator and the GCF liaison officer to ensure alignment, coordination, and follow-up on support provided to the Ministry.
- Facilitate technical working groups, investment consultations, project-design workshops, capacity building workshops and partner dialogues on climate-resilience development.
- Provide support to Eswatini in its role in hosting the SADC Regional Transparency Hub
- Produce and submit monthly and quarterly plan and progress reports and submit to the Ministry of Finance Focal Point for feedback and approval;
- Provide inputs to the preparation of presentations, advisory and briefing notes and/or position papers for the UNFCCC Conference of Parties as needed.
- Undertake other related strategic or technical tasks consistent with the assignment
