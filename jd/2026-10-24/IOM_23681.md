Organizational Context and Scope:
Established in 1951, IOM is a Related Organization of the United Nations, and as the leading UN agency in the field of migration, works closely with governmental, intergovernmental and non-governmental partners. IOM is dedicated to promoting humane and orderly migration for the benefit of all. It does so by providing services and advice to governments and migrants.
Under the overall supervision of the Senior Resource Management Officer and direct supervision of the Senior Information Technology Associate in the mission, in close coordination with the Information and Communications Technology (ICT) Division Central Team, the IT Associate will be responsible for the following functions.

Required Qualifications and Experience
Education

- University degree in Computer Science, Information Management Systems, or a related field from an accredited academic institution with minimum of two (2) years of relevant professional experience, or,
- High School diploma with four (4) years of relevant professional experience
- Professional Certifications in Microsoft and Cisco would be a distinct advantage.

Accredited Universities are those listed in the UNESCO World Higher Education Database.
Experience

- Experience and wide-ranging knowledge in the Information Management and Communications Technology, including:

- Implementation of IT Infrastructure solutions in a multi-site environment, including implementation and support of medium to large-scale WAN/LAN technologies, and on-premises and/or cloud-based servers, resources, and applications.

- Support and troubleshooting of ICT equipment hardware and software, including corporate and commercial applications from multiple platforms and operating systems.

- Experience in Identity lifecycle is an advantage

- IT security implementation including MFA, RADIUS and standard security policy

- Transmission Control Protocol/Internet Protocol (TCP/IP) networking, firewall, router, switch, Virtual Private Networking (VPN), network security, and other telecommunications technologies.

- Windows server platforms, where an understanding of Active Directory domain environment is a distinct advantage.

- IT Service Asset and Configuration Management, including monitoring and inventory applications and processes.

- SharePoint and related Office 365 Technologies, including automation and application

- Knowledge of the latest technological developments in Information Technology and Information Systems.

- Prior work experience with IOM or the UN System is an advantage.

Skills

- Relevant technical knowledge related to the daily operation and troubleshooting of WAN/LAN network equipment, devices and services, to ensure corporate connectivity, monitoring and highest uptime.

- Ability to work in a fast-paced multi-location environment including managing across multiple teams, functions and organizations often outside 'standard' working hours, leveraging on-premises and cloud computing solutions and systems.

- Effective resource management skills, strategic and creative thinking.

- Strong proficiency with end-user-level Microsoft Office 365 application and collaboration tools (in particular: Word, Excel, PowerPoint, MS Project, Visio, Teams, SharePoint, Onedrive).

- Ability to prioritize tasks and strong organizational skills.

- Effective problem-solving approach, ability to work in a cross-divisional team and ability to quickly understand IOM business processes.

Languages

- For all Applicants, fluency in English is required (oral and written).
- Working knowledge of Pashto or Dari is required.
- Fluency in all national language(s) are highly advantages.
Proficiency of language(s) required will be specifically evaluated during the selection process, which may include written and/or oral assessments.
