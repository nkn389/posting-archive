Introduction

Working closely with governmental, intergovernmental and non-governmental partners, IOM promotes humane and orderly migration for the benefit of all. Core Functions / Responsibilities:

- Assist in the overall management of the warehouse by receiving the goods, updating the warehouse records in the database, and dispatching the goods for operations in Kabul;

- Receive goods from vendors/transporters and inspect the quantity/quality to verify they all meet the same specifications and standards ordered;

- Maintain the warehouse always clean and wear the proper warehouse costume and personal protection equipment’s /Costume.

- Support the monitoring of loading/offloading activities at the warehouse whenever taking place and of the hired labour workers to cover the job properly.

- Verify documents received from the vendors related to goods received and services provided at the warehouse with Procurement and Logistics colleagues before payment is released.

- Assist in issuing the “Receiving Report Forms/Goods Receive Note” for the new arrival items into the warehouse and for issuing the “Stock Release Authorization Form/Waybill” when releasing items from the warehouse;Conduct physical inventory of items in the warehouse and provide updates on the inventory records upon completing each activity;

- Keep the entire warehouse facility well maintained, including ensuring that goods are stacked, stored and updating the stock cards where necessary;

- Prepare bi-weekly Inventory report to be shared with the Management and concerned Program Managers;

- Maintain security and safety measures within the warehouse premises in coordination with the supervisor and Security unit;

- Obtain documents with proper authorization/approvals from designated officer in case of releasing/issuing any of the warehouse items to any individual and/or unit within the mission;

- Send daily reports to Senior Warehouse Assistant in Kabul and make sure the inventory list is updated whenever there’s any movement

- Perform any other duties that may be assigned.
