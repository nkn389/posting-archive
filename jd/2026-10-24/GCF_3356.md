About the Green Climate Fund (GCF)
Set up in 2010 by the 194 countries who are Parties to the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change (UNFCCC), GCF takes its responsibility as the world’s largest dedicated multilateral climate fund to heart. GCF funds transformative climate projects across the developing world, supporting countries in their responses and actions to tackle climate change while fostering sustainable development within their communities.
Our country-driven approach ensures that our actions are tailored to the unique circumstances and national aspirations of each nation we support. Besides channeling public and private sector investment through a broad range of financing instruments, GCF builds the capacity of developing countries to take climate action through an extensive Readiness Programme ensuring countries are increasingly well-equipped and financed to manage their climate futures.
Headquartered in the Republic of Korea, GCF operates with a diverse and talented workforce of over 300 people.
Department Introduction
The Department of Investment Services (DINVS) supports developing countries and their partner institutions in accessing and effectively using GCF resources. The Department brings together GCF’s accreditation, Readiness and Preparatory Support Programme, and Project Preparation Facility functions to provide coordinated support across the pathway from institutional capacity strengthening and accreditation to project preparation. Working closely with regional departments and other relevant teams across the Secretariat, DINVS promotes coherent, country-owned and fit-for-purpose support that strengthens partner capabilities, facilitates access to climate finance and enables the development of high-quality climate investments.
Role Summary
The Pre-Accreditation Advisory and Capacity Services Officer supports the implementation of the Revised Accreditation Framework (RAF) by contributing to targeted, practical and application-focused advisory and capacity support to Direct Access Entity applicants during the accreditation review process. The staff member will support the Accreditation and Entity Relations Unit in line with their respective roles in relation to identifying accreditation-specific gaps, improving the completeness, quality and coherence of applicants’ submissions, and strengthening accreditation application for Secretariat and Accreditation Panel review, without implying endorsement of any accreditation outcome or affecting the independence of the Accreditation Panel.
Duties and Responsibilities

- Coordinate team members’ analysis of accreditation applications, screening requirements, Secretariat observations and Accreditation Panel feedback to identify clearly defined, accreditation-specific gaps that can be addressed through short-duration technical assistance.
- Support the prioritization and triage of Direct Access Entity applicants aligned with the Revised Accreditation Framework priorities, including applicants from underrepresented countries and regions, entities supporting locally led climate action, and other priority categories identified by GCF.
- Interact with Direct Access Entity applicants and support the coordination of expert staff and consultants to provide rapid, practical and application-focused advisory support on discrete gaps related to fiduciary standards, governance, environmental and social safeguards, gender, integrity, legal status or supporting documentation.
- Collaborate with colleagues across divisions and offices, including regional departments, legal, risk, compliance, safeguards, gender and readiness colleagues, to ensure effective support is coordinated, complementary to the Readiness and Preparatory Support Programme, and delivered in a timely and consistent manner.
- Support the coordination of targeted technical assistance through standardized but customizable support modules, including gap confirmation, documentation review, hands-on drafting and revision support, applicant validation sessions, and preparedness-for-review handoffs.
- Support the maintenance of records, lessons learned, tools and knowledge products arising from pre-accreditation advisory support to improve predictability, efficiency, fairness and consistency in RAF implementation, and perform other related duties as assigned.
