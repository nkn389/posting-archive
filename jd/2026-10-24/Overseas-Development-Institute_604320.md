Description

Purpose of Job

ODI Global is internationally recognised for its innovative and policy-relevant work supporting climate action in multilateral arenas. We advise multiple countries in the climate negotiations on topics including finance, gender, trade, adaptation, and loss and damage. We have supported three successive G20 Presidencies on raising and steering finance for climate action. We work with like-minded countries in diverse forums, ranging from expert groups such as the Expert Review on Debt, Nature and Climate to sovereign coalitions such as the members of the Coalition for Climate Action in Conflict and Fragile Settings.

We are now recruiting a Senior Research Officer with experience in multilateral, climate-relevant processes to help deliver our broad portfolio of work in this space. The successful candidate will contribute to developing, fundraising for, conducting and managing policy relevant, high-quality and innovative research relating to sustainable finance. They will help produce policy advice and undertake public affairs programmes, engaging with decision-makers and disseminating results more widely.

Main Duties and Responsibilities
Research and analysis:

- Specific technical inputs to ongoing research projects such as literature reviews, data collection and data analysis;

- Supporting research project activities or leading parts of research projects including field work, conducting interviews, holding workshops, producing written outputs and preparing reports for donors, under the supervision of a Research Fellow.

- Authoring or co-authoring research reports, policy briefs and other documents for publication

- Undertake some travel overseas for research related purposes

Business development:

- Contribute to the development of expressions of interest and full project proposals;

Project Management:
Under the overall direction of a Group Director or senior researcher:

- Liaising with and reporting to donors, managing budgets and consultants and the inputs of other project partners;

- Supervising interns and other short-term staff working within the team under the direction of a senior researcher.

Policy advice, public affairs and dissemination:

- Supporting the production of communications products from research findings;

- Organising and presenting key findings to key decision-makers and stakeholders, through events and meetings;

- Disseminating research results through ODI web pages, the media, conferences, etc;

- Liaison and networking with a wide range of agencies including attending meetings on behalf of Programme and providing short meeting summaries for distribution within the team and more widely

Collegiate life:
Contribute to the Institute’s collegiate life through:

- ODI publications and journals;

- ODI activities as discussion groups, staff meetings, working groups, interviews, research away days; and

- Intellectual exchanges with colleagues.
