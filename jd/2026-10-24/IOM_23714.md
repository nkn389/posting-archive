Project Context and Scope
The International Organization for Migration (IOM), in cooperation with Asian Development Bank (ADB) and the governments of Uzbekistan and Tajikistan, is implementing the project “Cross-border Health Security Partnership between the Republic of Tajikistan and the Republic of Uzbekistan”, launched in September 2026 under the ADB Technical Assistance entitled: Addressing Health Threats in Central Asia Regional Economic Cooperation Countries and the Caucasus. The project responds to persistent public health risks, including insufficient coordination between countries, gaps in disease surveillance and data exchange, and limited understanding of the health vulnerabilities of migrants and mobile populations. The proposed project foresees activities along the economic corridor Khujand-Tashkent, more specifically in the area of the Points of Entry "Fotehobod", district Mastchoh (Tajikistan) and "Oybek", district Bekabad (Uzbekistan).
Under the project, the health needs assessment of vulnerable groups and mobile populations (including migrant workers) in border communities near these Points of Entry is envisioned. Topics to cover are health profiles (including self-reported morbidity in particular chronic diseases) and socioeconomic status including mobility, barriers to receiving health services, health needs, the impact of infectious disease (chronic and/or outbreaks) on livelihoods and human mobility dimensions of communicable disease, and sex-differentiated effects of chronic infectious disease and/or outbreaks. The assessment will generate evidence on the health needs, vulnerabilities, barriers to access, and service gaps affecting vulnerable groups and mobile populations, including migrant workers, in border communities. The findings will help identify priority areas for intervention and inform practical measures to strengthen mobility-sensitive and migrant-inclusive primary health care services, improve community-based communicable disease prevention and response, and enhance access to appropriate health services for migrants, mobile populations, and host communities.

Objective

The overall objective of this assignment is to conduct a mixed-method (qualitative and quantitative) needs assessment of vulnerable groups and mobile populations, including migrant workers, in selected border communities of Tajikistan / Uzbekistan in order to generate evidence on their health needs, vulnerabilities, health-related risks, barriers to accessing health services, and priority concerns.

Tasks to be performed under this contract
Under the overall supervision of IOM Uzbekistan and IOM Tajikistan and in coordination with the international consultant, the National Consultant will perform the following tasks:

- Review, support the contextualization and translation (to Uzbek/Tajik/Russian) of the mixed-method assessment methodology and data collection tools, including the assessment scope and indicators, sampling approach, data collection framework, data quality assurance procedures, analytical methodology, structured questionnaires for border communities and mobile populations, in cooperation with international consultant and in consultation with IOM and relevant stakeholders.

- In cooperation with the Bilateral Task Force Group (BTFG), conduct stock-taking and desk review of border health and cross-border assessments, and map and identify key border communities and key vulnerable groups along the mobility corridor between the Points of Entry "Fotehobod" and "Oybek".

- Conduct a capacity development exercise on the data collection tools and the use of KoboToolbox for the BTFG;

- Support the planning and organization of field activities, including identification of selected border communities, consultation and coordination with relevant authorities such as primary health care, customs, border, veterinary or phytosanitary authorities, local NGOs, community leaders, in the selected border areas and along mobility corridors.

- Conduct an assessment using the agreed methodology and tools, including KoboToolbox, comprising of interviews, focus group discussions and consultations with relevant authorities, vulnerable and mobile populations in selected border communities and along mobility corridors.

- Clean, organize and validate quantitative and qualitative data collected through the assessment, identify data gaps or inconsistencies, and provide validated data to the International Consultant for further analysis.

- Prepare a field assessment report summarizing field activities, key observations, preliminary findings, challenges and country-specific issues identified during the assessment and provide inputs to the International Consultant for preparation of the overall assessment report.

- Participate in coordination and technical meetings with IOM, the International Consultant, government counterparts and other relevant stakeholders, and provide periodic progress updates throughout the assignment and present preliminary and final assessment findings, recommendations and priority actions as required.

- Develop a joint strategy on reducing health risks among the most vulnerable populations in border areas (women, older persons, children, persons with disability, and migrant and mobile populations) from chronic diseases and/or infectious disease outbreaks and incorporate inputs of the BTFG and international consultant

Deliverables

- First deliverable: Completed field assessment report, summarizing field activities, key observations, preliminary findings, challenges and country-specific issues identified during the assessment and provide inputs to the International Consultant for preparation of the overall assessment report. 10 days, deliverable by 20 November 2026.

- Second deliverable: Draft health needs assessment report, including analysis of findings, identified gaps and bottlenecks, preliminary recommendations and priority actions, prepared in coordination with the International Consultant - First instalment payment - 5 days, deliverable by 25 December 2026.

- Third deliverable: Finalized health needs assessment report, prepared in coordination with the International Consultant, including final recommendations and prioritized actions and a joint strategy on reducing health risks among the most vulnerable populations in border areas. - Second and final instalment payment - 5 days, deliverable by 15 March 2027.
