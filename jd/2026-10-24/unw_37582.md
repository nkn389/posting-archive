Background:

UN Women, grounded in the vision of equality enshrined in the Charter of the United Nations, works for the elimination of discrimination against women and girls; the empowerment of women; and the achievement of equality between women and men as partners and beneficiaries of development, human rights, humanitarian action and peace and security.

The UN Women South Africa Country Office (SACO) is based in Pretoria, South Africa. The Country Office collaborates with the government, Civil Society Organizations (CSOs), and the Private Sector to contribute to gender equality by formulating and implementing norms, laws, policies, programs, and services that ensure the human rights of women and girls, as well as increase their economic participation. SACO’s responsive programs include increasing women’s political participation and leadership, securing women’s economic rights, strengthening women's involvement in peacebuilding, ending violence against women, and promoting gender-transformative policies in government budgets.

The intensity and the scope of the work undertaken by UN Women South Africa Country Office, requires a high-functioning and resilient team, clear on the mandate, effective and professional in communicating, focusing on achieving office vision, and delivering on the programme, projects targeted results and joint initiatives.

UN Women South Africa Country Office seeks to hire an experienced consultant to support the objectives of the planned staff retreat as outlined below through well-structured discussions and engaging activities. Reporting to the UN Women Country Representative with dotted line supervisory to the Deputy Representative and Operations Manager South Africa Country Office the retreat consultant will deliver on the following objectives.

Objectives of the retreat:

- Building from previous retreats, continue to support the team in strengthening team building spirit and foster the bond across teams toward a more coordinated, peaceful, and productive working culture;

- To define major priorities and activities for the period 2026-2027

- To use tools that promote understanding, motivation, and teamwork, ultimately enabling us to effectively meet our targets.;

- Examining the SACO strengths, challenges, best practices, and takeaways from its accomplishments in prior years and identifying areas for improvement and, explore innovative approaches and potential solutions to address the identified challenges;

- Support the team to have constructive dialogue on vision, priorities, systems and dynamics that shape UN WOMEN’s work in the changing context of our work;

- Identify and discuss challenges and opportunities for Country Office

- To ensure an inclusive process and full ownership of the retreat agenda and outcomes, the planning team sought inputs from all participants for collective leadership and ownership of a successful planning and conduct of the retreat;

- To explore new ideas, innovative approaches and make suggestions that can strengthen accountability and enhance the office's performance and efficiency.

Scope of Work:

Under the direct supervision of the Senior Management in South Africa Country Office, the retreat facilitator is expected to facilitate and moderate the session. The session should be very engaging, participatory, and creatively organized in a culturally sensitive manner with the aim of helping staff reflect on the previous joint discussions with all staff such as all staff retreat held earlier and forge strategies for the following/upcoming year.

Duties and Responsibilities:

- In collaboration with the retreat planning teams, finalize the methodology, agenda incorporating feedback from previous staff retreats/meetings to ensure that issues raised previously are addressed.

- Facilitate and moderate key sessions of the retreats, ensuring maximum participation of the participants and the achievement of the retreats objectives and expected results.

- Facilitate team building activities during the retreats and conduct daily reflections highlighting day key discussions.

- In consultations with the retreat planning team, produce retreats reports that will highlight daily deliberations and key actions points.

- Clarify expectations regarding participant feedback, self-assessment, and any post-retreat evaluations.

- Fostering a positive and inclusive environment.
