Job Purpose:

EmpowerED is UNFPA's global initiative to expand universal access to comprehensive sexuality education (CSE) for adolescents and youth. The programme seeks to enhance their sexual and reproducti e health and rights (SRHR) and ensure they live a life free from violence and is implemented in Ghana in alignment with the UNFPA Ghana 8th Country Programme Document (2023 to 2027).

Ghana has a vibrant ecosystem of youth-led and adolescent girl-led organisations, networks, associations, digital influencers and content creators engaged in reproductive health education (RHE) advocacy and in wider youth development work, including climate and environmental action, agriculture and agribusiness, peace and security, governance and entrepreneurship. This ecosystem, however, remains largely informal and undocumented. Existing data on these actors tends to be localised or project specific, limiting the programme's ability to build structured, sustainable partnerships with them.

A mapping exercise is therefore required to identify and profile these actors across two tiers of regions: the EmpowerED programme regions of Greater Accra, Oti and Bono East (primary), and the other UNFPA Ghana programme regions of Eastern, Ashanti, North East and Volta (secondary). The mapping will assess the thematic depth, funding sustainability and capacity gaps of SRHR actors to inform a structured partnership model under the EmpowerED framework. It will also produce a comprehensive database that can serve as a reference for the National Youth Authority (NYA), AfriYAN Ghana and the Ghana Youth Federation in further engagement and the advancement of youth empowerment.

You would be responsible for:

- Review the EmpowerED baseline findings and relevant national reproductive health planning documents, including the Ghana Family Planning Costed Implementation Plan 2024 to 2030 and the National Strategic Framework on Adolescent Sexual and Reproductive Health.

- Develop and deploy a digital data collection tool to identify and map youth networks, organisations, associations, girl-led and women-led groups, content creators and influencers working on SRHR and RHE, as well as on climate and environmental action, agriculture and agribusiness, youth, peace and security, youth in governance and youth entrepreneurship, in the primary regions (Greater Accra, Bono East and Oti) and secondary regions (Eastern, Ashanti, North East and Volta).

- Work with the NYA Regional Directors in each of the seven regions to obtain and verify records of youth groups, organisations, networks and associations known to and registered with the Authority, and ensure that these are captured in the data collection and cross-referenced in the final database.

- Conduct an audit of existing interventions delivered by mapped SRHR actors, including out of school RHE delivery, digital campaigns and peer to peer counselling, and assess the credibility and rights-based grounding of digital content creators and influencers advocating on SRHR issues.

- Review the fmancial landscape of mapped organisations, including sources and sustainability of financing, and evaluate their technical and operational capacity gaps, including in monitoring and evaluation, financial management and RHE technical accuracy, to inform the design ofEmpowerED training modules.

- Conduct regional consultations, in collaboration with NYA regional offices and AfriYAN, to validate emerging findings with stakeholders.

- Prepare a final mapping report, comprising a structured database of youth-led actors disaggregated by region and thematic area, a sustainability assessment identifying organisations best positioned for longer-term partnership with UNFPA, and strategic recommendations for strengthening localised RHE advocacy and wider youth empowennent and engagement, with particular attention to the Greater Accra, Oti and Bono East regions. A shareable version of the database, prepared in line with data protection principles, will also be submitted for use by UNFPA, the NYA, AfriYAN Ghana and the Ghana Youth Federation.
