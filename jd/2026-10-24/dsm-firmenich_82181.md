Job title: Senior Specialist Regulatory Affairs Raw Materials
Location: Barcelona, Spain (hybrid)

Join our Raw Materials Regulatory Affairs Team, where we leverage technology and regulatory expertise to transform complex regulatory information into meaningful, actionable data. In this key role, you will contribute to global regulatory compliance assessments of Taste raw materials and suppliers through documentation review, evaluation, and the accurate encoding and maintenance of regulatory information in our systems. Your work will play a vital role in ensuring data integrity, enabling informed business decisions, and supporting the development of compliant products across Taste, Texture and Health. Be part of a team that drives regulatory excellence and enables business success!

Your key responsibilities

- Evaluate regulatory compliance and qualify suppliers for Taste raw materials in alignment with applicable legislation, raw material specifications, business requirements, and company standards, supporting procurement and business continuity.
- Conduct comprehensive regulatory and specification assessments of new Taste raw materials to support their introduction into the raw material portfolio.
- Review and interpret supplier documentation to assess regulatory compliance, identify potential risks or gaps, and support robust regulatory decisions.
- Encode and maintain accurate regulatory information in our system, ensuring data integrity and reliability to support global compliance assessments and business operations.
- Support internal stakeholders by addressing regulatory queries related to raw materials and providing guidance on regulatory requirements, qualification status, and relevant systems.
- Act as an advanced user and subject-matter reference for regulatory systems, supporting their effective use and contributing to the resolution of data and system-related issues.
- Lead and contribute to cross-functional projects and continuous improvement initiatives involving raw materials, regulatory affairs, systems, and validation processes.

We bring

- The opportunity to develop deep expertise in raw materials within a leading global Taste business, gaining exposure to a broad and diverse raw material portfolio, complex regulatory requirements, and cross-functional collaboration within a critical global team.

- A rich history and a promising future of bold scientific innovation and passionate creation with our customers;
- A space to grow by encouraging and supporting curiosity and an open mindset;
- A culture that prioritizes safety and well-being, both physically and mentally;
- A flexible work environment that empowers people to take accountability for their work and own the outcome;
- A firm belief that working together with our customers is the key to achieving great things;
- An eagerness to be one team and learn from each other to bring progress to life and create a better future;

You bring

- Bachelor’s degree in Chemistry, Biology, Food Science, Chemical Engineering, or a related scientific or regulatory field. A master’s degree is a plus.
- Minimum of 3 years in Regulatory Affairs, Quality, or a related field, preferably within the food, flavour, or chemical industry, with knowledge of applicable regulations, supplier documentation, and regulatory data management.
- Ability to synthesize complex information, communicate clearly to technical and non-technical audiences, and break down complex projects into actionable steps.
- Strong attention to detail and a proactive, solution-oriented approach, with the ability to prioritize tasks, follow through on actions, and seek guidance when needed.
- Fluency in written and spoken English. Additional languages (e.g., Chinese, Arabic…) are an asset.
