Purpose of Job

The Analyst will support the deployment of financial guarantee instruments with the Financial Institutions Business Group’s (FIBG) partner financial institutions (PFIs) across the EBRD countries of operation. The role will focus on implementing the internal framework for guarantee programme delivery, compliance and monitoring, and on supporting the associated technical co-operation (TC) programmes. The role will particularly focus on the operationalisation and implementation of portfolio risk sharing instruments, including donor-supported guarantee programmes. The Analyst will also contribute to other designated donor-supported programmes and FI PDI operations, particularly those involving guarantees.

Accountabilities & Responsibilities

Programme structuring, development and implementation:

- Assist FI PDI Guarantees Lead and Programme Coordinators or other designated staff of FIBG in establishing the appropriate business process flow necessary for operationalisation of the guarantee programmes based on the guidelines for FI bankers and PFIs in relation to structuring, implementation, monitoring and reporting of projects under the guarantee programmes.

- Support FI PDI Guarantees Lead or other designated staff of FIBG in setting up a dedicated programmes tracker and database containing guarantee programmes’ related information for internal guidance and information and keep it updated.

- Proactively engage in developing IT tools, forms and templates to facilitate successful implementation of the Programmes, with particular focus on the optimisation of PRS reporting templates across guarantee programmes.

- Assist Programme Coordinators with the design and review of the quarterly PRS sub-loan reports.

- Support Programme Coordinators with the structuring, launch and implementation of the related TC projects.

- Coordinate with OLs, PFIs, project consultants, internal teams as needed to ensure efficient and high-quality administration of guarantee claims and payments, monitoring and reporting.

- Assist Programme Coordinators and OLs with review of internal and external approval and legal documents, including incorporation of donor (and other relevant) pass-through provisions in agreements.

Coordination, reporting and visibility:

- Support Programme Coordinators on compliance with EBRD’s contractual obligations to the donors (and other counterparts) including with drafting of quarterly and annual reports to the donors and presentation of progress results internally and externally.

- Co-ordinate EU (and other) donor compliance issues alongside EBRD’s Donor Partnerships department, working in close coordination with the FI banking teams.

- Ensure coordination with other relevant teams for Programme implementation, monitoring, and reporting (Office of the General Counsel, Risk Management, Financial Control, etc.) and take part in designing appropriate processes, systems and documentation.

- Track and support visibility activities, ensuring coordination with the donors takes place as required under the legal agreements.

- The Analyst may be requested to support other FIBG blended finance facilities/programmes and TC projects, and perform tasks for the delivery of FI and FI PDI mandate.

Knowledge, Skills, Experience & Qualifications

- Relevant and substantive experience in banking, consulting firm, or an international financial institution or donor organisation, on grant fund-raising, product structuring, implementation, and monitoring.

- Expertise and/or experience of the financial sector, working with banks and/or non-bank financial institutions.

- Good knowledge about the EBRD, its business model and processes, as well as investment risk management approaches and practices, is an advantage.

- Good relevant undergraduate degree at a minimum, from a leading university in the country of education.

- Excellent written (report drafting, correspondence) and verbal communication skills.

- Excellent command of English is essential. Knowledge of EBRD country of operations language(s) will be beneficial.

- Advanced Excel skills.

- Excellent interpersonal (especially collaboration) skills.

- Ability to handle complex matters and find pragmatic solutions to issues.

- Proven, strong analytical skills, including the ability to interpret data.

- Strong computer literacy.

- Strong attention to detail, project and time management and workload prioritisation skills.
