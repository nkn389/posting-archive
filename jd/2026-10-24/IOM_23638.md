Project Context and Scope
Diaspora organizations have long demonstrated their capacity to intervene in humanitarian contexts, reaching even the most vulnerable and isolated communities. When a crisis strikes, they are among the first respondents, mobilizing financial resources and organizing aid delivery thanks to their networks and knowledge of local contexts. Their presence on the ground often precedes and extends beyond the emergency phase, placing them in a key strategic position to bridge the humanitarian- development- peace nexus.[1]
IOM has implemented diaspora engagement initiatives worldwide along humanitarian- development- peace nexus since the 1970s. In Italy, the Organization has been working on diaspora initiatives since the early 2000s, empowering diaspora organizations through dedicated trainings and supporting the implementation of diaspora-led development and humanitarian projects.[2]
Under the DRAFT II - Diaspora for Development Cooperation – Empowering Diasporas in Italy as Development Actors at Local, National and International Levels project, IOM COMED will design and deliver a humanitarian training programme for diaspora organization.
The first component will consist of five face-to-face training days delivered over two weekends, covering the following topics:

- Humanitarian programme cycle, including emergency preparedness
- Frameworks, principles and standards
- Actors and their mandates
- Inter-agency coordination mechanisms
- Field security and context-specific challenges
- Peace and recovery programming

The second component of the training programme will consist of a three-day humanitarian crash course for diaspora organizations whose project proposals are selected under the Emergency Grant Scheme. The training will be delivered prior to the launch of their projects and will provide participants with the knowledge and practical tools needed to operate in emergency preparedness and post-emergency recovery contexts.
In the event that IOM also activates the Rapid Emergency Response Fund, an additional intensive pre-deployment crash course of up to three days will be delivered online to selected diaspora organizations prior to project implementation. The course will provide participants with the essential knowledge and practical tools needed to operate in humanitarian settings, covering, in a condensed format, the key contents of the broader humanitarian training programme.

Tasks to be performed under this contract
Under the overall supervision of the Project Manager and in coordination with the Project Assistant – Focal Point Peace and Recovery, the consultant will:

Training Design

- Develop a tailored curriculum of a comprehensive training on humanitarian action for diaspora.
- Prepare workshop materials, including slides, case studies, and pre- and post-assessment tools.
- Develop one humanitarian crash course for DOs whose projects will be awarded in the frame of the Emergency Grant Scheme, focusing on emergency preparedness and recovery interventions.
- Develop a condensed version of the comprehensive training on humanitarian action, adapted for online delivery over a maximum of three days and covering its key topics and practical elements.
- Ensure a culturally sensitive approach towards emergency management throughout the training development and delivery.

Training Delivery

- Deliver a five-days workshop in Rome across two weekends to a group of twenty diaspora representatives selected by IOM.
- Use interactive approaches, including role plays, group discussions, and practical exercises to enhance learning.
- Deliver an online pre-deployment crash course to a group of five diaspora representatives whose DOs will be awarded by IOM in the frame of the Emergency Grant Scheme.
- If needs arise, deliver another crash course to a group of five diaspora representatives selected by IOM in the frame of the Rapid Emergency Granting Scheme.
- Ensure that a participative approach is adopted at all times and communication is clear and adapted to the participants’ understanding of the language.

Evaluation and Reporting

- Develop pre- and post-training assessment tools to measure knowledge and skills gained by participants.
- Submit training reports, including participant feedback, assessment outcomes, lessons learned, and recommendations for further capacity building.
