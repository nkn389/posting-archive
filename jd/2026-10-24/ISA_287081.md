Org. Setting and Reporting
This position is based in the Communications and Information Technology Unit (CITU) within the Office of Administrative Services (OAS) at the International Seabed Authority (ISA) Secretariat in Kingston, Jamaica. The ISA is an international organization established under the United Nations Convention on the Law of the Sea (UNCLOS) and the 1994 Agreement relating to the Implementation of Part XI of UNCLOS. The ISA is the organization through which States Parties to UNCLOS organize and control activities in the Area, particularly with a view to administering the resources of the Area. CITU is responsible for providing secure, reliable, resilient and effective information and communications technology (ICT) services and capabilities to support the Secretariat’s mandate and operations. Its responsibilities encompass ICT infrastructure and services, enterprise applications and systems, cybersecurity and information security, ICT governance and risk management, business continuity and disaster recovery, web and digital services, and technology-enabled business processes. The IT Manager provides technical and operational leadership for the ICT function and ensures that ICT services and capabilities respond effectively to the Secretariat’s operational and business requirements. The incumbent provides technical advice to management, coordinates ICT activities across the Secretariat, and oversees the delivery and performance of ICT services, including those provided through external technical resources and service providers.

Responsibilities
Under the overall guidance of the Chef de Cabinet and the direct supervision of the Chief, OAS, and within delegated authority, the IT Manager will be responsible for the following duties: 1. ICT LEADERSHIP, OPERATIONS AND SERVICE DELIVERY • Lead and coordinate the delivery of reliable, secure and responsive ICT services in support of the Secretariat’s operations and priorities. • Manage and coordinate the work of ICT staff and technical resources, ensuring effective allocation of responsibilities, performance, accountability and knowledge transfer. • Oversee the operation, maintenance and continuous improvement of ICT infrastructure, networks, applications, cloud services, enterprise systems and end-user services, coordinating specialized technical support as required. • Coordinate ICT readiness and support for the annual sessions of the Authority and other critical meetings and events. 2. ICT GOVERNANCE, CYBERSECURITY, RISK AND COMPLIANCE • Contribute to the development and implementation of ICT policies, standards and governance arrangements consistent with the Secretariat’s priorities and applicable requirements. • Identify, assess and monitor ICT, cybersecurity and information-security risks and coordinate appropriate mitigation, remediation and reporting. • Oversee appropriate information-security, access-management, vulnerability, monitoring, incident-management and information-protection controls. • Coordinate ICT-related audit, assurance and compliance activities and follow up on agreed recommendations and corrective actions. 3. ENTERPRISE APPLICATIONS, ERP AND DIGITAL SERVICES • Provide technical coordination and oversight for the Secretariat’s enterprise resource planning (ERP) system, enterprise applications, website and other digital services. • Serve as the ICT focal point between business owners, users, service providers and technical resources to ensure that business requirements are appropriately translated into effective technology solutions. • Coordinate system maintenance, enhancements, upgrades, changes and incident resolution, ensuring appropriate testing, authorization, documentation and knowledge transfer. • Support the improvement and digitalization of business processes, including opportunities for automation and technology-enabled controls. 4. ICT PROJECTS, TECHNICAL PARTNERS AND VENDOR MANAGEMENT • Coordinate ICT projects and initiatives from requirements definition through implementation, ensuring alignment with approved priorities, resources, timelines and operational requirements. • Manage and oversee relationships with external technical partners, service providers and other ICT vendors, including service levels, deliverables, performance and technical dependencies. • Provide technical input to ICT procurement and monitor contracts, licensing and service arrangements. • Ensure appropriate documentation, knowledge transfer, transition and continuity arrangements and advise management on technology options and associated risks. 5. ICT RESILIENCE, BUSINESS CONTINUITY AND MAJOR EVENTS • Oversee ICT business continuity and disaster-recovery arrangements for critical systems and services, including backup, restoration and recovery procedures. • Coordinate periodic testing of ICT continuity and recovery arrangements and ensure that identified weaknesses are addressed. • Coordinate ICT planning and readiness for the annual sessions of the Authority and other major meetings and events, including deployment, technical support and timely resolution of issues. 6. ICT PLANNING, ADVISORY, INNOVATION AND CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT • Contribute to ICT planning and priorities and provide technical advice to management on technology requirements, opportunities, risks and investments. • Identify opportunities to improve the efficiency, security, resilience and quality of ICT services and technology-enabled business processes, including the responsible adoption of appropriate emerging technologies. • Monitor ICT service, performance and risk indicators and use operational experience, incidents, audits and lessons learned to support continuous improvement. • Ensure that critical ICT documentation, architecture, procedures, system dependencies and institutional knowledge are appropriately maintained and transferable. • Perform other duties as required by the Chief, OAS, and/or the Chef de Cabinet.
