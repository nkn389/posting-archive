What we do

Since 1863, the International Committee of the Red Cross (ICRC) has worked to preserve human dignity and relieve suffering caused by war and armed violence.

In collaboration with our Red Cross and Red Crescent partners around the world, we do everything we can to deliver life-saving aid, reconnect families and locate missing people – helping those who need it most, regardless of who they are and what side of the front line they are on.

We engage with authorities and armed forces on all sides, often confidentially, pressing for access to detainees to improve their living conditions and urging compliance with international humanitarian law to protect non-combatants, including from digital threats.

Purpose

The Agronomist will ensure that the agricultural in emergencies program is in line with the overall Economic Security (EcoSec) strategy for Lebanon. He / She will provide strategic and technical support to EcoSec team in Lebanon with particular focus on supporting teams at sub-delegation level.

The Agronomist will provide key technical support and general program management to drive forward the EcoSec Lebanon agriculture strategy. This will be done through overseeing the review of a pilot program conducted in 2026 and overseeing the launch of larger programme focused on agriculture and livelihoods in 2027. The agronomist is expected to take a key leadership role in the implementation, monitoring and final review of the agronomimst programme in 2027.

Accountabilities & functional responsibilities

Under the supervision of the EcoSec Coordinator/Deputy Coordinator, the main responsibilities of the Agronomist are:

- Supervise the implementation of the EcoSec agricultural programme and the 2027 ‘agriculture in emergencies’ project

- Design or adapt agricultural data collection tools, organising the collection and communication of relevant data.

- Maintain a relationship with the counterpart at the Ministry of Agriculture (MoA) to ensure strong collaboration, particularly with reference to the Partnership Framework Agreement.

- Maintain up-to-date records of agricultural project implementation, including financial forecasts, requisition orders, frequently interacting with the cash assistance Focal Point for effective implementation.

- Provide timely reporting on agricultural assistance for the Quarterly EcoSec Report, Monitoring for Results, and budget use calculations.

- Liaise with ICRC Logistics, Finance and other departments to ensure the purchase and timely delivery of all required inputs in accordance with ICRC policies and procedures (e.g., for workshops, consultant fees, payment of volunteers and daily workers, etc.)

- Liaise with the Lebanese Red Cross (LRC) on continued collaboration on the agricultural assistance/livelihood programmes

- Maintain good relations with relevant interlocutors from the LRC, MoA, and the UN FAO and its partners.

- Coordinate as necessary with other ICRC departments on the implementation of agricultural programme.

- Be informed about Lebanese laws, regulations, and technical standards relating to agricultural inputs and activities.
