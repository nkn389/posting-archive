Introduction
Established in 1951, the International Organization for Migration (IOM) is the leading global organization in the field of migration. In line with IOM’s prioritization of protection-centred approaches and integrity, transparency and accountability, IOM is committed to the prevention of and response to Protection from Sexual Exploitation, Abuse and Harassment (PSEAH) across all its operations.
As part of the Office of Ethics and PSEAH (OEP), the PSEAH Unit leads IOM’s work to combat sexual exploitation and abuse and sexual harassment in coordination with offices and missions across IOM and counterparts throughout the UN, IASC, and humanitarian community. The PSEAH Unit’s strategy focuses on strengthening leadership and organizational culture; institutional accountability and transparency; training and outreach; victim assistance; and partnership and coordination
Organizational Department / Unit to which the Consultant is contributing
Under the direct supervision of the Senior Coordinator, PSEAH, the Regional Protection from Sexual Exploitation, Abuse and Harassment (PSEAH) Officer candidate will hold the following responsibilities.

Tasks to be performed under this contract
1. In coordination with the global PSEAH Unit, provide advice and recommendations to the Regional Director, Deputy Regional Directors and the Chiefs of Mission within the Region on strategic engagement, policy and programmatic decisions on PSEAH.
2. Provide SEAH case management technical guidance, support and oversight to IOM PSEAH Officers and Focal Points within country offices on victim assistance and ensuring a victim/survivor-centred approach, in coordination with IOM PSEAH case management team.

3. Maintain timely information-sharing and case coordination between country offices, the PSEAH Unit and other relevant HQ units on individual case supervision and victim assistance, as relevant.
4. In coordination with the global PSEAH Unit, ensure that IOM country offices in the region have the necessary mechanisms in place to facilitate access to assistance for SEA survivors.
5. Build the capacity of IOM PSEAH Officers and Focal Points within the region to respond to SEAH disclosures and ensure access to assistance.
6. Support and advise the IOM Country Offices to undertake risk assessments to identify SEA gaps and put in place appropriate mechanisms to mitigate risks.
7. Provide technical guidance and support to IOM PSEAH Officers and Focal Points on the integration of PSEAH within project proposals, budgets and reports.
8. Promote the application and sharing of best practices on SEAH prevention, risk mitigation, and response and document lessons learned from the field to continuously strengthen IOM’s PSEAH efforts.
9. Represent IOM within inter-agency PSEA fora, as applicable, and actively participate in seminars, meetings and events, advocating for IOM’s PSEAH initiatives across the Region to facilitate the sharing of resources, best practice and technical guidance.
10. Undertake duty travel as required.
11. Perform such other duties as may be assigned.
