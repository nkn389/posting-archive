What will you do in this position?
To develop and implement WFP Palestine external Communications plans/activities that support overall Country Office goals and WFP objectives.

Why work with WFP Palestine?
For decades, Palestine has been facing a protracted and complex protection and humanitarian crisis. The humanitarian situation worsened alarmingly after an upsurge in violence from October 2023.
WFP, through our dual mandate in humanitarian and development, provides life-saving food assistance to the most vulnerable and food insecure non-refugee Palestinians.
WFP provides logistics support to humanitarian and development partners, allowing them to reach more people faster, and at a lower cost to donors and the environment. By offering our cash-based transfer platform to the wider humanitarian and development community, coordinating logistics, and developing an inter-agency common feedback mechanism, WFP contributes to wider humanitarian efforts.

How can you make a difference?
Under the direct supervision of the Gaza Emergency Coordinator and technical supervision of Head of Communications in Jerusalem the incumbent will be responsible for:

1- Working with the Head of communications in Jerusalem to raise awareness and understand of WFP Palestine operations in Gaza among the public, opinion leaders and donors to strengthen WFP’s visibility and support fundraising activities.
2- Leading on the development and implementation of social media plan for Gaza to enhance coverage of and support for WFP’s activities.
3- Managing a communications team in Gaza to produce and deliver on a range of communications products as per the Palestine Communications Strategy
4- Produce communication materials, including press releases, media briefings, articles, social media posts, reports, and other products that inform internal and external audiences.
5- Generate and/or facilitate the production of donor specific visibility content including, text, photos, videos and audio for use across a range of integrated online platforms.
6- Establishing and developing relationships with local journalists and media outlets inside Gaza and help manage the regular flow of news and accurate information about WFP Palestine operations to the media, ensuring reputational risks are identified and addressed.
7- Working with the programme team to strengthen communications with communities in Gaza via a range of approved channels.
8- Track and analyse traditional and online media, to identify trends, emerging issues, reputational risks and recommend adjustments and solutions to improve the effectiveness of communication strategy and activities.
9- Establish effective collaborations and partnerships with internal and external counterparts to ensure a coherent communications approach, align activities, and close collaboration of involved actors.
Other as required, including operational information management tasks.
