BACKGROUND AND PURPOSE OF THE ASSIGNMENT:

The World Food Programme (WFP) is the world's largest humanitarian agency, fighting hunger worldwide. Over the years, WFP has made available its supply chain expertise, knowledge and network to governments, donors and UN agencies as a global good. The Data Scientist position will contribute to strengthening the evidence agenda of the Country Office through innovative analysis, including the development and training of artificial intelligence models tailored to food security and vulnerability, thereby enhancing the efficiency of VAM analysis and enabling more precise programme design and targeting

ACCOUNTABILITIES/RESPONSIBILITIES:
Under the direct supervision of the Head of VAME the consultant is responsible for:
 In collaboration with VAME GIS officer, Support the periodic updates, maintenance of the VAME geospatial visualization platform (AF Pulse), which integrates data from programme, assessment, monitoring, security, operations and supply chain with data collection, standardization and upload;
 Design, calibrate, and operationalize a probability model that estimates the likelihood of road disruption in proximity to conflict events.
 Update, validate, calibrate and operationalize a probability model that estimates acute malnutrition levels using acute malnutrition admission data and other related variables.
 Intergrade automated models into AF Pulse to strengthen the real-time disaster impacts and exposure analysis.
 Propose a replication and scale‑up roadmap, documenting migration pathways to alternative cloud environments, producing an open‑source replication toolkit, and identifying priority countries for adaptation of the model.
 Develop, test and refine geographical-level targeting criteria and models for emergency assistance, resilience, livelihoods and social protection interventions and geographical and beneficiary targeting to ensure maximum efficiency and equity in resource allocation, minimizing geographical bias.
 Coordinate and provide technical analysis, recommendations and learnings to continuously improve the early warning workstream.
 Other tasks as required by the VAME related to Early Warning Analysis and Systems.
