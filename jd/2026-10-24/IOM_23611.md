Introduction
Established in 1951, IOM is a Related Organization of the United Nations and the leading UN agency in the field of migration. Working closely with governmental, intergovernmental and non-governmental partners, IOM promotes humane and orderly migration for the benefit of all. It saves lives and protects people on the move, drives solutions to displacement, and facilitates pathways for regular migration, while providing services and advice to governments and migrants.
IOM is committed to fostering a respectful, inclusive and supportive workplace where all employees can thrive professionally and feel valued. By creating such an environment, IOM aims to better harness the full potential of migration and strengthen its support to people on the move.

Project Context and Scope
Within the framework of the IOM Development Fund project on mainstreaming migration and climate change perspectives into the National Adaptation Plan (NAP) processes in North Macedonia, IOM will support the Government of North Macedonia in integrating human mobility considerations into national climate adaptation planning.
The Junior National Consultant for Inclusive Climate Adaptation and Stakeholder Engagement Support will play a role in providing technical expertise, facilitating consultations and supporting the organization of technical meetings and workshops with relevant ministries and institutions.

Organizational Department / Unit to which the Consultant is contributing

CLIMATE ACTION DEPARTMENT
GREEN PATHWAYS: INTEGRATING HUMAN MOBILITY AND GREEN SKILLS INTO CLIMATE ADAPTATION PLANNING IN NORTH MACEDONIA

Tasks to be performed under this contract

- Technical meeting documentation (Activity 1.2.2): Agendas, background materials, presentations and concise meeting summaries prepared for up to five NAP technical meetings, including recommendations and follow-up actions.
- Stakeholder consultation documentation (Activity 1.2.3): Consultation agendas, guiding questions, supporting materials and summaries prepared for up to ten stakeholder consultations, together with contributions to a consolidated consultation report reflecting relevant gender-responsive and inclusive participation considerations.
- Written inputs to the NAP and adaptation-relevant documents (Activity 1.2.4): Draft technical comments, background notes, proposed text and recommendations prepared under IOM technical guidance, including relevant considerations on human mobility, environmental sustainability, gender equality and social inclusion.
- Monthly progress reports and timesheets: Monthly reports documenting completed assignments, progress against deliverables and relevant follow-up actions, accompanied by completed timesheets.
- Final consultancy report: A consolidated report summarizing the work undertaken, outputs delivered, key findings, lessons learned and recommendations.
