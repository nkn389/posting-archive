BACKGROUND AND PURPOSE OF THE ASSIGNMENT:
The Data Management and Analysis Assistant leads on data collection, cleaning, processing, and analysis activities to enable timely production of market price analyses, reports, and data products. The role ensures high data quality, the production of visualization tools, and leading the team with routine and ad‑hoc statistical tasks in line with established procedures.

ACCOUNTABILITIES/RESPONSIBILITIES:
Under the supervision of the head of programme, the incumbent will:

Data Collection and Management

- Support with setting up assessment systems for food security monitoring, through existing government exercises (including liaising with SCI) and new initiatives (i.e. mVAM).
- Support programme targeting - both geographic and household levels.
- Collect, compile, and clean quantitative datasets from internal and external sources.
- Verify data accuracy, completeness, and consistency following agreed standards.
- Maintain updated datasets and support routine data management processes.
- Data Processing and Analysis Categorize and process data using standard statistical and analytical methods. Conduct statistical analyses and oversee outputs and estimates using predefined models. Lead in extracting and analyzing national statistical datasets on food insecurity, poverty, and socioeconomic vulnerabilities (HIES).
- Data Visualization and Reporting Support Produce charts, tables, and visualizations to support analytical reporting. Lead on the development and maintenance of dashboards and data presentation tools. Prepare data inputs and supporting materials for reports and knowledge products.
- Coordination and Collaboration Work closely with the VAM regional office to support market price analysis and related outputs. Liaise with team members to ensure data requirements are clearly understood and met. Share technical inputs to improve data organization and workflow efficiency. Liaise with counterparts from sister agencies on analyses, surveys, and other related tasks.
- Monitoring Support the Monitoring Unit with providing strategic and technical guidance to ensure delivery of high-quality monitoring outputs. Develop and standardize monitoring frameworks, reporting templates, questionnaires, data collection tools, and analytical methodologies. Guide the analysis and interpretation of monitoring data to generate actionable insights for programme management and decision-making.
- Other Duties Assist the team with ad‑hoc statistical or data‑related tasks. Perform other related duties within the scope of the role as assigned by the supervisor.
- QUALIFICATIONS & EXPERIENCE REQUIRED:
- Education: Completion of secondary school education required. Post‑secondary training or coursework in statistics, data analysis, economics, information management, or a related field is A MUST.
- Experience: Minimum of five years of progressively responsible experience in data management, statistics, or analytical support. Demonstrated experience working with large quantitative datasets. Practical experience supporting statistical analysis or estimation techniques.
- Knowledge & Skills: Proficiency in spreadsheet software and basic statistical tools. Experience with data visualization and reporting tools is an advantage. Familiarity with data quality principles and structured data workflows. Knowledge of, or exposure to, market analysis or VAM‑related data is desirable. Good understanding of the local context in Iran.
- Languages: Fluency in the duty station language required. Working knowledge of English required.
