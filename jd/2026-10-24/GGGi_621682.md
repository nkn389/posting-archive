INTRODUCTION TO GGGI

The Global Green Growth Institute (GGGI) is a treaty-based international, inter-governmental organization dedicated to supporting and promoting strong, inclusive and sustainable economic growth in developing countries and emerging economies. To learn more please visit about GGGI web page.

POSITION INTRODUCTION

Under the guidance of the Regional Director of Asia, the Country Representative for Indonesia is responsible for the strategic leadership of the country program and the implementation of projects to achieve the organizational vision, goals, and objectives. The Country Representative is also responsible for the programmatic and operational management of the Indonesia country program and its relationship with the Government of Indonesia.

POSITION SUMMARY

The Country Representative manages and strengthens the GGGI Indonesia program, deepening and expanding GGGI’s program and presence in Indonesia. They will lead and direct the operational day-to-day activities of the country programs and ensure the implementation of all projects. They represent GGGI with government and international organizations in the country and thus acts as the focal representative of GGGI in the country for all GGGI-related activities and is responsible for cultivating and maintaining strong partnerships with the Government of Indonesia, development partners, and other stakeholders. They are responsible for ensuring cross-learning and fertilization of knowledge across GGGI programs and divisions.

The Country Representative will lead in implementing the current Country Planning Framework (CPF) as well as developing the annual detailed work plan, and other key corporate planning documents, cultivating new business opportunities and partnerships, and overseeing the program implementation in Indonesia.

The Country Representative will identify resource mobilization opportunities and support the development and execution of in-country resource mobilization strategies. They are responsible for overall financial and human resources management.

The Country Representative will spend their time on country-level programming but also significant time informing and contributing to Asia Regional Strategy Implementation and corporate-level programming (i.e., contributing to GGGI's thematic priorities and providing strategic inputs to ensure GGGI remains relevant in the country, Asia region and globally).

WORKING CONDITIONS
The Country Representative role is a G12 grade level in GGGI's international salary scale, details of which can be found in the GGGI Staff Regulations on GGGI’s website. In addition, for eligible international hires, GGGI offers a competitive expatriate package that includes a 20% annual base salary expatriate allowance, relocation support, and financial assistance for children's education. Eligible staff also receive annual home leave travel support, health insurance, a 15% organizational retirement contribution, and 27 days of annual leave, all governed by GGGI Staff Regulations. The appointment is for 36 months, renewable, subject to satisfactory performance and budget availability.

GGGI is committed to providing a work environment that is sufficiently flexible to accommodate diverse life-cycle challenges and assist Staff members in achieving a better balance between work and personal/family commitments, thus ensuring high performance, long-term productivity, and well-being.

Child protection – GGGI is committed to child protection, irrespective of whether any specific area of work involves direct contact with children. GGGI’s Child Protection Policy is written in accordance with the Convention on the Rights of the Child.
