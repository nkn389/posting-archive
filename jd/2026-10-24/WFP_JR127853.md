WFP Tajikistan seeks an independent consultant to conduct the final review of the US Government funded Tajikistan Food Security Safety Net Activity, covering implementation from November 2022 to April 2026. The activity combines unconditional food assistance, cash-for-assets and livelihood support, and food security and market monitoring to address immediate needs and strengthen resilience to shocks.

The review will assess performance across all three components and project locations, examine results and prospects for sustainability, and identify lessons and practical recommendations for future programming. It will assess relevance, coherence, effectiveness and efficiency, and examine evidence of contribution to resilience without assuming causal impact.Key responsibilities
· Review project documents and existing monitoring, financial and results data; develop the review methodology, evaluation matrix, sampling approach, tools and workplan.
· Conduct field visits, key informant interviews, focus group discussions and structured observations of assets. Analyse existing quantitative data; no new household survey is planned.
· Triangulate evidence on targeting, delivery, outcomes, implementation efficiency and asset sustainability, clearly explaining data gaps and limitations.
· Integrate gender equality, disability inclusion, protection and accountability throughout; ensure informed consent, confidentiality and independent judgement.
· Validate findings with stakeholders and prepare clear conclusions, lessons and prioritized recommendations with responsible actors and proposed timeframes.

Expected deliverables
Inception phase — 20 days. Approved inception report covering methodology, review matrix, sampling, tools, stakeholder engagement, analysis plan, ethical arrangements and workplan.
Field consultation phase — 20 days. Completed stakeholder consultations and field observations, followed by an exit debriefing presentation.
Analysis and reporting phase — 30 days. Draft report and stakeholder validation workshop; final report, executive summary, presentation and consolidated response to comments.
