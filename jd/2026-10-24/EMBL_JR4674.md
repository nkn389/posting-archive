The European Molecular Biology Laboratory (EMBL) is one of the highest ranked scientific research organisations in the world. The Headquarters Laboratory is located in Heidelberg (Germany), with additional sites in Grenoble (France), Hamburg (Germany), Hinxton (UK) and Monterotondo (Italy).

Responsibilities include daily husbandry and monitoring of laboratory animals maintained in isolators and IVCs under Specific pathogen free (SPF) conditions. Additional duties include providing technical services for scientists who use laboratory animals for research. Participation in the weekend duty rotation is obligatory.

Your Role

Organization of the team

- Overall supervision of users and animal technicians including scheduling of work and training new hires in all GF-related tasks.

- Oversee daily work in GF area to ensure completion according to SOPs.

- Handle short-term redistribution of resources (i.e. assignment of resources depending on changing needs in GF area).

- Liaise with the head of LAR and PI to ensure work is covered during holidays, etc.

Staff training and development

- Monitor progress of LAR techs and researchers working with GF colonies and train and re-train as needed.

- Provide hands-on GF training/advice to LAR techs and researchers as needed and according to approved policies and SOPs, applying a continuous development approach.

- Act as first point of contact for technical questions regarding GF colony.

Policies and compliance

- Understand and apply EMBL, IACUC, LAR and animal welfare policies.

- Participate in LAR/laboratory meetings/rounds as needed.

- Liaise with head of LAR to ensure H&S policies are properly implemented.

- Ensure animal environment is in compliance with EMBL policies, LAR SOPs and IACUC directions.

- Ensure that care, housing and husbandry of animals meet EMBL SOPs and policies.

- Participate in the writing and updating Standard Operating Procedures.

Area Supervisor

- GF colony operations in isolators

- Oversee daily GF colony operations and ensure daily practices and processes comply with applicable regulations.

- Responsible for the husbandry and care of all GF colonies, including dedicated equipment operation and maintenance (both preventative and corrective). Liaise with LAR supervisors as needed.

- Provide daily care and husbandry to GF/gnotobiotic mice according to SOPs, including preparation and sterilization of supplies (including process validation and QC/QA).

- Maintain GF/gnotobiotic animal colonies, including animal procurement, colony/line maintenance, cage inventory and record-keeping.

- Perform microbial colonization experiments and tissue collection of GF/gnotobiotic animals.

- Perform microbial testing protocols of gnotobiotic colonies and equipment using diagnostic PCR analysis and bacterial culturing protocols.

- Coordinate and manage scientific experiments and supports users, including planning, execution, monitoring, and documentation of involved experimental steps.

- Monitor and oversee repair and report malfunction of animal facility equipment. Contact outside vendors or appropriate EMBL personnel for equipment repair.

- Ensure adequate quantities of relevant stocks (reagents, consumables, etc.) are available at all times in the assigned area.

- Order supplies and consumables as needed.

- Oversee the receipt of animals, collection of census information, animal transfers within and between different animal areas/rooms.

- Participate in the execution of the Animal Health Surveillance program (sampling, coordinate with lab, handling samples, etc.).

Research support

- Interact with research investigators concerning research protocols, procedures and other related techniques. Advise researchers and staff regarding experimental techniques and procedural issues.

- Work with LAR management to address investigator non-standard husbandry requests.
