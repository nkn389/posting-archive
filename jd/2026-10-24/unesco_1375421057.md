OVERVIEW

The Sciences (SCS) Unit in the UNESCO Regional Office in Bangkok and Office for UN Coordination for Asia and the Pacific is mandated to promote and advance scientific knowledge for sustainable development, the ethics of science and technology, and social inclusion, in Thailand, Myanmar, Lao PDR, Singapore, and indirectly in Viet Nam and Cambodia, in cooperation with the country offices and the antenna office.

Against a backdrop of rapid modernization and digital transformation, multifaceted social transformation, and increasing social inequalities in South-East Asia, UNESCO seeks to harness the unique power of the scientific interdisciplinarity to develop relevant approaches in supporting evidence-based policy formulation, and promoting rights and ethics in the areas of science and technology.

Long Description

ASSIGNMENTS

The Individual Specialist will perform the following:

- Under the Ethics of Science and Technology Programme, advise and collaborate with national institutions in Lao PDR, Thailand, and Vietnam in implementing recommendations from the UNESCO country reports on AI Ethics Readiness Assessment Methodology (RAM):

- In Lao PDR, bring expert recommendations and guidance to support the government design and implement its first-ever National AI Strategy, and to co-lead the UNCT Task Force on Digital Transformation, including in developing a joint programme on the Future of Skills;

- In Thailand, provide technical support in implementing the AI Ethics and Start-ups initiative, as well as the AI and Environmental and Ecosystem Assessment programme with national institutions and private sector, both applying the principles of UNESCO Recommendation on the Ethics of AI with a strong gender-transformative approach;

- In Vietnam, provide technical support to the UNESCO Office in Hanoi in implementing the AI Ethics and Start-ups initiative with national institutions and private sector, both applying the principles of UNESCO Recommendation on the Ethics of AI with a strong gender-transformative approach.

2. Under the Management of Social Transformation (MOST) Programme, facilitate the development of a scientific interdisciplinary programme on Promoting the Science-Society Interface for Climate Adaptation in Asia-Pacific, including technological components. The programme consists of leveraging three UNESCO scientific programmes – MOST, Man and Biosphere (MAB), and UNESCO GeoParks (UGG) – to document and analyse communal knowledge pertaining to sustainable development and climate change adaption:

- Supervise the production of a research paper, using AI tools, documenting interdisciplinary scientific work in natural resources management, climate adaptation vulnerabilities, and deployment of development projects such as sustainable tourism in UNESCO designated sites;

- Lead and organise consultations meetings with relevant stakeholders and research institutions in support of the situation analysis work undertaken by the research team;

- Coordinate technical dialogues with UNESCO Field Offices in Asia-Pacific in contribution to the situation analysis, and the design of a regional initiative thereof, responding to the priorities and eligibility criteria of the Adaptation Fund;

- Lead the design and organisation of a regional UNESCO Conference on Science-Society Interface for Climate Adaptation in Asia-Pacific to discuss and endorse the findings of the research paper and the regional initiative with involved scientists and involved stakeholders.

3. Under UNESCO’s regional framework on sustainable and cultural tourism, formulate and facilitate the Sciences Unit’s technical expertise and contribution to the intersectoral programme design, with a particular focus on youth’s intercultural dialogue, and the promotion of cultural heritage for peace and regional integration in a context of digital transformation.

DELIVERABLES

The Individual Specialist shall submit the following deliverables to UNESCO:

1st Deliverable: Situation analysis of the start-up ecosystem and landscape in Thailand and Vietnam, especially in the field of AI, with specific entry points for integrating the Ethics of AI standards;

2nd Deliverable: Situation analysis of Thailand’s plans, visions, and anticipated challenges in terms of investments in data centres, with a particular focus on ethical implications for the environment, AI governance, and the future of skills;

3rd Deliverable: Draft programme document on Promoting the Science-Society Interface for Climate Adaptation in Asia-Pacific and related technical documentation;

4th Deliverable: Research piece on youth, sustainable and cultural tourism, and regional peace and integration in ASEAN.
