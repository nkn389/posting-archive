Project Context and Scope
The International Organization for Migration (IOM) cooperates with the Government of the Republic of Tajikistan to support the safe and orderly migration of Tajik citizens. This cooperation encompasses a range of services, including pre-departure preparation, capacity building, skills development, reintegration assistance, and other targeted support measures to enhance migrant protection and opportunities.
In partnership with the Migration Service under the Ministry of Labour, Migration and Employment of the Population of the Republic of Tajikistan, IOM has established a Safe Migration Hotline (+992 900 800 800). This Hotline, operating from 9:00-17:00 Monday to Friday, provides migrants with access to accurate information on migration-related matters, helping to ensure informed and secure migration decisions. The Hotline also provides legal consultations to migrants in-person, over the phone and through Whatsapp and Telegram groups. The Hotline operates from the premises of the Pre-departure Orientation and Consultation Centre (PDO) under the Migration Service. Its day-to-day operations are jointly managed by the Migration Service and IOM.
Objective
Under the direct supervision of the National Programme Officer of the Migration Solutions Unit (MSU), the Hotline Coordinator will be responsible for ensuring the effective, efficient and uninterrupted operation of the IOM Safe Migration Hotline, including the quality, accessibility and consistency of information and consultation services provided to migrants and the general public.

The Coordinator will oversee the day-to-day operations of the Safe Migration Hotline, supervise and support its team, ensure the availability of up-to-date and accurate on various migration routes and necessary tools and technical solutions, and provide direct consultations when required. The Coordinator will also be responsible for identifying and implementing opportunities to improve, expand and promote the Hotline's services, ensuring their continued relevance and responsiveness to the evolving needs of migrants and prospective migrants.

Tasks to be performed under this contract

- Provide expert support to operators in difficult or complex cases and direct consultations when necessary;

- Coordinate with Government and non-government partners to provide specialized consultations and ensure the referral of callers to relevant organizations, as appropriate;

- Develop, implement and regularly update Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), service standards and operational protocols to ensure consistent, efficient and quality service delivery.

- Strategic development and engagement

- Develop and implement a comprehensive Hotline Development Plan, outlining priorities and practical measures for service improvement, expansion, accessibility, outreach and sustainability;

- Strategically and efficiently coordinate the Safe Migration Hotline’s activities with the Migration Service and other relevant stakeholders to strengthen institutional cooperation and ensure alignment with national migration priorities;

- Promote and raise awareness of the Hotline through various channels, including social media, mass media, information campaigns and events organized by IOM and governmental, international and civil society organizations;

- Manage and maintain the Instagram and Facebook social media pages related to the Hotline;

- Identify opportunities to introduce new services, communication channels, digital solutions and partnerships to improve the Hotline's accessibility, effectiveness and responsiveness to migrants' needs.

- Identify capacity-building needs and ensure that Hotline consultants and operators have the necessary knowledge, skills, guidance materials and tools to provide accurate and reliable migration-related information and consultations. Organize and facilitate regular training, knowledge-sharing and learning sessions to continuously strengthen the professional competencies of Hotline personnel.

- Collect and analyze statistical data related to the operation of the Hotline and establish consistent data collection, management and reporting procedures to ensure data quality and reliability;

- Analyze consultation trends to identify emerging migration issues, recurring concerns, information gaps and priority needs of migrants, and provide regular insights and recommendations to inform IOM programming, awareness-raising, referrals and engagement with relevant partners;

- Provide accurate and timely reports of achievements, progress and challenges to IOM regarding the operation of the Hotline including service performance, quality, outreach and implementation of the Hotline Development Plan;

- Submit weekly and monthly reports, in compliance with confidentiality requirements and ethical standards in accordance with IOM procedures;

- Provide ad hoc support to IOM when requested.

- Hotline SOPs and quality assurance mechanisms established and implemented, with information resources updated at least monthly and regular capacity-building sessions conducted.

- 100% of weekly and monthly operational reports submitted within agreed deadlines, complemented by quarterly analytical reports containing key findings and actionable recommendations.
