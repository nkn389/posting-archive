The Team
The Gender Equality Division’s mission is to ensure women and girls in Africa and South Asia can enjoy good health, make their own choices, earn their own money, and be leaders in their societies. When women and girls have an equal chance to thrive and lead, everyone benefits. We know that women and girls are not a monolith. A woman’s experience and the barriers she faces are different depending on factors like where she lives, how old she is, and how much money her family have —alongside other factors including her race, caste, and education level.

We seek to address these compounding barriers through deep partnership with our grantees. As a philanthropy, our role is to listen to and learn from these experts. We take risks in new areas, prove concepts, and bring these ideas to governments, partner organizations, and private sector companies to scale. The Gender Equality (GE) Division builds off our existing strengths as a foundation in areas like global health and data, partnering closely with all of all our global divisions.

The Women’s Health Innovations (WHI) team under the GE Division facilitates the development and introduction of innovative health products and widens the innovation ecosystem to address conditions that disproportionately affect the lives of women in low- and middle-income countries. We currently invest across contraception, sexually transmitted infections (STI’s) and gynecological/menstrual health.

Your Role
As the Senior Program Officer, Regional Product Introduction, you will work with partners to lead investments and support the development and execution of product introduction and expansion strategies for priority products in the Women’s Health portfolio.

The PrIMA (Product Introduction and Market Access) initiative within WHI works to introduce priority products from the WHI portfolio and speed up access to them. Its focus areas are contraception, STIs, and gynecological and menstrual health. PrIMA invests in partnerships that support manufacturing, procurement, supply chain, and product introduction, so that these products stay accessible in priority geographies.

You will report to the Deputy Director of Health Systems for the Africa Regional Office (ARO), with a dotted line to the Deputy Director of PrIMA for WHI. This role is based in our Nairobi, Kenya office.
