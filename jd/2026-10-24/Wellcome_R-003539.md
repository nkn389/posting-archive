Wellcome Collection is a free museum and library. We believe everyone’s experience of health matters. Through our collections, exhibitions and events, in books and online, we explore the past, present and future of health.

Wellcome Collection opened in 2007 and currently welcomes around 400k visitors per year. We care for many thousands of items relating to health, medicine and human experience, including rare books, artworks, films and videos, personal archives and objects. We’re part of Wellcome, a charitable foundation supporting science to help build a healthier future for everyone.

We are looking for an Exhibitions Project Manager to join our team.

Where in Wellcome will I be working?
You will join Wellcome Collection’s Production, Planning & Design in the Curatorial & Public Programmes department. You will report to the Exhibition Project Management & Design Lead.

What will I be doing?
In this role, you will manage the delivery of complex and experimental exhibitions, projects, commissioned artworks and installations. You will ensure that design, production and installation of objects and interpretation are of a high standard, accessible and environmentally sustainable.
You will work collaboratively with curators, registrars, artists, designers and colleagues across Wellcome Collection to develop engaging exhibition experiences. From concept through to delivery, you will evaluate project needs, manage resources and identify the expertise required to achieve project objectives.

Is this job for me?
We are looking for someone who can:

- Build strong relationships, and work in an open and collaborative way with multiple project consultants, contractors and stakeholders
- Work with artists and curators to develop new commissions
- Commission exhibition design and fabrication, steering projects to be environmentally responsible, accessible and fit for purpose
- Contribute to a diverse and inclusive culture across the organisation, collaborating across departments.

To apply for this role please submit an updated CV and answer 2 application questions to demonstrate that you meet the minimum requirements for the role.

Minimum Criteria:

- Significant experience managing the planning, production and installation of large-scale temporary exhibitions in a museum, gallery or comparable cultural environment.
- Proven ability to manage complex projects, including budgets, schedules, resources, risks and competing priorities.
- Experience of working with artists to realise commissioned artworks.
- Experience of design commissioning; managing exhibition designers, contractors, fabricators and technical suppliers to make accessible and environmentally accessible exhibitions.
- Understanding of the conservation and care requirements of working with museum objects and artworks.

Our Hybrid Way of Working

We understand that our colleagues have commitments and personal interests outside of work and we strive to build a flexible working environment, in which people can perform at their best.
At Wellcome we have a hybrid way of working which is 3 days in our Euston Road offices (Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday) with the remaining 2 days working either remotely or in the office. It is sometimes necessary to work more than 2 days in the office. For example, exhibition build, installation or deinstallation periods tend to run Monday – Friday; it is also sometimes necessary to work in galleries when they are closed to the public on Mondays.
