Introduction
Established in 1951, IOM is a Related Organization of the United Nations and the leading UN agency in the field of migration. Working closely with governmental, intergovernmental and non-governmental partners, IOM promotes humane and orderly migration for the benefit of all. It saves lives and protects people on the move, drives solutions to displacement, and facilitates pathways for regular migration, while providing services and advice to governments and migrants.
IOM is committed to fostering a respectful, inclusive and supportive workplace where all employees can thrive professionally and feel valued. By creating such an environment, IOM aims to better harness the full potential of migration and strengthen its support to people on the move.

Organizational Context and Scope
Under the overall supervision of the Programme Support Officer and the direct supervision of the Information Management Officer, and in close collaboration with the Programme Units, the Senior Information Management Associate – Shelter and Non-food Items (S/NFI) will be responsible and accountable for serving as the technical reference for Information Management (IM) and geospatial work in support of the Shelter and Non-Food Items (S/NFI) programme and Monitoring, Evaluation and Learning (MEAL) functions. The incumbent will oversee data systems, dashboards, and geospatial products, interpret programme data, and provide technical support to programme management, Implementing Partners (IPs) and external stakeholders on information requirements, data quality and reporting to deliver accurate, accessible, and well-structured data that informs programme and management decision-making across the CO.

Required Qualifications and Experience
Education

- Bachelor’s degree in computer science, Information Management, Engineering, or a related field from an accredited academic institution with five years of relevant professional experience; or,
- High school diploma in the above fields with seven years of relevant professional experience.
Accredited Universities are those listed in the UNESCO World Higher Education Database.

Experience

- A minimum of five years’ experience in IM systems;
- Experience in managing and coordinating information flows and data management processes, including collecting, storing, processing, and analysing data to generate information products;
- Experience in programme implementation and information management for humanitarian projects, particularly in Shelter and Non-food Items and/or Common Pipeline programmes, is required; and,
- Experience in Airtable is an advantage.
Skills

- Knowledge of the latest technological developments in information technology and information systems;
- Proven ability to analyse statistical information;
- Demonstrated information management skills and understanding of different data collection methodologies; and knowledge of KoboCollect applications;
- Computer literacy, including knowledge of SQL databases, Microsoft Office, QGIS, and ArcGIS; and,
- Proficiency in using Airtable is a strong advantage.
