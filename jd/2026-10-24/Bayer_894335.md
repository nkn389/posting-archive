Regulatory Automation Developer

The Regulatory Automation Developer bridges regulatory business expertise and low-code automation development to accelerate the digital transformation of Regulatory Information Management (RIM) processes.
This role combines regulatory process expertise with hands-on automation development. You will help transform Veeva RIM processes through workflow automation, AI, and digital innovation, supporting the vision of faster, simpler, and increasingly automated regulatory operations.

Key Responsibilities

- Translate business requirements into scalable low-code solutions.
- Design, develop, test, deploy, and maintain automation workflows using platforms such as Node-RED, n8n, and other approved Bayer automation technologies.
- Ensure automations follow platform standards, security requirements, and GxP expectations.
- Support solution design by combining knowledge of regulatory business processes with platform capabilities.
- Promote automation-first thinking across regulatory operations.
- Monitor automation performance and identify opportunities for further optimization.

Key Qualifications

Required

- Bachelor's degree in Life Sciences, Computer Science, Information Systems, Regulatory Affairs, or a related field.
- 3+ years of experience in Regulatory
- Practical experience with Veeva Vault RIM
- Strong technical aptitude with a demonstrated interest in digital technologies, automation, and AI-enabled solutions.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
- Ability to communicate effectively with both technical and business stakeholders.
Preferred

- Experience with low-code/no-code automation platforms (Node-RED, n8n, Power Platform, or similar).
- Experience implementing automations within Veeva Vault environments.
- Knowledge of AI, LLMs, intelligent document processing, and agent-based solutions.
- Experience working in GxP-regulated environments.
- Familiarity with GitHub repository management
