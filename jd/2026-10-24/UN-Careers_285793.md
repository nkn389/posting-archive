Org. Setting and Reporting
OSAA is mandated with the provision of advisory, advocacy, coordination and monitoring services in support for Africa’s development. More information about the Office can be found at www.un.org/osaa. This position is located in the Strategic Management Unit of the Office of the Special Adviser on Africa (OSAA). Under the overall supervision of the Under-Secretary-General, the Senior Programme Management Officer will lead the strategic coordination and management of the three substantive functions of the Strategic Management Unit (programme planning, monitoring and evaluation; data and analytics; and strategic communications), to ensure the timely and quality delivery of the Unit’s institutional outputs, and to align the Unit’s contribution with the value chain of knowledge production, advocacy and policy influencing of the Office and of Programme 9 in support of Africa’s development. The incumbent will also support the USG in assuring the coherence of Programme 9 (United Nations system support for the African Union’s Agenda 2063) across its implementing entities. The incumbent serves as the principal interlocutor between the Unit’s functions, the Front Office of the Under-Secretary-General and the Director of the Division for Policy, Monitoring and Advocacy on cross-cutting organizational priorities and deliverables.

Responsibilities
Within delegated authority, the Senior Programme Management Officer will be responsible for the following duties: • Takes the lead to develop, implement and evaluate assigned programmes/projects, etc. in particular related to the integrated workplan of the Strategic Management Unit across its three substantive functions (programme planning, monitoring and evaluation; data and analytics; and strategic communications), including the establishment of common frameworks and guidance and the integration of functional workplans, and the Unit’s contribution to the value chain of knowledge production, advocacy and policy influencing of the Office and of Programme 9; directs subordinates in programme/project development, implementation, monitoring and assessment; directs review of relevant documents and reports; identifies priorities, problems and issues to be addressed and proposes corrective actions; liaises with relevant parties; identifies and initiates follow-up actions. • Ensures implementation of gender mainstreaming guidelines by assessing the implications for men and women of any planned action, including legislation, policies or programmes, in all areas and at all levels. • Directs data-based research and programme evaluation and presents information gathered from diverse sources to assess the impact of the Office’s programme and knowledge products. • Coordinates policy development regarding programme planning, data management and strategic communications workstreams within the Strategic Management Unit; reviews the assessments of issues and trends, preparation of evaluations or research activities and studies; provides senior advisory support to the Under-Secretary-General on policy, programme and institutional matters, including organizational change and reform initiatives. • Undertakes survey initiatives to assess the impact of the Office’s products; guides design of data collection instruments to optimize the efficiency and quality of the communication function and the interaction with the Office’s stakeholders and target audiences; reviews, analyzes and interprets responses, identifies problems/issues and prepares conclusions. • Supervises preparation and publication of various written and digital multimedia outputs, e.g. background papers, analysis, substantive sections of reports and studies, inputs to publications, press releases, blogs, flyers, etc., and ensures that communication products effectively visualize data and knowledge. • Directs substantive support to consultative and other meetings, conferences, etc., including proposing agenda topics, identifying participants, preparation of documents and presentations, supervising the development and implementation of event communication and advocacy plans, etc. • Manages outreach activities; liaises with stakeholders; leads coordination with institutional partners on programme planning, data and communications frameworks; designs and conducts training workshops, seminars, etc., makes presentations on assigned topics/activities. • Leads field missions, including provision of guidance to external consultants, government officials and other parties and drafting mission summaries, etc. • Coordinates activities related to budget and funding within the areas of responsibility (programme/project preparation and submissions, progress reports, financial statements, etc.) and ensures preparation of related documents/reports (pledging, work programme, programme budget, submissions to the Advisory Committee on Administrative and Budgetary Questions and to the Committee for Programme and Coordination, inputs on the substantive intergovernmental track including resolution negotiations and Programme Budget Implications, the Integrated Planning, Management and Reporting instrument, and the Strategic Management Application, etc.). • Serves as an effective spokesperson and establishes collaboration and partnerships with key officials at all levels inside and outside the Organization, including counterparts in the entities implementing Programme 9. • Ensures effective utilization, supervision and development of staff; Guides, evaluates and mentors staff • Leads team members’ efforts to collect and analyze data and provides insight to identify trends or patterns for data-driven planning, decision-making, presentation and reporting; oversees the Unit’s data governance function and code-based analytical infrastructure, ensuring that everyone can discover, access, integrate and share the data they need. • May perform other duties as required.

Competencies
PROFESSIONALISM: Ability to identify key strategic issues, opportunities and risks. Ability to generate and communicate broad and compelling organizational direction. Ability to communicate clearly links between the Organization’s strategy and the work unit’s goals. Demonstrated ability to provide innovative technical leadership by performing and/or overseeing the planning, development, and management of operations. Demonstrated ability to negotiate and apply good judgment. Able to analyze and interpret data in support of decision-making and convey resulting information to management. Shows pride in work and in achievements; demonstrates professional competence and mastery of subject matter; is conscientious and efficient in meeting commitments, observing deadlines and achieving results; is motivated by professional rather than personal concerns; shows persistence when faced with difficult problems or challenges; remains calm in stressful situations. Provides leadership and takes responsibility for incorporating gender perspectives and ensuring the equal participation of women and men in all areas of work; demonstrates knowledge of strategies and commitment to the goal of gender balance in staffing. ACCOUNTABILITY: Takes ownership of all responsibilities and honours commitments; delivers outputs for which one has responsibility within prescribed time, cost and quality standards; operates in compliance with organizational regulations and rules; supports subordinates, provides oversight and takes responsibility for delegated assignments; takes personal responsibility for his/her own shortcomings and those of the work unit, where applicable. TECHNOLOGICAL AWARENESS: Keeps abreast of available technology; understands applicability and limitation of technology to the work of the office; actively seeks to apply technology to appropriate tasks; shows willingness to learn new technology.
