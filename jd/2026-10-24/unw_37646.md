Background/Context

UN Women, grounded in the vision of equality enshrined in the Charter of the United Nations, works for the elimination of discrimination against women and girls; the empowerment of women; and the achievement of equality between women and men as partners and beneficiaries of development, human rights, humanitarian action and peace and security.

Within Serbia, UN Women provides technical assistance to national partners to implement international and national gender equality commitments, strengthen institutional mechanisms, and foster inclusive policy development.

Under the Gender Equality Facility – Phase III, UN Women is supporting national partners to integrate gender equality, non-discrimination, and accessibility standards in policymaking and EU fund management processes. Based on the request from the Ministry of Human and Minority Rights and Social Dialogue of the Republic of Serbia, UN Women seeks a consultant to conduct a comprehensive gender analysis of the green economy in Serbia, identifying opportunities, barriers and policy recommendations to advance women's participation and leadership in green sectors.

Description of Responsibilities/ Scope of Work

Under the overall supervision of UN Women Serbia’s Programme Analyst – Governance, the consultant will undertake the following tasks:

- Conduct a desk review of relevant policies, legislation, studies and available data related to gender equality and the green economy in Serbia.

- Analyze gender dimensions of employment, entrepreneurship, skills development and access to financing in selected green economy sectors.

- Conduct qualitative research (interviews and/or focus group discussions) with women working in green economy sectors to assess their experiences, opportunities and challenges.

- Identify key gender gaps, barriers and good practices.

- Develop recommendations for promoting women's participation and economic empowerment within the green transition.

- Prepare a comprehensive Gender Analysis of the Green Economy in Serbia.
