About the job
The Global Policy and Standards team is made up of subject matter experts who promote user trust and uphold Google’s policies. In this role, you will focus on driving the strategy and policy to help ensure Google has policies across its products to address egregious harms related to non-consensual intimate imagery and child safety. You will be part of a team that is part of a cross-functional effort to address child sexual abuse and exploitation online in order to protect children and NCII survivors, and to build user trust across Google and the technology ecosystem.
Individual pay is determined by factors including job-related skills, experience, and relevant education or training.

Responsibilities

- Drive the development of strategies and policies to ensure Google's products have policies for addressing egregious harms, specifically those related to child sexual abuse and exploitation.

- Develop foundational policy principles to protect child safety online. Manage high-impact policy escalations and suggest policy and process improvements.

- Work cross-functionally to develop and launch products that promote user trust and uphold Google’s policies.

- Track industry trends related to child safety, analyze policy issues facing technology platforms, and make recommendations on how to address these issues across multiple Google products.

- This role will be exposed to graphic, controversial, and/or upsetting content.
