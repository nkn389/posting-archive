The Wellcome Trust is a global charitable foundation. We improve health for everyone by funding science, leading policy and advocacy campaigns, and building partnerships.

We plan to spend £16bn over the next ten years, funding new discoveries in life, health, and wellbeing, and taking on three global health challenges: mental health, infectious disease and climate and health.

These challenges need the bold science we support, but they won’t be solved by science alone.

We have an exciting opportunity for a Product Manager (People Systems) to lead our People Systems team. You will be responsible for developing and implementing our People Systems strategy and overseeing the design, build, testing and maintenance of our systems. Your work will help make Wellcome the best place to work and support the delivery of our mission.

What will I be doing?
As Product Manager (People Systems), you will be responsible for developing and maintaining our HR systems, including Workday, to support effective processes and enhance the employee experience. In this role, you will:

- Develop and implement the People Systems strategy, ensuring that our systems underpin the delivery of our People Strategy

- Manage the People Systems roadmap and prioritisation process, ensuring that enhancements meet business and end-user needs; allocate resources; and oversee the implementation and embedding of change

- Lead the discovery, design and build of configurations across all Workday modules—including HCM, Compensation, Absence, Recruitment, Learning, Help, UK Payroll, Performance and Talent—and other supporting People Systems

- Lead the day-to-day management of all People Systems, including resolving complex issues, maintaining robust data and security controls, and managing upgrades and audit processes

- Work with product owners across the People team to ensure that systems meet business needs, maximise functionality and enhance the user experience, while supporting product owners through system upgrades

- Manage relationships with our system and application management service providers, ensuring that systems and services perform as expected

- Lead the People Systems team, supporting team members’ development and helping to make the Wellcome People team the best place to work.
