Project Context and Scope
The Displacement Tracking Matrix (DTM) gathers and analyzes data to disseminate critical multi layered information on the mobility, vulnerabilities, and needs of displaced and mobile populations that enables decision makers and responders to provide these populations with better context specific assistance. DTM has been widely recognized as one of the primary data sources on displacement, migration and human mobility across humanitarian response and development sectors including in Yemen.
Active in Yemen since 2015, DTM has successfully provide the evidence as basis for humanitarian action and expanding by generating evidence for broader human mobility through various component implemented in the country including Mobility Tracking, Flow Monitoring and Thematic Survey. In addition, the programme has also developed it capacity to support national workstream for population projection and global migratory routes.
