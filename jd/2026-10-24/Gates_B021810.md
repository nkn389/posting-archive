The Team
The Global Development (GD) Division includes a diverse range of program areas focused on finding creative ways to ensure solutions and products get into the hands of people in low-income countries. We focus on areas with the potential for high-impact, sustainable solutions that can reach millions of people. Our strategies include Emergency Response, Immunization, and Polio Eradication. A common theme across these programs is focus on innovative delivery, including an emphasis on strengthening primary health care systems. Our teams work in close partnership with Regional and Country Office Teams to align the foundation's health and development equity agenda with the government's broader priorities.

Within GD, the Exemplars in Global Health (EGH) program equips decision-makers with the evidence, insights, and practical “know-how” needed to accelerate and scale interventions that improve the health of the world's most vulnerable populations. Working with a global network of partners, EGH identifies and studies exemplars and best practices across health outcomes, primary health care systems, and service delivery strategies.

Through rigorous research, evidence synthesis, and timely analysis, EGH translates lessons from what works into actionable insights that help policymakers, practitioners, and funders strengthen systems, improve service delivery, and address pressing global health challenges.

We pursue this through:
1. The study of “positive outliers”—identifying countries or regions that have achieved exceptional progress in health outcomes and analyzing the drivers behind their success;

2. The study of emerging practices with global learning value, using prospective research and real-time analytics alongside implementation to meet the ambition of 'learning by doing';

3. The provision of internal advisory services to support teams in pursuit of the Foundation's three goals toward 2045; and

4. External partnerships to scale exemplary practices with governments, policymakers, global health initiatives, and multilateral development banks.

Your Role
The Program Officer, Maternal and Child Survival and Women’s Health (MCS + WH) manages Exemplars projects both as part of a team and independently, providing cross-cutting technical and partner facilitation support for the sub-team. The role focuses on leading projects that generate and synthesize evidence to inform strategies for maternal and child survival and women’s health, with an emphasis on translating analytics to actionable insights. The role reports to the Deputy Director, MCS + WH and will focus on developing practical ways to help priority partners generate and use learning on what works, where, and why.

Please note that relocation assistance is not available for this position.

What You'll Do

- Synthesize and package actionable insights and cross-cutting themes and lessons learned across the MCS + WH pillar to inform strategy and decisions for internal and external audiences; translate complex research findings into “what works” in clear briefs, presentations, and other materials.

- Manage the MCS + WH research and investment portfolio and lead select projects, including day-to-day execution of grants and contracts; tracking achievements, budgets, and deliverables; supporting grantee selection, scoping, and due diligence for new investments; and troubleshooting implementation challenges in partnership with grantees to keep projects on track.

- Manage and expand select relationships across the foundation, with a focus on broader women's health including Family Planning, Women's Health Innovations, and other Gender Equality bodies of work; understand key strategies, questions, evidence, debates and emerging directions.

- Support the Deputy Director with materials, analysis, and coordination on MCS + WH topics, and facilitate information-sharing across foundation teams working on related agendas as well as integration pathways and tradeoffs with other verticals and within broader health systems.

- Provide support for external engagement across MCS + WH, owning and building specific external relationships aligned with technical expertise; coordinate logistics, content, and follow-up for external meetings and convenings in the portfolio and support the Deputy Director and Senior Program Officers in preparing for leadership conversations and high-level external engagements.
