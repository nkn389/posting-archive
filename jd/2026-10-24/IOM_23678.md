Under the overall supervision of the Chief of Mission (CoM) and the direct supervision of the Project Officer (Community Resilience), and in close coordination with the Community Resilience team, the Project Associate (Community Resilience) will support the implementation of the project “Safer Schools, Stronger Shelters: Advancing Community Resilience in the Federated States of Micronesia.” The project aims to strengthen climate change adaptation (CCA) and disaster risk reduction (DRR) efforts through the mainstreaming of DRR/CCA within the education sector and the enhancement of community preparedness through Emergency Evacuation Shelters (EES). The incumbent will provide administrative, operational, logistical, monitoring and coordination support for project implementation, working closely with DECEM, FSM Department of Education (DOE), State Departments of Education, State Disaster Coordination Offices (DCOs), municipal authorities, community representatives and other project stakeholders.

EDUCATION

- Bachelor’s degree in social sciences, education, disaster risk reduction, climate change, environmental studies, community development, international development, public administration or a related field from an accredited academic institution with three years of relevant professional experience; or

- High School Diploma with five years of relevant professional experience.

EXPERIENCE

- Experience supporting project implementation, community development or humanitarian programmes.

- Experience organizing workshops, trainings, consultations and community meetings.

- Experience working with government departments, communities and development partners.

- Experience in monitoring, reporting, data collection and database management.

- Experience in administrative and operational support functions.

- Previous experience with UN agencies, NGOs or international organizations is an advantage.

SKILLS

- Good knowledge of project implementation and stakeholder coordination.

- Strong organizational and administrative skills.

- Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.

- Proficiency in Microsoft Office applications, including Word, Excel, PowerPoint and Outlook.

- Ability to maintain accurate records and prepare reports.

- Ability to work effectively in multicultural environments and remote community settings.

- Knowledge of climate change adaptation, disaster risk reduction or community resilience programming is desirable.

Languages IOM’s official languages are English, French, and Spanish.

- REQUIRED: For this position, fluency in English is required (oral and written).
- DESIRABLE: Working knowledge of local FSM languages, including Pohnpeian, Chuukese, Yapese or Kosraean, is an advantage.
