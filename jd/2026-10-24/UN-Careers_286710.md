Org. Setting and Reporting
This position is located in the CEB Secretariat, reporting to the Senior Inter-Agency Human Resources Advisor. The focus of the position is twofold: The first focus is to identify, structure and underpin data-and analytics-driven solutions in all areas of Inter-Agency Human Resources matters ranging from demographics and cost-related topics to behavioral aspects. A strong conceptual and scientific foundation is expected to underpin such solutions, in order to ensure objectivity and credibility of the proposed solutions. The second focus is to generate and communicate Human Resources-related data-driven analyses and insights in support of decision-making processes, by looking at historical trends, current data and predictions.

Responsibilities
Within delegated authority, the Data and Behavioral Scientist will be responsible for the following duties: • Works with stakeholders across the CEB organizations to identify opportunities for leveraging data to drive business solutions. • Develops and implements databases, data collection systems, data analytics and other strategies to optimize statistical efficiency and quality including automation scripts and internal software. • Applies causal inference and experimental methods such as randomized controlled trials, quasi-experimental designs, and causal modeling of observational data to estimate the impact of system-wide policies and inform evidence-based decisions. • Collects, mines, scrapes and analyzes data including those shared by organizations shared by organizations to drive optimization and improvement of inter-agency coordination in behavioral sciences, human resources, organizational management and compensation. • Assesses and tests the effectiveness and accuracy of new datasets and data processing procedures. • Develops custom predictive algorithms to apply to data sets. • Coordinates with different functional teams in CEB organizations to implement models and monitor outcomes • Performs other duties as assigned.

Competencies
Professionalism: Knowledge of the data analysis life cycle from ingest and wrangling to analysis and visualization to present findings. Excellent knowledge of statistical and computational methods, such as clustering, classification, correlation, dimension reduction, forecasting, machine learning, detecting outliers, and regression. Takes pride in the work for the organization and understands the impact that can be brought into the organization by allowing data-driven and evidence-based decisions. Ability to apply judgment in the context of assignments given, plan own work and manage conflicting priorities. Shows pride in work and in achievements; demonstrates professional competence and mastery of subject matter; is conscientious and efficient in meeting commitments, observing deadlines and achieving results; is motivated by professional rather than personal concerns; shows persistence when faced with difficult problems or challenges; remains calm in stressful situations. Takes responsibility for incorporating gender perspectives and ensuring the equal participation of women and men in all areas of work. Planning& Organizing: Develops clear goals that are consistent with agreed strategies; identifies priority activities and assignments; adjusts priorities as required; allocates appropriate amount of time and resources for completing work; foresees risks and allows for contingencies when planning; monitors and adjusts plans and actions as necessary; uses time efficiently. Accountability: Takes ownership of all responsibilities and honours commitments. Delivers outputs for which one has responsibility within prescribed time, cost and quality standards. Operates in compliance with organizational regulations and rules. Supports subordinates, provides oversight and takes responsibility for delegated assignments. Takes personal responsibility for his/her own shortcomings and those of the work unit, where applicable.

Education
Advanced university degree (Master’s degree or equivalent) in data science, computer science, information management, statistics, public administration, public information, economics, management or a related field. A first-level university degree in combination with two additional years of qualifying experience may be accepted in lieu of the advanced university degree.

Job - Specific Qualification
Not available.

Work Experience
A minimum of five years of progressively responsible experience in data science, data analytics, applied mathematics, information management or related area is required. Experience in combining data analytics and behavioral science (such as organizational psychology or behavioral economics) is required. Experience with data science and statistics-related programming languages such as R and Python, with SQL and relational database design and management is required. Experience with data governance, data quality, or data privacy and security practices is desirable. Experience with JavaScript and web frameworks, version control tools such as Git, containerization tools such as Docker and with API development and data integration are desirable. Experience in people analytics, workforce planning, or compensation analysis is desirable.
