Organizational Context and Scope
The International Organization for Migration (IOM) is the leading inter-governmental organization in the field of migration and works closely with governmental, intergovernmental, and non-governmental partners. IOM is dedicated to promoting humane and orderly migration for the benefit of all and does so by providing services and advice to governments and migrants.
IOM is a major actor in post-conflict recovery and reconstruction, and as such is heavily involved in livelihoods development and job creation programs. IOM’s strengths lie in its field presence around the country and operational capacity to rebuild community infrastructure, conduct vocational and other trainings, provide grant funding at local level to support business development, and support a variety of other areas key to recovery.
Under the overall supervision of Livelihoods Senior Programme Coordinator and the direct supervision of the Program Coordinator – Livelihoods, the incumbent will be responsible for supporting programs related to livelihoods portfolio under the area of responsibility. The post will be based in Baghdad, Iraq, with travel/roving to various regions/governorates within Iraq as needed to support livelihoods activities and teams.

Main Duties and Responsibilities:

- Support the Program Coordinator in planning, coordinating, and implementing livelihoods activities across the assigned governorates in line with approved workplans, donor requirements, and IOM policies and procedures.

- Support the Program Coordinator in the day-to-day operational follow-up of livelihoods interventions, including tracking implementation milestones and identifying delivery gaps.

- Provide day-to-day guidance and operational follow-up to the team leaders and relevant field teams to promote harmonized, timely, and quality implementation across locations.

- Support the Program Coordinator in beneficiary targeting, verification, prioritization, and selection processes to ensure transparent, criteria-based, and well-documented implementation.

- Undertake regular travel to field locations to monitor implementation progress, check the quality and timeliness of activities, identify operational bottlenecks, and support field teams in addressing implementation challenges.

- Support the Program Coordinator in strengthening the capacity of field teams through regular field presence, including on-the-job coaching, mentoring, troubleshooting, and reinforcement of standard operating procedures and tools.

- In coordination with the Program Coordinator, review field-level documentation, case files, activity records, and implementation updates to ensure completeness, accuracy, and compliance prior to onward submission and action.

- Track implementation progress against agreed plans, targets, and timelines, and followi up with relevant units on delays, payment issues, procurement actions, contractual matters, and other operational bottlenecks affecting delivery.

- Support and monitor timely and accurate data collection, consolidation, and reporting from field teams, and contribute to updates in relevant information management systems, trackers, and reporting tools.

- Conduct monitoring visits, spot checks, and validation exercises to verify implementation quality, reported progress, and strengthen compliance with agreed methodologies and standards.

- In coordination with the Program Coordinator, participate in internal and external coordination meetings with local authorities, partners, community representatives, service providers, and other stakeholders to support effective implementation and problem-solving.

- Support the Program Coordinator, when specifically delegated during the Program Coordinator’s absence, in maintaining routine operational continuity and day-to-day follow-up of livelihoods activities, while referring strategic, sensitive, supervisory, and higher-level management matters through the appropriate reporting lines.

- Perform such other duties as may be assigned by the supervisor.
