BACKGROUND AND PURPOSE OF THE ASSIGNMENT

In the context of promoting rural development and food security in Madagascar, it is essential to strengthen the capacities of farmers' organizations and to structure agricultural value chains. This will improve the competitiveness of agricultural products, increase producers' incomes, and strengthen their resilience to climate impacts.
This consultation is initiated to provide technical support to the country office teams in the implementation of the country strategic plan and technical assistance to the structures in charge of steering policies on food security, human capital and sustainable development.
The consultant will be responsible for providing technical and strategic support to farmers' organizations to develop sustainable and inclusive value chains. It will work closely with different local actors and institutional partners, including government, the private sector, and development organizations, to put in place concrete actions to improve the profitability and resilience of targeted agricultural value chains.
The candidate will also serve as Deputy Head of resilience and serve as acting head in his/her absence. Supervise, guide, and mentor the program's technical staff. This role provides technical leadership in designing, managing, and overseeing complex programs aimed at strengthening resilience against climate change, economic shocks, and food insecurity.
The Consultant will work under the supervision of the Chief of the Resilience Section and will receive guidance from the Head of Programme and Management as needed.

KEY ACCOUNTABILITIES (not all-inclusive, within delegated authority):

1. Value Chain Analysis: Identify and analyze priority agricultural value chains for rural development and food security. Assess the needs and challenges of farmers' organizations in selected value chains.
2. Capacity building of farmers' organizations: Provide training and management tools tailored to farmers' organizations to improve Supporting farmers' organizations in the development of business plans and marketing strategies.
3. Value chain development: Collaborate with the various actors in the value chain (producers, processors, transporters, etc.) to improve coordination and integration. Support the identification and implementation of solutions to reduce losses post-harvest processes, improve product quality and facilitate access to markets.
4. Governance and risk management support: Assist farmers' organizations in setting up effective governance mechanisms. Develop risk management approaches, particularly for and crop insurance tools.
5. Monitoring and evaluation: Develop a monitoring and evaluation system to measure the progress of farmers' organizations in implementing value chain strategies. Provide periodic reports on the progress of the mission, including results obtained, challenges encountered and recommendations for the future.
6. Deputy Head of Resilience Functions: Serve as Deputy Head of the Resilience Section, providing support in strategic coordination, programme oversight, stakeholder engagement, and ensuring continuity of leadership in the absence of the Chief of Resilience.
