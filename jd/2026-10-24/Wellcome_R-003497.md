The Wellcome Trust is a global charitable foundation. We improve health for everyone by funding science, leading policy and advocacy campaigns, and building partnerships. We have committed to spend more than £16 billion between 2022 and 2032. We fund discovery research and take on three global health challenges: mental health, infectious disease, and climate and health. We also work with researchers, governments, businesses and communities to help create the conditions for science to thrive and to turn evidence into better health.

Where in Wellcome will I be working?
You will be working in the Global Events Team within the Corporate Affairs/Studio Department, reporting to the Global Events Manager.

The Global Events team support events across the organisation that contribute to Wellcome’s objectives and ambitions. To live up to the priorities expected of Wellcome all events have participant wellbeing and health & safety at their core, the events are as sustainable as possible, and we aim to create a diverse and inclusive culture for all.
The team is responsible for organising in person, hybrid and virtual events; from conferences and meetings to networking workshops and multiday events, held at our office buildings, and at external venues around the UK and worldwide.

What will I be doing?
The Global Events Officer will be working in partnership with the Global Events Manager and Global Events Organisers, host areas, external event partners and suppliers to enable Wellcome to achieve its mission by supporting the team when organising a portfolio of low to high complexity events.
